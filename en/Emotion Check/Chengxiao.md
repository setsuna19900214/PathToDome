# Chengxiao Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[You see Chengxiao examining the coin hexagram. Suddenly, he turns to face you.]

Chengxiao: An interesting reading... It appears a minor tribulation is destined for you today, my friend. Not a severe one, but all the same, I'd strongly advise... keeping away from the kitchen.

- Many thanks for your wisdom, Master!

  Chengxiao: There is no need for such titles... When a friend's fate is in question, I simply find it difficult... not to pay close attention. Nothing more to it than that.

- So, what kind of "tribulation" are we actually talking about here?

  Chengxiao: Well, I suppose a small hint wouldn't spoil the surprise... If you are not extremely careful today, my friend, I suspect you will fall victim to... the culinary efforts of someone with no talent.

### Entry 2

[On a whim, you decide to organize a game of Eastian cards. You glance up and find Chengxiao standing not far off, his eyes already on you.]

Chengxiao: My friend, you're staring at me like a predator eyeing its next meal... Am I correct to assume you want something?

- Are you familiar with how card games are played these days?

  Chengxiao: Oh, that's all this is about... The rules may change, but the mathematics behind them remains predictable. I imagine... I can hold my own.

- Great, quit analyzing. We need one more. You coming?

  Chengxiao: Heaven has always been rather generous with me... my luck at cards is notoriously good. If I happen to ruin the mood... do try not to resent me for it.
