# Deren Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Deren is sitting on the couch in the Recreation Area, watching a movie.]

Deren: Are you interested in this kind of arthouse film as well?

- Interested.

  Deren: Then come over and watch it with me. It's just started.

- Not interested.

  Deren: Sigh, forget it. I'll have it all to myself.

### Entry 2

[Deren suddenly interrupts you in the middle of the conversation. She takes out a notebook and begins taking notes.]

Deren: Hold on... I have to note down what you just said.

- Why do you want to remember what I said?

  Deren: Because what you said just now is interesting. It will make a good line for the protagonist in the script I've been writing recently.

- Did I say something wrong?

  Deren: No. What you just said inspired me. I must note it down now so I don't forget it later.

### Entry 3

[The first half of the stage play rehearsal ends when the lights suddenly go out. In the darkness, you feel Deren's gaze from beside you.]

Deren: Big spectacles always go straight to people's heads. Kind of like this play, who knows what kind of mess or miracle the next act's gonna bring.

- Relax. I'll make sure everything runs.

  Deren: So you're sticking to the shadows, huh? Chief, we really are the same breed. For the stuff that actually matters, only the people on the sidelines ever get a clear look.

- I'm looking forward to it.

  Deren: Then don't be in such a hurry to slip away. I have a feeling this finale won't disappoint you, Chief.
