# McQueen Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[McQueen invites you to an art auction.]

McQueen: So, are you interested?

- Alright then, I promise you.

  McQueen: Trust me, you will be the guest that brings the biggest pleasant surprise.

- No, there's a mission today.

  McQueen: That's okay, I'll invite you again next time.
