# Yao Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Yao seems to have taken an interest in the warm light lamp in the corner of the hall.]

Yao: Is this... an imitation of the sun by humanity? A poor imitation, I might add.

- In a manner of speaking... Be careful. Don't damage the lampshade!

  Yao: With the shell removed, there's only a small spot of light underneath...

- Ah, it's just for illumination.

  Yao: Is that so? This is a far cry from what's used for illumination in my homeland...

### Entry 2

[Yao is staring out the window, an intense look on her face.]

Yao: Ah, it's you. Would you care to join me?

- Join you... in enjoying the scenery?

  Yao: Sungazing... allows a careful observer to see signs of what is to come.

- Really? What did you see then?

  Yao: The sun is radiant in a clear blue sky. An auspicious sign indeed... There will be no natural disasters in the near future.

### Entry 3

[On a street somewhere, a lantern event is taking shape. Colorful lanterns emerge under the hands of artisans while passersby hurry to and fro.]

Yao: The lantern event... is still being prepared. What kind of lanterns do you prefer?

- The brighter ones.

  Yao: Lantern light that cut deepest through the dark... Those are the ones people remember.

- The one you're holding looks lovely.

  Yao: ...Then this lantern is yours, Chief.
