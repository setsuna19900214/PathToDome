# Margaret Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Margaret waves at you, suitcase in hand.]

Margaret: I'm off on holiday to a sunny destination with the freshest air. Care to join me, Chief?

- I'd love to, but work keeps me busy.

  Margaret: You poor thing. Those old fogeys are working you to the bone. Perhaps I'll have a word with them.

- How do you get all this time off...

  Margaret: Traveling to learn local customs and meeting all sorts of important people are part of my job, you know.

### Entry 2

[Margaret is playing with a dove.]

Margaret: Care to guess what message this little one brought back?

- Intel from Fraser?

  Margaret: Always thinking about work, aren't you? It's a voucher for a banquet in DisCity. Would you do me the honor of joining me?

- Secrets about someone important?

  Margaret: An intriguing thought, but not this time. It's a voucher for a banquet in DisCity. Will you join me?
