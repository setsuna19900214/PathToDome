# Cabernet Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Today, while having lunch with Cabernet, she is staring at you thoughtfully.]

Cabernet: I wonder if I'll ever get the chance to taste Chief... I mean, a dish personally prepared by Chief?

- Tomorrow will do. I have quite some confidence in my cooking skills.

  Cabernet: Then I can't wait to feast!

- I'm sorry, I'm not very good at cooking.

  Cabernet: I see, it's understandable. After all, Chief has a lot to deal with every day.

### Entry 2

[Cabernet is watching a food show on TV, bored.]

Cabernet: If it were up to you, which dish would you prefer?

- Goldionville's specialty, White Cut Chicken.

  Cabernet: Yes, the flavor is clear and not mixed, indeed delicious and tender.

- Wine-braised Chicken.

  Cabernet: Oh? I happen to know a chef who's very good at this dish. Next time we're out on duty, I can take you to try it.
