# Hilda Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Hilda is staring at the vending machine in the lounge.]

Hilda: Does no one here keep coins for these machines anymore?

- It's all code-scan now.

- If you need coins, I can scrounge up a bag for you.

  Hilda: Sigh, to be honest, I can't even find the coin slot.
