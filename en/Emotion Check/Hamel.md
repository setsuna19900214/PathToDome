# Hamel Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[You see Hamel practicing dance moves in the Training Room.]

Hamel: Are you here... to be my audience?

- Well, when will we start preparing for the show?

  Hamel: I can do it right now if it's for you.

- I'm here to remind you that it's time for a mission.

  Hamel: Well... give me a moment to get ready.

### Entry 2

[Hamel trips on something in the bedroom.]

Hamel: Help... The bedroom... I cannot practice my dance moves there. It's too messy.

- Why practice dance moves in the bedroom?

  Hamel: Outside... my power... will disturb people.

- In that case, I'll send someone to help you clean it up later.

  Hamel: Thank you, Chief...

### Entry 3

[You receive an exclusive ball invitation from Hamel.]

Hamel: Chief, I've choreographed a new dance to celebrate this special day. I'm delighted you've agreed to come and watch me perform it.

- Just the two of us?

  Hamel: Of course, this is your special day, so I hope that within this illusion, my dance can fulfill all your wishes.

- Thank you for everything you prepared.

  Hamel: Before sunrise, just surrender your body completely to the music.

### Entry 4

[Ever since you heard Hella dragged Hamel into the band, you had been a little worried about her. But watching the rehearsal with your own eyes, you realize Hamel seems to be enjoying it.]

Hamel: Chief, so this is what a band is like? Different sounds weaving together, somehow blooming into something so vibrant and alive.

- That's the essence of an ensemble.

  Hamel: Ensemble... such a gentle word. Like holding each other's hands, stepping into the same dream together.

- Do you like this feeling?

  Hamel: Mm... it is strange to me, but... having others by my side feels really nice.
