# Murphy Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Murphy hands you a brand-new scratchcard.]

Murphy: Um... Chief, I read on the internet that a win around this time will guarantee good luck for the entire year ahead... Could you please lend me some of your luck? Just a tiny bit would be enough... Pretty please!

- Lend luck

  Murphy: Ah, sorry! I just slipped and fell while borrowing your luck... But that would mean I cleared all my bad luck during the process! I'm sure this time it'll be a win!

- Scratch it for her

  Murphy: Whoa! First prize?! Chief, you're super duper lucky! A whole year's worth of good luck is already in the bag! Don't let go of my hand. Let me borrow your luck a little longer, okay?

### Entry 2

[Murphy is hesitating over a decision.]

Murphy: I gotta head out for work today, but I really can't decide which lucky charm to wear... The daily horoscope says Mercury's in retrograde, and the almanac says inauspicious deities are on watch today. Chief, what charm do you think will protect me from doom?

- Don't wear any.

  Murphy: Ohhh... I see! With zero lucky charms, all the different kinds of bad luck will cancel each other out, and I'll end up super lucky!

- Take a few with you.

  Murphy: Great idea! Why didn't I think of that before? I'll go back and put on all my charms. I'll stay in full armor and leave no openings for bad luck!
