# Rust Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Rust sits alone by the window, nursing a drink.]

Rust: Lovely weather today. Perfect for a quiet drink.

- Want me to join you for a drink?

  Rust: I'd be honored.

- Drinking too much isn't good for you.

  Rust: Rest assured. I won't let it affect my missions.

### Entry 2

[Rust is writing something intently.]

Rust: A new mission? Please just give me a minute to finish this.

- Are you keeping a diary?

  Rust: Authentic, traceable memories are precious. I enjoy only the act of writing them down. Once it's done, I burn the pages.

- Are you writing a mission report?

  Rust: No, just some personal musings. Rest assured, I'll burn them once I'm done, leaving no traces behind. As for mission reports... you're the sole receiver of my reports, and I figure all those tedious formalities tire you.
