# Jasmine Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Jasmine is hiding something in her palm.]

Jasmine: You came to see me... Jasmine just happens to have something wonderful that I'd like to give you.

- Thank you, but you've already given me so much lately. You don't need to keep giving me things.

  Jasmine: For you, Chief, no amount of wonderful gifts could ever be too much!

- The security cameras caught you wandering the hallway for quite a while earlier. What were you up to?

  Jasmine: Mmm, I found this beautiful ball of yarn in a corner! Jasmine wanted to give it to you, but... but Jasmine couldn't help playing with it for a little while first.

[Jasmine looks at you with concern.]

Jasmine: You sneezed twice yesterday and four times today. Are you feeling unwell?

- Probably not. It's likely just dust mites and mold causing it.

- I've been working overtime a lot lately. I might be coming down with something.
