# Summer Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Summer hands you a mechanical arm, brimming with anticipation.]

Summer: Chief, come try this!

- ...I think this thing still has room for improvement.

  Summer: Well... I think you've got a point.

- Umm... do I have to chop off my arm to use it?

  Summer: Hehe... How are you going to put it on without cutting it off?

### Entry 2

[Summer is slumped down on her bedroom bed.]

Summer: Sigh... Why do people feel tired...?

- Sleep early and wake up early. Exercise more.

  Summer: Huh? Well, in that case, you might as well just kill me.

- Working the brain can also be exhausting.

  Summer: Hehe. It seems someone finally knows how to appreciate a genius like me.

### Entry 3

[A deep bass line rumbles from the rehearsal room. As you push the door open, you find Summer hard at work tweaking her instrument.]

Summer: Chief, perfect timing! The genius bassist Summer's ultimate parameter test is about to begin!

- Try not to bring the house down.

  Summer: Relax, I've already calculated the structural limit of the rehearsal room. As long as I stay under the threshold, there won't be any structural damage!

- That has real presence.

  Summer: Of course! Bass is the core driver module that holds the entire band together. Without me, the main cast's grand entrance would lose at least 37.6% of its impact!
