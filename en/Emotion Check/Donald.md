# Donald Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Donald is daydreaming, propped up against the balustrade.]

Donald: Hey, Chief, the sun's pretty great today, isn't it? Fancy coming out for a bit of sunbathing?

- Got a heap of work on my plate still...

  Donald: Hey, you know, sometimes it's okay to put work on the back burner for a bit...

- ...Okay.

  Donald: Come here, stand this way. Pretty nice, right?
