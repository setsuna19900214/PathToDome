# Ayiguo Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Ayiguo is having a chat with her spiders. The moment she sees you, she gleefully scoops one up and dangles it right before your eyes.]

Ayiguo: Little darling, look who's come to pay their respects to Granny!

- Your little darling is a real looker.

  Ayiguo: Of course. Granny doesn't keep ugly company. Careful now, don't go leaning in unless you're itching to find out what poison tastes like.

- You talk to them like old friends. You must be really close.

  Ayiguo: Not that close. Granny just hasn't found a better way to stay young forever.

### Entry 2

[Ayiguo has clearly taken great care with her outfit today. The colors and style are bold, and unapologetically so.]

Ayiguo: Staring, are we? Can't say I blame you. Granny Goldspider's fashion sense always leaves a person spellbound.

- It does fit you well!

  Ayiguo: Oh, such a charmer! Granny likes you. She really does.

- Definitely not what everyone else is wearing these days.

  Ayiguo: Trends come and go, child. Granny's been perfecting this look for centuries. Let's see their style last that long.
