# Eirene Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Eirene sets up the chess board and beckons to you.]

Eirene: Would you like to play a chess game with me?

- We can try some other games that aren't chess.

  Eirene: Hehe... There is no way for you to defeat me no matter what you do.

- Forget it. I know well that I cannot win this.

  Eirene: Don't turn me down so quickly. I'll give you a head start this time.

### Entry 2

[Eirene holds a financial statement and flips its pages.]

Eirene: Are you here to pay me for last time?

- ...What pay? Does it have anything to do with the Corruptors?

  Eirene: Your memories aren't very good. Of course, it's about disposing the giant Corruptors.

- If you put it that way... I'm afraid no one can afford that.

  Eirene: In that case, how about you use your own...

### Entry 3

[Quinn's logo flashes across your personal terminal, then an encrypted message appears.]

Eirene: I've got a deal for you that pays well. I need your answer today. So?

- Yes, President. Thank you, President.

  Eirene: Good choice. Payment's already in your account.

- May Quinn continue to prosper...?

  Eirene: Fast enough. The reward's yours.

### Entry 4

[You've received an anonymous encrypted email. The message contains only a photo of a chessboard, but you know it's from Eirene.]

Eirene: Chief, if you were playing as White in this losing position, what would your next move be?

- Let me get a read on the situation first.

  Eirene: Before making a move, you should first know where you stand. And whether you are the one moving the pieces, or merely one of them.

- What have you been up to behind my back?

  Eirene: Relax, it's just an invitation. Whether you want to be part of the game has always been entirely up to you.
