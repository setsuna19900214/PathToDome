# Demon Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Demon is cleaning his carbide armor.]

Demon: Maybe try this one? It should be able to handle many emergencies.

- In that case... I might as well give it a try.

  Demon: Alright. I'll go prepare a suit that fits you.

- I don't mind it, but it's heavy. It will make me clumsy.

  Demon: Sigh. What a pity.
