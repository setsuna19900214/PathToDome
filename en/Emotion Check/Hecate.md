# Hecate Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Hecate asks you about today's schedule.]

Hecate: Chief, what will you have me do today?

- Today's order is to rest well.

  Hecate: Yes. Got it.

- We don't have any suitable missions for you today just yet.

  Hecate: If there are no orders... what purposes are left in my life...?

### Entry 2

[Hecate is checking the records of past operations.]

Hecate: If I am gone, there will always be another Hecate. When that happens, I need you to forget me.

- I'll try my best to get used to it.

  Hecate: Please... discard all your concerns.

- You are irreplaceable.

  Hecate: ...I hope the same tragedy doesn't happen again this time.

### Entry 3

[Hecate seems surprised and uneasy at your unexpected visit.]

Hecate: Chief, the path ahead is blocked. Let me show you another way.

- Why is it blocked?

  Hecate: Because... because there are surprises Chief can't know about yet.

- Don't let them do anything dangerous, okay?

  Hecate: Yes, we've discussed it. They're all low-risk surprises, Chief, don't worry.

### Entry 4

[Having fallen asleep unconsciously, you awaken to find Hecate, who's been keeping watch over you for some time.]

Hecate: Chief, you're finally back. Everyone has missed you while you were away...

- I'm back.

  Hecate: Welcome home.

- It's been a while. How has everyone been doing?

  Hecate: We've all been taking good care of ourselves, but... we were all hoping you'd come back soon, Chief. Everyone is waiting for you.
