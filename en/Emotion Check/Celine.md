# Celine Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Celine repairs an old tome's page corner with tweezers.]

Celine: Chief, could you pass me the tool? If I move even a hair, I'll tear the page...

- Hand over the adhesive

  Celine: There. All set. This fragile old thing survives another year. To celebrate and to thank you, shall I pour us some tea?

- Hand over the restoration paper

  Celine: Filling in what's missing is its own kind of inheritance. See? The page is damaged, but the words endure. As long as someone cares enough to restore them, history never really fades...

### Entry 2

[Celine sits by the window, reading.]

Celine: You're here. Did you already finish the book you borrowed? You read even faster than I expected.

- "Dusk of Nations"

  Celine: Good taste. But books only take you so far... If you want the deeper story, come by my place. I've collected materials with secrets the printed version never touched.

- "A Brief History of Nightfall"

  Celine: Mm... curious about that dark chapter? The book softens it—reality was far harsher. Come sit with me, Chief. Let me read it to you.
