# Langley Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Langley sits on the seat in your office.]

Langley: Rookie, how is your job going?

- I'm doing my part for world peace.

  Langley: Good. But remember to watch yourself out there, rookie.

- I've carried out the Bureau's plan on schedule today.

  Langley: Oh? It's mediocre, but that's not enough.

### Entry 2

[Langley looks weary.]

Langley: Rookie, go make a cup of coffee for me.

- I think you need a rest more than a coffee.

  Langley: It's a mediocre proposal, rookie.

- You can try the Bureau's ground coffee.

  Langley: Good. The coffee's pleasant smell alone is enough to brighten up my mood.

### Entry 3

[You discover Langley's message on the performance review board.]

Langley: I've reviewed your report. Well done, Chief of Minos. You're now capable enough to live up to your title. I should prepare a congratulatory gift for you. It'll be presented at your upcoming inauguration ceremony.

- Thank you, I'm looking forward to it.

  Langley: See you at the celebration.

- So, do I get to pick my gift?

  Langley: Heh, looks like you've gotten bolder too. But that's fine. I like it when you take the lead.
