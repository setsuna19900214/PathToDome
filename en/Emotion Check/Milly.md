# Milly Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Milly cradles a romance novel, enraptured by its pages.]

Milly: Their love... it's so moving...

- Sounds like a good story. Can I read it when you're done?

  Milly: Of course! I have plenty more.

- But I've heard that love is always romanticized in fiction.

  Milly: That's because everyone longs for true love!

### Entry 2

[Milly sighs as she twirls a strand of hair around her finger.]

Milly: Has my hair gotten frizzy lately...? It doesn't seem as shiny as it used to be...

- Your hair is beautiful, like the golden sunshine.

  Milly: You're so sweet, Chief. You just made my day!

- Someone recommended a conditioner to me. It's even honey-scented.

  Milly: Oooh! I'll try it tonight!
