# Lichen Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Lichen reads a story about the phoenix's self-immolation and rebirth, and appears lost in thought.]

Lichen: If I burn this body to ashes, could I welcome a new beginning...? I wish to find out for myself...

- That's just a legend. There's no scientific basis for it.

  Lichen: Wise words, young friend... The greatest virtue of heaven and earth is the gift of life...

- Wait, you're actually trying it?! Y-You're on fire!

  Lichen: Ah... Nothing happened... The water remains above fire, and this matter remains incomplete...

### Entry 2

[Lichen shakes her ritual bell and is seemingly preparing for divination.]

Lichen: It appears I have nothing arranged for today... Perhaps I shall give a free divination for the first Chief that wanders through...

- O great master, may I know what my future holds?

  Lichen: Hmm... Young friend, you are no ordinary soul. I am not to disclose heaven's secrets... but if you simply do more good deeds...

- I decide my own fate, not the heavens.

  Lichen: One who stands beyond the hexagrams... So be it... I shall offer you my blessings, young friend...
