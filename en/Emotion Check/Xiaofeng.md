# Xiaofeng Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Xiaofeng approaches with a languid stretch, her mood clearly lifted. Her steps are light, the trap and chain on her ankle chiming softly with each stride.]

Xiaofeng: Mm... What a great nap! Hey, you wanna—wait, what are you staring at?

- Only you would turn this into an accessory.

  Xiaofeng: This thing on my foot? Hah! Stepped right on it while I was messing around. Looked pretty mean, so I figured—why not keep it? Hahahaha!!

- Isn't that a bear trap? Doesn't it hurt?

  Xiaofeng: This little thing? Please! Can't even tickle me. You really think that's gonna take me down?

### Entry 2

[Xiaofeng is sprawled across your office desk, limbs splayed in every direction. An empty liquor flask lies tipped over on the floor. With a resigned sigh, you walk over—]

Xiaofeng: —WHOA-ROAR!!!

- Ahhh!! Were you just pretending to be asleep?!

  Xiaofeng: Ahahaha! No way, that actually scared you? I really need to toughen you up when I get the chance!

- ...?

  Xiaofeng: Oh? You're not backing down? Ha! That's exactly why I took a liking to you, Tiny!

### Entry 3

[With the addition of several sociable Eastian Sinners, the Bureau's festivities have become even more vibrant.]

Xiaofeng: Hey, look who's here! Whoa, you've gotten even skinnier! And here I thought you couldn't get any thinner. C'mon, c'mon, I'm dragging you to the feast to stuff you full of good food!

- You got everything ready this quickly?

  Xiaofeng: Obviously! The really good stuff's being saved for later. But first, food in you, right now!

- Lead the way!

  Xiaofeng: Now that's what I like to hear! We're not stopping till we've eaten 'em out of house and home!
