# Yingying Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[A melodious song echoes through your office.]

Yingying: Did you enjoy my song, Chief? What would you like to hear next?

- I enjoy them all. Your singing helps me stay focused.

  Yingying: That's no surprise. Back in my hometown, people would pay a fortune just to hear me sing!

- Would you mind singing that song again?

  Yingying: I knew we shared the same taste, Chief! That one happens to be my favorite song!

### Entry 2

[Yingying is sewing a sachet for good fortune.]

Yingying: Oh, Chief! You're here! Help me pick out a color for the sachet cord!

- Use whichever color you like.

  Yingying: Oh? Are you saying you'll like anything I like?

- The color of wisteria would be nice.

  Yingying: In that case, I'll have to choose carefully! Now, which shade matches wisteria the best?

### Entry 3

[A potted plant with an unusual fragrance has appeared beside your desk. Upon closer inspection, its newly sprouted branches look strangely familiar.]

Yingying: Other than at the Wisteria Fete, the Wisteria Fairy will grant a wish to the first person who finds her on this special day. Who will be the lucky one?

- [Pretend not to hear]

  Yingying: Don't go, Chief! Seeing as you're the only person here, this wish is all yours. What would you like?

- Who could it be?

  Yingying: It's you, of course! Tell me, Chief, on the first special day we're spending together, what would you wish for?

### Entry 4

[The faint scent of wisteria blossoms wafts through the air, and before you realize it, you find yourself back at the wisteria fete.]

Yingying: Chief, when we meet my moms later, they're definitely gonna trap you with all their questions. Are you nervous?

- There's nothing to be nervous about.

  Yingying: That's true. You're always so good to me. There's nothing to worry about.

- Should I hold your hand?

  Yingying: Of course! What if we get separated here and the Wisteria Blossom deity sweeps you away? You may never find your way back, Chief.
