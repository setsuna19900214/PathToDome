# Hella Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Having finished her mission, Hella lies on the floor in your office.]

Hella: Ah, have you prepared any rewards for me? I hope it's DisCoins.

- What about the snacks from the Supply Office? This is all I have.

  Hella: Hehe. Now that's more like it. Be sure to bring more next time.

- No. But I might think about it when you've finished the mission.

  Hella: Huh? You are arrogant for a Bureau Chief. Don't you dare go back on your words once the mission is complete!

### Entry 2

[Hella looks surprised when she sees you.]

Hella: Tsk. Did Ninety-Nine tell you I was hiding here?

- That's right. She told me about it.

  Hella: Stop lying! Ninety-Nine will never betray me.

- I found you myself.

  Hella: Humph. You are... kind of impressive.

### Entry 3

[You find a voicemail from Hella in your inbox.]

Hella: Hey, dummy! Guess where I am right now?

- [Add new contact: Hella Device No.3]

  Hella: Forget it, you wouldn't know where it is anyway. Don't tell Hecate, but I'll be back in six days!

- [Add to favorites]

  Hella: I'm in WhiteSands! After leaving Tuco, I've run into many strange things. Once I sort everything out, I'll come back to see you! Wait for me, okay?

### Entry 4

[You return to the Bureau after a long absence and push open your office door. Hella is sprawled shamelessly in your chair. You notice your desk has been tidied up.]

Hella: Oh, finally remember the Bureau's door code, big dummy? A little later and this office would've been officially renamed Hella's turf.

- Thanks for keeping my office clean and tidy.

  Hella: Hmph, I didn't do it for you! I just couldn't stand how dusty this place was. You should do it yourself from now on!

- Have you been waiting for me here?

  Hella: W-who said I was waiting for you? I just happened to be patrolling around here when you got back, that's all!
