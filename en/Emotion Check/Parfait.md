# Parfait Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Parfait sits with her coffee cup, listening to the day's financial reports in quiet contentment.]

Parfait: Oh, my dear friend, please sit! Come listen to today's breaking news with me! I also have some freshly imported beans, here, try this—

- Is this coffee? Why does it taste sweet and sour...

  Parfait: Delicious, isn't it? I added some special coffee creamer that an Eastian gave me. All good flavors deserve to be enjoyed—it's such a waste to let them go unused!

- You've been listening for so long, did you gain anything from it?

  Parfait: Peaceful moments, wonderful drinks, and you coming to my side, that's the greatest gain of all.

### Entry 2

[Parfait invites you to appreciate an unfamiliar sonata.]

Parfait: Listen—the crisp rhythms reveal the composer's clarity of mind, while the unconventional melodies are bold yet harmonious, reflecting an interesting and unique artistic spirit. Truly a masterpiece!

- Who's the maestro who composed this piece? Do you know them?

  Parfait: Aha! Such excellent taste! It's by an emerging composer I recently discovered. How about we broadcast this heavenly piece at the Bureau? Let's give everyone a bit of cultural enrichment!

- It's alright, I guess. I can't really tell what makes it so unique...

  Parfait: That's rather disappointing, my friend. This is a new composer's debut work, but I have faith that she'll eventually create pieces everyone can truly appreciate!
