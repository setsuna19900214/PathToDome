# EMP Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[In the training facility, EMP seems happy.]

EMP: Humph. Today, I'll allow you to look at my magnificent face and admire it.

- Well then, the Uncrowned Ruler, what will we be doing today?

  EMP: Of course, we are going to play some games! Whether it's poker, dice, or mahjong. I'm fine with anything!

- ...If you keep up with this lackadaisical attitude, I'll send you to the Rust for missions.

  EMP: Eek! Just kidding... I'm just kidding!

### Entry 2

[In the training facility, EMP seems to be in a bad mood.]

EMP: Don't disrupt me. I'm practicing my archery!

- It's useless anyway. You don't need to practice.

  EMP: I don't want it! This looks much better!

- Are you sure it's fine that the Archer Queen doesn't know how to shoot arrows?

  EMP: Well, do you find any eggs in eggplants?
