# Augustus Emotion Check

### Entry 1

[Augustus spins a car key around her fingertip.]

Augustus: Care to go for a ride with me?

- Sure, I could use an outdoor break.

  Augustus: Come on, let's go have some fun.

- Are you trying to make me play hooky?

  Augustus: That's right. Should I kidnap you and make you my subordinate instead?

### Entry 2

[Augustus is inspecting the Training Room.]

Augustus: Is that all? Your training facilities could use some improvements.

- I think it's adequate. The Bureau isn't the military.

  Augustus: You're far too lenient with your subordinates.

- Then are you offering to fund said improvements?

  Augustus: No problem, I've got plenty of weapons to spare.

### Entry 3

[Augustus personally delivers your medal of honor.]

Augustus: For a rookie, Chief, your performance has been quite impressive. So, will you come with me willingly, or should I have my people take you? Though I suppose staying for your own celebration wouldn't be such a bad idea.

- So, care to join the celebration with me?

  Augustus: I was hoping you'd ask. Give me your hand.

- Thank you for the recognition, but let's enjoy the celebration first before you get other ideas.

  Augustus: What a shame, but what I want will ultimately be mine. For now, accept this honor I've prepared especially for you.

### Entry 4

[The celebration hasn’t begun yet, but Augustus has already changed into formal attire, pacing through the dim ballroom before the lights come on.]

Augustus: The war is over, so the victory banquet must not be taken lightly.
Chief, don’t go soft at a moment like this. A celebration after triumph is what lifts morale the most.

- No problem.

  Augustus: Then we'll lead the opening dance at the celebration together.

- I'm actually a bit tired.

  Augustus: I respect your honesty. Then your place is at my side during the celebration.
