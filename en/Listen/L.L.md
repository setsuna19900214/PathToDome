# L.L. Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

L.L.: Oh my, what a dull and gloomy place this is! Why don't we all forget our worries and have a good time? Let's do what the animals do: wear nothing, think about nothing, and just live in the moment!

#### Raise to Phase 1

L.L.: I wish our bond will be more unbreakable~

#### Raise to Phase 3

L.L.: Sleep and sink into this blue in total tranquility... Who will shatter this fanciful reverie?

#### Level Start

L.L.: Are you here to entertain me? That's great!

#### Victory

L.L.: What a pitiful sight! So many broken legs and arms. That's one big mushy mess...

#### Lose Battles

L.L.: How dare you cause trouble on my island?

#### Ultimate Skill

L.L.: Enjoy the release!

#### Die in Battles

L.L.: Goodbye, my everything...

#### Greeting

L.L.: Hehe, I like how we're tied up together now by this red string. If I want to, I can simply pull this string and take you to a happy land that only belongs to the two of us.

#### Conversation 1

L.L.: Here on Elysiland, whatever you want to do can be satisfied! You can fall in love, you can feast and drink, pick up fights, or just lie around and do nothing. As you wish! Everything here is unrestricted, and the only thing that matters is to have fun!

#### Conversation 2

L.L.: What's so good about the outside world? Can you be this free out there? Can you be this happy? I have so many friends here; I'm not lonely at all. I don't want to go outside and neither do you. Stay here with me!

#### Conversation 3

L.L.: If you open your eyes in the depths of the sea, you'll see... Never mind. No one ever dares to do that.

#### Special Conversation 1

L.L.: Those fools. They were having a good time with me underwater, but as soon as they heard someone calling their names from above, they swim as hard as they can until they get back to the shore. And I'm all alone again...

#### Special Conversation 2

L.L.: Both the sky and sea should be boundless, right? But why is my Elysiland surrounded by invisible walls? Is it because I still don't have enough friends here? Yes, that must be it!

#### Special Conversation 3

L.L.: Dive into the water with me. Let the water embrace you, merge with it. Listen to my heartbeat, be one with me...

#### Full Compliance

L.L.: No bait, no hook, but you successfully caught a beautiful little fish. She's happy, because now not only does she own the underwater world but also a smile, a hug, a person connected to her. Hold my hand. Let's go watch the sunrise together! I won't turn into foam or sink to the bottom of the sea. I'll always be here.

#### Idle

#### Annoyed

#### Special Attention

### Outfit 1: Abyssal Phantom

#### Attire Obtained

L.L.: Every shell, every pearl, every little fish on this stage was born for me. And now, you are here as well... Come, swim with us! Let us leave the dull reality behind, outside of the curtain.

#### Level Up

L.L.: Keep your eyes on me! Under this spotlight, I alone am the star of the show!

#### Greeting

L.L.: It is such a bore to have empty stairs as your partner... You'd better come earlier next time, or else... I don't know what I might do out of sheer boredom.

#### Ordinary Contact 1

L.L.: Look at those Betta fish... They lock eyes, raise their fins, and close in. They always confirm whether their opponent will flee before striking. They remind me of myself, especially on stage. When the lights focus, and the music reaches a crescendo... that is the most exhilarating moment, one that I will never miss.

#### Ordinary Contact 2

L.L.: This is just a rehearsal—why are you so on edge? Is being alone with me making you nervous... or are you afraid that I might devour you?

#### Idle

L.L.: Hmm? Why are you standing there motionless? Are you acting as a piece of coral in the scenery? Then... may I carve my name on you? That way... you'll be forever mine!

#### Annoyed

L.L.: ...Ah! You almost made me miss my cue! Only naughty audience members would deliberately disturb the performers, you know! Just so you are aware though, in this theater, catching the performer's attention isn't a good thing... Hehe, just kidding.

#### Special Attention

L.L.: In most plays, the little mermaid ultimately loses her voice, and even her life for love... But if that love is for you, then I am willing to store all my songs in shells and place them in your hands, along with the dagger.
