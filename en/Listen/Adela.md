### Outfit 1: Woodland Wanderer

#### Attire Obtained

#### Level Up

Adela: Fly high and far, to guide those who would drown in their sorrows.

#### Greeting

Adela: Here, let me remove this leaf from your hair... There's no need to be hasty, the banquet hasn't begun yet.

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Adela: Don't be afraid. The swallow-tailed kite is my eyes and my companion. See how its forked tail scissors, slicing through the locks of dark mist to guide you, ripping through the curtains of the banquet, bringing you to my side so that we could meet...

Adela: The bells of the banquet will soon ring, but the path is steeped with crawling branches. You need a clean, wide road where you can run freely. Please, allow me to stay by your side and trim them away for you. I would be delighted to be of service.
