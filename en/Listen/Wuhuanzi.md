#### Greeting

#### Conversation 1

Wuhuanzi: An herbalist should be altruistic, using medicine to remedy the world... Yet herbalists need money to live, bread to survive—hence treatment fees. While fees may vary, I charge nothing at all, hoping to gain the merit I seek... But why do I still see so few patients?

#### Conversation 2

Wuhuanzi: I know I don't look trustworthy enough for patients... I've tried everything—fake beards, traditional doctor's banners—but none have helped me gain any merit... Do I really have to brew some tonic to age myself by forty years?

#### Conversation 3

Wuhuanzi: No matter how prepared I am with an umbrella, I always run into someone who needs it more when it rains... Fate works in mysterious ways, far beyond comprehension. I've long since given up struggling against it...

#### Special Conversation 1

Wuhuanzi: The ancient medical texts say that when we reach a dead end, exploring new lands and customs can lead to new treatments and insights, an approach that never fails... This is why I embarked on this quest, seeking an answer—or perhaps, someone who can provide one...

#### Special Conversation 2

Wuhuanzi: I've been asked why I dare to defy fate... "If Death marks someone for midnight, who can keep them till dawn?" But I believe, if all things follow the natural order, then aren't my actions also a part of it? If I can save a life... doesn't that mean Death never intended to claim them?

#### Special Conversation 3

Wuhuanzi: Only after leaving my mountain sanctuary did I realize—while physical wounds are easy to treat, mending a broken spirit is far more difficult. Medicine alone cannot save those lost in despair... And I thought DisCity faced the same fate. But your deeds have shown me otherwise. Could you... teach me?

#### Full Compliance

Wuhuanzi: The world crumbles, and lives fade like frail weeds. I understand—I can save one, but not all. I can delay death, but not defeat it. They speak of an herbalist's touch reviving life, yet it is merely a futile fixation to defy fate. Nevertheless, what of it? Only a stubborn fool would try to remove a fallen tower and build it anew... Isn't that true, kindred spirit?
