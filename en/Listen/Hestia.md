### Outfit 1: Lambent Wishbearer

#### Attire Obtained

Hestia: Haste not, wish maker. The ritual is about to begin and the flame of wishes shall soon be kindled.

#### Level Up

Hestia: The lanterns of wishes are floating high...

#### Greeting

Hestia: Finally made it, did you? Worry not, there's still time... Is this your carefully made lantern... Well, despite its rather "unique" structure, it'll fly high into the sky. I'll help you.

#### Pat Head

Hestia: I-is this how mortals show affections?

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Hestia: Back on my first trial in the mortal realm, I struggled to find my way... But despite my blindness, I was able to see with my heart the care and love the villagers had for me... Perhaps that's what the Divine Emperor intended for me to understand...

#### Ordinary Contact 2

Hestia: Mortals possess unwieldy bodies, vulnerable to poison and destined to age, as frail as this light. Yet, they can warm others with their flickering flames, even someone as strange and blind as me. Therefore, I do not wish for their light to fade...

#### Ordinary Contact 3

Hestia: These lanterns are handmade by the villagers, each wish carefully written, stroke by stroke. Hung on the wisteria tree year after year, their heartfelt devotion alone is enough to lift them into the sky—even without my "extra help"...

#### Ordinary Contact 4

Hestia: The light flickers—sometimes bright, sometimes dim. The lanterns, too, come in all shapes: some delicate, some rough. Yet the vibrant festivity of this once-a-year event and the people's devotion to their wishes always shine through. Perhaps that's what it means when they say, "Our days are numbered, but our hearts remember..."

#### Idle

Hestia: Fear not... The flame will not burn you. Instead, it will bring warmth and carry you to the place your creator's heart intended for you to go...

#### Special Attention

Hestia: Your gaze ignites more fiercely than the lantern... resembling a bonfire or a blazing torch. It's like a mortal flame so captivating that even immortals can't bear to look away.

### Outfit 2: Dusk Aglow

#### Attire Obtained

Hestia: I am the priestess for the god of the Hearth, keeper of the sacred flame within this temple. Come, entrust your lost wishes to me, and I shall use the divine firelight to illuminate your path forward.

#### Level Up

Hestia: May their divine will shine through this place.

#### Greeting

Hestia: Whenever you come near, I hear the torch crackle and pop. That's the sound of the sparks dancing with joy. It means the god's holy fire enjoys your presence here... and so do I.

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Hestia: I will remain at the temple, praying day and night for that world of legends to return—a world where children worry about nothing and aren't forced to be brave in desperate situations, a world where adults protect children and allow them to grow up safely!

#### Annoyed

#### Special Attention

Hestia: I hear footsteps in the corridor... are you standing beside me? If you don't speak up, I'll just assume you want to stay and keep me company after the sun sets.
