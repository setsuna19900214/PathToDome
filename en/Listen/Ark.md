# Ark Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

Ark: Just tell me what you need! Don't worry, anything we have on board is yours to use! ...Huh? Illegal use of public property? Is it the Ark that you're after? I see. Then take this cannonball instead!

#### Raise to Phase 1

Ark: A mechanical arm upgrade? This a thank-you for that wool blanket last time? Zulu Zulu! Thanks a bunch!

#### Raise to Phase 3

Ark: This is my ship, my home, our settlement on the Ark! Anyone who wants to touch it, talk to my cannons first!

#### Level Start

Ark: The Ark's cannons are itching to fire!

#### Victory

Ark: Let's see who still dares to mess with my family!

#### Lose Battles

Ark: Turn the helm and set course for full retreat! Don't worry, I know when to turn back. Nothing is more important than your safety!

#### Ultimate Skill

Ark: Fire the main cannons!

#### Die in Battles

Ark: Don't be afraid... The Ark will get you to safety...

#### Greeting

Ark: Zulu Zulu! Welcome to my home! Don't judge these cushions woven from old canvas sail threads by the aunties. They're super soft! Sit here and have some sandberry tea Granny dried on the deck. Look at those dark circles under your eyes! You look more haggard than a lookout who's been on the mast all night!

#### Conversation 1

Ark: Don't take me for some dangerous outlaw! It's not like I enjoy fighting, but in WhiteSands, you only earn the right to negotiate by winning the fight. In this lawless land, firepower is the only law, got it?

#### Conversation 2

Ark: When I was little, my parents taught me that *Bravo Zulu* was the highest praise you could give to someone on a ship. That's why I dart around the deck all day using it with everyone I meet. Even though they don't understand it, they seem happy! It's become a catchphrase on the Ark. Try saying it? Zulu Zulu!

#### Conversation 3

Ark: Our settlement is usually quite chaotic. Accents, backgrounds, and habits often clash. I have to break up arguments all the time. Gives me such a headache! Once the ship starts sailing, everyone looks in the same direction. These people are my family, and I love every single one of them.

#### Special Conversation 1

Ark: It's hard to get a good night's sleep in WhiteSands, with sand and sparks flying everywhere. But in those rare quiet moments, the ship feels like it's floating in the air. The breathing, the footsteps, the vibrations, and the humming all become so clear. Makes me realize that life can also be peaceful. Unfortunately, such moments are hard to come by.

#### Special Conversation 2

Ark: Many people say I am the Ark itself, its incarnation, the soul of the sand ship. Sounds silly, right? But having heard it so many times, I've started to wonder. Maybe if I really were the ship, if I were a weapon, then I wouldn't fear failing to protect my family anymore, or lie awake at night after firing the cannons. Zulu Zulu, guess I'm just as silly.

#### Special Conversation 3

Ark: If there's really a place in this world where we could protect our home without these cannons, I'd want to go there with you. But don't try to fool me! I know it'd be a long journey. Until we get there, you better take good care of yourself! If things get rough, just get behind me and let me ram right through the obstacles!

#### Full Compliance

Ark: The Ark survives because of its firepower, yet that same firepower makes us coveted. A vicious cycle we can't escape, just like the endless WhiteSands surrounding us. I must keep sailing, keep firing. There's no shore in sight, no way to stop... But then you came along. Thank you, for being Ark's shore.

#### Idle

Ark: Oh, you've really fallen asleep, Chief... I'll leave you alone then.

#### Annoyed

Ark: Zulu Zulu, I'm here, it's alright... Auntie says I have this bad habit too. I can't help touching you to confirm you're really right beside me, only then can I feel at ease.

#### Special Attention

Ark: It's just another voyage. No need to make a fuss! I understand how painful it is to see your family get hurt. I won't make you go through that!

#### Dispatch

Ark: Oh, I know everything about supplies! Let me show you my skills as a housekeeper! After all, everything on the Ark, however big or small, depends on me!

#### Dispatch Complete

Ark: I'm back! There weren't many routes the ship could take, so I had to go a bit out of the way. But I brought you some souvenirs, enough to fill the entire deck!

#### Level Up

Ark: Zulu Zulu! Now you won't get seasick anymore!

#### Join Team

Ark: More fools who don't know better than to target us? Don't worry, I'll... blast them all to pieces!

#### Upgrade Skill

Ark: A mechanical arm upgrade? This a thank-you for that wool blanket last time? Zulu Zulu! Thanks a bunch!
