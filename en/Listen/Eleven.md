### Outfit 1: Rippling Heartthrobs

#### Attire Obtained

Eleven: You finally woke up! How do you feel right now? Still dizzy or nauseous? Thank goodness you're all right. You passed out from heatstroke. I was scared out of my wits... Luckily, I was close by, so I rushed to bring you back to rest.

#### Level Up

Eleven: Thanks for the cold drink. Let's cool off together. Cheers!

#### Greeting

Eleven: If you're still feeling unwell, how about I keep you company and talk to you for a while? My voice might make you feel better.

#### Pat Head

Eleven: Are you worried I might come down with heatstroke too? Hehe... don't worry about me. I'll be fine.

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Eleven: Have you ever heard of the "inside-out cooling method"? It's said that listening to creepy stories is a good way to cool down in summer. Let's give it a go. Well, I happen to have a story about this very beach. It's always bustling during daytime, but when night falls, strange things happen. Just the other night, a staff member was patrolling the grounds when...

#### Ordinary Contact 2

Eleven: Don't stray from the safe area while you're in the water, and remember to do a proper warm-up before going in for a dip. See those guys over there? Like you did earlier, they jumped into the water without warming up first and ended up with cramps in their legs. The other lifeguards are rescuing them now...

#### Ordinary Contact 3

Eleven: Still feeling hot? Okay, this towel may help. I've soaked it in cold water and wrung it out. Here, let me wipe your forehead and neck to cool you down... Feeling better now?

#### Ordinary Contact 4

Eleven: No. You're not fully recovered yet, so it's not a good idea for you to go back in the water, not even with a lifebuoy. If you're feeling bored, why don't you play with this little octopus plushie? You'll find it very satisfying to squeeze it.

#### Idle

Eleven: Even though you can't go in the water for now, it's still nice to just lie here under a parasol, watching the clouds drift by, feeling the breeze on your cheeks, and listening to the waves, don't you think?

#### Special Attention

Eleven: Why do you keep staring at me? You're at the seaside. Shouldn't you be enjoying the sea view? Well, if you really want to talk with me some more, we can take a walk along the beach after my shift. All right then, wait for my call.

### Outfit 2: Trickling Heartstream

#### Attire Obtained

Eleven: Now, please entrust every part of yourself to the scent of the incense, the flow of water, and the sound of my voice. Let me help you forget all your tiredness.

#### Level Up

Eleven: Every sound has drawn us even closer together.

#### Greeting

Eleven: I've been waiting for you. What would you like to hear today? A new melody, a verse, or a story that never ends...

#### Pat Head

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Eleven: Close your eyes. Now, slowly bring your attention to breathing. Breathe in... and out... You're doing great, now once more... Hmm? You're not quite focused, are you? Is it because of me?

You can hear the soft tinkling of water beside you as it trickles down the stream. Surrender your thoughts—just let this tranquil scene come to life in your mind and savor the warmth and comfort of this peaceful moment...

#### Ordinary Contact 3

Eleven: I wanted to create a relaxing, gentle, and soothing space for you. So, I chose the softest clothes, warmed up the room a bit, and adjusted the humidity so you can hear every sound even more clearly... Feels like I'm here waiting for you to come home and open your heart to me, doesn't it?

#### Ordinary Contact 4

Eleven: Do you like the scent of this candle? It's as if we're surrounded by a sea of late-spring flowers. And we're tucked away there, whispering little secrets that belong only to us, just like we're doing right now.

#### Idle

Eleven: What's on your mind? No, nothing... just a sudden urge to let you know that I am always here, by your side.

#### Special Attention
