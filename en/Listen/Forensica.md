# Forensica Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

Forensica: Forensica, forensic pathologist. Let me be clear from the start—I have zero patience for the living.

#### Raise to Phase 1

Forensica: One step closer... to the truth...

#### Raise to Phase 3

Forensica: You won't escape... this time.

#### Level Start

Forensica: The investigation begins.

#### Victory

Forensica: Work's done. Time to file the report.

#### Lose Battles

Forensica: This makes no sense... what did I miss...?

#### Ultimate Skill

Forensica: In the name of the dead!

#### Die in Battles

Forensica: My body... will reveal... the truth...

#### Greeting

Forensica: You're here. Any new cases today? I'm heading back if there's nothing to do.

#### Conversation 1

Forensica: I like staying in the autopsy room. Corpses are far more pleasant to look at than all those idiots wandering around here.

#### Conversation 2

Forensica: Another complaint about me from the new assistant? What did I do? I only told her, "Don't beat yourself up for messing up. This is not the first time anyway." I was trying to comfort her. ...Um, what? Is there a problem?

#### Conversation 3

Forensica: A forensic pathologist's job is more than just autopsies—it's also crime scene investigation. From contaminating evidence and tampering with scenes, to influencing conclusions... those with ulterior motives can interfere from the very start. You're doing a good job keeping this place clean. For now, at least.

#### Special Conversation 1

Forensica: Terrible personality? Harsh words? Hard to work with? I'm here to work, not to babysit my coworkers. They could learn a thing or two from corpses—at least the dead don't complain this much.

#### Special Conversation 2

Forensica: Corpses decay, but the patience of the living is even more fleeting. If you can't find the truth in time, you're stuck with living out the rest of your days with no answers. That's... what it means to be a forensic pathologist.

#### Special Conversation 3

Forensica: The dead are always the most vulnerable in this world—they're dismissed by society and abandoned by their families. Sometimes... I'm the only voice they have left.

#### Full Compliance

Forensica: Corpses never lie. It's always the living who do. I'm not staying here because I've regained faith in what the living call justice... I'm staying because I trust you. I trust you'll give me the chance to speak the truth in their stead.

#### Idle

Forensica: Dorothy, the cutest little bone in the whole world... Hmm, you're getting a bit fuzzy here. Let me fix that for you...

#### Annoyed

Forensica: Stop poking around. These are autopsy tools. You won't be needing them.

#### Special Attention

Forensica: Why do you keep staring at me? Is there something on my face? You're distracting me from my thoughts...

#### Dispatch

Forensica: What a pain... Can't I just go alone?

#### Dispatch Complete

Forensica: Got you the things you wanted. You promised to give me three days off. Leave me alone while I'm away. I'm not seeing anyone.

#### Level Up

Forensica: Forensic science advances, and so do I.

#### Join Team

Forensica: This wasn't part of our agreement. Don't get your hopes up.

#### Upgrade Skill

Forensica: One step closer... to the truth...
