# Helix Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

Helix: You have the shackles too, and the power to set things right. So why stop me from offering everything I am to the Great Presence? Are you blind... or just pretending not to see the sin crawling through this world?

#### Raise to Phase 1

Helix: You actually think that being harder to break and less capable of feeling pain... is a good thing?

#### Raise to Phase 3

Helix: Every ache is Its hand carving into me. Please... let the cracks run deeper.

#### Level Start

Helix: To serve the coming of the Great Presence, you should consider yourselves fortunate.

#### Victory

Helix: "With a staff of thorns the Herald comes, and this wayward age shall be unmade."

#### Lose Battles

Helix: The Great Presence does not place trials before us without purpose. I will not falter.

#### Ultimate Skill

Helix: Break me, if you can.

#### Die in Battles

Helix: Ah... pain, at last... it's been so long...

#### Greeting

Helix: We meet again, shackle bearer. You may as well stay and listen to Its teachings with me... Oh. I keep forgetting. Its words have never reached you.

#### Conversation 1

Helix: Why do I want to be broken? What a strange thing to ask. If this shell remains intact, how will the Great Presence see me, and the light within... the offering I've kept burning for It all this time?

#### Conversation 2

Helix: You Overgrounders cling to your "laws" and "codes." But when the innocent cry out, when blood runs in the streets, you do nothing. DisCity is beyond saving. Fight for it all you like; you'll only be dragged down with it when the judgment comes.

#### Conversation 3

Helix: The way you follow your rules... it reminds me of who I used to be. Deceived by the false Overground gods, believing every beautiful lie with all my heart. Wake up, shackle bearer. Walk with us, and we will be your family.

#### Special Conversation 1

Helix: Pruney fingers from water, wet socks clinging to my feet, even a tag scratching at my neck... it all annoys me. Without you, I would've forgotten that living is not just pain. It's also these small, stupid discomforts.

#### Special Conversation 2

Helix: The real me was destroyed along with that first shell. All I've wanted since is paradise after death. Then you came and rebuilt everything in my life. You showed me that paradise doesn't have to wait until I'm dead. Heh... are you hoping to win my faith away from It?

#### Special Conversation 3

Helix: The world you want is a kind one. I think I'm beginning to see it the way you do. Sometimes I even wonder... if you had been there, could I have stayed on this side of the line, like everyone else? ...But there is no "if."

#### Full Compliance

Helix: For so long, my world was just noise and pain. The only place I ever felt safe was the shell my parents made for me. But it broke anyway, as all things do. Then I built a new shell from hatred and devotion. The pain was gone, but so was the warmth. When you showed me your world and gave me a place of my own... this shell finally started to feel like home again.

#### Idle

Helix: You frown in your sleep. Do you feel it even there, in your dreams? The rot and suffering that fill this world? So who deceived you into guarding this festering order...?

#### Annoyed

Helix: Give up. I can't feel the warmth of your hand. This won't sway me.

#### Special Attention

Helix: People always stare at me like this. I can't feel things, but I can read their eyes. They're always cold. Yours, however... there's warmth in them.

#### Dispatch

Helix: Scouring out the unclean, gathering supplies... This is what we have always done. No need for concern.

#### Dispatch Complete

Helix: Even a contribution this small... It sees. Nothing escapes Its gaze.

#### Level Up

Helix: Now... I'm one step closer to It...

#### Join Team

Helix: Everything for the Great Presence.

#### Upgrade Skill

Helix: You actually think that being harder to break and less capable of feeling pain... is a good thing?
