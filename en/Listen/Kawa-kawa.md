#### Win Battles

#### Conversation 1

Kawa-kawa: If you encounter a sandstorm, look for a beacon nearby... like this one in my hand. Hide next to it, then you should be able to survive it.

#### Conversation 2

Kawa-kawa: Why do you keep catching those... they are called desert bandits, right? They hardly ever hit me with a gun. Sometimes they would ask me to be their guide and give me fresh water after getting them to places. I think they are nice people.

#### Conversation 3

Kawa-kawa: Hehehe, I'm not as impressive as you said, at least not as good as Dad and Grandpa. I learned all my geography knowledge from the book grandpa wrote.

#### Special Conversation 1

Kawa-kawa: Your food here is so delicious. Especially the broth—I would never have thought putting large chunks of meat and water together would taste so much better than roasting scorpions and lizards!

#### Special Conversation 2

Kawa-kawa: There are a few very strange places in WhiteSands with people looking stern and carrying guns everywhere. I get kicked out every time I go in for a break—but it's so spacious inside. How mean.

#### Special Conversation 3

Kawa-kawa: I always thought if I placed beacons all over WhiteSands, my dad would come back. But it's been seven years... I will finish his and Grandpa's plan, then I will go find him.

#### Full Compliance

### Outfit 1: Down the Rabbit Hole

#### Attire Obtained

Kawa-kawa: Late? Late for what? Mr. Pocket Watch, what are you talking about? It's awful enough I've had butterflies in my stomach all morning, and now you are broken too! What's next? Me being hit by a newcomer falling from the sky? ...Ouch!

#### Level Up

Kawa-kawa: The rabbit hole has a new guest!

#### Greeting

Kawa-kawa: Thank you, newcomer! You falling on me helped me remember what I need to do—going to the tea party! It's a rare chance to take in some calories!

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Kawa-kawa: Well well well, everyone in my burrow is my guest! But I not quite ready for the tea party... Oh, I've got it! Let's dash off to the Queen of Hearts' palace—it's ever so grand, and the food is delightful! Just mind that red-eyed rabbit, it does give one a fright!

#### Ordinary Contact 2

Kawa-kawa: Do you have a sweet tooth? Like jam, butter, scones... If you do, come to the tea party with me! Here, you must take this spoon so that you can say, "I'll only have a spoonful!"

#### Idle

Kawa-kawa: Hmm... This flower will make a lovely gift... Oh, and this mushroom—quite handy! And this herb, yes, yes! Just what we need to get through the week!

#### Annoyed

Kawa-kawa: Oh my whiskers! Careful, careful! My suitcase is packed with all the essentials we need to survive here. If anything happens to them, we'll be doomed!

#### Special Attention

Kawa-kawa: Yes, you're doing good. Stay close to me! It's not quite like the ground up here, and one wrong step could have me searching for hours to get you back!
