# Ayiguo Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

Ayiguo: Why are you hiding, little one? Do you recognize me? Afraid I might poison you? Haha! Everyone knows Granny Goldspider is a walking poison vault, but she adores pretty little dolls like you! Come on, call me *Granny* and I promise to go easy on you...

#### Raise to Phase 1

Ayiguo: Ah, this poison is perfected with countless silk threads and chirping bugs.

#### Raise to Phase 3

Ayiguo: They say only a fool would drink poison to quench his thirst, but this very poison is the key to my eternal youth. I'm sharing this secret with you now... Aren't you tempted?

#### Level Start

Ayiguo: Must I crack these old bones for something so trivial?

#### Victory

Ayiguo: How weak and pathetic. Can't even feed you to my tiniest bugs. Too bad.

#### Lose Battles

Ayiguo: You may think you've won, but venomous creatures are everywhere. Beware of them when you walk alone in the dark...

#### Ultimate Skill

Ayiguo: My silk nest is immune to the deadliest poison!

#### Die in Battles

Ayiguo: I won't... rot away here...

#### Greeting

Ayiguo: Sigh, the young ones these days don't greet their elders properly anymore as they did decades ago. You have some good manners though, child. Everyone here is so promising and polite, not to mention all of their pretty faces... Mm, it's decided, I'm staying!

#### Conversation 1

Ayiguo: Look at my sleeves, aren't they beautiful? Want to touch them? Want to... get acquainted with my babies hiding up my sleeves? Oh, don't be scared! The worst thing that can happen is touching a deadly poison and losing a hand.

#### Conversation 2

Ayiguo: Colorless, odorless poisons that kill instantly are nothing to fear. The truly terrifying poisons turn a beautiful face into festering sores and cover fragrant skin with pus-filled boils, transforming a normal person into a living corpse... I've treasured my beauty all my life—I'd rather be chopped into pieces than become something like that.

#### Conversation 3

Ayiguo: You've probably had a taste of the so-called Eastian martial world—nothing but role play of a bunch of petty schemers. Who are they to judge which act is heroic and which is not? Hmph! Your Granny Goldspider has managed to survive this long by following one single rule: Don't meddle in other people's business. Heh, being a hero is my last concern.

#### Special Conversation 1

Ayiguo: Oh, stop asking me for stories. Your granny has forgotten them all. One day, when you're my age, you'll understand that forgetfulness isn't a curse, it's a divine gift. Only by shaking off the dust of time can you keep moving forward without being crushed or trapped.

#### Special Conversation 2

Ayiguo: You get the deadliest poison by making venomous creatures devour each other, so only the strongest, the one most desperate to survive, can live. My babies are like this, and so am I. But don't we all live in the same way? Poison and human are not that different. If you ask me, people are worse.

#### Special Conversation 3

Ayiguo: Though I'm quite famous, if I put on a sweet, cutesy voice, I can still fool a few naive kids into doing me favors. You should see their faces when I get bored and tell them the truth! Some people call me out for lying about my age, but... hmph! I don't care what they say! It's my life and I'm going to live it beautifully!

#### Full Compliance

Ayiguo: I used to only know one way to live: Conquer people like I conquer venomous creatures, then conquer pain and fear and become the strongest poison. That way, I could lead a fearless, uninhibited life. But you've shown me that even monsters could be accepted and could enjoy a life without suffering. Turns out what I was looking for wasn't poison—it was you.

#### Idle

Ayiguo: You've ignored me the whole day. Guess I'm an empty nester now. Looks like I need to gather a few of my babies to give you some "special" treatment.

#### Annoyed

Ayiguo: Have kids these days lost all their respect for their elders? What's with all this poking? If you're so desperate for mischief, Granny will have to help you calm down...

#### Special Attention

Ayiguo: When I still roamed the martial world, some of those "heroes" looked at me this way too. Some feared my unchanging appearance, others coveted it... Which type are you?

#### Dispatch

Ayiguo: Now, you stay here and don't wander around. Granny's bringing back some pretty little trinkets you're gonna love, alright?

#### Dispatch Complete

Ayiguo: They've got such fine accessories in DisCity. How I love them! Hm? Your trinkets? Did I promise you that?

#### Level Up

Ayiguo: Oh, I'm glad to accept this gift. What a considerate child you are! I'll reward you with something nice once I've savored it all.

#### Join Team

Ayiguo: My dear babies, time to get to work! We need to teach these fools what it means to respect your elders!

#### Upgrade Skill

Ayiguo: Ah, this poison is perfected with countless silk threads and chirping bugs.
