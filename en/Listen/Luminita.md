#### Raise to Phase 3

Luminita: What's so scary about a haunted house? Even ghosts got homes, while the living are left homeless—isn't that the real horror?

#### Level Start

Luminita: Follow me! Don't worry, we won't run into any ghosts this time.

#### Victory

Luminita: That's a wrap! Deals always go smoothly when I take you with me.

#### Greeting

Luminita: A rare, limited-time deal! One single house will satisfy all your thrilling fantasies. Chief, why be scared when you can be excited? Open your checkbook and seize the moment! Don't miss the chance!

#### Conversation 1

Luminita: Can't sell an old, noisy house? No problem, let me help you rephrase that—well-established amenities, vibrant neighborhood, ultra-convenient lifestyle! Hey, how's that a lie? It's called sprucing up your pitch!

#### Conversation 2

Luminita: Chief, your roommates say you keep the AC too cold at night. They all caught colds standing by your bed last night... No way! Don't tell me you didn't know about them?

#### Conversation 3

Luminita: I've lived in many places. If I had to rank them... Hmm... That spot under the bridge was pretty nice. Excellent natural lighting, panoramic street views, and the city patrol woke me up every morning. Never had to worry about being late for work... Hahaha! I'm just kidding! You didn't actually believe that, did you?

#### Special Conversation 1

Luminita: Those so-called haunted houses out there are mostly only marketing gimmicks. At worst, they're just old and in disrepair. But once a house falls into my hands... well, that's another story entirely.

#### Special Conversation 2

Luminita: Try to avoid the old neighborhoods if you want to buy a property. Where I lived, they argued for years about installing an elevator, and there was this huge hole in the building that nobody ever fixed. Eventually, that hole became home to some stray cats. They even had kittens there, all of them fed so well by the neighbors they grew up round and chubby. And after that... well, there's nothing more to tell. The building was demolished.

#### Special Conversation 3

Luminita: I love hearing the jingling of keys. When I open doors, walk down the street, or hand them over to clients, a whole bunch of keys shaking and jingling, never stopping. It sounds like... a loud, warm *welcome home*.

#### Full Compliance

Luminita: With you, a home becomes more than just a roof and a key... You are that sense of security I've lost for so long, making me dare to believe again that I have somewhere to return to. Make a deal with me! Stay by my side, so I can keep this peace of mind for a long, long time!

#### Idle

#### Annoyed

#### Special Attention

Luminita: Need a talisman to keep your home safe and repel evil spirits? You're in luck! Check this out! Ugh... don't give me that look, I can't make it any cheaper. This is already the best deal I can offer.
