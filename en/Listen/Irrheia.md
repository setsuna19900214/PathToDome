# Irrheia Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

Irrheia: I'm weary of boredom and the predictable. Your arrival is far too ordinary. Why don't we... start over? I'll pretend this is our first meeting.

#### Raise to Phase 1

Irrheia: I eagerly await the next act you'll unveil for me.

#### Raise to Phase 3

Irrheia: The clock hands have begun to turn, and the curtain has risen... Come, I'll be waiting for you at the story's end!

#### Level Start

Irrheia: I'm ready. Let the cycle begin.

#### Success

Irrheia: I hope the next round surprises me just as much.

#### Failed

Irrheia: Heh, I suppose that was an interesting ending.

#### Ultimate Skill

Irrheia: The end and the rebirth, all in the matter of a single breath!

#### Die in Battles

Irrheia: At least this time... it was my own choice...

#### Greeting

Irrheia: Let me guess... is your visit today a predestined event, or an unexpected twist?

#### Conversation 1

Irrheia: When the water grows too still, I always drop in a stone. Whether it creates mere ripples or an explosion, that always depends on the fish's choice, or an act of betrayal.

#### Conversation 2

Irrheia: Most people enter the stage with their predetermined scripts. Their backgrounds serve as the opening lines, their experiences the chapter titles. Flip through a few pages... and you can already predict their endings. So boring it makes me yawn.

#### Conversation 3

Irrheia: My dear Chief, keep going, don't stop. I've long grown tired of those docile tales that submit to rules and tropes. Following you is the more interesting choice. You won't disappoint me, will you?

#### Special Conversation 1

Irrheia: On the stage I've built, some people always take the same dull path, time and time again, becoming such miserable wretches unworthy of saving... But I never expected you could lead them toward a different ending.

#### Special Conversation 2

Irrheia: My world was once confined by red tubes and perfect graphs, as monotonous as the daily test reports... until I witnessed someone willingly tear themselves apart in defiance of rules. After that, I could no longer ignore these restraints.

#### Special Conversation 3

Irrheia: The so-called family of mine was all about "family tradition," binding me with blood relations, making me willingly sacrifice myself. How absurd... But I've already made it clear to them that, now, I am the one who makes the rules in this family.

#### Full Compliance

Irrheia: I had a dream last night. In the cycle of fate, I passed countless exits. They were pitch black, bottomless voids. But in one of them, I saw you... I walked toward you without hesitation, only to die in your arms at the end. Strangely enough, when I woke up, I felt happy... I think I finally understand what kind of ending would satisfy me in this endless loop.

#### Idle

Irrheia: Spacing out like this from time to time is quite a novel experience.

#### Annoyed

Irrheia: Your methods of probing people are so unoriginal. Care to try something more... thrilling?

#### Special Attention

Irrheia: In stories, I'm the being people fear to face, yet you dare to gaze at me so boldly... Do you have a thing for witches? Crave excitement? Or perhaps you simply... like me?

#### Dispatch

Irrheia: I don't take on boring assignments. Don't disappoint me.

#### Dispatch Complete

Irrheia: Just some trivial things. Take them if you want. I have no interest in keeping them.

#### Upgrade

Irrheia: Playing by the rules is so dull. Great, I happen to be craving some new variables.

#### Pat Head

Irrheia: You always manage to come up with a surprising move. Fortunately, I don't mind your touch.

#### Joins the Team

Irrheia: I love situations in constant flux, where no one can predict what comes next...

#### Upgrade Skill

Irrheia: I eagerly await the next act you'll unveil for me.

### Outfit 1: Serene Daylight

#### Attire Obtained

Irrheia: What a coincidence, little Chief. It turns out the incredibly lucky dice roll this morning was a sign that I'd run into you today. You're not in a hurry, are you? Care for a coffee?

#### Level Up

Irrheia: I wonder what it would taste like with another packet of sugar?

#### Greeting

Irrheia: The aroma of coffee, the distant chatter, and... you, who arrived at just the right moment. It's not exactly a novel composition, but I'm quite satisfied with it.

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Irrheia: The afternoon sun is perfect, yet these passersby are all in such a hurry. They barely take anything in, turning themselves into some faceless NPCs. This cat, on the other hand, understands what it means to "live"—it's choosing to spend time in a wonderful way, idling away with the human it fancies.

#### Ordinary Contact 2

Irrheia: For some who dislike the bitterness of coffee, their solution is to add sugar. Meanwhile, others would choose to cope with it by creating strange flavors. Though not many might understand, I quite admire this... something about it is much like watching ordinary, busy humans challenge their boring lives. It's very intriguing.

#### Idle

Irrheia: To be able to sleep so soundly while being watched and recorded... You're quite bold, little Chief. Perhaps you've never even considered the possibility that this peaceful life is also a scene I've written?

#### Annoyed

Irrheia: It seems you've already learned how to demand attention just like that cat, who hasn't even woken up yet. Well... considering you're more lively than it is, I'll allow you to fool around a bit longer.

#### Special Attention

Irrheia: Why are you looking at me like that? Could it be that this "everyday life" has more impact on you than those exaggerated scripts?
