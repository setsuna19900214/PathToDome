# Echo Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

Echo: I'm a free musician, a star of the streets! The genius saxophonist who never forgets a note... Echo, at your service! Now, aren't you excited to hear me play a tune after that introduction?

#### Raise to Phase 1

Echo: A mere few pages of sheet music are no challenge to me at all!

#### Raise to Phase 3

Echo: The saxophone genius is about to give the most spectacular performance! You won't want to miss it!

#### Level Start

Echo: This invigorating march will be the opening theme of our battle!

#### Win Battles

Echo: That marks the end of Echo's perfect performance, thank you!

#### Lose Battles

Echo: Oops, I messed up the notes...

#### Ultimate Skill

Echo: Listen to this wonderful, miraculous melody!

#### Die in Battles

Echo: It's so cold... so dark...

#### Greeting

Echo: Hey you, sleepyhead. Should I play a tune to wake you up?

#### Conversation 1

Echo: Outfit, perfect. Stage, perfect. Saxophone, perfect! Okay, I'm ready to... Hey, don't touch my carefully arranged props! The ambiance matters to buskers too!

#### Conversation 2

Echo: Hmm, hmm... Oh, I'm thinking of a new song! I once read a quote that says art is inspired by life. It's spot on! I'm immediately filled with inspiration after eating!

#### Conversation 3

Echo: Slacking off? Of course not! I've memorized all those materials! Don't believe it? Quiz me then, I owe you one if I fail! If that's not enough, I can even recite the entire menu of the cafeteria for the past three weeks!

#### Special Conversation 1

Echo: Well, I'm sure people just haven't noticed me yet. It happens to all the geniuses! I just know that as long as I keep playing, everyone will fall in love with my music one day!

#### Special Conversation 2

Echo: Feeling down? Let me sing a song for you! Back in the orphanage, everyone loved my singing! Whenever I sang, people would laugh so happily! I wonder if my singing voice brings joy to others?

#### Special Conversation 3

Echo: I hope my performances can touch many, many people! Being watched, admired, and remembered... means I'd never be forgotten or feel lonely again! Just thinking about it makes me so happy!

#### Full Compliance

Echo: Don't tell anyone, but even a genius like me gets nervous when performing... But as soon as I feel your gaze from the audience, all my anxiety is gone, and my body is filled with strength! Because I know there's someone who'll always support me... Thank you, my number one fan!

#### Annoyed

#### Special Attention

Echo: Okay, okay, I know what you want! Here you go. Keep it safe though, the genius saxophonist's autograph is priceless!

#### Dispatch

Echo: Busking? That's my bread and butter! I've got this!
