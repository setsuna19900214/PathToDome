# Ceto Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

Ceto: How amusing. You dragged me out of the storm just to throw me into another battlefield? Fine. Let's see if you've got what it takes to last till the end, my new commander.

#### Raise to Phase 1

Ceto: Only the toughest and most heartless can survive the Black Tide.

#### Raise to Phase 3

Ceto: I am the last keeper of the lighthouse. Where I stand is its final line of defense.

#### Level Start

Ceto: Steel yourself. The sea is at its filthiest after a storm.

#### Victory

Ceto: Tch, are these wimps even worth drawing my blade for?

#### Lose Battles

Ceto: Those bastards got lucky this time.

#### Ultimate Skill

Ceto: The storm is here!

#### Die in Battles

Ceto: The tide has receded... Finally, I can return to my post...

#### Greeting

Ceto: Knew it was you from your footsteps. Even seabirds won't set foot here. Only suicidal idiots would come near... and you're one of them.

#### Conversation 1

Ceto: Life in the Black Tide is much simpler than it is here. No schedules, no rosters. Rest when it's bright, gamble with your life when it's dark. For a lighthouse keeper, a day isn't that different from a year.

#### Conversation 2

Ceto: Cassette tapes and ocean views aren't consumables. Have some if you want. Save your thanks, I'm very generous.

#### Conversation 3

Ceto: Listen to the ocean waves long enough and you'll notice a rhythm, like breathing, like a countdown... When that rhythm falters, it means the monsters are coming ashore.

#### Special Conversation 1

Ceto: Life and death, hot and cold. All just parameters and risk indicators to me. You always try to inject *humanity* into these boring military duties, disrupting my life... Tch, whatever. You've already disrupted it anyway.

#### Special Conversation 2

Ceto: When the storm here is at its worst, monsters get washed up from the western cliff. I guard that point, stepping on nothing but mud and bones. After a while, I can't tell if it's friend or foe beneath my feet, if it's me from yesterday, or tomorrow.

#### Special Conversation 3

Ceto: I thought I knew the lighthouse better than anyone, and you were just a burden who stumbled in... But thinking about it now, perhaps you understand what a true lighthouse is more than I do. You've found the beacon, while I'm still searching.

#### Full Compliance

Ceto: I always carry my comrades' dog tags with me, like I still belong to the crew. As for my own tag... I'll leave it at the top of Lighthouse Seven. If I'm gone, you come and take it. Not as a memento, but as a handover.

#### Idle

Ceto: Entertaining yourself quietly is the first lesson of survival on a lonely island. Congratulations, you've got the hang of it now.

#### Annoyed

Ceto: Go find a furnace if you're cold. It's not bad enough for us to be huddling together for warmth yet.

#### Special Attention

Ceto: Want to stay with me? Fine, but don't just stand there daydreaming. The sea wind has no mercy for the idle.

#### Dispatch

Ceto: Enough talk. I know what to bring. You won't starve on my watch.

#### Dispatch Complete

Ceto: Supplies are here. Enough to last us a while.

#### Level Up

Ceto: My blade is used to tasting blood. So am I.

#### Pat Head

Ceto: If you don't want that hand anymore, just say so.

#### Join Team

Ceto: Let's make this quick. The lighthouse cannot be left unattended.

#### Upgrade Skill

Ceto: Only the toughest and most heartless can survive the Black Tide.

### Outfit 1: Masterless Sail

#### Attire Obtained

Ceto: Listen, I can save you, but once you're on my ship, you'll need to pull your weight—raising the mast, swabbing decks, watching the sea. No one's going to clean up your messes. If you understand, grab my hand and climb aboard.

#### Level Up

Ceto: Let's conquer the waves!

#### Greeting

Ceto: Awake? Good, then let's set sail. The sea's rough today, so hold tight and don't get swept overboard. I don't have time to circle back and fish you out.

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Ceto: Those shadows wandering on the decks? Well, they're your crewmates too. You'll have to get used to them and get along with everyone. This ship couldn't have made it this far without them.

#### Ordinary Contact 2

Ceto: Listen to the waves long enough and you'll feel like they can take everything away: names, memories, directions... After a while, I'm not even sure what's left of me anymore.

#### Idle

Ceto: ...Do you hear that? There are other sounds in the waves... Hmph, those things are scratching at the hull again, waiting for us to fall in and join them.

#### Annoyed

Ceto: Move your hand any closer and I'll assume you want to test how sharp this blade is.

#### Special Attention

Ceto: I can see it in your eyes... the same madness as mine. Good, this ghost ship needs someone like you who "fits in" with the crew. Come, we can sail anywhere together, even to hell's harbor.
