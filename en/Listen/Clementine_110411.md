# Clementine Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

Clementine: "Truth is a dangerous thing. Even the ugliest truths can attract people, grasping them firmly by the heart"—just like you, right now. It's been a while, my dear detective. Are you ready for a new mystery?

#### Raise to Phase 1

Clementine: Look closely. The truth is often hidden between the lines.

#### Raise to Phase 3

Clementine: A vanishing passenger, a sealed carriage, a long and chaotic night... Open the door, detective. The story has already begun.

#### Level Start

Clementine: Take a guess. Who's the culprit this time around?

#### Victory

Clementine: "Once again, the detective solves the mystery and uncovers the truth." Would you like me to give you a little praise?

#### Lose Battles

Clementine: "There is no such thing as a perfect crime." Try again—you've already found the key to cracking the case.

#### Ultimate Skill

Clementine: And now—we enter the final chapter!

#### Die in Battles

Clementine: My revenge... I can't... stop here...

#### Greeting

Clementine: Hmm, I don't have any deadlines for once. Care to join me for a stroll?

#### Conversation 1

Clementine: The confrontation between culprit and detective is very much like the battle of wits between author and reader—neither side can let their guard down until the truth is revealed.

#### Conversation 2

Clementine: The secret to taxidermy lies in precision. You must study the structure of the bones, each stitch should be made with purpose, with every wing adjusted to the perfect angle... Writing is the same. Strip away the emotions, and only then can you capture the most perfect death.

#### Conversation 3

Clementine: Writers are always more inspired late at night, so I have a habit of taking walks in the evening—I'd find a good drink, bask in the moonlight, and try my luck on the streets. Then, as dawn breaks, the kind Public Security Bureau would escort me home as some poor soul who wandered into a crime scene. A perfect arrangement, wouldn't you agree?

#### Special Conversation 1

Clementine: Crafting a victim is far more complex than one might imagine—they exit the stage early, yet remain the crux of the entire case, silently commanding everyone's attention. Compared to writing some celebrated star detective, I find the unknown victim at the heart of a case far more interesting.

#### Special Conversation 2

Clementine: Stories are metaphors for reality—secrets that are never revealed, injustice forced into silence, a truth long buried in dust... Even if a story gives someone nothing besides the courage to face the darkness they once fled from, that is enough to give it meaning.

#### Special Conversation 3

Clementine: There's a cliched, unspoken rule in detective novels—the truth shall not be tarnished, and justice shall never be absent. Of course, reality is never the same... even so, I still adopt this "rule" in my works, over and over again... in the hopes that one day, someone like you comes along and makes it true.

#### Full Compliance

Clementine: My stories often mirror reality, and many of my characters are modelled after real people. Two people would meet, fall in love, walk together, turn on each other... But, even if they cannot escape death in the end, their existence is preserved in ink. Stories can make a bond eternal—just like them, just like... you and me.

#### Idle

Clementine: The deadline looms, yet the words aren't flowing... Hmm, I'm not in the right headspace. I'll only make mistakes if I force myself... perhaps I should just relax for today...

#### Annoyed

Clementine: You can pester me as much as you like, but you'll never get any spoilers out of this author's mouth.

#### Special Attention

Clementine: Two people, locked in a room together, their eyes meeting, the distance closing... The perfect setup for something special to happen, wouldn't you say?

#### Dispatch

Clementine: A field trip? Sure, I could use some inspiration.

#### Dispatch Complete

Clementine: My, this is certainly a pleasant surprise. Perhaps I should make use of this material for my next book.

#### Level Up

Clementine: How delightful. This case is just the inspiration I needed!

#### Pat Head

Clementine: Hm? You'd like an autograph? Sure, where would you like me to sign? Your palm? Your shoulder? Your back? Or perhaps somewhere more... unforgettable?

#### Join Team

Clementine: The story begins, a crime unfolds—and you... you just happened to be at the scene.

#### Upgrade Skill

Clementine: Look closely. The truth is often hidden between the lines.
