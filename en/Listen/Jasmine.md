Jasmine: The faster my blades spin, the louder the audience cheers. They throw flowers and jewels, pleading for more thrilling moves... Hehe, do they even realize? The prettier the dance, the deadlier it becomes...

#### Special Conversation 1

Jasmine: This place is so much nicer than anywhere I've lived before—shacks, tents... I've even slept in haystacks! But the thing I remember most? A little golden cage, gleaming so bright. I'd dance in it by day, sleep in it by night... It was so small, though. We'd have to hold each other very close to fit inside.

#### Special Conversation 2

Jasmine: These circular blades aren't just for show, Chief... Every night, they whisper in my ear, reminding me to harvest every last devil who took everything from me!

Reminds me of the breeze in my homeland. If you'd allow it, I would love to nestle close, just listening to your voice. No pain, no harm... only quiet moments, together.
