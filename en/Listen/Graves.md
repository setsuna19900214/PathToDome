# Graves Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

Graves: The vow has been made. From now on, we are partners... Does this mean I can stay here in this new home with you? We'll share meals, dance together, count the stars, and enjoy all the little joys of life? Never to be apart again?

#### Raise to Phase 1

Graves: I will cherish your blessing dearly, for our eternal bond.

#### Raise to Phase 3

Graves: I make this vow, to love and cherish you always, for better, for worse, for richer, for poorer, in sickness and in health, in joy and in sadness, in good times and bad.

#### Level Start

Graves: Let us gather to witness this grand wedding.

#### Victory

Graves: Will you stay next to me like this forever? I want to be the one to support and protect you as well.

#### Lose Battles

Graves: So even promises wither away...

#### Ultimate Skill

Graves: Love and pain are intertwined.

#### Die in Battles

Graves: Till death... do us part...

#### Greeting

Graves: I adore this little ritual... welcoming a loved one home with the softest, most devoted gaze. If only you could look at me that way too...

#### Conversation 1

Graves: Why do you think people exchange rings? I guess perhaps it's to make promises tangible. That way... they won't easily disappear.

#### Conversation 2

Graves: What's the world like outside this castle? I used to hear guests say that in Eastside, the air smells like money and the streets are paved with gold, and even cats and dogs can sing... Is all of that true?

#### Conversation 3

Graves: I wonder what happened to all those newlyweds joined in matrimony before the god of love? They all made their vows with such sincerity and affection... Surely they must all have lived happily ever after, right?

#### Special Conversation 1

Graves: There used to be a large pipe organ in the castle. The organist taught me how to play and I would help out at weddings when he was away... That's how I participated in the most important moments of some people's lives. It didn't matter that no one saw me or thanked me. I was just happy to help in my own small way.

#### Special Conversation 2

Graves: The bride's bouquet toss represents blessings and new beginnings. They say whoever catches it will soon find their own happiness. Though I was never welcome at weddings, I always hid behind the door and watched the delighted faces of those who caught the flowers. I couldn't help but smile along... I can barely imagine how wonderful it would feel if I could catch one myself just once.

#### Special Conversation 3

Graves: I've never actually tried it, but maybe... I could make a decent wedding officiant? I mean, I'm familiar with DisCity's wedding ceremonies, and even the Outland ones. Have you... ever imagined what kind of wedding you'd have?

#### Full Compliance

Graves: I once believed that simply by wearing this wedding dress, I could find true love and happiness, just like those other brides. But with you here, I'm beginning to understand that true happiness doesn't come from wedding dresses or ceremonies. It's something deeper from within... I still can't grasp it fully yet. Would you be willing to teach me more?

#### Idle

Graves: From the grave she rises, frost upon her wedding dress. The vows have decayed, her beloved eternally in rest...

#### Annoyed

Graves: Your hand is so warm. Is this the warmth lovers feel when they touch each other?

#### Special Attention

Graves: I always thought such a tender gaze was reserved only for the exchange of wedding vows... Only you look at me this way, as if I were a normal person and not some terrifying monster.

#### Dispatch

Graves: One must never be late for a wedding. Not a single precious moment should be missed.

#### Dispatch Complete

Graves: Here, this is a wedding favor I brought back from the ceremony... It symbolizes the most beautiful blessings. Take it please, will you?

#### Level Up

Graves: Is this your pledge to me?

#### Pat Head

Graves: You lifted my veil and saw how hideous I am... B-but even so, would you still be willing to give me a kiss?

#### Join Team

Graves: The music has started. Dear guests, please proceed to your seats.

#### Upgrade Skill

Graves: I will cherish your blessing dearly, for our eternal bond.
