### Outfit 1: Resonance of Splendor

#### Attire Obtained

Lady Pearl: The lanterns are lit, the strings are freshly tuned, and the flowers have not yet wilted. The stage before the curtain rises is the most intimate moment in all of Yunshao Manor. Were you not a kindred spirit, I would have never allowed you to witness this.

#### Level Up

Lady Pearl: There is no greater honor for this humble establishment than the applause of such distinguished guests.

#### Greeting

Lady Pearl: What compels you to linger in the corridors on such a beautiful evening? Do you not hear the music playing? Come now, tell me—would you like to hear something bold and unrestrained, or... something tender and heartfelt?

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Lady Pearl: They say the stage depicts peace and prosperity. It embodies everlasting joy in the mortal realm, but such performances grow dull after a while. What I've always wished for is for songs and dances to break through the wall that separates the stage and the audience. Now that would be a most fresh and spectacular sight, wouldn't you say?

#### Ordinary Contact 2

Lady Pearl: The audience watches the Sogdian Whirl and sees only the splendor of nations united in prosperity. But I know. I know that behind the stage, those young performers danced until their feet bled, and yet to them, that blood is no more than dye for their red silk. That savage determination—that's the true foundation of Yunshao Manor.

#### Idle

Lady Pearl: Countless people would die for an invitation to this grand banquet, and here you are, drifting off into dreams. Though... who's to say this grand performance isn't a fleeting dream in itself?

#### Annoyed

Lady Pearl: I have plenty of captivating treasures here. Since you seem so interested, shall I bring you to a feast for your eyes once the performance ends? What do you say?

#### Special Attention

Lady Pearl: This red and gold ensemble is most heavy. I only put it on to welcome your esteemed presence. But seeing as how you can't take your eyes off me, I'd say the effort was, in fact, worth its weight in gold.
