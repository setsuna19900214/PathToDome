# Jichuan Listen

### Standard voice (initial and ascension)

#### Obtain the Sinner

Jichuan: Jichuan of Northsnow Manor. A true honor to meet you today—your heroic reputation is well known. Please, do enlighten the owner of this ailing body with your superior techniques.

#### Raise to Phase 1

Jichuan: I understand the price of this power. The Heaven's way is immutable, and I accept it willingly.

#### Raise to Phase 3

Jichuan: The plum branch I hold is named "Bent Jade." Today, I shall swear by wind and snow, and forge these flowers into a sword. May this single strike bring clarity to this turbid world.

#### Level Start

Jichuan: Time to invite our guests into the trap.

#### Victory

Jichuan: It was a narrow victory. Thank you for going easy on me.

#### Lose Battles

Jichuan: A justified loss to a worthy opponent. I yield.

#### Ultimate Skill

Jichuan: Plum branch snapped; jade shattered. Cleanse this world with this power!

#### Die in Battles

Jichuan: The journey through this mortal world is still long... Finish it for me...

#### Greeting

Jichuan: You've returned at just the right time. The snow water has just boiled. Why don't you sit down for a cup of hot tea and indulge me with the tales of your travels? Allow me to glimpse the world through your eyes and ears.

#### Conversation 1

Jichuan: As a child, I often feigned illness to skip my duties. Now that I'm truly bedridden as I once wished, I find myself missing those days of running and playing. I suppose it's always the "unattainable" that we yearn for most, isn't it...

#### Conversation 2

Jichuan: Every time it snows, Cheepy and Chirpy burrow into my fur coat. Those two are quite smart. They know the warmest spot... Why are you looking at me like that? Are you cold too? Cough.

#### Conversation 3

Jichuan: The storybooks always teach the same lesson: *What goes around comes around.* But I've seen many evildoers living long lives, while the kind ones... meet untimely ends.

#### Special Conversation 1

Jichuan: The martial world believes that Northsnow Manor hides the peerless treasure, but I'm afraid all these heroes will be disappointed. This humble dwelling of mine contains only a wisp of plum fragrance, a body full of ailments, and an old tale.

#### Special Conversation 2

Jichuan: I dozed off while reading just now, and I thought I heard the plum branch break under the wind and snow. Phew, fortunately it was just a dream.

#### Special Conversation 3

Jichuan: I have a unique method to calm my mind—wisdom condensed from all my life experiences. I'll teach it to you now. If you ever feel challenged by the road ahead, recite this to yourself: *Be like a plum tree standing in snow, its roots firm and its branches light. Repel the icy wind outside and guard the divine spirit within.* I hope this can help protect your heart in despair... and steady your resolve in turbulent times.

#### Full Compliance

Jichuan: Though my days are numbered, meeting you makes me think Heaven has been fair to me for once. I shall entrust both my faith in justice and my remaining time to you. From now on, come hell or high water, I'll follow you without regret, until my last flame goes out.

#### Idle

Jichuan: Today's medicine looks even darker and more bitter than usual. I'm afraid no amount of candied fruit could mask the taste. Hmm, I see, it must have been over-brewed. I can't drink this!

#### Annoyed

Jichuan: Wh-what kind of behavior is this? I... I suddenly feel so dizzy... Oh my, oh my, my legs are giving out... I must excuse myself!

#### Special Attention

Jichuan: Just as staring at the snow for too long hurts your eyes, dwelling on worries for too long hurts your spirit. If you have any troubles, why not share them with me? I'm happy to do my small part by listening.

#### Dispatch

Jichuan: The medicinal decoction is still simmering. Please watch over it for me. Don't let it get cold before I return.

#### Dispatch Complete

Jichuan: This mission was completed as promised. Fortunately, they pitied my sickliness and let me end things quickly.

#### Level Up

Jichuan: I am grateful for your aid.

#### Pat Head

Jichuan: Such behavior is quite improper. Don't do that again...

#### Join Team

Jichuan: I apologize in advance for my rudeness.

#### Upgrade Skill

Jichuan: I understand the price of this power. The Heaven's way is immutable, and I accept it willingly.
