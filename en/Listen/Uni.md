### Outfit 1: Guise into Life

#### Attire Obtained

Uni: What sort of face are you looking for? One that launches a thousand ships? Name your heart's desire. I shall fulfill it.

Uni: My works have been "superficial", but from now on, I'll be able to capture the essence of the subject.

#### Greeting

Uni: You come here every day. Which skin here has caught your fancy? Heed my words: None of these monotonously beautiful faces is worthy of those stunning eyes of yours.

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Uni: To become a great skin painter, one must have seen beauty and ugliness in their endless variations, and touched countless bones with their infinite details. Otherwise, you can't fool the discerning eyes of shrewd mortals and will thwart the fulfillment of your customer's obsession.

Uni: Here is my rule: I paint all the features on the skin, except for the eyes. You use your own when you're back in the world of the living, however beautiful the other features I've created are. Ah, they have it right when they say that eyes are the window to the soul. What I wouldn't give for a pair of eyes like yours! So rare and so pure they are...

Uni: The cheekbone area needs a touch-up... Ah, still not as good as I hoped. Perhaps the bone structure itself is flawed. I need to start over.

#### Annoyed

Uni: Will you stop tugging at my sleeve? You're making my hand tremble. If the buyer goes home with a pockmarked face, they might visit your bedside in the middle of the night to give you a dressing-down.

Uni: You like watching me work? You can watch, but you'd better not disturb me, or... I might claim your most striking feature for myself.
