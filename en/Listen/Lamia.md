### Outfit 1: Hushed Tides

#### Attire Obtained

Lamia: Please don't be afraid. I... I am... uh... a mermaid who just owned my legs... Yes, I was captured by some merchant seamen and smuggled here. I managed to escape with your help. I'm not gonna hurt you, nor do I have the strength to do so. If you don't believe me, you can take the pearls that my teardrops turn into when I cry... as long as you agree to help me.

#### Level Up

Lamia: Thank you for freeing me from entanglement.

#### Greeting

Lamia: You've come just at the right time, savior. It just occurs to me that a brigantine arrived here from the Black Sea not so long ago. Could it be the vessel you've been looking for, the "Madame Maelstrom"? If you wish to check it out, I'll be happy to show you the way.

#### Ordinary Contact 1

Lamia: The elders warned me against greedy humans who would harm me for my appearance, tears, and flesh. But I know there are also good people like you in this world, pure in body and spirit, who must taste... Ah, I mean, who should be blessed with all the luck they need in their endeavors.

#### Ordinary Contact 2

Lamia: When you fulfill your dream of becoming a great sailor, please do visit me at my home. We live in the depths of Mother Sea, in a palace adorned with coral and gems, where music is played with conches and turtles... The undercurrents and whirlpools safeguarding us may be a bit dangerous. Nevertheless, I don't think they will pose much of a challenge to you.

#### Idle

Lamia: I sing in the deep sea, lulling you to sleep. As you fulfill your dream in dream, I'll have my feast complete.

#### Annoyed

Lamia: Ah... Oh, it's okay. You didn't hurt me. I got this wound from the sailboats and wish bottles while I was in the sea. It will heal on its own. Don't worry about me...

#### Special Attention

Lamia: Please don't... don't stare at my legs like that. I've just got them, and they are still fragile. But I think they will become strong enough very soon, provided that there's enough love... and nourishment.
