# Echoes of Phantasm

## Divine the Path

### Parable-0

A hermitage lay hidden from the bustle of the world. A disciple, having endured countless hardships, was at last accepted into the master's sect. Yet, day after day, the disciple swept fallen leaves in silence, never hearing a word from the master.

### F1-1 Truth Seeker

The Dao of Heaven cannot be defied. What is sought is not always found.

### Parable-1

One day, the disciple questioned this non-doing, which he had pondered day and night without finding an answer.
The master said, "Your heart still clings to purpose. That is why you can obtain no answer."
Then, he swept a sleeve across the desk. A copper coin fell to the ground. The disciple saw nothing, but heard a voice echoing in the air, "You shall journey with me now."
The disciple looked down and found a square hole framed by a round edge. Only then did the disciple understand that he had been a copper coin all along, hidden within the immortal's sleeve.

### F1-2 Departed

Once a soul departs through the three thousand realms, its bones can no longer be summoned back from the underworld.

### F1-3 Forsaken

In the boundless realm, seek the ones who have fallen from the Dao.

### Parable-2

The immortal walked through a forest and heard a starved bird's cry.
The immortal pointed toward the south, where it was warm. The bird flew there and survived.
The disciple asked, "Why not simply give it grain?"
The immortal replied, "The grain is man's mercy, yet when it runs out, the bird perishes. Spring brings life, and autumn brings death, thus migratory birds fly south by nature. I followed its nature and preserved its life. That is the art of non-doing."

### F1-4 Worldliness

You come to this mountain in search of the Dao. Yet how different is that from passing through the world without a name?

## Path's End

### F2-1 Wandering

Speak not of worldly matters when you meet another. Simply smile and gesture toward the drifting clouds.

### Parable-3

A great beast dwelled on the sea, and flocks of birds rested on its back. When the beast lunged to catch them, they all flew away.
The immortal touched its brow, and its urge to hunt vanished. The birds settled on it once more.
The disciple asked, "Is it not the beast's nature to hunt? Why suppress it?"
The immortal replied, "Man is born with desires, yet they must not be unrestrained. When the heart seeks nothing, it becomes one with heaven and earth. The art of non-doing requires the removal of greed."

### F2-2 Obsession

The soul is the seed of obsession. Given time, it will grow into something that towers over all.

### F2-3 Exile

This body has no place to return to. It stands alone in the vast unknown.

### Parable-4

In a dark place, the immortal found an old woman mourning her dead son. She tried to bribe the spirits by burning offerings, yet the smoke clouded her eyes, and even though her son's spirit stood right before her, neither could see the other.
The immortal summoned a breeze, blowing out the burning paper. Through tear-filled eyes, mother and son finally beheld one another. Before they made out each other's faces, their hearts already made the recognition.
The disciple asked, "Is this not a form of greed?"
The immortal replied, "People toil through life with their own pursuit. The art of non-doing does not strip away one's truest feelings."

### F2-4 Forbidden

A forbidden place. Whether it brings fortune or calamity, none can say.

## Born of Death

### F3-1 O Soul

O soul, return to the great void. In idleness and stillness, let the seasons pass.

### Parable-5

The journey ended, and they returned to the immortal's abode. The disciple asked again, "What is the art of non-doing?"
The immortal sighed, "The more you seek it, the further it slips away." Then the immortal cast the coin down through the clouds.
The coin fell into the mortal world, sinking into a river. Silent and still, it was washed by a thousand waves.
Through countless ages, its edges wore smooth, and its square hole filled with silt. In the end, it never found enlightenment.

### F3-2 Prophesy

Prophecy is a string; fate complements it with its hidden design.

### F3-3 Mortality

The living are passing travelers. The dead are those who return home.

### Parable-6

Then one day, the river ran dry, and the coin emerged. A child picked it up and laughed, "What a perfectly round little stone!"
The child tossed it into the forest. In midair, the coin's mind suddenly became clear.
It thought, "I once sought the straight path to the Dao. Now I am a useless circle."
When it hit the ground, it had neither form nor body. It dissolved into a gust of wind, scattering among the mountains and trees.
High above in the clouds, the immortal stroked his beard and smiled, "Now, you have found it."

### F3-4 Providence

If the great Dao persists in this mortal world, one need neither defy providence nor betray one's true nature.
