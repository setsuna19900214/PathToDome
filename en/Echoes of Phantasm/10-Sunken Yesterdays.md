# Sunken Yesterdays

## Sunken Solitude

### Stone Statue - 0

No one knows when it rose from the earth, nor does anyone ask how it climbed to the mountain's peak;
It is like a sigh, and also like an ominous prophecy;
Ancient corrosion and carvings slowly creep across the stone slate. Beneath the statue whose face has long blurred, death lies in heaps;
O distant one, when will this sea of despair be returned to tomorrow?

### G1-1 Prophesy

Humanity's best prophecy is the past that has already happened.

### Stone Statue - 1

When words become unbearable to behold, an unspeakable fear surges once more from the darkness;
The sky veiled in madness has become shrouded in shadow;
Here it began, and so here too it falls;
It endures upon the land trodden by countless souls, and perishes in the sorrow sung by countless voices.

### G1-2 Fear

An eternal gift named "Fear," said to be bestowed upon "the bravest of all."

### Stone Statue - 2

It bore witness as the storm descended from the heavens and broke the resilience of a city;
The chaotic time folded upon itself, reappearing and tearing apart;
Like a scar cut deep into blood and bone;
Was it the chaos between heaven and earth that made men forget the years, or was the calamity's horror truly unmatched even now?

### G1-3 Upheaval

The old city sinks, and change stirs abruptly.

### G1-4 Storm

When the storm arrives, salvation may be found.

## Visitor from Beyond

### Stone Statue - 3

It bore witness as a mountain-crushing tsunami swallowed a shore unfit for habitation;
The surging waves roared with fury and collapsed;
An invincible wall once vowed to crush all before it;
And from the depths of the sea, an ancient being drifts, breathing out an eternal lament.

### G2-1 Celestial Omen

The celestial omen hangs overhead like fate itself, the only sign and prophecy visible to mortal eyes.

### G2-2 Infinity

The gap between sea and sky—that is infinity.

### Stone Statue - 4

It bore witness as the pitch-black tide concealed lurking danger;
That which carries despair and fear reveals its true form;
Only then do they realize they are already ensnared in peril;
The undead that once devoured all is proof that a merciful being once bestowed its grace.

### G2-3 Boulder

Neither fool nor ignorant, and yet not stubborn as a boulder.

### G2-4 The Dead

Spirits that should have long faded wander the city, guiding your coming and going.

## Exalted Wail

### Stone Statue - 5

Next to the pitch-black sea, a traveler once passed by that legendary stone statue;
Chants echoed through the air, like a hymn, or perhaps a divine word;
How did it silently watch over all, and etch everything into the cracks of stone;
Faced with the many questions, the massive, unyielding rock spoke not still;
Why did the birds soaring through the sky leave no bones behind? Why do vanished spirits still linger here, unwilling to leave?
Why is the hymn of worship now a demonic sound of sacrifice? And why does no living footstep sound in this place?

### G3-1 Siren

How shall we sing of you? With blood, with faith.

### G3-2 Truth Seeker

One who seeks the truth of this world shall forever be bound by that same pursuit.

### Stone Statue - 6

When the earth roared and split apart, the souls buried beneath all dissipated into nothingness;
The dead sing atop the tower, and vengeful spirits wail upon the death bell;
Despair, fanaticism, and a sickly obsession are cast together into the sky. The truth seeker's heart laughs, buried deep in the earth;
You understand that this is the end of all things, and also the beginning;
In the prophecy, the tide of despair rose past mouth and nose. In it, all things dissolved gasp for air as they sink into the deepest sea;
But one day, when you hear the trembling lament of the statues within the city, that which awakens will be a new world.

### G3-3 Obsession

Human obsession is an incurable affliction.

### G3-4 The Torrent

The torrent of fate is a river that never rests; some will swim against its current, while others surrender to its depths.
