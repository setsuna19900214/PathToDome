# Fragmented Records

## City of Mist

### Rubbing 1

The mist falls like forgotten snowflakes, arriving silently before nightfall.
Soundless and not bound for return, the white shell slowly buries the past.
The mist is concealment, death dressed in a gentle gown,
not here to cause panic, just to whisper that it has already happened.

### H1-1 Mist

The city is shrouded by inexplicable mist, an omen of the impending calamity.

### Rubbing 2

The sea is silent, embracing every ebb and swirl,
just as it embraces wayward stars, sunken ships, nameless towns.
Every drop of seawater remembers a name,
yet not one drop questions why people have lost their words.
It only murmurs, and you can sink down,
like a pebble, like a star, then rise anew in the shimmering waves.
Weightless, all but blue remains.

### H1-2 Deepsea

Seawater gushes from nameless depths, absorbing the fog's pallor into its currents.

### H1-3 Calamity

The calamity descends from the sky, carving scars of pain and joy.

## Nameless One

### Rubbing 3

The night deepens, and the world tucks away its clamor.
The stars open their eyes in the sky.
They are not answers, merely a gradual elevation of the confusion,
peering with a detached stare from above.
Each tiny upward gaze
is an attempt to understand oneself,
and their reply
is a long night of brightness and silence.

### H2-1 Wisdom

When all is covered by silence and dust, only wisdom can decipher what remains.

### Rubbing 4

The fire only advances and rises,
like absolute resolve,
burning from the depths of night toward an unknowable dawn.
It consumes fear, incinerates excuses,
burns away that constantly yielding self,
all to rebuild upon the ashes,
a new city of will.

### H2-2 Courage

When danger and death follow like a shadow, only courage can light the way.

### H2-3 Willpower

And in utter descent and ascension, only willpower can transform oneself and the world.

## Moonlight Missive

### Rubbing 5

In the moonlight lies not finality,
but a possibility yet to take form,
like a trembling heartbeat in the womb,
gently unfurling in the darkness of night.
You will lean on it, but it cannot be grasped,
just as the future is still gestating,
truth is still forming.
Yet in this cascade of soft light, you already glimpse the world in its faint shapes.

### H3-1 Genesis

Thus, from the remains of death emerge spirits, heralding the ultimate transformation.

### Rubbing 6

The stele stands amid tempests and downpours
in the forsaken, crisscrossing wastelands,
silently inscribing names with silence,
preserving fragments that once pulsed,
destined to be forgotten.
With cuts, with ruin, with its weathered edges,
it remembers the wills that refuse to sink,
and those whose footprints have faded, whose hearts no longer beat,
how they devoted their lives to the questioning of time.

### H3-2 Assimilation

Madness. Sanity. The turbid waves of humanity churn, rewriting the causality of fate.

### H3-3 Separation

The perished fall deep, while the newborn rise high.

## Echoing Reprise

### Rubbing 7

Before words were created,
the world had already written its footnotes.
The ripples of water, the orbits of stars,
all unpronounced symbols.
We learn from the unknown,
examining things ancient yet new.
We analyze with truth,
transforming them into legible inscriptions.

### H4-1 Evolution

Amid endless change, new possibilities unfold.

### Rubbing 8

The scholar reaches out to touch it,
only to feel the shadow of the future self from years ahead,
blurry, clean, and silent.
When the mist disperses, the horizon remains gray.
Some departures never leave footprints behind.
For it is not an escape,
but a return to that dust-sealed, nameless monument that remembers it all.

### H4-2 Echo

The echoes continue to resound, awaiting departed and forthcoming visitors to play the notes.

### H4-3 Potestas

History becomes a secret, and those who unearth it gain power that transcends the unrevealed truth.
