# Thousandfold Dream

## City of Forging

### E1-1 Blade and Spear

Confronted by Blade Hunter and Spear Hunter. An initial trial of steel and fire.

### Steel Forge

Night descends, but the forge fire burns on. Steel casts a cold gleam in the darkness.

The forge's rumble gradually fades, leaving only a heartbeat synchronized with ancient gears.

A crimson mark is embedded in the bloodline, silently proclaiming an inviolable decree.

The last embers die down under the dark, vast night sky. Slowly, the prologue of a journey unfolds.

### E1-2 Rootless Blight

Confronted by Floating Seed and Manic Mutant. Please be prepared.

### Furnace of Gears

Deep in the furnace, the factory's gears shudder and roar, forming a low pulse that throbs through the night.

The warmth of furnace walls left by craftsmen of the old days lingers still. Step within, and you can hear the hushed melodies of steel, adding another mournful echo to the journey's hymn.

### E1-3 Molten Beast

Molten Beast appears. Defeat it swiftly and retreat before it's too late.

## City of Matrix

### E2-1 Sword and Cannon

Confronted by Internet Swordfighter and Digigunner. Data phantoms manifest as sword blades and gunfire.

### Radiant Matrix

Dazzling light signals surge among the matrices. Torrents of data fragments illuminate the somber city.

When strange symbols form exhilarating messages in dreams repeatedly, no one can tell if this is a longing of the subconscious or an actual invitation.

Deep within the endless stream of digits, the luminous flow of a beginning journey emerges.

### E2-2 Sand and Water

Confronted by Beach Volleyball Ace and Poisonous Jellyfish. Watch out for stings while engaging.

### Nexus of Light

Veins of light crisscross like rivers. Icy radiance circulates through mechanical cores. Pulses of flickering signals merge with heartbeat.

This is the city's heart, where countless streams of data converge and run silently toward unknown destinations.

### E2-3 Illusory Sea Fish

Illusory Sea Fish appears. Stay alert and break its disruption quickly.

## City of Talisman

### E3-1 Offense and Defense

Confronted by Macaque Master and Carapax Chimera. Attack without forgetting your defense.

### Dark Seal

Talismanic essence unfurls. Ancient laws murmur. Within the stillness emerge whispers from days long past.

A strange command takes shape from the shadowed void. A frigid mark settles upon a dark seal.

It utters no word, but points toward uncharted realms in the name of order. Thus the chapter unfolds, and fate quietly shifts its course.

### E3-2 Spirit Beasts

Confronted by Fortune Pixiu and Gourd Priest.

### Astral Spire

Talisman formations gleam. Celestial patterns ebb and flow. Countless constellations converge in the sky.

Here lies silence, calm as a tome. Only those who step within may read its chapters.

### E3-3 Sun Crow Ascendant

Sun Crow Apostle appears. Face the trial with caution.

## City of Sand

### E4-1 True Love

Confronted by Destined Lover with healing power. Please be prepared.

### Crucible of Sand

Hot sand churns. Mystic fire smolders. Slumbering marks of covenant stir to life in the silent night.

Ancient embers stir in the deep well, their feeble light refracting venerable phantoms.

Talismanic runes lie still and soundless, yet with blazing flames announce the path ahead to travelers.

And so the journey begins from here. In the boundless desert, fiery shadows sway in the wind.

### E4-2 Sand Bandit

Amid raging blaze and surging sand, only through endurance can you advance.

### Well of Flames

The well walls lie broken, and flames gleam from its depths. Mystic sand gestates unknown power within the bottomless well.

Ancient whispers surge up from shadowed strata, and within them, unfinished vows seem to echo still.

### E4-3 Tower of Sand

Scotch appears. Defeat it swiftly and retreat before it's too late.

## City of Assembly

### City of Thousand Faces

Bones of stone seal the ancient lamentations. Wind sweeps through crumbling walls, carrying chaotic whispers.

The ramparts collapsed long ago. Flameless light flickers, retreating from endless illusions, unable to tell lies from truth.

And there, between the dream's end and oblivion, Its will has quietly stirred to life. It speaks no name, reveals no form, but watches in the guise of a thousand faces as you travelers arrive.
