# Sentinel

## WhiteSands Flow

### X-1 WhiteSands Boarder

The desert reflected in the light, gradually fading away from its original color and transforming into an infinite sea of white sands.

### X-2 WhiteSands Surface

Like an indistinct mist, the wandering white sands gently brushed around Chief's body.

### X-3 WhiteSands Depth

Guided by an ineffable power, Chief sank into the sea of sand, falling towards the deeper depths.

#### Starting point of Level 1 of Phase 2

The illusory WhiteSands flow incessantly, like an underground river. At the point where these "rivers" intersect, a certain rift flickers, seemingly passable.

#### Hints

While exploring this place, you hear chaotic murmurings surrounding you. Something malicious is chasing you.

"Do not step into the first river you encounter, seek its other end." Someone whispers in your ear.

#### Gold Medal Point 1

An old stone slate, deeply buried in the WhiteSands, bearing evident artificial scratches.

"What time is it now?" Amidst the void, you seem to hear such a voice.

#### Gold Medal Point 2

An old stone slate, deeply buried in the WhiteSands, bearing traces of doodles.

Have you found them? Someone murmurs.

#### Gold Medal Point 3

An old stone slate, deeply buried in the WhiteSands, bearing many twisted lines.

These lines are interwoven, chaotic, and complex, making it difficult to discern what the engraver wanted to express.

Who... Who's there...

Why... Why? Why disturb us?!

### EX-X WhiteSands Phantom

Once again, the unknown monsters attacked. Enraged by the intrusion of an outsider into the depths, they swarmed towards Chief.

## Starsea Tides

### Y-1 Starsea Boarder

Passing through the bubble, Chief's consciousness seemed to be floating among a radiant sea of stars.

### Y-2 Starsea Surface

"Truth..." "This is the truth..." The compelling voice grew louder and louder as Chief pondered.

### Y-3 Starsea Depth

The compelling voice stirred back among the sea of stars, dragging Chief into deeper space.

#### Starting point for 2 stages and 2 levels

This bubble is filled with countless stars, and your consciousness is suspended among them.

You realize that the path you're taking through the bubbles follows someone's past traces.

This person had many targets to find and made many attempts to seek them. Just like the faint human figure you see through the spyglass, she is also one of the targets.

#### Hints

The light between the stars forms rifts. Before passing through them, you need to think carefully. What seems like a safe destination may not be the right path...

...And what appears to be a dangerous future may be the road you should embark upon.

#### Gold Medal Point 1

As you approach, you notice that this figure is flickering and blurry, seemingly communicating with someone, or pleading for help.

Your arrival interrupts the cycle of this image. The shattered crown gently falls to the ground.

#### Gold Medal Point 2

As you approach, you notice that this figure is flickering and blurry, but the astrolabe in their hand is very clear.

Your arrival interrupts the cycle of this image. The astrolabe gently falls to the ground.

Who... Who's there...

Why... Why? Why disturb us?!

## DisSea Vortex

### Z-1 DisSea Boarder

Passing through the bubble, Chief arrives at the DisSea, familiar yet unfamiliar.

### Z-2 DisSea Surface

Following the observed exit of the rift, Chief walks through the DisSea.

### Z-3 DisSea Depth

Unusual images appear in the distance, and Chief continues toward the depths.

#### Starting point for Phase 2 Level 3

You've been to the DisSea, but compared to here, even the DisSea seems all too "calm."

Screaming monsters, black liquid wildly twisting into various shapes in the air, and the faintly visible voices of temptation all around, are all assaulting your spirit.

But you find some listless illusions standing still, their gaze seemingly fixed in one direction.

With the shield in your hand, you can knock these monsters down from behind.

#### Gold Medal Point 1

You see a familiar stone slate, but this time it's much more complete, the content on it becomes barely recognizable.

Thousands and thousands of scratches. If each scratch represents a unit of time, how long has the engraver been here?

#### Gold Medal Point 2

You see a familiar stone slate, but this time it's much more complete, the content on it becomes barely recognizable.

This is a fresco. On it, one figure after another marches forward in a long queue. Their target is a crystal that is emitting red light.

At the end of the line, a shrunken figure is squatting on the ground, covering its head with its hands.

#### Gold Medal Point 3

You see a familiar stone slate, but this time it's much more complete, the content on it becomes barely recognizable.

These intertwined lines faintly form a figure. Maybe it's a map, or maybe it's just some more random murmurs.

#### Endpoint

After passing through countless whirlpools, you finally arrive at the exit of this bubble.

A man waits here, just as he has in the past.

These human shapes vary in size and appearance, but they all share one thing: looking at you. You feel this pressure coming at you from all directions.

Though no one speaks, you can understand what these glances are trying to convey. "Please take us... with you," they plead thus.

Why... Why? Why disturb us?!
