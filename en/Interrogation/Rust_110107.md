# Rust Interrogation

## Rust - Interrogation I

> Intelligence before completion:
> Following the Ash Campaign, suspicious figures wielding the Underground's Mania sorcery appear in the ruins of BR-005. You are a part of this joint arrest operation, but you've never expected to meet this familiar yet strange person under these circumstances...

> Intelligence after completion:
> Through the resonance of the shackles, you are certain this man with an unfamiliar face and voice is the same "Rust" who once aided you at the Drifter Camp. You manage to extract some critical intel from him, yet the reason he deliberately severed ties with you remains unknown. Before you can get any answers, "Rust" brazenly escapes from the Bureau and vanishes without a trace.
