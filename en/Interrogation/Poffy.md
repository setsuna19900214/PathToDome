# Poffy Interrogation

## Poffy - Interrogation I

> To secure her stay at the Bureau, Poffy deliberately emphasizes the dangerous nature of her abilities, her words hinting at some untold secret. However, none of the *crimes* she's mentioned are recorded. You decide to suspend the interrogation and conduct a more thorough investigation into her past.
> Ridiculously, when the crisis is finally resolved, the first thing she asks about is extra meal options.

Poffy: Whoa... So this is the famous *must-see spot* that every Sinner has to visit—the Interrogation Room?

Poffy: The lighting really sets the mood! These instruments are actually monitoring my vitals, huh. This chair... did you really install these screws yourself, Chief?

**【Advance】**

- ...

**end**

The interrogation room is dimly lit, with a table separating the two of you.

Sitting in her chair and swinging her legs, the young Sinner girl named Poffy looks around in all directions with curiosity.

Poffy: This table is so cool! It feels like we could shoot a spy movie here! Oh, a camera! Can Sinners stream from here too?

Poffy: Hello there! I'm going on camera now!

This should be the time to set aside the day's exhaustion and enjoy a peaceful dinner, yet here you are dealing with the Bureau's newest member and her relentless barrage of questions.

You open Poffy's file with a resigned sigh.

Five days ago, the Bureau received an anonymous tip.

The letter was short and simple. Rumors have been circulating online that a *lurking glutton* has been snatching food from passersby near the entrance to Eastside's street food market, and the suspect is believed to be a Sinner. This *lurking glutton* is allegedly a mukbang streamer and none other than Poffy the glutton.

After verifying the information, the Bureau's staff quickly obtained her residential address. When they arrived at her door, she showed no intention of fleeing. Instead, she had already packed her bags and was waiting at the entrance.

Such an enthusiastic welcome was certainly a first. As you stood there holding up your identification in bewilderment, a head wearing a brown hat poked out from the living room. You've stumbled upon an old acquaintance.

Luminita: Oh my, Chief! You're here too? What a coincidence! This girl here is one of my clients. I had no idea she was a Sinner!

Luminita: You need her to cooperate with the investigation, right? No problem at all! I already told her the Bureau has great food, and the Chief there is a wonderful person. She can totally settle in there without any worries.

Luminita: Back in the Bureau, don't you guys always say that *better care for Sinners starts with all of us*? She only likes eating a little too much. No need to be so hard on her, okay?

Luminita rattled off a bunch of sentences, leaving you no chance to interrupt. Then she turned to pat Poffy's shoulder, offering earnest advice.

Luminita: Though you're going to the Bureau, rent for this unit is paid annually and non-refundable! But... given that we've had a Sinner in this apartment, I can try to ask the landlord for a discount on the rent next year!

At the time, you didn't think too hard about it, simply focusing on bringing the cooperative Sinner back to the Bureau. Now it seems like you might be the one who got played?

Five days later, Nightingale stood in the Chief's office, looking utterly helpless for the first time.

Nightingale: Chief, Sinner Poffy's Emotion Test results came back normal. Her stats are at a stable, above-average level. Her social skills, cognitive function, and values all meet our standards for an ordinary person.

Nightingale: Over these five days, she's spent most of her time in the Bureau's cafeteria, sometimes even helping the staff with cooking. She's earned unanimous praise from the kitchen crew.

Nightingale: She posts photos on social media every day with titles like *What I Was Fed Today*. The posts are getting a lot of traction, and now her fans are extremely interested in what meals the Bureau serves every day.

Nightingale: She's also getting along very well with people in the Bureau. She's already given Wynn fashion advice, discussed trendy new restaurants with Ignis, and submitted twenty-six suggestions to the cafeteria.

**【Advance】**

- Doesn't that sound pretty good?

**end**

Chief: Doesn't sound like there are any issues? She's lively, cheerful, and gets along well with everyone she meets.

Then Nightingale reluctantly handed you a document, her expression troubled.

Nightingale: The logistics department just filed a formal complaint. According to the statistics, the Bureau's food reserves have decreased by... 37%. At this rate, our quarterly food budget will be exceeded in another two weeks.

Chief: ...

Nightingale: ...

Chief: *Having a big appetite isn't a bad thing, but eating the Bureau out of house and home definitely is...*

Chief: *Don't tell me she voluntarily came to the Bureau just to freeload meals...*

The girl sitting across from you beams with a bright smile, her fingers twining together slightly nervously. It's nearly impossible to harbor any suspicion toward that innocent face.

You shake your head, pushing that ridiculous guess out of your mind. Several faces flash across your mind. Every single Sinner who turned themselves in to the Bureau before her has been nothing but trouble. You can't let her appearance fool you!

Chief: Do you know why you were brought here?

Poffy nods earnestly.

Poffy: Of course I know! Otherwise I wouldn't have come along so willingly.

Chief: This interview should have taken place on your first day at the Bureau, but given your voluntary cooperation and the special circumstances, we're only starting today.

Chief: Most people would run at the sight of a Bureau agent, not pack their bags and wait at the door. Did you get tipped off, or did Luminita tell you something?

Poffy: Oh goodness... I'm just a small-time food streamer. How would I know anything about the Bureau? Actually... even if you hadn't come that day, I would have turned myself in anyway!

Chief: Luminita should have told you that Sinners here don't have the same freedom as ordinary people. Even knowing that, you still chose to come. Why?

Poffy is clearly a little flustered by your serious grilling, though she manages to maintain her smile.

Poffy: Because... because my ability is just too terrifying! If you don't keep me contained and just let me live freely out there, it'll definitely make a disaster!

Chief: Like snatching someone's fried chicken drumstick at Eastside's busiest food market?

Poffy: Oh, that drumstick smelled sooo good! Made me feel like someone was having a percussion session with chicken drumsticks inside my head. I was so hungry I was about to lose my mind... I just couldn't resist!

Chief: Quite a creative metaphor...

You study the girl smiling sweetly across from you, trying to read into her expression, but quickly give up. She seems genuine and friendly enough. No wonder everyone at the Bureau likes her.

But you still believe that as a food streamer who's signed with MCN, Poffy should be able to support herself financially. Her voluntary arrival at the Bureau and ready confession to her crimes suggest her motives go deeper than simply mooching for free meals.

Chief: I've encountered many Sinners—more than your age in years. Among them, quite a few indeed possess abilities that are truly terrifying.

Chief: Compared to them, or at least the vast majority of them, your special power and the impact you can cause are relatively mild. It's nothing worthy of concern.

You slide her file forward, where *Danger Assessment: A-Class* is clearly written. Feeling her abilities questioned, Poffy puffs out her cheeks in indignation.

Poffy: Hey, don't say that! I... I can be really scary when I'm hungry!

Poffy: I'd lurk around like a starving ghost. Whether it's a steamed bun in a homeless man's hand or a snack a child's munching on, no food can escape my clutches! I'll even charge into bakeries and devour every cookie the staff just finished baking...

Poffy: Simply put... I'm a potentially dangerous Sinner you should not overlook! If you don't detain me today, tomorrow's headline is gonna be *Girl Devours City's Last Bowl of Rice*!

You can't help but laugh at Poffy's exaggerated threats, shrugging dismissively.

Chief: Honestly, a charge like snatching snacks from people at the street food market... no one would've even bothered to arrest you if it hadn't been for the report.

Chief: If I saw a girl so hungry she was seeing stars, I'd offer her food myself.

Poffy is completely stunned. She stares at you blankly, her cheeks flushing bright red.

Her lips move slightly. Just when you expect her to muster the courage to continue arguing, her defiant spirit suddenly deflates.

Poffy: Can we take a little break? We've been talking for a while and I'm getting hungry... Um, am I allowed to eat here?

Now it's your turn to be stunned.

You were prepared for another round of verbal sparring, but now you're forced to call a halt. If what she said is true, that hunger makes her go insane, then...

**【Advance】**

- No food or drink in the interrogation room.

**end**

Poffy: Are you serious?! Is the Bureau really that strict?

Poffy's face falls. She fidgets anxiously, picking and scratching at things as she tries to cope with her hunger-induced anxiety.

You watch her every move closely, maintaining your stern expression as you continue questioning.

Chief: Are there any other harmful effects of your special power? Scaring people by stealing their food alone isn't grounds for long-term detention.

Poffy: I can eat thirty rice balls in one sitting!

Chief: ...That's only a threat to your wallet, not to anyone else. You're still young, but you should watch out for diabetes.

You let out a sigh and make a show of closing your notebook, preparing to stand up and end today's interrogation.

Poffy: We... we're not done yet! I've done quite a few bad things! I just... haven't told you everything! What if you let me out and my special power goes out of control and hurts my neighbors? That would be all my fault!

Poffy frantically bites her fingernails, then steels herself and suddenly lowers her voice, a mischievous grin spreading across her face.

Poffy: Once she's starved past a certain point, the big bad Poffy will go rogue! Whether it's cupcakes or people, they'll all become my food!

Poffy: What's even more terrifying is that I don't simply become insane. I also lose all memory of what's happened during an outburst!

Poffy: My first Mania outburst happened while we were recording a program. I chewed on a crew member's arm on the set, which not only interrupted the shooting but also almost brought on a huge publicity crisis.

Poffy: Though Mom paid a lot of money to scrub the videos and comments back then, if you dig deep enough, you should still be able to find some posts from that time.

Poffy swallows hard, her eyes darting around her backpack filled with snacks.

Poffy: The second time was when I was in high school. My hunger triggered a food explosion at school! It hurt my classmates and almost got the school into a scandal...

Poffy: Mom pleaded with everyone and paid a huge sum to keep the press quiet, so we managed to cover that incident up... Otherwise, I would've been arrested long ago.

Poffy: You may not understand just from hearing about it. But seeing is believing, I can give you a demonstration!

Hearing no objection from you, Poffy rummages in her bag and pulls out a bag of corn kernels, pinching one between her fingers.

With a loud pop, the corn kernel explodes suddenly, turning into a piece of popcorn.

Poffy: See? I made the kernel pop with my bare hand! And that's just 10%... no, 1% of my power! See how lethal I am as a Sinner?

Chief: ......

Seeing your silence, she grabs another handful of corn kernels. With a series of pops and crackles, the kernels quickly expand, and fluffy white popcorn spills from her palm as the aroma fills the entire interrogation room.

She frees up her other hand to grab yours, shoving all the popcorn into your palm.

Poffy: And mind you, I'm not even starving right now, yet I can already make food explode instantly. During a Mania outburst, my ability will truly scare you!

You hold the still-warm popcorn that continues to crackle softly, pick up a piece, and pop it into your mouth.

Chief: A human microwave, huh...

You can indeed sense the Mania stirring more and more vigorously within her. Perhaps it's because she hasn't reached that level of hunger yet, or maybe your presence is suppressing the Mania within her, but for now she still appears to be perfectly lucid and normal.

Grrr... Her stomach rumbles loudly in the interrogation room. Poffy looks at you with pleading eyes and swallows hard.

Poffy: Um... Chief, look, the popcorn's all ready. Can't we share? I'm really so hungry...

You sigh and pour the popcorn back into Poffy's hands.

Poffy lets out a small cheer, then starts crunching away happily, her face glowing with pure bliss.

Amid the gentle crunching sounds, Poffy's mood quickly settles, and the Mania energy in the interrogation room gradually stabilizes as well.

Poffy: Your file is missing some crucial information! None of the major incidents I caused are even in there. You should investigate more thoroughly!

You gently tap Poffy's file. As she said, the information in this file is incomplete. Apart from including Poffy's past family situation and child star experience, it indeed makes no mention of the two incidents she spoke of.

If it's really as Poffy said, that her mother played the role of *protector* in those incidents, it's possible they were never reported to the Bureau. You plan to assign someone to investigate more thoroughly once the interrogation is finished.

Chief: I'll arrange for someone to look into them. But I hope the understanding is mutual, Miss Poffy.

Chief: Currently, the incidents you've caused don't meet the severity threshold for detention, but given that you've requested to be detained... if you're really facing some difficulties, the Bureau will find a way to help you.

Poffy: Really?

Poffy's eyes flicker, seeming truly swayed by your words. She unconsciously reaches toward her backpack, but the moment her fingers touch the zipper, she pulls back and looks at you anxiously.

Poffy: ...Will you really help me?

Chief: If you tell the truth.

Luminita: Anyway, whatever it takes, you must stay in the Bureau. Don't let them kick you out!

Just when you think Poffy would lower her guard and spill the truth, she suddenly buckles at the knees and collapses to the floor, even positioning herself toward the camera.

Poffy: Boohoo... you're all so mean! You think I eat too much, so you want to kick me out!

Poffy: But I've been helping out too! I cooked lots and lots of delicious rice, and everyone praised me! What's a couple extra bites...? Just let me stay! I'll do anything. I can even wash dishes!

Not expecting this turn of events, you rub your temples in exasperation.

Chief: ...You should be cooperating with the investigation, not applying for a job at the cafeteria. We're not understaffed.

Poffy: This is Sinner abuse! I'm going to file a complaint! I demand arbitration! I demand legal intervention! This violates the Sinner protection law!

Chief: Is there such a law? Which provision does it violate?

Poffy: I, I don't know... Anyway, you can't just bully me like this! This is wrong! Boohoo...

As you said, the number of Sinners you've encountered indeed exceeds Poffy's age in years, but you've barely met any with such a childish temperament who would throw tantrums when losing an argument like Poffy.

You feel ridiculous as you watch the girl crying on the floor, but you're beginning to understand something. This little foodie is probably hiding some other secrets, but for some reason, she can't divulge them.

Chief: Alright, get up... You'll remain detained.

Poffy: For real? Can I get extra portions for dinner then? I've been in here for so long, I'm starving!

Poffy's expression immediately brightens, and she nimbly scrambles up from the ground.

Utterly speechless now, you scoff. This girl hasn't stopped snacking throughout the entire interrogation, and now she's still going on about being hungry. You finally understand how the cafeteria storage got cleaned out.

Chief: Fine, you can have my chicken drumstick. Happy now?

Poffy: Oh my gosh! Chief, you're so good to me! I knew you were a nice person!

Poffy patters out of the interrogation room. Your ears can finally have some peace.

**【Advance】**

- Phew...

**end**

You let out a deep sigh and gather up the snack wrappers Poffy has left behind, shoving them all into the trash can.

Chief: Well, it's just one more mouth to feed. Shouldn't be a problem... The investigation needs to continue anyway. Temporary detention isn't unwise.

Chief: But before the investigation is complete, I hope the Bureau's food budget can hold up... Should I apply for additional funding in advance?

The next day, Nightingale places a letter on your office desk.

Nightingale: The staff pulled an all-nighter conducting a detailed investigation into Poffy's background. The report will be updated soon. This letter was found in the mailbox at her front door. It hasn't been opened yet.

Chief: It seems she was too eager to seek refuge with the Bureau and forgot to check her mailbox before leaving.

Nightingale: That should be the case.

Nightingale reports on some other tasks and soon leaves.

Sitting at your office desk, you stare at the pristine letter with one line written on it: To Miss Poffy.

Perhaps this letter can answer your questions: Why does Poffy so desperately want to stay at the Bureau, even at the cost of losing her freedom? And why does she worry so much about the potential impact of her special power?

## Poffy - Interrogation II

> Following Mr. Fox's suggestion, you pose as her guardian and assistant, accompanying her to fulfill her contractual obligations. After witnessing her working conditions and learning about her past, you decide to help the girl escape her suffering and reclaim her freedom.
> Handing a key to someone is easy. The challenge lies in realizing that you're not the one holding it, and finding a way to take it back.

In the Chief's office, steam rises from two cups of hot tea.

Poffy stands in front of your office desk with her head lowered, occasionally stealing guilty glances at you.

Chief: *...Miss Poffy, this is your final warning. According to your contract, you must continue to fulfill your streaming obligations.*

Chief: *Any breach of contract will result in serious losses to the Company. In such an event, in addition to paying substantial liquidated damages, you will also bear full legal responsibility. The Company reserves all rights to pursue legal action and hold you accountable.*

You finish reading out the notice, then look up at Poffy.

Chief: Now, can you explain your reason for coming to the Bureau?

Poffy: Oh dear, nothing escapes you, Chief...

Poffy sticks out her tongue, trying to charm her way out of trouble by acting cutesy.

Poffy: My admirable, kind-hearted, and merciful Chief! Could you please just let this poor, innocent, and penniless Sinner hide in here for a while?

Poffy: The Bureau has detained soooo many Sinners already. What's one more, right?

You wave the bill in your hand. The number of zeros trailing the figure is staggering. The paper flutters to the ground, and Poffy lunges forward to snatch it up, crumples it into a ball, and hurls it into the trash.

She doesn't get up. Instead, she wraps her arms around your leg and breaks down in heart-wrenching sobs.

Poffy: Chief, you've got to help me!

Chief: Quit rolling around on the ground and clinging to people's legs...

You reach out to help her up, but notice her body is trembling slightly.

Poffy: They... they're bad people who'll do anything for money!

Poffy: They would lock me in a streaming room and make me eat all kinds of weird food, then just keep filming videos, nonstop...

Her words come in broken fragments, as if she's remembering something terrible. She's fighting against the memories this notice has awakened and everything she refuses to face.

Seeing her distress, you finally realize this isn't some act, but a genuine cry for help. You drop your teasing tone, your voice turning serious.

Chief: If you're being exploited, why not seek legal action?

Poffy shakes her head vigorously.

Poffy: It won't work... It's not that simple...

Poffy: Sob... If you don't let me stay, I'll be hunted down by those debt collector thugs, or get captured by the company again! It's horrible! Those were portions even I couldn't finish... I really can't gorge myself like that anymore!

Poffy cries for a long time until her stomach begins growling with hunger. She then asks timidly if she could go to the cafeteria to eat something. You nod and ask Nightingale to help comfort her.

After she's left, you call for Mr. Fox.

Mr. Fox: I've reviewed the contract. It's fully legally binding. Once signed, there's virtually no way to get out unscathed.

Chief: From what I understand, she appears to have suffered severe psychological oppression and physical abuse.

Chief: Is there really no way at all?

Mr. Fox: Who do you think I am? Of course there's a way, but it's one I wouldn't recommend.

Mr. Fox: Or, she could fulfill her contractual obligations for now and complete her current assignments. Then we'll find a way to get her out with minimal consequences.

Mr. Fox: You could pose as Miss Poffy's guardian or assistant and visit her workplace. We might uncover something useful.

Mr. Fox smiles, his eyes behind his glasses narrowing into gleaming slits.

Mr. Fox: This sort of thing is right up your alley, isn't it, Chief?

You pretend not to catch his teasing tone and carefully consider the plan. Finally, you let out a sigh.

Chief: This is the best option we have right now. At least... it's worth a shot.

Early the next morning, Poffy leads you to MCN's building.

Outside the fancy corporate tower, employees in immaculate business attire stride past with hurried steps, while Poffy beside you desperately tries to shrink behind your back, unwilling to be seen.

Poffy: Ugh... Just being back here makes my stomach hurt...

Chief: Poffy, can I ask you a question?

Poffy: Oh, sure. Go ahead.

Chief: You're a huge foodie. Isn't streaming yourself enjoying delicious food exactly what you'd want?

Poffy: That's what I thought at first, so I signed the contract... But things turned out very differently.

Poffy: Sigh, I don't really know how to explain it, Chief. You'll see for yourself in a bit.

Poffy uses her staff card to get you two into the building, then the elevator takes you up to a certain floor.

The chaos before you is shocking. Decorations and clothes hangers are scattered across the floor, while staff members navigate through the mess with practiced ease, as if this disorder were perfectly routine.

??: Hey, Poffy! Stop dawdling around over there!

Poffy: !!!

Poffy instinctively ducks behind you, but the speaker has already stridden forward and grabs her shoulder, dragging her directly toward the makeup room.

**【Advance】**

- Stop him

**end**

Chief: Hey! What are you doing?

??: So you're that so-called assistant of hers?

The man gives you a cold, appraising look, then turns his attention to Poffy, his tone growing even icier.

??: If you need something, make a proper request to the company. You can't just hire someone on a whim.

Manager: How many times have I told you? I'm your manager. You need to clear everything with me first.

Poffy's lips twitch, but she can't get a single word out. You catch her eye and signal for her to stay calm.

The manager shoves her into the chair in front of the makeup station, and the makeup artists immediately swarm around her. He keeps refreshing work documents on his device while continuing to grumble about Poffy.

Manager: Do you have any idea how much money you've cost the company by disappearing these past few days?

Poffy: But you literally told me that the company didn't give a damn about me...

Manager: ...And now you've learned to talk back! Sure, you're useless, but still one of our products. Have you ever seen a product on a shelf just up and disappear whenever it wants?!

Manager: You're going to make up for every stream and sponsor you've missed. I'm booking you for both day streams and night streams. Pull an all-nighter if you have to, I don't care!

You can barely stand listening to all this, but meanwhile Poffy just hangs her head as she recites the ad copy, her expression completely numb.

Manager: Get ready, rolling in! Fill lights over here!

Click! The massive fill lights blind your eyes, making them water reflexively. But Poffy, standing directly in their glare, suddenly springs to life as if someone has just flipped her power switch on.

Tucking away her earlier timidity and anxiety, she straightens her posture and instantly switches into her *streamer mode* with a beaming smile, greeting the audience beyond the camera professionally.

Poffy: Hey everyone, long time no see! I look chubbier? Wow, can't hide anything from you guys, can I? I've been eating well lately, indeed!

She makes a heart gesture at the camera, playfully bantering with her fans before smoothly transitioning to the main topic.

Poffy: Ta-da! Today's mukbang challenge: Rating Cursed Food! Are these suspicious-looking dishes actually edible?

The camera pulls back to reveal her streaming studio setup. Spread across the table are a purple-black jelly, some fluorescent green sushi, a charcoal pudding, and fried durian... All of the dishes are unnaturally colored, and their strange aromas mix in the air.

Poffy: Whoa, the colors don't look too pleasant!

She holds up a piece of jelly and gives it a shake for the camera, its slimy texture gleaming with an ominous sheen under the studio lights.

Poffy: Doesn't this purple thing look like an alien brain? Chat, can I get some encouragement please!

You glance at the stream's real-time analytics and see her views instantly rocket to #1 in the food category.

After hyping up her audience, Poffy heroically swallows the jelly, her smile freezing. A crew member turns away to dry heave, while Poffy quickly recovers and continues her commentary.

Poffy: Hmm... this tastes like... the tears shed after durian and glue had a breakup.

Poffy: As for the texture... it's all gooey and sticky, like biting into a broken pencil tip. But hey, it's still perfectly edible!

Next up is the fluorescent green sushi.

Poffy: It tastes like detergent mixed with wasabi. My mouth feels like it's just been deep-cleansed. Way too intense! My stomach's screaming!

Poffy: Honestly, this stuff's better suited for an April Fools prank. I wouldn't recommend actually eating it yourself! 3, 2, 1! Link's up!

The product link appears, and it sells out instantly. Poffy's eyes crinkle with delight as she laughs and makes teasing comments.

Poffy: Haha, I knew you guys would perk up at the mention of pranks. Huh? Make my assistant try some?

Poffy waves her hand dismissively, feigning a serious expression as she speaks.

Poffy: Nah! Took me long enough to find the right assistant. Don't wanna have to look for a new one so soon!

You cooperatively hold up a sign pleading for mercy, and she smoothly takes the grand finale from your hands. A smoking black pudding.

Poffy: Last but not least, here comes our final product! This is a real treat from our sponsor. Don't miss out!

Poffy takes a big bite, pauses for a few seconds, then smiles and gives a thumbs up while flashing two rows of now completely blackened teeth.

Poffy: It might look icky, but it tastes surprisingly good! The bitter richness of cocoa mingles with the sweet crunch of wafer. Underneath its tough exterior, the inside's actually soft and delicious! See, it even turns your teeth black so you can take the perfect goofy selfies! Hehe!

And with a few rapid taps, Poffy snaps six selfies with her blackened teeth. The chat is exploding with comments and likes. Next to her, you hold up the next prop, but a heaviness settles in your chest.

Coordinator: Hey, check the comments.

The coordinator leans over to direct the manager's attention to the screen. You move closer as well, and several harsh comments immediately catch your eye...

AAA Foodie: When did she get so fat? Has she been gorging herself?

Notice_Me: She's so fat it's making her ads less appealing.

BreadCart: I should go on a diet so I don't end up like her.

The sponsor's expression darkens considerably, and the manager quickly gives an apologetic smile.

Manager: Sure, the comments aren't exactly nice, but look at the engagements! Better to be talked about than ignored, right? Sales are through the roof too. That's what really matters, isn't it?

The tension eases slightly, but the manager immediately turns back to throw a stern glare at Poffy.

Chief: *So this is what being a food streamer is really like. Constantly pushing boundaries and even humiliating yourself for attention... It’s so far from her original, simple wish to just eat what she wanted.*

Chief: *What a shame everyone here has long grown accustomed to this culture. No one cares anymore.*

You watch Poffy in front of the camera. She looks like a wound-up doll, mouth curved in a smile while her eyes are hollow. You finally understand what she meant when she said *you'll see for yourself in a bit*.

Chief: *It seems that whatever passion you have, once it becomes work, it gets ruined a little.*

The two-hour stream finally ends, and Poffy lets out a long breath, like a soldier retreating from battle.

She drags herself back to the dressing room with heavy steps. You offer her a handkerchief, and she manages a weak smile as she wipes her hands, exhaustion filling her eyes.

Poffy: The taste was... well, hard to describe, but at least I got a full meal out of it, hehe.

Not wanting you to worry, she gives her belly a playful pat, trying to keep the mood light.

The next second, her manager storms over in a rage, hurling a thick stack of reports at her—

Manager: Take a good look yourself! The comments were all calling you fat and ugly! Nobody even mentioned the products!

Poffy frantically crouches down to pick up the scattered sheets. Her fingertips tremble so badly she can barely grasp them, her eyes locked on those harsh comments.

Poffy: I... I already tried my best to promote them...

Her voice sounds feeble, as if defending herself against the agent, but also against herself.

Manager: Tried your best? How many projects has the company set up for you? We've tried every single gimmick to get attention, and yet you're still getting crushed by that pretty girl from Hummingbird Entertainment!

Manager: How many times have I told you to lose weight?! People don't want to see another fat girl doing mukbang! Get any bigger and your career is over!

Those words seem to trigger something buried deep within Poffy. She whips around as if struck by a bolt of lightning, rushing toward the bathroom unsteadily.

Manager: Still running away? I don't know what I was thinking when I signed you! Once I'm done with my other tasks, I'll deal with you properly...

It isn't until the manager gets called away by the coordinator that your ears finally have some peace.

You follow Poffy, initially thinking she is just escaping from her manager's verbal abuse, but then you hear someone dry heaving in the bathroom.

When you arrive, Poffy keeps rinsing her hands in the basin. Even though her stomach has already been emptied, her body seems to be protesting habitually, forcing her to purge every last bit of what it deems excess.

Seeing you approach, she hastily wipes her face with the back of her hand, then buries her face in her palms and begins to cry with quiet, restrained sobs filled with anguish.

As if she's afraid of being heard, her sobs are so quiet they're nearly drowned out by the running water, yet they carry a suffocating despair.

Poffy: It should be a happy thing to have my stomach full... So why does it cause me so much pain now?

Poffy: I don't want to throw up, but my body's gotten used to it... I've always been a chubby girl, and back then everyone liked me... Why has it changed now...

Poffy: Why do I still have to lose weight even after I ran away from Mom? What am I supposed to do? I don't know anymore... Please tell me what to do... Chief...

You find yourself at a loss for words. All you can do is gently pat her on the back before leading her back to the makeup room to rest.
