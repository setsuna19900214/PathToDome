Donald: Honestly, the chef's magic here... A little buzz and who'd need a capture team, right? The Sinners outside would queue up in a heartbeat.

As he speaks, Donald flashes a thumbs-up to Kelvin and Ignis working their wonders in the nearby kitchen.

Donald: A bowl of noodles next, I reckon. Then, surprise me with dessert, and I'll be on my merry way.

Donald: Gotta have the ice cream, it's a hit here at MBCC, right?

Chief: Come to think of it, a shrink from Eastside Psychiatric Hospital... and one under Nirvana's thumb. How'd you even get in touch with him?

Donald: You know me, Donald, the walking Rolodex, pals everywhere. Knowing a shrink isn't exactly a shocker, is it?

Donald muses for a second, then remarks:

Donald: Oh, and a heads-up, the guy's not exactly Mr. Nice, tends to rub people the wrong way with his talk... Just don't mind that, he's actually alright at heart...

Chief: Right, I've got a handle on this.

Donald: Good as long as you know what you're doing...

Donald starts to speak, looking like he's got more to add, but he appears to be holding back a bit.

Chief: Out with it.

Donald: Ha, yeah... right, there's no point in mincing words with you.

Donald: Anyway, it's not like me to ask for help much, but this time... I really do need your hand in this.

Donald: I'm asking you, please.
