# Golan Interrogation

## Golan - Interrogation I

> Golan is confirmed to be a member of the Iron Gang. Though she still seems to be holding many secrets, the MBCC has preliminarily identified the target of this heist and is able to come up with a foolproof response.

Two weeks after a raid

MBCC Chief's office

You stare at the report freshly submitted today containing only a familiar sentence.

*"The heist will go on as planned on XX/XX at the usual spot in Syndicate. First come, first served."*

Two days ago, the MBCC's informant in Syndicate got wind that the local street gangs were plotting a *heist*.

Intelligence Agent: The contact could only dig up so much information. Now, all we know is that this heist is related to Mania weapons.

Intelligence Agent: But after several raids, those old hands have become more careful with their communication. The exact location of *the usual spot* remains untraceable to us.

Chief: What about the source of the Mania weapons?

Intelligence Agent: Unknown. The circulation of Mania weapons in Syndicate mainly relies on the black market and underground banks, but we've observed no records of large-scale transactions on these two networks recently.

You frown. If you can't pinpoint the exact location of the conflict, there's still nothing you can do despite having foresight of the impending disaster.

It's three days before the date in the intel. You've tried everything but still come up empty-handed.

Knock, knock.

The knocks on the door interrupt your thoughts. It's Nightingale.

She walks in, places a stack of files on the desk, and reports the results of the investigation.

Nightingale: I have good news and bad news.

Nightingale: The bad news is that we've sifted through all of our informants in Syndicate but to no avail.

Nightingale: The good news, which is in a way worse news, is that Sinner Golan who you've been keeping an eye on has been demonstrating many suspicious behaviors possibly related to this matter.

Nightingale: Not only did she go AWOL during a field mission, but she also triggered numerous sensitive keywords while browsing the dark web, including Mania weapon, Syndicate, Iron Gang, Bridgehead, and gang brawl.

When you first got the tip-off, you had already interrogated several Sinners with past connections to Syndican gangs, but didn't get any useful answers.

Among this group of Sinners interrogated, Golan's questioning went the fastest, because her file was just too clean.

Unknown origin, unknown criminal record, and stable M-value. The only point relevant to this case is that *the whistleblower of the heist was frequently spotted near Golan's rented home*.

At first, you thought it was just a coincidence, but after Nightingale submitted the report on Golan's recent abnormal behaviors, you feel things might not be so simple.

Two pages into the report and you ask Nightingale to arrange an urgent meeting with Golan.

Under the dim light, Golan sits twisted, mumbling something to herself while struggling with the loosened ribbons on her clothes.

Golan: First, make a loop with the left end and hold it down! Then weave the right end under the left end and straighten it. F... I got it wrong again. Oh! You're here.

The smooth cursing takes a sharp turn and gets stifled. Seeing you walk in, she immediately swallows what she's about to say and assumes a demure, ladylike demeanor.

Golan: Why the sudden meeting call? And in such a scary place, no less.

Golan: Don't forget I'm a straight-A Sinner. I always follow the rules and stay away from the dodgy stuff.

It's that feigned obedient look again. If you hadn't read that ten-page-long record of her recent abnormal behaviors, you might have been fooled.

Chief: A straight-A Sinner wouldn't sneak to the computer in the middle of the night to search for news about Syndican gangs, would she?

Chief: Bridgehead, Deluge, Iron Gang, Nine Dragons Teahouse, Cripple Alley... I'm afraid you can't even find such an exhaustive list of gangs in the MBCC's archives.

Golan: B-but isn't this common knowledge to all Syndicans? Haha...

Golan: I, I only heard others mention them before and got curious. I swear I'm not up to anything...

Chief: My investigation shows that besides the Bridgehead and the Iron Gang, the rest of the gangs have limited influence and are little known both online and among the general public.

Chief: I think you already knew information on those small gangs was scarce when you searched them... Only those who have encountered them on their turf know about them.

You thought pointing out her unusual knowledge of Syndican gangs would make this conversation easier. After all, most people give up denying when faced with conclusive evidence.

But instead, Golan starts looking for more excuses after seeing the evidence. You know you must quickly find a hole in her defense to smoothen this interrogation.

Golan: Oh, it's my poor memory again! Now that I think about it, an online friend on the dark web probably told me about them.

Golan: An ordinary girl like me would never dare to wander into territories controlled by gang bosses, let alone encounter them. Just the thought of it terrifies me!

Chief: But somehow going AWOL during a field mission to chat with the old man who passed on gang gossip didn't terrify you.

Golan: Ahaha... I admit I can be a bit too carefree sometimes. I just love having a casual chat with kind old people.

Chief: And during that casual chat, did you hand him a note to warn him about something unusual and ask him to notify the old lady running the shop at 201 Cross Street?

Golan: Tell you what, Old Rick likes Miss Sylvia. I was simply playing matchmaker for the two seniors.

Chief: What about that photo of the two of you in the old lady's shop, then? She said you always brought the Iron Gang underlings over to buy ice cream and that everyone said you were the best boss lady in the world.

Golan: You... She...

Golan: ...Sigh, Sylvia's too careless. How could she tell the cops everything...

It seems confirmed that Golan is involved with Syndicate's Iron Gang.

You don't have time to delve into her relationship with the gang. From the current info, Golan at least knows about the Mania weapons and the recent brawls happening in Syndicate. You must get more relevant intel out of her.

Chief: So you're a member of the Iron Gang, and recently you've racked up so many violations all pointing to Syndicate, gangs, and Mania weapons.

Chief: Are you planning to seize Mania weapons? With the Legion gone, if the Iron Gang wants to take this opportunity to rise as the top gang in Syndicate, Mania weapons are necessary. Am I right?

Realizing you're already treating her like a de facto gang member, Golan also drops her disguise and looks at you a bit derisively.

Golan: Pfft, as if the Iron Gang cares about this kind of title.

Golan: Moreover, with the Iron Gang's strength, they don't need Mania weapons to win the turf war. Only those losers would resort to these despicable means on the battlefield.

Golan: The Iron Gang has always fought bravely and honorably.

Chief: It seems the Iron Gang really is something.

Golan: Hmph, you don't say!

Golan lifts her chin and snorts with evident pride.

Chief: But if the opposing gang uses Mania weapons in the fight, the Iron Gang will naturally need something even more powerful to counter *honorably*, right?

Golan: ...What do you mean?

Golan suddenly falls silent, but soon she laughs again, stroking her head.

Golan: Oh, but that's impossible!

Golan: There are no more mass destruction Mania weapons left in Syndicate. Not long ago, they already used up the ones stashed away.

Golan: The Iron Gang... They're just...

Golan seems to recall something unpleasant and stops mid-sentence.

Chief: Did something happen?

Golan: ...Anyway, don't worry about it. They wouldn't dare touch Mania weapons again.

Golan: Those damned things do almost as much harm to yourself as to the enemies and often lead to uncontrollable consequences.

Golan: Unless they're planning a suicide attack, the deterrent and cost resulting from wielding a Mania weapon are just not worth it. Better to just snatch a ready-made weapon.

Golan: Unless all those hits have finally wrecked their brains, those guys with their short-sighted plans will never mess with Mania weapons again.

**【Advance】**

- Ask about the ready-made weapon

**end**

Golan: Well, the good stuff that's more stable, more lethal, reusable, and aims better, of course.

Such an ideal mass destruction weapon doesn't even exist in the entire DisCity, let alone in Syndicate.

You start to wonder if Golan is pulling your leg, but she speaks so firmly as if she's really seen it... used it, even.

Chief: So, whose hands is it in now?

Golan: In the most unexpected place, of course!

Golan: Those ******* think they can kick the Iron Gang down a peg and sit on the throne if they snatch it.

Golan: ******* idiots! It's already flown away like a butterfly...

Golan eyes you with a grin, looking indifferent about this coveted weapon everyone's fighting over and even quite happy about its disappearance.

Even more strangely, according to her, the Iron Gang seems uninterested in the weapon either.

Chief: If it's really so great, then why doesn't the Iron Gang want to seize it too?

Golan: They... They're capable enough. The use of this kind of thing will only hold them back.

Golan: The Iron Gang doesn't need this flashy... stuff. Rather, its members despise it, whether it's Mania weapons, or...

Golan mumbles and stops on something unintelligible.

But her unconscious annoyance at the mention of this weapon reminds you of something else you learned before entering the interrogation room.

Chief: Is the Iron Gang's aversion toward it related to the death of their heiress?

Golan: What did you say?

Golan freezes momentarily upon hearing that. Her eyes widen in disbelief and her fingertips start to tremble uncontrollably.

Golan: They're saying the Iron Gang's heiress is dead?

She still looks dazed, but the sharp alarm of the M-value detector reveals her true feelings at the moment.

Golan's sudden outburst and her familiarity with the Syndican gangs, especially the Iron Gang, make you realize something...

**【Advance】**

- Is she someone important to you?

**end**

The shock still hasn't left her completely, but she nods slightly.

Her emotions are so thoroughly exposed that she can't hide them anymore.

Chief: I'm sorry you had to hear about your friend this way.

Golan: ...I'm fine. It's a good thing. This might be a relief for her.

Chief: But the MBCC's received a tip-off that there will be a riot to seize Mania weapons in Syndicate in two days. The word about this got out just a day after the Iron Gang announced the death.

Chief: My speculation is that the Iron Gang wanted to cover something up with the heiress's death, but this act angered the other gangs. The upcoming riot has also arisen because of that dead heiress.

Golan: The riot is in two days?

Golan: But she, she's... Whatever she might have to do with the ready-made weapon, she's already dead.

Chief: It's not hard to deduce from your words that the ready-made weapon is powerful. If the heiress had any connection to it, then surely the gangs wouldn't let her go, dead or alive.

Chief: They might not even believe she's dead. If the Iron Gang is indeed their target, then they're probably planning to turn it upside down.

Golan: Damn... These ******* bastards!

Chief: So, is there any more information you can give me? I want to stop the riot over the Mania weapons, and you want to protect the Iron Gang. I think our goals are aligned.

Golan: ...I've told you what you need to know. You've broken it down more completely than I ever can.

Golan: Thanks to you, I've finally got a clearer idea of their plan.

Having digested your analysis of the situation, Golan gradually turns serious.

She lowers her head, seemingly deliberating over something.

You catch the moment she makes a silent promise to herself.

**【Advance】**

- What are you planning?

**end**

Golan: Oh, but what can I do? I'm all locked up here now. My hands are tied.

Golan: I'm only thinking...

It seems you can't get more information from Golan, so you decide to end the conversation.

Now that you've confirmed the upcoming heist might be related to the Iron Gang, you should start planning your response.

One day later

MBCC Chief's office

A call from Nightingale.

Nightingale: Chief, at 5:10 p.m. today, Sinner Golan broke free from her restraints and went AWOL on a field mission. Currently, her whereabouts are unknown.

You sigh. You knew Golan had been hiding something when the interrogation ended yesterday.

You believe Golan wouldn't lie to you, but whether it's for Mania weapons or that ready-made weapon she mentioned, the imminent heist will surely spark a gang riot.

But at least, you now know where to find Golan and uncover the truth.

## Golan - Interrogation II

> Golan refuses the MBCC's help, insisting she should settle this of her own accord. She must face the consequences of her own action.

XX/XX

The day of the heist

Somewhere in Syndicate

Nightingale: Chief, this is Cross Street downtown Syndicate, where Golan was last seen according to the investigation team's arrest report. Cross Street is a lawless zone, teeming with members of big and small gangs coming here to exchange information. A comprehensive search will be extremely difficult here.

With a gang riot imminent, there are only two possible reasons Golan would risk returning to Syndicate...

Either the Mania weapons truly exist and Golan has come back to help the Iron Gang win during the heist, or she's here to deal with that *weapon* for the Iron Gang.

Her disappearance here suggests that a safe passage to the Iron Gang is nearby. Finding her is the only clue to pinpointing the battlefield.

Nightingale: Meanwhile, the Public Security Bureau has just informed you that a gang brawl has erupted around Margaret Street. It's only one intersection away from your current location, but it's still uncertain whether it's related to the Mania weapons.

Nightingale: Given the precarious situation in Syndicate, please prioritize your safety and retreat ASAP once you find any clues about Sinner Golan.

The street is growing noisier when a large flag sweeps before your eyes. You instinctively duck behind the street, only then registering Nightingale's last instruction.

Chief: Okay...

Before you finish, you feel a familiar pull in the palm of your hand. It's Golan.

You take a step back, and the sensation of the shackles grows stronger.

Chief: Found you.

Ending the call, you bypass the main street, follow the fluctuating signs of the shackles, and run through the meandering alleys toward the summons.

Finally, the sensation of the shackles peaks in an abandoned alley reeking of fish. You've just stepped into the alleyway when you hear Golan's trembling voice.

Golan seems to be arguing with the woman in front of her. You tread lightly, trying to listen in on their conversation.

Brenna: Cub let you in, didn't he? I hope he'll enjoy collecting debts on the outskirts for the next three months. We're about to go to war and he dared let in a deserter who betrayed us in black and white. How can we trust someone who treats his job like a joke?

Golan: Miss Brenna... No, Mom! Listen to me! It's not Cub's fault. I forced him to tell me where you were.

Brenna: Blinded by sentiment even during a crisis. Sounds like someone you trained, indeed. Do you know all the gangs have their eyes on the Iron Gang now?

Brenna: It's my responsibility to the Iron Gang to forbid any cowardly deserter to sneak back in during this critical moment.

Golan: I was wrong... Mom, knowing you, I know you'll never forgive me. Punish me however you want, but can we at least wait till I finish fighting this battle...

Golan: Those riffraff have banded together because they think they can finally trample over us this time. I can't just stand by and watch their arrogance without doing anything!

Golan: I've grown a lot these past few months. I've learned how to use my abilities proficiently at the Bureau! I'm sure this time I can help everyone out, I swear!

You've never seen Golan act like this before. Both her bold behavior at the Bureau and the outrageous deeds documented in her file suggest she's not the demure lady she usually pretends to be.

But even under her obedient disguise at the Bureau, she rarely puts herself in the role of a subordinate.

Now, despite Golan's high volume, she's not the one in control of this conversation.

Brenna: No one cares if you stay or leave. The Iron Gang's victory never depends on one single person.

Golan: Please, Mom, can you just stop being mad at me for a second... You really can't do it without me this time. They're going all in with their firepower for this battle!

Brenna: Mad at you? Can't do it without you? Golan, all this time, you've never learned to read the situation.

The woman scoffs, her laugh not reaching her eyes.

Brenna: Sometimes I really wonder where your education went wrong. How did I raise such a naive fool like you?

Brenna: The lookouts from all the other gangs out there are watching you. They didn't believe your desertion before, and they won't believe you're dead now.

Brenna: Their plan is to have all their men launch a massive attack on us the second they get wind that you get in touch with the Iron Gang.

Brenna: Everyone in the Iron Gang has been denying any connection with you and planning to grit their teeth and weather the attack today. When the enemies arrive and find out you're indeed gone, that will be the end of it.

Brenna: And here you've swaggered back without even the slightest awareness of your circumstances... How am I supposed to explain to everyone that you want to fight alongside them now?

You faintly feel the woman glance at you as she talks, but it's so subtle you're not sure if she's really spotted you.

Golan: I don't want to wait for them to change their mind after they attack us. I don't want them to realize *Golan is indeed gone* amid the dead bodies of our people.

Golan: And if they find out we don't have any Sinners or Mania weapons anymore, then we'll turn into helpless prey! They won't let the Iron Gang live.

Brenna: Whether we live or die, that's the Iron Gang's fate. It has nothing to do with you.

Golan: You can't sacrifice so much for me... It's not fair to you.

Golan: I... I'll explain to everyone myself. I'm confident as long as I can lead everyone through this battle, they'll remain loyal.

Brenna: Did you see Sunny and Joy on your way here? They must be with Cub. Did you see their wounds?

Brenna: The Deluge guys cornered them in a stinking alley and beat them up to get your whereabouts. Sunny didn't talk even though they got her on her knees. Joy fought back, and they shoved his head to the ground and fed him dirt.

Brenna: They are loyal. If you show up, those idiots will definitely follow your orders. One word from you and they're ready to fight.

Brenna: But what about you? Can you still stand their loyalty?

Despite Brenna's sharp words, you can tell every member of the Iron Gang is willing to sacrifice themselves for Golan's safety. This much is consensus among them. But Golan can't accept this arrangement.

Golan looks like a child who has done something wrong, unable to defend herself. Even when she tries to emphasize how she can be of help, the confidence in her voice shrinks.

She still insists on staying, yet she doesn't know how else to convince the woman in front of her.

Golan: Once this is over, I'll accept any punishment for the responsibilities I'd shirked before according to our rules, and I'll...

Brenna: I don't have time for all this nonsense.

The woman interrupts Golan before she can finish.

Brenna: Your whereabouts have been exposed for quite some time now. I don't know how many people have discovered where you are, but everyone must be poised for action now.

Brenna: Go. The Iron Gang has nothing to do with you anymore.

She glances in your direction, and this time you can clearly feel her gaze landing on you.

Brenna: Even if you're not being tracked, that friend of yours probably is. They won't let any unfamiliar face go at this time.

Brenna: Let alone a cop this conspicuous.

It's only then that Golan notices your presence. She turns to look at you, a mix of despair and annoyance flooding her face.

She stares at her mother's back turned resolutely to her, all the questions she was suppressing and too afraid to ask now washing over her.

Golan: I don't understand...

Golan: If you wanted me to become a worthy and responsible heiress to the gang, why didn't you tell me the truth about our family's *business* from the start...

Brenna turns her back. You can't see the expression on her face. She simply sighs almost inaudibly.

Brenna: I had no choice.

Brenna: I've spent my whole life in the Iron Gang. I'm used to fighting and killing. You're different. I wanted you to have a choice.

Brenna: Since you chose them when the choices were presented to you, you have nothing to do with Syndicate or the gangs anymore.

Brenna: Get lost, you shouldn't have come here. Don't give us extra trouble for getting that cop hurt when the fight starts.

With that last sentence, Brenna ignores Golan and leaves.

Watching her mother's dwindling figure, Golan stands there somewhat helplessly.

Golan's mother was right. Someone might have discovered Golan's whereabouts. You hear a restless buzz in the distance. Shouts and gunshots.

The noise of the brawl grows near. Communication with Nightingale finally becomes smooth.

Nightingale: Chief, how's it going? The gang fight has started to spread in your direction. For your safety, please move to the suggested location and meet up with us.

Chief: Alright, I'll be there soon.

You want to take Golan and leave this place quickly, but she stands rooted to the spot, still looking in the direction her mother has gone in.

Chief: You can process all that later. We need to leave at once. We're not far from your base, right? If someone finds us, it'll only put your mother and friends in an even worse position.

Golan: ...Let's go. I'll cover you. Stay behind me and be careful.

Golan goes ahead with her head down. But luck's not on your side. Just as you reach the end of the alley, you run into two patrolling thugs.

Thug A: Holy ****! Isn't this our Miss Golan? You really are alive!!!

Thug A: People have been going crazy looking for you out there, and here you are, hiding in plain sight?!

Thug B: I just knew she couldn't be dead! I mean, what a loss will that be? We can take her back alive to the boss! We hit the jackpot, didn't we? Haha!

Golan: You better piss off now and keep your mouth shut out there, or I'll rip your jaw off.

Facing the menacing thugs, Golan quickly steps in front of you. She doesn't initiate an attack but tries to shake them off and keep moving.

A stained baseball bat pokes her on the shoulder.

Thug A: Tsk tsk, quit acting all high and mighty now. Do you think you're still in the ******* Iron Gang after you faked your death and ran away? You should see how miserable you are...

Thug B: Instead of being a street rat, why don't you surrender and join us? How about it?

Golan: **** off! You're lucky I've never beaten you up before, but don't push it. Join you? And become lowlifes like you?

Thug B: **** you!

Enraged, the thug picks up the baseball bat and swings it at you. Golan pushes you away and meets the attack with her parasol.

The thug who got hit picks up a brick from the ground indignantly and tries to throw it. You see it and rush over to shoulder the assailant off.

Golan immediately turns around to elbow the attacker.

Beaten up defenselessly, the thugs fall unconscious. But it seems that Golan hasn't recovered from the rush of emotions just yet.

Now you finally understand. Golan is the *dead* heiress to the Iron Gang. No one else should know about her appearance in Syndicate. So you tie up the unconscious thugs to prevent them from escaping.

Golan: Even these thugs almost got the better of me. My mom was right. I'm too weak.

Chief: You don't get stronger overnight. Give yourself more time and patience.

Golan: No need to comfort me. It's a good thing... to get a proper understanding of myself now.

Chief: Why did they say taking you back would earn them a reward? And that people are looking for you? Does it have anything to do with that ready-made weapon you mentioned before? Did you hide it?

The mysterious weapon, the subtle animosity between the Iron Gang and the other gangs, the heist... These things all seem to be connected by a thin thread, but the piece joining them together is still missing.

But if Golan was related to that weapon, the Iron Gang wouldn't have let her go so easily.

Perhaps because you've already seen her at a vulnerable moment, or perhaps to repay your help just now, she finally speaks after a long silence.

Golan: I've never *touched* any ready-made weapon. I didn't even know what a Mania weapon was six months ago.

Golan: You might not believe it, but I've never been involved in any dirty work, stuff like dealing Mania weapons, shootouts, smuggling... because my mom wouldn't allow it.

Golan: Since I was little, she's always kept me away from the gangs. She taught me combat skills for self-defense and only sent me to collect debts and put on a show with a bunch of underlings. She never let me near the real dirty work that got one's hands dirty.

Golan: Even if she accidentally slipped up in front of me, she refused to let me help. She even specifically created the harshest punishment within our gang regarding this.

Thud... Thud... Thud...

Cub: Boss lady... Boss... lady... Go get help, quickly... Bring more people... to save the boss.

Golan: Cub? What happened? My mom... You guys... Weren't you guys supposed to be picking up the shipment?

Cub: Hurry... Or it'll be too late...

Golan: It was only then I realized my mom took out the most powerful firearm from the safe to prepare for the biggest gang fight in years.

Golan: It was also the first time I realized that for an *infamous* Syndican gang to survive, you must take what belonged to others, and in the most violent way. You must become the biggest winner on the battlefield. Otherwise, those who failed to take anything would take advantage of the situation and prey on you.

Golan: By the time I arrived with reinforcements, our people were all injured or maimed, struggling to swat off thugs from all sorts of small gangs.

Golan: Mom! What happened to you? What happened here?

Brenna: Golan? What are you doing here? It's too dangerous. Go back!

Golan: At that time, all I saw was the wreckage of the battlefield. My once formidable mother was covered in wounds, still fighting while protecting a dying subordinate. Everyone else had gone down, leaving her alone to still protect those behind her desperately.

Golan: The battlefield was covered in unconscious bodies and blood. At that time, I had no idea what I was supposed to do, dazed as if I was sleepwalking.

Golan: But when I saw a thug pull out a Mania weapon and point it at my mom, my brain and body went completely out of control. An inexplicable rage exploded in me. I had never felt so angry in my life. I rushed over to protect her.

Enemy Thug: Caught off guard, weren't you? Hahaha, today is the day I'll take the life of this monster of a woman!

Golan: Mom! Watch out!

Golan: Maybe because I was too eager to protect my family, or because I was screaming *no* inside my head. For whatever reason, in that moment, it felt like some kind of power surged within me.

Chief: You mean, after you took that hit from the Mania weapon for your mother on the battlefield, you awakened?

Golan: Exactly. During that most severe gang fight, witnessed by everyone, I awakened as a Sinner.

It suddenly clicked why those thugs looked as if they had struck gold when they found Golan. And you finally grasped the true meaning of the so-called heist.

The mass destruction Mania weapon they're fighting for is Golan.

That *better* weapon Golan was hinting at was in fact herself.

In Syndicate, even a stray dog knows a Sinner is worth ten Mania weapons. If trained properly, a Sinner makes the best Mania weapon.

This explains why all the gangs are striving to come up with all sorts of plans and why they've become exceptionally active after discovering Golan's whereabouts.

Chief: But isn't it more dangerous to wander alone in Syndicate? Why not stay with the Iron Gang? At least you wouldn't have to face any threats alone.

Golan: Chief, this is not how you survive in Syndicate.

Golan: A living Sinner from a gang is much rarer than any resource or weapon. That's why from that day on, I became a moving target in the gang brawls who everyone wanted to test their strength with. The Iron Gang has never seen a peaceful day since.

Cub: Boss lady, this way! Please take a break here. I'll go and chase those guys away!

Golan: ...Don't go, Cub. We're better off hiding here. They'll give up sooner or later.

Cub: No way! They all think they're good enough to try us now. If we don't teach them a lesson, they won't stop pestering you!

Cub: Don't worry. You stay here. I'll handle it quickly.

Golan: ...

Golan: At that time, every day was a struggle.

Golan: On one hand, I thought about how to use this new power to eliminate all of the Iron Gang's enemies. On the other hand, I was afraid. I feared that all the people I cared about would falter on the battlefield, that to protect one meant to sacrifice another...

Golan: I tried to become a proper heiress, but I failed. I neither had the resolve to kill nor was smart enough to solve problems otherwise.

Golan: In the end, all I brought to the family was trouble, and I made the Iron Gang an easy target for other gangs.

Golan: Gradually, I began to want to escape... but every time I thought about running away, I felt extremely guilty. I even began to think if I hadn't taken that hit for Mom, I wouldn't have been in so much pain.

Golan: But when I snapped out of it, I felt like the most unforgivable scumbag in the world for having that thought.

Golan: Gaining such a formidable power should be a good thing. But why did it make me so sad...

Chief: That's why you moved into a rented home and tried to live like an ordinary person. That was the best solution you could think of to free you all.

Golan: Yes... I thought this would protect them, but I was too naive. I didn't know that those people wouldn't believe I had fled and would even use it as an excuse to provoke the Iron Gang.

Golan stops in her tracks, looking back. The small courtyard where she talked with Brenna is no longer visible, but she's still staring intently.

Golan: But I dare not go home, because I haven't gotten any stronger. In fact, no one expects anything of me. As long as I return, everyone will still do their best to protect me, just like what you heard a moment ago.

Golan: But... but every time I see the suppressed exhaustion in my mom, and the increasing injuries on Cub, Joy, and Sunny, I just can't face it...

Golan: Chief, am I in so much pain because I'm too weak?

Having spilled the deepest secret in her heart, Golan doesn't feel relieved. Instead, she feels overwhelmingly powerless.

This is the destination she wishes to reach, the home she wants to guard but finds no way to.

She may not have intended to confide in you, but the confession itself no doubt requires a great deal of trust. You want to do something in return for this precious trust.

Chief: If you no longer want to face this, the MBCC can take care of it. Solving the Mania weapon riot and retrieving Sinner Golan are two separate tasks. I can have someone take you back to the Bureau first, and then the MBCC will handle this gang fight.

Chief: If you want, this doesn't have to be your responsibility. Let it become the MBCC's job.

Your earnestness pays off. Golan's expression finally lightens a little.

Golan: Thank you, Chief. But no need.

Golan: I started this conflict, so I should also be the one to end it. I don't want so many people to die for me. I must repay my debt. I must help them.

Golan: Otherwise, even if I return to the MBCC, I will never find closure.

At last, guilt and indebtedness push her toward her lonely fight. This deepens your worry for her.
