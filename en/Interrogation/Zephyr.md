# Zephyr Interrogation

## Zephyr - Interrogation I

> The Sinner you encountered at the Eastside bar is Zephyr, a former pilot who was forced to retire after causing signal interference during a test flight.
> On that rainy night, you and the FAC thwart her hijacking attempt, leading to her forced detention by the MBCC. As you part ways, Zephyr's reaction makes it clear she hasn't accepted this outcome.

Eastside Central Avenue

Bar D

The clinking of glasses and the roar of the crowd drown out the bar's lyrical background music. A commotion stirs the establishment, leading to a collective eruption of cheers as patrons raise their glasses in unison.

Your consciousness slowly returns, but the alcoholic haze still clouds your mind. Through bleary eyes, you can barely make out the dim yet colorful lights and the swaying silhouettes of people around you.

All eyes in the bar are fixated on an orange-haired girl perched nonchalantly atop the counter.

???: Cheers to the world! Hey Jerry, did you see that? That's the prowess of DisCity's paper plane champion right there!

Jerry: No kidding, just another lucky shot, wasn't it? Ever since you started boasting about knowing magic and flying planes, I ain't take anything you say seriously after your third shot.

???: C'mon, you're the one I trust most here. How could you of all people don't trust me? No, no, this won't cut it—let me give it another shot. If I nail this one, you're gonna treat me to another drink, deal?

Jerry: Sure, why not? If you can pull it off, this one's on the house.

???: Just watch this. I'm gonna...

Her voice trails off as she becomes absorbed in whatever she's crafting. The bar's raucous atmosphere gradually dies down to hushed whispers and murmurs.

???: ...Hello, hello? Can everyone hear me...? Huh, looks like it went through.

Just as you are on the verge of slipping back into drowsiness, the fleeting peace is abruptly interrupted as the music cuts off, replaced by crackling static emanating from the speakers.

???: Jerry said if I can land this plane in... in the vase on that corner table, he'll buy everyone a drink.

The girl's vibrant voice slices through the serene background music. Springing to her feet, she theatrically blows on her paper plane and throws it into the air. The plane gracefully glides across the room, landing in the vase right before you. With it, the crowd bursts into a chorus of whistles and cheers, their jubilation magnified by the sound system.

Amidst the commotion, you discern something peculiar about her voice—it isn't merely streaming through the bar's stereo system. Instead, it appears to envelop you, resonating from much nearer sources, such as the terminal on your table.

Your shackles detect a faint Mania fluctuation. A suspicion forms in your mind, and as a precaution, you send your location to the Bureau through your terminal.

Orange-Haired Girl: Why sitting here alone staring at your terminal? Busy with something, or... waiting for someone?

A refreshing citrus scent wafts over you as her voice comes from behind, her orange hair falling beside you. You hastily flip your terminal face-down on the table, hoping she hasn't noticed your message. She seems oblivious to your quick movement, focusing instead on retrieving her paper plane from the vase.

You didn't expect her to approach you directly. Masking your tension, you try to appear like any other patron while tracking the time, hoping to keep her engaged until backup arrives.

Chief: Just a friend's message. She said this place is a great spot to unwind during the break, and now she's checking in to see how I'm liking it.

Orange-Haired Girl: So, what did you say? Care to satisfy my curiosity?

Chief: I told her this place is pretty cool—that you might even run into a paper plane contest winner here.

Orange-Haired Girl: Really? Looks like I made an impression! Well, if you want to learn the secret of my paper plane folding... buy me a *Free Bird* first. Maybe I'll think about it after I finish the drink.

Chief: How about this: if I get you two drinks, maybe you could also consider briefing me your name?

She breaks into a hearty laugh at your unsubtle attempt at small talk, turning to face you and resting her chin on her hand as she sizes you up.

After a moment, she gestures for you to hold out your palm.

- Hmm...

**end**

Her fingertip dances across your palm, sketching letters as your eyes track its movements, struggling to discern the meaning behind her gestures. However, the ticklish sensation impedes your focus, making it challenging to concentrate.

Noticing your fluster, she gives an amused smile and slows down, tracing each letter again while quietly pronouncing them.

You awkwardly follow her rhythm, slowly sounding out each syllable of her name—

Chief: ...Ze-phy-r.

Zephyr: That's right! Zephyr. That's my name.

As you say her name, Zephyr looks at you with evident delight.

Zephyr: This is your first time here, right?

Chief: Yes. You can tell just by looking?

Zephyr: Of course. You're a new face around here, and...

With cheerful enthusiasm, she leans right in and flicks your unfinished drink with her fingernail, making the glass ring with a clear chime.

Zephyr: You can tell a lot about someone from their drink! This bland choice is every newcomer's go-to, but I don't think it fits your vibe at all.

Your terminal vibrates. As you put it away after responding to the message, you notice Zephyr watching you with an unwavering stare.

Her pensive gaze causes you to falter momentarily. In a gesture of surrender, you spread your hands wide, releasing a weary sigh.

Chief: Work never really lets up, you know, even on my days off.

Zephyr: Yeah totally get it! Honestly, you look exactly like the type who'd bring a laptop to a bar for a meeting. Ugh, I'm getting exhausted just thinking about your life!

With practiced nonchalance, she averts her gaze, diverting her attention to the overcast sky beyond, all the while absentmindedly flicking the switch on her pager up and down.

At this close distance, you notice her pager is a model commonly used in transportation communications. Eyeing up the emblem on her jacket sleeve, you carefully choose your next words.

Chief: What about you? Being a pilot must be pretty swamped too, right?

Zephyr: Hmm... There was all that hassle with memorizing manuals and taking tests, but that's ancient history now.

Zephyr: But once you're soaring up in the sky, all the rest just fades away! Your worries stay down below, and when you're enveloped in that blue-ness, your think about nothing but pure joy.

She doesn't deny your guess about her profession. The moment your conversation turns to flying, her eyes light up with unmistakable enthusiasm.

The rain outside shows no sign of letting up. You decide to follow her train of thought.

Chief: Yeah I remember flying one or two times before, for work. It was indeed quite a view when you see that endless expanse of sky stretching beyond the horizon.

Chief: Though I imagine the joy of being a passenger is quite different from what a pilot feels, right?

Zephyr: For passengers... I suppose getting to your destination matters more than the flying itself, right? You're all about reaching your endpoint as fast as you can.

Zephyr: But for pilots—no, for me... I just want to stay up in the sky all the time... just a little longer... and longer...

Her cheeks, tinted by the alcohol, lend her words a raw, heartfelt flow. She lightly taps her knuckles against the window, your reflections mirrored in the darkened glass.

Zephyr: Sure, it's dark and rainy tonight, can't see a thing out there. But if you just pull back on the yoke and keep ascending, above those dense clouds - and further up - that's where you hit the bright sunshine.

Zephyr: The sky up there is clear as crystal, you know, just like this glass right here. When I'm up in the air, sometimes I almost feel like I could see my own reflection.

Following her gaze upward, you see the night sky shrouded in a deep, gray curtain of rain.

Chief: Must be pretty challenging to fly in weather like this, right?

Zephyr: Yep, that's the theory. You've got lousy visibility, slippery runways, cyclones, thunderstorms, and all that jazz...

Zephyr: But come on, who do you think I am? Like a bit of mortal danger would ever stop me from taking to the skies!

The intense rain and the late hour pose a dual challenge: not only do they render flying conditions hazardous, but they will also impede FAC's prompt response time.

You've already sent your location and Zephyr's description to Nightingale, asking her to search the archives and provide support. With FAC's arrival still some time away, you need to keep Zephyr engaged and under control.

You decide to keep things light with a playful tone.

Chief: Could you tell me more, like... what it feels like? I wish I could experience it myself, but it's not like anyone can just hop on a plane whenever they want, isn't it?

Zephyr: Says who? Look, if you really want to try it, how about we have a try right now?

Chief: Hmm...

A sudden hush falls over the conversation, leaving only the bar's background music to fill the silence.

Chief: Right now? You have a PLANE?

Zephyr: Not yet, but I can get one real quick.

Zephyr takes your lack of outright refusal as a yes. Her eyes sparkle with excitement. Rising to her feet, she reaches out her hand towards you, her expression brimming with anticipation.

It's as if a single nod from you would be all she needs to sweep away every obstacle and whisk you off to wherever her heart desires.

Chief: But with all this rain...

Zephyr: So what? We'll make a run for it! Come on, let's roll—!

Before you can finish, she grabs your sleeve and yanks you to your feet, making you stumble forward.

The bar door creaks as she shoves it open and lets it slam shut behind you. The icy rain lashes against your face, compelling your eyes to close, yet in the darkness, you can still sense the warmth of her hand firmly gripping yours.

Through the suffocating curtain of midnight rain, Zephyr pulls you along in a wild dash.

Chief: Can we actually fly in this rain?

Zephyr: Hey, I'm a licensed pilot! Trust me!

Zephyr: It's exactly this kind of sky that hides something extraordinary.

The rain immediately soaks through your clothes, the damp fabric clinging heavily to your skin. You trail closely behind Zephyr as she weaves through the empty streets of Eastside in the dead of night.

You consider pulling your hand away, but dismiss the idea—it's not yet time to startle this possible Sinner. Instead, you quietly activate your terminal's tracking, following her along until you uncover the destination she's heading towards.

Zephyr: You'll be my first passenger since I started flying! We can catch the sunrise together from up there—I'm getting all hyped just thinking about it!

She dashes ahead, spinning gleefully around lamp posts one moment, deliberately splashing through puddles the next. Her laughter rings out freely in the downpour, each moment lived with such wild abandon as if it were her last.

You squeeze sideways through narrow gaps between buildings, wade through knee-deep muddy water under bridges, and climb drainage pipes onto unfamiliar rooftops. Then, amidst blaring alarms slicing through the air, you make a daring leap from one rooftop to a balcony several meters away.

With no destination in sight, you begin to suspect that Zephyr's talk of flying might just be an excuse for this endless chase born from a whim. As you follow her, it feels like chasing after a kite set loose from its tether.

However, whenever you begin to lag behind, this kind kite halts—shaking raindrops from her hair with a hearty laugh—ready to catch you as you land.

After setting off countless alarms throughout the night, you finally come to a stop in front of a lit manor.

With mud smeared across her cheeks, Zephyr stares intently at the manor's back door, lost in thought.

Zephyr: Huh, an electronic lock—perfect! I know just how to crack this one.

Instead of tampering with the electronic lock directly, she takes a step back and raises the pager in her hand. The moment she presses the button, your shackles detect the same pulse of Mania that once emanated from your terminal back at the bar.

Chief: Zephyr.

Zephyr: Yes! ...Hold your horses, will ya—we'll be up in the air in a jiffy. Just bear with me a bit longer.

No sooner do the words escape her lips than the tampered electronic lock begins to emit a shrill alarm.

Chief: Zephyr, this is breaking and entering.

Zephyr: Come on, don't be a party pooper! I've done my homework—Mr. Woods hardly swings by this place a couple of times a year. Leaving stuff here untouched is just a waste.

She strides in with practiced familiarity, then rounds a corner and spins around with dramatic flair, proudly revealing the treasure behind her.

There sits a beautifully painted light aircraft.

Zephyr: This aircraft rocks the best specs available right now, top performance in its latest production run too.

Zephyr: Uh, such a shame, leaving it here gathering dust. I might as well take it for a spin and iron out any wrinkles while I'm at it.

You thought running through the rain to fly a plane was outlandish enough, but now, here she stands, nonchalantly explaining her impromptu aircraft theft as if it's the most natural thing in the world. You're torn between feeling absurd and simply acknowledging that this behavior is quintessentially her.

In the blink of an eye, Zephyr uses her power once again to break through the cockpit lock.

Now that you've confirmed both her identity as a Sinner and her intentions, there is no reason to let this farce continue. Your terminal vibrates in your coat pocket right on cue—the perfect moment to act has presented itself.

Just before Zephyr's hand reaches the control yoke, you grab her wrist. The crimson shackles pulse violently in your palm, piercing through her body.

Chief: Sinner Zephyr.

Your voice carries gravity as you gaze at her quivering pupils.

Chief: As Chief of MBCC, I'm issuing you an official warning. Cease your actions immediately.

Zephyr: ...What?

Abruptly, glaring spotlights illuminate from all angles, converging on the aircraft's cockpit.

Zephyr raises a hand to shield her eyes from the blinding light. Through the spotlights, Nightingale strides ahead of the arriving FAC reinforcements and quickly joins you at your side.

Nightingale: Sorry, Chief. The weather and signal interference delayed our arrival.

Nightingale: Former pilot Zephyr, discharged for signal interference with flight routes. Given your status as a Sinner and your multiple offenses, including unlicensed operation and property theft, MBCC will enact mandatory detention upon you. Your cooperation is demanded.

On the tarmac, FAC officers take Zephyr into custody. She offers no resistance, only fixing her gaze directly on you, the light in her eyes fading like the night rain.

Zephyr: If I'd been just a little faster...

She shows no surprise at your unexpected intervention, nor does she oppose the arrest. Her expression only hints at regret for letting the opportunity slip through her fingers.

The rain that has lasted all night finally ceases. On your way back to MBCC, you ponder Zephyr's last words, while the image of her face—etched with both regret and yearning as she was led away—remains vivid in your mind.

## Zephyr - Interrogation II

> You've confirmed that Zephyr's hijacking attempt wasn't premeditated. Her actions were primarily driven by her past experiences and thrill-seeking nature. Nevertheless, the subsequent flood of escape attempt reports makes you reconsider your judgment about her.
> A genuine understanding is yet to be established between you and Zephyr.

█ █, N.F. ██

One month after Zephyr's detention

Nightingale: Chief, I've completed archiving Sinner Zephyr's personal files and behavioral records. Please review them at your convenience.

- Take the files

You retrieve the files from Nightingale—noticeably thicker than when Zephyr was initially apprehended. The updated disciplinary reports are numerous, encompassing unauthorized absences, vandalism of public facilities, and various other infractions.

Nightingale: Zephyr has demonstrated strong resistance to Bureau regulations, persistently exhibiting defiant behavior. The higher-ups are particularly concerned about her recent attempted aircraft theft in Eastside and have requested a detailed investigation report from us regarding the incident.

Nightingale: Based on our assessment, the findings of this interrogation report are expected to influence the level of oversight required for Zephyr going forward. As for her... I'm afraid this interrogation won't be easy, Chief.

The records alone portray a clear image: someone as free-spirited and resistant to rules as her won't yield without a struggle.

Inside the interrogation room, Zephyr lounges casually on the chair's armrest, toying with a paper plane she crafted while waiting for your arrival. Yet the marks on her restraints tell a different story—clear signs of violent resistance.

Seeing your entrance, she retains her nonchalant demeanor. Though she straightens up slightly to face you, her posture still exudes informality.

Zephyr: Chief, how much longer are you going to keep me here...

Zephyr: We just got a bit carried away that night and crossed some lines. Now that we're sober, can't we just pretend none of it happened?

Chief: Using special powers for signal interference, breaking into private property, attempting an aircraft theft, and operating without a license or registration... The Bureau can't simply "pretend none of it happened."

Zephyr: But none of it really put anyone else in harm's way, did it? Flying in the rain was a gamble I chose to make, and even if I had crashed, I'm confident in my abilities to steer clear of causing harm to others.

Chief: If this was indeed a spur-of-the-moment decision, we could consider categorizing it as an attempted theft.

Chief: However, your actions were decisive and purposeful, with extensive knowledge of the aircraft's location and specs. All signs indicate premeditation on your part.

Chief: If you still don't grasp the situation, let me break it down: Woods is a top private aircraft supplier in Eastside, and his aviation operations carry considerable weight within DisCity. Just this fact alone is enough for authorities to tag you as a threat to DisCity's air safety.

Zephyr: In this verdict, except for the fact that my actions were "decisive and purposeful," isn't everything else just speculation?

Zephyr: You were there that day. You know it yourself—it really was just something that happened on a whim.

Zephyr: Now you're accusing me of "premeditation" after the fact? That's pretty sneaky.

Chief: Given what you did, proving the *existence* of premeditation is far more logical than proving its *absence*, isn't it?

Chief: And your past record does little to alleviate your suspicion—"Escape from orphanage at age 8," "Instances of disrupting public order in Eastside," "Allegedly organizing events that incited civil unrest"...

Chief: Your history will serve as perfect ammunition for others to scrutinize your every move. I doubt they'll be able—or even willing—to accept that you attempted this theft simply because it's a "spur-of-the-moment."

Zephyr: People always document my history based on their own thoughts of me. They didn't even bother to ask me before writing it. How can you be so sure that everything on it is accurate?

She presses her lips dismissively, showing utter indifference to the record of infractions laid out before her.

Zephyr: Why can't the story be like this? An eight-year-old girl broke free from her cage, encountered a series of adventures and hurdles, and voila! —Grew up into a brilliant, resilient adult.

Chief: That said, in your version, the story should continue like this: She suddenly decided to fly a plane on a rainy day and embarked on multiple daring adventures to reach her destination, only to fail in the final moments before dawn?

Zephyr: Mm-hmm, not bad! That's exactly the kind of story I like.

Her nonchalant, flippant tone show no sign of seriousness and your talk won't yield any useful results. Without confirming her motive for the theft, she will only face stricter surveillance measures.

You need to find an emotional trigger that will make her take the current situation seriously.

Chief: So, this free-spirited protagonist suddenly decided one day to turn over a new leaf, choosing to undergo the training and tests she despised most, all to become a *pilot*...

Chief: And then, coincidentally during her very first test flight, she caused the biggest route jamming ever in history. Miss Protagonist, isn't this all a bit too convenient even for a novel?

Zephyr: ...So this is how you talk into people?

Her smile freezes for a moment as her hands slowly clench into fists beneath her jacket sleeves.

Zephyr: Look, even if someone's done some wild things in the past—and sure, maybe not all of it was great—you can't just weave every little dot into some grand conspiracy.

Chief: I'm simply stating the most logical and obvious speculation based on your records and current words.

Chief: If I were to put that story you just told as your motive in the investigation report, people who read it would jump to far worse assumptions about you than I have.

Zephyr: ...Such dirty minds you cops have! Why do you always have to assume the worst just because of some dumb record from the past, huh?

Zephyr: Did that damn file even tell you that I was one of the *victims*, too?!

Her sudden raised voice shatters the stillness of the interrogation room, leaving a resonant echo in its wake. Under your relentless questioning, Zephyr's chest heaves as a mix of anger and hurt finally bursts through her facade.

Zephyr: On my very first flight ever, I was suddenly hit by that—that Mania or whatever it's called. Before I could even grasp what was going on, the control panel went haywire, and there I was, stuck in the cockpit, alone and helpless.

Zephyr: Sure, I love a good thrill, but I don't have a death wish, for crying out loud!

It was her very first flight. While the sky soared ever higher in the sunset's afterglow, she was plummeting towards earth.

In the cockpit, instrument needles spun wildly out of control. All contact with her fellow test pilots was lost. Warning lights flashed erratically in crimson, like a silent countdown to the demise of her own life.

Zephyr: Mayday, mayday... hah... (cough) Crashing on my first flight... bet there aren't many pilots who can claim that record.

The air pressure changed violently as her vision blurred in free fall... yet her senses were still pierced by the intense light from above. As she forced her eyes open to face the sun, she found herself with her back to DisCity, embracing the entire sky under the sun's blazing gaze.

To a pilot's ears, a crash always means absolute silence. *Beep*. In that perfect silence, the sound of reconnected signals pierced through.

!

She opened her eyes again amidst the pungent smell of disinfectant. The hospital room ceiling came into focus, along with her left leg suspended in traction.

Staff Member: The test results are back. The doctor says that your leg injury will have some serious long-term effects, which means you are no longer suitable as a pilot. The incident has settled, and given your injuries, they've decided to arrange your discharge from service.

Staff Member: I'm sorry. No one wanted this to happen. But, Zephyr... someone has to be responsible for this.

She stared at her leg, now held together with steel plates. It had only been her first flight.

Zephyr: I said, and I swear, if I hadn't struggled to restore contact with the control tower, everyone else up there would've been done for!

Zephyr: I wish someone had captured my heroic sacrifice for everyone's safety on camera. Even the Association never questioned me back then, so how did I end up being seen as some sort of dirty criminal?

The accident report indeed makes no mention of casualties from that incident. You thought it was just incomplete documentation, but now it seems more like a deliberate omission to shift responsibility.

Chief: Didn't you ever mention that you restored the signal connection? If that was verified, why would they still discharge you?

Zephyr: They told me they admired my bravery, saying I was let go all because my leg injury made me fail the physical... but the truth is, after my power kicked in, I didn't even feel the injury anymore...

The official record explicitly states that Zephyr was discharged for disrupting air traffic protocols, but her testimony indicates the Association must have known the truth.

If Zephyr's account holds true, she is far from being the mastermind behind the signal disruption, but merely a victim of a Mania outbreak who even paid for it with one of her legs.

Zephyr: So why do you people always jump to conclusions so carelessly? The minute you hear a story, you just slot people into your neat little categories without a second thought.

Zephyr: You're all the same—you, whoever wrote that file, that old coot at the orphanage, every single one of you! You're either painting me as a mastermind plotting some grand scheme or blaming my "troubled family history" for my "twisted personality."

Zephyr: My friends always say I'm an open book, wearing my heart on my sleeve, but everyone else just look past me, desperate to dig up some hidden trauma or ulterior motive.

Zephyr: But I'm right here in front of you, aren't I? If you really want to get to know me, see me for who I am—just ask me!

Her loss of composure is a rare sight. Rather than dwelling on your earlier misunderstanding, she seems more concerned with your choice to be made at this very moment.

As you hold her gaze, you come to understand that her principles are even more straightforward than you'd imagined. Leading with this angle in the investigation report might help dispel others' doubts about the theft incident.

Chief: I'm sorry. It was careless of me to judge you based only on your files. I apologize for that.

Hearing your words, Zephyr freezes for a moment. Her gaze falls away from you briefly, only to return with a newfound sparkle.

Zephyr: ...Well, didn't expect that. I was mad at you for like three minutes, but you said sorry! So, cool, now the good vibes have swept away my anger. I'm starting to dig that.

Chief: While I still can't guarantee the inquiry will all go smooth, I'll try to hear your full explanation before drawing any conclusions. Would that work for you?

You remain composed under her scrutiny as she narrows her eyes, trying to read you for a few seconds before giving a decisive nod.

Having come to an understanding with Zephyr, you revisit her case file. She clearly showed lingering fear while recounting the crash, but why does she remain so enthusiastic about flying again?

Chief: For something like this to happen, most people would probably steer clear of flying for a while, or even be traumatized just seeing a plane afterward. But you seem completely unfazed by what you've been through.

Chief: In fact, you still get quite excited when talking about flying again, like you did at the bar.

Zephyr: Cause if I don't fly again, my entire aviation career will end with nothing but an accident, a revoked license, and a broken leg.

Zephyr: I hate adventure stories that end mid-chapter.

Zephyr: The sky is sooooo vast! It couldn't care less whether I'm scared or nervous when I reach for its embrace, and it definitely won't be asking for any permits when I try to get up close.

At the mention of flying, Zephyr's eyes light up instantly—just like that rainy night when you talked across your drinks.

Zephyr: You know what, I've been to the tallest building in Eastside, and let me tell you, when you gaze out from up there—all of DisCity lies tiny beneath your feet, like you could almost see past the Rust straight to Syndicate in just one glance.

Zephyr: But when I'm up in the sky, even DisCity looks like a speck. The entire world just stretches out below, flat beneath my feet. It feels like I could go anywhere, reach any corner as long as I keep flying forward.

It was one dawn, before her first flight.

Zephyr and her friend snuck past security patrols to climb to the highest point in Eastside. As the horizon stretched out in the dawn light, she stood at the edge of the roof, her toes dangling over the precipice, watching the slumbering city beneath her in the refreshing breeze.

On an impulse, she spread her arms wide, drew in a deep breath, and shouted at the top of her lungs.

Zephyr: —I, Zephyr, will fly a plane—! I WILL FLY—UP HIGH—

Friends: Hey, didn't you say your parents passed away in a travel flight? Seems like you've totally got it over.

Zephyr: Why does everyone have to live in fear of the past? I'd rather honor their memory by finishing the journey they never could.

Zephyr: I, Zephyr! Will never let fear keep me trapped!

That was one dawn before she had ever known flight. To her, reaching for that uncharted sky was an adventure—a symbol of absolute freedom.

Zephyr: It was the same feeling when I decided to become a pilot, and the that night I tried to fly... I guess I just... love adventures where I can go anywhere, anytime, without anything holding me back.

Zephyr: Chief, can you see how I feel?

Unexamined judgments often lead to misunderstandings. But now, though you can't fully digest her words, you're starting to grasp why Zephyr pursues flying so relentlessly.

Her free-spirited upbringing shaped both her carefree nature and her unconventional expectations of society. That's why, after losing that freedom, she persistently rebels while searching for something to fill that void.

You realize this is also why she's been so completely honest with you about her past, including what drove her to attempt the theft.

—Because for what others see as life-changing decisions which require tremendous courage, Zephyr simply deems it as mere little adventures to be embarked upon without a second thought.

Chief: I'll classify the theft incident as a crime of impulse in the report, and I'll back up your statement with evidence of your personality and background.

Chief: If this report does dispel their doubts about you, I hope you will fulfill your obligations as a Sinner, from then on...

Zephyr: So once their verdict comes in, I can leave this place, right?

She eagerly cuts off your sentence. Met with her hopeful gaze, you maintain your composure and firmly shake your head.

Chief: Zephyr, you still have to remain under MBCC's supervision.

Chief: No matter how unique your experiences or beliefs may be, it doesn't change the fact that you're an Eastside citizen. A law-abiding citizen should never violate others' boundaries or private property in pursuit of their own ideals and freedom.

Chief: Moreover, the main purpose of MBCC is to detain Sinners, and actions like your blatant appropriation of private property are exactly what warrants detention.

Chief: All I can do for you right now is prevent others with ulterior motives from pinning additional charges on you.

Zephyr: I thought if I just explained everything, then...

Zephyr: Then tell me, what exactly do I have to do to get out of here?

Chief: I can't give you a definite answer right now. The parole process for Sinners involves multiple procedures. Not every Sinner is eligible for parole, and it will depend on the outcomes of your future evaluations.

Chief: But I can promise you this: I'll grant you as much freedom as my authority allows.

Chief: If you're interested, I can increase your participation in field missions or dispatch work. And of course, as long as circumstances allow, I'll do my best to secure short-term travel permits for you...

As you list out the flexible provisions in the Bureau's detention regulations, Zephyr's eyes glaze over with apparent disinterest.

Twirling a loose strand of hair around her finger, she maintains a heavy silence, her distant gaze fixed on you. The regret and yearning reflected in her eyes echo that rainy night when she was taken into custody.

After wrapping up the interrogation results, you escort Zephyr out of the interrogation room. Your footsteps echo in tandem down the hall, their steady rhythm only serving to amplify the unusual silence between you.

If Zephyr's boundless vitality that rainy night was unsettling, her current silence weighs on you just as heavily.

Suddenly, one set of footsteps drops out of the duet.

You turn around to find Zephyr standing before the glass door to the Activity Area. Today's simulated weather is sunny, and she stares at the artificial blue sky, her expression etched with profound melancholy.

**【Advance】**

- Offer words of comfort

**end**

Chief: What I promised you just now wasn't just talk. I'll work hard to secure your freedom here, but of course, it needs you to be cooperative too.

Chief: If you're into adventures, there are some fun-loving Sinners here at the Bureau. You could be friends...

She turns to gaze at you, and though her eyes hold no judgment, your words somehow die in your throat. A strange distance hangs between you—physically shoulder to shoulder, yet emotionally as far apart as the sky above.

After a moment, she changes the subject on her own accord.

Zephyr: Do you know what my parents said during our family trip, on the day they passed away?

Chief: Maybe... "have fun," "always be happy" or something?

Zephyr: I asked them where we were going.
