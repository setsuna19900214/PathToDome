# Eureka Interrogation

## Eureka - Interrogation I

> During the interrogation, a bomb left in one of the confiscated home appliances suddenly exploded. Eureka protected you with her invention and then pointed out that the mastermind behind this case could be the same person who tricked her into the black factory years ago—her old friend, Jessie.

This time, to your great surprise, the Public Security Bureau has sent a pile of "household items" as opposed to the usual towering stack of paperwork.

Chief: Rice cookers, hair dryers... Why is this coffee machine constantly pumping water? And this office chair, it's not mine, is it?

One must admit, this office chair looks quite soft and comfy. It perfectly meets all the ergonomic requirements of a modern furniture piece. However, just as you're about to touch the armrest, Nightingale stops you in terror.

Nightingale: Chief, please be careful! This chair will electrocute the sitter.

Chief: ...Electrocute?

You find it hard to believe your own ears.

Nightingale: Yes. These are household items that have recently become popular in the market, but they all have functional issues. For the specifics, please look at the report sent by the Public Security Bureau.

You take the report and open it. The first line that catches your eye is a warning from the Public Security Bureau: "Do not touch the following items. Risks of electric shock, injury from shooting, etc."

You frown and continue reading. The descriptions of the specific details are even more bizarre: when heated to 65 degrees, the lid of the rice cooker will automatically pop open and spray out rice at high speeds; even when set to cool, the hair dryer will suddenly spout fire...

Chief: ...Are these really household items? They all seem like weapons, don't they?

As you gaze at the dangerous items scattered on the floor, a vague thought pops into your mind: these outrageous "features" and offbeat modifications remind you of a certain "inventor" in the Bureau.

**【Advance】**

- Ask Nightingale about Eureka's recent activities

**end**

Chief: Nightingale, do you know what Eureka has been up to recently?

When you mention this name, a hint of exasperation flashes in Nightingale's eyes.

Nightingale: Eureka is researching how to modify shoe soles into surfboards, and then plans to promote their usage across the Bureau.

Surfboard shoe soles... Eureka does always come up with some incomprehensible oddities, but she has never had any ill intentions. These dangerous products are unlikely to be her work. However, her abilities and experiences may offer some help.

Chief: ...In that case, please prepare the interrogation room for me and notify Eureka to come over. I have some questions to ask her.

When you enter the interrogation room, Eureka is already there. She sits on the interrogation chair, twiddling her fingers, looking somewhat uneasy and tense. In order to calm her down, you decide to start with some trivial topics.

**【Advance】**

- Ask about the progress of her inventions

**end**

Chief: Eureka, how is the research on your surfboard shoe soles coming along?

Eureka: Oh, there's been progress! Based on my observations, each staff member at the Bureau walks at different speeds, so I plan to add a speed control system. This way, the soles can match different speeds and are less likely to cause collisions...

As you expected, you flipped a switch by mentioning her "specialty." Eureka immediately relaxes and starts to enthusiastically introduce her new invention.

Eureka: I also consulted Miss Labyrinth. She was very... friendly, and talked to me about many mechanical principles, as well as her operating methods... uh, I also tried to make a mini-Labyrinth based on what she said, but...

???: Clack, clack, clack!

A strange knocking sound suddenly rings out. Following the source of the sound, you see Eureka hurriedly stuffing a palm-sized metal robot into her tool bag.

The little robot seems to be deliberately defying Eureka. As soon as she presses it down, it immediately pops its head out again.

Eureka: Don't, don't worry, I'll get it to quiet down... Ah!

The little robot jumps onto Eureka's head, grabbing her hair and pulling it from side to side, laughing squeakily.

Eureka: Don't do that! Mini-Lab... Ah! Chief, watch out! It's coming for you!

Chief: !!!

After a chaotic chase, Eureka finally catches the little robot. She presses a screw on its back, and everything becomes quiet again.

Eureka sits back down on the chair, scratching her head awkwardly with an apologetic smile.

Eureka: Ch-Chief, I'm sorry. Mini-Laby is still in its testing phase. There are some small functional issues. I promise to fix it...

Seeing her like this, you can't bring yourself to scold her. You replace the reprimand on the tip of your tongue with a sigh.

Chief: ...Let's talk about the matter at hand. The reason I summoned you is because I want you to take a look at these.

You point at the household items piled at her feet. She seems to finally notice their existence, and curiously picks up a small coffee pot.

Eureka: They connected the power cord to the grinder... Hmm, this might actually generate a reverse power flow when activated. A very interesting way of generating electricity!

Eureka: This home audio speaker has twenty-two built-in amplifiers! If turned on, it will probably blow the decibel meter. If there's a sudden power outage in the Bureau and we need to sound an alarm, it would definitely come in handy!

Eureka: And this, oh my goodness, who would think to equip a portable shower head with a barbecue torch? A two in one contraption! Simply genius!

Eureka: ...Uh, Chief? Why are you hiding so far away?

Chief: ...Don't mind me, Eureka. What do you think of these things?

Eureka: They're highly eccentric, but very creative designs. Chief, do you know who invented these? If possible, could I... I think we would have a lot to chat about.

Chief: Unfortunately, if we find this inventor, we'd probably have to interrogate him or her at the Bureau right away.

Chief: These are no ordinary inventions, Eureka. Over a hundred civilians have been injured by them, some even critically. Some of the victims are still in the hospital fighting for their lives.

As if remembering something, Eureka becomes visibly uneasy. She nervously twiddles her fingers.

Eureka: Chief, I'm sorry... I didn't know they hurt so many people... But I promise, I didn't do this, and I have no knowledge of it...

Her eyes are a bit frantic. After looking around, her gaze settles on a dangerous electrical appliance at her feet.

As if making up her mind, she takes a deep breath, picks up a small electric kettle, and places it on her lap.

Eureka: I... I want to help you, Chief. If you need me to, I can start disassembling these devices right now. Maybe there are clues inside!

Eureka: ...Can I help? Even though I don't know much about investigating, I'm pretty good at dismantling machinery...

**【Advance】**

- Agree

**end**

Chief: Of course, Eureka. That's why I brought you here.

Eureka nods, squats down again, and gathers the items around her. She takes a tool out of her tool bag and starts to proficiently disassemble them.

This is your first time watching Eureka work up close. To avoid being disturbed, her workshop is located in a secluded area where few people pass by.

So when you see Eureka effortlessly dismantle the rice cooker that the report described as "an intricately modified and dangerous device that will attack at the slightest trigger" as she mutters to herself, you can't help but feel amazed.

Eureka doesn't seem to be just a maker of small, trivial inventions like she presents herself. Her thinking is quirky, and her behavior is unconventional. It's clear that she has more secrets.

Chief: Seems like a great inventor not only needs to be able to create, but also to dismantle other people's creations.

Eureka: Mmm, only by taking them apart can you understand how others think. One can learn a lot this way. Besides...

Eureka wipes the sweat on her forehead with an awkward smile.

Eureka: I'm not a great inventor, and have nothing to show off.

Chief: But I think you're amazing, Eureka. Not only can you invent those interesting little things, but you also understand these potentially lethal "weapons" very well.

Eureka: Hey!

Eureka's screwdriver falls to the ground with a clatter. She hastily picks up the tool, but accidentally knocks over the coffee machine next to her. She stands up in panic amidst the scattered parts.

You shake your head and reach out to pick up the coffee machine.

Chief: I mean no harm, Eureka. I think you know that you're a trusted friend to me. So, can you tell me more about your connection to this incident?

Eureka fiddles with her tool bag, folding and unfolding the strap a few times. With a soft sigh, she begins to spill the beans:

Eureka: I was once... tricked by someone into joining a factory, where I invented many things that they claimed were all put into use. But it was also there that I accidentally got contaminated by Mania-laced mechanical material, which awakened my power.

Eureka: After my awakening, I realized that it wasn't an ordinary factory, but an illegal one that secretly produced highly lethal weapons. My inventions were also taken by them to be modified into...

Chief: Weapons?

Eureka nods stiffly. Clearly, even though this happened a long time ago, she still finds it hard to accept that her hard work has been turned into something that could harm others.

Eureka: I, I was so angry and scared at the time that I couldn't control my power... By the time I came back to my senses, the factory had blown up. I fled and started roaming around... until I found that interesting toy factory by the Rust.

**【Advance】**

- Ask for details

**end**

Chief: Do you still remember the person who tricked you?

Eureka purses her lips and avoids your gaze, seeming to not want to elaborate on this topic.

Eureka: Uh, sorry, I don't really remember. Anyway, it was that person, that person, she...

Beep...

The conversation is interrupted by a sudden noise. You and Eureka both look down at the source of the sound.

Underneath the dismantled electric kettle that just had its heater removed, a bomb is lying quietly beneath the metal wire mesh, flashing an eerie light.

Eureka: Oh no! Someone just activated this bomb!

Eureka exclaims this as she quickly pulls out a palm-sized object from her pocket and throws it to the ground.

Mini Robot: Clack! Clack clack!

A dazzling white light forms a force field around you just as the explosion goes off!

The ground shakes, and many devices in the interrogation room are destroyed. Coughing non-stop, Eureka feels around the ground with her hands. After the smoke clears, you see a mini robot in her hand... the same "failed" invention that caused havoc in the interrogation room earlier.

Eureka: Cough... Let me check that bomb first... cough, cough...

Having just escaped from the explosion, Eureka is still in shock. She trembles as she gathers the bomb remnants and takes out a small device.

Eureka: The infection rate has exceeded the threshold. I recognize this Mania... it's from that factory I was at! All their raw materials were stained with this kind of Mania! I remember!

You gently press Eureka's wrist, trying to soothe her panic.

Chief: Eureka, look at this. Is this letter "J" also a special mark of the factory?

Eureka: No, this isn't a mark of the factory. This is... this is...

Eureka lifts up her head, her face covered in grime. She closes her eyes for a moment, as if making some sort of resolution, then says to you:

Eureka: It stands for Jessie. She was once my best friend, and the one who took me to the factory.

## Eureka - Interrogation II

> After hearing Eureka's story, you feel incredibly heavy-hearted. You never thought that someone like her who always seemed so light-hearted could have experienced so much pain and misfortune.
> However, just as you are immersed in your emotional feelings, Jessie suddenly appears.

The next day, at the abandoned factory on the outskirts of the city.

According to Eureka, this is where she used to work. The once grand factory buildings now stand as nothing more than ruins, with faint traces of explosions visible on the remains of the structures.

Eureka: There used to be a group of stray cats here. I wonder if the kittens have all grown up by now? I have no idea where they ended up... Huh? The automatic stirring feeding bowl I left at their den is still here? That was the first invention I made when I was a child...

Eureka mumbles to herself as she walks into the factory, her voice growing fainter and fainter. The desolate scene before her eyes takes her back to her past. The memory fragments are harrowing, but tell the story of her entire childhood.

Classmate: What is this? Have you been dumpster diving? Isn't this the can I just threw away? Did you steal anything else, oh great inventor? Hahahaha!

Eureka: No, I didn't steal anything... It's not useless! It's an automatic stirring feeding bowl I made for the stray cats at school... It's still not quite stable, but I made it all by myself... It's my hard work...

Teacher: Eureka, your grades have dropped a lot. They were already low, and now they've fallen even lower. Do you think you can get into university like this? How are you going to make your parents proud?

Father: We scrimp and save to pay for your education, and this is how you repay us?! With these terrible grades?!

Mother: Eureka, stop wasting time on those useless things. Can't you make us happy for once? Why are you always so naughty?

Father: Look at this garbage you're wasting your time on! You should be studying hard to become a rich financier, not burying your head in this junk all day!

Mother: Mommy threw away all your garbage for you. I put a stack of new exercise books in their place. You must complete them before the end of the term.

Teacher: I will give all the garbage you made in class to your parents. Go and tell them to come meet me.

Classmate: Freak! Weirdo! Garbage girl! No one likes you! Go to hell!

Eureka: Sorry, I started talking to myself again. I know no one wants to hear those boring things...

You shake your head. Although you've already read about them in Eureka's file, hearing these stories from her personally still makes your heart ache.

Chief: It's not your fault, Eureka. You just didn't meet anyone who could understand and appreciate you at that time.

Eureka: Yeah. Jessie said the same thing... But to meet someone like that is as hard as making a pair of shoes that can walk to the moon.

Chief: What was Jessie like?

The corners of Eureka's lips curl up ever so slightly at the mention of that name, yet the faint smile quickly fades into her memories.

Eureka: She was my best friend. Jessie... she praised my inventions as masterpieces. She said that while others might lead ordinary lives, I was different. I was born to create.

Eureka: Jessie was different from me. She was bright and cheerful and passionate about inventions. Everyone liked her and her grades were good... To me, she was exceptional. I knew we were from different worlds.

Eureka: But it's strange... unlike the others, she didn't treat me like air. She took the initiative to talk to me.

Eureka: I still remember the first time we talked. She saw the automatic ink filler I made for my pen. She said...

Jessie: Eureka! You're a genius!

Jessie: Ah! I'm sorry, did I scare you? My name is Jessie. I'm a mechanical engineering student. May I admire your work?

Jessie: It's amazing, Eureka! This is a masterpiece! You're the most talented inventor I've ever met!

Eureka: No one has ever praised me like that before. Her words... were like fuel to my engine.

Eureka: I got 56 and 32 for my main courses, and I don't have enough elective credits. I also racked up 73 absences, and haven't even been approved to take my finals... What should I do? I definitely won't graduate at this rate...

Eureka: I got yelled at again when I went home last time. My dad even hit me... If he sees these grades, he'll probably kill me. My mom will just cry and scream at me...

Eureka: I can't face them, and I don't know what to do in the future... How awful life is...

Eureka: Even a piece of broken glass thrown in the trash can be used to make a vase, but what about me... I might as well go be a capybara...

Eureka stands on the empty rooftop of her school building, staring blankly at the noisy field below. Complex emotions begin to well up in her.

Jessie: Eureka, are you daydreaming again? Are you crying?

Eureka looks up in surprise. Seeing who it is through her tear-blurred eyes, she throws herself into Jessie's arms and starts sobbing. The heavy burden on her chest seems to be relieved by her friend's kindness and understanding.

Jessie: ...I get it. Not being able to graduate is indeed a problem.

Jessie: But Eureka, have you ever thought that maybe graduating isn't the only way out?

Eureka: ...What do you mean...?

Jessie: My uncle has an innovative factory on the outskirts of Eastside. If you want, we can go work there together.

Jessie: We don't need a diploma from you for you to join us, Eureka. If you can't get it, then just forget about it! You're extremely talented! So what if you chose the wrong major and failed your courses?

Jessie: Don't let mundane matters constrain you. You were born to create!

Jessie's words ring out strong and clear, clearing the fog in Eureka's eyes like wind. They warm her up and make her relax again.

Uncle: Let's welcome the great inventor, Miss Eureka, to our team!

Eureka: Hello, I'm Jessie's frie...

Jessie: Enough with the chit-chat, Eureka. What do you think of this work uniform? I prepared it especially for you. Try it on!

Eureka: Thank you, Jessie... But, this color, I don't think it looks very good on me... Uh, I mean, sorry, I actually really like it...!

The sudden shift in her words soothes Jessie's furrowed brow. She gives Eureka a hearty pat on the shoulder.

Jessie: I knew you would like it. The moment I saw this outfit, I knew it was perfect for you, just like this place!

Eureka: Yeah... yeah...

Eureka looks around at Jessie's uncle and the workers, who are all beaming at her. They overwhelm her with their thunderous applause. It's something Eureka had never experienced before, and she can't help but say to herself:

Eureka: ...Yes, I think I belong here.

Uncle: Eureka, good news! Those electronic devices you submitted are excellent. We've discussed with the merchant and decided to put them all into production. This is a big order!

Eureka: Really?! That's great! This is the first time someone has ever said I did a good job...

Eureka flashes a shy smile, rubbing her oil-stained hands vigorously on her leather apron.

Uncle: The factory needs more of your ideas. Your thoughts are the incubators for the future, Eureka. You truly are a genius!

Genius, future, well done... words she had never heard from others before were now being used to describe her. Eureka realizes she's shaking with excitement.

Eureka: I'm working on a new invention. It's a mini propulsion engine made by simplifying the inside of a car engine and adding spring crossbow parts... The advantage of this device is that it can generate a huge thrust in an instant when handheld. As for how strong the force is, I still need to test it out...

Eureka: Maybe I can apply it to the legs. But if it's for people who have difficulty walking, I should add a speed adjuster... Yes, that's right, the speed adjuster needs to be at a reasonable height, so you don't have to bend down to operate it...

Eureka: Maybe it also needs a small temperature control system... Ah, I'm sorry, I was talking to myself again!

Uncle: Great! Go ahead and do whatever you want, Eureka. I hope to see the final version soon!

Eureka: Yes, okay, no problem! Thank you so much!

As she watches the middle-aged man leave, Eureka clenches her wrench excitedly, unable to calm down her pounding heart.

Jessie: Eureka, how many times do I have to tell you, you have to go to Uncle's place today!

Eureka: I can't, Jessie. If they find out that I've awakened my power, they'll surely kick me out! I can't leave the factory. That's the only place I have any value. Please...

Jessie: What nonsense are you talking about? Value? Your power is your greatest value now! Come on, follow me! Uncle is getting impatient!

The unfamiliar underground corridor is humid and stuffy. Mold spots grow in the corners, and the broken light bulb flickers nonstop. Jessie has a steely grip on Eureka's arm, and is practically dragging her along.

Eureka's pleading voice echoes in the cold corridor, but there's no response.

Jessie: Here we are. Go inside.

Eureka is pushed into the brightly lit room at the end of the corridor. She instinctively closes her eyes, then opens them, and sees a sight that makes her blood run cold...

An emaciated man is tied to a post with a dirty sack over his head that muffles his sobs and moans. The shirtless man's body is covered in scars, and it's obvious that these wounds were not caused by a single tool.

Uncle: Eureka, Jessie says you're becoming more and more disobedient lately. You took so long to come here after I invited you.

He has a modified gun tightly bound to his arm. The speed adjuster at the end of it is making a steady beeping sound that makes Eureka dizzy.

Eureka: That's... That's my...

Uncle: Your what? Body speed thruster... or adjuster, or whatever it's called... The name doesn't matter. What matters is that it's a great invention, right?

Eureka: No, it can't be attached to your arm. If the speed adjustment goes wrong, the recoil of the gun becomes a huge safety hazard. You can't... please take it off. Please, I'm really scared... please don't...

Uncle: Eureka. Do you think that just because you made these prototypes, you're so great that you can defy me?

Jessie's uncle slowly walks up to the bound man, raises his arm, and points the gun at his forehead...

Bang!

The man, who was begging for mercy just a second ago, silently drops his head.

Tick-tock, tick-tock...

Lab Staff: Boss, the data is out. 85% of the recoil from firing has been offset by our modified injector. This is not far off from the order requirements. A couple more adjustments should meet the target.

Uncle: Good job. Go clean this place up.

The employee bows and backs away. Eureka stands frozen in shock as dark red liquid pools around her feet. She stares at the limp, lifeless body still hanging from the pillar, and suddenly feels that none of this is real.

Eureka: He... he's dead...?

Uncle: Yes. He violated the factory's rules. After seeing the secrets here, he refused to join us, but planned to escape instead. That's treacherous behavior, isn't it?

Uncle: Eureka, this factory is my blood, sweat, and tears. If you were in my shoes, you wouldn't want your hard work to go down the drain because of others' misunderstanding, right?

Eureka: No, that's not it... My mini propulsion engine is an assistive device, meant to be attached to legs. Its speed adjuster is designed for those with leg mobility issues...

Eureka shakes her head vigorously, desperately and mechanically repeating the original intent of her invention in a hoarse voice.

Jessie: Enough! Eureka, when will you sober up?

Jessie: Don't you get it? The moment your little contraptions get turned into weapons is when they truly gain value. Now, they can truly be called "inventions."

Jessie, who had been silent all this while, suddenly speaks up. She stands behind Eureka, her voice as cold as a venomous snake. Eureka feels like she doesn't know her at all.

Eureka: Jessie...? Did you... know about this all along...?

Jessie: Of course. Why else do you think I had you join my uncle's factory? Out of kindness? Afraid you'd drop out and end up on the streets?

Jessie: Don't flatter yourself. I can't stand your pitiful look, Eureka.

Jessie spits out the last word coldly and turns around without looking back, disappearing into the darkness behind the door.

Eureka: What do you mean... Jessie... We were supposed to be best friends...

Eureka hears the sticky noise of the floor being scrubbed behind her and comes back to her senses. She turns to look at Jessie's uncle, who is fiddling with his beloved gun, and backs away while saying in a hollow voice:

Eureka: The rice cooker is still on musical mode back home... I need to go back to deal with it...

Uncle: Don't talk nonsense, Eureka. Jessie already told me. Your power can give these weapons even more strength. You must stay here. Make the most powerful weapon in the world for me! Bring me a huge order with countless money and checks!

Seeing Eureka's intent to flee, a few security guards rush up to twist her arm, halting her in her tracks.

Eureka: No...

Eureka struggles desperately, feeling intense pain in her arm. All that remains in her ears are the shrill and gruesome cackles of Jessie's uncle.

Uncle: Lock her in the laboratory we prepared. She's not allowed to come out without my permission.

Uncle: An inventor or a test subject. Eureka, which one do you choose to be?

Eureka: I...

Eureka: I won't stay here anymore!!!

The desire to resist and flee has never been stronger. Eureka yells out loud, fully unleashing her power for the first time. Several partially modified bombs on the workbench explode in response.

Boom—

The dazzling white light produced by the violent explosion instantly engulfs the entire laboratory.

After hearing Eureka's story, your heart is heavy and silent. You didn't expect an eccentric oddball like her to have also endured so much pain and misfortune.

Chief: I didn't expect this... I didn't know they forced you like that... I'm so sorry...

Eureka shakes her head.

Eureka: After leaving that place, I've been on the run. I kept myself busy by constantly looking for new inspiration and making new inventions to avoid thinking about the past... But it seems that I've come full circle and ended up back here...

??: Heh. Even today, you're still dreaming. You still think you're the victim.

The machine nearby suddenly starts up, and the light bulb on the ceiling begins to flicker. In the darkness, a woman's figure slowly appears.

Her venomous gaze is fixated on Eureka. She lets out a sneer.

Jessie: Long time no see, Eureka.

## Eureka - Interrogation III

> So what if they don't fit into societal norms? Those eccentric ideas will always have their place, and there will always be people who genuinely appreciate and cherish them, cheering for them even.

Eureka: Je... Jessie... How... how did you...

Facing this "old friend" for whom she has so many complex feelings, Eureka instinctively becomes panicked and timid. Jessie laughs scornfully, her taunting gaze brimming with mockery.

Jessie: Eureka, you still choose to be a clown instead of realizing your true value. I'm truly disappointed.

**【Advance】**

- Rebut

**end**

Chief: What do you know about value? You think your value lies in those things you made?

Eureka: A rice cooker that shoots out rice, a bean grinder with a drill... Jessie, did you make those...?

Jessie: Of course. Do you even need to ask?

Eureka: ...But why did you sell them to people who had no idea about their functions? Many innocent people got hurt by them!

Jessie: Ha, that's exactly what I want. Aren't these things quite powerful? They lull people into a false sense of security with their harmless appearance, and then unleash the greatest destructive power when they least expect it... This is something I learned from you.

Eureka: But I never made inventions to hurt people! And I don't care who is better than who... I just want... I just...

Jessie: You just want to be accepted, right?

Jessie interrupts Eureka's stammering, laughing to herself.

Jessie: Hehehe... Eureka, Eureka, what should I say about you?

Jessie: Do you know how many times Uncle told me, "Jessie, be nice to Eureka, we need her." "Jessie, why can't you be as good as her? If only you had half as much talent."

Jessie: It's because he valued your talent so much that he stubbornly believed that even if our philosophies differed, you would eventually join us and become a real partner.

As she speaks, Jessie's expression becomes twisted.

Jessie: Yet what did you do? You turned your back on his kindness, and even destroyed his factory! When the explosion happened, Uncle didn't make it out of the lab alive because he was trying to save those blueprints... The factory workers were as heartless as you. Once Uncle died, they took all the valuables and ran away!

Jessie: Good thing those buffoons couldn't tell what was truly valuable. I took all the materials contaminated beyond the threshold, and picked up some of your discarded trash... Those appliances were made from stuff I brought out from the factory years ago.

Jessie: I will prove to you, to my late uncle, and to everyone in this world, that I've never been inferior to you. I can be better than you!

Eureka: ...

Eureka ponders for a moment, looking at Jessie with some confusion.

Eureka: Huh? So, you made those inventions just to prove a point?

Jessie: Yes. I spent many years creating them. Admit it, those are truly remarkable inventions!

Eureka: They are indeed interesting ideas... but there are many problems in the actual execution.

Jessie: ! ?

Eureka: Hmm... let's take that coffee machine for instance. Connecting the power cord to the grinder can indeed generate a reverse power flow when activated, but the amount of electricity produced is too low to be efficient.

Eureka: Your audio speaker can reach ultra-high decibels, but there are too many amplifiers inside, which can easily malfunction and burn out the circuit.

Eureka: And that showerhead flamethrower... it's not easy to control the temperature. The high-temperature steam can cause the pipe to burst and injure the user.

Upon hearing this, the smug look on Jessie's face vanishes. She has no way of refuting Eureka, because all the problems mentioned are indeed present in her inventions.

Eureka scratches her chin and continues.

Eureka: So if it were me... I would use the same reverse flow power generation design for a washing machine. I would reduce the number of amplifiers for the speaker and change their layout to achieve the same or even better effect. As for the showerhead flamethrower, I would use two pipes, one for fire and one for water, and apply heat-insulating materials throughout.

Jessie: ...

Eureka: These measures are very easy to think of and convenient to implement. Why haven't you enhanced them further for such a long time?

Jessie: Shut up!

Jessie interrupts Eureka, pointing at her indignantly.

Jessie: So what if my inventions have flaws? They can still take your life!

As soon as Jessie says this, a thunderous roar erupts from the darkness behind her...

Countless lethal-looking weapons emerge: huge tanks, ammunition drones, mobile turrets, and many other unnameable monstrosities.

Chief: ...There's so many! How did she manage to secretly tinker with so many illegal weapons without the Public Security Bureau noticing?!

The next second, a shell flies in Eureka's direction.

Eureka: Oh no!

Eureka screams, but her hands are steady as a rock.

She quickly pulls out a pair of surfboards from her tool bag and stuffs them into her shoe soles, then kicks off, flying out at an incredible speed!

The shell leaves a huge pit where Eureka was standing, but she had already darted behind a large stone slab in the abandoned factory.

Jessie: Eureka, is there any point in hiding? Even if you burrow into the ground, I can dig you out!

This time, Jessie releases a drone. The drone's wings buzz, emitting an infrared detection line that continuously scans the area. Eureka quickly stuffs some bubble gum into her mouth, chews and spits it out, then loads it onto a slingshot-like launcher.

Whoosh... splat! The drone's camera is covered by bubble gum, completely disabling its surveillance function.

Jessie: What... what is this?!

Eureka: It's my new invention: a bubble gum launcher! I recycled and refitted it from the Bureau's security system. It has an automatic aiming function!

Jessie: Hmph, what random junk... You got lucky this time. There's no way your rubbish can defeat a tank!

Jessie waves her hand, and a heavy tank emerges from the side with a loud roar. It slowly aims its cannon at the stone slab that Eureka is hiding behind.

However, there's still no hint of panic on Eureka's face. She turns her backpack upside down, shaking out a bunch of plastic little frogs.

Ribbit ribbit ribbit ribbit ribbit... The noisy little green fellows hop over the stone slab to the other end and spit out long plastic tongues, stuffing the tank's cannon.

Bang... Kaboom!

The entire tank breaks apart like a misfired gun. Shining metal fragments are blasted into the air, illuminating the sky with spectacular, colorful sparks.

You watch Eureka skillfully "command the battle" from nearby. It's like watching a strange yet breathtaking performance. You can't help but admit that you really underestimated this quirky inventor.

Jessie: Damn it... what about this? Eureka, you remember this, don't you? You were really scared of it back then.

Eureka: ...

Eureka purses her lips. On Jessie's thin, scarred arm is the familiar arm-style gun that had haunted her for so many years.

Jessie: Over the years, I've modified and upgraded it repeatedly, significantly increasing the bullet capacity and firing speed. This time, even your ridiculous surfboard won't be able to escape my bullets!

Chief: What's the matter? Are you worried that you can't handle this gun?

Eureka shakes her head slowly.

Eureka: No, Chief, I think I can win. I... I'm just worried that I will lose control of my power again like last time...

Chief: You won't, Eureka. I'm here this time.

You give her a comforting and encouraging look. Endless power circulates between the links of the shackles.

Chief: I can help suppress your power. Just do what you need to do. Your inventions won't be exploited by others again. They won't become weapons that hurt people again.

A barrage of bullets sweeps towards you like a violent storm. Eureka raises a handheld fan. Backed by her power, the thin fan blades whip up a massive tornado, diverting countless bullets off their course.

The arm-mounted gun emits black smoke after firing its last bullet, loosening from Jessie's arm and falling to the ground. There's nothing left behind Jessie, but she stubbornly bites her lip so hard that it almost bleeds.

Jessie: That's impossible, I can't lose...! I've been preparing these things for many years... How could you possibly beat me!

Jessie: It's not over... It's not over yet!

Jessie suddenly changes direction, and throws something with a sparking fuse at you. Eureka's pupils constrict. In that moment, memories flood back like a tidal wave. She sees again the cowering civilians in the underground lab, their faces twisted in their final moments.

Eureka: Chief!!!

A bomb. Another bomb. You think back to the day this all started, in the interrogation room... there was also a bomb. You close your eyes, listening to Eureka's shouts and the sound of her footsteps, but this time, you feel strangely reassured.

Mini Robot: Clack! Clack clack!

A dazzling white light forms a protective shield around you. The sound of the explosion is blocked out, and you feel only a slight impact before quickly regaining your footing. At the same time, you hear sirens in the distance.

Jessie: How could this be... I put in so much effort... Why is it still the same result...

Jessie: There's still a way... There must be a way... I can still do something... Just you wait...

Jessie slumps to the ground in defeat, muttering to herself as she desperately searches through the wreckage for fragments of her inventions.

Chief: Enough, Jessie, it's all over.

Chief: The sheriffs will be here soon, and you'll pay for what you've done.

Things have come to a close. The sheriffs who arrive take Jessie away. Some stay behind to clean up the Mania-tainted machine parts, which have been mostly destroyed in the recent fight. Only a few remain in the ruins, tightly guarded.

No longer in her sharp and agile combat mode, Eureka reverts back to her usual absent-minded self, muttering to herself as she stares blankly ahead.

Eureka: Oh my, I've blown up this place for the second time... The cat hole is gone. The automatic stirring feeding bowl is also broken... What a pity. I was planning to take it back for the pets at the Bureau...

Chief: It's indeed a pity... but a bit of damage shouldn't be an obstacle for you, right?

You carefully pick out the fragments of the porcelain bowl buried in the sand, put them all into a plastic bag, and hand it to Eureka.

Eureka: ...Ch... Chief? What do you mean...?

Chief: Hmm... maybe you could try fixing it and then upgrade it when we get back?

Eureka: Ah, I got it! I'll add automatic cooking, heating, and cooling functions to it! That way, the pets can eat food that suits their taste at any time!

Chief: Great idea! Genius inventor Eureka's new invention will surely be a hit at the MBCC!

In response to your praise, Eureka scratches her head and smiles somewhat bashfully.

Eureka: Th... thank you, Chief... but I'm just a terrible inventor who has caused a lot of trouble for everyone...

Chief: Really? But the person who found Jessie, cracked all her dangerous weapons, defeated her, and successfully confiscated those dangerous Mania-tainted parts, is you. Don't always be so modest. You are the most amazing inventor I have ever met.

Mini Robot: Clack! A complete victory! Cheering for you! Clack clack clack! Great inventor! Eureka!

The familiar clacking sound comes from Eureka's toolkit, continuing your words on its own.

Chief: It can talk!?

Eureka: Huh? I don't know... Let me see...

Eureka looks around in bewilderment, holding the small robot at eye level with both hands.

The setting sun's afterglow envelops the bombed-out factory. She gently gazes at the little robot she created, softly stroking its smooth head, treating it with love and awe as if it were a living creature.

Here, is rebirth. The creator here is...

Eureka, the "terrible inventor."
