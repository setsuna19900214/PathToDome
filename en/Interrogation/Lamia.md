# Lamia Interrogation

## Lamia - Interrogation I

> Intelligence before completion:
> Although the Insatiable Fiesta event has come to an end, you still have doubts about the mysterious network organization, the Thaumas Society, so you decide to interrogate Lamia.

> Intelligence after completion:
> You finally know the inside story of the Thaumas Society and the hidden truths behind the President's death, but when the topic comes to Lamia's childhood, she suddenly loses control, forcing the interrogation to be suspended.
> At the same time, she gives you a location—Eastside Psychiatric Hospital. It seems to hide the reason for her loss of control.

### Interrogation Transcript

Knock knock knock...

Chief: Come in.

Nightingale: Chief, we've finished sorting the info on Sinner Lamia. After going through all the posts and rumors online, this is what we've got.

Nightingale hands you a folder. When you open it, there are only two pieces of paper inside.

One is her basic bio. The other is half a page of personal info unrelated to her role as "Ocean Messenger."

Chief: ...That's all?

Nightingale: Yeah. Sorry, Chief. Repeated investigations by the intelligence department yielded the same results.

Nightingale: Lamia's "Ocean Messenger" identity has covered up a lot about her own identity.

Nightingale: While the President of the Thaumas Society primed Lamia, he deleted a lot of information about her, and even altered her appearance for their livestreams. The technical department is trying to restore some of the data, but it will take time.

Chief: Okay, got it.

Chief: Then please arrange an interrogation in two days. It seems that we must learn about her past, including her involvement with the Thaumas Society, in her own words.

You see Lamia flinching visibly the moment the light turns on. She sneaks a glance at you and her voice trembles, just like when you first met her.

Lamia: ...Chief? What am I doing here?

Lamia: It's so dark and scary here... Did something happen? Is that why you brought me here...

Her eyes are downcast and full of worry, but not for herself—instead, she seems to be worried about the potential trouble you're facing.

**【Advance】**

- Explain the situation

**end**

Chief: Don't be scared, Lamia. You've behaved quite well since coming to the MBCC. You haven't violated any rules, and everyone has spoken highly about you.

Chief: I'm just here for a routine inquiry. I want to know some details about the Thaumas Society.

Lamia: ...Really? I'm glad I didn't cause trouble for you guys.

She sighs in relief and unknits her brows, revealing a faint smile.

Lamia: So, what do you want to know, Chief? I'll make sure to help you as much as I can.

**【Advance】**

- Ask about the Thaumas Society

**end**

Chief: I've been curious about the Thaumas Society since arriving to Ogygia Villa. What kind of organization is it?

You've been tracking this organization online and suspect that Sinners are involved, but it's too strictly guarded. Even if you pay to become a member, the chance of meeting a core member is slim.

Lamia: Ah... are you interested in becoming a Seafarer? We are a maritime culture research organization. We facilitate the exchange of ideas between ocean enthusiasts.

Lamia: All life originated from the ocean, so we believe it is the cradle of all souls. The deep sea is spiritual and wise, and holds the immense power to purify and enlighten.

Lamia: In addition, we believe there is another world at the bottom of the sea, where life that remained in the ocean gained intelligence and established their own civilization.

Chief: I've read that already on your homepage. I want to ask about the things not available to the general public.

Lamia: What do you mean?

Chief: For example... your offline gatherings?

Lamia: Ah, you mean our in-person meetings?

Lamia: Yes, the President regularly invites our most dedicated fans, as well as those with unique insights into maritime culture, to meet with me at Ogygia Villa. It's an opportunity to exchange ideas and explore the mysteries of the world together.

Chief: Just to exchange ideas?

Lamia: Yes... why do you ask?

She fidgets in her chair, and her eyes dart about. It's evident that she's hiding something, and... lying.

**【Advance】**

- Ask about the President

**end**

Chief: Speaking of the "President," what kind of person is he? How is your relationship with him?

Lamia: The President... is a very good person. I've always felt grateful towards him. He is patient and kind, and takes care of everyone, including myself. Anyone who has attended our meetings has more or less experienced that. So...

She clasps her hands over her chest and flashes a faint smile.

Lamia: We all admire the President. I pray every day that he gets the ocean's blessings that he yearns for.

Chief: So, you don't know... that he died in Ogygia Villa?

Your question seems to shock Lamia.

Lamia: Wh-what?! The President... he...!!

Chief: We found a corpse in Ogygia Villa. The MBCC's autopsy confirmed it to be the President of the Thaumas Society.

Lamia: How could this be... He... He said that we'd go back to the Society together after dinner...

Lamia: I... I was still worrying about missing that meeting... How could the President have... ended up in the sea...

Lamia lowers her head even more, resting her chin on her clasped hands. Tears brim in her eyes and tremble on her eyelashes.

Chief: So... you knew nothing about this?

Lamia: W-what's that supposed to mean? Of course I don't know anything about this! Surely you don't think I have something to do with this!

Lamia: I was with you and Miss Cabernet when it happened, remember?

Chief: You're a lousy liar, Miss Lamia.

You put the autopsy report on the table and push it towards Lamia.

Chief: I don't think you understood what I meant by "the MBCC's autopsy."

Chief: What happened in Ogygia Villa was a Mania incident, so all the evidence went through the MBCC's lab. We have evidence to prove that he died from a poisonous, hallucination-inducing Sinner's power.

The autopsy report completely blows her cover. She's now fully aware that there's solid evidence against her.

Lamia: Ah... Chief, I... it's not what you think! I-I really didn't want to, but I had no choice... Please trust me!

Stuttering in defense, she looks pleadingly at you with tearful eyes, as if you're her last hope for survival.

Chief: No choice? Why?

As if wanting to say something but hesitant and afraid to, Lamia lowers her head and clasps tightly onto the armrests.

**【Advance】**

- Ask her to tell the truth

**end**

You lean in close to her, softening your tone.

Chief: The people who planned that banquet will all be investigated and punished. If you hide anything from us, we might consider you to be an accomplice.

Chief: So... if you keep hiding the truth, I can't help you.

Lamia: ...All right, I'll tell you.

Biting her lip, she finally makes up her mind and starts to talk:

Lamia: Like I said before, I've always truly respected, appreciated and depended on the President.

Lamia: After all, when a freak like me came to this wholly unfamiliar world, had nowhere to go and almost starved to death on the streets, he was the one who took me in.

Lamia: The President gave me a place to stay, and made me realize that I, too, had the worth to keep on living.

"Wholly unfamiliar world"...?

Her odd wording makes you frown, but you continue to listen without interrupting.

Lamia: He helped me come to terms with my skin color. He also taught me how to comfort those driven to desperation by this cruel world at in-person meetings, so that they may rekindle their hope and passion for life...

Lamia: In fact, I often feel that the Seafarers and I are each other's saviors. I heal their wounds, while they make me feel accepted and loved.

Chief: ...So this is the truth of your in-person meetings? You use your power to comfort them?

Lamia: ...No. This is what I thought was the truth.

Lamia shakes her head, smiling bitterly.

Lamia: I wish I hadn't passed by the President's room that night. I wish I hadn't eavesdropped out of curiosity. Perhaps then I could've continued to live in my dream, and all those bad things wouldn't have happened.

Lamia curls up in her chair, seeming to recall a very painful scene.

Lamia: But in reality, he didn't do any of those things for the sake of those poor, tormented Seafarers. It was all for greed and money. I was just a tool... a puppet he used to trick others...

Lamia: It wasn't till that night that I found out everyone had to pay him a hefty "membership fee" to join my in-person meetings.

Lamia: Those poor, desperate Seafarers sought me for comfort because of how much they suffered.

Lamia: Yet the President exploited their pain and suffering for money. That's not something I can accept.

Lamia: I didn't want to go to that banquet and participate in his scams anymore... We had an argument in the Ogygia Villa's control room... He said there were plenty of others who'll do what I won't...

Lamia: He said there weren't that many honorable souls in this world, and that I could be replaced at any moment. He even decided to abandon me on the spot and kill me.

Lamia: I was so scared that I lost control... I swear, I really didn't mean to, I just wanted to live...

Lamia: I truly wanted to relieve the Seafarers' suffering. I truly wanted to help them, but ended up helping the President harm them instead.

Lamia: Maybe I'm just destined to be a monster who brings disaster to those around me... It was all an illusion. I didn't attain salvation, nor could I save others.

Lamia's voice is so full of pain and guilt that you can't help but feel moved.

Based on previous investigations, you know that such organizations are usually scams. They took in a desperate soul like Lamia and exploited her power to scam others.

All you can do is try to uproot such organizations and save one Lamia after another.

Lamia: Sorry, Chief. I choked up a bit. How embarrassing.

As you ponder how to comfort her, she wipes the corners of her eyes and flashes you a grateful smile.

Lamia: I've always been the one listening to others' troubles... It's my first time confessing my own troubles to someone else... It feels so good. Thank you for listening to me.

Chief: Sorry for bringing up these awful memories, but it's a routine procedure. Thank you for cooperating. I now have a general understanding of the Thaumas Society. I have one last question before wrapping up our interrogation today.

Perhaps surprised by how quickly the interrogation is ending, her expression becomes a bit off, but she quickly fixes it.

Lamia: Of course... Ask away.

**【Advance】**

- Continue questioning

**end**

Chief: You mentioned just now that "the President took you in"... That was indeed the case. We found an old nurse and learned some things from her. Perhaps... it's related to your past.

You put a voice recording pen on the table. Lamia stares at it with a grim look on her face.

Chief: If what she says is true, and you want to pursue the matter... we can offer you help.

Lamia: Is the past... of particular importance to the Bureau? I joined the Society at an early age. I don't remember what happened before then.

Lamia is being overtly resistant, making you puzzled as well as troubled. But you need to get to know her better—you hope that after leaving Thaumas, she can learn to survive without using her power or trickery.

Chief: Sorry. The Bureau's top psychiatrist will be right at the door. I will immediately stop the recording if you feel unwell... But I still hope that you can confirm its content.

Lamia nods lightly, and you start to play the recording. Amidst the rustling static, an elderly voice begins to speak.

Old Nurse: My my, how could I forget such a special child? I've been a midwife for decades and delivered thousands of babies, but I've never seen anything quite like her.

Old Nurse: That child was as gray as ash when she was born. Quite the bad omen! The doctors and nurses were all shocked. Even the mother screamed and fainted when she saw the child.

Old Nurse: Moreover, I heard that the mother died of illness not long after. Nobody dared to adopt that monstrous-looking child. They kicked her around like a ball for quite some time, until some distant relatives were pressured to take her in.

Old Nurse: I don't know what happened after that. The child was illegitimate to begin with, so her relatives took her from the hospital without filling in the proper papers. They didn't leave any information with us.

Lamia: I... I don't remember... I really don't remember...

Lamia mutters to herself as pain and panic creeps onto her face.

Lamia: ...Don't hurt me... Let me go... Let me go...

She begins to tremble uncontrollably, curling up into a fetal position. Her eyes lose focus and her breathing speeds up as she mutters incomprehensibly.

Lamia: Ah!!!

Lamia lets out a piteous scream. You quickly stop the recording and cast your shackles to stabilize Lamia.

Chief: Lamia, don't be scared. You're safe and sound in the MBCC. No one will hurt you here...

Lamia: ...

After a long silence, Lamia finally calms down.

Chief: Let's call it a day. Go back and get some rest.

Chief: You did well... I pushed you too hard. I didn't mean any harm. I just wanted to help you.

As you get up to lead her out, she suddenly grabs the corner of your shirt.

Lamia: Eastside Psychiatric Hospital

Chief: ...What?

As if making a difficult decision, she takes a deep breath and looks up at you again.

Lamia: Eastside Psychiatric Hospital. That's where you can find out about... my "past."

Lamia: You're right. Only by knowing this will you be able to help me...

Lamia: Help me, Chief.

## Lamia - Interrogation II

> Intelligence before completion:
> After the interrogation, with your questions, you go to the Eastside Psychiatric Hospital with Lamia.

> Intelligence after completion:
> With the sudden appearance of a relative, Lamia's disguise is completely torn apart. Her true face and the real inner world are finally fully revealed in front of you.

### Interrogation Transcript

Pedestrian A: Hey! Look over there! That girl has gray skin!

Pedestrian B: Gray skin... I feel like I've seen her somewhere before... Ah, it's Lamia! The Ocean Messenger of the Thaumas Society! I think I follow her online!

Pedestrian C: What? Is it actually her?! So she really is gray! I thought it was just a filter.

Pedestrian A: How can anyone have gray skin? It's just makeup, right? Let's get a closer look...

Pedestrian B: We mustn't miss such a rare opportunity! My camera is all ready to go! Quick, let's go closer and see where she's going...

You and Lamia are on your way to Eastside Psychiatric Hospital. Her eccentric appearance has attracted the attention of many passersby.

More and more onlookers gather around you, pointing excitedly and snapping photos.

You're unable to disperse the pestering crowd despite warning them repeatedly. As you prepare to shoo them away again, Lamia stops you.

Lamia: Let's just go, Chief. It's no use. You won't be able to get rid of them.

Lamia quickens her pace, dragging you through the insufferable crowd with her head bowed. You don't quite understand her approach.

Chief: ...Is she just going to let them do whatever they want?

Even you feel disturbed by all the eyes gawking at Lamia as if she were some zoo animal. How can Lamia tolerate it herself?

Lamia: What else can I do?

She shakes her head and says in exasperation:

Lamia: I'm... used to this kind of thing.

Lamia: Plus, I think they're just doing it out of curiosity or because they like me. At least they don't hate me, fear me, or want to hurt me. That's already more than I can ask for.

Chief: ...

As if she remembered something, Lamia's voice is tinged with pain and sorrow. Yet her pitiful expression gives you the same troubled feeling you got back in the interrogation room.

When questioned about her childhood earlier, her reaction was a tad dramatic and unnatural. The subsequent transition was also oddly fast for someone who deceives for a living.

You sigh to yourself as you look at Lamia, who is putting on a courageous smile.

Chief: Hope I'm overthinking.

Only upon reaching the psychiatric hospital do you manage to shake off the crowd, who gets dispersed by security guards at the door. Lamia leads you up the stairs, down a long corridor, and finally stops at a room near the end.

Lamia: ...

Her head still bowed, she rests her trembling palms on the door frame, but doesn't muster the courage to open the door.

Lamia: I'm so scared...

Lamia: I... never thought that I'd come back here and see him again...

Chief: Don't worry. I'll be by your side.

You comfort her softly and push open the door.

Chief: Hello...?

You see a trembling young man curled up in a corner of the hospital bed, his cowering appearance at odds with his brawny body.

Though facing the door, he ignores your greeting, and his eyes are glazed and unfocused. You fail to elicit any response from him, so you turn to ask Lamia.

Chief: Is this the person you wanted me to meet?

Lamia nods.

Chief: Who is he...?

Lamia: ...My cousin.

Chief: Cousin...? But you said you had nowhere to go and no place to stay...

Lamia: ...

Lamia doesn't answer. She takes out a mirror from her pocket and carefully holds it up in front of the man she calls cousin.

The moment the man's gaze meets the mirror, his dazed face contorts in pain and panic.

Cousin: Ahhh!!! Don't come over!!!

He shrieks hysterically and waves his arms wildly as if trying to fight off some invisible enemy, knocking the mirror from Lamia's hands.

Cousin: I was wrong! I won't talk back again!

Cousin: I'm so tired, I just want to rest for a while...

Cousin: ...Cough cough cough! I'm a dumb fish! I'm a monster from the sea... Please let go of me, I can't breathe...

The nurses outside notice the man's sudden outburst. They rush into the room, elbowing you and Lamia aside.

Lamia: That was what happened between me and him. To be more precise... That was what happened to me.

The nurses hold the man down and inject him with a sedative, restoring his sluggish demeanor.

Chief: ...

You realize what she wants to tell you by bringing you here.

Lamia: He held my head in the water tank... He was so strong that I couldn't fight back. I was choking to death. I just wanted to live, but then it turned out like this all of a sudden...

Lamia: I was so scared when it happened. I ran away from home and never went back, and never saw him again... I, I didn't know the consequences would be so severe.

Lamia: I feel sad and guilty seeing him like this. It was my fault. I could've handled things differently...

You're almost convinced by her again. But this proves that her odd shift in expression was to deliberately arouse your suspicion and make you come here with her.

Chief: ...Lamia.

You interrupt her.

Chief: What are you trying to prove by showing me this? To prove... that your actions were justified?

Lamia: Chief...

Surprised that you're suspicious of her intentions, Lamia freezes on the spot. Even the piteous look on her face freezes.

??: Monster! Shoo! Stay away from my son!

A shrill female voice breaks the silence. A purse comes flying through the door, striking Lamia's shoulder and knocking her back a few steps.

Lamia: ?!

Lamia: W-what are you doing here?

Relative: Ha, to seek justice for my baby boy, of course! I have to thank all your fans for sharing clips of you online. Why else would you take this route, if not to come to this hospital to see my son?

As opposed to "seeking justice for her son," the real purpose of her visit seems to be seeking compensation from Lamia.

Lamia: Uh huh.

Lamia sneers coldly. When she lifts up her head again, it's as if she's a totally different person. Dripping with icy scorn, she locks eyes with the woman without a hint of fear.

Lamia: What did I do? I just gave him a taste of what I went through.

The woman glances at you in a fluster, then puts her hands on her hips and raises her voice.

Relative: Don't slander us! Haven't we been kind enough to you? Who else would have taken in a freak like you?

Relative: We fed you and sheltered you. Shouldn't you be grateful, obedient and do whatever you can to thank us?

Relative: If it weren't for us, you'd be rotting away in some garbage heap. How would you get the glamorous life you have now? What an ungrateful brat!

Relative: S-stay back, or else I'll scream for help!

Lamia's reaction makes the woman back away a few steps. Fear dawns in her eyes as she realizes the girl in front of her is no longer the same as before.

At the same time, your shackles begin to stir—Mania is oozing from Lamia and whirling in the air.

Chief: Lamia! Calm down!

Lamia turns a deaf ear to your warning. She closes in on the cornered woman like a predator eyeing her prey, slowly raising her arm.

Lamia: Come on, hit me. Weren't you so good at that before?

Lamia: ...What's wrong? Too scared to do it now? Cowards like you who fear the strong and bully the weak really disgust me.

Lamia: Both you and your son can go to hell!

Chief: Ready to tell the truth this time?

Lamia: You've already seen my true colors. No need for me to keep acting, right?

The gray-skinned girl smiles at you from behind the glass of the detainment cell. She looks relaxed and at ease, totally different from her previous self.

Her mental state is much better than you expected—totally unlike someone who had been confined for two days.

Lamia: Ah... I must say, I can finally be myself now. It's so nice to ditch my mask.

Lamia: But I'm curious, when did you start doubting me?

Chief: Right after the interrogation. You shouldn't be that clumsy at lying—it's what you do for a living, isn't it? The fact that you deliberately exposed yourself... means that you want the poor Chief to match your pace.

Lamia: Even if I didn't do that, you'd still be able to match my pace.

She sighs dramatically, yet there's no hint of regret in her tone.

Lamia: But you know, my piteous persona worked really well with that old fart. In all my years in the Society, he never suspected a thing. Do you think it's because he's too stupid, or because you're too smart?

Chief: Enough with the banter. Let's cut to the chase.

Lamia: All right, what do you want to know about? Let me guess... my cousin?

Lamia: Hahaha, I see. Chatting with a smart one really saves time.

Lamia: Oh?
