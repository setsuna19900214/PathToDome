# Cinnabar Interrogation

## Cinnabar - Interrogation I

> The interrogation went surprisingly well. Finally, you have a rough idea of Cinnabar's past.
> The security operation is taking place next week. Both you and Cinnabar must get ready.

Nightingale: Got a call for you, Chief.

Nightingale passes the receiver to you. It's a call from Serpent Eye, a renowned private military company regularly hired by Eastside's upper class and highly reputed within the industry.

Man's Voice: My name is Alyosha. I'm in charge of Serpent Eye.

A man's voice comes through the phone. It sounds old, low, and slightly accented.

The same man phoned MBCC's office a few minutes ago, refused to reveal any information to anyone, not even to Nightingale, and demanded to speak with you—the Chief of MBCC—directly.

Chief: Hello, this is the Chief of MBCC. May I know your purpose of calling?

Alyosha: I need to requisition someone.

Chief: Requisition a Sinner?

Chief: For what purpose?

Alyosha: Deputy Speaker Reagan is going on a tour soon. He's appointed Cinnabar as his bodyguard.

Chief: With all due respect, requisitioning a Sinner to protect an official is not exactly a sensible request. In terms of both ability and emotion, Sinners are highly unstable...

Alyosha: Cinnabar is different!

Alyosha: ...Cinnabar is an elite in the industry. It's Mr. Reagan's very idea to requisition her.

Alyosha: The requisition document will reach you soon.

A disconnect tone from the receiver. Alyosha has hung up.

Cinnabar: I'm ready. You may interrogate me whenever you want.

Cinnabar initiates the conversation in the interrogation room. With no display of aggression, she's simply sitting there, straight-backed and with a sincere look on her face, looking like a friend who's ready to listen to your rant.

Except that she's not your friend. This subject named Cinnabar has had strict military training and is pretty famous among the mercenary and security industries. Her mild exterior could be nothing more than a disguise.

**【Choose One】**

- I need to learn about your past.

- Relax, it's only a chat.

**end**

Cinnabar: Are you referring to the crimes I've committed?

Chief: Not entirely. I'd like you to tell me more about yourself.

Cinnabar snickers, probably unconvinced by your pretext.

Cinnabar: Sure. Where should we start?

Cinnabar's friendly attitude makes you feel at ease. You pick up the first document on the desk.

From the 100% assignment completion during her early mercenary days, to the later zero on-duty casualty rate as a security consultant for Eastside's City Council, Cinnabar's entire career has been impeccably successful. Her résumé is too perfect to pique anyone's interest in discussing it.

You flip through the pages for a few minutes and finally spot an intriguing *talking point*.

**【Advance】**

- Talk about this curious case

**end**

Chief: On February 14th, N.F. 106, you single-handedly rescued Councilor Pend's daughter from a dozen of kidnappers?

Cinnabar considers it briefly.

Cinnabar: Do you mean Miss Hilda?

Cinnabar: Indeed, I took part in her rescue, but I didn't do it all by myself. My teammates at Serpent Eye supported me with crucial intelligence.

You take another glance at the document. Here comes the best part of the case.

Chief: The incident received long-lasting attention from Eastside's media. I assume you also got your fifteen minutes of fame through all this?

Cinnabar: For people in my line of work, it's not considered a bad thing to be known for your work performance.

Chief: But the report says you're well-known mainly because... Miss Pend confessed her love for you on TV?

Cinnabar flushes instantly.

Cinnabar: I... Ugh, Miss Hilda probably mistook me for a man. I clarified that with her afterward...

Chief: Is it? Earlier, when you were taken in by MBCC, she even wrote us a protest letter demanding your immediate release.

Cinnabar: ...

Chief: Besides, it seems that among those in high places in Eastside, quite a few young ladies have a thing for you.

Cinnabar: ...

Cinnabar: All I can say is that... I'm grateful for their kindness. But... regretfully, I don't hold affections of this sort for my clients. I find that irresponsible and unprofessional.

Cinnabar's reaction is amusing. But enough of awkward talks. Time is running out, and you still need to pry more information out of her.

**【Advance】**

- Ask about the incident two years ago

**end**

Chief: It seems that up until two years ago, you'd been much appreciated by Eastside's powerful and rich.

As the new subject is brought up, Cinnabar, who has been a little flustered just now, quickly collects herself.

Cinnabar: I'm truly grateful to my clients who trusted me fully, but protecting them was only my duty, regardless of their appreciation for me.

Cinnabar's response is intriguing. Whatever happened two years ago should be no simple matter.

The document describes a riot that erupted on Pasture Block in Syndicate two years ago. Neither the cause nor the outcome of the riot is clearly elaborated, and the only detailed part of the record suggests that "a security consultant was absent without leave when escorting the delegation from Eastside, and was subsequently wanted for turning into a Sinner."

Reading the document you've passed to her, Cinnabar gives a wry smile.

Cinnabar: Is that how they interpreted the situation...

**【Choose One】**

- Question Cinnabar's decision

- Question the clients' action

**end**

Chief: You were hired by the City Council, but went AWOL at the critical moment. That was out of character for you. What was your defense?

Cinnabar: I didn't have any. It happened as recorded in the report. I deserted my unit alone and left the delegation to my teammates at Serpent Eye.

Cinnabar: After that, I became a Sinner... I won't deny having committed those crimes—but I also do not regret committing them.

Chief: Dismissed from all job duties and wanted by the authorities for one single incidence of AWOL, the bigwigs of Eastside had no mercy on you at all, did they?

Cinnabar: That's not true. I indeed betrayed the trust of my clients, leaving them to my teammates at Serpent Eye.

Cinnabar: During my desertion, I was infected with the Mania. But even so, I tried to return to the City Council's station, causing a panic... That really shouldn't have been the behavior of a professional.

Cinnabar's response does not offer any explanations for her initial motive.

Chief: Could you tell me the reason why you deserted the unit?

Cinnabar: ...

Cinnabar: ...It was a gang war. Gang members intruded on a civilian block.

Cinnabar: I'd seen their brutal way. The low-ranking gangsters would not spare a target just because it was a woman or child...

Cinnabar: I couldn't just leave the civilians like that.

Cinnabar pauses, then continues.

Cinnabar: Their target wasn't the delegation, and a small gang of thugs wouldn't have posed a real threat anyway. That was why I judged that it was safe to leave the delegation to my colleagues.

**【Advance】**

- How did it turn out?

**end**

Cinnabar gives a gratified smile.

Cinnabar: I saved quite a few people.

Cinnabar: It hadn't been without incident, but what happened didn't change the outcome. What mattered the most was that they all survived.

Chief: In exchange, you became a Sinner, lost your job, and were wanted by the City Council...

Cinnabar: Given another chance, I would still make the same choice.

Cinnabar's description does not contradict the facts you know. It also explains why the City Council's document conceals what happened before the misfeasance.

Looking back, the City Council most likely wanted to make an example of her. Now that the controversy has died down, it makes sense to want to put Cinnabar back to work.

**【Advance】**

- You still have a chance to go back.

**end**

Chief: Your ability is undeniable. They shouldn't have let you go like that.

Cinnabar: Some things cannot be undone.

Cinnabar: Even if the City Council is willing to turn a blind eye to my past, my teammates... my colleagues at Serpent Eye will never forgive me.

Cinnabar: Serpent Eye suffered a huge loss for my action. Whether I did it to protect the civilians or for whatever other reason, betrayal is betrayal.

Cinnabar casts her gaze aside. Her eyes are dark.

Cinnabar: One cannot handle this job alone. Yet, I let down my comrades who trusted me with their lives...

She is wrong. Serpent Eye has not turned its back on her.

**【Advance】**

- Tell her about Alyosha's call

**end**

Chief: Earlier today, I received a call from a man named Alyosha.

Cinnabar: Alyosha?!

Cinnabar's eyes light up, even her body is beginning to lean forward.

Chief: He claimed to work for Serpent Eye. Do you know him?

Cinnabar: He is one of the founders of Serpent Eye, as well as the captain. How... is he doing? Is everything alright at Serpent Eye?

Chief: You haven't been in touch for long?

Cinnabar: No...

Chief: Serpent Eye is still the security company to serve the City Council and Eastside's bigwigs. Alyosha sounded well too.

Cinnabar: I see...

Chief: He's made a pretty unreasonable request, concerning you.

Cinnabar blinks, confused.

Chief: He wants me to let you out, so you can take care of a mission.

Chief: Serpent Eye wants to requisition you to protect the current Deputy Speaker of the City Council.

Chief: The Deputy Speaker plans to give speeches in Eastside soon. Just recently, he's received a death threat.

Despite having left her position for years, as an elite in the industry, Cinnabar's instinct kicks in quickly.

Cinnabar: Does the intelligence have any clues yet?

Chief: No. The guy sends threatening letters to Reagan's personal terminal every day, using virtual IPs. The intelligence hasn't been able to trace any useful information.

After thinking for a moment, Cinnabar promptly gives her professional advice.

Cinnabar: The person sending the threats is good with technology, shortwave communication equipment and an EMP device will be necessary. Hmm... We must also beware of remote bombs...

Chief: Looks like you're all ready.

Cinnabar lifts her head and looks dazed for a moment.

Cinnabar: ...I'm a Sinner. I work for you, I shouldn't go outside for personal matters.

Chief: Since the assassination of a councilor by a Maniac eight years ago, MBCC has been obliged to provide security support for any events involving officials. Of course, only when the organizer requires it. It won't be a personal matter if we send you.

Cinnabar: Serpent Eye is capable of protecting Reagan. I shouldn't be needed.

Cinnabar seems hesitant, perhaps not yet prepared to meet her old teammates.

Chief: My initial response to Alyosha was this: "Are you sure she can pull off such an important mission? Don't forget she's a Sinner who requires constant supervision."

Chief: But Alyosha said, "Cinnabar is different from the other Sinners!"

Chief: And these résumés on the desk—who do you think sent them?

Cinnabar: I...

Chief: Give your colleagues the trust they deserve. They very likely fought for this mission, for you.

Chief: Maybe they've long forgiven you. It's just that you haven't forgiven yourself.

After a brief moment of silence, Cinnabar's eyes are filled with determination.

Cinnabar: If this is truly what Alyosha and the others wish for... then I shouldn't let them down again.

Cinnabar: I accept this mission.

Chief: Then it's all set. By the way, out of MBCC's supervisory duty, I must accompany you throughout. After all...

Cinnabar: I understand.

Cinnabar: Rest assured, I will obey the rules.

Cinnabar: Rest assured, I will remain in your sight.

## Cinnabar - Interrogation II

> No one expected that this would end up like this. Though only a few were injured on site and the attacker was captured, it is still a troubling issue how Cinnabar and Serpent Eye went on their separate ways.
> Perhaps you should do something about it.

Female Stranger: Hello! If you don't mind, may I have your contact please?

Cinnabar: I... Ugh...

As you come backstage, you happen to see a female staff leaning close to Cinnabar.

Bewildered, Cinnabar shoots a helpless look at you.

**【Advance】**

- Help Cinnabar out

**end**

Chief: Excuse me, I have an important task to discuss with her. Can I borrow her for a minute?

Female Stranger: *Her*?!

The staff leaves in shock. Cinnabar lets out a sigh of relief at last.

Cinnabar: Thank you.

Chief: Don't mention it. The speech will begin in 20 minutes, are you ready?

Cinnabar: Serpent Eye is still doing the last round of security checks. They'll then take over from me here, and I'll be in charge of on-site staff deployment...

She's barely finished her sentence as you hear footsteps in the corridor. A man in his fifties is striding toward you.

Broad and strong, the man is more than a head taller than you and wears several terrifying scars on his face that will scare off most people. As he approaches, Cinnabar immediately stands up.

Cinnabar: Captain, is everything okay on-site?

From Cinnabar's slightly nervous tone, you learn his identity—the man before you is Alyosha.

Alyosha: Security check on the 172 audience members is complete. The EMP device has been set up on site as well.

Alyosha: Besides, there are five of Reagan's VIPs. Serpent Eye is not responsible for their security check.

Cinnabar: Can they at least... Never mind.

Cinnabar: I'll keep an eye on them.

Cinnabar says no more and simply lets out a sigh. It's not their place to question anything with political motives behind it.

Cinnabar: Captain...

Alyosha: Yes?

Alyosha interrupts Cinnabar's question with a frown, as if unwilling to utter even one word irrelevant to the mission. Cinnabar bows her head like a child who's done something wrong.

**【Advance】**

- Try to lighten the atmosphere

**end**

Chief: I believe in your ability, Cinnabar. You seem all tensed up, try to relax a bit.

Alyosha turns to look at you. His gaze is piercing, like a big cat zeroing in on its prey.

Also sensing his hostility toward you, Cinnabar steps forward to put herself before you.

Cinnabar: Captain, the Chief is here to assist us.

Cinnabar: Captain, please don't... treat my friend like that.

Alyosha narrows his eyes and glances at Cinnabar, then at you. A few seconds later, he turns and walks away wordlessly.

Cinnabar: I'm sorry, Alyosha... is not very sociable.

Chief: Don't be sorry, I can see that he's very responsible. You can never be too careful, right?

Reassured by your understanding, Cinnabar smiles. She glances up at the clock, then turns around to knock on the door of Reagan's lounge.

Cinnabar: Mr. Reagan, it's about time...

Before Cinnabar finishes, the lounge's door is opened.

Crowded by bodyguards in black suits, a well-dressed man walks straight in the direction of the stage without even sparing you two a single look.

Cinnabar signals you to follow Reagan to the venue with her.

Cinnabar grips your hand, and you two follow Reagan to the venue.

You take the first seat on the left side of the front row. As the security manager, Cinnabar stands beside you.

On the stage, Reagan begins his speech.

**【Advance】**

- Sit down and listen to the speech

**end**

It is apparent that Reagan is an excellent public speaker. His logic is clear, his emotions compelling and his speech convincing.

For the better part of an hour, he presents the plight faced by certain blocks in Syndicate, and the efforts he has made in order to solve the problem.

Toward the end of his speech, Reagan begins to give his thanks to those who support his project.

Reagan: ...These benevolent gentlemen have endorsed my Home Reconstruction Project and built a new community for the Syndicans that have lost their homes.

Reagan: They have won respect from all Syndicans, as well as admiration from me.

Reagan: Now, let us welcome them onto the stage, and allow me to have the honor to personally award them with the Medals of Benevolence issued by the City Council!

Greeted by passionate music and loud applause from the audience, several well-known Eastside real estate tycoons—the *VIPs* Alyosha has mentioned—stroll onto the stage from the VIP lounge.

Chief: Do you know these people?

Prior to being taken in by MBCC, Cinnabar lived in Syndicate. If the truth is really as Reagan said, Cinnabar is likely to know these people.

Cinnabar: I don't know them.

Cinnabar: Also... I've never heard of any Home Reconstruction Project. But I've heard that a recently constructed residential block has collapsed due to its weak foundation, and many have died in the accident... Hold on, something seems wrong with him!

You follow Cinnabar's finger and look toward the stage, one of the developers' faces is ashen, his lips are trembling, and he's striding toward Reagan.

Clack!

Before Cinnabar even has time to rush forward, the developer pulls out a pair of handcuffs from his clothes and cuffs himself and Reagan together.

Developer: I'm so sorry, Mr. Reagan... Please don't move, I beg you, please do not move, or we'll both die! That guy's installed a bomb on me...

After a brief confused moment, screams explode across the entire venue. An immense panic descends on the audience within seconds.

People are screaming and jostling against each other. You hear a child's cry from behind, but the moment you turn around, you are pushed onto the floor by the mass of people scrambling toward the emergency exit.

You try your best to get back up, but a pair of stomping feet on your back prevents that. Right at that moment, you feel a hand grab your forearm firmly.

Cinnabar: Hold onto me, don't let go!

Hearing Cinnabar's voice, you react immediately and clutch onto her arm, helping yourself to your feet.

The two of you wade through the flow of people and soon reach the base of the stage. You hear loud, raucous noise coming from the stage.

You try your best to get back up, but a pair of stomping feet on your back prevents that, and the following flow of people seems to have no end.

Cinnabar: Hey!

Against the icy blue screen, Cinnabar shields you from the crowd, facing you with her back. Turning to you slightly, she reaches out an arm.

**【Advance】**

- Catch her

**end**

You react immediately and clutch onto her hand, helping yourself to your feet.

Cinnabar: ...Are you hurt?

Chief: I'm fine. You need to stop what's happening on the stage!

Cinnabar: Don't worry, I'll handle it. You should get out of here first.

Cinnabar wraps her left arm tightly around most of your body, pushing the shield forward with her right arm to divide the crowd.

Cinnabar: Hold onto me, don't let go!

The two of you wade through the flow of people. You've lost track of time, until you finally hear loud, raucous noise coming from the stage.

Synthesized Voice: ...Reagan, you have only one choice: allocate funds at once to compensate all the families of the victims who died in the collapse of the buildings in Pasture Block.

Synthesized Voice: Take this as your final chance of atonement, or I'll blow you and this vermin up.

A synthetic voice comes out of the speaker attached to the developer's collar. He unbuttons his suit jacket to reveal the communication device beneath and the bomb around his waist.

Developer: I... I can give back the money I've taken, please don't kill me, I have kids...

Reagan stares at the developer coldly and remains silent.

Synthesized Voice: You have two minutes to decide.

Reagan still hasn't spoken.

Cinnabar: There is a fifteen-minute delay on the live TV broadcast, and everyone in the control room works for Serpent Eye... The attacker must be on site now.

Cinnabar: The attacker needs to observe everything on the stage at all times. The back rows near the exit are crowded with people, you can't see the stage clearly from there...

As Cinnabar reaches the end of her analysis, you meet each other's gaze, then each begins immediately to scan one half of the venue with unspoken coordination.

Soon, a trembling figure squatting under a desk captures your attention. The scrawny body is shaking violently, as if immobilized by fear. But in fact, he keeps peeking up at the stage all the while.

Your eyes meet. He quickly looks away even as you see a chilling light flash over his gaze.

**【Advance】**

- Notify Cinnabar

**end**

Chief: The fourth seat in the sixth row, under the seat!

Synthesized Voice: Time's up. Repay the dead with your lives!

Cinnabar: Launch the EMP!

Through the shortwave communication equipment, the Serpent Eye team receives the order. They immediately launch the EMP device. The strong EMP shock causes all electronic equipment to fail at once. Darkness falls upon the venue.

The next moment, Cinnabar leaps past the crowd, takes hold of the arms of the attacker who's about to escape, and subdues him on the floor. The detonator falls out of his hand and is stamped over by the crowd, turning into scrap metal.

Cinnabar: Target captured!

Half a minute later, you hear the sound of the uninterruptible power supply turning on, and the venue is lit up again.

**【Advance】**

- Check on the attacker

**end**

You quickly make your way to Cinnabar, and find that the person being pressed on the floor has a rather youthful face—the attacker is a teenager.

Chief: Well done, Cinnabar!

Several Serpent Eye members come over to pass some nylon ties to Cinnabar, with which she efficiently ties the attacker up.

Once her hands are free, Cinnabar's first reaction is to search for something in the busy crowd. When she sees that you're safe, she finally breathes a sigh of relief.

On the stage, Alyosha breaks the handcuffs with a pair of hydraulic tongs. Reagan rubs his reddened wrist with an undecipherable expression, then suddenly pulls out the gun from Alyosha's holster.

Bang! Bang!

As the gunshot dies down, the developer is found lying on the floor. His body twitches a few times, then goes completely still.

Reagan, having just killed a person, draws in a deep breath, lets it out slowly, and gives a perfect *high society smile* as he turns to Alyosha.

Reagan: Care to explain?

Alyosha: I'm really sorry.

Alyosha's face is ashy, but Reagan simply pats him lightly on the shoulder.

Reagan: I don't need someone who apologizes all the time.

Reagan: I prefer doers who deliver solid work.

He shoves the handgun back into Alyosha's hand, glances down once at the attacker on the floor, then makes for the emergency exit, escorted by two bodyguards.

Attacker: You son of a bitch, come back, I'm going to kill you! Even if we die, all of us Syndicans will come back to haunt you!

On the floor, the attacker is cursing while straining to lift his head. Reagan halts his steps and turns around, but not to respond to him.

Reagan: By the way, Cinnabar.

Reagan is smiling. For some reason, the smile feels very unpleasant.

Reagan: Who'd have thought, after two years of rolling in the dirt in Syndicate, even after you turned into a Sinner, you're still impressively good...

Reagan: Eastside is hungry for talents... Who knows? Even a filthy street rat like you might be useful somewhere.

Having said that, Reagan leaves without turning back again.

Within a few minutes, the audiences have all been evacuated under Serpent Eye's instructions. Those who remain are shrouded by an unsettling silence, only the attacker is still cursing hysterically.

Alyosha doesn't say a word as he comes slowly in your direction. He hasn't replaced his gun in his holster. The heavy footsteps make your chest feel tight.

Attacker: Go to hell, you spineless bastards!

The attacker is wriggling frantically on the floor. Alyosha stops in front of him.

Alyosha: Cinnabar, hold him down.

Everyone present understands Alyosha's purpose, but no one objects, except Cinnabar.

Cinnabar: Captain...

Bang!

Alyosha doesn't let Cinnabar finish and simply pulls the trigger. But as the gunshot rings in the air, the attacker is still alive. Terrified, he has shut his eyes. The bullet drops before his face.

Blue light is floating around the attacker. Cinnabar has expanded the shield and protected him.

Alyosha: CIN—NA—BAR!

The muscles on Alyosha's face are twitching.

Alyosha: This is the client's order... Hand him over to me.

Cinnabar passes her eyes over the young attacker on the floor, grits her teeth, and lifts her head to face Alyosha.

Cinnabar: I can't do that.

The shield still separates the two of them.

Alyosha: Do you have any idea how much it's cost Serpent Eye to make this happen?!

Alyosha takes a deep breath and suppresses the anger that threatens to explode any second.

Alyosha: Whatever happened in the past, I can overlook it. Now, get out of my way, don't make me say it a third time.

**【Advance】**

- Help Cinnabar

**end**

The surrounding Serpent Eye teammates are all staring at Cinnabar, but no one says anything.

If you don't help Cinnabar, no one will.

Chief: You don't have the right to kill him, Alyosha.

Alyosha doesn't budge, doesn't so much as look at you once. His gaze remains on Cinnabar still.

Chief: The attacker no longer poses any threat. According to the law, he must be trialed in court. Lynching is...

Alyosha: Shut up!

Alyosha growls at you. He looks furious, like a raging lion.

Alyosha: You're just an outsider, you have no business meddling in Serpent Eye's affairs!

Cinnabar: He is not an outsider.

Cinnabar: She is... not an outsider!

Alyosha is suddenly wide-eyed. For a moment, shock overpowers anger.

Cinnabar: The Chief has always been by my side... The Chief is my friend.

Cinnabar: She has been helping me and taking care of me... She is my... Friend! She's important to me!

Alyosha: So... you mean to say you're willing to betray Serpent Eye again for this *friend*?!

Cinnabar: I don't want to betray Serpent Eye, Alyosha, I've never once thought of that...

Cinnabar: Loyalty, courage, and determination... These are what Dad, José... and you have taught me. I've never forgotten them.

Cinnabar's chest is heaving intensely.

Cinnabar: But have you ever considered that, besides these, there are other more important and righteous things in the world?

Cinnabar: What's more important than a client's order is morality, justice, and the lives of innocent people...

Alyosha: For these obscure ideas, are you going to give up your career and Serpent Eye, stay in that shitty place, and work a job with no future, till the day you... die?

Cinnabar: I'm really sorry for what happened two years ago... but I do not regret it.

Cinnabar: To strive for these so-called obscure ideas, I'm willing to...

Alyosha: Enough.

Alyosha interrupts Cinnabar. He stands still before her, the anger has vanished from his eyes, leaving behind only weariness on his face.

After a long silence, he moves the handgun away.

Alyosha: Suit yourself.

Ignoring the sadness in Cinnabar's eyes, Alyosha turns around and heads toward the exit. The heavy footsteps echo in the empty hall like distant thunder.

If you let him go like this, Cinnabar will probably never get to meet him again.

**【Advance】**

- Try to make Alyosha stay

**end**

Chief: Hold on, Alyosha... Cinnabar has always cared about your team, cared about you.

Alyosha comes to a halt.

Chief: When she learned you'd phoned me, her first reaction was to ask: "How is Alyosha doing?"...

Chief: When you feel ready, have a talk with her. Give her a chance, and give yourself a chance too.

Alyosha starts a little at that. Before leaving, he turns back to look at you with a complex expression, but no words come out of him.

After everyone from Serpent Eye has left, Cinnabar retracts the shield.

Expanding the shield for such a long time has worn Cinnabar out. Having retracted the shield, she leans her back on the wall and slowly slides down onto the floor.

**【Advance】**

- Are you okay?

**end**

Cinnabar: I'm fine.

She doesn't sound *fine* at all.

Chief: Alyosha probably just needs some time.

Cinnabar: ...

You step up and steady her wobbly body.

Cinnabar lifts her head, her eyes are full of tears, and her body is shaking uncontrollably.

You sigh and pat her on the shoulder.

Cinnabar lifts her head, her eyes are full of tears. Suddenly, she wraps her arms around you. You can clearly feel her whole body shaking.

You sigh and pat her on the back.

Chief: Whatever happens, I'm on your side.

Cinnabar: ...Thank you.

## Cinnabar - Interrogation III

> Right before you part ways, you and the people of Serpent Eye exchanged your business cards. They promised to keep in touch with you and Cinnabar.
> You saw a happy smile on Cinnabar's face. And that is all that matters.

Two months have passed since the attack at the speech.

A lot has happened within these two months: The attacker has been detained, the Syndican block's residents have received compensation, several developers have gone bankrupt... The incident has a fairly *satisfying* ending.

Except for...

Cinnabar: The search warrant concerning Reagan still hasn't come through yet?

Chief: Reagan's background is far more complicated than we imagined. It's already a miracle to have been able to bypass him and compensate the Syndican residents.

You understand Cinnabar's concern. After all, as long as Reagan is still in power, Serpent Eye can suffer retaliation anytime.

**【Advance】**

- Comfort Cinnabar

**end**

Chief: I'll give another call to Serpent Eye.

You've already phoned Serpent Eye several times. As you've expected, no one picks up this time either.

You've lost touch with Serpent Eye.

Watching your perplexed face, Cinnabar begins to comfort you instead.

Cinnabar: Take it easy, I'm sure they're fine.

Three days later

Chief: Alyosha?!

Nightingale looks at you quizzically, unsure why you seem so agitated by the call from this Alyosha guy.

You take the phone from Nightingale.

Chief: Captain Alyosha? I haven't been able to reach you by phone. How's everything going for you?

Alyosha: Serpent Eye's number has changed.

Alyosha: Get Cinnabar on the phone.

That's just typical of Alyosha to not give you any respect, but now is not the time to be petty.

**【Advance】**

- Get Cinnabar on the phone

**end**

When Cinnabar learns that Serpent Eye is on the phone, she drops everything and rushes to your office.

Cinnabar takes the phone from your hand, pauses, and turns on the speaker.

Cinnabar: How have you been, Alyosha? Did Reagan give you any trouble?

Alyosha: Serpent Eye isn't working with City Council now.
