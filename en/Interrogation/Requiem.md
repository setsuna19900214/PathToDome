# Requiem Interrogation

## Requiem - Interrogation I

> Intelligence before completion:
> On August 4th, N.F. 116, the Underground virtuoso Requiem was secretly detained. Half a month prior to this, the Mania musical scores associated with her had spread widely throughout Eastside, causing numerous casualties. Since her detainment, multiple rounds of Interrogation have been conducted. Today, you once again step into that special Mania detainment facility located underground...

> Intelligence after completion:
> The virtuoso never willingly walked toward the Underground. She was chosen by it, forced into becoming a disseminator of the Cursed Melody.
> You don't know why the Underground chose Requiem, or how they took her, but you decide to help her untangle her chaotic memories and break free from the indoctrination.

August 5, N.F. 116

Underground, Undisclosed Location, Outside MBCC

Special Mania Containment Facility
