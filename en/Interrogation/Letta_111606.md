# Letta Interrogation

> And Letta, who has left home of her own accord, can't wait to embark on an adventure of her own. You had to agree to go with her.

When the Interrogation Room is lit up, the girl hugging the lantern still has her head tilted in a sweet slumber.

Letta: I did!

Letta: I could only retreat step by step with my lantern in my arms, until I was cornered into an alley by the Corruptors. At the moment they were about to reach me, I closed my eyes, and then...

With that, Letta flashes a cute, demure smile, but her eyes are filled with a prankster's satisfaction. The truth is plain as day now.

Faced with Corruptors during her escape on a foggy night, Letta unleashed her potential with her immense survival instinct. With the Hypercubes' guidance, she eventually awakened as a Sinner and saved herself from danger.

Grounded against her will, she frequently used this convenient power to sneak out and wandered in the fog, pranking and guiding lost passersby ignorant of the truth. That's the origin of the lantern ghost.

## Letta - Interrogation II

> After a series of oopsies about fake flowers in the garden, mistakenly entering a factory, and misplace confidence in an ad, you expected Letta to be lost and sad, but she remained enthusiastic, and said she had no regrets about her choice.
> To bring the adventure to a successful conclusion, you decide to continue to the last location.

Letta: According to the guide... this is our first destination today, the *ever-blooming flower field*!

Chief: But isn't this just a small park...?

You and Letta stand in front of a nameless park in an Eastside suburb, staring at each other.

Letta: Perhaps the flower field lies in this park...?

Chief: Possibly... But given the season, it's unlikely we'll see any flower field. Maybe that's why it's a *strange tale*.

Letta: Hmm... Let's go in and have a look!

???: Are you here to visit? Welcome!

Chief: Hello, we were wondering if, um... is this the *ever-blooming flower field*?

Staff Member: You're exactly right! Here, you'll enter a fairyland where flowers bloom in all seasons! To experience the miraculous scenery, you need only...

**end**

The park is not exactly big. After taking a small path, you see a field of vivid colors distantly.

Letta: Wow! That's the flower field...

Excited, she wants to get closer, but is immediately stopped by the straight-faced staff member.

Staff Member: Please don't go any closer, Miss. This flower field can only be viewed at a distance. Once the flowers are tainted with human scent, they'll wilt instantly... and this magnificent scenery will be no more!

Letta: Oh, I remember now, the strange tale does mention that. Too bad then...

Letta halts her steps awkwardly. Something feels off to you.

Letta: Ah, the wind...

Staff Member: Ah! My flower field!

You follow him and finally see the truth of the flower field. They're merely fake flowers propped up in the soil.

Chief: ...

Letta: I see, the ever-blooming flowers... are fake...

Staff Member: Sh-shush!

He refutes defensively as he tries to set the collapsed fake flowers right. But as soon as he fixes a bunch, the other ones get blown down again, so his effort is all wasted.

Chief: Don't waste your time. Those at the back have all gone down too.

You point at the fake flowers facing down on the distant slope. Finally, the staff member gives up and slumps down on the ground wearily.

Staff Member: I'm so sorry. As you can see, the ever-blooming flower field doesn't exist... The flowers are not real.

Staff Member: This park has poor operations and mediocre landscapes. It's going out of business.

Staff Member: But I wanted to make a final attempt. I pulled some strings and got these fake flowers that were going to the landfill and created the fake scenery...

Staff Member: Then I made up a lot of exaggerated rumors and spread them as much as possible, hoping to attract more visitors and sell some tickets.

Staff Member: Once I earn enough money, I can buy some seedlings and grow a real flower field...

Staff Member: But for now, the fake flowers remain fake... I'm really sorry. Please forgive me!

His honest confession somehow leaves you speechless.

Letta is still staring blankly at the *flower field*, as if still processing the disappointment from her ruined fantasy.

Chief: Letta, what do you think?

Letta: ...Oh, me? Well, it is what is it! I'm fine!

She snaps out of the daze and gestures to the staff member indifferently.

Letta: The park is still beautiful... I hope the next time we visit, we'll see a real flower field!

You say goodbye to the park's staff member. Letta leads you to the next destination enthusiastically, the *steel maze of the roaring monster*.

This is a rooftop piled with construction materials and pipes. The metal lies silently in the dark and divides the space into several dim diverging paths, indeed making the place seem like a maze.

Letta: Legend has it that when the night falls, the monster in the heart of the maze roars furiously to intimidate all intruding enemies...

Chief: But I hear no roars...

Just as you say that, the ground beneath your feet suddenly vibrates and the pipes around you begin rumbling.

Letta: ...Listen! The monster's roaring!

Letta feels the pipes carefully to detect the source of the roar and immediately starts going in that direction excitedly.

<ins>Letta: Come, Mr. Chief, let's go find the monster!</ins><br><ins>Letta: Come, Miss Chief, let's go find the monster!</ins>

Having been fooled by the previous strange tale, you don't have high expectations for the remaining destinations. But the environment here is kind of spooky... Perhaps it'll prove slightly more legit than the fake flowers at the park?

Letta: The path ends here... Oh, a door?

Fumbling along the pipes, you reach the end of the *maze* and find a door on the wall that seems to lead downstairs.

- Going in?

**end**

The lantern flickers. Letta holds your hand and leads you through that locked door.

Through your linked hands, the shackles pass all of Letta's excitement and anxiety to you.

Letta: I'm so excited... Chief, what do you think we'll see this time?

Letta: Is that the monster...?
