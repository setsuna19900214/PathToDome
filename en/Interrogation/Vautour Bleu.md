# Vautour Bleu Interrogation

## Vautour Bleu - Interrogation I

> Since returning to WhiteSands from Désir, Vautour Bleu seems to be thriving, her merchant caravan a picture of success. Yet her tip-off to you raise only more questions—
> A merchant never deals in losses, so what could she stand to gain? You accept her down payment and proceed as she said, but questions linger in your mind.

To my gray-cloaked partner,

Hail and well met.

The woman pens the opening lines of her letter in the light of the rising sun. Another trading day begins, the camp gradually filling with commotion, amid the sounds of cargo being unloaded, haggling, and crass arguments.

Yet there she sits at the heart of this bustling scene, basking in the sun, writing her letter with elegant composure.

Local A: Whoa, these folks must be new around here. Haven't seen a woman leading a caravan in ages... Really hopped up, aren't they? That woman must get some serious chops!

Local B: Shhh, shut it! Who gave you the nerve to run your mouth about their boss, eh? Don't say I didn't warn you. If they hear what you're saying, you can kiss that tongue of yours goodbye!

The two locals who regularly hang around the camp looking for gigs and freebies shuffle past, their eyes darting away from Vautour Bleu as if scalded. Yet she doesn't even bother to turn her head.

Accountant: Spices, all in Section A. Fuel's been loaded too... Huh, why aren't these numbers adding up?

Accountant: Ah, n-no, Boss, nothing! Rest assured! I'll make sure everything balances before we leave. Or you can take me as the penalty!

Vautour Bleu: What good would you be to me? There's hardly enough meat on your bones to be worth anything.

When Vautour Bleu waves her hand dismissively, the accountant scurries away with the notebook, grateful for the reprieve after being caught making a mistake.

Deputy Captain: Hey, Eiss! Have you seen Lyman?

Assistant: Wha—no! Probably passed out somewhere. Stop bothering me, I've got to see Boss!

With that, the short teenager bounces urgently toward Vautour Bleu in her lounge chair, grinning as he gives a playful salute.

Assistant: Morning, Boss! Here to report the spend! And I've got a few things to run by you too, hehe!

Assistant: First of all, about yesterday's diner for the crew. I was gonna put it on our book, but Finn insisted it was all on him 'cause he's local here... Said we can pay him back double once we calculate the profits, so we didn't spend a single penny!

Assistant: Then, the raw materials for color-shifting ink were cheaper than last time, so we bought nine extra crates. Also got three pressing machines...

Vautour Bleu: Engraving machines.

The woman, sorting the envelopes in her hands, corrects him offhandedly.

Assistant: Understood! The engraving machines are grouped with the turquoise, blue amber, and other jewelry in Section D. We'll unload everything together at the next stop. Also, we've scheduled one day for resupply. We plan to depart tomorrow morning. Your approval?

Vautour Bleu: It's settled, then.

Vautour Bleu: Before we go, mail this letter for me, along with those gifts in the back seat.

The deep blue envelope bears her unique vulture wax seal. It's clearly fancy stuff meant to mail through the exclusive routes.

Assistant: Is this... a letter to DisCity? Holy Boss, you've got connections there too!

A slight smirk tugs at her lips as wisps of smoke drift up, veiling her enigmatic expression.

Vautour Bleu: Connections? Hmph, just a former *neighbor*, nothing more.

To my gray-cloaked friend,

Greetings.

How have you been? I trust our mutual friends are settling in well after following you to DisCity.

Is our little homing dove still rushing about her delivery service? I suppose it's her way of finding herself—I can only hope you've lent her a hand along the way.

Has my freckled little brat learned to hold his tongue? It's better to behave in an unfamiliar place.

Talkative Juan always let his fists do the talking, but I'm sure you have more formidable figures there to keep him in check. And my dear Alba, I hope she rises to become the star she's always meant to be.

I've been trading along the western edge of WhiteSands lately. Seen many new things unknown to me during my more settled days.

I came across desert bandits. Boorish as ever, still hunting in packs and charging at their prey recklessly.

I also met a new tribe with rather unconventional artistic tastes. Trading with them required no words—quite the opposite, in fact. Absolute silence was their preferred language.

I've sent you a few souvenirs to adorn your office. I hope they'd spark interesting conversations with your guests.

I happened to pass by Désir the other day. That place where we spent those fleeting days together has vanished without a trace. Even that balcony we shared is gone with the wind, much like our memories.

Despite that, a shadow of doubt still lingers in your mind.

Vautour Bleu is a merchant for whom profit always comes first. So what drives her to reach out to you, share what she knows, and propose this deal? What does she really want?

You try to recall your time in Désir for clues, but the more you think, the less you figure out. Vautour Bleu's like a mirage in WhiteSands, ethereal, but potentially lethal if touched by.

Chief: Looks like we'll have to deal with it anyway, no matter how suspicious it is... Sitting here overthinking won't do any help.

## Vautour Bleu - Interrogation II

> The gunshots from DisCity's border seem to reach Vautour Bleu's ears. Snapped out of her thoughts, she's informed by her subordinates that she's got new visitors.
> Something's dawned on her then. Compliantly, she agrees to return to DisCity with the FAC for investigation, ready to face the end she has long seen coming.

WhiteSands stretches endlessly to the horizon. The blazing sun pours shimmering heat waves above the scorching sands. No living creature would dare traverse these dunes at this high noon.

Except this man. He's clad in desert gear stolen from the merchant caravan, its thick insulation layer and cooling circulation system allowing him to survive the lonely trek across WhiteSands.

His pack, once bulging with supplies, now hangs nearly empty. Only one last bottle of water and a few hardtacks remain.

Lyman: *Hah... hah... How many days so far? Guess... 19?*

Lyman: *That truck we stole wasn't half bad. Beat up here and there though, still ran fine. Wouldn't have abandoned it if it hadn't run out of fuel.*

Lyman: *Been walking for ages, are we? Lost track of time. Going on foot is slow, but we're used to it, aren't we, Evelo? Just like when we marched against desert bandits and disasters back then.*

The man stops to adjust his pack as if still carrying some heavy burden, tightening the straps across his shoulders. The grotesque scar on the back of his hand is clearly visible.

Lyman: *No weapons, no commanders with a functioning brain... and of course, no Hypercubes. We used to envy DisCity's FAC so much. Heard each of their soldiers got plenty.*

Lyman: *But what could we do? Our poor little town kept gambling on our lives, one after another. After you all died, it didn't take long for the whole party to fall apart... Hah, that's when I finally saw my chance to escape.*

Lyman: *I ran into that woman called Vautour Bleu and her caravan. Said she could take me to DisCity to live that "good life," but only if we make a deal first.*

Lyman: *To hell with her deal, f\*\*\* liar. That woman never planned to go to DisCity. She thinks I'm easy to fool around, huh? Ain't she the most useless one in the caravan?*

His body and mind teeter on the edge of collapse, his legs driven forward by nothing but hollow hope and burning hatred.

Lyman: *I stole their car, got us water and food, even their cargo. Took that navigation device off the car too.*

Lyman: *Once I reach DisCity, I'm gonna sell this gear. That should give us enough money to settle down there.*

Lyman: *If only we'd had gear like this back then... It'd probably have cost an arm and a leg, but at least Belmar would survive.*

"Evelo": That's right. Keep moving, Lyman. You must make it to DisCity. Do it for us.

He rubs his eyes, and through the writhing heat wave, he sees a few swaying familiar silhouettes. They wave to him, and one approaches, reaching out as if to help him up.

"Belmar": Go, Lyman. We can't make it to DisCity, but you will. See that promised land for all of us.

Lyman: *...You bastards.*

Is it their ghosts or a mirage? Or just hallucination? Never mind.

Lyman: *Just don't go yet... Stay with me, a little longer...*

Guard: Sir, we've got someone at the checkpoint. He's from WhiteSands.

Comissioner: Just one person? Not with a caravan? That's a restricted zone. How could he make it through?

Guard: Confirmed, it's just him. No anomalies detected in the surrounding area, his M-value within the safe range. Might be a refugee who got separated from the group.

Comissioner: It could also be a disguised Corruptor, or a spy from Outlands... Stay alert. Let's go and have a look.

At the foot of the border wall, Lyman lifts his head in a daze, confronted by the towering barrier that cuts off everything beyond.

Lyman: So this is... DisCity...

Lyman: Here, our dreams... we're finally here...

Before he becomes aware of the situation, several heavily armed soldiers appear and surround him.

Guard: Name? Age? Where are you from?

Lyman: ...Ly... Leonard. 30 years. I'm an... engineer. I'm from DisCity... was sent to Désir for a commission... I was heading home to visit my family when the desert bandits attacked.

Lyman: Here, my papers. I kept them on me... so the bandits didn't get them...

He recites the details of the fake identity Vautour Bleu forged for him two weeks ago.

Having no idea what kind of place Désir is, he can only dodge the guard's questions about its details by playing up his exhaustion and mental confusion.

Lyman: I'm an engineer from DisCity! My family's waiting for me!

Guard: We'll let you through once we verify your identity. Hold out your hand.

Lyman: What...

He watches stupidly as the guard takes his hand, collecting fingerprints and a blood sample to match with the database. An ominous chill crawls up his spine.

Guard: Sir, your DNA profile says your name's Lyman. You're no engineer. You're an active Outland soldier.

*Clack.* The familiar sound of guns being loaded pierces the air as multiple black barrels point simultaneously at Lyman.

Guard: Hands up! You are under suspicion of espionage and illegal entry. You are under arrest by the DisCity Border Guard!

Lyman: That's impossible! She promised me, she said she'd set it up for me... No, I-I'm not Lyman. My name is Leonard! I'm an engineer from DisCity!

Lyman: Look, this is our company uniform. This proves for me!

Guard: Who do you mean by *she*?

Lyman: She... Sh-she's...

Lyman still hasn't gotten over his grueling journey. He stares around in confusion, his muscles still twitching from the memory of constant exertion, eventually going into spasm.

Lyman collapses to his knees. When he raises his head, all he can see are the cold gun barrels pointed at him from every corner of the area.

That's when his dignity crumbles.

Lyman: Please, please... Mercy please! Just let me in! I'm not a spy. I spent a whole month crawling through WhiteSands to get here!

Lyman: I swear I mean no harm to DisCity! I just want a life. A life with no Mania and disasters!

Lyman: Y...you can lock me up, Sir. Do me investigation, as long as you want! Just please don't send me back. I can't go back to that hell!

Lyman gazes hopefully at the commissioner's pampered face, so different from the weathered, battle-worn features of even the highest-ranking officer he's ever seen.

DisCity's prosperity and peace are laid bare before him in the most visceral way. He's willing to cast aside every shred of his ego, begging this official for just a touch of the magnanimity befitting such a great city.

Comissioner: I regret to inform you, Mr. Lyman, that your actions constitute illegal entry. Moreover, your military status complicates matters as it interferes with Outland political protocols.

Comissioner: Therefore, we will deport you after notifying your home city. Until we're ready for that, you shall remain here.

The commissioner's hollow, bureaucratic sympathy crushes Lyman's last bit of hope, his features contorting with horror and despair.

Lyman: Deport me? No, I'm not going back. I'll die there! There's nothing there but suffering!

If I'm going to die... If I'm going to die anyway...!

He leaps up in a frenzy, breaking free of his restraints and charging toward the checkpoint.

Guard: Watch out! He's forcing through!

Comissioner: Stop him! Snipers, get ready.

Deaf to all warnings, Lyman fixes his eyes only on the barrier ahead, a fragile obstacle that seems so easy to break through.

It's there. Right there.

Everyone's dreams and hopes. The oases and springs that carried him through WhiteSands. Lying right there.

Let me through. Just let me through...!

???: Stop!! Stop there!! Don't...

BANG—!

Vautour Bleu: ——!!!

Vautour Bleu's pen slips, slicing through the letter paper with a sharp, piercing scratch.

Both pen and paper, her loyal companions in the past two weeks, are now ruined, like a sign of something imminent.

Vautour Bleu: ...Ruined, after all.

Vautour Bleu: Looks like the ending won't change no matter who's holding the pen.

With a soft sigh, she discards the spoiled paper and pen, deciding to abandon the letter altogether.

As the haze dances and fades before her eyes, she thinks she sees those long-lost young faces from her memories.

Those hopeful souls who once journeyed with her to DisCity, they too perished along the way.

There will always be gamblers willing to bet their lives on hollow dreams.

And yet...

And yet.

Assistant: Boss, is everything alright? You don't look well.

Vautour Bleu: Nothing. Just reminiscing about the past. Anything new?

Assistant: Yes. In fact, we... we've been surrounded!

Vautour Bleu: Look at you, boy, you're practically shaking. It's not the first time we get a run-in with desert bandits.

Catching Vautour Bleu's indifference, the assistant lowers his voice anxiously.

Assistant: No, this time it's not just some petty bandits. It's the FAC from DisCity! They're saying... you're involved in sneaking some Outland deserter into DisCity!

Assistant: They've hit you with some serious charges, like um... *motivating illegals and disrupting Outland relations*? They want to take you in for questioning!

Vautour Bleu: Oh? The FAC from DisCity?

A flicker of surprise crosses Vautour Bleu's face, but it quickly gives way to bitter realization.

Vautour Bleu: For them to come now, it must be about that incident. I was even counting on that kid. To change the ending of the story...? Just being naive I am, heh.

Vautour Bleu: Well then, a deal is a deal. It's time for me to hold up my end of the bargain.

Assistant: Boss! Are you really going? But if you leave now, the caravan...

The split second of bitter realization vanishes, and right in the next moment, her characteristic self-assured smile returns to her face.

Vautour Bleu: No need to worry, boy. They just want me to assist with their investigation. I'll be back once it's done. While I'm away, continue along the route as planned.

Vautour Bleu: You might not get the best deals without me doing the talking... But think of it as a chance to practice. I don't expect you to make a fortune anyway. Just try not to lose money while I'm away.

Night has fallen. In the dim light, the caravan crew sit around their campfires, some clutching ladles and spatulas, others holding bowls and spoons, all glaring viciously at the FAC operatives standing in their midst.

It almost seems one word from Vautour Bleu would send her people charging in with their makeshift weapons. And it is in this tense atmosphere that the caravan's owner approaches the FAC operatives with graceful tardiness.

FAC Soldier: Vautour Bleu, correct? We might need you to go with us to DisCity, and assist with our investigation.

In the flickering firelight, the FAC soldier's face is expressionless like a machine and the voice that comes out is also utterly flat.

Vautour Bleu: Sure thing. Cooperating with the authorities is the sacred duty of a humble merchant like myself. Though I must admit that I'm quite curious, how did you manage to find me?

FAC Soldier: That's not a question we can answer. But you have the right to ask the person in charge once we arrive in DisCity.

Vautour Bleu: Sometimes being flexible might serve you better, *sir*. That Chief of yours at the MBCC knows how to play the game.

She raises a hand to quiet her restless crew, her smile remaining as gentle as ever.

Vautour Bleu: Well then, shall we go, little bloodhounds?

## Vautour Bleu - Interrogation III

> The Syndicate in Madeleine's memories is a far cry from what Vautour Bleu sees now. Could such changes touch the desperate souls seeking passage to DisCity too?
> Lost in thought, she is startled by a knock on the car window—the one who reshaped the Syndicate stands outside.

The FAC's vehicle looks even fancier than customized cargo trucks. This is no doubt the result of Vautour Bleu's regular "contributions" to the officials. The big shots in DisCity clearly want no harm to come to her.

Once inside, Vautour Bleu helps herself to a cocktail from the fridge, which is clearly not a regular item in an FAC vehicle. She settles down to enjoy the drink with perfect composure.

FAC Soldier: As per our superiors' orders, we won't restrict your movements. However, we strongly advise that you remain sober until the investigation officially begins.

FAC Soldier: We wouldn't want you to be so intoxicated that you'll conveniently *forget* the true identity of Lyman, that Outland infiltrator, before the questioning even begins.

The FAC soldier sitting across from Vautour Bleu speaks stiltedly, his tone and gaze vigilant and hostile. To him, she's clearly nothing more than a common criminal disturbing the city' order.

But these low-ranking soldiers' standoffish attitude fails to intimidate Vautour Bleu a bit. She lounges in the leather seat languidly, playfully swirling her glass at the soldier.

Vautour Bleu: Easy, young man, this level of hospitality won't cloud my judgment. Besides... I'm already here to honor our arrangement. Relax, your boss won't be disappointed.

Vautour Bleu: But what about you all... This can't be an easy assignment, can it? You get no supplies out here in WhiteSands, only annoying desert bandits and disasters. And you have to sit here ramrod straight for days on end... Isn't it exhausting?

FAC Soldier: That's none of your concern. The FAC is well-stocked with Hypercubes. We've never feared desert bandits or disasters.

FAC Soldier: Besides, we've deployed top-tier equipment to expedite your transit to DisCity. In no more than three days, you'll be handed over to the Bureau.

Vautour Bleu: Oh? So I'm an important witness, aren't I? Am I in the VIP lounge now? Haha.

Vautour Bleu lets out a derisive laugh.

Vautour Bleu: How very fitting of the mighty DisCity.

Vautour Bleu: Back then, it took me three whole years to trek this same journey...

Western WhiteSands, Territory of House Noailles

Her voice trembled before she can even finish, dissolving into quiet sobs.

Now, the remaining members and relatives were overwhelmed by the profound grief of losing their family, friends, and leaders, as well as anxiety about their future. The disasters, the Hypercubes, the fate of their caravan...

The room fell in awkward silence. A name lied on everyone's tip of tongue, but no one had the heart to speak out.

Lady Madeleine, the eldest daughter of the Noailles. A natural-born businesswoman whose talent surpassed her brother's, she had already been handling multi-million contracts while still in her teens.

Yet she had never been into it. A lover of art, fine cuisine, flowers, and the pleasures of life, she had declined the offer to lead the caravan many times before. Could they really ask her to shoulder such a heavy burden that would overwhelm even the best leader?

Madeleine: ...

Outside the door, the girl pressed her forehead against the wood, listening to the family servants volunteering to share the burden of leadership. She clenched her fists, then slowly relaxed them.

In the shadow of the ornately carved door, a symbol of her family's glory, the girl composed herself, carefully arranging her face until it mirrored her father and brother's casual optimism. Then she pushed the door open, determinedly.

Madeleine: Everyone, enough. I will take it over. I will lead everyone to DisCity.

Biber: Madeleine...! Are you sure about this? Don't push yourself. Once I'm old enough, I could also lead us to DisCity!

Landon: That's right, Miss. We're an old and useless lot, but we can still...

Madeleine raised her hand to silence the words from her servants. Seeing the mixture of concern, hope, and disbelief in their eyes, she adopted a more serious tone and her expression grew more resolute.

Madeleine: I mean every word I said. It's settled. Though I can't guarantee success in leading all of us across WhiteSands, I can never be so heartless as to leave you all here to die.

Madeleine: Now, let me assign our jobs. Emma, Landon, take inventory of all sellable goods and pack them up. Make sure our packs are as neatly as possible.

Madeleine: Hugh, I know you have a copy of the map that Father and Brother carried with them. Bring it to me tomorrow morning.
