# Jasmine Interrogation

## Jasmine - Interrogation I

> Intelligence before completion:
> After the closing ceremony of the Great Expo, the Outland Sinner Jasmine was transferred to EDGE for containment as a DisCorps weapon bearer. Separated from the weapon at long last, she finally regained her sanity. But that may not be the good news it seems...

> Intelligence after completion:
> Jasmine is still smiling, the same smile she wore on the eve of the Great Expo. You know she cannot let go of the hatred and pain buried deep inside her, and you know her desire to reach out to Shattered Blade is about far more than just "making friends." But... for now, all you can offer are words you don't truly mean. What else can you do for her?

### Interrogation Transcript

Six months had passed since the Ash Campaign. The Fraser Unity Gala came to an end, and the strife between DisCity and the Farland Alliance settled into a tentative pause. The perilous Rancor weapons have slowly been brought back under control.

A long while passed after that. Then Jasmine awoke.

But what followed brought no good news.

March 3, N.F. 116

Special Containment Department of Banyan Fourth Hospital

Chief: What do you mean, "put her back under"?!

Chief: I read the reports from EDGE. LINK was working. It was stabilizing her Mania disorder. The numbers were solid for a while. She was supposed to be getting better. Banyan told me she'd be back soon. Now, just a few days later, you're telling me something completely different?

Doctor: Please, Chief, try to stay calm. We've overhauled our treatment protocols, and we score very highly on all humanitarian reviews. I understand your concern for the Sinner, and I can assure you, no irregular procedures will be performed on her.

Doctor: The LINK session was a success. The patient, Jasmine, has regained consciousness. The problem... well, it's what happened after she woke up.

He gathers the thick sheaf of reports and moves to pass them across, only to stop halfway. He hesitates, then speaks:

Doctor: ...Actually, this won't explain it well. You'd better come with me.

The deeper wards of Banyan Fourth Hospital carry a familiar chill. Its security and surveillance match the MBCC's suffocatingly rigorous standards. Here, too, the ones confined and studied are "patients" branded as dangerous lunatics.

As you press further in, the shackles stir with growing agitation. The madness of the Sinner tethered at the other end bleeds into you, driving your steps faster.

Yet before you arrive, a sharp, anguished scream cuts you down.

Jasmine: Stay back! Get away from me!!

Jasmine: My blades! Where are my blades?! Give them back! I want them back!!

Then comes a sight that grips your heart. Through the thick one-way glass, you glimpse Jasmine: hair wild and matted, expression savage. Inside the cold grey concrete isolation chamber, she hunches low to the ground, back arched like a furious cheetah, roaring, her gaunt frame made starker by the pose.

Burly orderlies have trapped her in a corner. Though she is unarmed, they approach with syringes, sedatives, and medical kits in hand, only to be forced back several meters with every cautious half-step. It's clear this stalemate has worn on for quite some time.

Doctor: Now you see the situation. Her mental state has deteriorated severely. She needs proper medication and psychiatric intervention. But as you can see, we haven't been able to get her to accept any form of treatment.

Before he can finish, an orderly is hurled across the room and slams hard into the one-way glass right in front of you. Several nurses scream, some breaking into sobs.

**end**

Through the one-way glass, you meet her bloodshot, frayed eyes at last.

She freezes.

Jasmine: ...?!

**end**

The distance between you is small now, and the barrier is gone. Jasmine sees you. A daze sweeps over her, like waking violently from a nightmare, like she cannot trust her eyes. Then, at the soft sound of her name, it is her body that reacts first.

Reeling, stumbling, her steps feeble, yet she rushes toward you in quick, desperate strides.

Jasmine: ...Am I dreaming? Chief, is it really you? I—

Jasmine: !

## Jasmine - Interrogation II

> Intelligence before completion:
