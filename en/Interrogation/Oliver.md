# Oliver Interrogation

## Oliver - Interrogation I

> Intelligence before completion:
> A new A-Class Sinner has been found in Eastside but has yet to be captured. Listening to the information from frontline officers, you feel that this Sinner seems familiar as if you've met somewhere before.

> Intelligence after completion:
> As expected, the "little trickster" that has been giving the officers a headache is Oliver. You've located Oliver's residence, and now you're just waiting for the plan to take effect.

### Interrogation Transcript

██ ██, N.F. ██.

As usual, the Bureau is busy arresting Sinners, but the situation seems extra tricky today.

Everyone is busy with their own tasks, so much so that they don't even notice your approach.

Nightingale: Any news?

Guard: That kid slipped away again... That little punk deliberately ran into the most bustling area and grabbed onto a few young people, crying out for *mama*.

Guard: Got everyone's attention at that intersection. We couldn't just take the kid away. If the Eastsiders knew a Sinner was there, it would cause panic.

Nightingale: Then just pretend you know where the mom is?

Guard: Tried that last time. When we passed by a shop, the kid asked to get off the car to look at the toys in the window, saying something like "I know I won't get another chance to see them." Our guys felt a bit sympathetic...

Nightingale: And you lost the target again? You had a whole team and you couldn't catch a single child?

Guard: That kid is too cunning. Left a lot of the Homeless' signs at the wrong forks to distract us!

The guard hands Nightingale a badge with a familiar drawing on it.

Nightingale picks it up and examines it carefully. Then she sighs...

Nightingale: This one is also fake...

Guard: Sigh... The most we've managed so far is that we've put a tracker on that child.

Clerk: Adjutant, the kid has entered that building again.

Clerk: If the news from our guards in Eastside is true, the kid goes back there every once in a while. We suspect that is the kid's real home.

Clerk: We're gathering information about the property. It'll be available later.

Guard: What's the plan, Adjutant? We have enough people. Should we break in?

Nightingale: It's in Eastside. If we cause such a big trouble just to catch an A-Class Sinner, our future operations may face more restrictions...

Nightingale deliberates with the report in her hands.

**【Advance】**

- Is there a problem?

**end**

Chief: Maybe there's something I can do.

Nightingale: Chief? I shouldn't have had to bother you with such a minor issue... I was negligent.

Nightingale: Although it's just an A-Class Sinner, the target is incredibly cunning. That kid seems aware that we wouldn't dare to escalate things in Eastside, giving us a lot of trouble.

Nightingale hands you the documents. The images captured by the surveillance camera seem familiar to you.

Chief: How did you find the child?

Nightingale: The sheriffs received a message from a civilian who had taken in a child who claimed to be a domestic abuse victim but then went missing in a few days. The civilian was worried something had happened to the child and contacted the sheriffs.

Nightingale: The sheriffs didn't take it seriously at first, but similar messages kept cropping up and more well-dressed young men and women were getting tricked. They finally had to take action.

Nightingale: They indeed found a child on the street that matched the description, but the child ran off at the sight of the sheriffs. During the chase, they discovered that...

Chief: The child is a Sinner who can generate very fine threads with special power.

Nightingale: Do you know the target?

Chief: I almost forgot about this kid... Do you remember the incident at the toy factory?

Nightingale: Sure... I've read your report. Was the target also involved in that...

Nightingale: Oh, I remembered... Oliver!

You nod and pass the documents to Nightingale.

Chief: You just mentioned that Oliver has been seeking help from well-dressed young men and women.

Chief: So we just need to match that requirement.

Oliver: Carrying her doll, She cries with her face covered...

Oliver: She has no children of her own, only a doll...

In the dim basement, where even light can hardly penetrate, a soft tune echoes like a ghost's whispers.

Crawling on the ground, the voice is very light but serious. The singer must be thinking carefully about something.

Clack!

The sound of something snapping.

The singing stops. Someone is walking, but no one is talking.

Oliver: Oh dear! Cousin, you're too careless!

Oliver: Why didn't you take good care of her, Auntie? Look, she's hurt herself again. It won't look good if it leaves a scar.

Oliver: Oliver hates ugly things. If you make a mistake and break my "toys", you're not a good kid anymore.

Oliver: Since I was little, Auntie has always told me that bad kids who don't behave will be punished.

Oliver: Now... You've misbehaved too. How should Oliver punish you?

Clerk: The investigation results from the Eastside Public Security Bureau have arrived.

Clerk: Chief and Adjutant Nightingale were right. Oliver does live in this house, but only returns every now and then and rarely stays for long.

Clerk: According to our investigation, Oliver lost both parents at a young age, and was adopted by aunt after that. This house is registered under Oliver's aunt and uncle.

Clerk: And they have a biological daughter, who is Oliver's cousin.

Clerk: But the three of them have gone missing for many years. They've stopped going to school or work.

Nightingale: What do their neighbors say?

Clerk: This is the strangest part! The neighbors speak highly of Oliver and say they are a very harmonious family.

Clerk: They still see the family gathering around happily every night!

Nightingale: If they're indeed a loving family, Oliver wouldn't need to frequently go out and ask for strangers' help, right?

Nightingale: There must be something more behind Oliver's relationship with the aunt's family... Chief, are you sure your plan will work?

Nightingale eyes your brand new outfit with a complicated expression.

Chief: Oliver only saw a gray rag doll in the toy factory. The kid won't recognize me like this.

Chief: I searched everywhere in the Bureau to put together this outfit. I'm sure it won't be a wasted trip for everyone.

You confirm the plan with all parties once more, and head toward the dilapidated house covered in cobwebs in front of you.

Knock, knock. The unhurried knocks on the door interrupt Oliver's pleasant reminiscence in the basement.

Oliver: How strange... Who would be visiting at this time...

Oliver: Alright, I'll be right back! Remember to behave yourselves while I'm away. Don't ruin our little secret in the basement.

Knock, knock. The person knocks again. Oliver comes to the door, a bit annoyed.

Oliver: Who is it? Is there something you need?

??: Oh, thank god, yes. Can you please help me?

??: I came here alone for a walk and got mugged... I gave them my phone and seized the chance to run away, but then I got lost around here.

Oliver: My family's not here. I'm alone...

Oliver fakes a helpless and innocent voice, seeming reluctant to open the door.

??: Cough, cough... I have some health issues that give me trouble breathing after running. Please, a cup of water and a place to sit will help me recover very soon.

Impatient, Oliver stands on tiptoes to look through the peephole in the door skeptically, but Oliver's expression brightens instantly at the sight of the visitor's outfit...

<ins>Oliver: Wow, how glamorous... Oh, I mean, please come in, sir!</ins><br><ins>Oliver: Wow, how glamorous... Oh, I mean, please come in, miss!</ins>

> As expected, the "little trickster" that has been giving the officers a headache is Oliver. You've located Oliver's residence, and now you're just waiting for the plan to take effect.

## Oliver - Interrogation II

> Intelligence before completion:
> You successfully enter Oliver's residence. The strange Mania aura, the missing aunt's family... To figure these out, you must first win Oliver's trust.

> Intelligence after completion:
> Discrepancies have been resolved, and amidst lies and deception, you've found the truth. This mistreated child has already taken vengeance, using even more extreme measures.

### Interrogation Transcript

You take a good look around the house.

Overall, the furnishings of the house are not much different from most ordinary families in Eastside, adequate but unremarkable.

A sturdy wooden table. Old-fashioned home appliances. Some items on the cabinets seem to have been removed, leaving only the dusty marks in their original positions.

Clearly, no one has taken care of this place for a long time. This matches the Bureau's findings.

Something does feel off here... If the aunt's family is indeed gone, why does Oliver return here so often? And what's the deal with the *harmonious family* the neighbors see?

But if they still live here... You carefully observe the surroundings. It's very quiet. Besides you and Oliver, you don't feel the presence of a third person.

Nightingale: Are you sure you don't need more support?

Chief: I'll be fine on my own. Oliver has been able to evade capture so many times, the kid must be highly vigilant. It'd look less suspicious if I go alone. I can gain Oliver's trust more easily and try to pry for more information.

Chief: If we directly take Oliver back to the MBCC for interrogation, I don't think the kid will tell the truth.

Chief: As for the peculiarity of this family, I'll have to go into the house and see for myself.

A Mania-like scent seems to permeate the space. You instinctively search for its source and glimpse a hastily closed door left ajar.

<ins>Oliver: What are you looking at, sir? You wanted to sit down and rest, no?</ins><br><ins>Oliver: What are you looking at, miss? You wanted to sit down and rest, no?</ins>

Chief: Oh right... Cough, cough... I must be too tired. I'm feeling a bit dizzy...

**【Choose One】**

- Where are the adults of your family?

- Are you home alone?

**end**

Chief: Staying alone in this house must be so lonely...

Oliver: The adults... My parents passed away a long time ago. I've been living with my aunt and her family ever since.

Oliver: But you know, I'm not their biological child after all... I understand it...

<ins>Oliver: It's okay, don't worry about me! Take your time and rest well, sir!</ins><br><ins>Oliver: It's okay, don't worry about me! Take your time and rest well, miss!</ins>

How unfamiliar this kind face seems to you. Oliver takes over the conversation skillfully and brushes the dust off the couch thoughtfully, inviting you to sit down.

Oliver: You must be exhausted after what you've been through, right?

Oliver: May I offer you a shoulder massage?

Before you even answer, Oliver moves closer. The bandage around Oliver's neck has come undone, revealing the bruises underneath.

Although it's clear Oliver shows them to you on purpose, you still play along and express your concern.

Chief: Oh no! Did you get hurt?

Oliver: Ah... No, it's nothing! You didn't see anything!

Oliver: I'm fine. It'll get better soon. It doesn't hurt at all...

Oliver tightens the sleeve even more. The dark bruises are particularly noticeable against the pale skin.

Chief: Don't tell me... your aunt and her family did this?

Oliver: ...

Oliver: It's Oliver's fault. Oliver couldn't win the affection of Auntie's family... I'm not good enough for them.

Oliver: They're the only family Oliver has left. The more they see me as a burden, the more I want to prove myself.

Oliver: I work and tell lies for them. I've tried so hard... But all I've ever gotten is punishment.

Oliver's hands shake with the sobs, but the massaging motion never stops.

Chief: Why don't you run away if they're treating you so poorly?

Oliver: ...Me? Where can I go on my own?

<ins>Oliver: Oliver is trapped here forever if I can't find someone kind and rich like you to take me in, sir.</ins><br><ins>Oliver: Oliver is trapped here forever if I can't find someone kind and rich like you to take me in, miss.</ins>

Oliver: Indeed... Auntie doesn't let me eat. My cousin forces me to wear her old dresses. My uncle vents his anger by beating me. But what can I do...

Oliver looks at you with envy, but then tactfully averts the gaze and continues to massage you conscientiously.

The kid knows when to push and when to take a step back. This must be a skill Oliver has honed over time. Fortunately, you are well aware of the purpose of your visit.

The dilapidated house is still quiet. The inexplicable surge of Mania is still active. You sigh as you look at the injuries on Oliver.

Chief: Oliver, do you think you're a good child?

Oliver: Huh?

Those forced tears haven't dried yet. Oliver looks up at you a little confusedly.

Chief: How they treat you has nothing to do with who you really are.

Chief: Sincerity and kindness... These are the true criteria of a good child.

Chief: If you think you're a good child, I'd be happy to help you escape from here and buy you the best toy in Eastside.

Oliver: A toy! Really? Does Oliver deserve something so nice?

Oliver skillfully avoids the topic of *good child* and focuses on the benefits you can provide.

You don't press Oliver for an answer yet and pretend to cough weakly.

Chief: I don't remember the last time I met a child I got along with so well! We've been chatting non-stop, I'm a bit thirsty.

Chief: Can you pour me a glass of water?

The change of topic is admittedly quite abrupt, but it's enough to coax a child. Thinking of the *toy* you mentioned, Oliver frowns but still nods and dashes off.

You quietly wait for a while. Once you hear the running water, you stealthily approach the source of the Mania activity.

Darkness.

Utter darkness.

You grope your way down the stairs and enter the basement behind the door. You hear some faint noise in the darkness, repeating over and over again in a fixed rhythm, reminding you of the long, disorderly legs of an arthropod.

The basement is dark, slippery, and humid. In the damp air, you can smell a rotten stench.

In the thick shadows, you vaguely see several overlapping human figures.

Chief: Are those... mannequins?

As you fumble your way closer, you see three lifelike mannequins. Dressed in old and discolored clothes, they lie haphazardly on a three-legged wooden table, miraculously maintaining their balance.

As you lock eyes with the three mannequins, the strange feeling in your heart grows stronger.

Chief: No, these are no ordinary *mannequins*...

You lean in a bit closer and notice that the *mannequins'* fingers are firmly pressing against the table. Their fingertips are straining to support the weight of their entire bodies, the bones almost protruding...

Chief: ...?!

???: Oops...

???: You've found out.

You hear Oliver's voice behind you. Tap, tap, tap.

Holding a toy unicorn, the Sinner steps toward you. The glass in Oliver's hand is empty.

<ins>Oliver: I was wondering why you chose to rest at my place, sir... It's quite bizarre, isn't it?</ins><br><ins>Oliver: I was wondering why you chose to rest at my place, miss... It's quite bizarre, isn't it?</ins>

Oliver: Well, well... Even more bizarre, I was already making myself sound so pitiful, yet you still had no intention of helping me.

Oliver: You even tried to teach me a lesson!

Oliver: Ah, I remember now. A seemingly gullible person who turns out to be so adamant and preachy... I think I've met someone like that before...

Oliver throws the empty glass onto the floor with a cold expression.

Oliver: You're that rag doll from the toy factory, aren't you?

The shattered glass breaks the unsettling balance. The three puppets can no longer control their bodies and fall to the ground all at once.

Before hitting the ground, they suddenly leap up in inhuman postures, firmly grabbing the table, their limbs flailing.

They're chatting and hugging each other intimately, their dancing limbs twisting in the opposite direction of their joints. They make the silhouette of a harmonious family as described by the neighbors.

What controls their movements are the dense fine threads in the room formed by the Sinner's special power.

These threads fill the entire space and also surround you.

Chief: Now that you've recognized me, let's have a heart-to-heart? I remember you once said that the toy factory was just a distasteful parody of a family? Then what is this...

Oliver: Shut up!

The threads wrapped around the three people are retracted all at once, converging into a force that shoots right in your face!

Chief: You're as impatient as always. Too bad, I'm no longer that rag doll you could push around.

Red spikes spread out and cut off Oliver's powerful attack, piercing into Oliver's body.

As the red light flickers, the Mania is temporarily suppressed and all those threads are rendered futile.

Oliver: Mmph! You...

Knocked down by the power of the shackles, Oliver cries out in shock. At the same time, the aunt's family seem to have regained their souls, screaming as they feel the irresistible pain...

Aunt: Oliver, you dirty little rat! Give it to me! Where did you hide it!

Uncle: Oh... I can repay you soon! Don't chase after me! Just sell that little punk to them!

Cousin: How dare you do this to us... Useless burden! We'll beat you to death and throw you out!

Biting back the pain, Oliver hugs the toy unicorn tightly and covers its ears.

The chaotic and excited murmurs sound especially harsh in the basement. You send a call to Nightingale...

Chief: Sinner Oliver has been shackled and successfully detained.

Chief: I've found Oliver's family too...

Oliver: Haha, you call them... my family?

Slumped on the floor, Oliver lets out a snicker, curling up around the toy and muttering...

Oliver: There's nothing I despise more...

Oliver: Than *family*...

> Discrepancies have been resolved, and amidst lies and deception, you've found the truth. This mistreated child has already taken his vengeance, using even more extreme measures.

## Oliver - Interrogation III

> Intelligence before completion:
> Since being detained in the Bureau, Oliver has been extremely uncooperative. Does Oliver really have no self-awareness? Or is Oliver deliberately avoiding admitting to being a "bad kid"?

> Intelligence after completion:
> Within the battered unicorn toy, a glimpse of hope yet remains, its heart as clear and precious as a gem. This is Oliver's most treasured possession.

### Interrogation Transcript

??: Oliver... When I'm gone, you must listen to your aunt.

??: From now on, your aunt, uncle, and cousin will be your family...

??: You're a very sweet child, Oliver. You'll definitely get along with them, won't you...

??: It's all Mommy's fault... I've left you nothing...

During the trip back to the Bureau, Oliver resisted hard with all kinds of tricks and sweet talks. After being left in the confinement cell alone for a few days, Oliver finally begins to calm down.

Bleary-eyed, Oliver seems to have just woken up, but doesn't appear surprised to see you.

Oliver: Hey, if it isn't Chief who's best at tricking Oliver?

Oliver: Oliver behaved so well while you were away! I didn't run away or cry! I'm a good child, aren't I?

Oliver: As a reward for my good behavior, can I go home now?

Oliver trots over enthusiastically, holding the toy and leaning toward you, looking just like a child longing for company.

But you've already seen the real Oliver in the factory and that house of mannequins.

**【Choose One】**

- Sit back down, Oliver.

- Quit playing dumb. You can't fool me.

**end**

Chief: You're too smart not to understand your current situation.

Your stern attitude seems to have genuinely taken Oliver aback. The kid returns to the seat forlornly.

Oliver: Tsk... You were more fun as a doll and a sketchy rich person.

Oliver: Now, you're no different from those stinky, annoying adults.

Oliver looks away and mutters into the toy. You can see the kid's knees shaking. After all, a place like this is too intimidating and stressful to a Sinner of Oliver's age.

Chief: Oliver... This is the MBCC, a Sinner supervision facility. We only carry out the interrogation to learn more about you. It won't take long.

Your tone softens slightly.

Chief: I said I'd be happy to help you escape from that house of mannequins and buy you the best toy in Eastside. That wasn't a lie.

Chief: I also want to hear the truth from you.

Chief: Let's talk about those people you tricked in Eastside.

Oliver glimpses the list you've collected and looks up innocently.

Oliver: Tricked... What do you mean? I have no idea...

Chief: Oliver!

Oliver: Oh, I simply fell in front of them accidentally, showed them my injuries accidentally, and told them I hadn't eaten in days accidentally...

Oliver: They all voluntarily helped Oliver. Are you saying that's Oliver's fault too?

Chief: ...

Ignoring your warnings, Oliver smirks cunningly, head tilting as if certain you can't catch any solid evidence.

Chief: You won't stop lying even now? Your aunt's family is completely under your control, where did those scars on your body come from?

Chief: The Bureau's doctor has already examined them. They are all self-inflicted!

You throw out those badges of the Homeless that Oliver used while escaping.

Chief: What about these fake badges then? Are they an accident too?

Oliver: What are these? Oliver has no idea.

Oliver: Did you guys pick these up somewhere? Some poor child must have dropped them. You must return them quickly.

Feigning innocence, Oliver pushes the evidence back to you, along with your last bit of patience and sympathy.

Chief: ...I'm giving you one last chance, Oliver.

Chief: I believe not all Sinners are as irredeemable as people say. Many of the Sinners I've encountered simply had no other choices.

Chief: You just said you wanted to go home. Why not start by explaining what's going on at home?

Chief: If you were treated unfairly or hiding a secret, I'll try my best to help. But first, you have to tell the truth.

You pull out a stack of photos showing the horrific scenes from the basement of that house.

Chief: We just saw them not long ago. Don't tell me you don't know what you're looking at.

Chief: Why did you do this?

Oliver: Why... Oh, Oliver wants to know why too...

Oliver: Why must Oliver eat their leftovers, use their worn-out things, and wear their hand-me-downs...

Oliver: I tried so hard to please them. No matter what they did, I always smiled. Why did they still scold me, order me around, call my smile fake and disgusting? If they thought I was a burden, they could have just thrown me away!

These grievances are genuine. You can tell these emotions are not a lie.

Chief: It's them who mistreated you. You've already told me that, and I believe you.

Chief: So you did all this out of revenge?

Oliver: Revenge? Oliver is a good child. I would never take revenge on them.

Oliver: Oliver promised to get along with them. I must keep my word!

Oliver: ...Did you know how easy it is to control the puppets? It's just like playing house. With our shadows cast on the window, we look just like a happy family!

Oliver: Cousin pours water for me, Auntie cooks for me, and Uncle takes me to school...

Oliver: Everyone believes it. They think Oliver is a good child! Oliver did it! I'm so smart, aren't I?

Oliver laughs gleefully, hugging the toy and rocking the interrogation chair.

You scrutinize Oliver's face closely, but not even a trace of regret can be found.

You sigh and tidy up the scattered evidence on the table, then stand up and make your way out.

Oliver: Wait, where are you going? Are you going to leave Oliver alone here?

Oliver: What did Oliver do wrong? Why won't you let me go home!

Chief: You know full well what you did wrong... There's no need to continue the interrogation.

Oliver: Huh?

Chief: I forgot to tell you that after the toy factory incident, the MBCC investigated everyone involved.

Chief: We've found that the transformation is not random. Most people turn into the object they're most familiar with.

Chief: If I'm not mistaken, Eve turned you into a toy unicorn, didn't she?

Oliver: ...What, what are you bringing this up for?

Chief: Nothing... I specifically allowed you to bring the toy into the interrogation room.

Chief: I wonder how this toy would feel, having heard what you just said.

Oliver: ——!!!

Mother: It's all Mommy's fault... I've left you nothing...

Oliver: That's not true! This unicorn Mommy gave me is my favorite.

Mother: Silly child. What would a toy do for you...

Mother: Take this, keep it safe...

A priceless opal necklace was sewn into the heart of the toy.

Oliver: I don't want it! I don't want this! I just want Mommy to be with me, by my side!

Mother: Shh, don't cry... Mommy can't do that anymore. It'll be with you on my behalf.

Mother: From now on, you have to be a good child on your own. Listen to your aunt and uncle, get along with them, and grow up healthily...

Mother: I know my Oliver is the sweetest child...

Oliver: That's not true... Oliver hasn't been good...

Oliver: I haven't been good at all... I'm sorry... Mommy...

Oliver: But I have no other choice... Don't go... Wait for me...

Oliver: Wait, don't go...!

Oliver suddenly grabs you as you're about to get up and leave, tears welling up in those big eyes.

Oliver: They made me do it! Auntie and her family gambled all their money away! They wanted to take Mommy's necklace from me, to pay off their debts!

Oliver: I was obedient until then! I listened to everything they said! I'd thought it all out. I'll be able to leave and support myself once I grow up!

Oliver: They went too far! Oliver wanted to be a good child! But I had no choice!

Oliver: Please don't...

Oliver: Please don't hate Oliver...

Oliver: Nightingale! Have you seen Chief today?

Nightingale: Chief is very busy now. What do you need?

Oliver: Oh, I thought so... I've tried to look for Chief many times but I always get the same answer.

Oliver: It's okay. I understand Chief's work is very important...

Oliver: Can you pass a message to Chief please, Nightingale? Just one sentence!

Nightingale: What is it?

Oliver: Oliver has been eating well these past few days! I've also attended the foundation classes arranged by the Bureau!

Oliver: I've learned a lot of new things! Everyone says Oliver is a fast learner and that I'm bright!

Oliver: Oliver doesn't lie anymore... I've also lent a hand to many people...

Oliver: I guess... Oliver has become a good child...

Oliver's head drops lower and lower.

Oliver: That's more than one sentence... Forget it. I don't want to leave a message anymore.

Nightingale: That's not a problem. Also, I happen to need to see you too.

Oliver: Is it about... the bad things Oliver did before? You're going to punish me, aren't you?

Nightingale: No, Chief has something to give you.

Oliver: ...This is!

Chief: If you think you're a good child, I'd be happy to help you escape from here and buy you the best toy in Eastside.

The child runs into the giant colorful toy and starts crying loudly, like a newborn in the most loving embrace in the world.

> Within the battered unicorn toy, a glimpse of hope yet remains, its heart as clear and precious as a gem. This is Oliver's most treasured possession.
