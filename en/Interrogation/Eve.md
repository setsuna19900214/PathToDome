> Intelligence before completion:
> The rescue team found Eve in a crack in the factory's stone slabs and immediately sent her to the nearest clinic for treatment. However, the situation does not look promising...

> Intelligence after completion:
> The fire in the factory has been extinguished, and the incident seems to be over. You need to embark on a journey for new crises, and it's time for Eve to make her choice.

**【Advance】**

- Eve?

**end**

Eve: Where are you going, Chief?

Eve: I've lost... all my motivation...

Chief: Once you do, you'll be able to *stand up* and *walk out* of the factory, enduring the pain.

Chief: Whether to return to the beautiful dream or to see a new tomorrow, tell me your answer when I come back.

Chief: No matter what your answer is, this time, I will support you.

The door of the interrogation room opens from the outside. Nightingale is waiting for your instructions with a team of guards.

Nightingale: The car is ready to take Eve back to the clinic for continued observation and treatment.

Chief: Good, we should set off now.

The medical staff enter the interrogation room and help Eve onto the wheelchair, but her gaze follows you the entire time.

That look of incomprehension, shock, and curiosity never leaves you until the door closes.

Eve: What is my motivation...?
