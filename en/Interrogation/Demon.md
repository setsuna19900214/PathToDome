# Demon Interrogation

## Demon - Interrogation I

> Demon's reaction today only adds more mysteries to the case. Though we have enough evidence to prove him guilty, we still have no idea what his motives are.
> This is especially true considering his unusual reaction to the bracelet at the end. The city council claimed that he was a cold-blooded, murderous psychopath, but what you saw suggested otherwise.
> Further investigation is required.

30 Days

Until the Trial

The door slams shut behind you. You sit in front of the notorious Eastside murderer.

Demon: ...

Demon raised his head and looked at you for a second.

The faint aroma of blood lingered in the air. Perhaps his wounds had not completely healed from the recent surgery.

Chief: Demon Schilder, I have some things to ask you.

Demon: ...

Demon raised his head again, as if sizing up prey with his eyes.

Not long ago, this man had been on the run. It took a sixty-hour chase to catch him, and after he was caught, the Bureau removed more than 300g of shrapnel from his body. Moreover, he was given a lethal dose of sedatives just four hours ago.

Even so, he still exuded a terrifying presence. You could feel that Demon was someone who could seriously hurt you.

There was no hint of gratitude in his voice.

Chief: Think about your situation, Demon.

Chief: A lot of people want to kill you. Only the Bureau can protect you. You better cooperate.

Demon: Let them try.

Demon didn't even bother to lift his head in the face of these threats.

You knew Demon would be uncooperative. Someone warned you a few hours ago.

Comissioner: These are all the files on Demon Schilder.

Chief: ...

Documents piled high on the desk before you. Across the desk, the commissioner beamed at you.

Comissioner: Chief, Demon Schilder must be handed over to the City Council. His execution is long overdue and should have been carried out eight years ago.

Comissioner: He averted justice eight years ago and killed a dozen city councilors since.

Comissioner: He is a crazed murderer. His execution will undoubtedly appease public anger.

The commissioner paused, observing your reaction. After a while, he continued:

Comissioner: We've arranged a trial for him in thirty days. We brought you these case files and evidence.

Comissioner: You can investigate and interrogate Demon as you wish. We believe you can get to the bottom of things...

The conversation continued for another while. Near the end, the commissioner stood up to say goodbye. Suddenly, he seemed to remember something, and took out a small box from his briefcase.

Comissioner: Oh right. I almost forgot this.

Comissioner: If Demon refuses to talk, show him this.

Inside the box was a dainty bracelet.

The City Council provided all the information needed for this interrogation. Although it was unsettling that they were interfering and demanding to execute one of the Bureau's Sinners, they did present a strong case supported by evidence.

Unless you uncover extenuating circumstances about the case, your superiors won't intervene. The only path awaiting Demon would be execution.

**【Advance】**

- We have to talk about your case.

**end**

Demon squinted at the files in your hand.

Demon: The City Council gave you these?

His voice dripped with contempt at the mention of the City Council.

Chief: Yes.

Hearing your answer, Demon sneered coldly and stopped responding.

You picked up the first file.

Chief: Former Special Task Force Vice Captain Finn fell from his apartment balcony on December 24th, 105. In addition to injuries sustained from his fall, there were also knife wounds all over his body. We presume that he was interrogated and tortured before his death.

Chief: You did this?

The case had ample evidence. Witness testimonies, the recovered murder weapon and even video footage from the victim's home all proved Demon was the murderer.

Demon: That's right.

Demon confessed so easily.

**【Advance】**

- Why did you kill him?

**end**

Demon: He owes me.

Chief: Why did you torture him?

Demon: To extract info about my next prey. And also...

Demon: ...to make some money along the way.

Sounded like Demon harbored a deep grudge against Vice Captain Finn.

Demon answered so bluntly that you couldn't think of what else to ask. So you picked up the next file.

Chief: Councilor Dole Young's body was found in the Eastside sewage system on October 17th, 105.

Chief: Fatal wound to the head, presumably caused by blunt force. The victim's skull was completely crushed, while his spine and ribs suffered multiple fractures. Did you do this?

Demon: Yes.

Demon gave another blunt answer.

Chief: Did you hurt him to extract information about your next prey as well?

Demon: No.

Demon: I just wanted him to die as painfully as possible.

Shadows loomed over Demon's face, clouding his expression.

Chief: Sedgwick Hendrold, head of the City Council Security Department, was injured by an assailant on April 7th, 106 on Eastside's Saint-Saëns Avenue. When the ambulance arrived, he was pronounced dead at the scene...

You stopped talking. Those icy eyes already gave you the answer.

You breezed through a dozen cases in a few minutes. Demon's answer was the same for each one.

You understand why the City Council pressed you so hard to hand over Demon—the twelve deceased were all city councilors, and all were brutally tortured before they died.

Chief: Do you feel guilty when you commit murder?

Demon: Guilty?

Demon: I was simply crushing some maggots.

Demon's voice was cold and indifferent.

You still haven't figured out his motive. You couldn't imagine what he had gone through to reach his current state of mind.

**【Advance】**

- Ask about Demon's motive

**end**

Demon: ...

Demon: Revenge.

His answer was crisp, heavy, and ominous, like a deadly undercurrent.

You could tell he wasn't lying when he spat out that word.

Chief: What did they do?

Demon: ...

This time, Demon did not answer you.

There was a long pause. Demon didn't answer, so you picked up the last file.

Councilor Abel Schilder, the first of Demon's victims. On July 6th, 104, Demon killed him in full view of the public.

**【Advance】**

- Did you kill this person?

**end**

You weren't expecting much from Demon's next answer. It would probably be another absent-minded confession.

But things turned out differently from what you expected. When Demon heard the name "Abel," his face changed.

You suddenly noticed something. Abel Schilder, Demon Schilder... the two shared the same surname. The dots were starting to connect.

Chief: Abel was your foster father?

Demon wore a strange expression on his face. You couldn't tell if it was sadness or rage.

Demon: It's been eight years. I didn't expect anyone in Eastside to still remember him.

Chief: Eight years cannot erase murder.

Demon: Who knows? Eastsiders forget easily.

Chief: So you killed him?

Demon: That's right.

Chief: Why? He was your foster father. Were you exacting revenge on him, too?

The file stated that Demon had been a Syndicate member as a teenager, but was later adopted by Abel. Thanks to Abel's support, Demon found a job at the FAC.

Abel was clearly his savior.

**【Advance】**

- Question Demon's motive for murdering Abel

**end**

Demon closed his eyes. When he opened them again, the cold and ruthless expression returned.

Demon: Eight years ago, Abel mutated into a Corruptor after he was struck by an Mania weapon during a campaign speech.

Demon: At the time, I was the Captain of the Special Task Force. Eliminating Corruptors was my responsibility.

Demon's tone remained flat, as if talking about something that had nothing to do with him.

However, you acquired new information. The City Council made no mention of Mania weapons.

Eight years ago, Eastside, Mania weapons... all these unrelated pieces of information were jumbling together. The truth was becoming harder and harder to decipher.

Chief: There is no reason for the City Council to withhold such important information. Do you have evidence? Or any relevant witnesses to prove your claim?

Demon: Listen...

Demon suddenly interrupted you.

Demon: Don't waste your time.

Demon: I'm not interested in playing this childish detective game of yours.

Demon's patience was wearing thin.

You didn't know which question triggered him, but too many unresolved issues remained. The interrogation could not end yet.

**【Advance】**

- Why do you hate the City Council?

**end**

Demon: ...

Shadows loomed over his gaunt, lowered face.

Chief: Answer me.

Demon: ...

Chief: The Bureau is not affiliated with the City Council. I'm not your enemy.

Chief: If you keep refusing to cooperate, I won't be able to help you even if I uncover extenuating circumstances about this case.

Chief: One month from now, the City Council will hold a secret trial. Once you are convicted, the City Council will immediately take you away to be executed...

Demon: ...

A cold laugh echoed in the darkness. Demon glared at you, then closed his eyes.

By now, the interrogation was basically a closed case. Demon confessed to the murders, but refused to mention any further details. There wasn't any more information or evidence.

Except for the commissioner's strange box.

Chief: Do you recognize this thing?

Demon: ...

Demon didn't open his eyes.

You waited for a while, but Demon didn't respond. You opened the box and took out a bracelet, which clinked crisply in your hand.

Demon opened his eyes.

Demon: ...

Demon: ...

Demon: Ve...

Demon mumbled something quietly, but you couldn't hear clearly.

Chief: What did you say?

Demon: Where... did you find that?

A sense of indescribable dread filled the room.

The ominous aura made your scalp feel numb. You subconsciously gave the answer.

Chief: The City Council...

Demon: ...!

Demon's body suddenly stiffened, and his face contorted like a terrifying beast.

Demon: Give me...

You shivered in fear. Your instinct told you to flee, but your legs went limp.

Demon: Give it... to me!

There was a painful ripping sound. Torn strips of cloth fluttered and tinged the air with the scent of blood as Demon lunged towards you.

At the same time, you heard a soft metallic sting. It was the sound of something sharp piercing the skin.

Demon fell to the floor with a thud.

Nightingale stood behind you, tranquilizer in hand.

Nightingale: Group 1, please update the data. The subject needs twice the amount of sedatives as before. Group 2, take the subject back to his cell.

As Nightingale spoke, guards lifted Demon onto a stretcher.

Demon hadn't completely lost consciousness yet. He fixated his eyes on the bracelet in your hand, his pupils gradually dilating.

Demon: I want to... kill...

Before he could finish, the guards carried him out of the interrogation room.

This was how Demon's interrogation ended.

Until the Trial

Demon: ...

As you wriggled your body, you realized that your personal tracker was still on you. The Bureau would be able to track your location.

Demon: ...

Demon: Don't move.

Demon: I can spare you...

Demon's facial muscles twitched slightly, but he didn't answer your question. Perhaps he was unsettled by the alarm and was planning to leave.

Demon: Everyone who had anything to do with this must die.

Demon raised his head and took a deep gulp of air, gradually calming down.

Demon: ...Eight years ago, perhaps I would've believed you.

Demon: But not anymore.

Demon: I know you're just stalling for time to wait for help. I won't give you any more chances.

You struggled to get up, trying to defend yourself.

As you wriggled and writhed, the bracelet you wanted to use for questioning slipped out of your pocket and fell to the ground.

Demon: !

Demon's pupils shrank abruptly. Though this was the second time he saw the bracelet, he still couldn't conceal how shaken he was by it.

You remembered the question you wanted to ask during the interrogation, as well as the question that came up when you were browsing the files.

Nightingale: If your guess was correct, the bracelet given to you by the City Council belonged to her.

Nightingale: Abel's biological daughter, Demon's sister.

- Say her name

Chief: Is she important to you?

Demon: She is my family... my little sister.

Demon's expression gave the answer you were looking for.

He picked up the bracelet gently from the ground.

He raised his hand as dim rays of light shone through the curtains. The white bracelet swayed slightly. There was a gentle look on his face that you've never seen before.

Chief: Where is she?

Demon: I... don't know.

Demon: I'm a fugitive, while she's a councilor's daughter. I can't go meet her.

Demon: Vera is the only family I have left... I must find her.

**【Advance】**

- I will help you find her.

**end**

Chief: The Bureau has sufficient manpower and a well-developed intelligence network. Finding Vera won't be difficult as long as you can provide the appropriate information.

Chief: I'm not your enemy, Demon. Come back with me. You shouldn't shoulder all the burden by yourself.

Demon didn't respond. He just stared at you quietly.

Thankfully, the hostility had vanished from his eyes.

After a long time, Demon responded.

Demon: Thank you...

Demon walked to the window and opened the curtains. Blinding sunlight poured in from outside, and for a moment you couldn't open your eyes.

Demon: I've met people like you. None of them survived.

Demon: Good people don't live long in DisCity, but murderers like me can survive until now.

Demon opened the window. A grassy breeze diluted the musty odor in the room.

Demon: This is a battle I must fight alone.

Chief: Wait...

Demon: You have impressive men working for you. They will find you.

Demon leaned on the window sill. He covered his wound with one hand and gazed out the window.

Was he just going to leave like this? In his current state of health, it would only be a matter of time before he got caught.

Chief: You can't just leave like this! The City Council will sent their troops to arrest you. If you land in their hands...

You hadn't noticed that the old wooden door had been open for some time. A man in a hat stood by the door.

Mysterious Man: Cough...

Mysterious Man: Sorry, I didn't mean to interrupt your conversation.

Demon: Who are you?!

Demon immediately became vigilant. He didn't notice the man's arrival either.

Mysterious Man: It doesn't matter who I am. What matters is what I heard. It seems that in fifteen days, a show trial will be held against you.

Mysterious Man: I have a plan to break the game... do you two want to hear it?

The man smiled faintly. He didn't wait for an answer, but took off his hat and walked in.

Mysterious Man: I think you do.
