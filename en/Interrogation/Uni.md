# Uni Interrogation

## Uni - Interrogation I

> Uni mocks those missing persons that they got what they deserved. As the interrogation progresses, you gradually realize Uni has her own agenda.
> You were told that Uni is an irreplaceable part of the star-making industry, thus you decide to accept the councilor's invitation and infiltrate the cocktail party to uncover the truth.

You come into the interrogation room. The young woman who is about to be interrogated is drumming her fingers on the armrest of her chair, impatience writ large on her face.

You put the evidence bag on the table. That's when her patience runs out.

Uni: I came all the way down here. And you locked me up in this messy room?

Uni: Sorry, but I don't have the time to tidy it up for you.

She sounds insincere and sardonic.

Chief: Nice to meet you, Uni.

You take a quick look around the room, with its spotless floor and walls and neatly arranged table and chairs. It could not, by any stretch of the imagination, be called messy.

Chief: Well, this is an interrogation room. I don't think we should make it fancy, as long as it fulfills its purpose.

Uni: This room lacks symmetry, is what I'm saying. The lighting, the furniture, even your haircut... It just makes my skin crawl.

Uni: Your eyes, though, are a different story. They're beautiful. Have you ever considered adjusting the other parts of your face to make them go better with those eyes?

Chief: ...

The way she's looking at you...

It's an appraising look, as if you were a piece of furniture for sale. It has never left your face since you entered the room.

**【Advance】**

- Are you speaking as a cosmetic surgeon?

**end**

Uni is a cosmetic surgeon who owns an underground clinic. She is little known by the general public, but well known in certain Eastside circles.

She may be one of the wealthiest doctors in all of Eastside. Rumor has it that in the past two years, her services have helped many people get on the fast track to stardom.

The cosmetic surgeon, who is now sitting in front of you, doesn't strike you as a go-getter who has made it. This might be attributable to the fact that she has an aura of sullenness, which is accentuated by the way she talks and her general attitude.

Chief: I hear you can change your clients' faces according to their exact requirements. With the right price, they can get a perfect new face that will catapult them into stardom and change their lives forever.

Uni: That's about the size of it. I have surgical skills, they have money. It's a two-way street. And it's fun to fulfill their so-called desire to advance with my hand.

Uni: It's a legit business. Are you interested, Chief?

**【Advance】**

- It's your clients that I'm interested in.

**end**

Uni runs her appraising eyes from your chin to your lips, and finally to your eyes.

Uni: It's a win-win situation, really. I get paid, and my clients leave the clinic happy with their new looks.

Chief: Not all your clients, I'm afraid.

Chief: Some of them didn't get to leave.

Chief: Do you remember seeing these people?

Uni gives the pictures a cursory glance before looking back up at you and replying in a casual tone.

Uni: I do. They were my clients. I met them only once or twice. But I haven't seen them since their cosmetic procedures.

Chief: The thing is, according to the witnesses, they were last seen on the day they went to your clinic. It seems that you were the last person they saw before they went missing.

Chief: Where are they now?

You scrutinize her face, trying to pick up clues from her facial expressions.

Uni: How should I know? I'm a cosmetic surgeon, not a brain surgeon. If I had the ability to control their brains—if they had ones, that is, I would've asked them to give me more money, instead of making them disappear.

Uni: I hope you're not accusing me of doing something unsavory during the procedures to make them disappear.

Uni: Well, you know, I had no reason to do that to them. We were basically strangers. Our relationship was purely business.

Uni: Unlike you, Chief, I don't have the means to make humans disappear without a trace. I'm just a humble cosmetic surgeon.

**【Advance】**

- You do have the means.

**end**

You reach out your hand. The shackles, glowing bright red, run out of your hand and latch onto Uni's wrist. As they make to reach deeper, they collide with another object, which gives out intense purple light upon impart.

The shackles bounce back. You almost lose balance. When you regain your footing, you look up and see Uni's strained face.

Uni: So those are the fabled shackles.

Chief: Now tell me the truth. I'd hate to resort to any special means again in this interrogation.

Uni: Shackles, the good old shackles, the weapon of the powerful against us Sinners.

Uni: (Sighs)

Uni's demeanor relaxes. She turns her gaze away from your face to a corner of the room. She seems to have lost all interest in you. What has brought about the change, you have no idea.

Uni: I thought you'd be different. It turns out you're just like the rest of them.

Uni: You want to know me?

Uni: Shall we, Chief?

The shackles tremble when you're about to come face to face with the mirror, which, without doubt, has been tainted with Mania.

But nothing out of the ordinary happens.

All you see in the mirror is your own reflection.

Chief: What do people normally see in this mirror?

Uni: A version of themselves that they desire to see. It's a reflection of their deepest desire, their deepest secret. They see the face they long for. And then I'll come in and make that face a reality.

Uni: So do you like what you see?

Uni can't see what you're seeing in the mirror, yet her eyes growing more intense. When her fingers travel from the frame to the surface of the mirror, you have a strange feeling that it's your actual face that she's stroking.

Her uncomfortably intense gaze is filled with eager anticipation. What's she expecting?

**end**

Almost instantly, all false expressions disappear from Uni's face. She watches your expression with unblinking eyes.

Chief: Is there something wrong?

Uni: I found it...

Uni: A blank canvas. I've been right all along. You are the missing piece of my puzzle!

Uni's voice rises. A ripple breaks the smooth surface of the mirror, and the air in the room thickens, making it harder to breathe.

The shackles shake. Uni wraps her hands around the mirror frame, holding it so tightly that her fingertips go white. Her eyes bore into yours, and your eyes burn. *Is this her power?!*

**【Advance】**

- Release the shackles

**end**

The red shackles cut through the thick air, shatter the mirror, and confine Uni to her chair.

And there's one more thing that's bothering you.

The moment when the shackles broke the mirror, you saw mirror fragments in the air angled towards Uni.

Yet in those broken pieces, her reflection was nowhere to be seen.

## Uni - Interrogation III
