# Thani Interrogation

## Thani - Interrogation I

> Intelligence before completion:
> MBCC operatives rescued a girl from unidentified assailants in WhiteSands. Investigation reveals that this girl named Thani suffers from severe Aurum Secretum infection and is suspected to have close ties with the Aurum Secretum Research Institute. You need to interrogate her as soon as possible.

> Intelligence after completion:
> Through your interrogation, you confirm that Thani is indeed connected to the Aurum Secretum Research Institute and is a key witness to the BR-005 explosion incident. You demand that she remain at the Bureau for treatment, and promise to find her companions as soon as possible, but Thani seems to be having second thoughts.
> The next day, Thani is gone.

### Interrogation Transcript

Refugee Boy: Thani, can we... can we rest for a bit? Dima can't walk anymore.

The children's voices pierced the silent night of WhiteSands. They'd left trails of tiny footprints in the sand. Beneath their refugee cloaks, their faces were all young yet weary.

The girl leading them stopped immediately, turned around, and gazed up at the pale moon hanging in the night sky.

Thani: Then let's camp here for the night. Hmm, it's a bit exposed out here... but we should be fine! Shura, can you help me?

A frail figure darted out from the group. The girl looked barely older than ten. She pulled out various tools and supplies from her backpack and handed them to Thani.

The children sat in a circle with Thani standing in the center, skillfully building a fire and cooking. Shura produced a waterskin from her backpack and handed it to the companion beside her. The children each took just a small sip before passing it to the next person. The firelight illuminated their faces.

Refugee Boy: Hey, Dima, why aren't you drinking?

Refugee Girl: Thani... we've been running in WhiteSands for so long. Will we... ever get back to the refugee camp?

As soon as the girl spoke, the chattering children instantly fell silent, looking down and fidgeting with their fingers. For a moment, the only thing they could hear was Thani's spoon stirring and clinking against the pot.

Refugee Girl: Does that mean... I'll never see Mom and Dad again?

Refugee Boy: Dima, please don't...

Refugee Girl: If I'd known it would be like this, I should have just stayed with Mom and Dad...

Refugee Boy: Stay with them? They were long gone! Dead! Don't you get it? Is that what you want too, to die with them?!

Refugee Boy: ...Oh no, d-don't cry!

Thani: Who says everyone's gone?

Thani stopped what she was doing. The crying ceased instantly. The girl looked up, meeting Thani's smile.

Thani: Don't believe what those DisCity people say!

Thani: Granny Afra once told me that we children of WhiteSands are blessed by the Desert Goddess. Even when we *die*, our souls become sand and we live on forever, always staying with our families.

Thani: Everyone's been away from home for so long that they've forgotten this truth. No matter how cruelly the DisCity people treat us, they can never truly separate us. We've fooled them all!

Thani's vehemence grew with every word, and she thrust her arm upward, slicing it through the air with a fierce gesture.

Refugee Boy: Thani... that sounds like... something Granny made up to comfort you...

Thani: Hehe, I used to think the same thing. But you know what? I can still hear Granny's favorite song in the wind sometimes.

Refugee Girl: Thani, can you really hear it?

Thani: Of course! Don't you trust my ears?

Refugee Girl: Then... can you try to see if you can catch any messages Mom and Dad left for me?

Thani: Sure thing! Let's see...

Thani closed her eyes, cupping one hand to her ear while gently resting the other on the girl's shoulder, as if to soothe her anxiety.

The night was still, yet she heard the children's hopeful breaths, barely suppressed. In the distance, a lone beast growled at the moon. Blown by the wind, the sand rippled like waves, spreading across the darkness.

Suddenly, her brow furrowed slightly. Her eyes remained closed, but her body froze for an instant as if catching some signal, her breath momentarily caught.

She could hear it now. Footsteps were approaching across the undulating sand, faint yet distinct.

Thani opened her eyes, the smile still on her face.

Thani: I heard them! Your mom and dad are warning us about approaching danger. We need to pack up quickly now, but don't worry. They'll help hold back those bad guys for a while!

Shura: Thani? Could it be... those people from the institute again?!

Thani: Shh... pack up your things quickly now. Shura, lead everyone back the way we came. Remember, don't make any noise.

Thani raised an index finger to her lips in a shushing gesture. Her eyes, however, already turned toward the distance, as if she'd spotted something.

Shura: Thani, aren't you coming with us? In that case... I'm staying too!

Thani: I can't let them go alone. Remember those trail markers we've been using? I'll follow them to catch up with you all.

Shura: B-but if you don't come with us, everyone will be terrified! What if... what if!

Shura looked away. Thani watched her, a rare shadow shrouding her eyes for just a moment. But her smile quickly returned, just as it always did.

Thani: Trust me, I won't be like those adults... I promise I'll catch up with you in three days. Three days, tops!

Shura looked up at that familiar smile, the one that always made even the worst disasters feel tolerable, and finally nodded decisively.

Shura: Leave it to me then! I promise I won't lose a single one of them before you come back!

Thani bent down and stoked the fire higher, the smoke providing cover as the children hastily packed up and helped one another disappear into the distance.

Now, with her companions gone and the desert beasts asleep, Thani turned around. What she faced was nothing but waves of black sand, surging toward her in relentless torrents.

The waves were getting closer, growing larger.

Sandy wind whipped her face, slicing her cheeks. Thani flinched from the stinging pain, but quickly steadied herself, planting her feet firmly into the sand.

Thani: Now, I'll need the blessings from all of you back at the refugee camp...

She murmured to herself, lifting her hand to touch the metal bracelet on her wrist. In the next moment, her fingers clenched tightly around her wooden staff as she swept it across the ground, kicking up clouds of sand.

Thani: Hey! You! I'm right here! Come and get me!

Nightingale: This is the Outlander girl our patrol rescued from WhiteSands.

Chief: Did you capture her attackers?

Nightingale: No. Though they were outnumbered, the attackers knew WhiteSands better than we did and were clearly well-trained. Once they realized they were losing, they retreated without a second thought. We've conducted a few searches since then, but so far there's been no sign of them.

Nightingale: However, we have managed to gather some information about the victim's identity.

Nightingale turns her head, and you follow her gaze toward the interrogation room. The girl sits in a chair, occasionally glancing at you both through the glass.

Nightingale: Her name is Thani. An A-Class Sinner with severe Mania corruption. On top of that, she has been exposed to Aurum Secretum radiation.

The medical report in your hand reads: Severe Mania resulting from Aurum Secretum infection. The subject exhibits hypersensitivity for all five senses. Under high-stress situations, she may also experience symptoms such as fainting and shock.

Chief: Aurum Secretum... What's her connection to Fraser?

Nightingale: Given that our patrol found her near the Aurum Secretum Research Institute, we suspect she may have direct ties to the facility, or possibly even to BR-005, but...

Nightingale shakes her head.

Nightingale: We currently have no concrete evidence linking them. She was recently attacked and is still in shock, which explains her guarded attitude toward us.

Nightingale: But I believe you can gain her trust.

Chief: I see. Open the door for me, please.

The interrogation room's seat is hard and cold. Though Thani has tucked the hem of her jacket beneath her to avoid direct contact of her skin with the metal, she can't stop fidgeting uncomfortably.

She unconsciously touches her wrist, which is now completely bare.

Chief: We're keeping your bracelet for now. It helps manage your Aurum Secretum symptoms, doesn't it? It's taken quite a bit of wear and tear though. We'll return it to you once it's repaired.

Thani remains silent. You give her a gentle smile.

Chief: This is the Minos Bureau of Crisis Control, an organization that shelters and rehabilitates Sinners. I'm the Chief here. The people who rescued you from your attackers and brought you here are also our staff.

Thani: Th-thank you all...

Thani: What about those bad guys? Did you catch them all?

Chief: I'm afraid we didn't catch any of them. We only managed to save you.

Thani: Oh, really...

Thani: Then did you see anyone else? Besides the attackers?

Chief: There were others?

Thani: No! I, I was just... wondering! I just think I got really lucky... Hehe, I'm the sole survivor, aren't I...

She says this, but her hands can't stop fussing with the hem of her clothes.

Chief: *Just wondering*?

Thani glances at you, then quickly averts her eyes.

Chief: I understand you're frightened. You were attacked and woke up in a strange place. But please trust me. We mean you no harm. We only want to help you.

Chief: If you know who attacked you, or have any other relevant information, please tell me. Including anything about these *others* you mentioned.

She opens her mouth, about to say something, when the overhead light suddenly flickers. She flinches, shielding her eyes with her hand. When she regains her composure, she glances around the room with newfound suspicion, presses her lips together, and decides to remain silent.

She can't bring herself to trust you in this setting. You decide to change tactics and try to find another way past her defenses.

Chief: When our team rescued you, you had no food supplies or tools, not even a water bottle. Without these essentials, no one could survive more than three days in the desert.

Chief: Unless you weren't the one carrying these necessities.

Thani: I did have supplies with me! I was carrying several kilos of dried food. But... but...

Thani: The desert bandits took everything! That's right! Those thieves are ruthless. They stole all my food and water, even took my can opener...

Chief: You said you had *several kilos of dried food*. Was that all just for you?

Thani: Huh? Y-yes. How else would it be enough for... I mean, enough for me...

Chief: You were lugging several kilos of food, plus water and tools, on your own, trekking through WhiteSands for months? You must be some kind of miracle.

Thani: Don't judge me by my size! I'm actually super strong! Back at the refugee camp, I even helped build houses...

Chief: Which refugee camp?

You cut her off abruptly, giving her no time to think. Thani freezes, her face visibly turning red.

Thani: Geez, whichever you think it is! Just some random refugee camp! Why do you have to dig into everything...

Chief: So why did you leave the refugee camp? Sure, it might not be the most comfortable home, but it must be better than wandering alone?

Thani: I just wanted to! There's no why... The folks there... the folks at the camp bullied me! No, wait... it was because of the desert bandits! Hmm... no, there was a sandstorm! I left because...

You interrupt her again.

Chief: Or, was the refugee camp *destroyed*?

Thani: ...What?

She falls silent, staring at you in shock. You move closer, looking directly into her eyes.

Chief: The place where we found you isn't far from the bombed Aurum Secretum Research Institute.

Chief: From what I know, there used to be a refugee camp near the institute. Now, like the facility itself, it's been completely burned to the ground.

Chief: You claim you're from the refugee camp and that you're being hunted by unknown pursuers. At the same time, you're also infected with Aurum Secretum.

Chief: I'll stop going around in circles. Tell me, what is your connection to the Aurum Secretum Research Institute?

Thani snaps back to reality, her eyes darting frantically around the room. Finally, with a defiant tilt of her chin, she says:

Thani: ...Okay, okay! You're right, I'm from there. But I'm no refugee, I'm...

Thani unconsciously yanks at her tattered sleeve.

Thani: I'm a researcher from the Fraser Institute! So you'd better let me go right now, or else... or else...

Seeing your unfazed expression, her voice gradually dwindles.

Thani: Or else... something bad will happen...

Chief: Even if you really were from the Fraser Institute, we still couldn't just let you go. Your life would be in constant danger out there. And not just because someone's hunting you down.

You sigh and pick up a document from the desk.

Chief: Our doctors have examined you. You're suffering from severe Mania infection. Without immediate suppression treatment, you'll soon lose your mind completely and turn into a Corruptor.

Thani: How is that possible...? My mind is perfectly clear right now, except for a few minor side effects...

Chief: The corruption in your body has progressed thoroughly. Those Aurum Secretum marks on your skin are proof. You may feel relatively normal now, but once your Mania flares up, you'll deteriorate much faster than others.

Chief: Thani, you don't have much time left.

Thani's body begins to tremble. You soften your voice to comfort the shivering girl before you.

Chief: But don't be afraid. I have a way to help you.

Thani: R-Really?!

Chief: Yes, this is part of my job. But first, I need to know what really happened to you and why you were wandering in WhiteSands.

Thani's eyes dart nervously as her lips tremble. You notice her defenses weakening and adjust your tone accordingly.

Chief: I know you don't trust me. When you asked if I'd seen anyone else, you were checking whether your companions were safe, weren't you?

Thani: I... I was just afraid you might have killed them...

Chief: On the contrary, we want to protect all of you.

Thani: Why should I believe you? The people at Fraser said the same thing before, and then...

Chief: Because we're investigating the incident related to the Aurum Secretum Research Institute. And by that I mean we're seeking the truth, not silencing witnesses. If you and your companions are connected to the facility, then ensuring your safety is our absolute priority.

Chief: Furthermore, as I mentioned, I'm Chief of this Bureau that shelters Sinners, which means your safety matters to me.

Your voice softly fades away, followed by a long silence. Thani hangs her head as tears fall silently onto her knees.

Finally, she tilts up her face, wiping away the dampness with her hand, a glimmer of tears still lingering in her eyes.

Thani: I did grow up in the refugee camp outside the Aurum Secretum Research Institute. Until that massive explosion happened. I remember that day, when the entire sky went dark.

Without a doubt, that *massive explosion* Thani refers to is BR-005.

Chief: What happened before and after the explosion?

Thani: We found out the Aurum Secretum Research Institute was experimenting on refugees, turning them into monsters. So we helped blow the place up.

Thani: But after it was all over, suddenly, the adults said they needed to attend a *sacrificial ritual*... They followed Saffar, and then... the explosion happened, and they all turned into monsters just like the Apostles.

Thani: ...They died.

Thani's voice gradually fades until it's barely audible.

Thani: Before they left, they told me to take care of the other kids and stay alive. I originally planned for us to keep living at the refugee camp. But after that, the institute came for us and burned everything down. I overheard them saying that they had to *eliminate all evidence*.

Thani: So I took everyone and left. I thought maybe we could find somewhere to stay in WhiteSands.

Chief: So you are the *evidence*. Living, breathing *witnesses* who know what happened.

Thani: I don't know anything about what happened inside... I just... wanted us all to survive together.

Chief: Where are your companions now?

Thani: Still in WhiteSands. I promised them I'd go back. They left markers that we used to leave when playing games. Only we know about them.

Thani bites her lip, her shoulders trembling slightly.

Thani: They're still waiting for me to come back. I promised them...

Chief: I'll help you bring them back. Tell us about these markers so we know where to find them.

You stand up, retrieve a piece of paper and a pen from the desk, and hand them to her.

She hesitates for a moment before taking the pen with a tremulous hand. But just as she's about to write, her hand suddenly stops. She looks up at you.

Thani: ...You said I'm very sick. If I actually die from this illness, will you still look for them?

Chief: Yes. Your health condition is a separate matter from their rescue mission.

You give her a smile.

Chief: Besides, we won't let it come to that.

Thani: All right...

She lowers her head and slowly begins drawing symbols on the paper. But her hand shakes, making her drawing slow and messy. When she finishes, she looks up again.

Thani: So... when you find them, will I be able to stay with them?

You pause for a moment, not answering her directly.

Chief: This is a facility reserved for Sinners. So, if none of your companions show signs of Mania like you do, we'll place them in an orphanage where they'll be taken care of and receive education.

Thani's shoulders slowly slump with each word you speak.

Thani: So... I'll be separated from everyone? Even if... even if I recover?

Chief: It won't be a goodbye. You'll get to visit them, I promise.

Thani: No. Then I don't want to be treated anymore. You all claim you're helping me, but you're just tearing us apart!

She tries to stand up abruptly, but something seems to scratch against her skin. She instantly curls up with a sharp hiss of pain, pressing hard against the spot where her bracelet once was.

Chief: You want to protect them, don't you? You've done everything possible to help them escape from the refugee camp and evade the pursuers... If you want to keep them safe, you should start by getting yourself treated, so they won't have to worry about you.

You approach slowly, squatting down to meet her at eye level.

Chief: I can help you. I'll place my shackles on you, which can control your Mania and alleviate your Aurum Secretum symptoms.

Chief: Please trust me.

She grits her teeth wordlessly, her eyes filled with conflicted emotions. After a long moment, she finally speaks in a small voice.

Thani: ...What exactly are these shackles?

Chief: Put simply, it's a link between a Sinner and myself.

Thani: A link... So, once I have these shackles on me, you'll be able to find me whenever you want?

Chief: Technically speaking, yes, that is part of its function. In your current situation, it would also allow me to check on your safety at any time.

Thani purses her lips.

Thani: Can I... have some time to think about it...?

Under the harsh lights of the interrogation room, Thani's face is deathly pale, sweat pouring down her skin, yet her eyes remain defiant. After a long moment, you finally nod.

## Thani - Interrogation II

> Intelligence before completion:
> You follow the signal to an intricate cave system in WhiteSands. Here, you find yourself locked in a cat-and-mouse chase with a mysterious figure.

> Intelligence after completion:
> You spend a night with Thani and her companions in WhiteSands, witnessing the bonds between them and gaining some insight into Thani's insistence on staying with her family.
> But that very night, you're visited by uninvited guests. You and Thani try to buy time while waiting for reinforcements, but the Fraser assassins soon have you cornered. Just as all seems lost, a figure steps between you and death.

### Interrogation Transcript

Inside the dark cave, you advance slowly, feeling along the clammy, rough rock faces.

*Thani has fled the Bureau in haste, forgetting to remove the ankle monitor the hospital puts on all its patients. The data shows that she's snuck onto a sand ship and then jumped off mid-journey before wandering aimlessly through WhiteSands.*

*You've finally tracked her to this maze-like canyon cave system. Knowing she still doesn't trust you, you order your squad to wait outside while you venture in alone.*

Your fingers brush against several scratches on the rock face. They're rough and hastily made, clearly left there by someone.

Despite being in WhiteSands, not even a whisper of the desert's dry air reaches inside this cave. This place is so cold and humid, it's definitely uninhabitable. Yet somehow, there are plenty of signs of human activity.

Right at your feet lies a rope trap, cunningly positioned in a deceptive spot that nearly makes you lose your balance.

Chief: *Judging by the condition of the rope... this trap was set quite recently.*

But since entering this cave, you haven't heard a single footstep other than your own.

While you are pondering this, you suddenly hear faint giggles coming from somewhere ahead.

Chief: *Is that... a child?*

You carefully step over the rope trap and enter a slightly more spacious area ahead, only to find even more traps lying in wait.

At the end of these intricate rope networks are sharpened wooden stakes, rigged to pierce any intruder who triggers the trap.

Chief: ...

You pull out your detector and carefully navigate around these crude traps, moving toward the source of the earlier sounds.

Meanwhile, in the darkness nearby, two girls are pressed tightly into a rift in the rock face.

Shura: Come on, take the bait...

Thani: Shhh... Be patient. Though this is indeed trickier than I expected... If all else fails, I'll go out myself as bait.

Shura: No way! You only just made it back to us, Thani. You can't let those bad people catch you again... I'll do it!

Thani: Huh? Don't go out there! Wait!

Shura: Ah!

A scrawny girl crashes to the ground in front of you, and you instinctively step forward. Immediately, you hear something swishing through the air.

The sound of a trap being triggered.

A wooden stick flies toward you, hitting your shoulder strap, but it's not nearly as deadly as it appears. You feel only a slight impact against your chest. The stick falls to the ground, rolling several times.

Thani: Shura! Are you okay?!

Shura: Thani! Run! Don't worry about me! My spirit will stay with you all forever!

Shura: You gray-haired fiend! If you must catch someone, take me instead!

Shura's impassioned voice echoes throughout the cave. Small pebbles occasionally sprinkle down from the ceiling, as if even the rocks are moved by her dramatic pledge of self-sacrifice.

Meanwhile, you and Thani exchange bewildered looks speechlessly.

Thani: What? It's you?! I thought it was them...

Chief: ...I came here to find you.

Thani: ...

Shura: Ugh... Thani... m-my foot really hurts...

Thani: Let me see... I think you've sprained it. It'll probably swell up in a day. This is going to be a problem...

Chief: Here, use this for now.

You produce a small tube of ointment from your pocket and offer it to Thani. She hesitates for a moment, but eventually takes it.

Thani: Th-thank you...

Thani meticulously applies the ointment to the girl's ankle, but there's also a large patch of skin scraped off her knee that has started to bleed.

Chief: I can carry her on my back.

Thani: ...All right then, please take her.

Chief: Come...

You kneel down in front of the girl. Thani helps her climb onto your back. The weight you feel is disturbingly light.

Chief: Your name is Shura, right?

The girl on your back gives a reluctant grunt of acknowledgment.

Shura: Don't think you can cozy up to me and Thani so easily, gray-haired fiend...

Chief: ...

With Thani leading the way, the dark cave feels considerably less threatening. She moves with nimble steps, avoiding loose rocks and slippery moss underfoot, as well as the various traps her group has set.

In the darkness, the monotonous yet distinct sound of dripping water only emphasizes the cave's deathly silence. You can clearly hear your own slightly labored breathing, and the faint echo of Thani's footsteps ahead, bouncing between the rock faces.

After what feels like an eternity, finally, the space before you suddenly opens up.

You stand at the edge of a steep cliff, facing a desert stretching endlessly to the horizon. Night has already fallen, yet the moonlight is almost blindingly bright. The wind from the cave entrance carries the dryness of sand, quickly evaporating the moisture from your clothes.

You set Shura down and hear her uneven footsteps dwindle in a certain direction. Your eyes follow her to discover a group of children in tattered clothes huddled against the rock face.

Thani: I'm back!

Thani announces jubilantly. Spotting you beside her, the children study you with wary yet curious gazes.

Seeing their reaction, you open the backpack you've been carrying.

Having been wandering in WhiteSands for so long, hunger is no longer a strange sensation to Thani and her companions. Anticipating this, you specifically packed essential supplies and food before setting out.

Chief: I brought some things you might find useful.

The children stare transfixed at the food and medicine you pull out, their previous wariness forgotten as they swarm around you in an instant.

Compressed biscuits, antibiotics, bandages... All common items found in the Bureau.

Thani: Wait! I don't think you can eat that!

Thani nimbly stops a small child who has somehow snuck up beside you and was just about to gnaw on a medicine box. You sort the edible items from the inedible ones, then grab a few cans and open them for the children.

A boy is nudged forward by the others, eyeing you warily while clutching the opened cans against his chest.

He dumps the contents of the cans into a worn copper pot balanced on a few stones. Beneath it flickers a feeble flame.

Thani: That's Kash, our master chef. And that's Dima beside him. They are brother and sister.

Thani: Hehe, don't let his silence fool you! He's usually quite the chatterbox, with a temper to match!

The boy stirring the pot clearly hears Thani's comment. His lips tighten into a pout, his face glowing red in the firelight as he stirs visibly harder.

You sit with the children around the fire as the warm aroma of food fills the air, drawing small, eager murmurs from their hungry lips.

A familiar figure limps toward your group.

Shura: The soup's ready! Here you go!

Shura: This one's for Thani, and this one's for Master Fiend.

Shura even sounds a little proud, as if she's just completed an important mission.

Chief: *Master Fiend*...?

Thani: You should take that as a compliment!

You accept the bowl, finding the ingredients you shared earlier floating in the broth alongside a few berries.

The children cradle identical bowls, trying to savor each spoonful yet unable to resist gulping down the soup. In their crude firepit, flames continue to dance.

You take a sip. The broth is much blander than what you're used to at the Bureau. Salt is clearly a precious commodity for these children.

From across the circle, Dima watches your reaction with curious anticipation. When she catches your eye, she timidly offers you a tiny piece of bread crumb, softened from being carried close to her body.

In the firelight, Thani sits beside you, her eyes crinkling with contentment. After a while, she begins humming an Outland melody you've never heard before.

*By day I bury the seeds of light, by night I gaze at the starry sky.*

*Winds sweep through the forgotten door, bringing songs from my faraway home...*

As the song fades and the bowls empty, the children, with their bellies now full, huddle together and drift off to sleep, their breathing growing slow and steady.

You and Thani sit near the cave entrance, backs pressed against the cold rocks as you keep watch over the sleeping children.

Thani: They haven't slept this peacefully in ages. The last time was... maybe right after we left the refugee camp?

Thani speaks in a whisper, careful not to wake the children.

Chief: What about you? You look... barely older than them, yet you need to watch over everyone.

Thani: Hehe... Compared to them, I'm practically a grown-up. I promised the adults back at the refugee camp that I'd look after these kids, keep them safe.

Thani's fingers absently trace the sharpened stick resting across her knees, her makeshift weapon for self-defense.

Chief: Thani, come back with me.

Chief: Your Mania contamination is too severe. If this continues, you'll turn into a Corruptor.

Chief: My shackles can cure you, and they'll also give you the power to truly protect these children.

Chief: You can't live like this in WhiteSands forever. Coming to DisCity would be best for all of you.

Thani: I know... But there's only one thing I care about.

Thani: We're going to be separated, right?

Chief: ...Yes.

Thani lowers her head, avoiding your gaze.

Thani: The people at the institute once told me the same thing.

Fraser Researcher: We've got your medical report.

Thani held her breath nervously. The room was suddenly devoid of noise.

After a few seconds, the researcher's face brightened with a friendly smile.

Fraser Researcher: Congratulations! Your medical results are excellent. Welcome to the Aurum Secretum Research Institute. From now on, you're an honored member of our facility.

Thani: R-really?! I can stay at the institute and perhaps one day become like Synex?

Fraser Researcher: Absolutely. We see tremendous potential in you. Who knows? You might even surpass Synex herself.

Fraser Researcher: Go ahead, place your handprint here. Someone will come for you shortly. Starting today, you're no longer just another orphan from the refugee camp.

Thani: Oh, but what does that mean? I am from the refugee camp.

Fraser Researcher: It means you don't have to go back to that miserable life of never knowing when you can have your next meal. Here, we'll provide you with nutritious food and medical care, and we'll nurture you into someone exceptional.

Thani: What if... I want to go back?

Fraser Researcher: Oh dear, that would be a problem. According to our protocols, once you're part of the institute, you can't go back.

Thani: ...But that's no trouble at all! I know the way! I can go back by myself...

Fraser Researcher: No, you cannot go back, and you can never contact anyone from the refugee camp again. Those are the rules, and even I have no say in the matter. Just settle in here. Don't you want to become like Synex? She once went through the exact same thing.

Fraser Researcher: Don't look so glum. Think about it, do you really want to go back to the refugee camp? Do you really miss the constant hunger, the endless fear that the next minor ailment might finally kill you?

Fraser Researcher: And those people at the camp? Nothing but lazy parasites. If they found out how special you are, they'd use every trick to exploit you until there's nothing left.

Thani's fist tightened with each word from the researcher, until her fingers brushed against several syringes hidden beneath the sheets, supplies she'd secretly stashed away and was hoping to sneak back to the refugee camp.

She pulled one out.

Thani: ...Take this!
