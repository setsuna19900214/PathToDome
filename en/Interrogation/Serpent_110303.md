# Serpent Interrogation

## Serpent - Interrogation I

> Serpent told you that to know the truth behind everything, you must enter the "theater" with her.
> She kissed your fingertip and brought you together into the haziness to find happiness.

This darkness feels familiar.

So is the sensation. It feels as though a snake has wrapped itself around your neck, cold and suffocating.

Chief: Serpent...?!

Serpent: Hehe... Take a guess! Is this a dream, or reality?

Serpent: Or maybe... neither. Maybe we're in a realm of pain!

Serpent materializes by degrees in the darkness. She is on top of you, her greenish-gray eyes quivering.

A warm drop of liquid alights on the corner of your mouth. It glides into your mouth, and the tip of your tongue tastes something faintly salty. It's a teardrop, you realize.

Chief: Are you... crying?

The feeling of constriction around your neck intensifies. You have no time to reflect on what the teardrop represents, because you don't want to miss the window of opportunity.

There is a flash of red light, and a tangle of tendrils flows out of your palm and onto Serpent.

The second your psyche is connected to hers, countless cries of pain, sorrow, and hatred flood your consciousness.

The darkness recedes. Your ears start humming, but you know what just happened wasn't a hallucination, for Serpent is lying in your arms.

Soldier: Found Chief!! Medical team, hurry up!

Soldier: Chief, are you all right? Should we do anything about that Sinner?! She just whisked you away, like some kind of magic trick...

Chief: I'm okay. She passed out. I've used the shackles on her. But something is amiss with her.

Chief: We're going back to the Bureau for now. Give her the best treatment. I need to talk to her as soon as possible.

Soldier: Yes, Chief!

It has been ten days since the Ambrosia incident. You have been searching for Serpent, hoping to prevent a recurrence of the incident.

But locating a masterfully-camouflaged snake in the forest is no easy task. Besides, nothing untoward has happened in these ten days.

It could be the lull before the storm, however. Serpent is still at large. You can't feel at ease, nor can you let your guard down, because she could be waiting for a chance to fight back.

Then in your most recent operation, she, much to your surprise, gave herself up.

Serpent: Ow, the light is harsh...

Serpent: Is this how the MBCC treats someone who just came to?

Serpent: Oh, I get it... I don't deserve to be treated nicely, because you don't think of Sinners as human beings, right?

Chief: We didn't mean to make you feel that way.

Chief: You've just woken up, and now you have to stay in an interrogation room. This is hard on you, I know. But... there is a ton of unfinished business we have to deal with now.

Serpent: Yes, yes... a ton of business, some out in the open, some hidden in the dark...

Serpent: Chief, you're very busy indeed. Haha.

Chief: I'll cut to the chase. Where is the Bottle of Dreams now?

Serpent: ...

Serpent: Why does it matter? No matter where it is, now... I am in your hands.

Serpent blinks at you. Her voice is saccharine.

Chief: As the Chief of MBCC, I have to be cautious.

Chief: After all... experience has taught me that Sinners who're willing to give themselves up tend to be more dangerous.

Serpent: It seems you've been through a lot, Chief... How about giving Ambrosia another go? It might just help you let go of those painful memories.

Serpent: Oops, I almost forgot...

Serpent: Ambrosia can't bring *you* happiness.

Serpent tilts her head, and gives you a smile that reveals her pointed canine teeth.

Serpent: But that doesn't matter. Happiness... Happiness is something that can be made.

Serpent: You know how happiness is made, right? You've seen it; you've been there.

Chief: Making happiness... Are you still so hung up on Ambrosia?

Serpent twirls a stray lock of hair around her finger, studying your eyes as if in admiration. It's some time before she speaks again.

Serpent: You've eliminated Marv already... Why are you still worried about Ambrosia?

Chief: What I'm worried about is the Bottle of Dreams you took.

Serpent: ...

**【Advance】**

- Ask about the source of the original Ambrosia

**end**

Chief: You told me it's *Marv* that created the original Ambrosia and used it to bewitch humans before sucking their life force dry to turn them into Corruptors. Marv was nothing like those Mania-turned monsters in the FAC records; it's powerful and elusive.

Serpent: Your point?

Chief: You said you came upon it by accident... Can you tell me more? When and where did you come upon it? And where did it come from?

Serpent doesn't seem eager to answer your questions. She looks away to stare at the clock on the wall, beating time to the passing of each second.

Serpent: I don't remember, and... I don't think it's important.

Chief: You don't remember, or it didn't happen at all?

Serpent: What do you mean?

At first, the MBCC found nothing useful when it went through the files on the Sinners. Then, thanks to a thorough investigation conducted by the 9th Agency, you found something worth noticing about her identity.

Chief: I have reason to believe that... you created Ambrosia and the monster with your power.

Serpent takes a sideways glance at the files on the table without turning around to look at you.

Chief: A few years ago, pet snakes were all the rage in Eastside, and a girl who could speak to snakes was the talk of the town for some time on social media.

Serpent: ...

Chief: Shortly afterward, an accident happened: a pet snake attacked its owner. It prompted the introduction of a bill in Eastside prohibiting its citizens from keeping large snakes as pets; and those who do, do so at their own peril.

Chief: The Public Security Bureau still hasn't found that pet owner, and in the end, they gave a verdict of "death due to mistakes made in raising of a pet snake."

Chief: You were the girl who could speak to snakes, weren't you? And you spoke to the snake of that victimized family.

Serpent: What if I was? I was no longer there when they were bathed in intoxicating happiness.

Chief: I know that.

Chief: I discovered through my investigation that the family did get their pet through some illegitimate channel. I followed up the lead and found something amiss.

Chief: Other than number, weight, activeness level, there's an additional item of information on their purchase record.

You point at the handwritten information on the photo.

Chief: *Foodstuffs*. It didn't specify the type of food or the weight. Simply *alive*.

Serpent: Yes, it meant me.

Serpent: That's why you think I was the perpetrator?

**【Advance】**

- It's just a logical suspicion.

**end**

Serpent looks at you, her lips set in a pout of annoyance.

Serpent: They played with fire and got burned. And you're blaming me. Be reasonable, Chief.

Serpent: Anyway, they signed me over to an entertainment company, which, in a way, freed me from the hellhole.

Serpent: Hmph. I was happy with my life, why would I get myself tangled up with them anymore.

The smirk on Serpent's face lingers; she seems certain that you can't do anything about her.

The first case was closed as the police couldn't find anything suspicious. At the time of the incident, Serpent was already an employee of **** Entertainment. How could she have had anything to do with the case?

Seems like she is not going to be honest with you. Looking her in the eye, you decide to change the subject.

**【Advance】**

- All right. Let's talk about something else.

**end**

Chief: Let's talk about the entertainment company you just mentioned.

Serpent: What's there to talk about? There's nothing illegal about them signing me. The family... got me a temporary ID card.

Chief: That's not what I want to talk about.

Serpent: ...

Chief: Your performance with snakes was a huge hit. You became quite a celebrity on Eastside's underground novelty-show scene, and you were often hired to perform at various venues.

Chief: You brought in handsome revenues for the company. That's why they covered up your involvement in the incident.

Serpent: Yeah, right! Like that shitty company would do something like that for me; they wouldn't even give me a bigger cut.

Chief: Of course, the authority given to us has allowed us to learn more about these two cases.

Chief: For example, both in the pet snake case and in the entertainment company case, the scenes had been trashed, turned completely upside down.

Chief: It looked like the work of some extraordinary monsters... Corruptors, I suppose.

Serpent: ...

Chief: Ever since then, you have been using Ambrosia to... to torture them, am I right?

Serpent: What if I have? Like I said, I was creating happiness! I am helping them build sweet dreams!!

Serpent: So what if they end up vomiting up black sludge, end up turning into Corruptors? Those painful memories are supposed to be discarded! Why... why are you resisting Ambrosia?! I want the happiness, the sweet dreams of you all!!

Serpent spits out her words like venom. She huffs and puffs, her shoulders heaving, as if saying those words were a great effort for her.

You've known a thing or two about Serpent's obsession with happiness. But in District 14, when she stirred things up, she was not as uncontrollable or hysterical as she is now.

The shackles are throbbing like crazy. Dimly, you sense Serpent's feelings. Her life force seems to be in the grip of some mysterious entity, making her go berserk.

Serpent: You... You wouldn't understand their happiness, let alone their pain.

Chief: You don't understand their pain either... You simply ignored it.

Chief: Indulging yourself with sweet dreams can't fix everything, Serpent.

Chief: I once met a Sinner who lost herself in an amusement park of her own. I don't know if I helped her, but such excessive indulgence brought her only pain.

Chief: I don't want you to suffer the same fate.

You rise to your feet, walk around the table to stand in front of Serpent, and look her in the eye.

Serpent: How can you be so sure that it will cause me pain?

Serpent: You can't experience happiness or have sweet dreams, and here you are, sitting on your high horse criticizing me?

Serpent lifts her head, staring at you like a kid hoping for an answer to a question just asked.

Chief: I'm not criticizing you.

Chief: The sleepwalkers' symptoms have been worsening since day one, and Ambrosia is getting more potent. If you don't keep your power and the Bottle of Dreams in check, you'll suffer even more...

Chief: Stop losing yourself in the Realm of Dreams. Stop using Ambrosia. Serpent, could you please tell me where the Bottle of Dreams is?

Serpent falls silent, so does the interrogation room.

**【Advance】**

- This is very important.

**end**

Chief: It's dangerous to indulge in those sweet dreams that can't bring you true happiness.

Serpent remains silent. You continue persuading her, to no avail.

Serpent: My happiness, my dreams... Chief, you asked me why I came back.

Serpent trailed off, and when she speaks again after a long pause, her voice is as cold as ice.

You feel cold on the nape of your neck. A small snake has found its way there without you knowing it, and it is hissing in your ear.

Serpent looks up at you from her seat, a chill in her eyes.

Chief: Ambrosia doesn't work on me.

Serpent: I know.

Serpent: I just want to make good on my promise. In the last minute, I want to give you happiness. So, now I'm going to present to you... a grand circus show.

Serpent leans forward ever so slightly, whispering to you in a hypnotizing tone.

Serpent: Chief... Would you give me your hands?

You raise your hands, which Serpent takes in hers gently and brings close to her lips. Her palms are as cold as the snake around your neck.

Serpent: Good... Aren't you afraid that I might do something, Chief?

Chief: Surely you can sense that the shackles are already on you.

Serpent: Hehe... Yes, I can.

Chief: Which means... you are at my mercy. If anyone should worry, it should be you.

Serpent: Aren't you confident that you won't get lost in the Realm of Dreams? Take another gamble with me then. You do everything you can to break and untangle the mess to find the answer, and I... I will risk everything I have to give you happiness.

You could use the shackles to force her to tell you who gave her Ambrosia and where the Bottle of Dreams is. But that wouldn't solve the problem at its source.

You are not concerned about or afraid of what she's going to do to you. For one thing is sure: you must shatter her twisted obsession with happiness and sweet dreams, much the way you must shatter the Realm of Dreams.

Chief: Okay then, take me to the show.

Looking at her pointed canine teeth, you give her a faint smile.

The shackles tremble, and your psyche and Serpent's respond in sync. It's as if you're laying everything bare for each other to see, but at the same time, hiding everything from each other.

Serpent: Fantastic! My beloved Chief, my beloved audience...

Serpent: Let's go enjoy the merriest, grandest circus show!

The announcement is followed by a gentle kiss on your fingertip.

Chief: Ow!

You feel a sudden stab of pain in your fingertip, and look down. A scarlet blob is blossoming.

Serpent: Really sorry, Chief.

Serpent doesn't let go of your hands. Instead, she tightens her grip and bends down to kiss your fingertip for a long second.

Your vision blurs, and you feel something moving up from your fingertip where it has been punctured. You start feeling lethargic.

You have a vague feeling that you are still in the interrogation room. And then a white mist rises around you; you give yourself over to that lethargic feeling, sinking deeper and deeper...

Serpent: The awake one does not enter the Realm of Dreams... Come in to my story then.

You come around to find yourself surrounded by darkness. You have a vague feeling that you haven't left the interrogation room, but everything around you is blurry.

Serpent's voice turns dreamy, and your vision goes blurry for some reason. You feel there's something wrapped around your body.

Then it hits you: You're feeling what the girl is feeling. She stowed away with others in a truck trailer, but little did she know that she was to travel with snakes.

Girl: Eastside... there's happiness to be found... in Eastside?

Girl: Will my pain become... less severe, if I find... happiness?

Just before her life drains away completely, something in her vision seems to inject into her a new desire for life...

Girl: Let me go find happiness, Let me... live!

You feel your heartbeat increases and your breathing quickens. Every inch of your body is in excruciating pain, but at the same time, you feel itchy all over, an itch that runs deep and thorough, as if there's a new body growing inside of you.

It wants to free itself from the fetters of the past, to rid itself of all long-established weaknesses...

You're soaked through with sweat when it's done and dusted. With an effort, you lift your head to look around. The girl has freed herself from the snake's embrace and is now sitting next to it.

Girl: Thank you... for showing me the visions. I'll get that kind of happiness, right?

The girl strokes the snake gently, her vertical pupils filled with ferocious longing. The snake, which almost killed her a moment ago but utterly fears her now, is lying at her feet, as docile as a pet.

You sense the reason why the snake is afraid of her. It's because she holds extraordinary powers, and because she is even crazier than the snake—she has become a Sinner.

You shield your eyes with your hand against the sudden glare.

Man: What the **** is this?

Girl: Hahaha! Dreams, happiness, everything here is going to be shrouded in happiness!
