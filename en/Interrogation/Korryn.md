# Korryn Interrogation

## Korryn - Interrogation I

> Intelligence before completion:
> The MBCC receives an uninvited guest. Sinner Korryn claims to have kidnapped "your precious little mouse" and demands a private meeting with you.

> Intelligence after completion:
> You venture into WhiteSands and successfully use Tuco as bait to draw out Korryn who has escaped from the Bureau.

### Interrogation Transcript

DisCity Outskirts, Near WhiteSands

MBCC Field Operation in Progress

Field Agent A: Report! Transport mission complete. Squad 3 standing by for orders.

The vehicle hums quietly as the soldier with the comms device falls silent for a moment. Then, he nods and responds.

Field Agent A: Affirmative. No intel on the target was gathered during the operation. Understood. We're beginning our return now. ETA to Bureau in 45 minutes.

The call ends, and he removes his headset. His companion at the wheel glances at him with concern.

Field Agent B: The other squads haven't found Hella either?

Field Agent A: Nope. No one's been able to contact her, and we have no info on her whereabouts. The Chief sent people to search the market where she was last seen. Hope there are some leads there... Wait!

His eyes widen sharply—a shadowy figure is standing in the middle of the road, directly in their path.

Field Agent A: Look! Someone's ahead! Hit the brakes!!

The brakes screech as the soldier slams down on them, but momentum keeps pushing the heavy military vehicle forward.

Bang!

The vehicle's front end dips and bounces back up with a dull thud, stopping just before hitting the figure. The young driver, hands still gripping the wheel, looks up with beads of sweat on his face.

Field Agent B: Wh-what in the...

The figure plants her leather boots on the hood and crouches forward.

The young woman leans down toward the windshield, brushing aside her wind-tousled hair to reveal a radiant smile.

???: Hey there, my dear MBCC friends!

???: I hear your boss lost a precious little mouse recently? What a coincidence—I picked up a dusty little thing during a stroll in WhiteSands—

She tilts her head and dangles a standard-issue MBCC notebook in her hand.

???: How about it? Why don't you take me back for a little chat with your *boss*?

???: Well, well, Chief... you've finally come to see me.

She lounges casually in the interrogation chair with a playful smirk, completely at ease.

Perched on her shoulder is a little lizard that seems oddly out-of-place in this setting. When you enter, the lizard even gives you a little nod, as if to say hello.

???: I heard you're quite the busy bee—thought you'd keep me waiting for a day or two. But you came so soon! I haven't even finished my tour of the Bureau.

**【Advance】**

- Korryn, right?

**end**

Chief: You wanted to have a chat with me? Let's begin.

You ignore her banter and take a seat directly across from her. Unfazed, she narrows her eyes with a smile and cuts to the chase.

Korryn: I see you've done your homework on me, so I'll cut to the chase. Tell me, Chief, does this look familiar?

Korryn leans forward, and with a flick of her wrist, slides a notebook across the table to you.

It's a standard-issue notebook with a dark cover bearing the MBCC logo—the kind every Bureau employee carries.

**【Advance】**

- ...What does that mean?

**end**

Korryn: Lean in and take a closer look...

You remain silent, so Korryn leans in closer. She flips to the first page, which is filled with messy handwriting and a sketch of a snarling little mouse—you recognize it immediately as Hella's work.

She taps the paper, a hint of menace in her voice.

Korryn: Get it now, Chief? Your precious Hella is in my hands—and this is the proof.

Korryn: I wanted to bring something more convincing, but she wasn't very cooperative. I didn't want to hurt her, so I just helped myself to some of her belongings.

Korryn: Well? Believe me now?

**【Advance】**

- This is indeed Hella's work.

**end**

Chief: You're right, this is Hella's handwriting.

You calmly close the notebook and, under Korryn's puzzled gaze, activate the comms device beside you.

Chief: Please bring in some drinks and refreshments—from the VIP menu please, with both hot and cold beverages. As for the snacks and fruit...

Chief: Do you have any dietary restrictions, Miss Korryn?

You look up at Korryn, making a show of the MBCC's legendary hospitality. She plays along, feigning surprise.

Korryn: My, my, Chief, what's this about? Trying to bribe me?

Chief: Not at all. I just want to resolve this situation as peacefully as possible.

After arranging everything as requested, you end the call. The door opens, and a young clerk enters with drinks and refreshments.

Chief: You have Hella, and I'm concerned for her safety. I don't want to put her at risk by provoking you... or any accomplices you might have.

Chief: Let's be frank. Where is Hella? And what do you want?

Instead of answering your questions, Korryn helps herself to some snacks without hesitation.

Korryn: Heh, relax, Chief. This is a solo job—no accomplices involved.

Korryn: WhiteSands has been pretty rough lately, and jobs are scarce. I have even less money than morals right now—no way I'd split the loot with anyone else.

Korryn: No need to worry too much. I'm not some violent thug—I'm just in it for the money. I don't want to hurt anyone. As long as...

Korryn wipes the crumbs from her lips and holds up four fingers.

Korryn: If you can give me this number—I promise, your precious Hella will be home first thing tomorrow morning!

Forty thousand? Four hundred thousand? Four million? Unable to decipher the bounty hunter's code, you furrow your brow in confusion.

**【Advance】**

- How much are you asking for?

**end**

Korryn: No, no, no—not DisCoins. What I want is Hypercubes.

She presses her four fingers together and taps them lightly on the notebook in front of you. Her voice rings out as sharp and clear as the tapping sound.

Korryn: Hypercubes are the good stuff. Those Nirvana bigshots keep 'em locked up tight—we can't even get near their routes without getting shot. We all want to get our hands on some. And now I have the perfect opportunity!

Indeed, Hypercubes are hard currency in WhiteSands—high value, high demand, and low supply... The perfect target for *extortion*.

But even with your authority, there's no way to get such a massive amount of Hypercubes immediately.

**【Advance】**

- Hypercubes? This many?

**end**

Korryn: That's right. This many Hypercubes.

As she repeats your words, Korryn leans in even closer across the table.

Korryn: I've done my research. As Chief, getting this number of Hypercubes shouldn't be too hard.

Korryn: Think of it as a safekeeping fee for Little Hella. A small price to pay for her safe return—quite a bargain, really.

**【Advance】**

- ...

**end**

A bargain? You're not so sure about that. But for Hella's sake, it looks like you'll have to dig into your personal funds this time.

You lower your head and exhale deeply before looking back up at her.

**【Advance】**

- Very well. I accept your terms.

**end**

Chief: I don't care how, but Hella must be back at the MBCC tomorrow morning, safe and sound.

Korryn: Oh, perfect! As decisive as I'd expect from the MBCC Chief. It's a deal!

Your acquiescence clearly pleases Korryn. She relaxes back into her chair.

Korryn: Didn't you read my file already, Chief? I, Korryn, am famous for being honest and efficient in all my dealings!

Korryn: By the way, you want my contact info? For any future kidnapping requests—or other services! Call me and I'll give you a discount.

Chief: Let's focus on the present matter.

You raise one hand to shush her and respond to messages on your comms device with the other.

Chief: The Hypercubes have been secured and are on their way. Remember, if you go back on your word—

Korryn: If I break my word, may lightning strike me down and my name be forever erased from WhiteSands! Don't worry, I know the rules of the game. Your little mouse will be returned without a hair out of place.

Just as Korryn finishes speaking, footsteps echo from beyond the door. Her *payment* has arrived.

Instead of getting up, she briefly glances at the door before turning her attention back to you. Her lizard matches her piercing gaze.

Korryn: One, two, three... How interesting. Chief, does the Bureau always get heavily armored personnel to deliver Hypercubes?

**【Advance】**

- Well, it's valuable cargo after all.

**end**

Chief: Hypercubes are a crucial military resource, so it's normal for the FAC to handle their transport. And as it happens, I've just asked them for a small favor...

Chief: ...to apprehend Sinner Korryn, proceed to WhiteSands, and recover our MBCC colleague.

The footsteps grow louder. With the deal off, the squad members outside no longer need to conceal their presence.

In mere seconds, the squad will have the interrogation room completely surrounded and the only exit secured. Korryn will have nowhere to run.

The woman sighs and lazily raises her hand.

Korryn: Oh dear, I really didn't want to resort to this, but you leave me no choice...

Korryn: Come, say hello to *Sheriff*!

Before she finishes her sentence, Korryn suddenly flings her arm upward—her green lizard leaps high into the air, lunging straight for your face!

**【Advance】**

- Wha—?!

**end**

The green lizard opens its mouth, and its slender tongue flicks out a knuckle-sized smoke bomb. The device clatters to the floor, releasing thick plumes of smoke.

BANG! The security team reaches the door and bursts in.

You cover your nose and mouth with one hand while reaching out with the other, trying to grab something—anything—but Korryn's silhouette flips backward and vanishes into the smoke.

Eugene: Locate the Chief! Apprehend the Sinner!

The smoke continues to spread until it fills the entire interrogation room. Your vision is completely obscured by thick white clouds. You can hear the security team's footsteps nearby, but can't pinpoint their location.

You take a few steps backward, trying to find a wall for orientation, but instead bump into a warm body.

Korryn: Oh... dear... Chief—

Her voice comes from behind as the cold barrel of a gun presses against the nape of your neck. The Mania radiating from her paralyzes your body, leaving you completely immobile.

Korryn: How does it feel to have your own trap backfire?

Korryn reaches out to retrieve her little *Sheriff* from your shoulder.

Korryn: I know, hiding a smoke bomb in my lizard's mouth to slip past your security check was a bit unscrupulous, but...

Korryn: If you hadn't broken our deal first and tried to arrest me, I wouldn't have had to resort to such an underhanded trick, right?

Korryn: Now that negotiations have fallen through, I'm afraid your little mouse belongs to me!

The gun barrel moves away from your neck as Korryn shoves you forward.

Eugene: Chief!

Eugene quickly pulls you behind him. On the other side, two guards collapse to the ground simultaneously as Korryn's figure vanishes down the far end of the corridor.

Eugene: The reinforcements from Adjutant Nightingale will be here any moment. If we seal off the exits now, we can still catch her!

Chief: No... stand down. Let her go.

You reach down to retrieve several of the scattered files at your feet.

These are the investigation files you didn't get to use yet—the MBCC's complete dossier on Korryn. A lone bounty hunter, WhiteSands Market, several untraceable hideouts...

And one key name: Tuco.

You circle several locations from the files in red on the WhiteSands map. Though scattered, they form a rough circular pattern—too perfect to be coincidence.

Chief: She wouldn't have dared come here without an escape plan ready.

You circle an empty spot in red, then hand the map to the security officer beside you.

Chief: I need information on every city, town, and village in this area. Her accomplices are somewhere out there, and they've most likely got Hella with them.

Chief: And one more thing—I need you to go to WhiteSands Market...

The MBCC has issued a new bounty notice: The infamous Tuco swindlers have been apprehended, and the MBCC is now seeking the individual responsible for bringing them to justice.

—Within two hours, the news had spread throughout all the major markets on the outskirts of WhiteSands.

Merchant A: Huh? Tuco? What's Tuco? Has anyone been to that town before?

Merchant B: Ha! Justice is finally served!

Merchant B: Those heartless swindlers finally messed with the wrong people. Serves them right to get caught!

Merchant A: Oh? You know about Tuco? Don't tell me you're the one who turned them in! The notice mentions a big reward!

The merchants continue their hushed discussion, unaware that someone has been listening to them for quite some time.

Korryn: Excuse me, mind if I take a look at that bounty notice?

Merchant B: Oh... oh, sure, here you go—

Caught off guard by her sudden approach, the merchant hands over the crumpled bounty notice with slight hesitation.

Korryn: ...

A contact number and address is listed at the bottom of the page. The deadline is... today.

The security on the MBCC sand ship is more lax than expected. Korryn slips past the patrol units almost effortlessly, successfully infiltrating the lower deck.

Under the cover of the shadows along the wall, she silently approaches the Power Core Control Room and she expertly picks the locked door with a twisted wire.

Breaking just one thing here would immobilize the entire ship. Whether they replace the ship or repair it, either option would give her enough time to escape deep into WhiteSands with the whole Tuco gang.

Korryn: ...Heh, those lockpicking skills are really paying off.

With a silent smirk, Korryn quietly turns the handle and leaps into the darkened room—

Korryn: —!!!

A net drops from above as combat units instantly surround her with their weapons aimed.

Korryn looks up to find countless guns pointed at her. Behind the expressionless soldiers, a familiar figure sits beside the eerily glowing blue power core. She lets out a chuckle.

Korryn: Well, well... so this is where you've been waiting for me?

Korryn: Planting false intel, clearing out the guards... Tsk tsk, you crafty adults are so different from the kids. Little Hella is much more endearing than a sly fox like you!

Opportunities always work both ways.

You eye Korryn as she raises her hands in surrender, then rise and walk towards her.

Chief: It's been a while. Shall we continue our interrupted conversation?

Chief: —My dear defeated opponent.

In front of the Security Bureau at WhiteSands Market

Chief: Whether or not you're telling the truth, stealing is wrong. But I hope that once you're able to support yourself, you'll choose a more honorable—and safer—way of living. Otherwise...

Chief: Set course back to the main WhiteSands route.

Before Korryn can say anything, you enter the cabin and issue the command.

Korryn: Oh good, round after round we go. Adventure games are my favorite!

Korryn: Who said I wasn't looking for a chance to escape?

Korryn: Ahhh... That hurts...

Korryn: Oh, my dear Chief! If I jump out of a sand ship, you'll jump out of one too?!

Security Bureau Clerk: Thank you for dealing with the bandits! Be careful on your journey—WhiteSands has been having unusual weather lately, with lots of sandstorms and sand beast attacks—

Through the wall of swirling sand, you vaguely make out the silhouette of a building ahead.

Chief: To the left—cough... the left! We can take cover over there!

The howling wind thrusts sand into your mouth when you try to speak. Unable to talk, you and Korryn silently feel your way toward the shelter.

The wind grows stronger by the second. Korryn's gunfire becomes more frequent, and the creatures' shrieks are practically next to your ear... then, finally, your hand touches the door of the shelter.

You shove the door open and dive inside, pulling Korryn along with you.
