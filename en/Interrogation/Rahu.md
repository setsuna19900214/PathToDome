# Rahu Interrogation

## Rahu - Interrogation I

> Intelligence before completion:
> A few days ago, Sinner Rahu, the FAC's special operations unit, was transferred to the MBCC.
> All she brought with her was a transfer order from an organization you know nothing about. All you know is that the person who issued the order wields unimaginable power and has a secret motive.
> You must respond.

> Intelligence after completion:
> No matter what Rahu's motive is, there is something strange about her attitude towards Conrad Cage.
> The visit to him will be key to uncovering the truth about Rahu.

### Interrogation Transcript

██, ██, N.F.113

MBCC Headquarters,

Chief's Office

9th Agency Member: So, you believe that Nirvana, or perhaps someone behind them, was the driving force behind the two Rustfire crises in N.F. 104 and N.F. 113, correct?

9th Agency Member: The truth, huh…

The official from the 9th Agency takes out a well-sealed document from his briefcase and places it on the office desk with a smile.

9th Agency Member: This is what you're looking for... The 9th Agency won't prevent you from probing for the so-called truth. But remember…

Rahu: State your purpose.

Under the bright light, the woman's face is shrouded in shadow.

**【Advance】**

- Routine Interrogation

**end**

Rahu: You call this a routine interrogation?

Rahu looks around. The cramped interrogation room is filled only with the hum of cold surveillance equipment and halogen lamps.

Chief: You've accepted your *transfer* to the MBCC and you're an S-Class Sinner. Undergoing this form of investigation is your duty.

Rahu looks into your eyes, and after a moment, a cold, emotionless smile appears on her face.

Rahu: Yes, Commander.

You take out a document envelope labeled with Rahu's name and place it on the table.

**【Advance】**

- Tell me about your experience at the FAC.

**end**

Rahu glances at the envelope on the table.

Rahu: Why ask if you already know?

Chief: Files often contain omissions and errors. I'd like to hear your version.

Rahu doesn't waste more time and begins to talk about her past.

Rahu: I was born in Syndicate, and was recruited by the FAC in N.F. 102. I'd worked for the FAC for a total of 11 years since.

Rahu: Until last week. The higher-ups thought I could be of more use here.

Rahu doesn't seem too pleased when she mentions *the higher-ups' decision*.

Chief: Is that all? Can you go into more detail?

Rahu: What kind of detail?

Chief: For instance, the reason you joined the FAC.

Rahu: Reason?

Rahu laughs, as if it's an unwarranted, foolish question that's been asked too many times.

Rahu: Ha... To survive.

Rahu: Life in Syndicate is more dangerous than in the FAC.

Chief: As far as I know, the FAC doesn't often recruit from the West District. How did you come on their radar?

Rahu: A squad was carrying out a mission in Syndicate at the time. I met their captain, who referred me to the raw recruit camp for trial training.

Chief: And the captain's name is?

Rahu: ...

Rahu pauses for a moment, then casually says.

Rahu: I don't remember.

Chief: Really?

She's lying.

The file states that after completing her training, Rahu joined that captain's squad. For almost two years, they participated in over twenty operations together.

Chief: As far as I know, you were part of that captain's squad up until the squad was wiped out in N.F. 103.

Chief: You've repeatedly demanded the *truth* about the DisSea catastrophe from your superiors. You've even gone as far as barging into the FAC executives' office and making a scene. Cost you two demerits on the record.

Chief: Now, tell me again you don't remember the name of that officer?

Rahu doesn't respond and just stares intently at the file in your hand for a long time.

After a long silence, she suddenly begins.

Rahu: Post-traumatic stress disorder... I've received treatment from the FAC.

Rahu: What I don't need to remember, what I shouldn't remember, I don't. This is better for me, and for everyone else.

Rahu's statements are consistent with the records in the file.

Clearly, the counter-interrogation training she received at the FAC wasn't wasted. Dwelling on this topic is pointless, and might even result in accusations of *veteran persecution*.

But you know she's not telling the truth, or at least not all of it. You casually flip through the file, trying to move past the previous topic.

Chief: I'm glad you were able to move on from that experience.

Rahu: ...Glad?

Rahu questions, and you can detect a barely noticeable hint of anger in her tone.

Even though she's the one who brought up things like "having received treatment" and "better for everyone," her irritation at your words still peeks through the careful concealment.

Chief: We're not enemies here, Rahu. We're now colleagues who are about to begin a long-term working relationship.

Chief: Your eleven years at the FAC have proven your loyalty and capability. As long as you can clear up the *minor flaws* on your record, I would naturally be grateful to have such an excellent subordinate.

You explain. Meanwhile, you abruptly stop flipping the file, landing on the last page.

Chief: In July N.F. 113, you left the FAC headquarters to go to Erica Villa. What for?

Taken aback by your sudden change of topic, Rahu hesitates slightly, but quickly recovers.

Rahu: Vacation.

Chief: That's not a vacation spot for ordinary people.

Rahu: ...It was a private matter. I'm not answering that.

Chief: You were there to meet someone, weren't you?

Chief: You went to Erica Villa behind the back of your colleagues at the FAC, and there you met a certain *big shot*...

Rahu: Haha, I underestimated you...

Rahu's snicker interrupts you.

Rahu: The intelligence unit of the MBCC is quite something. What a bold move.

Rahu's smile disappears without a trace, leaving only a cold stare.

Rahu: But you better keep your subordinates in check. Don't overreach yourselves.

The threat in her words is clear, and you respond calmly.

Chief: That's none of your concern. My sources are quite reliable.

Rahu: The 9th Agency, right?

You ignore Rahu's almost provocative question and continue with the previous topic.

Chief: What was your real purpose for going to Erica Villa?

Rahu: Why don't you ask your reliable sources?

Chief: I want to hear it from you.

Rahu: ...

Rahu: They sent out a distress signal on the public channel. I received it, and then I went. It's as simple as that.

Chief: Before responding to the distress signal, did you know a high-ranking official from Paradeisos was in Erica Villa?

Rahu looks directly at you, as if she's scrutinizing you for your motive. After a few seconds of silence, she suddenly bursts into laughter.

Rahu: Haha, will you take no for an answer?

Chief: ...

Rahu: Of course, I did. If the Paradeisian official wasn't there, why should I care if they were dead or alive?

Chief: So you went for the Paradeisian official in the first place? Why?

Rahu: To butter them up so I could leave the FAC. Why else?

Chief: You'd been serving in the FAC for over ten years, why the sudden switch?

Rahu: It wasn't sudden. I'd been wanting to change jobs for a while.

Her tone no longer carries the tension of someone being interrogated, but is instead filled with casual mockery and indifference, as if she sees through you.

Rahu: Indeed, I had served in the FAC for over ten years and participated in hundreds of operations, big and small. Since BR-002, my colleagues came and went. Those older and younger than me have all died, none of them had a good end.

Rahu: I want to live. Those mercs working as bodyguards for Paradeisos make it to retirement alive, why can't we FAC soldiers do the same? Have we not shed enough blood already?

Rahu: A job from a Paradeisian bigwig can't be that bad.

Rahu leans back slightly and you hear the creaks of the chair.

Chief: What about your squad? You just left them behind like that?

Rahu: Not all of us are saints, ${PlayerName}. FAC soldiers are nothing more than ordinary people who feel lucky to survive another day.

Rahu: Having been through so many brutal battles, it's only natural that one would want to plan ahead.

Rahu: Not to mention that I don't owe them anything. I'm not irreplaceable to them either.

Rahu's voice is cold, and the facts she states are consistent with the records. Ethically speaking, she indeed had no obligation to stay in the FAC forever.

But something just doesn't sit right with you. Logically, what she says makes sense, but it doesn't exactly match her character.

These words seem like a bastion built of countless lies, so deliberately designed and seamless that it's virtually impossible to spot a crack.

Whoever this *designer* is, they must possess unimaginable power. Such means and power can't be found everywhere in DisCity.

The big shot from Paradeisos, the puppet master behind Rustfire, and the *opportunity* Rahu has mentioned. These elusive fragments are beginning to overlap in your mind.

Chief: Did that Paradeisian official accept your proposal?

Rahu: ?

Rahu seems stumped by the question, caught off guard without a well-prepared response.

Chief: Now you're here in the MBCC, not by the side of that big shot.

Chief: Have you been abandoned?

Rahu's expression gradually turns icy.

Chief: Or, is this the *good job* that big shot has offered you? Transferring to the MBCC?

Chief: Let's see... You're an experienced and powerful combatant, an elite who can be entrusted with important tasks anywhere.

Chief: Why give up such a capable assistant and send you to me?

Chief: To kill me? No, too inefficient. To watch me? Possibly.

Chief: But why would that big shot ignore the 9th Agency and directly monitor me instead? Is it about Rustfire? Or maybe it's related to the attack I suffered at the Drifter Camp...

Rahu: Hahaha...

The cold, abrupt snicker interrupts you.

Rahu: I've seen many like you who thought they could see through everything and grasp the truth.

Rahu: Guess what happened to them?

Rahu's smile is overlaid with a shadow.

Chief: Are you threatening me?

Rahu: Consider this a threat if you will.

Rahu: Your *curiosity* is too brazen, whether it's about the truth or that big shot... Your thoughts are practically written all over your face.

Rahu: Stop deluding yourself that you can pursue the *truth*. Know your place, you're only the chief of this little bureau. Or else, you risk your life.

Rahu: You've been doing well so far. Stop now, and they might overlook this little misbehavior.

Rahu: But if you insist on pushing further, well...

Rahu looks up, her eyes flashing with a dangerous light.

Rahu: DisCity is built on the bones of those who betray and cross the line. No one can protect you, not even the 9th Agency.

Rahu: You're dealing with that kind of *big shot*.

Rahu's tone is incredibly calm, but what's hidden beneath the calm surface makes your hair stand on end for a moment.

You take a deep breath, trying to calm yourself.

Chief: Thank you for your advice, Rahu.

Chief: I think I can tame my curiosity. I can get the information I need without crossing the line.

Chief: At least I won't end up like Conrad Cage.

Rahu: ...Conrad?

You catch a complex emotion flickering in Rahu's eyes at the mention of this name.

Chief: Don't you know?

Rahu: I'm not sure what you mean by that.

Her face no longer changes, all emotions hidden behind those cold eyes.

Chief: Conrad Cage joined the FAC in N.F. 100. He was in the G47 squad just like you. In N.F. 103, he missed the DisSea operation due to injury. He and you ended up being the only two survivors of the G47 squad that year.

Chief: After that, he took over the position of the previous officer, becoming your direct superior. For over a decade, you served in the same squad.

Chief: Until █ months ago, shortly after you went to Erica Villa, Conrad's squad suffered an accident in a battle...

Chief: In short, he was the only survivor in the whole squad.

Chief: Or rather, you two are. After all, you're still officially part of the G47 squad.

Rahu's pupils suddenly contract. What baffles you is that she seems to be in complete disbelief after hearing these facts from you.

As if she just heard it for the first time.

Chief: Rings a bell, doesn't it?

Chief: Ten years ago, you belonged to the same squad. He was absent due to injury, and you were the only survivor of the battle.

Chief: I thought you two had a good relationship. After all, he was your direct superior, and also the one who vouched for you when you left the squad to go to Erica Villa...

Rahu: What happened to him?

Rahu interrupts you.

Chief: ...A lot.

Chief: According to the FAC, Conrad has been affected by Mania and is facing serious mental issues.

Chief: He blames everything in DisCity on conspiracy and that he's seen... Well, in short, he's been spreading some anti-social rhetoric.

Chief: Not only that, he planned an attack against some important person and seriously jeopardized public safety. Fortunately, the plan was exposed before it could cause major damage.

Rahu: And what does this have to do with me?

Unconsciously, Rahu's expression changes from suspicious to cautious.

Chief: Relax, you're not under suspicion yet. However...

Chief: However, Conrad's treatment isn't going well. He's undergone rigorous psychological training in the past, which is now working against him. He specifically refuses treatment from doctors and has even shown highly aggressive behaviors.

Chief: A professional institution has contacted the FAC, claiming they can take over him and provide him with appropriate treatment. However, the FAC hasn't accepted the offer yet.

Rahu: ...

Chief: They're seeking help from us, an organization experienced in dealing with those infected with Mania... Perhaps because they know that you've recently been transferred here.

Chief: You see, the MBCC has always been an open book to other departments.

You shrug and try to subtly slow down the conversation to gauge Rahu's reaction. But her expression remains unchanged as always.

Chief: They probably don't expect much from me. Instead, I think they're hoping that you, Conrad's former comrade, can provide some help.

Chief: Of course, I completely understand if you don't want to go. All I can do is try to get Conrad's version of the *truth*. Hopefully that'll at least help the FAC's medical department analyze the cause of his mental disorder...

Rahu: I'll go.

Rahu's answer is curt but firm.

Chief: Great.

Chief: This is your first field assignment in the Bureau. Break a leg.

Rahu doesn't respond to that. Somehow, the two of you seem to have reached a mutual understanding.

Rather than to help a former comrade, she's probably agreed to go in order to monitor any potential meddling on your part. But that doesn't matter.

You've achieved your goal.

Conrad: ...

Chief: Nice to meet you, Mr. Conrad Cage...

Conrad: Rahu?!

Conrad: Haha... Hahaha... How dare you come back?

Rahu: To pick up your bones.

Conrad: Haha... You still remember that...

Conrad: And who is this?

The man's glance shifts to you, eyes filled with undisguised hostility and wariness.

Chief: I am ${PlayerName}, the Chief of the Minos Bureau of Crisis Control.

Rahu: What exactly happened?

Rahu doesn't respond to the man's accusation and only presses him for his side of the story.

Conrad: What happened? Hahaha...

Conrad: The same that happened in N.F. 103. Everyone died, right before my eyes and without reason. I was the only one lucky enough to have been salvaged like a piece of garbage...

Conrad: I'm beginning to understand what you felt back then, Rahu. I even thought you were the crazy one at the time. Turns out I just hadn't seen the truth yet.

Conrad: Someone must pay for this... Paradeisos... will pay for this...

The man's voice is filled with spite and grudge and black veins are emerging on his face.

**【Advance】**

- Inquire Conrad for information

**end**

Chief: What exactly is this *truth* you're talking about?

Conrad: Heh...

The man lets out a cold snort.

Conrad: Why should I tell you?

Chief: Anyone, even Paradeisos, must be held accountable for their crimes. This is a rule I live by.

Chief: But there must be evidence that can prove all of this...

The man squints at you, then his gaze shifts to Rahu beside you. After a moment, he speaks.

Conrad: Of course I've got evidence... Proof that Paradeisos killed Varna... and sent us on a suicide mission for nothing... West District, Inner Bay, DisSea, everything they've meddled in over these ten years...

Conrad: The question is: Why should I believe that you're not one of them?

Conrad: Paradeisos has got plenty of lapdogs.

As he gives these answers, the man's gaze is fixed on Rahu, as if expecting her to react in some way.

Rahu: Where's the evidence?

Rahu's voice is cold and her eyes are locked on Conrad. Before you can say anything, you feel an intimidating aura about her, warning you to shut up and back off.

Rahu: Those are all false information, lies fabricated by the Underground to confuse people. You've been used. Hand it over to me, I'll handle it.

Rahu: FAC soldiers are obedient, disciplined, and dedicated to the battlefield. You are the most honorable and honest people who haven't seen true evil. You don't have the time to seek the truth and validate it.

Rahu: It doesn't matter who gave you this *evidence*, it's all fake. They're just using you to undermine Paradeisos.

Rahu: Hand it over.

Conrad: ...That's all you have to say to me? Even now, you've got nothing else to tell me?

Conrad: To hell with the Underground! False information? You ever wonder who gave me this fake evidence? ...You did!

Conrad: This is the evidence you collected yourself! You wanted this! The real Rahu wanted this! You went through hell for the truth, and listen to what you're saying now!

Conrad: You're not Rahu. Who the hell are you?!

The man screams maniacally, breaking free from his restraints and lunging at you. His almost pitiful wails fill the air, bringing with it a wave of contamination.

Rahu pushes you aside and catches Conrad's flailing hands, pinning him to the ground. She mercilessly drives a Hypercube into his palm, brutally subduing this man driven to madness by grief. Her movements are swift, precise, and without hesitation.

Meanwhile, your shackles are subtly pulsating.

Rahu: I am Rahu. I am the lucid one.

Rahu: You're simply caught in the illusions of your past, lying to yourself. If you stay too far from the true reality, you'll only die trying to reach it.

Rahu: Look ahead, Conrad, just like you once told me... Look ahead! Weren't you always the level-headed one?

Conrad: ...Look ahead?

Conrad: Like a mad dog, your rage was satisfying, and it freed me from my hatred. I was only able to be *level-headed* because you vented the anger for all of us.

Conrad: And look at what you've become. How am I supposed to keep looking ahead?!

The man jerks his head up. Mania has eroded his sanity, allowing his deepest emotions to spill out uninhibitedly. His dark pupils are filled with despair and grudge.

Conrad: I shouldn't have let you go to that villa... I shouldn't have let you go alone... What happened there?

Conrad: Was it Paradeisos? Or those assassins... What did they do to you? Why did you become like this...?

Conrad: Where is Rahu... Where did my last comrade go...

Conrad: Did you kill her?!!!

The man screams hysterically. The veins on his face twitch uncontrollably and dark scars appear on his pale skin. His emotional outburst is causing his Mania to flare up.

Rahu's Hypercube is almost pressed into his body and it's being rapidly depleted. Contamination keeps spilling out and starts to corrupt Rahu's body, her face showing crimson traces of Mania...

But she doesn't let go. Rahu holds her grip on Conrad firmly, staring intently into his eyes. You see intense emotions in her gaze. The shackles tremble violently. You can't tell whether it's from Conrad's Mania or Rahu's overflowing emotions, but now is not the time to get distracted.

You must react at once.

**【Advance】**

- Use the shackles

**end**

Chief: Rahu, step aside!

You impose a *prohibition* on her. In that instant, anger burns in her eyes. She looks as if she's about to pounce and bite at your neck just to protect that man. For the first time, you see the real her, and you make sure to remember this.

Then you try your best to subdue this furious Sinner, almost exhausting the power of the shackles to force her to step back. She must stay away from the contamination, and you must do what has to be done...

Chief: Hang in there, Conrad!

Conrad's Mania is on the verge of eruption, and you use all your strength to suppress it with the shackles. You've been warned by the medical department that compensating for those on the brink of Corruptor transformation is extremely dangerous, but this man cannot die.

Whether it's to save a good man's life, a ruined truth, or the Sinner behind you who's almost at her breaking point... Conrad cannot die. This much is clear to you.

The man finally opens his eyes. After the torment of Mania, his gaze is now confused, cloudy, and shattered. He glances at Rahu, then at you. Conrad slightly opens his mouth.

Finally, he speaks.

Conrad: Again... Not enough... Just a little longer...

Squirming, he extends his hand to you.

Conrad: Just a little longer...

The man's cloudy eyes turn to you but fail to focus.

**【Advance】**

- Promise Conrad

**end**

His eyes remain closed, but he grips your hand tightly with surprising strength, his fingers stroking your palm as if cherishing this moment of tranquility and clarity.

But you quickly realize something is off. The rhythm of the man's fingers is too regular, as if he's conveying a short message to you...

Ornstein 47 301 Ornstein 47 301 Ornstein 47 301...

He repeats it over and over for fear that you don't understand his intention.

**【Advance】**

- Let him know you've taken note.

**end**

You firmly grip the man's hand. He understands your meaning.

Conrad: Haha... Get lost... All of you, get lost...

The man closes his eyes wearily, falling silent. But he's obviously still alive, his Mania temporarily stabilized.

You look at Rahu. Her emotions have been perfectly concealed. The knight in black silently stands behind you, watching the two of you without giving away any of her true feelings. Only the forgotten cold sweat on her forehead is proof of her fleeting true self.

Chief: He's safe for now. Someone will take care of him. Let's go, we still have things to discuss.

She watches as you help Conrad onto the bed and cover him with a blanket. Wordlessly, she watches the man trapped in despair and pain fall into a deep sleep. Rahu simply closes her eyes for a moment, then looks at you.

Rahu: Yes, Commander.

Leaving the dim sickroom, you and Rahu are back on the other side of the door.

You nod to the guard and drag your still-exhausted body through the deserted corridor.

The corridor is empty and quiet save for your and Rahu's footsteps. Suddenly, you hear her icy voice from behind.

Rahu: What did he give you?

As expected, she noticed Conrad's discreet gesture. She must also remember that soldier's secretive nature.

You make sure there's enough distance between you two and judge that you'll probably be able to use the shackles for self-defense, then turn around and spread your hands completely.

**【Advance】**

- Are you interrogating me? Me, your *Commander*?

**end**

Rahu: No, but it's my duty to keep the *Commander* safe.

Rahu: Conrad was reckless and flouted the taboo, and he ended up in this state. He's not a Sinner. Your shackles can't suppress the contamination in him. He's already become a huge threat to DisCity.

Chief: Are you asking me to banish him? Or kill him? You know I won't do that.

Rahu: I know you won't. He's useful to you.

Rahu: Then heed my advice. Keep this confidential. Lock him up in the toughest prison cell you can find and post your best fighters to guard him around the clock. Otherwise, the people who provoked him will surely come knocking on your door.

Rahu: This is all... for your own safety.

Chief: Of course, I will do as you say.

**【Advance】**

- If you're so keen to protect him through me...

**end**

**【Advance】**

- Just kidding.

**end**

Chief: I'll apply to the FAC to transfer him to the Bureau. Although he's not a Sinner, my power did work on him just now. We could use an extra observation subject.

Chief: And I am indeed interested in him. I really want to know... what is it that he is fighting so desperately for all by himself.
