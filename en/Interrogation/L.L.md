### Interrogation Transcript

The weekend weather is splendid, with the clear skies carrying not a wisp of cloud. Even the gentle breeze is adorned with the faint scent of newly bloomed flowers.

Noon is the busiest time of the day, and the streets teem with bustling crowds. Despite having to regularly soothe herself with water, Lore-Ley doesn't want to miss any of the views before her.

Lore-Ley: I read online before that the human eye has a higher resolution than any camera, and it's true! Look at the foliage under the sunlight! Such a beautiful green! Online pics can't compare to the real thing at all!

At first, she trailed behind you timidly, but now she's running around like an excited critter bursting with curiosity for everything around her.

She skips along under the shade of the trees, where light specks fall through the canopy and sparkle on her feet with each step.

Even a trivial thing like this is enough to make her happy for quite a while.

Lore-Ley: Hmm... If feels a bit suffocating to walk under those tall buildings. This isn't what they call "megalophobia," is it?

Lore-Ley: Look! The birds are standing in a row on the wire... Ah, they all flew away.

Lore-Ley: A leaf has fallen off the branch over there! I can definitely catch it before it hits the ground!

Lore-Ley: ...

She would suddenly stop and inhale the floral scent in the air, then eagerly share with you the subtle differences between the flowers on this street and the last.

She would also crouch by the greenery to touch the soil, buds, and cement pavement on the other side, then carefully compare their differences.

She sniffs her soil-stained fingers and reflects to herself.

Lore-Ley: It feels slick and cool, but it smells like this? It's different from what I imagined...

Hearing her mumbling to herself, you squat down beside her and rub some soil with your fingertips.

Chief: If it were a rainy day, the smell would change again.

Your childish actions draw curious glances from onlookers, but you don't mind. You listen to her intently and respond accordingly.

Immersed in her own world, Lore-Ley is talking a lot more. After years of being confined in a tub, things that are ordinary to most people are a brand new experience for her.

This might be the first time in her life she can truly step into, observe, and explore this vast world. You want her to experience as much as possible during this meaningful opportunity, and not let any of her expectations fall short.

Lore-Ley: Change again? To what? I often saw people online say they like the "smell of soil after the rain," but what kind of smell is that exactly?

Chief: Hmm... I guess it's a moist smell?

Lore-Ley: What does "moist" smell like? Is it the same as the smell of wet clothes?

She continues to ask, but this question stumps you. You're not sure how to answer, so you awkwardly scratch your head.

Chief: Uh... It's hard to explain in words. How about we wait for a rainy day and then come out to smell it together?

Lore-Ley: Sure, I'll remember that!!

She nods vigorously, her smile bright and vivid. She summons her cute little manta ray and solemnly jots down the promise between you and her.

Lore-Ley: ...Huh? Could that be...!

Chief: Wait...

Chief: Looks like it really likes you.

Lore-Ley: I've watched a lot of cat and dog videos online, but I've never seen them in real life. They seem even livelier and cuter than in the videos.

Chief: Want to take a look? Maybe you can even pet them if the staff allows.

Lore-Ley: ...Really!?

Her eyes light up as she looks at you, brimming with joy and anticipation. You smile and nod.

Chief: Of course.

The pet store owner kindly agrees to your request. After cleaning your hands and clothes as requested, you're allowed into the activity area.

In a sunlit corner of the activity area, you sit quietly and observe the girl play happily with the puppy.

The puppy energetically barks and jumps, eagerly fetching the ball again and again. Lore-Ley can't help but laugh when the puppy licks her fingers.

After a while, the puppy gets tired and falls asleep on Lore-Ley's lap. Lore-Ley strokes the puppy's white fur, murmuring to herself.

Lore-Ley: So this is what touching an animal feels like. Warm, dry, fluffy, and very soft... so different from my skin, which is always cold and slippery from the water.

Chief: What are you thinking about?

Lore-Ley: Ada also had a puppy like this one.

Chief: Ada?

Lore-Ley: Ada... was a friend I met online a long time ago.
