# Enfer Interrogation

## Enfer - Interrogation I

> Enfer, too, knows little about the Witching Hour's power, but you both are certain it is related to Sinner power.
> Enfer wants you to visit Belphegor with her again for an investigation.

The woman in the blue dress is sitting up straight in the interrogation room.

As usual, there's a smile on her face, just like the first time you met her.

Enfer: It's been a while. Sorry for causing you trouble while I was asleep.

After the Witching Hour incident was resolved, having absorbed the manic power, Enfer awakened as an S-Class Sinner. She fell into a coma and has only come back to consciousness a few days ago.

Having heard from the doctor that she's been recovering well, you decided to come and meet her.

**【Advance】**

- Don't worry, I was just doing my job.

**end**

Enfer: Your job... Haha...

Enfer: That word makes our relationship sound... Quite distant.

Enfer raises her hands and gestures the distance between you and her.

Chief: Sorry. This is just a standard procedure.

Chief: I don't mean to make things difficult for you.

Enfer: No worries, me neither.

Enfer: But... I don't think you came all the way here just to say hi, Chief.

Chief: Indeed, I didn't. But still, I'm glad to see that you're well.

Enfer: It's all thanks to you.

Chief: That's good.

Chief: I know this could seem a bit... rushed, but there are things I want to know, too, so...

Chief: If you think you can't answer these questions at this moment, we can take our time.

Enfer: Go ahead. I'm not that fragile.

Chief: Here's some information about the current situation of the Belphegor Art Museum.

Chief: Although you've already absorbed the Witching Hour and I've put the shackles on you, we still haven't figured out where the Witching Hour's power came from.

Enfer stares at the documents and photos you've laid out on the desk wordlessly.

Her fingers touch the photos gently. In the photos, almost all the artwork displayed in Belphegor have been crushed into pieces.

Chief: I'm sorry. Perhaps I shouldn't have shown you these...

Enfer: That's alright.

Enfer: It wasn't you who broke them.

Enfer: Go on.

You look at Enfer, grit your teeth, and continue.

Chief: The power of the Witching Hour was strong enough to make a regular Sinner evolve into an S-Class Sinner.

Chief: Even though I was there, I still can't figure out the origin of this power.

Chief: Its origin is unknown, the cause of evolution is unknown, and most terrifying of all... even its limit is unknown.

Chief: You must know that Eastside... has always been prejudiced against Mania. They refuse to take charge of the cleanup work in the Belphegor Art Museum.

Chief: Since we still can't fully comprehend the Witching Hour... in a sense, the fear and chaos brought by it haven't come to an end yet.

**【Advance】**

- Enfer... I need your help.

**end**

Enfer draws back her hand thumbing the photos and lifts her head to stare at you firmly.

Enfer: I don't know...

Chief: But...

Enfer: Before you put the shackles on me, I didn't really know what a Sinner was.

Enfer: And I have no idea why the Witching Hour's power could turn me into what you refer to as an S-Class.

Enfer: But since it could cause such an outcome, perhaps it means... the Witching Hour's power is the same as a Sinner's power.

Enfer props up her face and seems to be caught up in her thoughts.

Chief: So... you mean to say that the Witching Hour's power has the same origin as a Sinner's power?

Enfer: Yes... So...

Enfer: Where does a Sinner's power come from?

Enfer looks at you with a faint yearning in her eyes.

You raise your hand and gesture for her to look at the blue heart-shaped accessory on her own chest.

Chief: I think Sinners' powers probably come from their own hearts.

Enfer: ...

Enfer: If you're right...

Enfer: You're the Chief, the Sinners' manager... You ought to be right.

Enfer: According to you, could it be that the Witching Hour's power came from my heart?

Chief: I think part of the Witching Hour's power came out of Parma's stones from which your sculptures were made, and part of it came from the visitors' inner fear.

Chief: As for the power to accept and absorb everything, awaken as an S-Class Sinner, and defeat the Witching Hour... That came from your heart.

Enfer gives a laugh and then falls silent again.

Chief: When Sinners... awaken, their hearts are usually filled with some kind of thought, emotion, or... desire.

Chief: Does that remind you of anything?

Enfer looks at you and laughs again.

Enfer: Both you and I experienced the Witching Hour firsthand, do you still not understand?

Enfer: For me, my heart has always been yearning for fear.

Enfer: That's the truth in people's hearts. That's the truth of the world.

Enfer taps on a photo on the desk. In the photo, there's a broken sculpture.

Enfer: For example, the traces of wind...

Enfer: Everything in this world, even the wind, leaves its traces.

Enfer: After it spreads sickness, wails, and despair all over the world, what's left is this devilish dead branch.

Enfer: These dead branches are all around us. That's the truth told by the wind.

As Enfer pushes the photo in her hand away and is about to tell you about the *truth of fear* in the next photo, she halts.

The devastated sculpture in that photo is Sosia.

Enfer: But Sosia... seemed to be different...

Enfer: She's indeed a piece I created. Yet, she seemed to understand me better than I do.

Enfer: She believed that I was lost and weak... She thought my fear was not pure, and that I didn't understand myself.

Enfer: ...She thought that I've never abandoned love, all this time.

Enfer frowns and recalls the words Sosia once shouted at her.

Enfer: But... I have abandoned love such a long time ago...

Enfer: I have seen how fake, how fragile, how defenseless love is...

Enfer: When the land cracks and dark clouds gather in the sky, only fear can keep us barely alive.

Enfer: How can love... still possibly exist? Or perhaps I should say... How can it possibly still exist in me?

Enfer is deep in thought again. You shake your head slightly and ask her a question.

Chief: Have you ever heard of that fable?

Chief: The North Wind and the Sun.

Enfer looks dazed for a second, then nods at you. She seems interested in your words and signals you to continue.

Chief: The North Wind and the Sun have a bet on which of them can make people take off the coat they're wearing.

Chief: No matter how hard the North Wind blows, no matter how it raises the dust and lowers the temperature, it only makes them pull the coat tighter and tighter.

Chief: Meanwhile, the sun emerges behind the clouds and shines over the land, making everyone sweat. All the passers-by wearing coats begin to take them off.

Enfer: So... you think I'm the North Wind?

**【Advance】**

- No.

**end**

Chief: I just want to tell you that, the North Wind and the wails do exist, no matter what. But they are definitely not the only things we perceive.

Chief: Even as we walk along the end... There is love, and sunshine.

Chief: Without love... how do you create these works of art that you deem as your own children?

Enfer: ...

Enfer: ...It's just fear.

Chief: You're lost, Enfer.

In your mind, you can feel the shackles bound on Enfer throb. Your words have made her waver.

Enfer: Yes... I am indeed a little lost.

Enfer: I've always thought I'm pure enough, but I can't even refute you promptly.

Enfer: What kind of thoughts and emotions was I having when I became a Sinner?

Enfer: And when I created them... Was there really... only fear?

Enfer gives a sigh, then smiles at you.

Enfer: You don't know the origin of the Witching Hour, so you've come to ask the *Witch*.

Enfer: Surprise, even the *Witch* doesn't know where her power comes from!

Chief: That's okay. I can look for it with you.

Enfer: Huh?

You look at Enfer and give her the same smile as hers.

Chief: I've said that before. Wherever you go, I'll stay beside you.

Chief: So... If you want to find out why you're lost, where your power comes from.

Chief: I'll go with you.

Though a little surprised, Enfer accepts your kindness with a smile.

Enfer: Thank you, Chief.

Enfer: In that case... why don't you come with me to Belphegor?

Chief: Back to the art museum?

Enfer: If we want to find the source of the Witching Hour's power, that should be the best place to start!

Enfer: Who knows, I may be able to recall something from my past creations.

Enfer: Also... Didn't you say no one dared to clean up the pieces inside?

Enfer: Who else would be more suited for this job than us?

Chief: That's true.

Chief: In fact, the FAC initially wanted me and my Sinners to handle this as well.

Chief: It's just that... I must first confirm that my Sinner won't go manic under the influence of the Witching Hour again.

Chief: In other words...

You wave your hand in front of Enfer.

**【Advance】**

- I must make sure you'll obey me.

**end**

Enfer: Hehe...

Enfer props her chin on her palm, her eyes curving with her smile.

Enfer: Am I not obedient enough, Honored Chief?

## Enfer - Interrogation II

> Enfer re-creates a small sculpture with the fragments of her works. You find on the sculpture a tiny heart-shaped mark, just like the one present on most of Enfer's works.
> But that mark is missing on a painting in the art museum painted by Enfer when she was little...
> Enfer seems to recall something and hopes that you can accompany her back to her home.

Days later, you and Enfer finally step inside the Belphegor Art Museum again.

Sunlight pours in through the glass and shines evenly over the chaos in the museum. Dust and debris float in the air like lost moths struggling to find light.

Enfer: Well, this place is surely in ruins.

Even without the bewildering *magic*, you still recognize you're in the exhibition hall where Sosia was displayed.

But all the art exhibits in the exhibition hall have been wrecked into pieces, not just Sosia.

You vaguely recall Enfer has said that the works displayed in this hall are like a record of her own growth.

Chief: Actually, we can leave this to the end...

Enfer: It's okay. I'm not that vulnerable.

Enfer: "The heart can melt even sadness."

Enfer: You've seen it before.

Chief: Yes, neither of us needs to cry.

Enfer: That's right.

Enfer puts down the tools in her hands and picks up a fragment, examining it carefully.

Enfer: Sleeper Under Fear. Apparently, it reminded many viewers of sleepless nights troubled by nightmares.

Enfer: But I guess now it can finally have a good night's sleep.

Sweeping away the debris of different materials, Enfer gathers the fragments of this piece in a corner, piling them up.

This is like a home for all the slumbering art.

Enfer: "Under the heavy weight, you and I are both within the *iron frame*. The bars seem so brittle and delicate, yet impossible to break..."

Enfer: But when the night passes, the iron frame finally snaps...

Watching Enfer piecing her art together among the fragments, you suddenly feel sorry for her. You pass her a tissue and apologize to her.

Chief: I'm sorry. Perhaps I was too self-absorbed with my proposal.

Enfer: Why would you think that?

Chief: I know you treat your art like your children... Asking you to face this kind of... this kind of situation directly, maybe it's kind of cruel.

Chief: I didn't think it through.

Enfer takes the tissue and shakes her head slightly.

Enfer: You don't have to apologize. I asked to come with you.

Enfer: Also... you don't need to feel sad for me.

Enfer comes to the platform that was used to display Sosia, lifting her head and staring as if Sosia is still standing there.

Enfer: How empty...

Enfer: Even her *friends* who once kept her company have all become broken pieces.

Enfer gently picks up a white fragment which used to be part of a small sculpture surrounding Sosia, probably an eye.

**【Choose One】**

- Do you want to repair her?

- You will create even better works.

**end**

Enfer: Repair?

Chief: These are all your favorite works, aren't they?

Chief: Of course, I believe you will certainly create something even more perfect than this in the future...

Chief: But I know you love her very much.

Enfer smiles and says nothing. You think she's still hesitating, so you continue.

Chief: We've already resolved the Witching Hour. She won't be an issue.

Chief: The Sinners have already brought all sorts of bizarre belongings to the Bureau anyway. It's only one more sculpture, I think... Nightingale won't say no.

Chief: But I know these are all your favorite works.

Chief: We've already resolved the Witching Hour. They won't be an issue.

Chief: The Sinners have already brought all sorts of bizarre belongings to the Bureau anyway. It's only one more sculpture, I think... Nightingale won't say no.

Enfer: So you want me to repair them?

Chief: You love them very much, don't you?

Enfer: Indeed.

Enfer nods at you and picks up a bunch of fragments.

But you find that she's not repairing Sosia, but carving something new with her hammer and chisel instead.

You hear lots of clinks and clanks, but they don't bother you at all. You lean against the platform and listen to the sound peacefully.

It sounds like a beak knocking on an eggshell.

Enfer: ...Huff.

A moment later, the sound stops. You lift your head and see Enfer propping up her face on the platform and staring down at you with a smile.

Enfer: I thought you fell asleep.

Enfer: Come and see my work, will you?

She reaches out a hand to you, pulling you up like inviting you to dance.

Next to her are a few small sculptures carved with the fragments.

Chief: What... are these?

The broken pieces have been sculpted and polished by Enfer and turned into several cocoon-like objects.

But the irregular shapes make you feel like something is struggling inside them.

Enfer: Just some artistic residue.

Chief: They're one of a kind.

But even you can tell that these small sculptures... are indeed nothing close to Enfer's previous works.

You don't know how else to console Enfer, so you pick up the small sculptures and examine them carefully.

You see that in the center of each sculpture, there's a tiny heart-shaped mark.

Enfer: You can be more honest with me.

Enfer raises her hand and taps on one of the sculptures lightly. The cocoon falls instantly.

Enfer: These things... can't even compare to what I made three years ago.

Chief: You're... exaggerating.

Enfer: No, I'm not.

Enfer scans the empty exhibition hall. The room filled only with broken fragments is like an airtight prison. Suffocating and heart-wrenching, it feels impossible to break free from it no matter how hard you try.

Enfer: But that's nothing to be afraid of.

Enfer: That's... how an artist should be.

Enfer: As we create, we struggle, we grow, we go back to zero, and eventually we evolve into our complete form.

Enfer spreads her arms, flying up the blue fabric of her dress. You feel as if you're seeing a pair of wings lined with shiny scales.

Enfer: The works we created are the proof that we tear ourselves apart time after time.

Enfer: Like breaking one cocoon after another, eventually, a stunningly beautiful moth is born.

Enfer: And...

She turns back and looks at you, smiling.

Enfer: After impressing everyone, it'll plunge into the flame and leave the world with a perfect residue.

Enfer: These artworks...

Enfer hands a fragment to you. It's a piece of alabaster with pure coloration. Its sections are rough, but the outer surface is finely polished.

The dented carved lines resemble a veil on a woman's body blown by the wind. A heart-shaped mark is also hidden among them.

Enfer: These are the cocoons artists shed off, or the marks left by a fire that destroyed everything...

Enfer: Even if they're completely broken, they're proof that the artist once existed.

Enfer: It's just that... fragments tend to be forgotten by the world.

Enfer: That's why... I want to create an unforgettable, perfect mark.

The fragment is being crushed in Enfer's hand, falling through her fingers.

Enfer: No matter what perplexity lies ahead... I'll destroy it.

**【Advance】**

- I believe in you.

**end**

Enfer: Haha... You're so adorable.

Enfer: Thank you, I guess.

Enfer: After all, I've made the decision to crush the perplexity troubling me.

Enfer: Sosia has failed, and I'm stuck in a bottle right now.

Enfer: But that doesn't mean I'll keep failing. Just like how I crushed Sosia, I'll find the reason for my weakness, crush this bottle, and break the cocoon once again.

Enfer: No matter how much pain I'll have to endure.

Enfer: That being said...

Enfer drags out the word, making you a little nervous.

Enfer: If we don't start cleaning up soon, I'm afraid we'll experience the *Witching Hour* again.

You shrug and begin tidying the place with Enfer again.

As you're working, you pay extra attention and notice that there's also a heart-shaped mark on the fragments of other works as you expected.

You recall what Enfer said and suddenly realize that if an artwork is a mark left in this world by the artist...

Then likewise, the marks on these works are also the traces the artist wanted to leave in the world!

Chief: So what exactly... do they mean?

Chief: Hmm... but this painting...?

You pull out several fragments from under the rubble and piece together an oil painting.

You've seen this painting in the Witching Hour. From the strokes, you can tell this was painted when Enfer was very young.

But it's not its immaturity that makes it special... This painting doesn't have that heart-shaped logo on it.

**【Advance】**

- Ask her directly

**end**

Holding the painting, you come next to Enfer.

Enfer: Oh? You found it.

Enfer: How nostalgic...

Chief: If I guess it right... You painted this when you were little?

Enfer: Yes, when I was very young... When my mother was still alive.

Chief: Mother...

Chief: Enfer, did you realize that... there's a special mark on all of your works?

Enfer: Mark...?

Chief: Right... A heart-shaped mark.

Chief: And not just the works displayed here...

You pick up the cocoon-shaped sculpture on the platform and show Enfer the mark.

Chief: You even unconsciously carved this mark on the pieces you casually made just now.

Enfer takes the painting and sculpture, examines them carefully for a while, then speaks again.

Enfer: This painting... was painted when I was very young. It's a copy of a photo...

Enfer: You know, my mother was an oil painting artist, and I learned some oil painting techniques when I was little.

Enfer: But... ever since my mother passed away, I've almost never painted again.

Enfer: To organize the Witching Hour, I dug out all of my works, and this exhibition hall happened to be missing an oil painting.

Enfer: I found this painting by chance and filled the empty spot with it.

Chief: But there isn't that heart-shaped mark on this painting.

Enfer touches the mark on the sculpture, frowning slightly.

Enfer: But...

Enfer: Why did I carve this mark on it...?

Enfer: It seems to have crept into my bones, branding this mark in the depths of my consciousness.

Enfer bows her head, as if wanting to burn a hole in the painting with her gaze.

Enfer: I left this mark on my works unconsciously. But why?

Enfer: And when did I...

Enfer places the painting on the platform and stares at it for a moment before turning to you.

Enfer: I want to know what this mark really means.

Chief: You left this mark unconsciously. Sosia also said there was something you had been unwilling to let go of...

Chief: I'm wondering... could this be the source of your perplexity?

Enfer's eyes light up and she looks at you with a surprised and thrilled look on her face.

Enfer: Then... are you willing to go and search for this source with me?

Before hearing your answer, Enfer grabs your hand.

Enfer: Come, go home with me!

## Enfer - Interrogation III

> Not all of Enfer's artworks are destroyed. Her masterpiece, which has brought her fame, is still kept in the studio. It's her awakening of fear, as well as fear's restraint on her.
> When Enfer discovers the heart-shaped mark on the sculpture, the unstable power leaks again, allowing the sculpture to... devour Enfer using its remaining power.

After leaving the museum, Enfer brings you to a residence near the suburbs.

The carved black marble door reminds you of the *Gate of Hell* in the museum.

Seeing you gazing at the door, Enfer guesses your thought.

Enfer: Scared?

Enfer: Relax, it doesn't have that kind of power.

Enfer smiles and drags you into the depths of the residence.

You pass by a poorly maintained garden. Had all of these withered flowers bloomed, they would have looked the same as the flowers in that painting.

Enfer: Go inside the small room behind the garden, walk downstairs, and you'll find my studio.

Enfer: Be careful. The stairs there are a little steep.

Pushing open the door and taking the stairs step by step, you and Enfer gradually sink into darkness.

Just as you begin to feel worried, with a snap, Enfer switches on a lamp carved into a grimacing pixie.

The pale light illuminates the path beneath your feet and somehow you feel that it leads straight to hell.

Enfer: But... twenty years ago, this was once my father's studio too.

Enfer: He created countless works here...

Enfer: Including that sculpture, The Truthful One.

Chief: It looks like... a studio full of stories.

Enfer: Would you like to hear... those stories that took place in its belly?

Enfer is walking ahead of you. She lifts her head to look up at you while you bow your head slightly.

Enfer: Did you notice we're in a perfect position... for a hug?

**【Advance】**

- Do you need a hug?

**end**

Enfer: Yes, in exchange for the stories, but not now.

Enfer continues walking down and switching on the lights on the way. The blinding white lights keep extending, unraveling with the stories Enfer is telling.

Enfer: Though I was born into a family of artists... there was nothing artistic about the union of those two.

Enfer: Just like breeding purebred cats, when you put two sets of excellent genes together, you're bound to get even more excellent genes.

Enfer: Heh...

Enfer presses the button. White light pours down, coating her with a layer of fragile and blinding light.

Enfer: My mother tried to love her husband and tried to manage this home that appeared happy and artsy to outsiders.

Enfer: She was a master of oil painting. As soon as I learned how to walk, she taught me how to hold a paintbrush.

Finishing the last step, you feel that you've entered a spacious room. The lights are switched on one after another, and you watch Enfer walk forward.

As the girl before you is telling her stories, she picks up a long chisel on the workbench, swings it in the air like she's painting something, and finally lowers her hand limply.

As if she's spilled paint outside the canvas, and the painting has lost its meaning.

Enfer: But Sosia was right. What can love bring us?

Enfer: Eventually, she lay beneath his feet.

With a clank, the long chisel is dropped on the floor.

Ignoring it, Enfer walks straight to the depths of the studio, the only part of this place still drenched in darkness.

Enfer: Thunderstorms can bring a different kind of stimulation. He needed inspiration, needed caprices as visceral as lightning and thunder.

Enfer: So he ignored her cries and pleas and ignored me who came following the noise... Pain can rouse the truth, and fear can elicit inspiration. He didn't hesitate to make us sacrifice for him.

Enfer glimpses at the chisel by her feet, her reminiscing gaze is filled with an icy chill.

Enfer: With the clinks of the chisel inside and the howling thunderstorms outside, eventually... he carved her fearful expression in the depths of my mind... He also completed his masterpiece...

Enfer: For the first time, I understood the meaning of fear and what it represented...

Enfer turned to look at you. Your slightly shocked expression satisfies her.

Enfer: Truth, and power.

Enfer: I thought that would be my end as well... Who'd have known...

Enfer: Perhaps it's because he needed a witness! I was his witness who saw his breakthrough... No one could ever say he was not as good as his wife again. No one could ever mock him for not having anything to show for his work.

Enfer: He took everything she'd given him and turned it into a sculpture. His most acclaimed work, The Truthful One.

Chief: This story sounds a bit different from the one you told me in Belphegor.

Enfer: Hmm... Take a guess... Which one is true? Which one is false?

Chief: As long as it's told by you, either could be true.

Enfer smiles wordlessly and switches on the last light in front of her.

Under the light, every part of the entire studio is clearly visible. All kinds of sculpting tools are scattered everywhere, covered in dust and debris.

The most unsettling sight is the white sculpture standing quietly before Enfer, in the depths of the studio.

It's completely white, yet it feels twisted, dark, and cold. Both his facial expression and body language show that he's going through immense fear.

Enfer: As his witness and successor, surely I'll have to create a piece that can shock the entire world, just like he did.

Chief: On August 20, BR-004 erupted and your father went missing...

Chief: Which means he was...

Enfer: Inspiration always dries up quickly for someone who's totally disloyal to the world and to nature...

Enfer: He found me. He told me that he loved me, so I must be like my mother and sacrifice for our family, for him...

Enfer: But I'd always remembered the thunderstorm on that day.

Enfer: And so I... also made him sacrifice for my work, and just like he did... now I own a work of art praised by everyone.

Enfer: Do you want to see it? The work that made my name.

Enfer reaches out a hand to you again.

She's already sealed her fear of her father in this sculpture. Then why is her hand still shaking?

You go ahead and look at it with Enfer.

Enfer: It was... an ultimate sight. Inspiration, mixed with fear, terror, and panic, was exploding.

Enfer: In that ring of red flames, I once again confirmed the feeling in my heart, confirmed the purpose of the works I'd created.

Enfer: He carved fear in the depths of my consciousness, giving me great power...

Enfer: So I, who walk in hell and peep at the truth, ought to present everything I perceive.

She says with fascination, as if once again reminiscing what she saw at that time.

But when you finally see the sculpture clearly, cold sweat breaks out on your back. Not only because of the fear oozing out from the inside of this sculpture, but also because you recognize it...

In the Witching Hour, an illusion broke Sosia's fear. And this sculpture... is the spitting image of that illusion!

Chief: Enfer... This sculpture wasn't displayed in the Witching Hour, was it?

Enfer: Of course not. I've... always kept him here.

Chief: But...

Chief: Sosia's fantasy of fear was broken by him.

Enfer: ...That's impossible.

Enfer: I've already conquered it... I won't succumb to the fear he gave me anymore. Why...?!

Through the glass shell, Enfer is glaring viciously at the sculpture she created.

Her gaze scrutinizes it slowly inch by inch, as if trying to fathom how her old self created this piece.

But as soon as she sees the chest of the sculpture, she looks dazed.

Enfer: Why... is it on here as well?!

You see it clearly too. On the chest of this contorted sculpture, there's a small heart-shaped dent.

Enfer: Haha... I see... I see... I was already imprisoned by weakness on that rainy night...

Enfer: That's why I... I got broken by you?!

This is her work, a piece she believes with which she conquered fear, and the reason why she is completely restrained by fear...

Enfer lets out a low laugh. Her hands press on the glass, scratching it with her nails, making an uncomfortable noise.

Enfer: I should be just like him. I should become even more skillful and create a perfect piece that makes people discover their inner fear!

Enfer: Let everyone witness what the world really looks like!

Enfer: But why... why do I fear him?! Why am I so weak?! Why must I be restrained...?

The surrounding temperature is dropping suddenly, and the pale light bulbs are making an unpleasant electrical buzz.

The sculpture inside the glass protection remains stationary, but somehow it makes you feel like... it's waiting for a chance to charge at you.

The shackles throb in your mind, prickling your spirit. You feel a familiar power awakening.

**【Advance】**

- Comfort her

**end**

Chief: You're not weak, and you are absolutely capable of creating a perfect piece!

Chief: If this mark is your weakness and perplexity, I'll help you find out the reason it exists and we'll resolve it together.

Chief: Your power is fluctuating. This is not a good sign. Enfer, calm down.

Enfer bows her head, as if not taking in anything you're saying.

You raise your hand to pull her back...

Noise of something breaking: Bam—!!

In a mere moment, the glass display windows surrounding the sculpture shatter amid the shaking. The darting broken glass cuts your cheek, leaving a red mark.

Chief: Enfer! Don't give in! Your ability is not stable enough now, be careful...!

Chief: Enfer, your power is going out of control! It's starting to influence the surrounding art again!

Chief: You're in a very dangerous state now. Come, give me your hand!

*The False One*: Shut up!

The white liquid suddenly hardens and pierces right at you, forcing you backward and away from Enfer.

And while you can't approach Enfer, the melted, soft liquid wraps around her feet and begins devouring her from the bottom up.

*The False One*: I know... Only I know that you...

*The False One*: You're searching for... your weakness... your perplexity!
