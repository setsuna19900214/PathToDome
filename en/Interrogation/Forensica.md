# Forensica Interrogation

## Forensica - Interrogation I

> Forensica seems wary of you and the organization you represent. Still, you managed to secure authorization to examine one of the bodies for her. She knows you're just as determined to uncover the truth behind the Origami Killer—perhaps that's a decent place to start.

Forensica stares blankly into space. Her eyes are calm, and she appears to be lost in thought. You notice her fingers gripping the pocket of her clothes.

She's remained uncooperative ever since her arrest, refusing to answer any questions.

By now, you can make out the shape of the object from it being pressed against the pocket—it's a pinky bone.

Chief: Let me introduce myself. I'm the Chief of the MBCC, and I'm in charge of your interrogation for this case.

Forensica doesn't move, but her gaze sharpens as it falls on you. You know that look. It's wariness, one you'd find in a cornered animal. She looks you up and down, sizing you up.

Despite never having met before, she seems to be extremely hostile towards you. She keeps her mouth shut, repulsion written all over her face.

Chief: Body theft may not be a serious crime, but it still involves endangering public safety and desecrating human remains.

Chief: I heard you used to be a forensic pathologist. You should understand the charges better than I do.

When you mention her work as a forensic pathologist, Forensica can't help but slip her hand into her pocket.

You notice the movement, and the finger bone is now partially visible at the edge of her pocket.

Chief: Do you have anything to say about what you've done?

Chief: ...Or rather, is there anything I can help you with?

Forensica lets out a cold laugh and finally speaks, her tone extremely sharp.

Forensica: Help me? You hypocrites would be doing me a great favor already if you'd just go far, far away.

Chief: So you do need help.

Forensica: No comment.

It seems her wariness isn't directed at you specifically, but rather a certain group of people.

The records show that Forensica used to work at a forensic examination center before being dismissed for certain reasons.

These days, while she might appear to be an empty shell, she never actually gave up on forensic examination and related work.

In order to make a living, she became Clementine's consultant, providing expertise in forensics for her novels.

She's exactly as described in Clementine's novels—eccentric, peculiar, and guarded.

Perhaps it's because the novel's portrayal was so vivid. Even though she should be a complete stranger, you feel as if you've known her for ages.

This sense of familiarity intrigues you, and makes you more patient. You want to understand what Forensica is truly thinking.

Chief: If you don't want to talk about the case, we can discuss something else.

Chief: How's Dorothy doing?

Forensica: ...I don't know what you're talking about.

You take out the bestselling book, "Murder on the Praeva Express," and set it in front of her.

Chief: I'm sure you know this book—you're basically the same person in and outside of it.

Chief: We may have never met in person, but we made quite a good combo in Clementine's novel, didn't we?

Chief: In the story, we worked together to find the killer. As a token of our friendship, you gave me a finger bone.

Chief: That finger bone came from your collection. Dorothy, if I recall correctly.

Forensica: You know Clementine?

Chief: More than that, we've had many in-depth conversations.

Chief: Clementine mentioned how all the forensic expertise in her novels came from you. She even went as far as to say that you're her most trusted consultant.

Forensica reaches out and closes the novel you had opened. The dust jacket is covered in highly positive reviews and recommendations.

Forensica: That woman... I suppose she's a decent novelist. At least she respects the facts.

Whether it's because of Clementine, or because you acknowledged the novel, Forensica's hostility, while not entirely gone, has noticeably diminished.

Chief: Clementine holds you in very high regard, you know. She said you're a rare genius whose knowledge and intuition far surpass the ordinary.

Forensica: A genius? I highly doubt a real genius would end up being locked up in a place like this, waiting to be judged.

Forensica: What exactly are you getting at anyway? Are you trying to approach me as the same character that's in the novel? Or are you just trying to brag about how much you read?

Chief: I was trying to bridge the gap between us. Doesn't seem to be working, though.

Chief: That said, we did work together to find the killer in the novel. Perhaps we can do the same now.

You pull up the files on the various charges against Forensica. Nearly every single one points to her investigating something.

Chief: I just want to know why you did this. Are you investigating the truth behind a particular case?

Forensica: Truth? As if such a thing exists.

Forensica: Everyone just wants to close a case, bury the body, and move on as quickly as possible. That's the only "truth."

Forensica: Everyone has their own agenda in this world. Whoever holds the power gets to decide what "truth" is.

You seem to have struck a nerve. Forensica grows increasingly agitated, her choice of words becoming much harsher.

Chief: You should know that we found the truth behind the arson case about the orphanage.

Forensica: So what? If you'd found the truth sooner, Clementine wouldn't have had to write that novel in the first place.

Forensica: Clementine wouldn't be who she is today, and her friends would still be alive.

Forensica: Bloated institutions full of red tape, breeding nothing but parasites and incompetent trash... What right does a system like that have to pass judgment on anything?

Chief: Gathering evidence takes time. As a forensic pathologist, you should understand that better than anyone.

Forensica: It's BECAUSE I'm a forensic pathologist that I know just how idiotic that "due process" is!

Forensica: The evidence is right there in front of us, yet people abuse their authority to manipulate procedures and turn justice into nothing but empty words!

Forensica glares at you, her hand curled tightly in her pocket, the fabric of her clothes trembling slightly from the force of her grip.
