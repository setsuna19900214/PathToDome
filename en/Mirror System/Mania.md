### Bureau

The Minos Bureau of Crisis Control (MBCC), a secret organization that detains and controls Sinners. Formerly part of the FAC, it's now led by the 9th Agency and handles specific Mania cases.

### Function

The MBCC detains and controls Sinners to minimize the risk. On top of that, the MBCC Chief leads those Sinners who are under control on missions to address Mania incidents.

### Location

The MBCC headquarters sits in a secluded place on the fringe of DisCity. Its non-Sinner-related departments are located overground, while its Sinner-related departments are located mostly underground.

### Organizational Structure

The basic organizational structure of the MBCC:
1. Chief, leader of the MBCC assisted by an adjutant, is responsible for leading special operations and handling cross-departmental affairs.
2. The supervision department is responsible for supervising and managing non-combatant Sinners, and for installing facilities for the exclusive use of the Sinners.
3. The Sinner-tracking squad is responsible for tracking down Sinners out in the field and assisting Chief in trailing them.
4. The support department is responsible for offering Chief and Sinners assistance with their missions. It includes an intelligence office, a medical care office, a supply office, and others.

## Mania

Mania-001

Mania is a mutation unique to humans that causes abnormal mental and physical changes in individuals. First discovered in DisCity in N.F. 84, its cause is still unknown.

### Mania

Mania is a mutation unique to humans that causes abnormal mental and physical changes in individuals. First discovered in DisCity in <ins>N.F. 84</ins>, its cause is still unknown.

### Manifestations

People infected with Mania will become slaves to extreme feelings and emotions, such as hysteria, paranoia, and insatiable appetites for sensual pleasures; at the same time, their physical strength will increase exponentially, and their physical appearance will change beyond recognition. Eventually, they will go deranged and turn into grotesque monsters that have no will of their own.

### Causes

Sinners create Mania and draw power from it.

Correction: Mania is not of itself a form of power or mutation, but an irreversible alteration of the human psyche. According to reliable sources, the spread of Mania has nothing to do with Sinners, but has a lot to do with the proliferation of Mania Crystals and Mania weapons, or with outbreaks of widespread mental contamination.

### M-value

The M-value is used to quantify the severity of the Mania infection. The higher the M-value, the severer the infection. For each individual, there is an M-value danger zone. The danger zone is an M-value range in which an individual will turn into a Corruptor if their M-value falls within that range.

### Keylan Constant

Statistics from the Ring Research Institute show that, if the M-value is higher than a specific value (the Base Value), it will increase significantly over time, and if it is lower than the Base Value, it will remain relatively stable. To better define the phenomena, the researchers came up with the Keylan constant q, which is now used as a basic unit for measuring the M-value. Only if an individual's M-value is higher than q will he or she be viewed as a Maniac.

### Mania Resonance

Mania Resonance occurs when individual entities are in close proximity to one another. Maniacs destabilize others' mental conditions and cause cross-spatial spread of Mania.
According to research data from SALVA Hospital: "Mania Resonance can be triggered by Maniacs' tissue, secretions, sounds, odors, and sometimes even by their very existence."
In N.F.104, it was confirmed for the first time that Mania Resonance could also occur between Black Rings. BR-000's Mania Resonance spurred the formation of BR-002.

### Rule Discrepancy

Recent studies suggest that Mania might be a sign of conflict resulting from the discrepancies between two or more sets of rules.

### Application Principle

According to the latest research on the so-called Almighty Bow, Mania weapons and Ring weapons share certain similarities in their manufacturing principles, both relying on the combination of Ring substance and spiritual energy. However, their final forms are drastically different, possibly indicating that the purity of spiritual energy plays a crucial role in their creation.

## Sinner

Mania-002

For reasons still unknown, certain Maniacs will awaken as Sinners who possess superhuman power.

### Sinner

For reasons still unknown, certain Maniacs will awaken as Sinners who possess superhuman power.

### Mania

Sinners create Mania and draw power from it.

Correction: Sinners draw all of their power from Mania, but don't spread it themselves.

### Types of Tendency

Drawing on their analysis of a sample of Sinners, researchers divide Sinners' abilities into six categories according to their personal characteristics:
1. Catalyst Tendency: Sinners in this category are perceptive and observant, highly attuned to people's feelings. They are able to access and influence the feelings of those around them.
2. Arcane Tendency: Sinners in this category have an extreme personality and an active imagination. They are able to turn what they imagine into reality, sometimes unknowingly, creating supernatural phenomena that defy the laws of physics.
3. Fury Tendency: Sinners in this category have a strong sense of self and despise any form of restraint. They have a burning desire to tear everything down, even if it means the destruction of their own bodies.
4. Endura Tendency: Sinners in this category are tenacious to a fault. They have a tremendous amount of stamina and a great sense of responsibility, ready to tackle any danger or resistance that comes their way when they are trying to reach a goal or keep a promise.
5. Reticle Tendency: Sinners in this category have great powers of concentration. They can focus on a target for a long period of time, and by so doing, they can have a thorough and accurate understanding of their target, which, in turn, allows them to manipulate the target.
6. Umbra Tendency: Sinners in this category are uncannily intuitive; they are guided by intuitions when it comes time to make a decision. And they loathe being stuck in a rut, and tend to break the status quo, both internally and externally.

### Derangement

An individual will turn into a Corruptor if their M-value falls within their M-value danger zone. The lower limits of Sinners' M-value danger zones are much higher than those of non-Sinners', but Sinners can turn into Corruptors nevertheless.

### Scourge Sinner

In theory, Sinners restrained by the shackles experience minimal M-Value fluctuations. However, under extreme conditions, shackled Sinners can still undergo sudden M-Value spikes, pushing them beyond their sanity threshold in a short period.
If such a Sinner possesses extraordinary mental fortitude to achieve a second awakening, and the compensation effect between them and the shackles' owner goes beyond normal limits to establish a deep shackle connection, they may become a Scourge Sinner.
Scourge Sinners remain classified within the S-Class range, but after their second awakening, both their Mania characteristics and ability manifestations may mutate.

### Compensation Effect

After establishing a shackle connection, the shackles' owner can shoulder part of the burden of Mania contamination for the Sinner, maintaining the Sinner's M-Value in a stable state.
This shackle effect is known as the compensation effect. Although the shackles' owner possesses extremely high resistance to Mania contamination, the compensation effect still has its limits. If a Sinner encounters intense contamination in a short period, it can cause tremendous mental and physical strain on the shackles' owner, potentially endangering the lives of both parties in severe cases.

## Hypercube

Mania-003

The Hypercube is the only non-renewable mineral that can help humans defend against catastrophes and protect them from Mania.

### Hypercube

The Hypercube is the only non-renewable mineral that can help humans defend against catastrophes and protect them from Mania.

### History

After the Perishing Star struck, Dr. Keylan, leading an investigation team built around a core of scientists, ventured into DisSea, the location where the Perishing Star collided with the earth. He came out on the other side with Hypercube, a substance that can contain Catastrophes. Since Hypercube formed as a direct result of the collision, it is found exclusively in DisSea of DisCity.

### Main Applications

Technologies developed around Hypercube are applied to the suppression of Black Rings, and the containment of local Catastrophes.
1. The Catastrophe-containing system is mainly used in keeping Catastrophes in check, in order to mitigate their negative impacts on people's lives.
2. The Mania-blocking device, also known as the anti-Mania device, comes in different sizes. A large anti-Mania device generates a protecting force filed that suppresses the expansion of an active Black Ring and prevents the spread of contamination. A small anti-Mania device, like the standard-issue blocker used in the FAC, can help protect the user's sanity by keeping Mania at bay.
3. The Mania-monitoring network is a network of monitoring devices installed all around Eastside. It detects the presence of Mania by monitoring Mania's interference with the Hypercube-generated force fields.

### Blue Rain

The Blue Rain is a Hypercube-based extensive-cleansing technology, which is deployed after an outbreak of large-scale Mania contamination to disinfect the whole of DisCity. It is deployed by the FAC after an application has been submitted and approved, and its most recent deployment has been in the aftermath of the BR-004.

### Hypercube Monomer

A substance found within a secondary Black Ring. Hypercube Monomers come out of a Black Ring slowly. The Ring Research Institute deduces that this phenomenon is akin to the precipitation process of a solute in a super-saturated solution.

### Crystal Flower

Research samples of Crystal Flowers are extremely scarce. What has been proven by research is that these flowers, like Hypercubes, have the ability to restore sanity and purify contamination.

## Black Ring

Mania-004

### Black Ring

A Black Ring is an area that causes widespread, severe mental contamination. It appears when a Mania crisis goes beyond its limits and is the ultimate form of a Mania catastrophe.

### Black Ring's Ramifications

Black Rings cause Mania to spread widely and unpredictably, turning into Corruptors any human beings near or inside them. It's extremely dangerous to get trapped inside a Black Ring, because Mania contamination inside is highly concentrated and can turn a human being into a Corruptor almost instantly.

### Black Ring's Behaviors

A dense, extensive area of mental contamination can induce reality warping within its confines. Most Black Rings cause space-time distortion, antigravity, and electromagnetic interference, and some Black Rings have their own special characteristics. Matter and energy undergo preternatural transformations within a Black Ring, as if some kind of unearthly energy is pouring out of another dimension to encroach on and fuse with the physical world.

### Ground Zero

The appearance of a secondary Black Ring—that is, any Black Ring that comes after the BR-000, is accompanied by a powerful Sinner going manic. The Sinner is said to be Ground Zero for that particular Black Ring, and seems capable of controlling it.

Correction: The awakening of a Corpus results in the appearance of a Black Ring.
So far, there are two known conditions for the awakening of a Corpus.
1. A Corpus invades and fuses with an S-Class Sinner.
2. Maniacs seized by the same type of emotion gather in large numbers.
In general, the appearance of a Black Ring requires an excessively high concentration of Mania, and the higher the concentration, the more powerful the upcoming Black Ring will be.
If a Black Ring is left unchecked, it will grow more powerful over time.

### Corpseborne

A Corpus is a substance or creature infused with an excessive amount of Mania. There are many Corpora in DisCity, and they are believed to have human-like emotions and consciousness even while lying dormant.
When a Corpus awakens, its emotions and consciousness grow stronger. A Sinner that has fused with an awakened Corpus is called a Corpseborne, which will become the center of Mania contamination.
The appearance of a secondary Black Ring is tied up with the birth of a Corpseborne, and the in-depth inquiry into the cause of the Square Black Ring has confirmed that Corpsebornes are the source of Black Rings. When a Black Ring appears, it distorts the laws of physics in its surroundings and contaminates the minds of those in its vicinity, and all the Corruptors within the Black Ring are subject to mental interference from the Corpseborne.
According to the information gathered by Parma and his lackeys, a Corpseborne can control the Black Ring he or she has created. That's why he refers to a Corpseborne as *Ring Master*, or simply *Master*.

Mania-004-5

Currently unknown, but it was suspected to be a mixture of the chaotic collective will of disaster refugees and a Corpus.

The Apostle Black Ring was essentially a massive, amorphous Apostle of Ashes. Black Ring rifts covered its surface, and its interior was extremely chaotic and confined.

## Corruptor

Mania-005

A mentally unhinged, physically distorted monster formed by those who have become deranged from Mania.

### Corruptor

A monster born from Mania, with a deformed body and no sanity.

### Causes & Features

Cause:

When a Maniac's M-value nears a dangerous threshold, the pressure on their mind and body will exceed their limit, leading to both physical and mental derangement and rebirth as a monster.

Mutation symptoms:

1. The physical body mutates, and sanity disappears. The course of the mutation is affected by both internal psychology and the external environment.

2. Some powerful Corruptors will spawn a "core" inside their bodies from the intense emotions they experience at the time of derangement. The core is said to store the Corruptor's last shred of consciousness, but there is yet to be conclusive evidence for that theory.

### Perniciousness

Corruptors have the strong desire to attack, and will attack all targets within their range of perception. Corruptors also have the risk of spreading Mania through mass Mania Resonance.

### Classification

Simply put, there are two types of Corruptors: native and mutated.

Native: Born in areas of high contamination and are greatly affected by external environmental factors.

Mutated: Transformed from Maniacs who reached the point of derangement, and are greatly affected by internal psychological factors.

## Aurum Secretum

Mania-007

Aurum Secretum is an advanced technological product exclusive to Fraser, possessing powerful resonance effects. With the assistance of auxiliary materials and devices, it can be used to create various Aurum Secretum products.

### Aurum Secretum

Aurum Secretum is an advanced technological product exclusive to Fraser, possessing powerful resonance effects. With the assistance of auxiliary materials and devices, it can be used to create various Aurum Secretum products.

### Characteristics

Aurum Secretum is a metallic substance that exhibits both resonance and amplification properties. It has multiple forms and naturally exhibits geometric structures in its inert state.
Due to its resonant and amplifying effects, when Aurum Secretum undergoes coupled activation with other elements, such as Hypercube, Mania byproducts, and Ash Crystal, it generates diverse interference phenomena.

### Source

The raw materials used for Aurum Secretum are sourced directly from Apostle of Ashes fragments. Through specialized runes and alchemical sorcery supplied by the Underground, the raw materials undergo multi-stage processing—including deactivation, dilution, alloying, and final purification—to yield the finished Aurum Secretum products.

### Effect

1. Resonance control devices, crafted by integrating high-purity Aurum Secretum with Ash Crystal, permit designated personnel or Sinners to briefly synchronize with Apostle of Ashes projections. This synchronization enables them to disrupt the Apostles' behavioral logic or even exert direct control over them.
2. Protective devices constructed from Aurum Secretum offer partial defense and mitigation against Mania. However, these Aurum Secretum-based protections also demand Hypercube consumption. As they primarily function by resonating with and amplifying Hypercube's protective properties, they remain incapable of fully eliminating reliance on Hypercube.
