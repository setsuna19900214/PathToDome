## Eastside

Dis-E-001

A district known as Eastside on the east bank of the Rust of DisCity. After DisSea Explosion, Eastside built many anti-Mania devices. It has gained stability with the Mania-monitoring network and has been prospering since.

### Eastside

A district known as Eastside on the east bank of the Rust of DisCity. After DisSea Explosion, Eastside built many <ins>anti-Mania devices</ins>. It's gained stability with the Mania-monitoring network and has been prospering since.

### Regional Info

Eastside is the most developed region in DisCity, or even in the world.
1. Industries: Eastside's major industries include housing, commerce, and entertainment. Since N.F. 99, its entertainment industry has been booming, and has now become the pillar of its economy.
2. Population: Eastside is the most populated region in the world, and its population is still growing.
3. Economy: Apart from having a thriving domestic market, Eastside, on account of its monopoly over Hypercube ownership, also receives a continual influx of money from outside its border.

### Regional Atmosphere

Life in Eastside is like a never-ending party.
1. Life: Life in Eastside is packed with all forms of entertainment. The Eastsiders' daily life is awash with commercial advertisements, cultural events, and celebrity culture, among many others.
2: Culture: The Eastsiders have grown so accustomed to their great abundance of riches and resources that nothing but what is novel and exciting can pique their interest. And they look down upon and are hostile to the Syndicans.

### Mania Policy

With the help of a Mania-monitoring network, The Eastside authorities identify Maniacs and banish them to West District. The City Council is adamant about keeping Eastside free of Mania to maintain stability and security. Once a Maniac or a Mania event is detected, the FAC will be called upon to handle the situation promptly.

## City Council

Dis-E-004

DisCity's highest governing body, directly elected by its citizens, that holds multiple supervisory and policy-making powers while remaining under the oversight of Paradeisos.

### City Council

DisCity's highest governing body, directly elected by its citizens, that holds multiple supervisory and policy-making powers while remaining under the oversight of Paradeisos.

### Function

The city council is responsible for establishing municipal regulations, reviewing budgets, and overseeing policies, with the authority to propose, deliberate, freeze, and remove cabinet members.
It operates multiple committees that manage affairs such as finance, public security, technology, disaster response, and others, and holds final decision-making authority on matters involving civil rights, public resource allocation, and inter-city-state relations.
The council exercises direct oversight and accountability over the following major cabinet departments: the Treasury Department, Bureau of Commerce and Trade, Public Security Bureau, Bureau of Energy and Industry, Outland Affairs Bureau, and Nirvana Office. Each department must regularly submit operational reports to the council, undergo questioning and budget reviews, and secure council approval for key personnel appointments and dismissals.

### Speaker

The Speaker of the City Council serves as DisCity's highest elected official in practice, carrying dual responsibilities for both administrative and legislative coordination. Through the Speaker's Committee, the Speaker oversees the city council's functions while also acting as the primary liaison with the cabinet government. In this role, the Speaker serves as the central hub for policy announcements, public affairs mediation, and crisis management.
The Speaker cannot simultaneously hold office as a councilor, serves a four-year term, and may be reelected for one additional consecutive term.

### Speaker Election

The Speaker of the City Council is not elected internally by the council but is instead chosen through a direct citywide popular vote. Each registered voter casts one ballot for Speaker, with the election held concurrently with councilor elections.
1. Eligibility is granted to any resident aged 18 or above, who has maintained continuous residence in DisCity for over five years and possesses DisCitizenship.
2. Active FAC members will have their voting rights temporarily suspended for the duration of their service. These rights will be reinstated upon completion of service and formal discharge from active duty.

### N.F. 114 Special Act

To further clarify the definition of DisCity's jurisdiction regarding the residency period criterion for voter eligibility, this Act establishes the following supplementary rules for calculating residency periods in specific regions:
For regions recently included within DisCity's jurisdiction, residents' duration of residency shall be calculated starting from the official date on which the region comes under City Council administration.
For regions that were once temporarily separated from council administration, residents' duration of residency shall be calculated starting from the date of reintegration into administration.

## Eastside District 3

Dis-E-005

Eastside District 3 serves as DisCity's political hub, where legislative, administrative, and judicial authority is centralized.

### Eastside District 3

Eastside District 3 serves as DisCity's political hub, where legislative, administrative, and judicial authority is centralized. Designed around Central Park, the district accommodates key facilities such as the Speaker's Residence, DisCity Memorial Hall, Eastside Public Security Bureau, and the DisCity Archives.

### Speaker's Residence

The Speaker's Residence is situated in the central area on the north side of Central Park in Eastside District 3. It forms the heart of the city council's operational complex, serving as both the Speaker's official residence and the council chamber. The east and west wings span multiple floors, accommodating committee and cabinet meeting rooms, offices for administrative agencies, and the city-state emergency command center.

### December Surprises

During the winter months of N.F. 114, preceding the Speaker Election, a string of consecutive attacks struck Eastside District 3. Beginning with the cabinet assassination case and culminating in the Blood Memorial incident, the wave of relentless political violence severely destabilized the district's security environment.
In the aftermath of the election season assaults, Eastside District 3 elevated its security protocols, deploying stricter Mania monitoring measures and instituting comprehensive surveillance systems to deter Outland infiltration.

## The Great Expo

Dis-E-006

The Great Expo is an inter-city-state exchange summit initiated by DisCity in N.F. 34, held every four years with the goal of restoring human communication networks and fostering resource and technology sharing.

### The Great Expo

The Great Expo is an inter-city-state exchange summit initiated by DisCity in N.F. 34, held every four years with the goal of restoring human communication networks and fostering resource and technology sharing. However, due to the upheavals of wars and disasters, multiple sessions of the Great Expo were suspended.
In June, N.F. 115, driven by Fraser's initiative, Eastside constructed Cristallia near the original site of the first Great Expo in West District's Keylan Square area to host the 13th Great Expo.

### History

Keylan Square was constructed in N.F. 33 in honor of Dr. Keylan. The following year, it hosted the inaugural Great Expo, welcoming over 40 city-states. The fledgling DisCity unveiled the Hypercube technology to the world, paving the way for collaboration among city-states in the Nightfall Era.

Beginning in the N.F. 60s, the Outland wars repeatedly disrupted the Great Expo, with only a handful of sessions held as temporary gestures of peace.
In N.F. 106, the 12th Great Expo was convened. Despite facing setbacks and challenges, it ultimately yielded positive outcomes, and the following year, all city-states successfully signed a comprehensive ceasefire agreement.

### Cristallia

Cristallia is divided into a main hall and an auxiliary hall. While the two facilities stand on opposite sides, each hosting distinct public exhibitions, countless connecting corridors unite them—symbolizing the mutual exchange and communication between city-states and perfectly reflecting this Great Expo's theme: "Be One Again."
In addition to the public exhibition areas, Cristallia's upper floors house private exhibition spaces, accessible exclusively to delegations from various city-states and Eastside's political and business representatives.

### Closing Ceremony Attack

On June 25, N.F. 115, during the Great Expo's closing ceremony, Outland extremists launched a desperate attack on the exhibition, but the assailants were swiftly subdued by the FAC due to preemptive security measures.

## Ring Research Institute

Dis-E-007

A research institute under United University, now operating largely as an independent entity.

### Ring Research Institute

Originally a branch under the College of Science at United University, the Ring Research Institute steadily rose in prominence due to the critical nature of its research focus. It eventually became an independent institution, achieving exceptional academic standing in fields related to Black Rings, Hypercubes, and Mania.

Nowadays, the Ring Research Institute only maintains limited contact with United University for select projects, yet it wields considerable influence within the university's broader ecosystem nonetheless, enough to sway personnel decisions and funding allocations. Beyond its dedicated campus within United University, the Institute operates laboratories throughout Eastside and has established a major hub for monitoring, experimentation, and research on the outskirts of BR-003, known as the BR-003 Observation Hub.

### United University

Founded in the early days after DisCity established independence, United University aimed to preserve civilization and consolidate the city's academic resources. In its early years, it made no distinction between the humanities and the sciences, with its founding mission centered on recovering and safeguarding human knowledge. It was seen as a symbol of DisCity's determination.

As the threat of Mania intensified, the city's demand for cutting-edge technology surged, and resources as well as policies grew to favor science and engineering more. United University was gradually divided into multiple discipline-specific campuses, with science-oriented colleges far outpacing the humanities in prestige, funding, faculty size, and influence alike.

Now, United University represents the pinnacle of academic achievement in DisCity across practically all fields, medicine aside. However, the spirit of equal footing between the arts and sciences that once defined it is all but gone.

### "Heterogeneous Trial"

A classified program led by EDGE-02 and secretly executed within the Paradeisos-Ring Research Institute framework.
Centered around the Banyan Medical College Hospital, the program's screening scope covered Eastside, the West District, and various satellite towns. It conducted baseline aptitude assessments on newborns and continuously collected samples throughout their childhood.
Screening criteria included intelligence, physical capability, Mania tolerance, environmental adaptability, and behavioral compliance. Following multiple rounds of evaluation and elimination, exceptional individuals who passed the final selection were sent to Paradeisos for special training. Dedicated files were created for other final-round candidates for long-term monitoring, and they could still be admitted upon meeting the required standards.
The program's ultimate goal was to transform gifted individuals into a resource that could be mass-configured and deployed.

### BR-003 Observation Hub

A large-scale hub for monitoring, experimentation, and research established on the outskirts of BR-003. The facility is situated at a high altitude, concealed by an unclassified dimensional technology that renders it invisible to conventional observation.

Leveraging the unique Mania field conditions surrounding BR-003, the Observation Hub focuses primarily on cutting-edge research topics such as Mania dimensional eigensolution and entanglement. These studies may bear some connection to the Hub's concealment technology.

## Central Library

Dis-E-008

The official central library of DisCity, commonly known among locals as the Grand Library.

### Central Library

Formally known as the DisCity Central Library, it is commonly referred to by locals as the Grand Library. Its history predates DisCity's independence. According to accounts, the earliest items in its collection were documents and manuscripts restored by surviving scholars in the early days after Nightfall. The library was then relocated and rebuilt several times, eventually becoming what it is today following the official establishment of DisCity.

The library houses over a million texts and digital volumes, including books, documents, local chronicles, and various databases open to the public. Rumor has it that the collection also includes a number of sensitive files and classified records belonging to DisCity.

For materials requiring special clearance, the Central Library employs a multi-tiered information access management system. Accessing restricted documents not only generates an access log but also triggers real-time notifications to the respective information custodians.

### Head Librarian

The current Head Librarian, Celine Fabius, also serves as DisCity's chronicler of this generation. She has preserved the complete histories and even the memories recorded by every past chronicler, but this inheritance came at a cost. Having absorbed the Maniafied memories from all her predecessors, she herself became a Sinner.
