# N.F. Chronology

## N.F.01

N.F.01

### N.F. 01

In the first year of Nightfall, the Perishing Star struck, turning the surrounding land into deadly zones and triggering supernatural disasters everywhere.

### The Perishing Star Struck

An unrecorded celestial body fell out of orbit, plunged into the atmosphere, and collided with the earth. In a matter of weeks, a colossal death zone took shape, with the collision point as its center.

### Catastrophes

After the Perishing Star struck, a myriad of supernatural disasters, called Catastrophes, ran rampant around the world on a grand scale. The environment deteriorated abruptly, the human population dwindled rapidly, and progress in technology and science came to a sudden halt.

### Nightfall Proclamation

While the world was being ravaged by Catastrophes, the bid for resources intensified the enmity between the various cities, posing an almost insurmountable barrier to the reconstruction of civilization. A series of conferences were held, and in the end, the representatives of these cities signed the Nightfall Proclamation, which ushered in the Nightfall Era.

## N.F.21-25

N.F.21-25

### Hypercube

Dr. Keylan discovered the <ins>Hypercube</ins>, a key anti-catastrophe resource, and used it to contain the catastrophe. After that, DisCity was built.

### Expedition

In N.F. 21, Dr. Keylan assembled an investigation team built around a core of scientists. Risking their lives to explore the depths of the death zone, they ventured into the location where the Perishing Star collided with the earth, in the hope of finding a solution to the crisis.

### Survivor

In N.F. 23, shortly after the investigation team entered the death zone, all communications were cut off. Many years later, Dr. Keylan, the sole survivor in the team, walked out of the dead zone, carrying a unique resource that could resist Catastrophes and save humanity: Hypercube.

### Trailblazer Initiative

Under the direction of Dr. Keylan, the District of DisSea entered a peaceful period, during which the first immigrants arrived to mine for Hypercube.

### E.D.G.E.

The Exploration, Defence and Governance Entente (E.D.G.E.) was drawn up by a group of eminent figures of vision, with Dr. Keylan in charge, and then signed in N.F. 25, one after another, by all the other cities that had survived into the Nightfall Era.

### Founding of DisCity

The signing of the E.D.G.E. marked the founding of DisCity, where the world's only Hypercube-mining area was located. In accordance with the entente, DisCity would transport some of the Hypercube it acquired to the other cities, which, in return, would provide DisCity with some of their own resources and other forms of support.

## N.F.60-84

N.F.60-84

The history of DisCorps, from the formation of the WhiteSands Guerrillas to the DisSea Explosion.

### Bloodstained White Rose

On December 26, N.F. 61, Damon, DisCity's first democratically elected Speaker of the City Council, was assassinated during a speech.
Captain Brock Francis of the 8th Company of DisCity Defender Corps defected to WhiteSands, where he established a guerrilla force to resist the metropoles.

### Guerrilla Tactics

By N.F. 64, with support from DisCity domestic factions such as Nirvana Port and the EDGE Laboratory, the WhiteSands Guerrillas repeatedly raided shipping routes connecting Nirvana Port and various metropoles with hit-and-run tactics, inflicting massive losses on the metropoles.
To completely eradicate the guerrilla influence, the metropoles replaced the original route patrols and spent a year assembling an elite Metropole Alliance force of over 10,000 elite soldiers, vowing to eliminate the WhiteSands Guerrillas.

### The Triumph of WhiteSands Valley

In the autumn of N.F. 65, after more than six months of aggressive campaigns, the Metropole Alliance penetrated a valley in an attempt to surround and annihilate the remaining WhiteSands Guerrillas.
On October 22, N.F. 65, after implementing decoys and despite suffering enormous casualties, the Guerrillas utilized the Tide of Ashes to destroy the Metropole Alliance that outnumbered them dozens of times over, while also seizing vast quantities of advanced metropole weaponry.
Afterward, the power balance between the two sides reversed, with the Metropole Alliance suffering consecutive devastating blows.

### Nirvana Port Nuclear Explosion

On June 13, N.F. 66, in response to the major defeat at WhiteSands Valley and other losses, one of the metropoles deployed a nuclear weapon preserved from an earlier era without warning. The attack struck Nirvana Port's main structure directly, intending to intimidate DisCity's domestic factions backing the WhiteSands Guerrillas.
The explosion instantly annihilated a significant portion of the port area, causing over 20,000 instant deaths and more than 100,000 indirect casualties, severely crippling trade between Nirvana Port and the metropoles. Although the EDGE Laboratory's efforts managed to contain the subsequent radioactive contamination, surrounding areas and their residents still suffer from indelible effects to this day.

### Founding of the Independence Army

On August 27, N.F. 66, inspired by Captain Brock, DisCity Army mutinied and declared their allegiance to the WhiteSands Guerrillas.
About two weeks later, on September 15, Captain Brock delivered an independence speech, officially establishing the DisCity Independence Army. That same evening, Captain Brock succumbed to the radiation poisoning he had suffered three months earlier. Dravyn Steel assumed command of the Independence Army.
The DisCity Independence Army consolidated forces from various DisCity regions, Nirvana Port troops, and militias such as the mine guards, thus beginning the DisCity War of Independence that would last for six years.

Following an official war declaration against DisCity, the metropoles reorganized their combined forces into an army of more than 100,000 soldiers, engaging the newly formed Independence Army in a lengthy war of attrition.
On the Nirvana Port defense line alone, more than 40,000 Independence Army soldiers sacrificed their lives over several years defending DisCity, yet they still could not overcome the enormous power disparity between the two sides.
Eventually, the Metropole Alliance breached Nirvana Port and advanced into DisCity, attempting to destroy its remaining military strength in a single destructive strike with multiple high-yield nuclear weapons. The Alliance's HQ had already prepared comprehensive post-war reconstruction plans for DisCity.

### Operation Skyfall

In N.F. 68, seeking a decisive advantage, Dravyn Steel located the Underground's sage ritual site with the help of a prodigy intern from EDGE.
The ritual's purpose was to create entities known as *sages* that could control and spread Mania power by harnessing the endless violence and blood lust from the battlefield. However, due to Dravyn's interference, the Rancor runes eventually escaped the Underground's control, and were appropriated by the Independence Army.
That same year, with EDGE's full support, the first generation of Reaper weapons incorporating these runes was created. By resonating with and amplifying Catherine Augustus Andohar's abilities, the Independence Army successfully neutralized the nuclear crisis.
The continuously improved Reaper weapons that followed reversed the power balance between the two sides, sending the Metropole Alliance into decline.

### Independence of DisCity

On December 26, N.F. 72, Dravyn Steel, leader of the Independence Army, officially declared the independence of DisCity, assuming the role of the first Speaker of the City Council of the new DisCity.
By N.F. 74, armed with Reaper weapons and Catherine Augustus Andohar's power, DisCity had eliminated all hostile neighboring small to medium-sized cities, established multiple satellite towns, and secured its own safety and prosperity.
That same year, Catherine Augustus Andohar entered cryosleep.

### Tyranny of DisCity

After N.F. 74, DisCity did not cease its external military campaigns. Using the pretext of a state of war, Dravyn Steel continued to serve as Speaker of the City Council, implementing harsh internal policies. Despite significant economic growth after liberation from metropole colonization, the living conditions of citizens saw no improvement.
This situation persisted until the DisSea Explosion in N.F. 84. Encouraged by EDGE-01, the FAC was formally established, with Dravyn serving concurrently as its first Commander-in-Chief, redirecting most of DisCity's military focus toward defending against Mania.

### DisSea Explosion

A huge explosion occurred in DisSea, contaminating and ruining the mines there. Hypercube production dropped sharply, leading to the decline of the West District's mining industry.

### BR-000

The first-ever <ins>Black Ring</ins>, also called the Primary Black Ring, appeared in N.F. 84. DisSea Explosion and the Black Ring led to the direct death of 87231 people, and a three-month period of heavy contamination, which turned more than 200 thousand people into <ins>Maniacs</ins>.

### Grace River

The Grace River separated West District from Eastside, limiting the flow of residents from one region to the other, and acting as a buffer against the spread of Mania.

West District Order Management Regulations was passed by the City Council in N.F. 99. From this point on, West District became a de facto autonomous region, belonging to DisCity in name only.

### Syndicate

West District was soon overrun with violent gangs. On paper, these gangs were subject to the authority of DisCity, but in reality, they were pretty much their own bosses. Slowly but surely, West District, also known as <ins>Syndicate</ins>, came to be viewed as a land of sins by the citizens of DisCity.
