# Outlands

OUTSIDE

## Outlands

Dis-O-001

### Outlands

In DisCity parlance, Outland refers to all other human settlements outside DisCity and its satellite towns. They were mostly formed by the catastrophes brought by the <ins>Perishing Star</ins>. Most Outland towns have their own niche resources, but in the Nightfall Era, the Hypercube has an absolute strategic edge, so Outland is still mostly under DisCity's control in terms of trade.

### Metropole

The metropoles were the global hubs of capital and military power before the Nightfall, spearheading early post-calamity city-state investment initiatives and Hypercube mining programs. The original EDGE Protocol was jointly ratified by the metropoles and independent pioneers.
In N.F. 24, building upon the original protocol, five metropoles pooled resources to establish DisCity along the DisSea Border.
The metropole system functioned as a "trans-city-state control consortium," holding pricing dominance over industrial and strategic resources during post-calamity reconstruction, as well as command over the first-generation calamity prevention models. In DisCity's early years, all Hypercube export routes, resource distribution, and law enforcement frameworks operated under restrictions from the metropoles.

### Nirvana Port

Nirvana Port was initially co-governed by the metropoles and DisCity. As it gradually expanded, the port and its connected WhiteSands shipping routes became the lifeblood of the entire continent, leading to the emergence of the Nirvana group, which controlled its trade operations.
Benefiting from conflicts between the various metropoles, DisCity's localist faction gradually rose to prominence within Nirvana. They secretly supported the development of the WhiteSands guerrillas, providing them with intelligence and supplies.

After the nuclear explosion at Nirvana Port, during the Independence War, the port became the Independence Army's first defense line against the Metropole Alliance. Adrian Sterling, commander of the defense line, distinguished himself with illustrious military achievements here.
After Catherine Augustus Andohar left DisCity in N.F. 103, she took over the ruins of Nirvana Port and used it as the base for her assembling Shattered Blade. When necessary, utilizing Rancor resonance and certain parts of the port's structure, the ruins of Nirvana Port can achieve limited aerial mobility.

### Outland Affairs Bureau

The Outland Affairs Bureau (OAB) is a governmental agency responsible for coordinating and managing the city's external affairs, such as foreign trade, diplomacy, and immigration.

### Fraser City

The largest known city outside DisCity. The wealthy and powerful Campbell family lives in Fraser City. It has a firm grip on almost every important sector in the city—politics, finance, trade, and others. Under its stewardship, the city's commerce and logistics services have flourished over the years, ranking among the best in the Outlands. Today, Fraser City's economy is second only to that of DisCity.

### Mayek

The northernmost town in the continent, about 2000 km from DisCity. In the Nightfall Era, the townsmen, not wanting to lose their precious source of energy, did what they could to keep their nuclear power plant afloat and paid a dear price for it. Unfortunately, Catastrophes ravaged the town with increasing frequency and eventually reduced it to rubble. Only one survivor was found, by a rescue team from DisCity, in the ruins of the town.

## WhiteSands

Dis-O-002

A large desert that surrounds DisCity. Its name comes from its abundant white sand.

### WhiteSands

The large desert that surrounds the outskirts of DisCity. It's named for its full of white gravel particles. WhiteSands is a harsh natural environment that periodically erupts with <ins>Tide of Ashes</ins>, and the only human activity found is near the "routes" and the oasis.

### Transportation

There are several official routes running through WhiteSands, jointly built by the City Council and Nirvana Logistics. Special technology is deployed to allow giant sand ships to float above and travel along these routes, which are equipped with anti-Catastrophe devices. As a rule, only DisCity's official sand ships are allowed to use the official routes. According to Nirvana Logistics, getting too close to any of the routes will bring "disastrous consequences" on an unauthorized user.

### Environment

After the Perishing Star struck, Maria City's environs, for some unknown reason, were reduced to a white desert frequented by monstrous sandstorms.
After the founding of DisCity, the authorities built several official routes equipped with anti-Catastrophe devices in WhiteSands. In the area not covered by the official routes, people have to get by with small Hypercube devices.

### Population

WhiteSands has a sparse population. Most of the population is found in the trading posts and intelligence stations near the official routes. The rest are either residents of the small settlements set up around the oasis, or desert bandits who travel between the oasis and the official routes.

### High Seas

WhiteSands is part of DisCity, but it is more like a no-man's-land, due to its harsh environment. The residents of the settlements near the oasis and the roaming desert bandits refer to the area not covered by the official routes as the "high seas," and they don't see themselves as citizens of DisCity.

### WhiteSands Valley

WhiteSands Valley took its name from its geological foundation, WhiteSands, and consists of natural ravine clusters in the heart of WhiteSands. Light reflected off its exposed rock layers casts a bone-white hue across the valley floor. Combined with its rugged terrain and labyrinthine structure, it's also earned the nickname *Death Valley*.

## Tide of Ashes

Dis-O-003

Supernatural disasters that have long plagued various regions of WhiteSands and threaten DisCity regularly.

### Tide of Ashes

The supernatural disaster that has covered all areas of WhiteSands for a long time, periodically encroaches continuously towards DisCity.
Mixed in with the sandstorm are a large number of abnormally distorted creatures that differ greatly from the form of the Corruptors. According to the investigation, these creatures are suspected to come from a dimension different from reality, and their paths always point to DisSea.
The ever-activating Tide of Ashes is preventing DisCity from continuing to expand its routes, and is causing all kinds of dangerous secondary disasters.

### Ash Crystal

Ash crystals are the embodiment of the power of an unknown being. Ash crystals can drive sandworms, confuse people, and even trigger the Tide of Ashes.

### Dreamflower

The power of the Dreamflower was exploited to wreak havoc on WhiteSands' ecosystem. In a joint operation staged by the Outland Affairs Bureau and the MBCC, the MBCC Chief eliminated the out-of-control Dreamflower.

## WhiteSands Settlements

Dis-O-004

Human settlements in WhiteSands in DisCity's suburbs, usually built around oases in WhiteSands.

### Settlements

Human settlements in WhiteSands in DisCity's suburbs, usually built around oases in WhiteSands.

### Sutter Village

The closest village to DisCity. As the environment in this small village continues to go downhill, life is getting tougher for the villagers. Some have stayed, surviving from one day to the next, while others have left, looking for a new life in Eastside.

### Hess Village

DisCity's satellite village. Hess Village is a farming village that provides DisCity with produce in exchange for Hypercube and other resources.
In recent years, a large number of villagers have left the village, and its population has aged markedly, due to a mass exodus of young villagers. And to add insult to injury, the proximity of Hess Village to WhiteSands makes it an easy target for the desert bandits.

## Eastia

Dis-O-005

A continent beyond distant seas that has maintained low-volume Hypercube trade with DisCity over the years.

### Eastian Continent

A fabled distant and vast foreign region possessing mysterious technologies and a unique culture distinct from DisCity.

### Information

A natural geographical barrier separates the eastern continent and DisCity, therefore information is relatively scarce.
1. Geography: The eastern continent has complex terrain with a variety of landforms.
2. Economy: Due to ongoing disasters, its economy is relatively underdeveloped, though it has experienced prosperity at times thanks to its unique seers.
3. Industry: Agriculture is the main industry. No precise data on specific industrial composition. DisCity media often exaggerates their luxury goods industry, but such coverage generally proves false upon verification.
4. Population: The population of the eastern continent is mainly clustered in flatter areas like plains and hills, separated into blocks. They are primarily concentrated in a few central cities, with a smaller portion scattered across various towns.

### Continental Atmosphere

On the eastern continent, myths and customs are inseparable from daily life.
1. Life: Since Nightfall, the eastern continent has been under the constant threat of Mania disasters.
2. Culture: A myriad of myths and festive customs exist. In geographically isolated regions, even neighboring towns can practice different customs.

### Related to Mania

Due to the lack of direct Hypercube production, the eastern continent has developed unique methods to counter Mania over its long battle against the catastrophe.
1. Seers: Roughly equivalent to local anti-Mania technicians, typically individuals or Sinners with exceptional spiritual power. Their main duty is to conduct secret rituals as well as to shape and strengthen collective will. When necessary, they serve as vessels of the will to confront Mania.
2. Secret ritual: A mystical technology commonly used in the east to shape and strengthen the collective will, usually held by seers.
3. Ritual tools: Tools commonly used during secret rituals that are generally crafted by seers.

## Sunsink City

### Sunsink City

A central city located in the eastern part of the eastern continent. It seems to be a new city only established in recent years.
In reality, this Eastian city emerging out of nowhere was built on the ruins of the disaster-struck Sunset Town.

### The Sun Chaser

An investigative organization founded by scholar Du Ruo and residents of Sunset Town that aims at uncovering the connection between the distant Black Ring and the unusual disasters.
In N.F. 113, the founders of the Sun Chaser followed the trail of the Sun Crow Apostle with the Eastian delegation and arrived at DisCity after their long journey.

## Fraser

Dis-O-007

Fraser is the Outland trade hub outside of DisCity, with many smaller cities either dependent on Fraser or under its direct control.

### Fraser

Fraser is situated at the northwestern edge of the continent and is the world's largest city-state after DisCity. It maintains a dominant role within the Farland Alliance due to its formidable trade, mining, and industrial strength.

### City Info

Fraser is the Outland trade hub outside of DisCity, with many smaller cities either dependent on Fraser or under its direct control.
1. Industry
Leveraging its old-world economic foundations, Fraser has preserved relatively intact technological and industrial infrastructure. Its specialized sand ships, designed for traversing WhiteSands, operate continuously, shuttling between various city-states.
Additionally, Fraser boasts a highly developed financial sector and luxury goods industry.
2. Population
Fraser's core urban area has a population of only about one million, while numerous affiliated satellite towns and small settlements sprawl beyond the main city. These peripheral areas house large numbers of marginalized workers and post-calamity refugees. According to the latest census data, the entire Fraser system's population exceeded 12 million in N.F. 110, though the actual figures remain disputed due to the lack of official statistics.
3. Economy
Fraser controls multiple key mineral veins in the northwest and amasses substantial wealth from the Outlands through trade agreements and WhiteSands' exclusive transit mechanisms. It is also one of the few city-states capable of conducting diversified trade with DisCity.

### Fraser Senate

The Fraser Senate is an oligarchic council composed of senators appointed by powerful families and specialized industries. It serves as the ultimate decision-making body overseeing governance and foreign policy. Currently, the Senate is severely divided, with senators holding one another in check.
As a result, the Senate often reaches its decisions through internal dealings and resource exchanges. Its foreign policy frequently wavers between various approaches, reflecting a typical state of interest-based equilibrium.

### Fraser Gold Palace

The Fraser Gold Palace is not a single structure, but rather a palace district comprising a series of interconnected buildings, including the Senate Chamber, the Treasury, and venues for upper-class social gatherings. It serves as both the center of political power and a symbolic stage for financial and diplomatic affairs.
Within the Sky Tower standing at the center of the Gold Palace, a spherical metal sculpture symbolizing "Unity and Development" is erected, which is regarded as the emblem of Fraser's spirit and order.

### The Unity Gala

The Unity Gala is a grand celebration held by Fraser to commemorate the end of the Ash Campaign and the signing of the Peace Treaty. Its motto is: "The storm has passed and the future is here. With humanity united, peace is shared by all."
The Unity Gala aims to reassure the public and promote peace, but in reality, it is an elaborate performance carefully orchestrated by the Fraser Senate to manipulate the Farlands and further deepen conflict.

### Luminaris Society

From its inception, the Luminaris Society has never been a mere academic research institute, but rather a special organization that amasses substantial resources and maintains its own armed forces. Over time, while conducting research on Aurum Secretum and Mania, the Society also cultivated a large number of Aurum Secretum Warriors, actively engaging in indoctrination and propaganda.
The Luminaris Society wields considerable influence both within Fraser and across the Farlands. It is at once a scientific research institution and an embodiment of ideology and military might.

## Farlands

Dis-O-008

The Farlands specifically refers to cities in regions beyond WhiteSands that have no direct trade relations with DisCity.

### Basic Information

All cities outside of DisCity can be referred to as the Outlands, while the Farlands specifically refers to cities in regions beyond WhiteSands that have no direct trade relations with DisCity.
The Farlands are located in the wastelands and highland regions beyond WhiteSands. These small cities, towns, and settlements have harsh living conditions and scarce resources, and their inhabitants are mostly refugees displaced by disasters and war, surviving on food and energy resources provided by Fraser.
Detailed information has yet to be compiled.

### Farland Alliance

The Farland Alliance is a loose political coalition established with Fraser's aid. On the surface, representatives from various parts of Farlands deliberate on matters together, but in reality, they are constrained by Fraser's support framework and public opinion. Thus, outcomes of negotiations are often manipulated by Fraser.
As a political alliance, it is plagued by mutual distrust, a fragile balance of power, and constant conflicts and disputes, making unified action nearly impossible.
