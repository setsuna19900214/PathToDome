# DisCity

## City Overview

DISCITY

### DisCity

Dis-001

DisCity is a new city that mankind built near DisSea after the Perishing Star struck. It's the world's center of civilization today.

#### DisCity

DisCity is a new city that mankind built near DisSea after the <ins>Perishing Star</ins> struck. It's the world's center of civilization today. Split into two sides by the Rust, the west side of DisCity is the center of its Hypercube mining industry, while on the east, Eastside is a modern industrial and entertainment district. As its mining industry dwindled, the West District gradually became a hideout for illegal organizations and a battleground for violent conflicts. In DisCity, that place is more commonly known as *Syndicate*. With the city council's relocation, Eastside has become the most prosperous district. Besides the two main districts, settlements in WhiteSands and satellite towns have also been built.

#### Hypercube

In the early days of the Nightfall Era, humanity was struggling amid wave after wave of supernatural disasters. Dr. Keylan came to their rescue with his discovery of <ins>Hypercube</ins>, on the back of which DisCity was built. The city has been growing ever since, attracting immigrants in droves. Today, DisCity is the only mega-metropolis in the continent, overflowing with glamour and prosperity.

#### Management Structure

With the authorization of Paradeisos, the City Council takes charge of the day-to-day running of DisCity, while the FAC, DisCity's main armed forces, is charged with addressing Catastrophe-related affairs, which include supernatural disasters, Black Rings, Mania, and Sinners.

#### Syndicate

<ins>Syndicate</ins>, originally West District, is a region ruled by violent gangs, as stipulated in West District Order Management Regulations. In Syndicate, conflict and Mania spread wide and far, and unsavory events crop up here and there.

#### Eastside

A district known as <ins>Eastside</ins> on the east bank of the Rust of DisCity. After DisSea Explosion, Eastside built many anti-Mania devices. It's gained stability with the Mania-monitoring network and has been prospering since.

#### The Rust

<ins>The Rust</ins>, originally the Grace River, was a precious river that streamed from the desert cities to DisSea. After the outbreak of BR-001, it's become extremely uninhabitable for humans and is now an isolation belt that separates Eastside and Syndicate.

#### Syndicate

Syndicate was formerly known as the West District. According to the West District Order Management Regulations, the autonomous city is ruled by gangs, making conflicts and Mania rampant in this breeding ground for crime and malice.

#### Regional Info

Social progress in Syndicate is in a spiral of decline.
1. Industry: Syndicate's Hypercube mining industry was once the pillar of its economy, but was shut down after DisSea mining area was annihilated.
2: Economy: Syndicate's GDP has been decreasing, with revenues largely generated in gray-area industries.
3: Population: Syndicate's population has been falling year by year, down to 6.5 million from the all-time high of 9.26 million in N.F. 84.

#### Regional Atmosphere

There is an atmosphere of hostility and tension in Syndicate, a result of the ubiquity of unscrupulous organizations. The region is infested with disorder, violence, and conflict.
1. Public security: The crime rate is persistently high; the public security bureau in Syndicate has no power whatsoever over the local forces.
2. Education: Official educational institutions are non-existent. Statistics show that Syndicate has an illiteracy rate of close to 80%.
3. Culture: Graffiti, rapping, street dance, rock and roll, and other underground activities are very popular.

#### West District Order Management Regulations

In N.F. 99, West District Order Management Regulations was signed. Since then, Syndicate has legally been a self-governing region run by non-governmental organizations, belonging to DisCity in name only.

#### Mania Policy

Left to its own devices, Syndicate hasn't failed to put any measure or policy in place to guard against Mania. Mania Crystals and Mania weapons are widely used in armed conflicts, resulting in the spread of Mania throughout Syndicate.

#### Eastside

A district known as Eastside on the east bank of the Rust of DisCity. After DisSea Explosion, Eastside built many <ins>anti-Mania devices</ins>. It's gained stability with the Mania-monitoring network and has been prospering since.

#### Regional Info

Eastside is the most developed region in DisCity, or even in the world.
1. Industries: Eastside's major industries include housing, commerce, and entertainment. Since N.F. 99, its entertainment industry has been booming, and has now become the pillar of its economy.
2. Population: Eastside is the most populated region in the world, and its population is still growing.
3. Economy: Apart from having a thriving domestic market, Eastside, on account of its monopoly over Hypercube ownership, also receives a continual influx of money from outside its border.

#### Regional Atmosphere

Life in Eastside is like a never-ending party.
1. Life: Life in Eastside is packed with all forms of entertainment. The Eastsiders' daily life is awash with commercial advertisements, cultural events, and celebrity culture, among many others.
2: Culture: The Eastsiders have grown so accustomed to their great abundance of riches and resources that nothing but what is novel and exciting can pique their interest. And they look down upon and are hostile to the Syndicans.

#### Mania Policy

With the help of a Mania-monitoring network, The Eastside authorities identify Maniacs and banish them to West District. The City Council is adamant about keeping Eastside free of Mania to maintain stability and security. Once a Maniac or a Mania event is detected, the FAC will be called upon to handle the situation promptly.

### City Council

Dis-E-004

DisCity's highest governing body, directly elected by its citizens, that holds multiple supervisory and policy-making powers while remaining under the oversight of Paradeisos.

#### City Council

DisCity's highest governing body, directly elected by its citizens, that holds multiple supervisory and policy-making powers while remaining under the oversight of Paradeisos.

#### Function

The city council is responsible for establishing municipal regulations, reviewing budgets, and overseeing policies, with the authority to propose, deliberate, freeze, and remove cabinet members.
It operates multiple committees that manage affairs such as finance, public security, technology, disaster response, and others, and holds final decision-making authority on matters involving civil rights, public resource allocation, and inter-city-state relations.
The council exercises direct oversight and accountability over the following major cabinet departments: the Treasury Department, Bureau of Commerce and Trade, Public Security Bureau, Bureau of Energy and Industry, Outland Affairs Bureau, and Nirvana Office. Each department must regularly submit operational reports to the council, undergo questioning and budget reviews, and secure council approval for key personnel appointments and dismissals.

#### Speaker

The Speaker of the City Council serves as DisCity's highest elected official in practice, carrying dual responsibilities for both administrative and legislative coordination. Through the Speaker's Committee, the Speaker oversees the city council's functions while also acting as the primary liaison with the cabinet government. In this role, the Speaker serves as the central hub for policy announcements, public affairs mediation, and crisis management.
The Speaker cannot simultaneously hold office as a councilor, serves a four-year term, and may be reelected for one additional consecutive term.

#### Speaker Election

The Speaker of the City Council is not elected internally by the council but is instead chosen through a direct citywide popular vote. Each registered voter casts one ballot for Speaker, with the election held concurrently with councilor elections.
1. Eligibility is granted to any resident aged 18 or above, who has maintained continuous residence in DisCity for over five years and possesses DisCitizenship.
2. Active FAC members will have their voting rights temporarily suspended for the duration of their service. These rights will be reinstated upon completion of service and formal discharge from active duty.

#### N.F. 114 Special Act

To further clarify the definition of DisCity's jurisdiction regarding the residency period criterion for voter eligibility, this Act establishes the following supplementary rules for calculating residency periods in specific regions:
For regions recently included within DisCity's jurisdiction, residents' duration of residency shall be calculated starting from the official date on which the region comes under City Council administration.
For regions that were once temporarily separated from council administration, residents' duration of residency shall be calculated starting from the date of reintegration into administration.

### The Great Expo

Dis-E-006

The Great Expo is an inter-city-state exchange summit initiated by DisCity in N.F. 34, held every four years with the goal of restoring human communication networks and fostering resource and technology sharing.

#### The Great Expo

The Great Expo is an inter-city-state exchange summit initiated by DisCity in N.F. 34, held every four years with the goal of restoring human communication networks and fostering resource and technology sharing. However, due to the upheavals of wars and disasters, multiple sessions of the Great Expo were suspended.
In June, N.F. 115, driven by Fraser's initiative, Eastside constructed Cristallia near the original site of the first Great Expo in West District's Keylan Square area to host the 13th Great Expo.

#### History

Keylan Square was constructed in N.F. 33 in honor of Dr. Keylan. The following year, it hosted the inaugural Great Expo, welcoming over 40 city-states. The fledgling DisCity unveiled the Hypercube technology to the world, paving the way for collaboration among city-states in the Nightfall Era.

Beginning in the N.F. 60s, the Outland wars repeatedly disrupted the Great Expo, with only a handful of sessions held as temporary gestures of peace.
In N.F. 106, the 12th Great Expo was convened. Despite facing setbacks and challenges, it ultimately yielded positive outcomes, and the following year, all city-states successfully signed a comprehensive ceasefire agreement.

#### Cristallia

Cristallia is divided into a main hall and an auxiliary hall. While the two facilities stand on opposite sides, each hosting distinct public exhibitions, countless connecting corridors unite them—symbolizing the mutual exchange and communication between city-states and perfectly reflecting this Great Expo's theme: "Be One Again."
In addition to the public exhibition areas, Cristallia's upper floors house private exhibition spaces, accessible exclusively to delegations from various city-states and Eastside's political and business representatives.

#### Closing Ceremony Attack

On June 25, N.F. 115, during the Great Expo's closing ceremony, Outland extremists launched a desperate attack on the exhibition, but the assailants were swiftly subdued by the FAC due to preemptive security measures.

### Ring Research Institute

Dis-E-007

A research institute under United University, now operating largely as an independent entity.

#### Ring Research Institute

Originally a branch under the College of Science at United University, the Ring Research Institute steadily rose in prominence due to the critical nature of its research focus. It eventually became an independent institution, achieving exceptional academic standing in fields related to Black Rings, Hypercubes, and Mania.

Nowadays, the Ring Research Institute only maintains limited contact with United University for select projects, yet it wields considerable influence within the university's broader ecosystem nonetheless, enough to sway personnel decisions and funding allocations. Beyond its dedicated campus within United University, the Institute operates laboratories throughout Eastside and has established a major hub for monitoring, experimentation, and research on the outskirts of BR-003, known as the BR-003 Observation Hub.

#### United University

Founded in the early days after DisCity established independence, United University aimed to preserve civilization and consolidate the city's academic resources. In its early years, it made no distinction between the humanities and the sciences, with its founding mission centered on recovering and safeguarding human knowledge. It was seen as a symbol of DisCity's determination.

As the threat of Mania intensified, the city's demand for cutting-edge technology surged, and resources as well as policies grew to favor science and engineering more. United University was gradually divided into multiple discipline-specific campuses, with science-oriented colleges far outpacing the humanities in prestige, funding, faculty size, and influence alike.

Now, United University represents the pinnacle of academic achievement in DisCity across practically all fields, medicine aside. However, the spirit of equal footing between the arts and sciences that once defined it is all but gone.

#### "Heterogeneous Trial"

A classified program led by EDGE-02 and secretly executed within the Paradeisos-Ring Research Institute framework.
Centered around the Banyan Medical College Hospital, the program's screening scope covered Eastside, the West District, and various satellite towns. It conducted baseline aptitude assessments on newborns and continuously collected samples throughout their childhood.
Screening criteria included intelligence, physical capability, Mania tolerance, environmental adaptability, and behavioral compliance. Following multiple rounds of evaluation and elimination, exceptional individuals who passed the final selection were sent to Paradeisos for special training. Dedicated files were created for other final-round candidates for long-term monitoring, and they could still be admitted upon meeting the required standards.
The program's ultimate goal was to transform gifted individuals into a resource that could be mass-configured and deployed.

#### BR-003 Observation Hub

A large-scale hub for monitoring, experimentation, and research established on the outskirts of BR-003. The facility is situated at a high altitude, concealed by an unclassified dimensional technology that renders it invisible to conventional observation.

Leveraging the unique Mania field conditions surrounding BR-003, the Observation Hub focuses primarily on cutting-edge research topics such as Mania dimensional eigensolution and entanglement. These studies may bear some connection to the Hub's concealment technology.

### Central Library

Dis-E-008

The official central library of DisCity, commonly known among locals as the Grand Library.

#### Central Library

Formally known as the DisCity Central Library, it is commonly referred to by locals as the Grand Library. Its history predates DisCity's independence. According to accounts, the earliest items in its collection were documents and manuscripts restored by surviving scholars in the early days after Nightfall. The library was then relocated and rebuilt several times, eventually becoming what it is today following the official establishment of DisCity.

The library houses over a million texts and digital volumes, including books, documents, local chronicles, and various databases open to the public. Rumor has it that the collection also includes a number of sensitive files and classified records belonging to DisCity.

For materials requiring special clearance, the Central Library employs a multi-tiered information access management system. Accessing restricted documents not only generates an access log but also triggers real-time notifications to the respective information custodians.

#### Head Librarian

The current Head Librarian, Celine Fabius, also serves as DisCity's chronicler of this generation. She has preserved the complete histories and even the memories recorded by every past chronicler, but this inheritance came at a cost. Having absorbed the Maniafied memories from all her predecessors, she herself became a Sinner.

#### The Rust

The Rust, originally the Grace River, was a precious river that streamed from the desert cities to DisSea. After the outbreak of BR-001, it's become extremely uninhabitable for humans and is now an isolation belt that separates Eastside and Syndicate.

#### Regional Info

Chronology: The river started drying up in N.F. 50; its ecology started deteriorating rapidly in <ins>N.F. 84</ins>, when DisSea Explosion took place; and following the Endless Carnival Incident, its environs became almost entirely uninhabited, and the river itself became a dumping ground.

The Rust's ecosystem is still on a downward trajectory. This godforsaken place is a breeding ground for unknown mutations, and an inspiration for DisCity's horrifying urban legends.
Environment: The river bed is clustered with mountains of garbage dumped by the two regions over the years. It is chock-full of contamination of many kinds, unfit for human habitation. In its vicinity roam a great number of dangerous Corruptors. How they have come into being, no one knows for sure, and they're hard to get rid of.
Traces of Human Existence: A small group of people can still be found in the Rust area. These are called Scavengers. They subsist by scavenging, and they have a high resistance to Mania contamination.

### Outlands

Dis-O-001

#### Outlands

In DisCity parlance, Outland refers to all other human settlements outside DisCity and its satellite towns. They were mostly formed by the catastrophes brought by the <ins>Perishing Star</ins>. Most Outland towns have their own niche resources, but in the Nightfall Era, the Hypercube has an absolute strategic edge, so Outland is still mostly under DisCity's control in terms of trade.

#### Metropole

The metropoles were the global hubs of capital and military power before the Nightfall, spearheading early post-calamity city-state investment initiatives and Hypercube mining programs. The original EDGE Protocol was jointly ratified by the metropoles and independent pioneers.
In N.F. 24, building upon the original protocol, five metropoles pooled resources to establish DisCity along the DisSea Border.
The metropole system functioned as a "trans-city-state control consortium," holding pricing dominance over industrial and strategic resources during post-calamity reconstruction, as well as command over the first-generation calamity prevention models. In DisCity's early years, all Hypercube export routes, resource distribution, and law enforcement frameworks operated under restrictions from the metropoles.

#### Nirvana Port

Nirvana Port was initially co-governed by the metropoles and DisCity. As it gradually expanded, the port and its connected WhiteSands shipping routes became the lifeblood of the entire continent, leading to the emergence of the Nirvana group, which controlled its trade operations.
Benefiting from conflicts between the various metropoles, DisCity's localist faction gradually rose to prominence within Nirvana. They secretly supported the development of the WhiteSands guerrillas, providing them with intelligence and supplies.

After the nuclear explosion at Nirvana Port, during the Independence War, the port became the Independence Army's first defense line against the Metropole Alliance. Adrian Sterling, commander of the defense line, distinguished himself with illustrious military achievements here.
After Catherine Augustus Andohar left DisCity in N.F. 103, she took over the ruins of Nirvana Port and used it as the base for her assembling Shattered Blade. When necessary, utilizing Rancor resonance and certain parts of the port's structure, the ruins of Nirvana Port can achieve limited aerial mobility.

#### Outland Affairs Bureau

The Outland Affairs Bureau (OAB) is a governmental agency responsible for coordinating and managing the city's external affairs, such as foreign trade, diplomacy, and immigration.

#### Fraser City

The largest known city outside DisCity. The wealthy and powerful Campbell family lives in Fraser City. It has a firm grip on almost every important sector in the city—politics, finance, trade, and others. Under its stewardship, the city's commerce and logistics services have flourished over the years, ranking among the best in the Outlands. Today, Fraser City's economy is second only to that of DisCity.

#### Mayek

The northernmost town in the continent, about 2000 km from DisCity. In the Nightfall Era, the townsmen, not wanting to lose their precious source of energy, did what they could to keep their nuclear power plant afloat and paid a dear price for it. Unfortunately, Catastrophes ravaged the town with increasing frequency and eventually reduced it to rubble. Only one survivor was found, by a rescue team from DisCity, in the ruins of the town.

#### WhiteSands

The large desert that surrounds the outskirts of DisCity. It's named for its full of white gravel particles. WhiteSands is a harsh natural environment that periodically erupts with <ins>Tide of Ashes</ins>, and the only human activity found is near the "routes" and the oasis.

#### Transportation

There are several official routes running through WhiteSands, jointly built by the City Council and Nirvana Logistics. Special technology is deployed to allow giant sand ships to float above and travel along these routes, which are equipped with anti-Catastrophe devices. As a rule, only DisCity's official sand ships are allowed to use the official routes. According to Nirvana Logistics, getting too close to any of the routes will bring "disastrous consequences" on an unauthorized user.

#### Environment

After the Perishing Star struck, Maria City's environs, for some unknown reason, were reduced to a white desert frequented by monstrous sandstorms.
After the founding of DisCity, the authorities built several official routes equipped with anti-Catastrophe devices in WhiteSands. In the area not covered by the official routes, people have to get by with small Hypercube devices.

#### Population

WhiteSands has a sparse population. Most of the population is found in the trading posts and intelligence stations near the official routes. The rest are either residents of the small settlements set up around the oasis, or desert bandits who travel between the oasis and the official routes.

#### High Seas

WhiteSands is part of DisCity, but it is more like a no-man's-land, due to its harsh environment. The residents of the settlements near the oasis and the roaming desert bandits refer to the area not covered by the official routes as the "high seas," and they don't see themselves as citizens of DisCity.

#### WhiteSands Valley

WhiteSands Valley took its name from its geological foundation, WhiteSands, and consists of natural ravine clusters in the heart of WhiteSands. Light reflected off its exposed rock layers casts a bone-white hue across the valley floor. Combined with its rugged terrain and labyrinthine structure, it's also earned the nickname *Death Valley*.

### Fraser

Dis-O-007

Fraser is the Outland trade hub outside of DisCity, with many smaller cities either dependent on Fraser or under its direct control.

#### Fraser

Fraser is situated at the northwestern edge of the continent and is the world's largest city-state after DisCity. It maintains a dominant role within the Farland Alliance due to its formidable trade, mining, and industrial strength.

#### City Info

Fraser is the Outland trade hub outside of DisCity, with many smaller cities either dependent on Fraser or under its direct control.
1. Industry
Leveraging its old-world economic foundations, Fraser has preserved relatively intact technological and industrial infrastructure. Its specialized sand ships, designed for traversing WhiteSands, operate continuously, shuttling between various city-states.
Additionally, Fraser boasts a highly developed financial sector and luxury goods industry.
2. Population
Fraser's core urban area has a population of only about one million, while numerous affiliated satellite towns and small settlements sprawl beyond the main city. These peripheral areas house large numbers of marginalized workers and post-calamity refugees. According to the latest census data, the entire Fraser system's population exceeded 12 million in N.F. 110, though the actual figures remain disputed due to the lack of official statistics.
3. Economy
Fraser controls multiple key mineral veins in the northwest and amasses substantial wealth from the Outlands through trade agreements and WhiteSands' exclusive transit mechanisms. It is also one of the few city-states capable of conducting diversified trade with DisCity.

#### Fraser Senate

The Fraser Senate is an oligarchic council composed of senators appointed by powerful families and specialized industries. It serves as the ultimate decision-making body overseeing governance and foreign policy. Currently, the Senate is severely divided, with senators holding one another in check.
As a result, the Senate often reaches its decisions through internal dealings and resource exchanges. Its foreign policy frequently wavers between various approaches, reflecting a typical state of interest-based equilibrium.

#### Fraser Gold Palace

The Fraser Gold Palace is not a single structure, but rather a palace district comprising a series of interconnected buildings, including the Senate Chamber, the Treasury, and venues for upper-class social gatherings. It serves as both the center of political power and a symbolic stage for financial and diplomatic affairs.
Within the Sky Tower standing at the center of the Gold Palace, a spherical metal sculpture symbolizing "Unity and Development" is erected, which is regarded as the emblem of Fraser's spirit and order.

#### The Unity Gala

The Unity Gala is a grand celebration held by Fraser to commemorate the end of the Ash Campaign and the signing of the Peace Treaty. Its motto is: "The storm has passed and the future is here. With humanity united, peace is shared by all."
The Unity Gala aims to reassure the public and promote peace, but in reality, it is an elaborate performance carefully orchestrated by the Fraser Senate to manipulate the Farlands and further deepen conflict.

#### Luminaris Society

From its inception, the Luminaris Society has never been a mere academic research institute, but rather a special organization that amasses substantial resources and maintains its own armed forces. Over time, while conducting research on Aurum Secretum and Mania, the Society also cultivated a large number of Aurum Secretum Warriors, actively engaging in indoctrination and propaganda.
The Luminaris Society wields considerable influence both within Fraser and across the Farlands. It is at once a scientific research institution and an embodiment of ideology and military might.

### DisSea Landmarks

Dis-S-002

Special locations or landmarks in the DisSea area.

#### Broken Frontline

To inspect the condition of the Black Ring and contamination within the DisSea area, the FAC has constructed many monitoring stations along the shallows. They're administered by the District of DisSea and are in charge of monitoring and research.
However, as contamination continues to worsen, numerous native species attack the cities irregularly, so the District's function has gradually switched from monitoring and research to combat and defense. Using the outer monitoring stations as foundations, various elite squads of the FAC have collectively built several impregnable fortifications that form the Broken Frontline.

#### Inner Bay

Inner Bay is a small lake area on the edge of DisSea, separated from DisSea by a large dam. Before the DisSea Explosion, the Inner Bay area was once the *safest* mining zone in DisSea and its underwater mining technology was state-of-the-art.
Although Inner Bay seemed only mildly affected by the DisSea Explosion on the surface, the frequent abnormal phenomena occurring in its deep waters eventually crippled its mining industry. For a while, only the underground mines along the Inner Bay's coast functioned normally. These mines that relied on manual mining were only officially abandoned in N.F. 104.
Nowadays, known also as the Oblivion Pit, Inner Bay has become an important research base of the Ring Research Institute.

#### Illusory Moon

Due to the scarcity of data, the existence of the Illusory Moon is yet to be proven.
The Illusory Moon is a bizarre phenomenon described by an extremely small percentage of Sinners. The witnesses claimed to have occasionally spotted a contorted *Illusory Moon of runes* in the sky near DisSea.
According to the preliminary research done by the Banyan Medical College, the Illusory Moon is a hallucination Sinners experience on the brink of a mental breakdown that doesn't truly exist in the physical world. However, the reason why multiple individuals could experience the same hallucination is so far unclear.

Since the reappearance of BR-002, Ring activity in DisSea has begun fluctuating periodically. When it reaches its peak, a visible object resembling a red moon can be observed in the sky, which has been dubbed the Illusory Moon.

Nightmare Space is a sort of illusory realm. The source of its constituents is currently unknown. Activity in this space will lead to immense pressure on the human mind and eventually depletion of sanity.

After investigation, the identity of the Mania bodies emerging alongside the Illusory Moon is suspected to be ██. They are allegedly related to the ██ expedition initiated by Keylan in N.F. 21.
