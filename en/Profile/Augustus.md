# Augustus Profile

DOB: July 14

Height: 175cm

Date of Detainment:

### Past Records

**1**

The subject's full name is Catherine Augustus Andohar, the youngest daughter of the Andohar family in the early Nightfall era. Having experienced the Independence War from the start, she later reached an agreement with DisCity's supreme commander to voluntarily enter cryosleep until her services would be needed again.

**1**

The subject is a human born and raised during the early Nightfall era, so some of her values were shaped by that bygone period and may diverge from modern views. Since Chief has already taken measures to restrain her, and considering that she has shown no obvious aggression while executing missions perfectly, the Bureau has determined there is no need to put her under heightened security or back into cryosleep.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject is able to manipulate magnetic fields. Her manipulation range and the maximum weight she can bear far exceed the limits of ordinary Sinners. With her ability, she can control any metal object, including moving and stopping it, or attacking and defending with it at will.

**Awakening Signs**

When the subject was still a child, her family was once kidnapped. Her exceptional resistance forced the attackers to use Mania weapons on her, but she ultimately defeated all of them. Having come into close contact with Mania weapons, she awakened as a Sinner.

**Corruption State**

The subject displays no obvious physical abnormalities, but she is prone to long-lasting periods of belligerence. Preliminary hypothesis suggests this is caused by both Mania and her inherent personality. Close monitoring and management are constantly required.

### Tracking Records

**3**

A prominent commander of DisCorps who had been put into cryosleep years ago by DisCity's supreme commander and was only recently awakened. Due to her involvement in the war triggered by BR-005, she has joined the Chief of the Minos Bureau of Crisis Control, operating as a partner.

**3**

The subject is the youngest daughter of the Andohar family, the arms manufacturer that earned its infamy during DisCity's early wars. Augustus displayed exceptional leadership and quick thinking from a young age, marking her as a promising future military commander. Doted on by her family her whole life, she always received what she desired, so she came to believe that a strong fighter must possess powerful weapons as well as the ability to come out of any battle the victor.

**3**

Dissatisfied with her family's clandestine manipulation of the war and wanting to protest their attempts to arrange a political marriage for her, she actively engaged in the battlefields of DisCity and the Outlands. At first, she only joined in the battles for the sake of fighting, without any political intent.
However, after the DisCity War of Independence broke out, she was moved by the Independence Army's beliefs and determination. She became their leader and helped them achieve victory in their fight for independence.

**3**

After the war ended, impressed by her undefeated record and combat prowess, the DisCity Independence Army wished to cryogenically preserve her so she could exist eternally as a *weapon* to deter all those who might challenge the fruits of their victory. She readily agreed. However, in N.F. 103, she was awakened by the second commander of the Independence Army, who demanded she eliminate all of DisCorps's opponents. This enraged her, and she ultimately refused to carry out the mission and instead assassinated the commander.

### Supervision Records

**4**

After joining the Bureau, the subject continues to act entirely as she pleases, ignoring the Bureau's rules. Violations include but are not limited to: entering restricted areas without permission, supplying certain Sinners with weapons, and attempting to incite *war* within the Bureau.

**4**

Recognizing her extraordinary military leadership, the Bureau invited her to conduct several lectures on the topic. However, after several excellent lectures, the Bureau received a complaint/inquiry from the subject, claiming that she wished to recruit the attendees to join her *Minos Corps*. Currently, her lectures have been discontinued.

**4**

The subject treats her missions as *wars*, thus after completing each mission, she hosts a ball within the Bureau to celebrate her victory, always taking the opening dance. Sinners such as Hamel and Parfait are frequently invited to attend and dance with her.

**4**

The subject considers Chief very capable and a worthy *comrade* that is her equal, paying close attention to all of Chief's actions. She trusts Chief almost unconditionally and is willing to entrust her own sword to Chief for safekeeping.
