### Past Records

**1**

The subject is the current Head Librarian of the Central Library of DisCity. Using historical research as cover, she covertly collects and compiles information that involves a large amount of highly classified intelligence.
Further investigation reveals that, beyond her official duties, the subject has repeatedly made contact with an unidentified suspicious individual. She is also suspected of acting on their instructions to selectively preserve, alter, and distort historical records using her position of authority. Since her activities appear to serve the specific agenda of an unknown faction and could be exploited to destabilize society's consensus, immediate detention is recommended.

**1**

The subject hails from a family of official historians. She was once forced into exile, losing the right to record history. She later regained the position of Head Librarian after some arduous negotiations and restored limited freedom to continue her work. The alterations and distortions to historical records made during her tenure were in fact a survival strategy under extreme pressure. The pretense of compliance was a means of preserving the right and freedom to continue collecting and safeguarding authentic history. She later became an assassination target by the faction involved, sustaining severe injuries that resulted in complete memory loss.
Current assessment: The subject's behavioral patterns have stabilized following the onset of amnesia, and her threat level has decreased significantly. Continued observation is recommended, with no further control measures required at this time.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can convert her own memories into Mania energy, recreating the scenes and physical components within those memories in reality. The fidelity and duration of the recreations are directly proportional to the importance of the memories consumed.

### Tracking Records

**3**

The subject is an officially registered Sinner under the authority of Paradeisos. She was already on record prior to Chief's appointment, and has long been affiliated with the Central Library, working as a historian. While Chief was trying to learn more about DisCity's history, Schorl introduced the subject to Chief, and the two subsequently became involved in the investigation of the "Maris Andre" historical record tampering case.

**3**

She oversaw the day-to-day operations of the Central Library and had long been engaged in the collection and compilation of historical materials. Having traveled extensively to gather and organize documents from various periods, the subject produced bound records that restored the history of lost eras. While she rarely makes public appearances today, she remains highly reputed within academic circles.

**3**

She is gentle and reserved by nature, never one to provoke others. She possesses an excellent memory for historical documents and expertise in historiography, enabling her to efficiently authenticate materials and identify signs of tampering or fabrication. She has long been committed to uncovering and faithfully conveying historical truth, and holds zero tolerance for any deliberate distortion or falsification of historical records.

**3**

Her family had been history keepers since before Nightfall and had donated historical documents to help found the Central Library. The family was later struck by social unrest. Born a Sinner, the subject spent her early years in seclusion and was self-taught. But even after she escaped her confinement and went into hiding, her actions were always guided by her family's belief: Truth and knowledge should be made honestly and openly available to all.
