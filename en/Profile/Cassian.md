While aboard Euphoria, the subject had the bones of all his fingers removed. This extreme despair and agony directly triggered the awakening of his power.

**Corruption State**

The subject suffers from moderate corruption. While he exhibits no visible physical manifestations, his mental state is unstable and prone to severe fluctuation.

### Tracking Records

**3**

The subject survived the Euphoria explosion and was brought to Eastside by associates. After being discharged from medical care, he wandered the streets until the Chief took him to the Bureau under the pretext of assisting with the investigation.

**3**

The subject was originally the only son of a nouveau riche Eastside family. He received an elite education and was cultivated in the arts, once moving within upper-class circles. After his family fell victim to a conspiracy and was systematically destroyed, he boarded Euphoria to pursue vengeance.

**3**

In reality, the subject is from a common Eastside family. Raised to be cautious, he has always restrained his true self through measured words and actions. He does not identify himself as a member of the upper class and has always maintained an internal distance from it.

**3**

Following his family's downfall, the subject made vengeance his sole purpose. Upon achieving this goal, he lost all interest in living. The investigation reveals that his early experiences fostered an intense resentment for Eastside's entire upper class and a desire to dismantle the social barriers it upholds.
