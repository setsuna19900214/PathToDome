### Past Records

**1**

The subject was once a member of the Black Ring Expedition. The expedition found her and took her in after entering DisSea.
Although her vision is heavily impaired, the subject seems to have the ability to sense Mania disasters and other dangers. With this gift, she once served as the Pyromater Sect's Priestess in Stor Town outside DisSea.

**1**

Currently detained by the MBCC. No significant abnormalities or harmful incidents have been observed to date. The subject remains within the control of the MBCC.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject carries a long metal torch. The flame at the end of the torch burns constantly and can provide basic light and heat in any situation. Additionally, the flame can set Corruptors on fire, lessening Mania's harm to the environment to an extent.

**Arrest Record**

The subject appeared on the day of the full Illusory Moon, causing a large-scale blackout in DisSea. The MBCC's Chief detained her personally. Her body was severely corrupted by contamination and her mind was no different than that of a Corruptor.

**Corruption State**

In a fully corrupted state, her arms have carbonized and she has nearly lost all her humanness and rationality, making communication impossible.

### Supervision Records

Hestia. Female. Approximately 153cm tall in the infected state. Dressed in white clothing resembling a ceremonial gown. Her body is severely corrupted by highly concentrated contamination, but the infection rates of the torch she carries and her highly carbonized arms are surprisingly low.

**4**

**4**

**4**

### Mind Restoration

**5**

After being detained during the Illusory Moon operation in DisSea, the subject has been placed in solitary confinement for observation and treatment. Since all current communication methods have proven futile, the MBCC has received the order for Chief to conduct dream dives with the subject.

**5**

The subject was once known as Tia, a priestess from a town near DisSea. For unknown reasons, the subject was sent into DisSea, where she was saved and taken in by the expedition. The subject went missing around N.F. 21 after getting separated from the crew.

**5**

In her early years, the subject wandered WhiteSands with several companions, surviving through hardships. Due to some incidents, most of her companions perished. In the following years, the subject and her remaining companions survived by luring wanderers for the desert bandits.

**5**

Around N.F. 19, the subject went to a town named Stor in WhiteSands and became the so-called *priestess* there. The subject maintained a high status in Stor Town until she entered DisSea at the beginning of N.F. 21.
