### Past Records

**1**

The subject is the founder and de facto governor of Désir, an Outland immigrant town situated outside DisCity. She also leads a clandestine commercial organization operating along DisCity's borders, wielding considerable influence throughout WhiteSands and the border areas. Despite her mysterious background, she maintains a widespread reputation.
As the head of this organization, the subject's frequent activities along the borders pose a severe security threat to DisCity. Given their adverse impact, immediate detention is recommended.

**1**

Interrogation reveals that the subject's past experiences have shaped her as a pessimist, who firmly believes in the inevitability of tragedy and the immutability of the future. Given that, she dedicated herself to protecting dreams that were doomed to shatter, which ultimately precipitates into the Désir incident. Following Chief's intervention and sharing of personal stories, her fatalistic attitude began to soften. She later made a deal with Chief, voluntarily accepting the shackles in exchange for a more promising future. Her threat level has since decreased, her merchant caravan now a valuable trade partner to the Bureau.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject is able to capture and store others' disillusionment—an invisible substance akin to a compound of spiritual energy. Through specific mediums, she can manipulate these accumulated illusions and convert them into physically destructive force of detrimental magnitude.

**Awakening Signs**

Frequent exposure to disasters in her hometown left the subject with latent Mania contamination. While trading in WhiteSands, she seemed to have formed a certain kind of contract with a mysterious power in the region, awakening her special power.

**Corruption State**

Awakened during the latent period, she shows no physical signs of mutation. She dreams frequently, mostly peaceful and pleasant ones.

**3**

Her privileged upbringing distanced her from commerce, but a series of disasters left her family in desperate straits. Assuming the alias Vautour Bleu, she led her surviving relatives on a perilous journey to DisCity, which unfortunately claimed the lives of all her loved families before her arrival.

**3**

Having lived through the trauma herself, she chose to conceal the truth and deceive the townspeople, taking them under her wing with various methods, such as the datura pollen.
