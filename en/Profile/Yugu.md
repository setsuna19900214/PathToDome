### Past Records

**1**

A mysterious storyteller from Eastia who has gained widespread acclaim in DisCity for her vivid and ingenious tales.
After careful verification, the subject has been identified as the primary culprit responsible for introducing the Mania-infected wisteria into DisCity, posing a serious threat to the city and its residents. When confronted with these accusations, the subject claimed to have no malicious intent, stating she was merely seeking *the end of the story*.

**1**

During her detention, the subject has displayed a cooperative attitude and actively assisted all investigations. Although findings indicate she still has an unhealthy obsession with tragic narratives, under Chief's guidance, she has not exhibited any further extreme behaviors. The two reached an agreement during her interrogation, and she now views Chief as essential to fulfilling her artistic pursuits, expressing willingness to remain voluntarily under the Bureau's supervision.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's special power allows her to *create* and *record* stories through narration, projecting people or objects influenced by her Mania into these narratives. The degree of projection depends on her mental strength and the physical capabilities of those being projected. There has been at least one documented case where a person's entire existence was directly and completely projected into her story. Within these narratives, she serves as the *controller* who can alter characters' bodies and even their destinies, though using this ability depletes her own willpower. Staying in the subject's story world for too long may potentially affect a person's existence in reality.

**Awakening Signs**

Under the subject's influence, her Mania-plagued friend fell apart in illness and became a Corruptor. Upon witnessing this tragedy of a wonderful person meeting ultimate destruction, the subject suddenly realized this was what she had been seeking all along. Wanting to hear her friend's stories, in a moment of frenzy, the subject awakened the ability to resurrect her friend within her own fictional reality.

**Corruption State**

Moderate degree of corruption, with partial deformation in the oral cavity. She is mostly lucid and can lead a normal daily life, though in rare instances when her fervor turns uncontrollable, she could suddenly become overly immersed in her narratives.

### Tracking Records

**3**

After the wisteria fete concluded, the subject voluntarily remained in DisCity and did not resist the MBCC's apprehension and supervision, actively cooperating with all investigations. When questioned about her motives, she claimed she had developed an interest in DisCity and Chief, though the veracity of this statement is still to be determined.

**3**

The subject was born into a prestigious family in Eastia. Her parents worked in artistic fields and maintained a harmonious relationship. Growing up in a happy and loving family, she received an excellent education, read extensively, and never experienced significant setbacks. Because happiness came too easily, she lacked a concrete understanding of the idea of hardship.

**3**

The subject's family later relocated to escape the frequent disasters. During their flight, she witnessed the true face of catastrophe outside of books for the first time. The despair and suffering of people shook her immensely, marking the beginning of her later obsession with tragic destruction.

**3**

After recognizing her obsession with tragedy, she decided to embark on journeys, constantly seeking out cities in turmoil. She collected the stories of their downfall, which she would then dramatize and retell. Over time, her reputation as a storyteller became an urban legend that spread throughout Eastia and eventually reached DisCity after diplomacy between the two regions was established. Currently, she voluntarily remains at the Minos Bureau of Crisis Control, claiming she wishes to stay near Chief to witness the city's ending.

### Supervision Records

**4**

During the early period of detention, the subject adapted well, maintaining her customary unhurried pace of life. The Bureau's library became her most frequented location, where the rich collection of DisCity stories provided her with abundant inspiration for her tragic narratives.

**4**

The subject is considerably knowledgeable about flower planting, yet plants under her care inexplicably wither. Unlike other Sinners who lack gardening skills, she is not discouraged by this outcome. Rather, she takes pleasure in it, finding a unique beauty in withered plants.

**4**

During her free time, the subject seems to maintain friendly relationships with most people in the Bureau and enjoys chatting with other Sinners to collect their stories. Additionally, she regularly holds storytelling sessions where she adapts and narrates new tales she has gathered.

**4**

The subject habitually compiles her travel accounts and collected stories into bound volumes. Starting with the first volume about a *storyteller leaving her home*, she plans to chronicle everything until the day she meets her own destruction. After deciding to remain at the Bureau, she's added a blank page at the end of her collection, reserved for documenting the ending she and Chief will meet together.
