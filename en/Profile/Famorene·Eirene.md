### Past Records

**1**

The subject hails from the Campbell family, a prominent house in Fraser, and deliberately concealed her identity as a Sinner. She directed enterprises under her name to engage in illicit operations and hostile takeovers, in an attempt to encroach upon the Hypercube industry and undermine the economic foundations of DisCity. She later orchestrated her own detainment into MBCC, suspected of harboring greater ambitions.
On the eve of the Cursed Melody incident, the subject made contact with the Sillage Society to obtain guidance on the Underground's Mania power, and leveraged the Mania power of the Apostles controlled by the Underground in an attempt to further enhance her own abilities. Given the subject's existing strategic capabilities and current power amplification, maximum-level surveillance is to be maintained.

**1**

The subject's current abilities are now nearly on par with those of an Underground Sage. She once secretly devised schemes to break free from Chief's control, but was ultimately thwarted by Chief and once again subjected to the shackles' restraint. Due to her highly synchronized link with Chief, the subject likewise fell into a coma after Chief lost consciousness. At present, her mental state and all other readings remain within controllable parameters. As one of the few currently controllable holders of Underground power, the subject possesses extremely high research and strategic value. She is to remain in containment for observation, under the full authority and monitoring of the Bureau.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's original ability has become deeply coupled with Aurum Secretum sorcery and the Apostles' power, further enhancing her combat capabilities. She can now summon energy resembling a chessboard vortex as the core of her sorcery, triggering high-energy Mania reactions that continuously devastate the target area.

**Awakening Signs**

After making contact with the Sillage Society, the subject gained inspiration from the Underground way and absorbed the power of Underground Apostles, further intensifying the Mania within her. Following Chief's re-intervention through the shackles, her abilities broke through their original limits, driven by her own desires.

**Corruption State**

Crystallization is now evident across multiple sites on the subject's body, and her mental state has grown more obsessive than before. Though lucid and fully coherent in her reasoning, she exhibits a pronounced increase in power output, carrying a risk of rampage. Ongoing restraint by Chief is required.

### Supervision Records

**4**

The subject frequently manifests a chessboard using her power to serve as a makeshift game set. As her matches reach their climax, the intrusion alarm has repeatedly been triggered by excessive energy readings, drawing considerable complaints from the security department. The subject dismisses the matter as "a fuss made by those who fail to grasp a stronger being's power," yet readily pays the false-alarm fines nonetheless.

**4**

Following her transformation, the subject no longer plays matches in person; instead, she animates a chessboard through her power to take on challenges on her behalf. With challengers steadily increasing, the construct has since taken up fixed presence in the Recreation Area, now treated by Sinners as a 24-hour self-service chess device. To date, the autonomous chess set remains undefeated.

**4**

Following her transformation, the frequency of conflicts between the subject and other Sinners has notably declined. The subject typically terminates disputes with the rationale that "the strong should be tolerant," attributing others' incomprehension to "the poverty of imagination among the weak." The Bureau has recorded this improvement in her social conduct, though the Sinners involved have requested the retraction of the latter remark.

**4**

Following further escalation of her power, the subject has shown diminishing interest in mundane wealth, redirecting focus to the rare gemstone market, and specifically, the acquisition of top-grade emeralds. Resulting market activity drew attention from multiple business tycoons; emerald prices spiked temporarily. The subject only dismissed the matter with a laugh, stating it was personal: "a gift prepared for a certain family member."

**4**

Following her reclassification as a Scourge-level Sinner, the subject has displayed a marked indifference toward most matches, yet continues to reserve a fixed private match time for Chief. When Chief fails to appear, the subject typically resets the board and plays the match out alone to its conclusion, without seeking another opponent. And she claims that Chief is now the only one who might still be capable of defeating her.

### Mind Restoration

**5**

The subject continues to orchestrate Quinn- and Campbell-related affairs from behind the scenes, and has begun covertly probing intelligence concerning the Apostle of Ashes, the Underground, and the Sillage Society. Her operations are conducted with considerable stealth; she has repeatedly evaded Bureau surveillance and has met with multiple suspicious individuals, including her brother, Adelaide.

**5**

Investigation indicates that the subject made contact with the Sillage Society through the Quinn Group, obtaining intelligence on the Underground's Mania power. She has since repeatedly redirected resources under her name, suspected of paving the way for further action.

**5**

The subject deliberately orchestrated contact with forces connected to the Underground, displaying intense interest in both their power and their objectives, and ultimately absorbed power from them to significantly enhance her own abilities. Her actions were not the product of uncontrolled impulse, but a deliberate act of annexation following long-term planning.

**5**

On the eve of the decisive battle, Chief successfully dismantled the subject's scheme and rebalanced her Mania state through the shackles. Owing to the deep intervention of external Mania, Aurum Secretum sorcery, and the shackles themselves, the subject's abilities underwent a transformation, and she has been formally placed under Scourge-level surveillance.
