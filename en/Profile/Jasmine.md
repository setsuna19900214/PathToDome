### Past Records

**1**

The subject was a member of an Outlands delegation that visited DisCity during the Great Expo, operating under the guise of a performer. During her performance, her abilities destabilized, resulting in extensive property damage. Given the severity of the incident, immediate detention and further investigation are recommended.

**1**

Investigations indicate that the subject is a victim of an Outland war. During the conflict, she endured severe physical and psychological torture, leading to a loss of rationality and distorted perceptions. As a result, conventional treatment methods proved ineffective. However, after receiving comfort and guidance from the Chief, her cooperation has significantly improved, and her aggressive tendencies have diminished. With the Chief's support, she is now working to overcome her trauma and reintegrate into a normal life.

**1**

The subject is, in fact, a covert Outland extremist who infiltrated DisCity with the intent to assassinate high-ranking FAC officials and undermine DisCity's security. The attack she initiated during the Great Expo's closing ceremony resulted in significant casualties and widespread destabilization, warranting immediate detention and stringent containment measures.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can form a deep resonance with specialized War weapons, enabling their physical attacks to be infused with Mania. This infusion forces victims into a state of uncontrollable bleeding.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject claims to have accidentally come into contact with residual battlefield Mania during the Outland war, an exposure that triggered her awakening as a Sinner.

**Corruption State**

Corruption levels are critically high, and her mental state is profoundly unstable, marked by symptoms including auditory hallucinations. Despite this, her ability to disguise herself is extraordinary. At first glance, she could easily pass as an ordinary human. However, when subjected to extreme excitement, the scars inflicted by Mania would begin to glow across her body.

**Awakening Signs**

During the Outland war, the subject was captured by enemy forces during a battlefield sweep. Exposed to Mania from enemy weapons, she awakened to newfound abilities, and with them, a burning vengeance.
