### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can convert intense positive emotions—such as affection, admiration, and awe—into high-energy light beams using a symbolic object as the medium. This symbolic object is typically a piece of fan gear she carries with her. The energy produced is inherently unstable and may unintentionally damage nearby objects if it goes out of control.

**Awakening Signs**

Trace amounts of Mania had accumulated within the subject over time. During an idol event, exposure to a crude mania weapon triggered an accidental awakening of her special ability.

**Corruption State**

The subject exhibits a mild level of Mania corruption, with no visible signs of mutation detected on her body. Her dark circles and depressed state are believed to stem from causes unrelated to Mania. The occasional bursts of energy she displays are suspected to be a distorted emotional response induced by Mania and warrant continued observation.
