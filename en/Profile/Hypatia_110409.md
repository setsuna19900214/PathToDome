### Past Records

**1**

A legitimate Campbell offspring and the youngest researcher in the history of the Fraser Institute, the subject has made significant contributions to the advancement of Aurum Secretum technology. She typically devotes herself to academic pursuits and rarely appears in public. This exchange marks her first official visit to DisCity.
According to the 9th Agency's investigation, the subject is deeply involved in a highly classified project at the Institute. This project receives covert backing from the Campbell family. Specific details remain unknown and require further investigation.

**1**

The subject demonstrates strong self-control, maintains a positive attitude, and actively cooperates with the Bureau's investigation while providing support for special operations. Researchers at the Institute have also highly commended her exceptional academic abilities. Upon confirmation, it has been determined that the subject does not require mandatory detention, but only needs to report periodically to the Bureau for routine Sinner examinations.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

She can manipulate matter upon physical contact, transforming its properties and restructuring its composition. Special metals processed by the subject can resonate with Mania substances, exponentially amplifying their power.

**Awakening Signs**

She was already proficient in using her abilities when visiting DisCity. It is speculated that her powers had awakened during her time in Fraser, though the specific cause remains unknown.

**Corruption State**

She displays a mild degree of Mania corruption with no visible symptoms on her body. She remains lucid, though she still experiences occasional mental fatigue and insomnia after using her abilities.

### Tracking Records

**3**

She represents the Fraser Institute on diplomatic missions to DisCity, exchanging Mania-related technology with the Ring Research Institute. While she enjoys various privileges as an Outland envoy, her status as a Sinner requires her to be registered with the MBCC and regularly examined.

**3**

The subject hails from the Campbell family of Fraser. Displaying exceptional intelligence from a young age, she demonstrated remarkable talent in scientific research. After being granted special admission to the Luminaris Society, the subject quickly made breakthroughs in refining Aurum Secretum technology, earning her the title of Fraser's Star of Tomorrow.

**3**

To further optimize the practicality of Aurum Secretum, the subject voluntarily requested a transfer to the Furor research station in the farlands region. However, for reasons unknown, shortly after arriving at Furor, the subject changed her research focus from Aurum Secretum armaments to defensive Resonance experiments.

**3**

In N.F. 115, BR-005 erupted in the Furor region of the farlands. Reports indicate that the subject was involved in the BR-005 breakout from start to finish. Following the Black Ring detonation, the subject severed ties with Fraser and ventured deep into the farlands in an attempt to locate newly-formed Hypercube mines left behind by BR-005.

### Supervision Records

**4**

Coming from the prominent Campbell family of Fraser and always busy with her official duties, even when she works at the Institute, she usually has her daily life taken care of by personal attendants. Despite that, during her temporary stays at the MBCC, she has declined such services offered by the Bureau and insists on maintaining her own quarters.

**4**

Meanwhile, she appears to be compiling a travel bucket list, though her numerous responsibilities consistently force her to postpone these travel plans.
