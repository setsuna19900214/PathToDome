### Past Records

**1**

An Aurum Secretum resonator affiliated with Fraser. While there is no conclusive evidence, multiple indicators suggest the subject formerly served the Campbell family as a shadow operative, and was involved in numerous assassination cases.
Investigation reveals the subject was an early test subject at the Fraser Institute and was closely related to their Aurum Secretum research. The subject has now defected from Fraser for unknown reasons. She is extremely dangerous and must be immediately detained.

**1**

Following her capture, she has accepted the shackles and established cooperation with the MBCC, regularly returning for Aurum Secretum treatments. Despite her unpredictable behavior, her combat value in special operations cannot be overlooked. She will be temporarily released to WhiteSands to monitor Apostles of Ashes. At present, she shows no further escape intentions and her condition is manageable, but regular assessment of her mental state and loyalty remains necessary.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Through Aurum Secretum resonance, she can acquire properties of Ash Crystals. When activating this ability, her hands transform into massive Apostle claws that cut through any material that comes into contact, rapidly breaking down the substance.

**Awakening Signs**

Her special power was artificially triggered. It is theorized that Fraser had forced her to resonate with Mania through experiments and stimulation with the Underground's sorcery. The awakening process was involuntary and accompanied by intense mutation and severe mental shock, likely causing irreversible effects on her personality.

**Corruption State**

Physically, she exhibits visible signs of Mania mutation and traces of Aurum Secretum corruption, with her hands severely mutated. Mentally, she is extremely unstable and chronically agitated.
Additionally, having been affected by Aurum Secretum for so long, her lifespan has been drastically shortened. Currently, the corruption is gradually stabilizing under the control of the shackles, but it remains difficult to determine how long she can still live.

### Tracking Records

**3**

She primarily appeared in Fraser and its surrounding regions. During her time as a Campbell shadow operative, she participated in 47 assassination and espionage missions, claiming countless lives. After the BR-005 explosion, she defected from Fraser and settled in WhiteSands. Shortly after that, she encountered Chief and initiated a conversation.

**3**

Around N.F. 85, she arrived at the Fraser Aurum Secretum Research Institute with her parents from their hometown. Severely corrupted and mutated by Mania, she was selected as a research specimen by the Fraser Institute. Raised in an extremely dehumanizing environment from childhood, she was one of the few survivors among numerous test subjects. She achieved resonance with Aurum Secretum and gained the ability to utilize Ash Crystals.

**3**

According to her testimony, Fraser was already planning the Fifth Black Ring Project while advancing Aurum Secretum technology and creating test subjects with the Underground's assistance. After extensive experimentation and selection, the subject was chosen to be sacrificed as ground zero of the Black Ring. In response, she orchestrated the facility's explosion and defected from Fraser.

**3**

After leaving the Fraser Institute, her whereabouts were unknown for a while, until she appeared behind Fraser's lines during the Outland war. She sabotaged and prevented Fraser's further actions involving BR-005, helping to shift the tide of the war.

### Supervision Records

**4**

She visits the MBCC from time to time to provoke Chief, seeking a proper duel. Each time, Chief arranges for powerful Sinners within the Bureau to train with the subject. After training, she receives Aurum Secretum treatments in a designated room.

**4**

During her stays at the MBCC, food supplies in the cafeteria often run out and extra budget is required for special meal provisions. Investigation reveals the subject strongly prefers raw, cold meat and shows utter contempt for finely-prepared cuisine.

**4**

Her temperament has led her to develop numerous unorthodox applications of Aurum Secretum technology that diverge from conventional theories. This innovation is unprecedented in both Fraser's records and academic studies. Researchers frequently invite her to participate in academic discussions, but all invitations have been turned down due to lack of interest.

**4**

As of now, the Aurum Secretum corruption in the subject's body is under effective control. However, she still often shows up at Chief's office without permission, demanding her shackles be strengthened, even threatening Chief with her own life. Despite numerous warnings, she remains unrepentant and insists on behaving in the same manner.
