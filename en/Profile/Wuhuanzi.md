### Past Records

**1**

A wandering herbalist from Outlands. The subject claims to have received years of systematic medical training in Eastia, possessing solid professional knowledge and clinical experience.
However, upon verification, the subject is found to hold no officially certified medical license. Furthermore, she has repeatedly exaggerated her patients' conditions in a fear-mongering manner. The medicinal herbs and treatment methods she uses are not approved in DisCity and present significant safety hazards. As she is confirmed to be a habitual offender of unlicensed medical practice, an urgent secondary assessment of her threat level is required.

**1**

A background check confirms the subject to be a professional doctor from Eastia. With a knowledge system that differs significantly from that of DisCity, her unlicensed practice was not intentional. After experiencing a series of disasters including the destruction of her hometown and the deaths of loved ones, she once struggled to understand the meaning of saving lives. However, she never abandoned her calling, continuously seeking answers through her medical practice across various regions.
Under Chief's guidance, the subject has gradually let go of ideas troubling her, choosing to follow her heart. She no longer practices medicine in search of meaning, but out of her own kindness and wish, simply because she *wants* to save people.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can release spiritual energy through self-made talismans, thereby altering the state of physical matter to attack, confine, or heal. However, this ability is not fail-safe and the ultimate effects are unpredictable.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject's hometown was struck by a severe disaster. During this time, her parents and teachers tried their best to save lives but in vain, eventually being consumed by the disaster one after another. After witnessing these events, she suffered an emotional breakdown, awakening her special power, which saved her life under these extreme circumstances.

**Corruption State**

The subject displays only mild corruption with no visible mutations. Though lucid most of the time, she often develops pessimistic ideas.

### Tracking Records

**3**

During her travels, she was unexpectedly drawn into the fete of the wisteria, which led to her encounter with Chief. After assisting in resolving the incident, due to personal considerations, she voluntarily requested to be detained by the Bureau as a Sinner.

**3**

The subject has a passive and pessimistic personality, yet she is adamant about healing others to earn good karma. She firmly believes in the principle of karmic cycles, convinced that by performing good deeds and amassing good karma, she can reverse her constant misfortune.

**3**

She was born into a medical sect in Eastia and studied medicine from a young age. However, her hometown was later destroyed by a disaster. Despite the desperate efforts of her parents and fellow sect members—all healers themselves—they were unable to change the situation, and ultimately all perished. This event caused severe psychological trauma to her, profoundly shaking her faith as a healer.

**3**

Having witnessed the futility of medical knowledge against the Mania disaster, she once questioned the meaning of medicine itself. During her travels, she claimed to save lives merely to *earn good karma for her own profit*, yet in reality she consistently provided medical assistance at minimal cost, subconsciously still searching for her true purpose as a healer.

### Supervision Records

Despite multiple attempts to dissuade her from this practice, such efforts have only seen very limited effect.

**4**

The exotic Eastian medical practice once became a fad among Sinners. However, due to her mystical and pessimistic diagnostic style, many remain skeptical of her conclusions despite the ultimately positive treatment outcomes.

**4**

To help strengthen Chief's health, she's developed a complex daily regimen combining the foundation of Eastian medical arts with the essence of DisCity medicine. After one month of compulsory implementation, Chief's finally managed, through persistent negotiation, to reduce the dosage from three bowls of medicine daily to just one.
