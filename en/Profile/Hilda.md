### Past Records

**1**

The subject is a metropole agent who infiltrated DisCity during the Independence War. While undercover, the operative intercepted and decrypted numerous high-priority intelligence reports. Following capture, the subject was forcibly confined to the Deep Restricted Area, where she initiated a consciousness shutdown to evade interrogation. The subject exhibits pronounced self-destructive tendencies.

**1**

Rigorous assessments indicate the subject's self-destructive tendencies are gradually diminishing. Memory distortions and thought disorders induced by the LINK technology are also showing progressive recovery. Due to the sensitive nature of the subject's identity and capabilities, authorities have strictly denied bail. However, in accordance with the Chief's advice, no additional restraint measures will be enforced beyond sustained observation. Her abilities may provide unexpected tactical advantages in future operations.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject possesses the ability to extract concealed secrets from targets, materializing them as Mania strips covered in telegraph code. Her unique rapid decryption capability allows her to access opponents' memories and vulnerabilities through intelligence decoding, though this process requires an activation period.

**Awakening Signs**

During a special operation, the subject was exposed to smuggled Ash Crystal and Rancor contamination of metropole origin. This multiple-exposure event forced an abrupt awakening, resulting in her transformation into a Sinner.

**Corruption State**

The subject suffers from a deep-seated mental contamination, with minor physical mutations manifesting as a direct result of her psychological state. Under standard conditions, her condition does not impede daily functioning; however, she periodically experiences acute episodes of complete mental breakdown and dissociation.
