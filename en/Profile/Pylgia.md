### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's weapon is a diving harpoon, but she does not usually fish with it and instead only uses it for self-defense when attacked by hostile marine life.

**Arrest Record**

The subject appeared on the day of the full Illusory Moon, causing a widespread blackout in the DisSea region. She was personally detained by the MBCC's Chief. Her body shows signs of contamination and corruption, and is already transforming into a Corruptor.

**Corruption State**

The subject exhibits moderate corruption, retaining both humanity and rational thought, though her body is already manifesting irreversible physical changes typical during the Corruptor transformation.
