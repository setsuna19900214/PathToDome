### Past Records

**1**

The subject was formerly a core technical specialist within Eastside's forensic examination system. However, her license was revoked due to severe violations of professional ethics. The subject then went into hiding within the Syndicate, accepting private examination and consulting requests anonymously.
The subject exhibits an eccentric personality and maintains an abnormal fixation on specific cases. Her abilities have been utilized to evade tracking and to relocate sealed biological specimens. Given the subject's unclear motives, obsessive temperament, and apparent tendency to overstep boundaries, immediate detainment is recommended.

**1**

The subject's actions are not driven by pathological compulsion or destructive intent. The bodies she relocated are all involved in complex cases ruled as uncontested deaths despite existing discrepancies. She aims to determine the true cause of death through unconventional autopsy methods when procedural justice fails.
While the subject exhibits severe social maladjustment, she ultimately provided critical testimony to Eastside law enforcement. Her core behavior remains driven by a principle of "truth above all." Under shackles with clearly defined operational parameters and through direct communication with Chief, the subject's willingness to cooperate and stability can be significantly improved. Her professional expertise also holds exceptional value.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can enter a state of low-visibility for brief periods and extend this invisibility to people or objects through direct physical contact. The ability is better suited for evading pursuit, covert transportation, and close-range surveillance evasion. Provides limited enhancement in direct combat.

**Awakening Signs**

According to the subject's account, she was tracked and targeted for elimination by certain forces. She fled into areas near the Rust's contamination zone to seek refuge. Under mortal threat and high contamination exposure, she triggered a Mania reaction and awakened her abilities.

**Corruption State**

The subject's corruption level is moderate. No mutations on the body have been observed. Mental state shows significant fluctuation, and the subject exhibits low social engagement and constant irritability in daily life. She is capable of maintaining extreme composure and focus during professional procedures, but experiences notable emotional rebound afterwards.
