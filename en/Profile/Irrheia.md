### Past Records

**1**

A staff member of Nightwrought, a murder mystery game parlor in Eastside. She plays the "Witch of Rewind" in the establishment's renowned script Cirrenfold, and is also its writer and director, having been in charge of its setup, game rules, and plot progression.
The subject and the murder mystery game space she constructs are closely connected to Mania. The intense, uncontrollable plot likely permanently affected the personalities of its players. With the high potential risk, her immediate detention is necessary.

**1**

The subject's exact background could not be ascertained through interrogation. But communications with other parties and further tracking reveal that she has worked at the murder mystery game parlor for many years. Previously, she had only used her power to construct illusions, but never to alter civilians' personalities or commit crimes. She only wanted to bring out the admirable qualities within the participants through her plot, entertaining them while alleviating her own numbness and boredom. Following Chief's personal persuasion, her interest has shifted to Chief and the real world. She no longer offers fictional experiences to others, and is restricted by Chief's shackles. Threat level is now under control.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject constructs enclosed spaces using her notebook as a medium, generating hyper-realistic "scripted illusions" within a certain range. By using the "Aurum Secretum Hourglass," she can further control time and bend physical rules within the illusions, constraining those within the script. Her ability to manipulate and control is extremely potent.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject claims that during the Mania outbreak, she encountered a Sinner from the Outlands and obtained the Aurum Secretum device left by the Sinner. She came into close contact with the rampaging Mania power and awakened her own special power as a result.

**Corruption State**

Corruption level is moderate with no obvious visible signs on the body. Nearly all Mania power has been channeled into the notebook.

### Tracking Records

**3**

In the Cirrenfold script, the subject had always been the game's master, appearing very rarely in early stages. Only after Chief later discovered signs of Mania did she finally engage actively as the main antagonist. After the experience ended, Chief confirmed she indeed worked at Nightwrought when revisiting the venue, and thus invited her to cooperate with the investigation.

**3**

She came from a rich family, but was born merely for medical purposes and existed as a "donor." From childhood, every detail of her life was strictly controlled to ensure optimal physical health. Her formative years were devoid of any stimulation, and serving her family was all she knew. Later, due to advances in medical technology, she was no longer needed as a blood donor, and she lost all her life's purpose.

**3**

When she embraced Mania and obtained her special power, she was moved by the sensations and felt alive for the first time in her life. From then on, that became her way to escape from boredom. She began pursuing thrills and conducted her first experiments on her parents and elder brother. But the unsatisfactory results made her turn toward scriptwriting. She has not been vengeful toward her family since.

**3**

The two records above are both based on the illusions in the script written by the subject, so she might have taken some creative liberties. As the subject has made testimonies under other identities, the authenticity cannot be confirmed. Despite that, her account regarding her motive seems genuine. All her actions are based on her pursuit of thrills, and the scripts are merely her tools.

### Supervision Records

**4**

The subject maintains a messy daily schedule, never eating, sleeping, or doing other regular tasks at fixed times. She claims this is her way to govern her own life instead of letting a timetable dictate everything. Many night owls within the Bureau buy this philosophy. However, she considers staying up late regularly another form of routine, and thus refuses to join them.

**4**

The subject eats at irregular times, often cooking "creative" meals using ingredients from the cafeteria during off-hours, experimenting with inventive flavor profiles and food combinations. A certain Sinner was once traumatized after consuming her leftovers and swore to never steal others' food again. The cafeteria consequently gave the subject a "Food Safety Contribution Award."

**4**

The subject isn't good at recognizing people, only selectively remembering individuals she considers interesting or vibrant. She treats everyone else with a fixed set of polite gestures, earning her the nickname "NPC" from some Sinners. The subject's social behavior is highly selective, and she tends to observe rather than engage actively.

**4**

The subject is suspected of using unknown means to monitor Chief's missions. She has previously adapted Chief's operations into immersive scenarios for other Sinners to playtest, widely circulated within the Bureau. When Chief's field operations fail, Chief often experiences alternative, ideal endings in dreams. The subject denies her involvement, only claiming that her scripts were "inspired by real people."
