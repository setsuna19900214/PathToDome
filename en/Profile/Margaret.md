### Past Records

**1**

The subject has been stationed in Fraser for years as a member of a diplomatic delegation. Officially serving as a peace ambassador, she is in fact a secret agent who has spearheaded multiple high-risk infiltration operations, established an intricate network of contacts, and demonstrated exceptional manipulation techniques.
During the Peace Gala incident, she was tasked with intervening in a Fraser conspiracy aimed at further manipulating the farlands to seize control of the Hypercube mines. The mission ultimately ended in failure. Given the critical nature of this operation, the relevant department has raised concerns that the failure may have been on purpose. Further interrogation is urgently required.

**1**

Interrogation reveals that the subject's special power awakened when experiencing a conflict in the farlands as a child. She regards peace as her own work of art, an aesthetic of order to be designed and orchestrated at will. Following BR-003, disagreeing with DisCity's current direction, she voluntarily undertook an infiltration mission in Fraser. While her actions were dishonest, they resulted in no casualties and, on multiple occasions, successfully advanced the peace process.
Although her core mentality is extremely self-centered and her methods of manipulation are discreet, her operational logic is clear, and her true motives have been exposed. Furthermore, she is restrained by the shackles and under close surveillance by Chief. Her situation is currently manageable. Continued long-term behavioral assessment and ideological monitoring are recommended.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can establish a psychic contract with targets through formal gestures such as hand-kissing or cheek-kissing. This causes targets to lose any desire to attack, instinctively avoid hostile behavior, and even begin to trust or obey the subject.

**Awakening Signs**

According to her, during a diplomatic visit to the farlands with her father as a child, she was kidnapped during a conflict. At the moment of crisis, her special power awakened, allowing her to negotiate with her captors and ultimately securing their cooperation and her release. The subject is a childhood friend of Ms. Langley Blackett and Ms. Bernadette Cavendish. Her special power accessory was obtained through one of them, though the subject has never agreed to join.

**Corruption State**

Awakened during the latent period. No physical signs of mutation. Possesses exceptionally strong mental power, enabling perfect control over her own emotions and psyche.

### Tracking Records

**3**

Following the Peace Gala incident, she was recalled to DisCity to receive a commendation. In reality, it was due to suspicion from her superiors regarding her performance during the mission. Rather than evading supervision, she immediately presented herself at the MBCC, requesting to be interrogated and consenting to be shackled and investigated while remaining calm and cooperative throughout.

**3**

She is descended from Paradeisos nobility. As a child, she accompanied her father on tours through the farlands, witnessing the cruelty of war and the effectiveness of diplomatic mediation. This solidified her commitment to peace. Following the BR-003 incident, she was ordered to participate in negotiations aimed at weaponizing the Black Ring. Disillusioned, she chose to travel to Fraser, seeking to carry on her vision of peace through her own methods.

**3**

She has admitted to deviating from DisCity authorities' plan by covertly supporting the farlands' autonomy, resulting in DisCity's failure to secure the Hypercube mines. This confirms suspicions of her "betrayal." The subject claims that while events took an unexpected turn, her objective has remained unchanged: to achieve peace with her own strategy, while seeking Chief's trust and support.

**3**

She cultivates a gentle persona, yet takes pleasure in manipulating others and intended to incorporate Chief into her own schemes. Her facade was ultimately seen through by Chief. After Chief exposed her machinations and demanded intervention and supervision, after a brief silence, she ultimately chose to compromise, accepting full surveillance over her actions and espionage activity.

### Supervision Records
