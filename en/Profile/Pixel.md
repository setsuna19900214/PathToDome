### Past Records

**1**

Operating entirely anonymously under the "computer head" avatar, the subject has been involved in various kinds of academic fraud and numerous academic dishonesty scandals. Further investigation reveals that she holds substantial incriminating evidence against reputed academics and the upper echelons that can destabilize the entire academia's power structure. She is also capable of arranging the timed release of such information that can potentially trigger a chain of mutual whistleblowing.
The subject's activities constitute systematic damage to Eastside's academic ecosystem, and the destructive potential of the information she holds is unpredictable. Immediate detainment is thus recommended.

**1**

Interrogation reveals that the subject was once admitted to a prestigious university with exceptional grades, but was later forced to join an academic fraud ring led by her professor due to her mounting student loans. Ultimately made the ring's scapegoat, she was expelled from school and banned from academia. This caused her to lose all faith in the corrupt system. Since then, she has adopted the identity of "Pixel," offering academic fraud services as a means of building an underground intelligence network.
While her methods are rather extreme, her sole objective is to dismantle the systemic academic corruption from within and restore order in academia. She is clearly aware of the consequences of her actions and willing to be held accountable. Given that she has cleared her name and achieved her goal, her actual threat level is believed to have decreased significantly.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Her ability allows her to will desired objects into existence, functioning much like a 3D printer. However, these objects contain subtle structural imperfections and eventually degrade due to data corruption.

**Awakening Signs**

She claims to have become infected with Mania from contact with contaminated biochemical substances during an experiment. After being expelled from university and banned from academia, the ensuing sense of irony and despair pushed her into intense emotional turmoil, which ultimately triggered the awakening of her power.

**Corruption State**

She suffers rather severe corruption. Her mental state is abnormal, exhibiting manic and dissociative tendencies. Nevertheless, the "computer head" is a physical manifestation of her special power, not a result of corruption. She displays no significant physical signs of mutation.
