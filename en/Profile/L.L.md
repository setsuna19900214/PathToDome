### Past Records

**1**

The *island owner* of the famous dark web forum Elysiland. The subject has turned the site into a virtual world where everything is allowed, stimulating and trapping people's consciousness with the light and digital particles of the internet.

**1**

The subject's extreme behavior can largely be attributed to her inability to connect with the outside world, which can in turn be attributed to her illness and the impact of being abandoned by her parents at an early age. The subject's actions are not malicious, but stem from a deep-seated longing for stable connections with others and a yearning for freedom. She is currently being guided by Chief to engage with the real world and improve her understanding of it, and she has started making friends offline.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's awakened ability resembles a computer worm, contaminating data through her digital pet that follows her everywhere. Guided by her will, it duplicates and sends different electronic symbols to different targets, interfering with their central nervous systems. Investigations reveal that these data signals range from high-quality to junk in quality.

**Awakening Signs**

Due to the stress and despair brought by her condition, the subject lost all hope in life and initially intended to take her own life in the bathtub. At the brink of death, her virtual relationships saved her, simultaneously awakening her power.

**Corruption State**

The subject's corruption is severe but mostly affects only her mind, so she shows no obvious crystallization or marks on her body.

### Tracking Records

**3**

The subject was the mastermind behind the sensational Elysiland mental contamination incident. With the Bureau's technical support, Chief infiltrated Elysiland and successfully arrested her, resolving the Elysiland incident.

**3**

The subject suffers from an extremely rare case of congenital hydrophilia. This genetic disease causes full-body muscle pain and weakness which can only be alleviated by soaking constantly in water. With Chief's shackles, she can come out of the water and walk freely for a short time, though her skin must be kept moist. Research indicates that a complete cure is impossible.

**3**

Since her earliest memory, the subject has never left the bathtub at home. Her early understanding of the outside world came entirely from her parents. Initially attentive, the subject's parents grew weary over time and bought her a computer to keep her occupied and avoid conversations with her.

**3**

Upon acquiring the computer, the subject became addicted to the online world. However, her condition prevented her from forming lasting connections with people. The dark side of the internet began to affect her, making her irritable. The subject's parents eventually abandoned her, leaving only an online bank account through which they regularly sent her money, never to be seen again.

### Supervision Records

**4**

Upon arriving at the Bureau, contrary to the subject's lively and talkative online persona, she displayed rather severe social anxiety and avoided eye contact and face-to-face conversations. If an interaction was inevitable, she might even bring the person to the computer to conduct the chat online.

**4**

The subject is addicted to online shopping and lacks self-control. She once accumulated so many packages that they overflowed from her room into the Bureau's corridor.

**4**

In the subject's early days at the Bureau, her inability to converse directly with other Sinners and her dependence on her computer brought her great inconvenience. Later, Sinner Eleven lent her an old personal device so the subject could communicate through typing, which got her through the transition period.

**4**

Perhaps because Chief's shackles allow her to come out of water, the subject frequently visits Chief's office for a chat or asks the Chief to take her out. Her behavior disrupts Chief's daily work to a certain extent.
