# Necresta·Hella Profile

### Past Records

**1**

Previously active in Syndicate's violent districts, the subject was involved in multiple gang incidents before being transferred to the MBCC for being a Sinner, where she participated in numerous Black Ring operations.
After the West District Black Ring incident, she traveled to WhiteSands in Outlands to broaden her horizons, and later became involved in the BR-005 explosion while investigating the Underground's inheritance.

**1**

Having been directly infected by BR-005, she still retains residual contamination in her body. When the balance of her self-healing ability and Mania is disturbed, this contamination may overflow again. Despite that, under the shackles' control, she is currently mentally and physically stable, making her an important combat asset for the MBCC.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's original self-healing ability has been significantly enhanced. Additionally, under the influence of the shackles, her self-healing ability and the Black Ring contamination in her body have reached a balance, granting her the power to control the wisps and substantially increasing her combat capabilities.

**Awakening Signs**

After she was contaminated by BR-005, the Mania within her body intensified, triggering a second awakening.

**Corruption State**

After the BR-005 incident, her mental state has recovered, but her body still bears some scars from the corruption that are unlikely to heal.

### Supervision Records

**4**

After acquiring her new ability to control the wisps, her pranking skills have also leveled up. She frequently lurks around late at night pretending to be a ghost, manipulating objects to hover in the air to scare passersby, a tactic that works without fail.

**4**

After losing her preferred steel pipe, she now primarily uses wisp blades in combat. However, an anonymous source claimed to have witnessed the subject swinging one such blade like a pipe skillfully when she got excited in a battle.

**4**

She's been observed to occasionally wake up from nightmares during the night, a condition that improves when sharing space with others. Typically, after such episodes, she would gather her blanket and pillow and run to another Sinner's room, though the subject vehemently denies this behavior.

**4**

During her travels in the farlands, she kept an adventure journal, which was unfortunately lost during the Black Ring explosion. She claims she intends to write a book about her legendary adventures, though she's never moved past the first chapter of her manuscript.

**4**

Even after being reclassified as a Scourge Sinner, she has not abandoned her old bad habits. She still enjoys inventing different ways to steal pocket money from Chief's wallet. Rather than restricting this behavior, Chief has decided to engage in ongoing defense of the wallet. To date, the subject's record stands at three wins, twelve losses, and one draw.

### Mind Restoration

**5**

In N.F. 113, following the High Tower Black Ring incident in West District, she requested to leave the MBCC temporarily and departed for WhiteSands with a Legion convoy to undergo self-training.

**5**

In N.F. 115, while investigating Fraser's inheritance, she was caught in the BR-005 explosion and fell unconscious under the Black Ring's control. She subsequently moved with the Apostle swarm toward the DisCity border.

**5**

During the BR-005 eruption, she was trapped within the core of the Black Ring. Due to her unique nature, she became the anchor supporting the Black Ring's movement. To prevent this Black Ring from further impacting the battlefield, DisCity authorities designated the subject as a priority target for elimination.
As the war evolved and the elimination plan progressed, Chief ultimately broke into the Black Ring and successfully rescued the subject under unusual circumstances.

**5**

After the BR-005 incident, the subject officially returned to the MBCC. Due to the intensification of Mania within her body, the subject's abilities have been significantly enhanced, leading to her reclassification as a Scourge Sinner following multiple evaluations.
