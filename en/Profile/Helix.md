### Past Records

**1**

The subject is a peripheral Underground member with a long-term covert presence in DisCity. She has taken part in the Underground's Overground operations multiple times, and is suspected of aiding in sorcery setup, procuring controlled materials, and providing follow-on support. Previously selected as a key instrument and vessel for an Underground scheme, she attempted to fulfill that role at the Cursed Melody performance. Upon being stopped, she turned hostile toward the Bureau personnel sent to rescue her and obstructed their operations.
The subject demonstrates unwavering loyalty to the Underground, with a high risk of escape and repeat offenses. Immediate detention is thus recommended.

**1**

Review and tracking verification confirmed that the subject's family was wiped out by hired killers after her parents refused to hand over exploitable research results to malicious capital interests. She was then betrayed by welfare hospital staff, abandoned at the Rust, and taken in by the Underground immediately after her power was awakened. There, she underwent systematic indoctrination and became a member.
Within the Underground, the subject was only ever an expendable pawn kept unaware of the organization's true intentions, discarded once she had carried out her indoctrinated role. Accordingly, the subject's wrongful conduct should be attributed to deliberate manipulation by the Underground. She is fundamentally a victim who received no rightful aid at any stage, and the original charges should be corrected and revoked.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Upon activation, the subject's ability briefly reinforces her body: the porcelainization of her skin intensifies, her body becomes unbreakable, wound recovery accelerates sharply, and she gains resistance to Mania corruption. As a result, she experiences a near-total absence of pain sensation in her normal state.

**Awakening Signs**

Interrogation records indicate that the subject's family was murdered by hired killers in her childhood, and she was likewise implicated and abandoned at the Rust. Exposure to an outburst of Underground sorcery, combined with severe Mania shock and emotional turmoil, triggered a passive awakening.

**Corruption State**

The subject exhibits high-level Mania corruption. No significant abnormalities beyond skin porcelainization and fractures, but bodily sensation is nearly absent, leaving her oblivious to pain and injury. She shows innate deficits in emotional perception and expression, a tendency toward extreme thinking, and blind faith in Underground ideology.
