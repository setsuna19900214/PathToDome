### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can designate any object as a commodity, using Mania to amplify others' desire for it and draw them toward the target. The longer the ability remains active, the more irresistible the attraction grows. If the designated target is a living being, their mobility will be impaired to a certain degree.

**Awakening Signs**

After her family's downfall, the subject drifted to Syndicate, where she was deceived into transporting and selling Corruptor remains to make a living. During this work, she accidentally came into contact with Mania contamination and awakened her abilities when faced with mortal danger.

**Corruption State**

The subject displays mild corruption without evident physical abnormalities, and her mental condition remains largely stable. However, under Mania's influence, the obsessive aspects of her personality have become heightened.
