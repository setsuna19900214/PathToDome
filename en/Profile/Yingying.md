### Past Records

**1**

The Wisteria Fairy who's lived over a thousand years, documented in Eastian legends. Every New Year, she holds a fete to grant blessings, attracting numerous devotees who come to make wishes. However, rumors also claim the subject is a spirit transformed from a millennium-old wisteria tree that drains people's Qi and eventually their lives, and that none who attend her fete ever return alive.

**1**

The subject is a survivor of the Perishing Star's fall in N.F. 01. The collective dying memories and intense survival instincts of thousands at the fete merged with her, awakening her as a Sinner. For a century, whenever the fete reappeared, she always sacrificed her power and life energy to appease restless spirits and protect visitors caught within. After a chance encounter with Chief, and thanks to Chief's persistence, the subject, despite being severely corrupted by Mania, gained a sliver of hope for revival after the fete subsided. According to her, Chief embodies her purpose and hope for a new life, and she wishes to remain by Chief's side forever.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can use a wisteria flower staff to manifest a blooming wisteria tree. Its fragrance and branches can dominate the minds of her prey, draining their mental energy and consciousness to serve as *nutrients* that nourish her power.

**Awakening Signs**

In N.F. 01, the Perishing Star fell, triggering widespread disasters that struck a small town. Beneath the wisteria tree of this town, the subject endured both Mania contamination and physical devastation from the calamity. Overwhelmed by the power of the townspeople's wishes, she awakened her special power, becoming a Sinner and shouldering the survival desires of the entire town. From then on, she sustained her power and illusion by absorbing the Qi and consciousness of the living.

**Corruption State**

Severely corrupted, her physical body remains inseparable from the Mania contaminants.

### Tracking Records

**3**

After the wisteria Mania was defeated, the century-old fete illusion was dispelled. The Sinner's physical body, having suffered severe corruption, could not be detached from the Mania remains. After days of continuous searching, Chief finally was able to connect with her spiritually through a new sprout at the bottom of the ruins.

**3**

The subject's recovery has seen little to slow progress, requiring generous Qi transfers from Chief to maintain minimal improvement. Upon realizing this process would seriously endanger Chief's health, the subject has become very reluctant to consider her own revival, refusing further spiritual contact with Chief.

**3**

Recently, an Eastian delegation has visited the Bureau. Claiming to be descendants from the subject's homeland and having learned of her stalled recovery, they've proposed to bring her back to her origin to facilitate her recuperation. After thorough verification, it's confirmed that the delegation members have deep historical connections to the original location of the wisteria fete. The delegation has demonstrated their sincerity and offered multiple guarantees for the subject's well-being. Their proposal is currently being carefully considered.

**3**

Of her own volition, the subject has declined the Eastians' proposal to bring her back to her homeland, choosing instead to remain in DisCity under Chief and the Bureau's care. Currently, the Bureau has already agreed to provide the Eastian representatives with regular remote communication and convenient visitation channels, ensuring unobstructed contact between the two sides.

### Supervision Records

**4**

During the early days of detention, due to her extremely fragile condition, constant supervision was required. However, records indicated that the staff responsible for her care were significantly more likely to be late the next day. When questioned, the staff all claimed the cause was unknown, merely stating it was hard to get out of bed due to the pleasant dreams they recently experienced. Upon learning of this situation, Chief decided to personally take care of her in order not to jeopardize the Bureau's attendance rates.

**4**

Recently, Chief has received numerous reports of paranormal phenomena, including but not limited to unexplained floral fragrance and incidents of people being struck on the head by random falling wisteria blossoms. Some staff have even reported sightings of an indistinct figure humming foreign melodies on the trees of the Outdoor Activity Area. So far, Chief has not taken any measures regarding these reports, merely instructing everyone to remain calm and not be too concerned.

**4**

The subject is naturally drawn to lively environments. Having been stuck in the fete illusion for a century, she always maintains a profound interest and curiosity toward all things and people. She often listens attentively to other Sinners discussing various topics and enjoys the atmosphere of everyone gathering together to chat and laugh.

**4**

The subject has meticulously crafted two wisteria bracelets for Chief. One features perpetually blooming wisteria, symbolizing everlasting affection. The other remains dormant and silent, only unfurling when the subject is nearby. She claims that the fragrance will remind Chief of her, so Chief won't forget to meet her every day.
