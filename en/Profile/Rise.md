# Rise Profile

### Past Records

**1**

The subject studied at a prestigious Eastside academy and later interned at Nirvana. She is the sole survivor of the mysterious attack on Nirvana. Investigations reveal that during her internship, she had many conflicts with the victims and also exhibited violent tendencies, making her a suspect in this case. Given the case's severe social impact, she should be immediately detained and investigated.

**1**

Investigations show that the subject accumulated excessive stress from longtime workplace bullying, leading to her destructive outburst and awakening as a Sinner. Since being detained by the MBCC, various indicators show that her stress levels have gone down. She is currently behaving well and promoting reforms to the Bureau's work environment with the Chief's backing.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can extract data from herself or the internet to create weapons. The lightsaber is her most frequently used weapon, and can cause significant physical damage in a short period of time.

**Awakening Signs**

After repeatedly witnessing and experiencing workplace bullying, the subject lost control of her emotions from the accumulated stress and awakened her Sinner power in a fit of rage.

**Corruption State**

Her corruption level is mild, but when the subject experiences intense emotional fluctuations or activates her power, digitalized mutations appear on her skin.

### Tracking Records

**3**

After leaving Nirvana, the subject was invited by the Chief to join the MBCC. During her interview, she revealed her identity as a Sinner and was detained.

**3**

While interning at Nirvana, the subject demonstrated excellent work abilities. However, due to her pursuit of efficiency and unfiltered communication style, she often openly pointed out her superiors' mistakes, leading them to treat her badly in return.

**3**

The subject is exceptionally averse to exploitation and injustice at the workplace, leading to a buildup of unresolved pressure. She can only rely on playing squash to vent her anger and frustrations.

**3**

The sci-fi novel "Legend of the Star Realm" was the subject's favorite during her youth, and its protagonist Anniken greatly influenced her worldview. The image of Anniken wielding a lightsaber to fight evil and uphold justice left a profound impression on her. Thus, her power also manifests in the form of a "lightsaber."

### Supervision Records

**4**

After joining the MBCC, the subject demonstrated outstanding efficiency at work, completing tasks with astonishing speed and execution. However, she burns through the Bureau's budget by causing additional public damage from time to time. She says that she will try to avoid this in the future.

**4**

The subject has a strong sense of time and strictly adheres to her daily schedule. When the Bureau's time-reporting system was seven seconds out of sync, it was the ever-punctual Rise who was the first to notice.

**4**

The subject draws a clear line between her work and private life. While maintaining collegial relationships with most Sinners, she does not pursue excessive socialization outside of work. However, she does befriend Sinners sharing similar interests. For example, someone once saw her discussing the implementation of the "DisCity Labor Ordinance" with Mr. Fox.

**4**

The subject is dedicated to ensuring normal working hours for MBCC employees, and demands a corresponding increase in overtime pay. She has repeatedly suggested submitting a joint letter of protest to uphold everyone's legal rights. Rumor has it that the Chief was the first to sign this letter.
