### Past Records

**1**

The subject belongs to an unknown organization. While he was active in DisCity, he made contact with various Syndicate groups, including Rustfire and Legion. On official records, he was deceased during the BR-002 Incident in N.F. 113.
In N.F. 115, the subject resurfaced in Fraser City. Following contact with Margaret, he became embroiled in a diplomatic crisis. After triggering an anomalous Mania reaction within the catastrophe tear zone, he vanished.

**1**

Through interrogation and observation, it has been confirmed that "Rust" is the same individual as the operative from thirteen years ago. Both have actively engaged in negotiations with the Underground, working toward the exploration of the Black Rings and the pursuit of world peace. As he is currently shackled to Chief and has been assessed as posing no risk of erratic behavior, he has been permitted to continue gathering intelligence and conducting investigations outside the Bureau.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Anomalous Black Ring-like reactions have been detected within his body, allowing him to distort reality with Mania, reshaping and enhancing his physical form. When he "dies" from fatal injuries outside the Ring, his consciousness returns to the Black Ring. There, it seeks out a human whose mind has been destroyed by Mania and is on the verge of transforming into a Corruptor, yet whose body remains intact and unmutated. The subject's consciousness can then invade and replace the host's psyche, taking possession of the body.

### Tracking Records

**3**

He drew the Bureau's attention after triggering anomalous activity in the BR-005 ruins. Since the power sensed from him by the shackles matched records of a certain operative on file, Chief ordered the subject transferred to the Bureau for investigation.

**3**

Using biometric data as proof, he denied that he was the operative "Rust" who died thirteen years ago, and escaped the Bureau with assistance from unknown parties. He subsequently appeared near BR-002 in West District and reestablished contact with several old acquaintances in Rustfire.

**3**

After parting ways with Chief, he faced the Underground's pursuit alone. Information disclosed by the 9th Agency revealed that his anomalous corruption state and biometric changes were both linked to past experiments of the Underground. The subject had long since defected from the Underground and dedicated himself to the destruction of the Black Rings. However, he has since refused to pledge loyalty to any organization.

**3**

As a special operative, he has spent years navigating between various factions, including the Underground, Rustfire, and DisCity's authorities. His ever-changing body and the overwhelming weight of his past have made lasting relationships impossible. Though he yearns for genuine bonds, he has chosen to continue pursuing the remnants of the Black Rings. Nevertheless, he has expressed gratitude for Chief's care, vowing to protect this bond in his own way, until the day he truly dies.
