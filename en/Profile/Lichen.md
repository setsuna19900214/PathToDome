### Past Records

**1**

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Her weapon is the Pellucid Bell, which can set Mania "on fire," dispelling the target's disguise or any distortion caused by special power, thereby reaching the target's core to strike them or relieve them from agony. She can also summon physical, blood-red flames that effectively suppress Mania.

**Awakening Signs**

She appeared on the day of the full Illusory Moon, causing a dark, widespread anomaly in DisSea, as well as the unusual manifestation of the old Maria City. Subsequent investigation confirmed a strong correlation between the subject and the disturbances in that area. She was personally detained by the MBCC's Chief. Her body has been assessed to be exhibiting severe contamination, and her mental state is no different than that of a Corruptor.

**Corruption State**

Severely corrupted, with contaminants and crystals enveloping her entire body. She has lost all normal human cognition.

### Mind Restoration

**5**

After being detained during the Illusory Moon operation in DisSea, the subject has been placed in solitary confinement for observation and treatment. Due to the special nature of the Sinner, the MBCC has received the order for Chief to personally conduct dream dives with the subject.

**5**

Born Li Xianling in Eastia, the subject displayed strong spiritual sensitivity from a young age and was taken in as a disciple of the Tianyi Sect alongside her uncle, who was close to her in age. During her early studies of the Daoist arts, she demonstrated exceptional comprehension and practical abilities, while her rebellious tendencies and desire for independent exploration were also apparent.

**5**

Following the outbreak of the disasters, her master disappeared at an observatory. The Tianyi Sect experienced a resurgence due to rumors of this "Banished Immortal" who had prophesied the disasters. She continued to search for her master's whereabouts by conducting divination and investigations. Upon reaching adulthood, she left the sect of her own accord, traveling throughout Eastia to verify sightings of the Banished Immortal and gather information about the Daoist arts and about the disasters.

**5**

Around N.F. 21, many people in Eastia began seeing the Banished Immortal in their dreams, including the subject and her uncle. She exhausted her family fortune and connections to investigate her master's whereabouts and the "gateway to the immortal realm," which turned out to be where the Perishing Star had landed. She then joined the Expedition alongside her uncle.
