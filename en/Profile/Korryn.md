### Past Records

**1**

An elusive bounty hunter in pursuit of high commissions, running at large around the WhiteSands trade route. The subject lingers across various factions and rarely initiates any moves, employing secretive methods to complete her jobs.
Investigation reveals her involvement in multiple recent incidents related to the trade route. The subject poses a severe threat to WhiteSands' Hypercube transportation and requires immediate detention.

**1**

Investigation reveals that the subject and Tuco residents' previous actions were driven by survival instinct. Following their cooperation with the MBCC, Tuco residents can now obtain reasonably priced supplies through the Bureau and will no longer disrupt the trade route's security.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Able to infuse weapons with Mania to deal massive damage. Due to her style of acting, her special power produces greater effect on firearms.

**Awakening Signs**

According to the subject's testimony, she was caught in the Tide of Ashes during a mission. To survive, the subject chose to deliberately expose herself to Mania, awakening as a Sinner.

**Corruption State**

The subject's corruption level is moderate. She exhibits heightened mental agitation when using her special power, with visible Mania patterns on her body. Nevertheless, the subject remains sane and her ability is still within control.

### Tracking Records

**3**

Having entered DisCity covertly from WhiteSands, the subject deliberately revealed herself near the MBCC, proposing a deal to Chief as a bounty hunter.

**3**

The subject's exact birthplace is unknown. She was adopted by Tuco's townspeople as a child and raised on Tuco's nomadic ship. Later, the subject became a bounty hunter, working with accomplices on bounty contracts to obtain survival supplies.

**3**

To survive, Tuco's townspeople must regularly relocate their base to avoid sandstorms and pursuers. During one such evacuation, the subject was caught in the Tide of Ashes while aiding her companions' retreat. 32 hours later, she was found severely wounded in the desert three kilometers away, heavily corrupted by Mania and awakened as a Sinner.

**3**

Investigation reveals that following the West District BR-002 incident, anomalies in WhiteSands intensified and disasters became frequent. Aware of these changes, to protect her Tuco companions, the subject chose to journey alone to DisCity to seek out a way to ensure Tuco's survival.
