### Past Records

**1**

The subject works at the Eastside Public Security Bureau's information desk. She became a Sinner after contracting Mania while on duty. On the way to turn herself in to the Bureau, she pulled Chief and multiple Sinners into an unknown dimension.
According to her testimony, her special power triggers persistent misfortune affecting herself and those around her. This power is uncontrollable, and its magnitude fluctuates observably according to the subject's emotional state. Immediate detention is recommended.

**1**

Follow-up investigation confirmed that the subject inadvertently helped the Bureau avert a potential Mania crisis. With proper control, her ability's impact can be minimized. Currently, she has returned to normal social life and is only required to undergo routine examinations at the Bureau.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's special power originates from her consciousness. Her words, thoughts, and actions all subconsciously stimulate the generation of Mania energy, which then affects her surroundings. Her ability alters events in reality, producing relatively unfavorable outcomes. Overall, this manifests as what people commonly refer to as bad luck.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject works at the Public Security Bureau's information desk and has handled cases involving the Black Rings and Mania. She later became infected with Mania after exposure to a contamination source, thus becoming a Sinner.

**Corruption State**

She exhibits mild corruption with no visible physical marks. Her mental state remains generally sound, though Mania interferes with her rational thinking during critical moments.
