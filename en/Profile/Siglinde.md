### Past Records

**1**

The subject is a senior wartime liaison officer from DisCorps, with impressive abilities in communication technology and intelligence management. She is primarily responsible for liaising on intel and tactics between the FAC and DisCorps, serving as the link between the two parties.

**1**

When she first joined the MBCC, despite facing considerable skepticism, she voluntarily accepted the shackles due to tactical considerations, maintaining a cooperative attitude. Under Chief's supervision, she has displayed no suspicious behavior during her collaboration with the FAC. Her high efficiency and excellent task completion have gradually earned her internal trust, so special surveillance is no longer necessary.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can utilize a mobile transmitter to generate an electromagnetic barrier field around herself and emit pulse radiation, interfering with enemies' telecommunications signals, electronic equipment, and neural feedback, causing intense adverse impact.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject inherited a Rancor that originally belonged to her family member, awakening her special power while taming and mastering the weapon.

**Corruption State**

She displays no obvious physical abnormalities, but given that she's good at pretense and mentally stable, it is impossible to determine the extent of Mania contamination affecting her mind. The only proof of her Mania corruption is her frenzied state during combat.

### Tracking Records

**3**

A senior wartime liaison officer dispatched by DisCorps. Due to her special status, arrangements were made for her to be personally supervised by the MBCC Chief.

**3**

The subject was born into a military family, with both parents serving as frontline liaison officers for DisCorps. Although they were often separated, as a child, she deeply admired the adults in her family. However, during the Outlands war, her parents both perished in battle and were posthumously awarded the highest honors by DisCorps.

**3**

Out of admiration for her parents, she voluntarily joined the youth training camp of DisCorps that nurtured future officers, undergoing high-intensity military training, tactical courses, and political education. She not only inherited the Rancor from her family but also chose to become a liaison officer, much to the apprehension of her instructors, who saw great potential in her combat abilities. Nevertheless, she remained firmly determined to follow in her parents' footsteps.

**3**

The subject admits that she once adopted the belief of the conservative faction of DisCorps, valuing victory and conquest above all and disregarding concession as weakness. Therefore, she once thought that the FAC was unworthy of military honor, as they blindly submitted to Paradeisos's authority. However, after fighting alongside the FAC, she recognized her own narrow-mindedness, and the DisCorps ideology instilled in her since childhood began to waver, leading her to decide to make changes.

### Supervision Records

**4**
