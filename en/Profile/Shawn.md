### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

By transmitting sound waves through the device, she can seize *affection* from specific individuals, leaving them in severe despair and depression, and even rendering them completely dysfunctional in extreme cases. The subject will absorb the extracted *affection* and receive a boost to her mood and power.

**Awakening Signs**

After being, as she claims, betrayed by someone she loved, the subject attempted revenge, only to be ridiculed and cursed. Upon realizing the love had always been one-sided, her special power awakened.

**Corruption State**

The subject shows only mild signs of corruption and no physical abnormalities. However, having stayed mostly at home and devoted almost all of her energy to cultivating her online relationships, her mental state is poor and requires close monitoring.

Coming from a middle-class family in Eastside, she befriended a female neighbor when she was young. As adults, they trained together as voice actors and participated in a voice acting audition. The subject's charming voice and appearance won her a decent place in the audition.
