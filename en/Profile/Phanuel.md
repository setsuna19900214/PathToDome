### Past Records

**1**

The subject is Eastside's latest viral sensation, a multi-talented idol with a flawless appearance and a sweet, innocent image. With average singing, dance, and acting skills, as well as a mediocre fashion sense, she lacks any standout specialties. However, she is highly active on social media and adept at maintaining her popularity through fan interactions.
In reality, the subject is a Sinner with a special ability. Her abnormal popularity is directly linked to her mind-influencing power. Given the far-reaching potential of her ability and the threat it poses to society, immediate detention is recommended.

**1**

Investigation confirms that the subject was simply carrying out her idol activities as arranged by her agency and was unaware of any ulterior motives. Due to the lack of further evidence and the risk that the subject might be silenced or exploited again, the Bureau has decided to place her under long-term detention for continued observation.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Her smile induces a dreamlike euphoria in those who witness it, causing them to experience either extreme excitement or utter peace while developing an abnormal affinity for and obsession toward her.

**Awakening Signs**

Following a significant traumatic event, the subject's special power awoke under acute stress, garnering a great deal of attention and sympathy with her innocent, disarming smile.

**Corruption State**

While no significant physical abnormalities have been detected, the subject exhibits paranoid tendencies and an unusually high need for attachment, attention, and adoration. She dreams frequently, and her dreams invariably center on the happiness derived from being worshipped with fervent devotion.
