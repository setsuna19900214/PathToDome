### Past Records

**1**

The subject is an analyst in the Public Security Bureau's Forensics Division, known for her calm, rigorous, and almost ruthless professional standards. While assigned to the Bureau to provide assistance, she was transported to an unknown dimension.
She discovered key clues in the unknown dimension. Further questioning and investigation are recommended.

**1**

The subject's past records reveal that her special power has not caused actual harm to public safety. The analytical model she developed was deployed in combating criminal activity but was later suspended due to an unforeseen incident. Related reports protected the institution's overall interests but concealed the truth, putting undeserved blame on the subject and her work.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can perceive the physical properties of her targets, allowing her to identify their weaknesses. In her eyes, the world is composed of countless constantly changing parameters and formulas.

**Awakening Signs**

She has been in contact with or exposed to a Mania-contaminated environment. During her university years, she was confronted with uncontrolled emotional outbursts and chaotic sensory stimulation. Amid extreme helplessness and cognitive overload, she perceived the anomaly, awakening as a Sinner.

**Corruption State**

She exhibits mild corruption with no visible physical abnormalities. Her mental state remains stable under most circumstances, but the stability fluctuates abnormally when she is confronted with events that are beyond her control or logical comprehension.

### Tracking Records

**3**

Following the incident's conclusion, she stayed at the Bureau, where she continued to work and fully cooperated with the investigation. Upon assessment, her emotional state was found to be stable, posing no security threat. As she was already a registered Sinner when she first joined the Public Security Bureau, she soon left of her own accord after updating her information.

**3**

She was brought up within a cognitive framework built around absolute rationality. Within her worldview, reality operates according to strict, immutable causality. Order and reason constitute her unshakable creed. Her sudden visit to the Bureau was precisely due to her prediction of a potential threat that had not yet appeared but posed extreme danger.

**3**

The turning point in her life occurred during her university years. A friend whose personality was the antithesis of her own caused the order and rationality she had always upheld to waver. However, this clumsy yet genuine friendship ultimately met a cruel end, catalyzing her awakening as a Sinner.

**3**

The incident during her university years inspired her to develop a model for predicting social behavior. This garnered widespread attention and led to her recruitment by the Public Security Bureau. However, the project was eventually forced to shut down due to certain individuals' intervention. She was subsequently transferred from her core position to the Forensics Division.

### Supervision Records

**4**

She has rather harsh standards for the cleanliness of the surrounding environment. All items within her sight must be kept highly organized. During her time at the Bureau, she repeatedly took the initiative to organize personal items for Chief and other Sinners, treating it as an activity to relieve stress.

**4**

She is a very serious person who never jokes or smiles. She once attempted to chime in on a casual chat among Sinners, but turned the conversation awkward as she could not understand their humor. Subsequently, she conducted systematic, quantitative research on the subject of humor, but it yielded no substantial progress.

**4**

She has an excellent gift for puzzle-solving. She plays many video games of this genre and writes guides for them. She also shows considerable interest in the escape room experiences popular in Eastside. However, due to her unusually strong logical thinking and abnormal puzzle-solving speed, she has rated most such experiences as lacking in logic and challenge.

**4**
