### Past Records

**1**

An orphan from the farlands, the subject was raised by residents of a refugee camp. In search of a cure for her illness, she frequently infiltrated Fraser Aurum Secretum Research Institute by posing as a janitor.
Investigation reveals the subject may be directly involved in the Fraser Aurum Secretum Research Institute incident. Further research is recommended.

**1**

The subject was involved in the Aurum Secretum Research Institute incident in an attempt to save the refugee camp residents and thwart Fraser's plans. Otherwise, she has never been accused of using her abilities to harm others. Currently, she resides at the Bureau as a refugee Sinner and has adjusted well to the new environment. She regularly visits her former family at the DisCity Orphanage.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's five senses have been significantly enhanced under the influence of Aurum Secretum, but not without side effects. She exhibits abnormal sensitivity to external stimuli, which has been alleviated by the shackles. After being treated, she is now able to communicate telepathically with Chief within a certain range.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject's special power awakened after she was directly exposed to leaked Aurum Secretum radiation during a transport accident.

**Corruption State**

The subject displays severe corruption. Though she remains lucid most of the time, Aurum Secretum marks are visible on her skin.

### Tracking Records

**3**

She was rescued by MBCC operatives when being attacked in WhiteSands. After her identity as a Sinner and infection status were confirmed, she was detained and placed under the Bureau's supervision.

**3**

She grew up in a refugee camp outside the Fraser Institute. She was involved in the Aurum Secretum Research Institute incident, and witnessed the entire BR-005 explosion incident. Following these events, as a key witness, she was persistently hunted by Fraser forces and was thus in exile through WhiteSands with other surviving children from the refugee camp.

**3**

After the awakening of her power, she was recruited by the Aurum Secretum Research Institute. Not wanting to be separated from her family in the refugee camp, she later refused the offer and escaped from the facility. It was speculated that the subject may have refused the shackles and escaped from the Bureau for the same reason, returning to WhiteSands.

**3**

Due to her early life experiences, she viewed the refugee camp residents as her most precious family. After witnessing their deaths in the Black Ring, she developed a profound aversion and fear toward separation. Following the resolution of the crisis, she gradually began to heal from her trauma, accepted the shackles, and returned to the Bureau with Chief.

### Supervision Records

**4**

She is skilled at various janitorial tasks and shows exceptional enthusiasm for such work. Despite the Bureau being equipped with housekeeping robots, she insists on providing cleaning services. However, her carelessness often causes her to overclean and thus results in troublesome consequences.

**4**

She is passionate about research and invention, dabbling in both physics and chemistry. However, she seems to lack the required technical skills, so her creations typically fail to function or, in worse cases, spontaneously explode. She frequently seeks help from Summer and Eureka to improve her invention capabilities.

**4**

She has an optimistic, cheerful personality and enjoys helping others. Since arriving at the Bureau, she has come to view the Sinners here as her family, frequently aiding those in need. She is particularly good at caring for younger Sinners, and often plays hide-and-seek with them.

**4**

She can communicate telepathically with Chief within a certain range, sharing sensory experiences and temporarily accessing portions of each other's memories. As a result, Chief often becomes immediately aware of the various embarrassing situations the subject encounters in daily life.
