### Past Records

**1**

The second of the Kisugi sisters, a beauty with a lively and cheerful personality. By day, she runs the Cat's Eye Café. At night, she's an art thief. As a skilled art thief, she is the one responsible for executing operations among the three sisters. An agile and courageous soul, nimble and full of stamina—she always steps up to protect others.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

This ability allows the subject's body to be lighter and more agile. She can easily move in ways that were impossible before her Awakening. It also enables her bones and muscles to develop protective mechanisms similar to those of animals, allowing the subject to survive falls from great heights without mortal danger.
