### Past Records

**1**

A young, streetwise real estate agent with extensive professional experience who has built a reputation in Eastside for specializing in selling cheap haunted properties.
Investigation confirms that all residential properties handled by the subject have been deliberately cleaned up and contain unusual residue of Mania, suggesting extended presence of Corruptors. Currently, the subject stands accused of harboring Corruptors and is under serious suspicion of spreading Mania. Immediate detention and thorough investigation are recommended.

**1**

Further investigation confirms that the suspect harbored Corruptors solely to fabricate supernatural phenomena in these haunted properties, with no intention of harming others. During her detention, under Chief's guidance, she has gradually let go of her obsession with houses and the concept of home, reflected on her past actions, and is now willing to consider the Bureau as somewhere she could return to. Following a risk reassessment, she has been granted parole and routinely returns to the Bureau for a short stay to report her updates.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can imprison specific targets with her weapon, forbidding them from moving or attacking, causing them to gradually lose their strength.

**Awakening Signs**

On a stormy night, wandering Corruptors accidentally invaded the subject's dilapidated dwelling. Under attack and on the verge of dying, her resolve to defend her home triggered the awakening of her ability, allowing her to imprison Corruptors.

**Corruption State**

The subject displays only mild corruption with no visible physical abnormalities. She remains lucid most of the time and acts with clear purposes.

### Tracking Records

**3**

Following the Morveth Castle incident, after her identity as a Sinner was exposed, the subject followed Chief back to the Bureau to register her credentials and undergo relevant investigation. Throughout the process, she showed no resistance and remained compliant, but responded evasively when questioned about certain key information.

**3**

The subject is from Eastside. Her family struggled financially when she was young and she had to lie about her age to support herself, thus accumulating extensive experience in agency work from an early age. She claims that selling haunted properties is a way to make money, and in doing so she gets to fulfill the demand of a niche market while attracting thrill-seeking customers interested in touring such locations.

**3**

The subject lost both parents at an early age and was raised by her grandmother. Their only home was fraudulently seized and demolished by an unscrupulous real estate developer without any compensation, leaving her family homeless. Unable to afford Eastside's rising housing costs, she was forced to relocate frequently, constantly moving homes. The nomadic lifestyle proved too burdensome for her elderly grandmother, who eventually fell ill from exhaustion and passed away.

**3**

The experience of losing her childhood home and the death of her only family induced in the subject an unusual obsession with houses. In order to help others in Eastside in similar situations buy their own homes, she exploits the properties of Corruptors to fabricate supernatural phenomena, driving down the prices of these artificial *haunted houses*. When necessary, she would even go as far as harming herself to reach her goal.
