### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject possesses the ability to sense and manipulate calories. She can detect and regulate the thermal state of food within a certain range, and can instantly detonate the internal energy of food items. Therefore, the subject typically employs food as her weapons.

**Awakening Signs**

Her special power awakened when she was forgotten by a film crew at a high M-value shooting location near the edge of the Rust. Her power manifested due to her hunger and fear.

**Corruption State**

She displays mild corruption with minor physical mutations. She remains lucid most of the time, but will lose control when experiencing extreme hunger.
