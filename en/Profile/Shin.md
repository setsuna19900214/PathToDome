### Past Records

**1**

The subject was an oceanographer in the Black Ring Expedition's Deep Dive unit, as well as the de facto leader of this unit. Besides her scientific work, due to her extensive maritime experience, she also served as the naval surgeon and handled other responsibilities.
According to reports, the subject was one of the original initiators of the Black Ring Expedition, and the entire Deep Dive unit consisted almost only of her followers she had met during her earlier scientific explorations and academic career.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

When the tentacles controlled by the subject come into contact with an organic life form, its Mania level will surge explosively and all special powers will be enhanced. But at the same time, the body of the organism in contact will undergo rapid decay and soon lose its own consciousness, becoming an abyssal creature.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject appeared on the day of the full Illusory Moon, causing an abnormally high tide in the DisSea area. She was personally detained by the MBCC's Chief. Her body was wrapped in numerous fleshy tentacles and severely corrupted by contamination, but she was still capable of communication.

**Corruption State**

The level of corruption is extremely high, with her body structure now almost completely different from that of a human, but the degree of visible mutation on the surface is not severe. Her rationality is still intact, and her communication abilities even exceed the average person.

### Mind Restoration

**5**

After being detained during the Illusory Moon operation in DisSea, the subject has been placed in solitary confinement for observation and treatment. Due to the special nature of the Sinner, the MBCC has received the order for Chief to personally conduct dream dives with the subject.

**5**

The subject was born into an academic family before Nightfall. Both of her parents were university lecturers and many of her relatives were well-known oceanographers. Under her family's *nurture*, she quickly grew to become a *rising star* in the field of oceanography. She was assigned to conduct research on exploration vessels before she was even an adult.

**5**

During an oceanographic expedition, the crew fell victim to natural disasters and extreme environmental conditions. This led to panic and conflict within the expedition team, ultimately resulting in a horrifying tragedy. The subject was the only survivor of this expedition.

**5**

As the sole survivor of the expedition, the subject received attention from academic circles upon her return to society. She was quickly appointed as a professor at a prestigious institute. In the following years, she began preparing for expeditions into restricted deep-sea areas, eventually becoming one of the initial founders of the Black Ring Expedition.
