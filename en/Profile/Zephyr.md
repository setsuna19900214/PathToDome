### Past Records

**1**

The subject used her special power in a premeditated attempt to take control of another aircraft, and was subdued and arrested during her act.
Further investigation confirms the subject is from Eastside and formerly served as a pilot, but was forced to retire after causing severe disruption during her first test flight. Given the dangerous nature of her past actions, immediate detention is recommended.

**1**

Interrogation confirms the flight radio interference incident was the result of a Mania-induced outburst of her special power rather than an intentional act. Aviation means a lot to her, and even during her time in the MBCC, she continued seeking opportunities to pilot an aircraft again. Following an attempted aerial escape, she reached an agreement with Chief to comply with the Bureau's bail conditions in exchange for restricted freedom.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can interfere with signal transmission using a wireless pager as a medium. In combat situations, she can manipulate intercepted signals to influence physical targets and neural pathways, suppressing pain and distracting enemies.

**Awakening Signs**

Triggered by the Eastside Mania incident, her special power awakened during a test flight. Her subsequent loss of control disrupted the air traffic control system and communication equipment, resulting in a helicopter crash.

**Corruption State**

The subject exhibits mild corruption with no visible physical abnormalities. Her mental state fluctuates occasionally, often manifesting in sudden energy bursts.

### Tracking Records

**3**

Initial contact between the subject and Chief occurred by chance at an Eastside bar. After confirmation of her Sinner identity and her intent to hijack an aircraft, Chief successfully apprehended her at the scene with the sheriffs' help. She was then transferred to the MBCC for detention.

**3**

The subject completed her formal aviation training and obtained the official pilot certification in Eastside. Her exposure to Mania resulted in the flight radio interference incident. The subject suffered injuries from the crash and was then forced to retire after only one test flight. Despite not being qualified to pilot an aircraft at the moment, she remains obsessed with flying.

**3**

The subject's parents have divorced and since established new families. She gets along with her parents' new partners and grew up alternating between homes, giving her more diverse life experiences than her peers. Raised in this open-minded family environment, she's grown up to become a spontaneous, free-spirited individual.

**3**

Early on in the subject's pilot career, she did not fully understand what flying meant to her. When her plane crashed during her first test flight, as she plummeted through the sky, she finally felt the true meaning of freedom. Since then, she has continuously sought ways to heal her old injuries and another chance to fly a plane.
