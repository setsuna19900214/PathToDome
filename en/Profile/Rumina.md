# Rumina Profile

### Basic Information

Name: Rumina

Gender: Female

DOB: June 26

Height: 148 cm

Date of Detainment:

Case: Syndicate Wax Factory Fire Case

### Past Records

**1**

The subject is the figure from the "Hot Spring Inn" urban legend that once went viral online. She currently works at Nightwrought and plays the role of a hot spring inn attendant.
Investigation reveals the subject's connection with the fire at a Syndicate wax factory—the place where the "Hot Spring Inn" urban legend happened. While there were no casualties at the scene, large amounts of Mania residue were detected, necessitating further investigation into her.

**1**

Following her interrogation, it was confirmed that the subject didn't cause the Syndicate wax factory fire on purpose, but simply lost control while using her power for the first time. Through Chief's guidance, the subject has successfully overcome her notion of a "safe house," which hindered her from connecting with the world. Despite her timidity, she is making diligent efforts to live and learn like a normal girl.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can instantly encase targets in boiling wax, transforming them into wax figure-like objects. This silences targets and renders them immobile while melting them internally.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject witnessed a family member's death in a wax pool at a Syndicate wax factory. During this incident, she herself also fell victim to brutal violence, leading to the awakening of her ability under the emotional breakdown and extreme fear.

**Corruption State**

Corruption level is severe. Her body displays some cracks from the mutation. Her consciousness sometimes turns into a muddle, and she confuses past events with present reality when her mind collapses.

### Tracking Records

**3**

After the Witchwoven Folio incident, the subject expressed her inclination to enter the Bureau for personal reasons. After learning of this through Irrheia, the Bureau brought her back for registration procedures, and related investigation was conducted on her.

**3**

The real world often feels strange to her. When she first joined the Bureau, she repeatedly caused small "accidents." She has carried Mania since a young age and has always been frail. Growing up poor, she lived in a crammed "safe house." All this resulted in her underdeveloped cognition. Her parents both died when she was young, and she was raised by her elder brother, on whom she was extremely dependent, though she has since lost most memories of him.

**3**

Being each other's only family, the subject and her brother shared a deep bond. Her brother took loans from gangs to save his sister's life. Unable to repay the enormous debt, he was cruelly pushed into an industrial wax pool and died tragically. The subject also awakened as a Sinner during this process. Her body mutated and her mind was damaged, yet she refused to leave the wax factory, remaining there until the "Hot Spring Inn" urban legend spread.

**3**

Her traumatic childhood caused the subject to develop an extreme obsession with owning a "safe house." Unable to distinguish her own emotions and desperate to protect this hard-won safe space, she will regard all intruders as bad people and punish them, while simultaneously trapping those already inside and refusing to let them leave.

### Supervision Records

**4**

The subject always stays in her room in the Bureau, singing broken nursery rhymes. Investigation reveals that she simply enjoys doing housework while practicing songs from new scripts by Nightwrought. Many fans in the Bureau have since taken to predicting the content of their next big project based on the new songs she practices.

**4**

While learning home organization from fellow Sinner Mira, the subject expressed disappointment over the lack of hot spring baths in the Bureau. Requested by Mira and other hot spring enthusiasts, the Bureau then installed several hot spring baths in the logistics zone, including a medicinal bath, a sulfur bath, and a rose bath, for the subject and others to enjoy and relax in.

**4**

The subject enjoys cooking and frequently invites fellow Sinners of similar age to sample her dishes. These Sinners always quit the cafeteria with grimacing faces. Foodies such as Poffy and Cabernet have since provided considerable help. The subject is now able to make edible food, and says she will keep working hard to improve her skills.

**4**

The subject frequently secretly keeps track of Chief's whereabouts, watching Chief closely while hoping Chief can reciprocate with the same level of attention. With a lot of time and patience, Chief has now become quite adept at improvising picture book stories.
