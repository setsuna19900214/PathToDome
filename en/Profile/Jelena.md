### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's special power, Mania Rain, utilizes resonance from the Mania Crystals in her hand chain to manipulate a special metal. Her power is relatively weak and has only one effect. Her power seems underdeveloped at the moment, but not excluding the potential for greater capabilities.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject claims that her power awakened during a Mania attack in Eastside. Since then, her M-value has consistently remained at low levels, and she demonstrates no other abnormalities than her ability to manipulate the special metal.

**Corruption State**

The subject has no visible signs of corruption present on her body and her mental state appears stable. However, her emotional responses have been found unusually subdued compared to the average person.

**Ability Summary**

The subject's special power, Mania Rain, utilizes the Mania Crystals in her hand chain to manipulate mist, creating a rain-like phenomenon. Those affected by it will receive an enhancement of their special powers, but in return, both their bodies and minds will fall under the subject's control.

**Awakening Signs**

During the encounter with an Apostle of Ashes in her Outland hometown as a child, the subject became infected with Mania and awakened as a Sinner. Later, under ■■■■'s guidance, she further enhanced her power with Ash Crystals and evolved into an S-Class Sinner.
