### Past Records

**1**

The subject is a lighthouse keeper at an abandoned border watch outpost on the WhiteSands border, and the sole survivor at Lighthouse OT-N07.
Due to prolonged isolation and exposure to Rancors, she exhibits highly aggressive behavior and indiscriminate hostility. She is highly prone to violence and going rogue, and has lost the normal perception of time and space due to the Black Tide's influence, therefore posing a clear threat to the city's security. Detention under maximum security is recommended.

**1**

Current evaluation indicates she has developed complete immunity to the Rancor's mental corruption, capable of suppressing her own bloodthirst and even infusing her own consciousness back into the weapon. While her character remains cold and volatile, she has not displayed unprovoked aggression. It is worth noting that the subject displays an exceptionally high sense of justice and duty, demonstrating the resolve to protect non-combatants on the frontlines and thus should be considered a powerful and reliable asset in combat.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

She possesses extraordinary survival instincts, which unconsciously activate in desperate situations, intensifying her battle willpower and transferring it to her Rancor dagger. Through the combined effect of her strong will and the Rancor's bloodthirst, she can manipulate storms, wielding a powerful field that diminishes the enemy's morale.

**Awakening Signs**

Born on the WhiteSands battlefield, she miraculously survived a Mania-contaminated environment while still an infant. Her Sinner abilities were awakened by her infantile survival instinct, while her innate resistance to contamination granted her exceptional compatibility with the Rancors.

**Corruption State**

The Rancor has inflicted severe mental corruption on her. While showing no physical symptoms, she exhibits serious pessimistic and paranoid tendencies and uncontrollable violent impulses. When she loses control, she also displays extreme self-destructive tendencies.

### Tracking Records

**3**

During the WhiteSands Black Tide incident, she inadvertently rescued Chief. Under the extreme circumstances, they established a brief partnership. Following the incident's resolution, the subject was officially relieved of her lighthouse keeper duties and departed from her original station.

**3**

During its prime, DisCorps established a network of border watch lighthouses along the WhiteSands border, equipped with the Rancors. The subject was originally an orphan from the WhiteSands battlefield taken in by Lighthouse Seven. Denied official acceptance by DisCity authorities due to unverifiable identity, she was instead raised by the keepers of Lighthouse Seven. Later, the subject voluntarily joined the lighthouse keepers and completed Rancor training.

**3**

Records indicate that the nuclear contamination at the ground zero of Operation Skyfall mixed with Mania to form the Black Tide. This contamination spread rapidly, destroying all border watch lighthouses. DisCity never responded to the distress signals. Surviving lighthouse keepers rallied at Lighthouse Seven, but all soon perished due to resource shortage, Rancor corruption, and battles with Tide Crawlers. The subject was the sole survivor.

**3**

As the sole survivor, she continued to send distress signals to DisCity as ordered. Due to the spatiotemporal distortions caused by the Black Tide, these signals were never transmitted successfully. When supplies were exhausted, she resorted to consuming Black Tide monsters for sustenance. During this period, she attempted suicide multiple times, but the Rancor intervened in her consciousness, forcing her to live on. Only upon contact with Chief did she finally receive effective assistance from DisCity, ultimately leading to the elimination of the Black Tide contamination.

**4**

**4**

She can accurately predict the weather, repeatedly outperforming official weather forecast systems. Since she also has extensive outdoor survival knowledge, the Bureau has suggested she conduct training sessions, but she has refused every time. Only when Chief made the request personally did she finally compromise, agreeing to answer questions privately but refusing to give lectures.

**4**

After Chief pleaded for mercy, she relented but seemed obviously disappointed. Much later, she finally revealed that her initial motivation was to ensure Chief's survival.
