### Past Records

**1**

The subject is a bestselling author from Eastside, specializing in crime mystery novels. Her works have garnered much attention due to their uncanny parallels to reality. Details of the crimes depicted in her writing are strikingly similar to multiple unsolved murder cases in Eastside, suggesting a direct connection between her and said incidents.
Following an anonymous tip and subsequent investigation, it can be determined that the subject excels at concealing both her trails and true identity, being able to adopt multiple personas depending on the situation. The subject appears to have a clear target for every action and may manipulate specific individuals if need be. Given that the subject possesses potential psychic interference capabilities, immediate detention and isolated interrogation are recommended.

**1**

Following interrogation and cross-referencing, the subject is confirmed to be a survivor of the Redwood Orphanage arson case. She personally witnessed the judiciary's corruption in the hands of those in power and the silencing of her loved ones. The subject herself also faced continuous threats during her pursuit of the truth. Her prolonged covert investigations and utilization of literature as a method to expose the truth stem from extreme distrust in systemic failures, showing no tendencies to cause indiscriminate harm.
The subject maintains her hostility toward power structures but retains lingering hope in those who execute justice, demonstrating potential for cooperation and controllability under the restraint of shackles. Continued observation under strict supervision and appropriate guidance is recommended.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can connect with other living beings through Mania, probing their deepest desires and interfering with their psyche. While the ability is active, bite mark-shaped corruption marks appear on the target's body, gradually fading over time.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject awakened as a Sinner in her early years. The catalyst for her awakening remains unclear. She claims to have been infected during field research, though the veracity of this statement is yet to be verified.

**Corruption State**

The subject displays mild corruption, but notable mutations are present in the ears and oral cavity. Activation of her ability triggers severe neuropathic headaches, requiring medication for relief. Chronic sleep disorders and her dependence on pharmaceuticals have formed a vicious cycle, placing her physiological functions at risk of potential collapse.

### Tracking Records

**3**

The subject came to MBCC's attention following an anonymous tip and was initially cooperative during the investigation. Following the "Murder on the Praeva Express" serial event, the subject disappeared. Subsequently, multiple bizarre cases occurred in Eastside. Evidence at the crime scenes suggests the subject is the potential mastermind.

**3**

The subject was raised at the Redwood Orphanage in DisCity, where she had lived since childhood, and she cared for younger children there. Gifted in storytelling and writing, she had once planned to study literature at Eastside University. In N.F. 105, the Redwood Orphanage caught fire during the night, claiming multiple lives. The subject became one of the few survivors, having been away at the time of the incident.

**3**

In N.F. 107, the survivors of the Redwood fire resolved to seek vengeance. Inspired by a certain novel from before Nightfall, they decided to execute their plan aboard the Praeva sand ship. Follow-up investigations revealed that the Praeva lost contact 27 hours after departure. All passengers were reported missing and presumed dead. The subject, as an orphan of the Redwood Orphanage, did not board the vessel but likewise vanished without a trace, only to resurface in N.F. 116 as a novelist.

**3**

Investigations reveal that all characters appearing in "Murder on the Praeva Express" were based on real individuals. Records pertaining to those connected with the Redwood fire had been extensively altered and deleted. By embedding genuine clues to the Redwood case within her work, the subject's true aim was to spark public discourse and draw out the arsonist.

### Supervision Records

**4**

Due to severe insomnia, the subject is frequently active at night. She has a particular fondness for moongazing and stargazing. She once attempted to modify her Indoor Artificial Sunlight System to include a moonlight mode, but the modification failed, resulting in persistently erratic lighting. When questioned, the subject's explanation was, "I majored in humanities. This sort of work isn't my forte."

**4**

When other Sinners request her to tell them a story, the subject typically obliges without hesitation. However, complaint tickets reveal that her stories invariably contain intricate criminal methodologies, alibi construction techniques, and graphic details on corpse disposal. Multiple individuals have developed a fear of being alone at night as a result, forcing the Bureau to reorganize room assignments.

**4**

The subject has continued writing even while detained, frequently interviewing fellow Sinners about their lives to gather material for her novels. However, as she tends to immortalize those she admires as victims in her stories, some Sinners are visibly hesitant when approached for interviews, asking, "Do I gotta die too...?"

**4**

The subject claims her next work will be modeled after Chief. To this end, she frequently requests private meetings under the pretense of conducting in-depth research, with sessions running far too long to be standard interviews. She would also leave behind notes from their conversations on Chief's desk, with a wine-scented bookmark tucked inside, marking the story between her and Chief as "to be continued."
