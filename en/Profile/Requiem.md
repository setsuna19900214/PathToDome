### Past Records

**1**

The subject is an internal member of the Underground faction. Her specific duties are unknown, but she is referred to as a "virtuoso" by other Underground members, and is closely tied to the Mania musical scores circulating in Eastside.
Considering the extremely dangerous nature of the Underground's Cursed Melody, and that the subject may be the performer, immediate detainment is required to contain the scope of impact.

**1**

According to investigations, the subject did not voluntarily join the Underground, but was forcibly brought in by lower-tier members. She herself has no understanding of the organization's various misdeeds and holds no intention to harm others. After deepening her understanding with Chief, the subject has even sent signals expressing hope for communication and reconciliation between both sides. Following Chief's assessment, a decision was made to detain her for long-term observation.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The music performed by the subject can influence the emotions of listeners, producing a phenomenon of consciousness attunement that transcends numerous physical barriers. The triggering conditions and accompanying effects are highly complex and require continued observation as well as further research.

**Awakening Signs**

The trigger for the subject's awakening remains unclear and difficult to trace. It is speculated that it may be related to the Underground faction and its Cursed Melody.

**Corruption State**

The subject's consciousness is affected by Mania, resulting in blurred self-identity and overlapping memories due to excessive empathic resonance, along with a high degree of psychological dissociation. While she retains her rationality and ability to communicate, the intense consciousness attunement has caused abnormal rejection of bodily tissues, drastically shortening her lifespan.

### Tracking Records

**3**

In August, N.F. 116, the Underground's Cursed Melody began circulating in Eastside. The MBCC Chief led a team to infiltrate a secret gathering of the Sillage Society, successfully detaining the performer of the Cursed Melody, virtuoso Requiem.

**3**

The subject formerly went by the name "Cecilia," hailing from a musical family in Eastside. She began learning the violin at a young age and quickly demonstrated remarkable talent, becoming a rising nova in Eastside's music scene. However, several years ago, while participating in a music competition, she was injured in a backstage fire following the finals and was subsequently sent to a hospital for treatment, after which her whereabouts became unknown.

**3**

According to investigations, following her injury, the subject was abducted by a fan who was a peripheral member of the Underground, and was awakened through an incomplete ritual involving the Cursed Melody. The subject successfully awakened as a virtuoso, but her mind and body were deeply corrupted by Mania at the same time. She was later found and protected by the Underground faction, remaining hidden until resurfacing in N.F. 116.

**3**

On August 11th, N.F. 116, the Underground virtuoso Requiem infiltrated BR-003, using the Black Ring to amplify the influence of the Cursed Melody. Her aim was to summon the Underground's Shepherd through a ritual and unleash a massive shockwave.
Ultimately, through working jointly with FAC, the Ring Research Institute, the Shattered Blade, the 9th Agency, and even EDGE, the MBCC Chief successfully averted this crisis. The summoning ritual was destroyed, and the Third Observatorium confirmed the death of the Underground virtuoso Requiem.

### Supervision Records

**4**

The subject has exceptional hearing and possesses perfect pitch, able to flawlessly replicate a piece of music by ear. Because of this, she is also highly sensitive to anything close to being off-key. However, rather than voicing her criticism directly, she would quietly watch over those who are out of tune from behind, silently praying that they find their way back to the correct pitch, at which point she leaves satisfied.

**4**

The subject is highly skilled at maintaining her violin, but due to poor life skills, finding her spare rosin and strings is in itself a challenge. Occasionally, a large stash of previously requested maintenance supplies would turn up somewhere in her room. When this happens, she carefully sets the old tools alongside the new ones. Yet when maintenance is needed again, she would still have to search for a long while.

**4**

Due to the effects of consciousness attunement, alongside confusion, the subject's tastes have also been affected. She frequently displays the food preferences of different people, often believing she loves a certain dish, even though her body struggles to tolerate it. This has resulted in reactions similar to allergies, causing her weight to decrease. Ultimately, the subject had to undergo a full physical examination, with her meals being confirmed and prepared by professionals.

**4**

The subject prefers performing without sheet music, believing that melodies flow between the strings and the human heart, and that it is best experienced through the music itself. Recently, however, she has occasionally been seen sketching notes onto a sheet of staff paper. It is said that the subject wishes to compose a new piece inspired by Chief, and has invited Chief to be the very first listener.
