### Past Records

**1**

The subject is one of the key individuals involved in the Euphoria explosion incident. A formerly celebrated dancer aboard the vessel, she was at the epicenter of the blast. She sustained severe injuries and was subsequently taken into custody by the Bureau.

**1**

Due to prolonged abuse and mental conditioning, the subject developed an extreme fixation on a savior figure. The Euphoria explosion was triggered when she unintentionally unleashed Mania powers beyond her control in a desperate attempt to resist her captors and save a friend. Psychological assessment confirms the act was devoid of malice or harmful intent. She is now undergoing treatment and rehabilitation under the Chief's supervision to facilitate psychological healing and personal growth.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's power allows her to manipulate the mental and emotional states of others. This is an extremely dangerous ability; it can overstimulate neural pathways and adrenal glands to a fatal degree, inducing death through extreme physiological stress.

**Awakening Signs**

As a child, the subject was sold to the lower echelons of Syndicate because of her blonde hair and later traded to Outland desert bandits. After suffering extensive abuse, a chance rescue cemented the archetype of a "savior" in her psyche. This ideal later catalyzed her awakening as a Sinner during a subsequent moment of abuse.

**Corruption State**

The subject exhibits a moderate level of corruption. While her mind has been severely affected, her ingrained performative demeanor allows her to maintain a facade of normality and seemingly sound mental health.

### Supervision Records

**4**

**4**
