### Past Records

**1**

Born into an immigrant merchant family, the subject is now a student at a private school renowned for its strict regime. Not only is she a perfect daughter in her parents' eyes, she is also a straight-A student for her teachers. According to close examination of her files, the subject has never used her power to instigate trouble or harm anyone.

**1**

Interrogation reveals that the subject mistook malicious impulses for negative emotions due to familial and educational influence. Having repressed her true feelings for years, she failed to form any meaningful emotional attachment and learn to relate to other people, resulting in her perverse ways of venting. As Chief helped the subject find an appropriate outlet for her pent-up feelings and a correct way of internalization, the subject was enabled to learn about the truth, get over hard feelings, establish wholesome emotional attachments and embark on a healthy path.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can control and rewrite the genetic factors in spores, making them grow rapidly into large mushrooms for various combat purposes. The subject can also use her rewriting ability to change her appearance, breathing, heartbeat, and growth rate, even reducing them to a fungal level (almost zero).

**Awakening Signs**

According to the subject and her mother's accounts, the subject became a Sinner due to maternal infection. Her abilities awakened under the emotional influence of her mother who was placed in extreme circumstances, enabling the subject to camouflage herself for self-preservation.

**Corruption State**

The subject's corruption level is moderate, but her camouflage ability prevents any visible mutations. She's sane most of the time but often has radical thoughts.

### Tracking Records

**3**

She's even earned social capital for her family and is therefore their *favorite*. However, beneath this facade, the subject continues to suffer scrutiny and neglect due to certain past events.

**3**

While still in her mother's womb, the subject was infected with Mania because of a family feud. Although she awakened as a Sinner and protected herself and her mother, the premature death of her other sibling led to silent rejection from her family members. Despite the subject's slightly egocentric character due to Mania's influence, she has never harmed anyone and is not the *monster* her prejudiced family makes her out to be.
