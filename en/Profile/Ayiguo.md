### Past Records

**1**

The subject has long been active throughout Eastia and is frequently involved in martial world affairs under the alias "Granny Goldspider." Her whereabouts are elusive, and she is feared for weaponizing venomous creatures and poison magic. Despite appearing as a young girl, she is a well-esteemed figure among local groups and martial organizations.
Investigation reveals the subject is famous for her exceptional ability to manipulate toxic substances. She has repeatedly deployed untested biological toxins against specific criminals during conflicts, causing public panic. Given the subject's capricious motives and lack of moral constraints, immediate detention is recommended.

**1**

Following interrogation, the subject is suspected to be a survivor of the tragic case of "Immortal Poison" production in Eastia a century ago. Forced into prolonged exposure to high-Mania and toxin-filled environments during the creation of a so-called elixir of life, she became severely infected early on. She relies on the consumption of deadly poisons to maintain her internal balance out of survival instinct rather than any intent to harm society.
While she appears willful and amoral, her core needs are simple and clear: to live on in human form. She has voluntarily contacted Chief and accepted the shackles, indicating willingness to cooperate and potential for rehabilitation. Currently under control when given clear constraints and continuous guidance.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can control her self-bred venomous creatures and silk produced by her spiders, as well as manipulate objects woven from this silk. She can significantly enhance the silk's strength and durability, granting it waterproof, fireproof, and damage-resistant properties. She also coats the silk and woven objects with strong toxins, enabling close-range defense and toxin release.

**Awakening Signs**

According to the subject's testimony, she was placed in a pool of mixed poisons to produce the "Immortal Poison," where she was bitten by various venomous creatures and other victims. While dying from the mixed toxins coursing through her body, her intense will to survive and fear of mutation and decay triggered a burst of Mania, awakening her power. She escaped from her original captors following this incident.

**Corruption State**

The corruption level is severe. Visible mutations and traces caused by prolonged presence of toxins are found on her body (abnormal discoloration and scars on both hands, etc.). She is lucid most of the time. Some degree of deviation in personality and self-perception.

**3**

She regards her human form as a prerequisite for survival, displaying intense fear toward any physical alteration or mutation. She often emphasizes her aversion to degenerating into a monster, but refuses to explain the cause of her change, only admitting that her physical condition is continuously deteriorating.

**3**

Her physiology differs significantly from that of typical Sinners. While she has extremely high poison tolerance, she can feel pain normally, meaning she regularly risks pain in exchange for survival.

**3**

She came from an ordinary family in Eastia, but was later abducted. Constantly exploited within the "Immortal Poison" longevity-promoting scheme, she was forced to consume deadly poison for years while struggling to survive a cruel, selective environment. This experience has extended her life abnormally and weakened her moral sense, pushing her to put survival and keeping her human form above all else.
