### Past Records

**1**

A tomb raider who primarily operates in the WhiteSands region, frequenting caves and burial chambers in the area.
Investigation units have discovered Mania-contaminated artifacts circulating in the market. All such items have been found to be linked to the subject. The subject is suspected of spreading Mania and causing significant harm to society. Detention and supervision are thus recommended.

**1**

Investigation confirms that the subject began her illegal activities after being coerced into theft as a child. She has no attachment to her past and does not intend to go back to that life since entering the Bureau. She has adapted well to the Bureau's rules, keeps a healthy daily routine, and has shown significant improvement in her social abilities.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

She can enhance both her own and others' weapons, increasing their power and durability. Additionally, weapons enhanced by her manifest special properties.

**Awakening Signs**

She once served as a scout for a bandit gang and frequently ventured into tombs. She became infected with residual Mania within the ruins and awakened her abilities.

**Corruption State**

She displays mild corruption with no physical abnormalities. She remains lucid most of the time.

### Tracking Records

**3**

She encountered MBCC operatives by accident while exploring a tomb alone in WhiteSands. Her identity as a Sinner was exposed, leading to her immediate arrest.

**3**

She was born on the outskirts of DisCity. As an orphan, she was taken in by a bandit gang and forced to enter tombs as an expendable scout from an early age. During this period, she was repeatedly betrayed by her playmates, gradually losing her ability to trust others.

**3**

Her special power awakened during an operation, after which all members of her bandit gang disappeared. This incident is suspected to be linked to the subject. Ever since then, she began living and traveling in WhiteSands alone. Although she occasionally assembled teams for tomb explorations, her lack of trust in others prevented her from having any fixed crews.

**3**

Due to her past experiences and prolonged isolation from society, she is emotionally detached and extremely rational. She is unable to comprehend others' emotions, beliefs, or faiths. She harbors intense distrust toward others, accompanied by a strong need for control and extreme aversion to accidents and variable factors.

### Supervision Records

**4**

She is passionate about extreme sports. The regular exercise equipment at the Bureau fails to satisfy her adrenaline addiction. Following her request, the logistics staff have installed rock climbing and small parkour facilities in the exercise area for her use.

**4**

Her interest in repairing and modifying mechanical devices has led to friendships with certain Sinners such as Summer and Eureka. Their group frequently gathers to tamper with facilities within the Bureau, occasionally causing damage. They have been reprimanded and forbidden from making further *enhancement* to public facilities.

**4**

She rarely initiates interactions with others at the Bureau, and consequently seldom enters into conflicts. However, tensions may arise when she works with Sinners who hold strong beliefs or display more capricious thinking patterns. The subject's poor communication skills occasionally cause these tensions to escalate.

**4**

After living at the Bureau for some time, she has inevitably engaged in numerous interactions with other Sinners. During this process, she frequently has trouble communicating with them and often turns to Chief for assistance. Although she struggles to express her true thoughts, Chief always seems able to understand her intentions and express them on her behalf.
