# Helga Profile

### Basic Information

Name: Helga

Incident: Mania purification operation in the Farlands

### Past Records

**1**

The subject was formerly an ace sniper and leader of an underground armed organization formed by the people of the farlands, operating under the codename Hyena. She possesses extensive tactical experience and exceptional infiltration capabilities.
Following the conclusion of the Fraser Peace Gala, the subject continues to operate in the region as a farland representative, maintaining order and peace throughout the farlands.

**1**

The subject currently serves as Speaker of the Farland Alliance, leading the reconstruction of order and community in the farlands. The leadership and decision-making capabilities she has demonstrated during numerous operations will lay a solid foundation for the farlands' future. Her prior collaboration with Chief is regarded as the beginning of a promising relationship between the Farland Alliance and the Bureau.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

She has keen eyes that possess the abilities of night vision, extreme long-range sight, X-ray vision, and slow-motion visual perception. Combined with her exceptional marksmanship, she is capable of executing highly precise hits on targets under extreme conditions.

**Awakening Signs**

She had been subjected to prolonged exposure to residual Mania weapon contamination on the battlefield, causing abnormal physiological changes. Following the death of all her comrades in combat, the resulting intense psychological trauma awakened her ability.

**Corruption State**

She exhibits mild corruption with no visible physical abnormalities. Her mental state remains stable under most circumstances.

### Tracking Records

**3**

She visited the Bureau alone as a farland representative, seeking Chief's assistance and cooperation in addressing Mania contamination issues in the farlands.

**3**

During her operations in Fraser, she usually claims to be a mechanic, presenting herself as an easygoing and helpful ordinary resident, earning the trust and affection of local youth and children alike. As a leader, she is a reputable and influential figure among the populace of the farlands.

**3**

She was born in Fraser's refugee zone. Orphaned at a young age, she survived by working odd jobs at marketplaces and repair shops. She later enlisted in Fraser's military to make a living. Owing to her exceptional abilities, she was quickly promoted to company commander. However, during a defensive operation, her squad was abandoned by Fraser's high command. All her comrades perished and only she survived.

**3**

After escaping the battlefield, she turned to resistance operations against the Fraser Senate, carrying out multiple assassinations targeting Fraser's leadership. She later joined forces with like-minded individuals to establish an underground armed organization composed of farland civilians, dedicated to resisting Fraser's rule and oppression.

### Supervision Records

**4**

She can repair all mechanical objects. During her visits to the Bureau, she frequently accepts repair requests from other Sinners. She is also adept at crafting novel inventions from salvaged parts. The toys she leaves behind after each visit circulate among the younger Sinners. Some have even become dedicated collectors of her creations.

**4**

She is unexpectedly good at competitive games and is recognized as one of the Bureau's top players. Sinners frequently challenge her to matches during her visits, and she typically wins with ease. However, witnesses have reported seeing her practice alone at gaming consoles late into the night, suggesting her gaming prowess may not be entirely innate.

**4**

She is popular among younger Sinners for her approachable manner and good humor. She actively participates in group activities and blends in easily with others, occasionally leading the group in harmless pranks or games, though she always knows where to draw the line and holds no records of misconduct. Overall, she has a positive influence on the Bureau's atmosphere.

**4**

Following their joint operation, she has come to regard Chief as a leader and partner who shares her convictions. Despite belonging to different factions, she demonstrates a high degree of trust and respect toward Chief, acknowledging Chief's decisiveness and sense of responsibility during the operation. She has explicitly stated that should Chief require assistance in the future, she will provide support at any cost.
