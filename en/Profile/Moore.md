### Past Records

**1**

A core member of the Nirvana Cybersecurity Center, she possesses excellent network-related skills and knowledge, and was among the first to issue warnings about the dark web. Since the Gray Mirror incident, she has been involved in and provides technical support for the MBCC's dark web investigations.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's special power doesn't have obvious physical signs, but investigations show that her intelligence and information processing capabilities have been significantly enhanced by Mania.
With the subject's technical skills and special equipment, she can create a unique digitalized Mania-based physical firewall, providing complete defense against most known attack methods.

**Awakening Signs**

The exact time of the subject's infection hasn't been determined, but her mental state suggests it was not recent.

**Corruption State**

There are no obvious signs of corruption on the subject's body, but her mind has clearly been affected by Mania, showing issues like drowsiness and forgetfulness.

### Tracking Records

**3**

A core member of the Nirvana Cybersecurity Center, she exposed her identity as a Sinner while helping out with the MBCC's dark web investigations. Subsequently reassigned to the MBCC by the Cybersecurity Center, she is detained and managed there as a Sinner.

**3**

Leveraging her outstanding skills, the subject was able to detect the dangers of the dark web at its earliest stage. After a thorough investigation, she issued warnings to the authorities. These warnings were not heeded until the later stages of the Perfect Vote incident.

**3**
