### Past Records

**1**

The subject claims to be the captain of *the Ark*, piloting the sand ship to conduct extensive rescue operations throughout WhiteSands.
The vessel operated by her was confirmed to be a former DisCity sand ship, warranting immediate further investigation.

**1**

Investigation confirms the subject has never engaged in unprovoked aggression during previous operations, only attacking other vessels for self-defense. Through proper guidance, she has learned to control her aggressive impulses. Currently, she has adapted well to the Bureau and regularly operates the Ark alongside her former crew members, continuing rescue operations in WhiteSands.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject can resonate with the Ark, enhancing some of the sand ship's functions, and can channel some of these abilities through her human body, including for combat, communication, movement, and other purposes. When this happens, her body undergoes changes in response to the sand ship's condition, such as increased body mass and enhanced dermal defense.

**Awakening Signs**

During a rescue operation, she was attacked by bandits attempting to seize her ship. She sustained injuries from their low-quality Mania weapons, resulting in Mania contamination. In the mental breakdown, her special power awakened for the first time.

**Corruption State**

She displays mild corruption with no physical mutations. She remains lucid most of the time but exhibits abnormal aggression and loses control when exposed to specific triggers.

### Tracking Records

**3**

The sand ship piloted by the subject was discovered in WhiteSands by the FAC. It was confirmed to be a former DisCity sand ship. Additionally, the FAC detected Mania contamination surrounding the vessel, prompting them to request the MBCC's assistance in tracking the subject.

**3**

According to her, the sand ship's name is the Ark. She broadcasts survival knowledge via the ship's radio, sharing her coordinates and inviting listeners to actively seek her assistance. When first-time passengers board the ship requesting help, she typically takes them in with enthusiasm and kindness.

**3**

Investigation reveals that the Ark essentially functions as a mobile community, with all crew members being rescued wanderers. The subject treats them as family, while displaying utter coldness toward those she deems outsiders. When her crew members' safety is threatened, she even exhibits strong aggressive impulses.

**3**

The subject's parents were former DisCity researchers who spearheaded the design and construction of the Ark. Repulsed by the former DisCity regime's actions, they took the Ark and left the city, beginning to rescue refugees in WhiteSands. Under their influence, the subject adopted helping others as her personal mission. However, after witnessing her family murdered by someone they had rescued, her belief was shaken. Since then, when facing crises, she frequently resorts to violent means to maintain peace.

### Supervision Records

**4**

She is skilled in sand ship repairs, particularly familiar with the structures of certain older models, and frequently volunteers to assist mechanics with routine upkeep and inspection of the Bureau's vessels. However, ships modified by her often end up with extra unnecessary weapon components. When questioned about this, she always claims it's *for safety considerations*.

**4**

She claims to have years of broadcasting experience and can handle the job of a radio host, so some Sinners have invited her to collaborate on livestreams. However, her actual broadcasting ability demonstrated in these programs has been questioned. Viewers describe her as *very enthusiastic, but seems to be rambling to herself most of the time*.

**4**

On board the Ark, she is in charge of mediating internal conflicts among crew members, and is able to resolve the issues most of the time. However, this is only thanks to her authority as captain rather than her mediation skills. When she first joined the Bureau, she was similarly enthusiastic about settling disputes between Sinners, but rarely with success.

**4**

Since being detained, she's gradually come to view Chief as her family. Whenever she learns Chief is about to undertake a high-risk mission, she instantly becomes ferociously protective. Only when Chief personally reassures her can her emotions be calmed. During combat missions, she always insists on remaining at Chief's side.
