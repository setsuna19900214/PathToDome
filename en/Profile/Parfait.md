# Parfait Profile

### Basic Information

Name: Parfait

Gender: Female

Birthplace: Syndicate

DOB: January 28

Height: 167cm

Date of Detainment: 

Alignment: Death

Ability: Golden Credibility

Incident: Eastside Counterfeit Currency Incident

### Past Records

**1**

The subject identifies herself as "Parfait Medici" and holds the positions of Executive Director and Investment Advisor at Tulip Holdings, an Eastside-based investment firm. Renowned for her consistently accurate predictions of financial market trends, she earned the moniker "Investment Guru" within Eastside's investment circles.
Beginning in mid-N.F. 115, doubts emerged regarding the legitimacy of her identity. She has since faced allegations of involvement in multiple economic crimes, including the fabrication of an Outland Hypercube mine that has led to substantial economic losses and widespread social repercussions.

**1**

The subject has not utilized her Sinner abilities for a considerable time. Upon investigation, no significant fraudulent activities were uncovered within her venture capital firm. The counterfeiting case from her past has been resolved. Given the minor sum involved and her early provision of substantial compensation to the affected parties, no additional charges were pursued. Presently, the subject remains active in Eastside's financial industry and continues to be in high demand among financial professionals and affluent business figures.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject has the ability to manifest others' trust in physical form. Once she earns their confidence, she can convert that belief into tangible objects, including but not limited to currency and goods. However, should this trust be lost, the materialized items will vanish accordingly.

**Awakening Signs**

During the years the subject has been active in Eastside, she has never publicly used her abilities. However, investigations indicate that her powers had already awakened by the time she first appeared at Eastside social events under the "Parfait Medici" identity.

**Corruption State**

The subject's Mania corruption level is relatively mild, showing no obvious surface infection symptoms and retaining clear mental faculties. However, the subject exhibits abnormally high levels of social activity in daily life, which is suspected to be a side effect of her Sinner abilities.

### Tracking Records

**3**

The subject experienced a credibility crisis following a series of events in May N.F. 115 and subsequently avoided public appearances in Eastside for the next six months. As her reputation deteriorated, she was revealed to be under suspicion for involvement in a counterfeiting case that had taken place years earlier in Eastside. As a result, she became a wanted individual by both Nirvana and the Financial Inspection Team, leading to her eventual detention by the Chief personally during a field operation.

**3**

The subject claimed to be an "orphan of the Medici family" who had been living abroad for years. Her debut at Eastside social events instantly attracted widespread attention. Through a series of high-risk financial ventures, she secured substantial profits for numerous investors. After amassing significant wealth, the subject became a frequent presence at art exhibitions, earning acclaim for her discerning taste and generous patronage of artists, eventually being hailed as a "model aristocrat."

**3**

According to investigations, the subject actually originated from Syndicate and possesses no noble bloodline whatsoever. Her startup capital consisted of counterfeit money created using her Sinner abilities. Because it was completely identical to genuine currency, it temporarily caused market panic. However, the actual amount of counterfeit money she produced was only around 110,000 DisCoins.

**3**

At the N.F. 115-116 Spring Concert, the Eastside investment community became convinced that the subject had secured a partnership with the West District Hypercube mine. Overnight, her venture capital firm, Tulip Holdings, surged to the top of the market, with stock and corporate bond prices maintaining an unprecedented high. This unexpected windfall not only restored her standing in Eastside society but also rendered the charges brought by the Financial Inspection Team and Nirvana effectively moot.

### Supervision Records

**4**

The subject had no permanent residence in Eastside and primarily stayed at various high-end hotels. This arrangement persisted until her detention by the Chief. Although she has since been granted an exemption and is no longer subject to long-term confinement, she continues to frequent the Bureau under the guise of "visiting friends."

**4**

The subject's "Parfait's Dinner Party" was once the most exclusive and coveted private event in Eastside. On the surface, they promised little more than "the chance to dine with Parfait." Yet financiers and investors clamored for invitations, pouring fortunes into securing a seat and pulling every string imaginable, all for a shot at winning the favor of the so-called "investment guru."

**4**

The subject cultivates a vast social network, with connections spanning every sector of DisCity. Her associates range from affluent merchants and financial elites in Eastside to childhood friends from Syndicate. Following the N.F. 115-116 Spring Concert, she no longer concealed her ties to Syndicate and has since openly accompanied her Syndicate friends to high-profile venues across Eastside.

**4**

The subject holds the Chief in high esteem, often publicly commending the Chief's artistic taste and sophisticated conversation, while also emphasizing their "close relationship." Some speculate that her actions are strategically designed to align herself more closely with the Chief, thereby bolstering the investment community's confidence in her ability to contribute to the West District Hypercube mining project. However, she dismisses this interpretation, insisting that her admiration stems solely from "genuine personal appreciation."
