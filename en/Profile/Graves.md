# Graves Profile

### Basic Information

Name: Graves

Gender: Female

Birthplace: Syndicate

DOB: November 2

Height: 166cm

Date of Detainment: 

Alignment: Death

Ability: Lovelorn Kiss

Incident: Morveth Castle Incident

### Past Records

**1**

Wearing a tattered, soiled wedding dress, the subject wanders the abandoned Morveth Castle and is dubbed the *Ghost Bride*. Her presence is highly destabilizing and she demonstrates bizarre behavior, mental instability, as well as an extreme obsession with weddings and vows.
After Morveth Castle was abandoned, spirits gathered there, giving rise to a legend that making wishes here could reconnect one with the dead, attracting many gullible visitors. Immediate detention of the subject is recommended to prevent the legend from escalating and to curb further Mania contamination.

**1**

Investigation reveals that the subject is innocent and kind-hearted by nature, and has never used her special power to harm others. According to her own account, someone left a ring in the castle, which she picked up. Under the ring's influence, she made a wish and accidentally summoned Chief to the location.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject possesses highly dangerous neural control capabilities, able to hijack and distort the target's perceptions. Her special power can deprive sensory abilities and thus self-control of the target. Alternatively, it can also maximize sensations, transforming a light touch into excruciating pain, causing the target to collapse from fear and phantom pain.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject was born a Sinner, contaminated with Mania from birth.

**Corruption State**

The subject exhibits high-level Mania corruption, with severe physical mutations on her body and visible signs of Corruptor transformation. Additionally, her mental state is unstable. When subjected to certain stimuli, she experiences symptoms such as hallucinations and clouded consciousness.

### Tracking Records

**3**

During the Morveth Castle incident, the subject encountered Chief due to her obsession with weddings and companionship. After the event concluded, her wish was fulfilled and she voluntarily accepted detention and investigation by the MBCC.

**3**

The subject was born into a regular family in a small town on the outskirts of Syndicate. Contaminated with Mania from birth, she was abandoned by her fearful parents at a church in another town. After being taken in by Sister Elsie, the subject became part of the church community, but her life was never happy, as she remained feared by others and grew up in isolation.

**3**

As the only church in the town, all weddings and funerals were held there. Weddings became the only source of solace for the subject. She learned a lot about weddings and was gifted an old wedding dress that had lost its owner. However, as more and more residents left and the nun passed away, the church was eventually abandoned.

**3**

With nowhere else to go, the subject could only wander the dilapidated castle in her wedding dress, accompanied only by spirits. Day after day, the forgotten castle gradually became known as the rumored *posthumous wedding hall* and the subject became the legendary *Ghost Bride*.

### Supervision Records

**4**

She knows everything, down to the most obscure trivia, when it comes to weddings. Familiar even with Outland marriage customs, she is certainly qualified as a wedding officiant, though unfortunately she has never had the opportunity to put this knowledge into practice.

**4**

The subject's whereabouts are unpredictable. She likes wandering aimlessly in pitch-dark places, often scaring people unintentionally. However, contrary to the *Ghost Bride* legend surrounding her, she is surprisingly timid herself and has trouble sleeping every time she watches a horror film.

**4**

Having only lived in the church before coming to the Bureau, the subject has many misconceptions about various aspects of life. Since her arrival, she seems at times awed or frightened by all modern electronic devices. For example, she's reluctant to use a communication device or be captured by the surveillance cameras, and she seems reverent toward microwave ovens.

**4**

Romance-related holidays are particularly important to her. She often sends handwritten, wax-sealed letters inviting Chief to *go and make vows together at some wishing stone* or *put a love lock bearing both their names on some bridge*. Though Chief does not fully comprehend these rituals, the invitations are occasionally accepted when the schedule permits.
