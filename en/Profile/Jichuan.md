# Jichuan Profile

### Basic Information

Name: Jichuan

Gender: Female

Birthplace: Eastia

DOB: September 25

Height: 161cm

Date of Detainment: 

Alignment: Fraud

Ability: Three Powers Formation

Incident: Northsnow Manor Incident

### Past Records

**1**

The subject is the current manor master of Northsnow Manor, long known for her broad knowledge and benevolent character. She is highly esteemed among the common folk at the mountain's foot for always helping and healing people.
After investigation, the subject is suspected of trapping certain martial world figures using the manor's unique terrain. Given the egregious nature of the case, immediate detention is highly recommended.

**1**

Interrogation reveals that all her actions were driven by her pursuit of justice as a martial hero. Since becoming a Sinner, she has never harmed a single innocent civilian. After being rescued and detained by the MBCC, she has demonstrated exemplary conduct. Though her physical condition remains feeble, she continues to actively cooperate with Chief's work, participates in various Bureau affairs, and offers counsel and strategies.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Her weapon is a plum branch named Bent Jade. By channeling the Mania within her body through Bent Jade, she can deploy the Three Powers Formation, greatly enhancing her own or a designated target's abilities to accomplish the impossible. However, each use of this power accelerates the corruption of her body.

**Awakening Signs**

While investigating a case among the common folk in Eastia, she was framed and became infected with Mania. Through sheer force of will, she awakened her power and became a Sinner.

**Corruption State**

The corruption is extremely severe. Her internal organs have been completely corrupted by Mania, and visible signs of mutation can be found on her body, needing to be concealed with clothing. Her mental state remains stable, but her physical condition is critically endangered by Mania.

### Tracking Records

**3**

After the Northsnow Manor incident, she left behind a letter foretelling her death and vanished without a trace. Given her critical condition, Chief swiftly deployed a rescue operation. After days of tracking, they finally located the subject with assistance from informants in Eastia.

**3**

Her parents were revealed to be the Medical Sage couple renowned in Eastia. They lived in seclusion at Northsnow Manor for many years, protecting the region with their medical skills. The subject was a bright but free-spirited child. Deeply influenced by her parents' heroic deeds, she became a fervent follower of the way of the martial heroes.

**3**

Once, in disguise and on an adventure outside, she met a young beggar named Mudling. Finding themselves kindred spirits, the two became close friends. Shockingly, Mudling later died at the manor gates, poisoned and mortally wounded. To uncover the truth, the subject left the snowy mountains for the first time, but was repeatedly obstructed by unknown parties during her pursuit of the murderer. All key clues were destroyed and she fell victim to a trap, becoming infected with Mania.

**3**

To draw out the true culprit behind the old case, the subject spread rumors about a martial arts codex, luring multiple Eastian Sinners to gather at the manor. After confirming that all involved parties had betrayed their virtuous principles, she resolved to use herself as bait to purge these villains. During her plan, she identified Chief as a worthy successor to her ideals of a proper martial hero, entrusting Chief with the hope of carrying on her unfinished aspirations.

### Supervision Records

**4**

She has a strong sweet tooth. She often uses drinking bitter medicine as an excuse to consume large quantities of candied fruits, osmanthus cake, and other sugary foods before and after. However, it's been observed that her interest in sweets has not diminished in the slightest when she is not taking medicine.

**4**

When her pets copy her own strategy, feigning illness to win sympathy from her, not only does the subject fail to correct them, but she also empathizes with them, hindering the progress of the weight management program.

**4**

During her detention, she has quickly won the hearts of both Bureau staff and Sinners alike with her amiable nature and extensive knowledge. However, due to her extremely frail constitution, the medical department has imposed strict limits on her socializing time.

**4**

She sets aside two hours each week to play chess with Chief. She initially had to hold back, but as Chief's skills improve, the two can now exchange quite a few moves. During breaks between games, she often provides insightful strategic analysis regarding Bureau affairs.
