### Past Records

**1**

A resident of Désir outside DisCity, the subject served as the town's mail carrier, a vital link of the social and information network. Hence she was suspected of ties to the de facto governor of the town.
Further investigation reveals no direct connection to the leader Vautour Bleu or her group. However, the subject's special power and trusted position allowed her to indirectly empower the said group's illicit activities while spreading Mania throughout the town. Immediate detention is recommended.

**1**

Interrogation reveals the subject's tragic past filled with traumatic events. Betrayed by fellow villagers and devastated by the loss of her parents, the subject became disillusioned with their ideal of "a messenger's sacred duty to foster genuine connections." She used her special power to forge letters to retaliate against the townspeople she viewed as "driven by desire" and insincere. Nonetheless, under Chief's guidance, she recognized her own lingering doubts as well as hopes, began to confront her past, and rediscovered her aspirations, finding her path anew.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Using envelopes as vessels, the subject can access memories of the addressers and manipulate their soul fragments to forge reply letters. In combat, she transforms accumulated letters into defensive barriers, cutting projectiles, or fiery attacks.

**Awakening Signs**

The subject awakened her power during an encounter with a Corruptor and became infected with Mania. During the incident, she discovered that her parents had long deceased and all the letters she'd received had been fake. The severe emotional trauma triggered her descent into vengeance.

**Corruption State**

The subject displays mild corruption with no visible mutations. Though lucid most of the time, she often develops radical ideas.

### Tracking Records

**3**

The subject was exposed as a Sinner during Chief's undercover operation in Désir and was confirmed to be abusing her special power. After Désir collapsed, she was apprehended by Chief and transferred to the Bureau for detention and investigation. The subject has been cooperative and on her best behavior since then.

**3**

The subject was born in an impoverished Outland village. A fragile child remaining homebound due to health conditions, she learns things only from the books and letters brought back by her parents, who were both mail carriers. She inherited their ideals of delivering genuine feelings and fostering human connections as a sacred duty.

**3**

Not seeing parents for long since their last delivery, the subject ventured out to search for them, only to discover they had long died. The letters she had been receiving were forged by the villagers lusting after the inheritance, even their names stained and dishonored all this time. The revelation caused her severe emotional trauma, devastating her beliefs.

**3**

Following her awakening of power, the subject began composing and delivering forged mail as her revenge against humanity, completely abandoning her previous code of ethics. Evidence indicates that her and her fellow villagers' coming to Désir was also driven by a forged invitation letter, possibly part of her broader revenge scheme.

### Supervision Records

**4**

After arriving at the Bureau with her cherished bicycle, the subject was warmly invited to join the Cycling Club. Unaware that the club focused on competitive cycling, she participated in her first event with her slow and wobbly riding style, finishing last. Other members later collectively gifted her an indoor electric bicycle for use within the Bureau.

**4**

Though the subject has always cherished reading, her isolated upbringing limited her access to books. At the Bureau, she has thrived in the new environment and become a regular visitor to the library. She soon discovered that the "DisCity Classics" series she had read in the past were in fact bootleg editions with significantly altered content, much to her shock.

**4**

During her early days at the Bureau, the subject sought to build trust by delivering forged letters, inadvertently fostering numerous but shallow relationships. Only after rediscovering her true purpose and no longer relying on forged letters for emotional comfort did she begin forming true and genuine friendships.

**4**

The subject handpicked a set of decorative stationery along with a fountain pen, ink, envelopes, handmade wax seals, and postal stamps as a gift, inviting Chief to share thoughts and book reviews through written letters. Likening it to keeping a shared diary more than making pen pals, Chief has gladly accepted her proposal.
