### Past Records

**1**

The subject is a powerful Sinner residing on Eastia's White Tiger Mountain, revered by the residents below as the White Tiger Mountain Lord. She typically lives deep in the forests with wild beasts as company, though she occasionally ventures down the mountain into human marketplaces, where she eats, drinks wine, and enjoys performances.
After forming a bond with Chief at Northsnow Manor, she developed an interest in Chief and DisCity. Together with her white tiger, she snuck into Syndicate with a merchant caravan. The tremendous destructive power she and her white tiger possess resulted in unrest and panic in the area. Immediate detention and deportation back to Eastia is required.

**1**

Interrogation confirmed that the subject did not intend to harm the city or the humans in it, so her label as a dangerous individual was lifted. However, her unauthorized entry still violated the law and deportation was inevitable. Chief made proper arrangements and saw the subject off on her return to Eastia, agreeing to invite her to visit DisCity on a different occasion.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Having been raised by a female tiger, the subject can communicate with beasts and uses a beast-taming bell attached to her hand to command them to fight alongside her. She does not rely on conventional weapons, but mostly just attacks with her overwhelming physical strength.

**Awakening Signs**

As a young child, she was abandoned in the mountains, and her special power awakened after she was infected with Mania. Due to her power, a female tiger took her in as a cub and raised her.

**Corruption State**

The degree of corruption is severe. Mania has affected her physical structure, resulting in an enhanced physique, sharpened senses, feline-like behaviors (such as requiring extended periods of sleep), and physical mutations such as tiger ears and stripes. Her mental state is normal, but having lived far from human society, she is poorly socialized, mostly thinking and acting like a wild beast.

### Tracking Records

**3**

Following the Northsnow Manor incident, the subject grew bored of Eastia and infiltrated a merchant caravan to travel to DisCity in search of Chief. Due to multiple destructive incidents caused en route, she was identified as a dangerous individual. She responded decisively to the subsequent capture attempt and even managed to abduct Chief.

**3**

She was raised in the secluded mountains by a female tiger since childhood. She can communicate with wild beasts and possesses inhuman stamina and combat capabilities. Investigation shows that the subject occasionally descends from the mountain to experience human life, showing considerable interest in fine cuisine, wine, and theatrical performances. She has also picked up the unsophisticated speech patterns of the common folk living at the foot of White Tiger Mountain.

**3**

She displays minimal assimilation when it comes to the moral standards of human society, and typically prefers to stay out of human affairs and simply observe. She believes in the law of the jungle and likes to live without any restrictions. Her ideal state is to remain an unfettered sovereign of her mountain domain.

**3**

Despite claiming to detest human affairs and morality, she always repays those who have helped her. Though she denies the title of "Tiger Deity," she accepts offerings from local residents and actively fulfills her duty to guard the region, demonstrating an unspoken sense of responsibility and connection to human society.

### Supervision Records

**4**

She possesses an extremely vigorous appetite and is a big meat lover. When her dietary needs are not met, she shows her dissatisfaction continuously. Night shift logistics staff have repeatedly detected terrifying murmurs echoing through the corridors and kitchen, whispering sentences such as "Gimme meat... Gimme meat..."

**4**

Her behavior is rather uninhibited and often accompanied by strong unconscious destructive tendencies. During her detention, she repeatedly caused accidental damage to internal security facilities and triggered alarms due to excessive movement or uncontrolled strength. Maintenance costs increased significantly since she had joined the Bureau.

**4**

She frequently appeared adorned with miscellaneous trinkets, most of which were gifts from residents at the mountain's foot. According to local belief, giving gifts to the mountain lord brings good luck. After this legend spread throughout the Bureau, the number of trinkets on her multiplied, turning her into a walking display rack of charms.

**4**

Having received little to no education, she lacks common sense. During her detention, to help her adapt to modern human life, Chief set aside several hours each week to give her remedial literacy courses. However, she showed little willingness to learn, often falling asleep within seconds, even dragging Chief to take a nap with her. After several failed attempts, Chief decided to conduct the remaining lessons without any written materials.
