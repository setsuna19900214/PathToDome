### Past Records

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's weapons are a sword and copper coins. After the sword is thrown and leaves his hand, it shifts into coins. It's exceptionally sharp against usual targets, and possesses a deterring effect on anomalous beings.

**Awakening Signs**

He appeared on the day of the full Illusory Moon, causing a dark, widespread anomaly in DisSea, as well as the unusual manifestation of the old Maria City. He was subsequently detained personally by the MBCC Chief. His body has been assessed to be exhibiting severe contamination, and his mental state is no different than that of a Corruptor.

**Corruption State**

Severely corrupted, with contaminants and crystals enveloping his entire body. He has lost all normal human cognition.

### Mind Restoration

**5**

After being detained during the Illusory Moon operation in DisSea, the subject has been placed in solitary confinement for observation and treatment. Due to the special nature of the Sinner, the MBCC has received the order for Chief to personally conduct dream dives with the subject.

**5**

Born Lyu Buzhou in Eastia, the subject entered the Tianyi Sect alongside his niece Li Xianling from a young age, demonstrating exceptional talent in Daoist studies and conducting himself with a calm and upright demeanor. From childhood, the subject possessed the ability to foresee the future. He attempted to use this gift to avoid misfortune and alter the course of events, but never succeeded. During this process, he began to question the relationship between what is predetermined and what can be shaped by human action.

**5**

After the calamity, the subject witnessed the futility of relief efforts and left the Tianyi Sect. He enrolled in a formal institution where he earned his doctorate, and later became a university professor. Together with Li Xianling, he investigated Corruptor incidents across the Eastern Continent and proposed the hypothesis that "the calamity may be linked to human consciousness."

**5**

In N.F. 21, many people in Eastia began seeing the Banished Immortal in their dreams, including the subject. The subject was among those who experienced the dream. Despite the ill omen he received in divination, the subject still chose to accept Li Xianling's invitation to join the expedition, with full knowledge of the dangers ahead and the near-certain fate that awaited. He desired to uncover the source of the dream's power and the unexplored possibilities of destiny.
