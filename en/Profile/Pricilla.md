# Pricilla Profile

### Basic Information

### Past Records

**1**

One half of the phantom robber duo Fox and Rabbit, the subject goes by the code name *Rabbit*. Serial theft suspect whose crimes involved an inestimable sum. It's unclear where the money has gone. The serial thefts have inspired multiple copycat crimes, seriously undermining social stability.
Later snuck into an important organization of the EDGE with a partner, jeopardizing the intelligence security of the DisCity. The subject must be strictly supervised.

**1**

According to reliable sources, the victims of the Fox and Rabbit theft cases are almost exclusively billionaires and politicians with a questionable track record. The so-called *theft trend* they've set only lasted briefly before it vanished like other fads.
The MBCC has detained both the subject and her partner Tetra. Their close relationship makes supervision easier and currently there's no risk of information leakage.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

Able to distort space with her power, enlarging or shrinking a target area or space. The suitcase created with her power can store a large volume of weapons and resources. It can even be used as a hiding spot or an emergency exit.

**Awakening Signs**

According to the subject's account, the subject was cornered following a failed theft attempt and the fear induced her awakening. The subject was able to distort the space and escape the life-threatening plight in the end.

**Corruption State**

With scarce Hypercube resources, Zart Village lacks protection. But the nature of the subject's body is unique and it has developed a certain resistance to contamination. Both the corruption level and mutation speed are low and the mental state is stable.

### Tracking Records

**3**

Snuck into the MBCC with the accomplice and intended to steal property information from Chief's office using their special power. Security discovered the subject's escape trail in time and a close investigation resulted in the subject's detainment.

**3**

Born in a suburban village closest to the DisCity's border where resources were scarce. Living an impoverished life, the subject and her mother depended on each other. The subject's mother, who had an honorable character and an amazing reputation, died of sickness after being bedridden for three years.

**3**

At seventeen, the subject dropped out of school due to financial reasons and left her hometown to work in Eastside in hope of saving up for her mother's treatment. The subject worked several jobs during the day and was active as a heroic robber during the night. The *Robber Rabbit* identity was known to no one other than Tetra, her partner in crime.

**3**

Part of the loot stolen by the subject in the early years was found in welfare centers and the rest was all returned to the respective owners after the subject's mother's passing. The subject met Tetra during the loot-returning process and the two then resumed activity as a duo.

### Supervision Records

**4**

Unwilling to submit to rules or teaching, the subject has repeatedly managed to escape from the Bureau despite having announced the escapes beforehand. She calls it an *escape game*. Out of the 175 *escape games* she's played with Chief, Pricilla has had 174 losses and 1 tie.

**4**

Passionate about flamboyant and bizarre disguises and possesses masterful transformative makeup skills. Many times, she's been surrounded by curious onlooking Sinners for her dramatic and strange guises. This has brought a strong party vibe to the Bureau and seriously disturbs the strict execution of routine management work.

**4**

Is friendly with many Sinners and often visits other Sinners in their detention rooms outside the permitted hours. It's impossible to police her visiting means. The staff have been reassuring themselves that "it's good for fostering the friendship among Sinners" and tolerating her roaming from one room to another.

**4**

Once, the subject got drunk and volunteered to perform magic tricks. Toward the end of the performance, she slipped into the suitcase she always brought with her and vanished. The next morning, a still unconscious Pricilla was found next to Chief's bed. She claimed she had no idea why she was sleeping in Chief's bedroom.
