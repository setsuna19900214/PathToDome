# Pine Profile

### Basic Information

Name: Pine

### Past Records

**1**

A current affairs commentator who went viral during the election period and the owner of a media channel *The Hedgehog's Eye*. She actively publishes content across various platforms.
The subject's channel focuses on conspiracy theories, actively digging up and exposing the controversial pasts of selected individuals. Her content contains a lot of unverified information that may lead to significant adverse consequences. Immediate detention is mandatory.

**1**

Records about the subject's past indicate that she once held hope for the future, but Worthington's deception destroyed her faith. She became convinced that the only way to survive in the dark was to blend into it, achieving goals by muddying the waters.
Currently, the Bureau monitors her external activities and article publications to prevent her from causing social unrest. The subject is gradually beginning to trust and approve of Chief. Her pessimistic attitude unchanged though, her behavior has become significantly less disruptive.

### Ability Performance

**Ability Summary**

The subject's special power makes her presence almost imperceptible, facilitating stealthy movement during intelligence gathering.
Her weapon is a custom-made launcher capable of shooting out surveillance gadgets such as cameras and listening devices.

**Awakening Signs**

She once got involved in a conspiracy. Upon discovering the truth behind and refusing to submit to another individual involved in the plot, she was almost murdered. It was during this critical moment her special power awakened.

**Corruption State**

Her corruption level is mild, with no visible physical abnormalities. Her mental state remains stable most of the time, only showing occasional fluctuations when writing about gossip or revealing scandals.

### Tracking Records

**3**

The subject first encountered Chief during the Eastside election. After exposing scandals and inciting unrest during that period, she was brought back to the Bureau by Chief for investigation and supervision.

**3**

Since her childhood, the subject has been independent and unsociable. She developed her understanding of the world's darker side and human depravity by wandering the streets, observing different social classes, and assuming false identities to experience their lives firsthand. To further comprehend the structure and workings of society, the subject began focusing on the upper echelons in charge of maintaining social order, and later resolved to become a journalist to expose the true nature behind their pristine public image.

**3**

Due to practical constraints, the subject failed to become a news journalist and instead began a paparazzo job. Once, while tailing a B-list celebrity, she accidentally discovered the celeb's amour and subsequent dispute with Vick, then-councilor of Shedde Town. Later that night, the celebrity was found dead at home. Pine immediately rushed out an exposé suggesting the councilor's possible involvement in the star's death, which however was banned by her superior. After confronting her boss, she resigned.

**3**

Having quit her job, the subject continued investigating both the celebrity's death and Vick's activities, attempting to expose his corrupt dealings. With assistance from Worthington, a "righteous" commissioner, she successfully sent Vick to prison. However, she happened to discover that Worthington fabricated the evidence and was using her to eliminate Vick and secure the position for himself. Her refusal to continue working for Worthington almost cost her life, but she survived thanks to her newly awakened special power.
This incident taught the subject the dark nature of the political circle, prompting her to create her own media channel "The Hedgehog's Eye," where she began exposing the crimes of Eastside politicians by mixing fake news with facts.

### Supervision Records

**4**

Shortly after moving into the Bureau, the subject surreptitiously installed surveillance cameras and listening devices in every corner, including but not limited to trash bins, ventilation ducts, flower pots, and dining tables. The technical team is currently conducting a systematic sweep to locate and remove these devices.

**4**

The subject maintains a highly irregular schedule, typically resting during the day and working at night. Hence she has no trouble missing precisely every single meal at the Bureau's cafeteria. Consequently, the subject single-handedly consumes over half of the Supply Office's monthly stock of instant noodles and other fast food.

**4**

Many of the Bureau staff members and Sinners are devoted readers of The Hedgehog's Eye. While the subject shows no interest in proposals related to fan meetings or fan clubs, she is actively recruiting informants within the Bureau to provide her with leads and scoop.

**4**

Having spent some time together, the subject has become extremely curious about Chief and is planning to launch a new column in her channel titled *Chief's Untold Stories*, dedicated to documenting Chief's past and secrets. Recent days the subject is gathering information through her sources, becoming a major headache for Chief.
