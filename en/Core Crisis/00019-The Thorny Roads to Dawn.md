# The Thorny Roads to Dawn

## ReN8-0 Warrior

> As dusk draws near, warriors of the farlands gather, yet consensus remains beyond their reach.

Twilight approaches, the last scent of blood carried away on the wind.

The old woman's steps across the sandy earth are both firm and light. After days of long travel, she finally lifts her dust goggles, revealing a pair of stern, penetrating eyes.

From the fortified hill ahead, figures rise one after another from behind sandbags and mounted guns, waving to her with unguarded joy.

Camp Guard A: It's Helga! Helga's back! Lift the lockdown!

Camp Guard B: Should've given us a heads-up, Helga! We'd have rolled out the welcome wagon!

Helga: Heh, save the fuss. Just let 'em know I'm here.

Inside the Farland Alliance council tent, the leaders have just concluded a hollow discussion. The real question—whether to bind their future more closely to Fraser—has, since yesterday's incident, been settled in a consensus.

This is the scene Helga walks into. From the seat of honor, Jabara scrambles to his feet, ready to surrender the chair to the rightful authority—the former revolutionary soldier and current Farland Alliance diplomat newly returned from Fraser.

Helga: Stay put. You look comfortable there, and I'm good standing. Wouldn't get a clear view of all your faces from way back there anyway.

Her gaze sweeps over the companions she hasn't seen for so long.

There is Yokna, who has governed the rift valley for years, reliable in minor matters, yet maddeningly indecisive when the stakes are high. Jabara, guildmaster of the merchants, has spent a lifetime chasing coin; he lacks vision, but can acquire anything, anytime. And Rael, all impulse and rough edges, but a superb warrior and an even better trainer...

The farlands would not function without them. Knowing their flaws all too well, Helga had instructed them before leaving to convene a proper council on the matter of Fraser and DisCity. Now, as her eyes land on the blackboard at the far end of the tent—the word FRASER stark upon it—a faint conclusion settles in her mind.

Helga: Let's hear it. What's the situation?

Jabara: Ahem. We trust you've been well? With the unrest along the route, and no word for so long, perhaps bringing some of our own escort would have been wise in hindsight...

Rael: Hmph. And who's to say "our own" ain't got DisCity spies mixed in? With her skills, she is safer moving alone. Not all of us need a whole damn guard detail just to cross a road.

Yokna: Enough. If our intel on DisCity wasn't shit and Fraser wasn't breathing down our necks, Helga wouldn't have had to risk her neck for us in the first place.

Yokna shoots a glare at the two men—all talk, no substance. She spits out her toothpick and launches into the story of everything that's happened since Helga left.

Yokna: Never would've guessed... that kid's Hypatia Campbell. No wonder she knows Aurum Secretum tech like the back of her hand. I liked her—still do—but... the proof's right there.

Helga's brow tightens. She remembers the girl as they met in Fraser days ago, but Margaret never mentioned authorizing a journey this deep into the farland territory. And the man with her... almost certainly a DisCity spy. Then there's also the Luminaris Society to consider...

Her eyes flick toward Saina, hunched in the corner, silently honing her blade without a glance upward.

The Luminaris Society's influence spreads through the farlands on the reputation of its Aurum Secretum Warriors. They already have the people's trust. Add a Disian to the mix, and this situation can only sour.

Helga: Seems the schemers got restless while I was away.

Helga: They all think they're hunters. All convinced there's treasure buried out here in these WhiteSands...

Helga: First they feed off our dead comrades' despair, then they prey on the living's hope. Fine. Can't blame us if we bag the prize before they do.

Helga: Step into these swallowing sands, and be ready for 'em to be your grave.

She steps to the blackboard and begins methodically laying out the intelligence gathered since her infiltration of Fraser City.

Helga: DisCity hasn't won. But we haven't lost, either. What matters now is this: neither DisCity nor Fraser has a damn clue where the Hypercube veins are since the Black Ring vanished.

Helga: That Black Ring, the one our farland kin poured everything into, is just gone. It's not that they won't tell us. They don't know where it is, either.

Helga: This "Unity Gala" is nothing but a consolation prize. A peace offering to shut us up.

The tent, after days of heated argument, falls abruptly silent. The leaders' eyes meet in flickers, then dart away.

Hands clasped loosely before her, Helga meets each gaze in turn, steady and deliberate, reading the unspoken conflict on every face.

Yokna: At least Fraser's still trying to get on our good side. They get it. We're all tied together. Sink or swim as one. They wouldn't just ditch the farlands when things get rough. Why else send that Society rep here to show off their Hypercubes?

Though her voice carries a hint of hesitation, Yokna continues to argue for the value of the Luminaris Society's Hypercube technology. Her people need that mine, and her determination to secure them is absolute.

Jabara: Can we trust any of it? What if... Fraser's just looking for a polite way to back out of their promises?!

Jabara's mind works with the volatile speed of a market ticker, churning out ideas that defy all conventional logic.

Rael: **** 'em all!

Rael: How many times have those DisCity mutts ground us into the dirt?! The only reason the farlands are still on a leash is 'cause we got no real way to fight the Mania. If it weren't for the Aurum Secretum tech, we'd all be corpses already.

Rael: That Black Ring that can mine Hypercubes was paid for in Furorian blood. And then it just happens to get lured right to the DisCity border? Whose damn scheme is this, huh?

Rael... Guess there's no point in even trying.

Helga: Farland Alliance... From the very first day, I've never once seen you all stand as one.

Helga: But this time... you've really outdone yourselves.

Helga swallows her rebuttal. With a silent, inward sigh, she turns to leave, only for Yokna's voice to cut through the tent's tension.

Yokna draws herself up, extracts a briefing document from her coat, and lays out their next plan.

Yokna: We've captured that DisCity Outland Affairs Officer who showed up after. Calls herself Margaret Kervoz. She's already secured in our rock prison.

Yokna: Care to question her?

Helga: ...The DisCity Outland Affairs Officer?

Rock Prison

A fortress of stone and despair. Prison cells are gouged into the sheer cliff face, each sealed by a heavy, steel-reinforced door. The only opening is a small ventilation window, its frame so savagely eroded by relentless wind and sand it has nearly melted away.

Beyond that barrier is nothing but a sheer, hundred-meter drop. The sole access is a rickety hanging cage. To leave otherwise would require wings.

Inside one cell, the prisoner appears almost serene. With slender fingers, she delicately peels open a withered grain to feed a small bird resting in her palm.

The groan of the prison door breaks the quiet. She turns her head and smiles.

Margaret: I'd almost thought you'd forgotten me. I'm pleased you've decided to visit at last.

## ReN8-1 Diplomat

> The diplomat from DisCity is well detained. She has her own opinions on the farlands and Fraser.

Margaret: Last time, emotions were running rather high. I'm not sure how much you truly heard. Perhaps I should reintroduce myself properly.

The harsh surroundings cannot diminish her innate grace. She greets each visitor with a measured smile and the impeccable bearing of a hostess receiving guests in a salon.

Margaret: You must be Ms. Yokna, of the rift valley encampment. We met briefly before. Your people were... somewhat less cordial than you.

Yokna: That depends on the company, miss.

Margaret: And you are Mr. Rael. I've heard stories of your beautiful wings.

Margaret: Ah, Mr. Jabara. You may not recall, but we have met twice before: at the N.F. 106 Great Expo, and again at the embassy reception in Fraser.

Jabara's expression tightens. He swivels toward Helga, urgency sharp in his movement.

Jabara: As you know, I've traveled extensively for the Alliance over the years. It's... not surprising certain attentive parties might have noted my movements...

Margaret: I am aware that each of you leads a city-state or community in the farlands. You represent countless ordinary people who call it home.

Margaret: As the Peace Ambassador, I have long wished to speak with you all directly about the future of the farlands. Yet my messages have been met with silence...

Margaret: Now, it seems, we finally have the chance.

Rael: **** your peace! The farlands never asked for peace from DisCity, not when it's built on our people's blood. We only want blood for blood!

Jabara: They say this one's silver-tongued in Fraser, yet leaves no trail. Now she has let two DisCity killers slip right through our grasp. We can't afford to be gentle.

Margaret: Please... let me explain.

Margaret: Consider, if this were truly a trap laid by hostile forces from DisCity, assassins would have been lurking around the ruins. The moment you emerged, they would have overwhelmed you.

Margaret: But that is not what happened. I came to you alone, unprotected, to clear their names and to speak with you directly.

Margaret: The real culprit has used your prejudice to frame two innocent people. Their goal is to sow deeper hatred between the farlands and DisCity.

Her voice holds a gentle warmth, and her manner radiates genuine sincerity.

Margaret: I know I am not welcome here. You may even see me as an enemy. The hatred between DisCity and the farlands has festered for so long it feels unbreakable. And now, we have just endured another war.

Margaret: But I had to come. For the sake of the *peace* I believe in.

All eyes watch the woman with wary scrutiny. Though their captive, she stands unbowed. Her posture is straight, her bearing unbroken and resolute.

Margaret: In my memory, the farlands and DisCity were not always at odds. When I was a child, my father often traveled here for diplomatic exchanges.

Margaret: It is true that during its expansion, the DisCorps government committed many atrocities against the farland people. That ended with the government's collapse after the establishment of EDGE in N.F. 103. Afterward, the farlands and DisCity entered a period of reconciliation.

Margaret: The turning point was N.F. 104. That year, DisCity suspended Hypercube exports to the farlands, declaring them an emergency strategic resource and imposing a full embargo.

Margaret: That embargo rapidly soured relations between us. By then, I was already in Fraser, hearing only one narrative—

Margaret: It claimed DisCity was hoarding vast Hypercube stockpiles while leaving people of the farlands to die in catastrophes, all while secretly selling them back at exorbitant prices to drain the farlands of their wealth.

Margaret: But the truth was quite different.

Margaret: In N.F. 104, a massive, unexpected explosion occurred in our Hypercube mining operation, deep in the West District mine...

Margaret: What you now call the Furor Black Ring is actually the fifth Black Ring. What exploded back then was the second.

Margaret: Yes... We understand the farlands' suffering because DisCity endured a similar calamity long before.

Margaret: That Black Ring destroyed vast infrastructure, and countless workers were lost to the Mania. Hypercube mining ground to a halt; far from exporting, DisCity itself faced a severe resource crisis.

Margaret: While we fought desperately to contain the Black Ring and restore production, the farlands not only offered no aid but launched waves of public condemnation and protests against us.

Margaret: Tell me... who was pulling the strings behind the scenes? And who stood to gain?

Yokna: Get to the point. What are you trying to say?

Margaret: Fraser acts as the middleman in trade between the farlands and DisCity. After the Hypercube shortage post-N.F. 104, Fraser stockpiled most of the Hypercubes that DisCity did export, then spread the lie that DisCity had imposed a monopoly and embargo, all to stir hatred.

Margaret: We tried to make amends. At the N.F. 106 Great Expo, we hoped for a peaceful gathering to unite all sides. Instead, Fraser detonated BR-003, sabotaging the Expo I had worked to organize and directly sparking the year-long Outland War.

Margaret: Do you truly believe BR-003 was a Ring Weapon deliberately created by DisCity? When such a thing emerges, who suffers most? Do you think we welcomed it?

Margaret: Fraser fears contact between us because they know the buried truth will surface. When it does, the people of the farlands will clearly see who they should truly stand with.

Margaret: I did not mean to assign blame, only to set the record straight. To you, they claim DisCity restricts trade; to DisCity, they express grave concern over the Farland Alliance's hostility. All while Fraser keeps its own hands clean.

Rael: I won't listen to your twisted words! No one but Fraser ever lifted a finger to help us. The Aurum Secretum Institute, the Anti-Mania devices—which of those wasn't built with Fraser's support?

Margaret: In fact, we have maintained normal trade relations. Mr. Jabara, you are aware that recent Hypercube shipments to the farlands came through deals with Nirvana, all at fair prices. I should add—I personally facilitated those agreements as well.

Jabara: W-what... how did you...?

Under the weight of their murderous glares, Jabara mops his brow, his hand coming away slick with cold sweat. Finally, with a visible internal shift, he steels himself to confess everything.

Jabara: Yes, alright! After the Great Expo, I secured the exclusive transport routes between our territories. When Fraser halted the Hypercube trade because of the war, I arranged several shipments through Nirvana. If not for the sandstorms and the Apostles after the Black Ring, those Hypercubes would already be in every town!

Rael: You ************!

Jabara: Why are you shouting? Haven't you used Hypercubes too? I did this for everyone's good, and this is the thanks I get?!

Jabara's DisCity smuggling was the last thing Rael expected. Incensed, he turns his fury away from the traitor and fixes it on Margaret.

Rael: Spare us your divide-and-conquer tricks! Everyone knows Jabara's a weathervane who bends with the wind, and Yokna wasted her time at that worthless Expo because her nerve failed. Disian, our blood once soaked this sand from your blades. You should've known then, some of us don't forget...

Margaret: You take pride in wielding Aurum Secretum, don't you, Mr. Rael? Even Ms. Campbell never imagined her brilliant creation would birth such a twisted form of hope in these sands.

Margaret: But she didn't develop Aurum Secretum for war, or to turn on other humans. It was meant to fight the Black Rings, to survive catastrophes. When did the Aurum Secretum Warriors' purpose shift to vengeance? Who twisted your aim with hate, and now leads you chasing the Black Ring to your graves?

Margaret times her pause perfectly, watching the flush of anger darken Rael's face.

Margaret: Heh... I believe I've clarified the past grievances. I trust you'll judge wisely.

Margaret: Now, let me present what I've actually come to offer you.

Margaret: Recently, BR-002, the second Black Ring lurking beneath the West District mine, was eradicated. The threat is gone, and new mining operations have been revived.

Margaret: The West District is rebuilding its facilities. Once production resumes, the new Hypercube output won't just make up for the deficit since N.F. 104; it will surpass previous levels.

Margaret: We will soon be free from the scarcity that has plagued us, and this prosperity, I sincerely wish to share with all of you in the farlands.

The room explodes into a clamor of shock.

Jabara: Y-you mean... D-DisCity's new mine... the Hypercubes... you'd share with us?

Margaret: Of course. Provided we maintain a peaceful partnership.

Yokna: But as Rael said, the last time Disians showed up in WhiteSands, it brought only blood. If you truly want to negotiate, DisCity should show good faith first, just as Fraser has.

After a moment of visible struggle, Yokna grits her teeth and forces the words out.

Yokna: Fraser has already named their price. If DisCity wants to win us over, you'll have to offer more!

Jabara: Ah! Right! If that Society representative wasn't killed... we could've had our own Furor mine. Having our own would be far better than trading with DisCity!

Yokna: Exactly. Extracting Hypercube veins is difficult, but Fraser has promised the Luminaris Society will help us establish our own mining operation.

Yokna: We trust the Luminaris Society far more than your shadowy Nirvana!

Margaret: ......

Margaret: Heh... The Luminaris Society?

Margaret: Haven't you realized it yet? Who do you think staged that incident in the Furor ruins?

The warmth vanishes from her voice, leaving only a cold edge.

Margaret: Let me tell you something: DisCity sent our most skilled prospectors to camp near the Furor ruins for half a month. They surveyed for over ten days and found no trace of Hypercube deposits.

Margaret: The Luminaris Society's claim that they can find Hypercubes in Furor—what a daydream!

With a subtle gesture, Margaret calls a dove to her hand.

The small creature tilts its head, its soft cooing delivering Hypatia's logical assessment.

Hypatia: The Hypercube radiation logs show *absolutely* no residual signatures!

Hypatia: Those signatures cannot be scrubbed. The only logical conclusion is... there is no Hypercube in Furor. Not now, not ever.

A deathly silence hangs for a breath, then chaos erupts in the cramped stone cell.

Jabara: What?! You're saying there are no Hypercubes in those ruins? Impossible!! I've bet everything on this!

*—No Hypercubes in Furor... The Luminaris Society lied to us?*

*—No, no. The real liar must be this woman right in front of us!!*

*—It's this damned Disian!!*

Amid the chaos, a figure lunges toward Margaret. A sharp kick sends them stumbling back with a heavy thud. Into the cleared space steps Helga, her face stern.

Helga: Enough. Stop making fools of yourselves. Get out, all of you. I need to talk to this Disian alone.

Resuming her seat, Margaret unfolds her ornate, delicate fan. With practiced grace, she lifts it to conceal half her face, leaving only her enigmatic smile visible behind its frame.

Margaret: Thank you, Helga. I knew our friendship meant something.

Margaret: Now, it's just the two of us, like when you came to Fraser to kill me. Did you know you're quite bad at that job? Guerrilla warfare and assassination are entirely different. The first only needs conviction. The second requires letting go of all sense of conscience.

Margaret: Take Rael, a pure assassin. He isn't swayed by what he hears or sees. But you, my friend... you believe what your eyes show you.

Helga: Save your breath, Margaret. Your tricks don't work on me.

Helga: You're good at your job. You spun a web in Fraser for over a decade. So why are you in our hands today?

Helga's words are a scalpel, cold and deliberate, laying bare a raw nerve in Margaret.

Helga: You're short on people. Your so-called friends don't always listen. Now you're doing the dirty work yourself, even stooping to use Campbell's daughter.

Margaret: Now you're being unfair. I genuinely admire my young friend.

Margaret: She's kind and strong, but so often lost... the kind that makes you want to lend a hand. I've always believed she'll find her own way.

Margaret: Fraser is the one truly using you. They stirred your people to cultivate the Black Ring, then turned them into Aurum Secretum Warriors to die on the front lines. Don't you want payback for a blood debt like that?

Helga's eyes narrow. Behind her gray irises, a storm of memory churns—smoking gun barrels, wounds that never truly healed. Her body almost trembles, a tremor born of old pain.

Dead embers flare to life.

Helga: Revenge doesn't move me. If I were that easy to sway, I wouldn't have stepped into this cage myself; I'd have just let them kill you.

Helga: You're an Outland Affairs Officer and a spy. DisCity must've invested a lot in training you. So I'm sure you know why the farlanders hate you all so much...

Margaret: Grievances need settling. People want peace. That's exactly why I... chose this path. In the end, nobody wants war except the butchers who profit from it. Today's DisCity fights only for peace.

Helga lets out a short, incredulous laugh.

Helga: Fighting for peace? Don't make me laugh. After BR-005 fell, DisCity could've pushed for total victory, but you stopped. You ask me if DisCity didn't want to win? It's not that you didn't want to. You *couldn't*.

Helga: The DisCorps hang over your heads like a sword. They've earned their honors but can't go home. You've got a new mine in the West District, but after months of bickering, production hasn't even started. You're pressured from all sides, with massive wartime investments in Hypercubes and manpower waiting to be paid back.

Helga: How could you afford to break Fraser completely? What you really want is to dig up the power networks behind it, use them as leverage, and bleed them slow through blackmail. Your real goal is "mutual profit"!

Helga: So the serpent in the shadows turns into a dove released for peace, singing pretty while flashing its colors to fool the world.

Margaret's elegant smile stiffens, her teeth catching briefly on her lower lip.

Helga: Is there really room to drive a wedge between the farlands and Fraser? Or are we just bait in your game—cast out, fish hooked, line reeled in, then the catch slaughtered?

Helga: But now you're the stranded fish here in WhiteSands. Nobody believes you. Nobody helps you. I know about your "influence." It's temporary. Once they sober up, everything you've said will be just wishful thinking.

Margaret: And you? How much better are you than me? Your followers are fools helping to count the profits of their own slaughterers. What can you do besides scream for blood and more violence?

Margaret: You know better than them. Your trip uncovered plenty of Fraser's schemes. But who can you actually tell? I bet since you got back, you've been listening to endless squabbling, stuck and unable to move.

Margaret is right, Helga realizes. She isn't suited to the assassin's subtle craft. Her first impulse, provoked by mere words, was to reach for her gun.

Kill her? Then lead a brutal purge of every threat to the farlands? The logic of a soldier was clear.

But she is not that revolutionary commander anymore, the one who knew only how to aim and fire.

Without hesitation, Helga rises to her feet.

Helga: You have until dawn. Choose: be escorted to Fraser as a prisoner, or crawl back to DisCity. Whatever friendship we had is done.

Helga storms out, the door crashing shut in her wake. Outside, the clang of a bell cuts through the air, calling the pulley operator to duty.

Margaret: You can refuse my offer. But you know the truth: alone, you can't change any of it. The farlands will keep being used, lied to, and led down a path of ruin.

The rattle of shackles echoes as the iron cage ascends. Margaret finally rises to her feet. Her words come then, clear and unshakable.

Margaret: Stop limiting yourself. Look up. See the wider world. If history has shown that relying on Fraser is no longer rational, why not try a new path?

Margaret: After I'm gone, the farlands' fate will continue. Ten years, twenty, a century from now, in a future you won't live to see. Stuck here in WhiteSands, bled dry generation after generation... Is that really their only destiny?!

Finally, the iron cage grinds upward and out of sight. Silence reclaims the prison. Margaret lowers herself back onto the cell's only chair, the one with a broken leg.

It wobbles treacherously, but she finds her center effortlessly, a subtle smile curving her mouth.

Margaret: Helga, I'm afraid you're the one truly trapped in this desert.

Margaret: As for me...

Her gaze drifts to the prison window. The birds perched along its ledge stir as if sensing a silent command, their wings a sudden flutter before they scatter across the distant, open sky.

## N8-1 Runaway

> Having broken free from the web of conspiracy, Hypatia and a gravely wounded Rust begin their flight.

Across the sprawling encampment, only a few figures are packing their belongings. Most vendors barely look up, their attention already returned to their work. A well-dressed visitor approaches one of the stalls.

Vendor: The big blow's changed course, so we're pullin' up stakes. Ain't much left on the shelf. If you see somethin' you need, best grab it now before it's all boxed up.

Vendor: There you go! In a bit of a rush, are we?

Vendor: Ah, sweets for the young lady! Takes me right back to my own girl. Here, take the lot. A little sugar helps any journey sit lighter, and it looks like you've got a serious one ahead. You and your brother mind yourselves out there in the grit, y'hear?

Vendor: Whoa now, hold on! That's far more than we could ever use. You're too generous. Please, take some back. We don't hold with that sort of thing around here. Driving prices up just makes it harder on folks elsewhere tryin' to get by.

Vendor: I've already taken more than enough. Oh, well... bless your heart, miss, you're too kind. Well... if you're sure, I won't say no! Can't believe my luck, makin' a proper sale like this on my way out the door. My girl'll be tickled pink. Thank you, truly.

Still chattering, the vendor finally accepts the money pouch from Hypatia, his face lighting up with unrestrained delight.

Hypatia: We should be the ones thanking you. Truly, we are grateful for the supplies.

Hypatia: If it's not too much trouble, could you help bring these to the car? My brother is rather occupied at the moment. I'm afraid he can't step away.

The vendor, helping to load the goods, lets out a cheerful chuckle when he spots Rust in the vehicle.

Vendor: So you're the brother, eh? Keep your wits about you in the deep desert. Something's stirred up the beasts lately, more'n usual. You look after your little sister, now.

Rust: Haha, of course! What sort of brother would I be if I didn't? The kind better left as worm-food in the WhiteSands, I suppose.

This "brother" has a face pale as chalk, yet he wears a brilliant, incongruous smile. His words are even more bizarrely out of place. The vendor can only stare, bewildered, before mustering an uncomfortable smile in return. Hypatia smoothly steps in, ending the conversation.

Hypatia: Goodbye, and thank you, for everything. I wish you and your daughter all the best.

The moment the vehicle door shuts, Hypatia's smile vanishes, washed away by a tide of worry.

Wind screams, whipping sand into a biting haze as the open-top vehicle tears across the desert.

Hypatia grips the wheel, knuckles white, her foot jammed against the accelerator in a rigid lock.

Where can they go? Where should she go?

Hypatia: *Supplies are secured. But... what's our next step?*

Hypatia: *The communicator remains silent. With no word from Margaret, the farland mission is at a standstill.*

Hypatia: *And Mr. Rust's condition... he can't move yet. Perhaps we should seek out the "contact" he spoke of. At the very least, we can get him to safety...*

Just as she sinks deeper into thought, the vehicle jolts violently. Hypatia snaps back to reality. An ash monster is already on the roof, peering down at them.

—Bwak.

The creature is knocked clean off by a single blow from their newly purchased desert specialty: an extra-long, extra-dry, super-hardened bread. The culprit of this ridiculous maneuver is none other than the legendary agent Rust, who now slumps in the passenger seat with eyes squeezed shut, his forehead slick with sweat, like a feverish patient caught in a delirious dream.

Rust: Focus. We are technically running for our lives.

Hypatia: Mr. Rust, you've... recovered already?

Rust: Hard to rest when the driver is philosophizing and misses the local wildlife moving in.

Rust props himself up halfway, movements strained. He reaches to peel the Aurum Secretum sutures from his skin, then offers them back to her.

Hypatia: ...

Hypatia: Mr. Rust... what truly caused these wounds?

Hypatia: I recognize traces of an Underground origin. Similar, yet not quite the same. They leave lasting damage, don't they?

Hypatia: Given the severity, perhaps it would be best to reach out to that "contact" you mentioned. If they are still nearby, they could escort you to a place where you can receive proper care.

Rust: And then what? Send you off on a solo rescue mission for Margaret?

Hypatia: I...

Her face turns pale as she bites her lip.

Rust: Look, it's fairly obvious these aren't your garden-variety lacerations. This is a potent, volatile... secret power. When you're a fully-fledged legendary spy someday, you can file a formal inquiry.

Rust: Right now, I need to rest and concentrate on accelerating my recovery. If you're truly in need of a conversational partner...

Rust: Look. That bird is back.

Hypatia: ...It's back?

Through the shroud of ashen dust, a wing of white feathers emerges from the distant haze, descending like a promise of divine guidance.

Rust: DisCity's Peace Ambassador has come to you.

This construct is different. It is formed almost entirely of concentrated special power, using Aurum Secretum only as a binding agent. This requires the host to pour a vast reserve of their own energy into it, forging what is essentially a personal "clone."

Margaret: ...Hypatia... is everything going smoothly...

Margaret: Can you hear me... or... have you not yet reached a secure location...

Hypatia: ...

Finally, the tears she has held back for days break free, streaming hot down her cheeks.

Hypatia: I-I can hear you! Ms. Margaret? Are you alright? Are you safe?

A familiar, gentle voice flows from the little bird's beak. It flutters its wings, lifting in an unsteady arc, then hovers before the vehicle's windshield.

Margaret: I am well. The people of the farlands are extending every courtesy... Thanks to the intelligence you provided, our dialogue is proving most pleasant...

Hypatia: I... I'm just so relieved to know you're safe.

Hypatia: I'm sorry... I failed the mission you entrusted to me... and I put you in great danger.

Margaret: You mustn't worry. You have already performed admirably. I'm transmitting coordinates to a location where I have arranged for support and a safe house.

Margaret: You may rest and recuperate there. Once you have delivered the intelligence that you found inside Furor regarding the Underground to my specialists, we will proceed with the next phase of the mission.

Hypatia: Understood, Ms. Margaret. I'll set out at once... and I'll get there as quickly as possible. Please stay safe!

Margaret: Do not be afraid. Whenever you have need of me, I shall be with you...

Margaret: And I trust a certain legendary agent will, of course, honor his commitment and guide you with his utmost diligence. Isn't that so, Mr. Rust?

With that, the bird attempts to land on Rust's head. He smoothly intercepts it by donning his hat, a silent smile his only reply.

Helga: ...Name's Helga, with the Farland Alliance. You can trust me. We're tracking two DisCity spies who pose a severe threat to the Alliance, and I need your eyes. Just tell me straight: have any outsiders come through here?

Vendor: DisCity... spies? No wonder... No, ma'am, I sure haven't. Never laid eyes on a Disian in my life! DisCity's a world away. What'd they be doin' all the way out here?
