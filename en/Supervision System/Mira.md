# Mira Supervision System

### 「A Piece of Cake」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠

The Bureau's team-building dinners in the cafeteria were once chaotic events that ended up with numerous broken dishes. That was until Mira volunteered to serve dishes and maintain order. Even the rowdiest troublemakers now behave impeccably in her presence, reducing damage and mess to an all-time low.

- Fill me in on the secrets, pretty please!

### 「Born a Fighter」

Location: Hypnotherapy Room

Urgency: ⚠

During seasonal shifts and within the close quarters, flu outbreaks have swept through the Bureau, affecting everyone—except Mira. When asked about her health regimen, she offers one simple response: "Indulge in good food and choose meat over vegetables."

- Is this the WhiteSands version of a health regimen?

  Chief: Don't think I can follow that.

### 「Business Acumen」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

After joining the Bureau's cafeteria management, Mira has introduced mystery meals at fixed prices—customers might pay for chicken wings but end up with a steak. She's even offered a guaranteed rare WhiteSands-styled roasted beef for every ten orders. While concerns arise about potential gambling tendencies among the Sinners who pool their purchases, the unexpected outcome is a notable drop in food wastage within the cafeteria.

- So, she's making use of the Sinners' big appetites?

  Chief: Now that's some sharp business acumen.

### 「Bonfire Substitute」

Location: Energy Furnace

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Scheduled Reminder: The Energy Furnace Ghost anomaly, as reported by several Sinners, is estimated to happen again in 10 minutes. Chief, please look into this promptly.

Chief: It hasn't been long since the last oddity. Perhaps the Eastians are onto something about the Bureau needing a shift for better luck?

- Resignedly head to the Energy Furnace

  Chief: Let's check the reports... Huh, every account talks about the same strange happening—"A dancing shadow and spooky singing by the Energy Furnace"...?

  Chief: Drawing from my vast experience with Bureau oddities... it's likely just someone singing and dancing there. Let's investigate a bit closer. It can't be that spooky... can it...?

  By the Energy Furnace, where the heat is so intense that most would shy away, a graceful yet otherworldly shadow dances along the wall. It sways to the beat of its own tune—a haunting melody with a peculiar rhythm, though the lyrics remain indistinct...

  Chief: ...Mira? Is that you?

  - Try to call out the name

    Mira: Oh my, Chief? What brings you here?

    The shadow halts, and the singing abruptly ceases. You observe as it contorts in odd, twisting movements—likely adjusting garments. And then, before your eyes, the stunning Outland girl appears.

    Mira: Do you... miss Tuco as well? Hehe, WhiteSands might be more barren than DisCity, but it has its own unique charm that really grows on you.

    Chief: I totally agree with that, but that's not why I'm here. Let me guess... you're not using the Energy Furnace as a substitute for the bonfires back in Tuco, are you?

    Mira: That's right! The Bureau is very strict about fire safety, you see. I'm not allowed to start any fires in the Outdoor Activity Area.

    Chief: Well... I guess the creepy shadow is simply a trick of the light due to the air density around the furnace, and the unsettling singing probably sounds spooky because the Eastside Sinners aren't accustomed to Mira's folk tunes... Mystery solved.

    Mira: Hmm? I'm not entirely sure what you're getting at, Chief, but since you're here, why not join me for some dancing and singing under the night sky? I've even brought some drinks—the kind that goes perfectly with bonfires and folk tunes!

### 「Deceiving Appearance」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Chief: For once I've finished work early. Time to savor this rare, peaceful afternoon...

Thud! Crackle! Whoosh! Zoom!

Chief: And that's that... Well, not sure what's going on, but those noises don't sound promising. Sigh, I should go check it out.

- Rush to the scene

  Eureka: Super Tornadito, where do you think you're going?! No, not there. Stop right now!

  Mira: ...Hmm? Did something just dash in here?

  Eureka: It's a little tool I created to assist with pipe cleaning. I set it up to recognize pipes and spot objects in the dark, but something glitched, and now it just keeps drilling into any dark patch it comes across...

  Mira: Oh, so that's why it made a beeline for the gap behind the refrigerator? I see what's happening now. Can it get out on its own?

  Eureka: Hmm... I don't think so. When I was designing it, I just assumed pipes would have two openings, allowing it to exit the other side. I never thought about programming an escape protocol...

  Chief: But the Bureau's refrigerator is up against the wall with no way out, right? Do we just wait for it to pop out somehow? And with all that force, won't it wreck the fridge if it keeps thrashing about in there?

  Eureka stands there with a bewildered expression, evidently caught off guard by this turn of events. The situation appears to have hit a dead end, with even Mira's demeanor turning grave. However, before long, her comforting smile reemerges.

  Mira: Come on now, no need for those worried looks. I've thought of the simplest solution. Just leave it to me.

  - Could it be...

    In the following instant, Mira inhales deeply, locates the optimal pivot point on the refrigerator, and begins applying force. You and Eureka gaze in amazement as the refrigerator glides across the floor, emitting a spine-tingling screech, slowly lifting from its initial position.

    Chief: Mira, while I appreciate you helping us out, the Bureau's refrigerator is really heavy... You don't have to exert yourself this much. What if you pull a muscle?

    Mira: Oh, this is nothing really. Back in Tuco, I used to carry all the supplies for my motel by myself. I just don't want anything happening to the cafeteria—I made a promise to look after it well.

### 「People Reader」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Sinner A: That fortune teller from Phantom Entertainment is incredible! They can tell your deepest secrets at just one glance!

Sinner B: It's all just a show. I mean, who can actually do that? If someone had real powers like that, they'd have been locked up here already!

Sinner A: I wouldn't be so sure. Some seasoned folks are just naturally good at reading people, you know? Wait, wasn't Mira running a motel before? She should have that skill too, right?

Mira: Hehe, you're flattering me too much. My motel was just a modest spot, nothing extraordinary like you're picturing. I definitely can't pull off anything like reading people at a glance.

However, encouraged by the enthusiastic Sinners, Mira eventually gives in and takes a few minutes to observe an unfamiliar Sinner before diving into her character analysis.

Mira: Hmm... I'd say you're a kind and patient person. You likely find joy in tending to plants, and you're probably quite skilled at household tasks as well, right?

Sinner B: Haha, no chance! She's got a fiery temper, her room's a disaster, and she has no interest in plants! See? I told you nobody can really read someone at a glance!

Observing the events play out and biding your time until the crowd dissipates, you make your way over to Mira, who sits on the grass with her knees pulled up to her chest.

Chief: ...Mira, you got those readings wrong on purpose, didn't you?

Mira: Oh my, Chief, what makes you think that? Surely you don't actually believe I can see straight through someone at first sight, do you?

Chief: But you managed to completely misinterpret everyone's personality—with uncanny precision. Doesn't that require just as much insight as getting it right? Besides, you were the only motel owner in town.

- No, but...

  Mira: Who knows? Maybe the motel stayed in business simply because everyone was kind enough to put up with my incompetence?

  Chief: No need to act clueless with me. I won't reveal your secret. I'm simply curious—why do you keep your skill for reading people hidden from them?

  Mira: It's rather straightforward, actually. Managing a motel isn't just about offering food, drinks, and a place to rest—it involves handling odds and ends, along with information. And if you wish for guests to lower their defenses and spill all their secrets...

  Mira: You have to make them believe you're utterly clueless. If they realize how perceptive you truly are, they'll never let their guard down around you.

  I see, so a good motel owner needs both the skill to read people and the wisdom to appear less perceptive than they are.

  But that should be just a survival tactic in WhiteSands, correct? Unless... Are you still trading information now?


  - Show understanding

    Mira: I knew you would understand, Chief. You always do.

    Chief: But how do I know your answer isn't just another way to make me lower my guard?

    Mira: Hehe, I guess it boils down to who's better at reading people... doesn't it? Don't expect me to drop any clues, Chief.

  - Express doubt


  <br>

  Mira: Oh, how could that be possible? The Bureau is so peaceful under your guidance, Chief. What need could there be for information trading here?

  Chief: The more innocently you deny it, the more I feel that there's something happening behind the scenes...

  Mira: No need to be so suspicious, Chief. You understand better than anyone the limits of a humble motel owner like me. Like you mentioned, it's merely a survival tactic.

  Chief: Give me a moment... I'm trying to recall if I've unintentionally disclosed any of my sources to you...
