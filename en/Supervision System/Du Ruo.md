### 「The Impression of DisCity」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You find that Du Ruo has set up a small table in the Outdoor Activity Area, on which are a few small canisters. She seems busy.

- Go and check

  As you come closer, you realize that in addition to the canisters, there is a row of pouches lined up on the table. Du Ruo opens them, sniffs at their contents, and adds some to this canister or that.

  Chief: Du Ruo, what have we here?

  Du Ruo: Ah, it's you, Chief. It might not be obvious, but I'm making incense.

  Chief: So many ingredients are needed? If I didn't know any better, I'd say you're making some sort of ointment.

  Du Ruo: Hehe, even the ingredients for the rarest types of incense can look unremarkable sometimes.

  Du Ruo doesn't stop the task at hand, which she is performing with such ease and grace that you can't help but think of it as an art form.

  You stand watching for a while. The whole process seems bewilderingly complicated.

  Chief: ...May I ask what is the scent of this incense you're making?

  Du Ruo: The scent of DisCity.

  Chief: There is such a scent?

  Du Ruo: Well, there will be when I make it.

  Chief: So you're capable of creating new scents...

  - Show amazement

    Du Ruo: I've been to many places, seen many landscapes, and met many people... I want to commemorate them and keep my memories of them alive, besides writing about them.

    Chief: By making incense?

    Du Ruo: Yes. In Eastia, there's a general belief that nothing brings back memories faster than scents.

    I burn incense, because the familiar scents never fail to make me feel relaxed and peaceful.

    Du Ruo: Chief, would you like some of this incense with a DisCity scent when it's ready? You can light it when you find yourself far away from home.

### 「The Appeal of Calligraphy」

Location: Hypnotherapy Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

A large amount of smoke is detected in the hypnosis room. A fire might have broken out... Go and check it out?

- Go immediately

  You arrive at the hypnosis room to find it thick with smoke. Du Ruo and some staff members are working together to extinguish the last of the flames.

  Upon inquiry, it is found that the fire was caused by the improper use of an electrical appliance by a staff member, and that the new comfortable soft furnishings inside were made of flammable materials. The fire has left the room a complete mess.

  Chief: I'm glad that no one got hurt. I'll have the logistics department remove the burnt items and clean this up.

  You're just about to contact the logistics department using your communications device when Du Ruo puts out her hand to stop you, a grin on her face.

  Du Ruo: Are you throwing away this entire row of decorative curtains?

  Chief: Yes, why?

  Du Ruo: Can I have them?

  Chief: They are badly burnt. What do you want them for?

  Du Ruo: Well, that's a secret~

  Chief: ...

  A few days later, artistic curtains, adorned with burn marks and Du Ruo's calligraphy, appear on the scene. Yanyan is so captivated by these curtains that she decides to use them as a permanent backdrop for the gatherings of the Eastians at the Bureau.

  ...

  This new art form takes the Bureau by storm. Within a few short days, it has become all the rage. Many Sinners go so far as to burn fabrics and take them to Du Ruo, who will grace them with her calligraphy.

  Chief: Stop them from committing arson within the Bureau, and make them pay for the replacement fabrics.

  - Arson is prohibited at the Bureau!

### 「My Path, Alone But Not Lonely」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Since her arrival at the Bureau, you've often seen Du Ruo scribbling away in a notebook.

Chief: Du Ruo, what have you written in your notebook, if you don't mind me asking?

- Curious

  Du Ruo: Just day-to-day matters, all trivia... If you wish to run a check just to be sure, you will have my full cooperation.

  Chief: I'm not suspecting you of doing anything wrong. I'm just curious; I see you write in it every day.

  Du Ruo: Thank you for your trust. Here you are. Take a look if you want to.

  You flip through her notebook. It is indeed filled with trivia.

  Instructions on how to operate common household appliances, tips for using public transportation, customs and taboos in DisCity... No detail is too small for her.

  Du Ruo: See, I wasn't lying to you.


  - Ask Du Ruo about the purpose of the notes

    Chief: What are these notes for?

    Du Ruo: Isn't it obvious? Recordkeeping is part of my professional life as a scholar.

    Chief: There is nothing scholarly about these notes. They're practical.

    This strikes me as a guide to life in DisCity. What's the purpose?

    Du Ruo: I haven't given it much thought. I suppose I'm writing this for the sake of those who come after me.

    Chief: Those who come after you?

    Du Ruo: Other Eastian scholars who might come to visit DisCity someday.

    Du Ruo: If this measly effort of mine can help them overcome some of the obstacles they might face on their journey, then I'd say that I've done my small part as a forerunner.

    Du Ruo's voice lowers, and in the dim light, you see the earnest look in her eyes.

  - Take a closer look


  <br>

  You turn to the last page and are surprised to find that there is writing on it too. The handwriting is elegant, but the words have been written with a firm hand.

  *This is but a fool's errand, some might mock. Yet there will come a day...*

  *There will come a day when peace and prosperity reign, when Eastian scholars travel far and wide.*

  *My path, alone but not lonely.*

### 「The Lost Pouch」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

One day, shortly after you finish work, EMP comes rushing into your office, eyes brimming with tears.

EMP: Chief, you've got to help me. Please help me!

Chief: What have you gotten yourself into this time?

EMP: Sob... I didn't mean to. I was in the storeroom, and I mistook Du Ruo's pouch for a target prepared by the staff for my archery training, and then...

- And then?

  EMP: And then I hung it on a hook and shot arrows at it. I hit the target, and it flew away. And I can't find it now!

  Chief: ...

  EMP: What do I do now, Chief? I've searched every inch of the Outdoor Activity Area. I've heard she almost always carries that pouch on her person. I...

  EMP's monologue is cut short by a gentle knock on the door that has been left ajar. It opens wider to reveal Du Ruo.

  Du Ruo: Good evening, Chief. I wonder if you've seen my pouch somewhere.

  Chief: Hmm...

  Du Ruo: I'm sorry to have disturbed you. But this is a matter of the utmost importance. The pouch contains a rare treasure that can change one's fate.

  EMP: ?!!

  Du Ruo: Where did I put it? Ah, it was the storeroom in the Outdoor Activity Area. I put it there this afternoon, and when I came back for it, it was gone.

  Du Ruo crosses her arms and rests her chin on her hand, a study in composure. She is ostensibly talking to you, but keeps stealing suspicious glances at EMP.

  EMP: I can't take it anymore. Sob... It's me. I'm the one who lost your pouch. It's all my fault. I'm so sorry.

  While apologizing to Du Ruo, EMP tugs at your sleeve stealthily, signaling you to help her.


  - Stay out of it

    EMP is sweating. Seeing this, Du Ruo decides to stop teasing EMP and adopts a gentle tone when she speaks to her.

    Du Ruo: Ah, it's just a small thing. No need to be so nervous.

    Du Ruo: This pouch was meant for someone in trouble, after all. Think of it as a gift from me to you.

    EMP: Huh?! Really? Y-You're not mad at me?

    Du Ruo: Why would I be? You've already apologized. You're a good kid; you owned up to your mistake.

    - Tell EMP to be careful not to mistake other people's belongings for her own again

      Chief: Because if you make the same mistake again, you might have to open the pouch to save your life with the treasure inside—assuming you can find it first.

  - Help EMP


  <br>

  Chief: We'll carry out a thorough search. I'll arrange it now.

  EMP: Chief, I... I think I should go and search the Outdoor Activity Area again.

  EMP is trembling all over. After letting her go, you briefly explain the situation to Du Ruo. You also tell her how sorry EMP is about losing her pouch, and that you'll get some staff members involved in the search.

  Du Ruo: No need. The pouch is empty.

  Chief: Empty?

  Du Ruo: Absolutely.

  Chief: But you just said the pouch contained a rare treasure that can change one's fate. Was that a lie?

  Du Ruo: I did say that, but it wasn't a lie.

  Du Ruo: It does contain a rare treasure.

  Chief: ?

  Du Ruo is still smiling, but you have the sense that there is something different about her smile now.

  Du Ruo: In my opinion, no one or nothing can change your fate except yourself. When you open the pouch and find it empty, you will either fight to the death with grim determination or...

  just give up the fight and resign yourself to your fate. If the latter is the case, a powerful tool wouldn't go far in helping you get out of whatever trouble you've found yourself in.
