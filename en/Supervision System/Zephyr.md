# Zephyr Supervision System

### 「Nightcap」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

The light in Zephyr's room is always among the last to go out in the detention area. Well into the night, you can often still hear Sinners' laughter and clinking glasses from her room.

- I'm glad you're making friends, but don't forget to get some rest.

### 「Oath」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠

Zephyr has been reading a popular adventure novel. The protagonist likes to swear on his parents' names whenever he makes a bet. After finishing the book, Zephyr decides she'd make a better protagonist since she has two sets of parents, meaning she could swear twice.

- That's one way to think of it.

### 「Night Song」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

After dinner, a tipsy Zephyr accidentally patches her broadcast signals into the public channel. That night, her off-key singing echoes through the Bureau until the small hours.

- Reprimand her

  Zephyr accepts the lecture with all due humility, though she makes no promises about behaving next time.

### 「Sleepless Chief」

Location: Management Center / Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Knock, knock, knock. A series of urgent raps on your office door breaks the late-night silence.

- Get up and answer the door

  Forcing your drowsy eyelids open, you rise from the makeshift cot and make your way to the door.

  You open the door, only to feel something ice-cold pressed against your cheek. The shock jolts you wide awake.

  Zephyr: Chief! You're still up at this hour too!

  With a bright smile, Zephyr withdraws her hand and presses the same cold can against her own cheek, giving you a playful wink.

  You glance up at the digital clock that reads 3 a.m., then down at your pajamas.

  Chief: Ah, right... still awake.

  - Be polite

    Zephyr: Perfect! Wanna go stargazing with me?

    Chief: At this hour? Maybe some other time...

    Zephyr: I heard there's gonna be a simulated meteor shower in the Outdoor Activity Area tonight! I've already gone and done some scouting. The visibility is just perfect!

    Zephyr: This is my first meteor shower since joining the Bureau. Wouldn't want to go with anyone else.

    Chief: ...

    - Well, I'm up anyway.

      You follow Zephyr to the Outdoor Activity Area. While waiting for the meteors on the lawn, you doze off unwittingly.

      When you open your eyes again, dawn is already breaking. Half of Zephyr's jacket is draped over you, while she leans against your shoulder, fast asleep.

### 「Paper Plane」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

When you return to your office, the door is ajar and paper planes are scattered everywhere outside and inside.

- Remove the paper plane on the doorknob

  On the paper is a big smiley face, accompanied by Zephyr's flamboyant signature.

  - Remove the paper plane under the chair

    Zephyr: Hey Chief! This is Zephyr No. 10 planning a break-in of your office. Surprise!

    Zephyr: P.S. The nine other planes have all crashed heroically. Here's hoping No. 10 will accomplish its mission. Pretty please!

    - Remove the paper plane stuck between the couch's cushions

      Zephyr: Chief, Chief! So, funny story: I can't remember what I did with my last mission report, so I won't be turning it in just yet!

      Zephyr: I did write it though. I just can't find it anymore!

      Chief: ...

      - Check paper plane No. 17

        It's Zephyr's missing mission report.

### 「Run Away」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Coming out of a meeting, you hurry through the Bureau's corridor to the next one, another last-minute addition to your schedule.

Chief: Sigh.

Before your sigh even leaves your mouth, a hand shoots out from a nearby office cubicle and yanks you inside.

The next second, a finger is pressed against your lips.

Zephyr: Shh... don't shout. Chief, it's me.

Chief: Zephyr? What are you doing here?

Zephyr: Waiting for you, of course!

Chief: What for?

- Waiting for me?

  Zephyr: Well...

  Just then, footsteps approach in the corridor outside. Zephyr holds back the rest of her unfinished sentence.

  Staff A: These meetings had nothing to do with the Bureau. Why did we have to attend them?

  Staff B: Stop complaining. At least it's done for us now. I heard Chief still has to go to more meetings.

  The footsteps haven't faded away yet. Zephyr leans in with a smile and whispers in your ear like she doesn't want others to hear her.

  Zephyr: I'm here to rescue you from all this boring stuff!

  Zephyr: Just for today, Chief, how about we *run away* together? It'll be our little secret!

  So where are we *running away* to?

  You just heard it. I still have work to do.


  - Where to?

    Zephyr: Where? I have no idea, but once we take that first step, I'm sure the answer will come!

    Zephyr: I know we can't actually leave the Bureau, but there's still plenty of fun to be had right here! Get some fresh air, climb a tree, jump in the pool, then grab drinks in our wet clothes, haha...

    Zephyr: Just come with me! I'll make sure you have the time of your life. You'll forget about your busy schedule and all your worries!

    Chief: Why does it sound like you're just using me as an excuse to do all these things?

    Zephyr: Shh, don't call me out like that!

    Well, seems like you can't argue with her. Seeing Zephyr's bright smile, you accept her invitation and make up an excuse to skip that unimportant meeting.

    After all, you get pointless tasks like that every day, but a chance to run away from your routine only comes once in a blue moon.

  - I still have work to do.


  <br>

  Chief: I'll see if I'm free after I'm done with my work.

  Zephyr: I knew you'd say that.

  She reaches out and slips a button-sized signal transmitter into your coat pocket.

  Zephyr: When you're done with work and start missing me, just give me a beep!

  Zephyr: I'll be there right away! But for now, I'm off to have some fun on my own.
