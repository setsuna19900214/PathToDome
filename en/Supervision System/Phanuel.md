# Phanuel Supervision System

### 「Formulaic Apology」

You've received another handwritten apology letter from Phanuel. The content is sincere and heartfelt, yet even after reading it through, you still can't quite tell what she's actually apologizing for.

- Nothing major has happened lately either... huh.

### 「Empty Schedule」

Since joining the Bureau, whenever there's nothing on her schedule, Phanuel would simply sit perfectly still in her room. Well, aside from the fact that she'd flash a sweet smile whenever someone passes by.

- Set up a daily schedule for her

### 「Selfie Review」

The Bureau has recently opened up applications for Sinners to update their file photos. As you walk past the photo room, you find a long line already forming at the door.

A Sinner nearby wearily informs you that Phanuel, who went in before everyone else, has been taking photos alone in there for the past hour.

- Check things out

  Stepping inside the photo room, you find Phanuel standing before the camera. The screen beside her is filled with shots that are nearly identical to one another.

  Chief: Did you run into some problem?

  Phanuel: Chief! Perfect timing!

  Phanuel: Every single photo Phanuel took is absolutely perfect, but I don't know what I should do next...

  Phanuel: No one from the staff has come to help Phanuel pick the best photo... Could it be that I've messed up somewhere?

  - Help her decide

    Chief: A file photo isn't a promotional shot. As long as it's clear and meets the requirements, it's fine.

    Chief: Let me take a look... Just make sure your ears and eyebrows are showing. Now then, go take one more shot.

    Phanuel follows your directives and finally gets her file photo updated.

### 「Idol Mode: On」

You notice that after finishing a small task, Phanuel will sometimes pause in place for three seconds.

If no one nearby reacts in a way that satisfies her, she'll casually repeat the same action as if nothing happened.

- Ask why she does this

  Chief: Why did you dump the water just to refill it right after?

  Phanuel: Because Chief didn't compliment Phanuel, obviously!

  Phanuel: From your position, you had a perfect view of Phanuel's best angle! My Cherubs all agree it's the one that shows off my looks the most, you know.

  Phanuel: But you just stood there with a blank look and didn't react at all. That's why Phanuel had no choice but to do it again!

  - Give a suggestion

    Chief: You're not an idol anymore. Ordinary people don't go out of their way to do things like that.

    Phanuel: Ordinary...? What does that mean?

    Phanuel: Are you hoping Phanuel can bring a little more joy to everyone's lives, Chief? Don't worry, just stay tuned for Phanuel's upcoming special performance!

    Chief: ...

    Phanuel doesn't seem to have understood you at all. Looks like this is about as much help as you can offer her.

### 「Special Giveaway」

You've received a report stating that Phanuel has been repeatedly damaging public and private property.

After reading through the report carefully, you learn that whenever someone shows Phanuel even the slightest bit of kindness, she takes whatever object happens to be nearby and signs it without asking.

You track Phanuel down and explain the situation to her, but it seems her brain really is just wired differently from normal people.

Phanuel: Huh? But Phanuel's autograph brings happiness to everyone, right?

Phanuel: Not to mention, it's an exclusive perk that only Cherubs at the Bureau can get!

- Keep explaining

  You attempt to explain to Phanuel the difference between signing something and damaging property, only to find a stack of handmade handshake vouchers and hug vouchers sitting on her desk.

  Chief: What is all this?!

  Phanuel: It's a new special gift! Phanuel made it to show everyone my gratitude!

  Phanuel: From now on, anyone at the Bureau will get a handshake voucher for smiling at Phanuel. And anyone who helps Phanuel gets a hug voucher! If you want some, Chief, Phanuel can give you a few right now as a special bonus. Just don't tell the others, okay?


  - Confiscate everything

    Chief: Tokens are prohibited within the Bureau. I'll take care of these vouchers for you.

    Chief: And from now on, no signing anything other than your practice book.

    Phanuel: Huh...? Phanuel doesn't quite understand, but if it's Chief's decision, Phanuel will cooperate.

  - Restrict use


  <br>

  Chief: You can keep the vouchers, but you may only sign the vouchers themselves. No more signing other people's belongings, and no forcing anyone to accept the vouchers either.

  Phanuel: Hmm... but that means the world will have fewer and fewer rare collectibles.

  Phanuel: Still, I'm sure everyone will find happiness after getting a voucher that's been personally signed by Phanuel!

  Phanuel's plans have clearly hit a wall. But with the number of related complaints dropping significantly, you're more than satisfied with the outcome.

### 「Heart-Shaped Cookies」

Word has reached you that Phanuel's heart-shaped cookies have put several Sinners out of commission, so you head straight to the scene.

You already knew that Phanuel's supposed "homemade desserts" were all fake. Anyone at the Bureau who's seen her true culinary skills firsthand should know better than to fall for it.

That is, until you see Poffy clutching half a charred cookie in her hand.

- Speak with the victims

  Chief: Poffy, why would you eat a burnt cookie?

  Poffy: ...Chief... I finally... got to try the legendary heart-shaped cookies with a perfect rating... I thought it was... a limited-edition flavor...

  Chief: ...

  After sending Poffy and the other Sinners with upset stomachs to the infirmary, you finally turn your attention to the most conspicuous troublemaker in the crowd.

  To your surprise, however, Phanuel steps up to apologize first.

  Phanuel: Chief, this is all Phanuel's fault! Is everyone okay? Please, let me go and heal them with my smile!

  Phanuel: All of my dearest fans used to be so happy when they got these heart-shaped cookies from Phanuel... They all said they were delicious.

  Could it be that Phanuel chanted the happiness spell too many times while mixing the batter, and it was just too much for everyone to handle?


  - Make her take a class

    Chief: Have you ever tasted these cookies yourself? If you still want to connect with everyone this way, you'd better start by learning how to bake properly.

    Phanuel: Okay! Phanuel will complete the baking course with flying colors, so that I can convey my sweet feelings to everyone!

    After that, Phanuel begins attending baking lessons regularly.

    Her cookies are still bizarrely shaped and indescribable in flavor, and no one has dared to voluntarily try one yet.

    But at the very least, Phanuel now has something she can wholeheartedly dedicate herself to.

  - Suggest taking a class


  <br>

  Chief: Have you ever tasted these cookies yourself? How about starting from the very basics?

  Phanuel: But isn't it enough as long as Phanuel can give love and happiness to everyone? A true Cherub will always receive them.

  Phanuel clearly does not understand what the problem is.

  So, for the sake of everyone's health at the Bureau, you decide to ban Phanuel from sharing any of her baked goods with others.
