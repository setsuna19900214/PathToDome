# Evie Supervision System

### 「Emergency Lighting」

The Bureau has been hit by a sudden power outage, and you find everyone using Mimi-meow light sticks for illumination.

- These are so bright. Thanks, Evie.

### 「Purchase Request」

Evie has submitted a leave request along with a list of daily necessities to purchase. The last item on the list, however, reads "Mimi-meow Limited Edition Cheer Set," highlighted in red three times and written in bold text.

- Approve

### 「Another All-Nighter」

At four in the morning, the Bureau's system suddenly issues an abnormal behavior alert. After fixing three spreadsheets for her colleagues and compiling Mimi-meow's fan engagement data, Evie finally passes out, with the keyboard being her pillow.

- Let her sleep

### 「The Last Red Ginseng」

Receiving red ginseng supplements from Evie during overtime has become a regular part of your routine.

It's past midnight already. You get up to stretch, but Evie is nowhere to be seen. Just as you start to wonder if she has fallen asleep at her desk again, you notice a figure standing at the door to your office.

- Go check

  You open the door to find Evie standing in the hallway, clutching a bottle of red ginseng supplements. It appears as if she's been standing there, hesitating for a while.

  Chief: Why are you standing out here?

  Evie: Oh... Chief. Sorry, did I get in your way?

  Evie: I actually meant to give this to you. You've been working a lot of overtime lately, and your dark circles look worse than they did a few days ago...

  - Decline the supplement

    Chief: Keep it for yourself.

    Evie: Huh? O-okay... I'll do just that then.

    Evie: Truth is, Mimi-meow's limited merch, fan fundraiser, and special live show are all happening in the same month, so I might have... gone a little over budget.

    This is the last bottle of red ginseng I have left. Sob...

    Chief: All the more reason to keep it for yourself.

    By the time you part ways with Evie, you can barely keep your eyes open. Maybe instead of relying on supplements to push through, the two of you should just work less overtime.

### 「Working Remotely」

Even joining the Bureau hasn't quite been able to shake the corporate drone out of Evie.

She still opens her work apps during breaks to fix spreadsheets, coordinate tasks, and write up meeting minutes for her former colleagues. Old habits die hard, it seems.

And you only found out because Evie accidentally sent you a meeting summary instead of the personal information form she meant to fill out.

- Ask for more details

  Chief: You're still doing work for your old company?

  Evie: I'm so sorry, Chief! It's... not really work though. I'm just taking a quick look.

  Evie: My colleague said it was urgent, and it just feels wrong not to help when they ask...

  - Issue a network ban

    Chief: Starting today, your personal terminal is restricted to the Bureau's internal network only.

    Evie: Huh? But won't that cause trouble for others...? But... Well, if you say so, Chief.

    A few days later, having lost access to her external work apps, Evie looks noticeably healthier.

    Before long, though, she starts cautiously asking if there are any work files she can help sort through.

    Getting her to actually relax is going to take a while.

### 「The Courage to Say No」

In order to help Evie learn to say no, you sign her up for a counseling session at the Bureau.

When the instructor tells you Evie never showed up, you're a little puzzled. At the same time, part of you wonders if that counts as her saying no to something for once. Still, you decide to see why she skipped.

- Find Evie

  You find Evie weaving between the Sinners' rooms in the detention area, holding an armful of miscellaneous items.

  Seeing you've come to question her, Evie looks visibly nervous.

  Evie: Ah... I-I'm so sorry, Chief! I really was planning to go. I even cleared out my whole afternoon for it.

  Evie: But someone needed my help, and they said my Mimi-meow charm was cute... so I couldn't say no.


  - Make her attend the session

    If Evie can't even refuse these requests right in front of her, she'll never have the chance to learn how. She needs a push.

    Chief: You have another session tomorrow. Clear your schedule for it, and make sure you go this time.

    Evie: U-understood, Chief!

    The session ends, but it doesn't seem like Evie has learned to say no just yet.

    That said, after seeing you personally order Evie to attend the counselling session, the other Sinners have noticeably cut back on how often they ask her for favors.

  Evie: Th-there's no need for that, Chief! Anyone who appreciates Mimi-meow's charm can't be a bad person, after all. Who knows, maybe they'll end up stanning her someday too!

  Evie: But... if you'd like to watch a Mimi-meow live show with me, Chief, that's a different story!

  I can walk you through everything, starting from her pre-debut days. I promise it won't take up any of your work time... probably!
