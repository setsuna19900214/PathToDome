# Garofano Supervision System

### 「Gossip Master」

The "consultation room" that Garofano set up in the Bureau is extremely popular. If you haven't been there yourself, you'd never guess it's the hottest gossip hub in the Bureau.

- Turns out everyone has a hidden side...

### 「New Year Gift」

As the New Year rolls in, Garofano, with her tailoring magic, whips up patterned knit gloves for everyone in the Bureau. But oddly enough, only yours are without a design.

- What a mysterious person...

### 「Special Tutoring」

You ask Garofano to coach some of our rookie Sinners on the ins and outs of "Going Undercover." Without a moment's hesitation, she is all in, keen on spilling all her secrets and tips to the young guns.

- Thank you!

### 「My Wardrobe」

You bump into Garofano at the cafeteria during lunchtime.

The instant she sees you, she's like, "Hey, come grab a bite with me!"

- Take a seat, and catch up for a bit

  Chief: Garofano, wasn't it "Mrs. Braun" you used as your cover name when you first came around?

  Garofano: Of course, Chief.

  Chief: I'm a bit curious...

  Garofano: Hmm? Got any info you'd like me to look into, Chief? For you, I'd knock 30% off my usual rate.

  Chief: Honestly, I'm just curious. How many fake IDs do you have up your sleeve? How'd you make them so bulletproof?

  Garofano takes a sip of her espresso, giving you a teasing look.

  Garofano: Come on. I was expecting you to pry about Nightingale's hidden crush... or something along those lines.

  Garofano: ...Let's put it this way, Chief, do you count how many clothes you have in your wardrobe?

  Chief: Now that you mention it, I've never really counted... Wait a minute, Nightingale has a crush?!

  Garofano: But every piece of clothing you own is something you cherish, isn't it?

  Chief: Indeed... but Nightingale...

  Garofano: The same principle applies to my identities. But I can't tell you how I obtained them, they are my trade secrets.

  Garofano: But have you ever thought that maybe each of my aliases is inspired by a real person?

  Garofano: Maybe the reason they're so bulletproof is that they've all, at some point, truly lived in a corner of this world.

  Chief: So that's how it is... So Nightingale...

  Garofano: I've finished eating, let's chat more next time, Chief.

  After saying this, she picks up her empty tray and leaves, leaving behind a mysterious silhouette.

### 「Important Person」

Chief: Ugh... my uniform has a tear...

If that button hadn't hit the floor as you stood up, you probably wouldn't spot this tiny hole that seems to come out of nowhere on your outfit.

- Try to fix it

  Maybe you're just too worn out, but you managed to rip another hole in your already torn uniform.

  Chief: This isn't going to work...

  - Call for help

    Chief: With work tomorrow, I'll need to hit up Garofano for some help.

    When you find Garofano, she immediately puts her task on hold and eagerly assists you, giving you a sense of warmth.

    Garofano: As the head of the Bureau, you've got to always mind your image, right?

    Garofano: ...Here, drink this and you'll have a good night's sleep.

    Taking the uniform from you, Garofano doesn't dive straight into the repairs. First, she serves you a piping hot mug of cocoa.

    You cradle the warm drink, sitting on the side, as you watch Garofano carefully patch up the old school uniform, one stitch at a time.

    Chief: Thank you, Garofano.

    Garofano: Chief, got anyone in your life who means the world to you?

    Chief: Well... I guess I do.

    Garofano: Funny enough, as I patch up your outfit, memories of that certain someone come flooding back.

    Garofano: Feels like I was just sewing for them like this, right up until...

    Garofano: Forget it, let's not talk about the past. Here.

    She hands you a uniform that is as shiny and new as if it had never been mended.

    Chief: You've got some serious skills, Garofano.

    Chief: If you ever want to chat, feel free to come to me.

    Garofano: Heh, that's something I should be saying to you, Chief.

### 「Be Careful!」

On a rare, free weekend afternoon, you head to the library hoping to find a good read. There, you unexpectedly bump into Garofano, who's also on the hunt for a book.

The one she's after is on a high shelf, and she's having a bit of a tough time reaching it.

- Go help her

  Chief: Garofano, let me help you...

  To your surprise, because the shelf has seen better days, as you grab that book, a few others topple down from above. One is coming right for your eyes.


  - Protecting Sinners is Chief's responsibility.

    Chief: Watch out, move!

    Without hesitating, you dive in front of Garofano to protect her. But after a soft "whizz," the expected crash never comes.

    When you look, you realize Garofano was quick on her feet, launching a couple of silver needles that pinned the book right back to the shelf.

    Chief: That was close... Thank you, Garofano.

    Garofano: Chief, remember, we're all Sinners. Handling little issues like this is second nature to us.

    Chief: You're right. Sorry, I wasn't thinking.

    Garofano: I wouldn't let my "possessions" get hurt. You can count on that.

    Chief: Uh, possession?

    Garofano: Haha! Forget I said anything. Let's tidy up. We don't want to disturb others trying to read here.

  - Protect yourself


  <br>

  Chief: Watch out! The book is falling!

  Garofano: Leave it to me.

  You instinctively cover your eyes, waiting for the crash. But it never comes.

  When you look again, the tumbling books have been expertly pinned to the shelf with silver needles.

  Chief: Well...

  Garofano: "Like a mama bear protecting her cub?"

  Chief: It's an odd comparison, but kind of spot on.

  Garofano: Alright, I was just kidding. Come on, Chief, let's clean up together so we don't disturb others reading.

  Chief: There's something off about it, but maybe it's best not to overthink it...
