# Famorene·Eirene Supervision System

### 「Free Intel」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

Eirene voluntarily hands a piece of intel over to the Bureau, noting in her message that this time she wants nothing in return.

But a chip offered for free only makes you more wary of what she's really after.

- Hold it now and verify later

### 「Observation Log」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

You discover Eirene has been compiling an observation log about you.

What she records is not your private affairs, but a single question: when crisis comes, do you choose offense or defense?

- Was letting me find this part of the plan too?

### 「Equivalent Exchange」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

During an ordinary conversation, Eirene suddenly throws a question at you.

Famorene·Eirene: Chief, tell me. When a person deliberately bares a weakness to another, what is usually the reason?

Recklessness? Folly? Or a most artful lure?

- Answer the question

  Chief: If you mean yourself, I'd guess it's a lure.

  Famorene·Eirene: How unsparing of you. I thought you might at least offer a few courtesies and grant me a gentler answer.

  Famorene·Eirene: All the same, I do have a rather good deal to propose.

  A piece of intel that will interest you, in exchange for you naming a weakness of your own. What do you say?

  - Refuse

    Chief: I'm not about to accept terms you've laid out.

    Famorene·Eirene: Heh... Very good. Your weakness is indeed not so cheap.

    Famorene·Eirene: Had you taken your seat at this bargaining table, I might have begun to wonder whether I'd overestimated you.

### 「Jaded by Victory」

Location: Training Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Even after the ability assessment test concludes, the researchers are still tensely cross-checking the monitoring data, while Eirene has already settled back into her work emails, unruffled.

But she doesn't seem pleased. If anything, she looks rather weary.

Famorene·Eirene: They logged every reading and even marked every fluctuation range down to the last detail.

Famorene·Eirene: Sadly, every result came out exactly as expected, with nothing worth a second glance. No different from Quinn's annual report.

- Inquire

  Chief: Are you bored?

  Famorene·Eirene: Heh. Power, strength, leverage... once you have enough of them, most so-called rivals become utterly dull.

  Famorene·Eirene: The ending's already decided before the first move is made. A win like that isn't worth much.

  - Warn

    Chief: Even so, you can't go stirring up more trouble for the Bureau just to find some excitement.

    Chief: Everyone's already swamped, and Quinn isn't handing the Bureau's staff an extra year-end bonus.

    Famorene·Eirene: Don't put it so harshly, Chief. I'm just hoping someone might make a move on the board I didn't see coming.

    Famorene·Eirene: If you're so worried about me, then you'd best stop me in time, every single time.

### 「Ledger of Favors」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Eirene begins offering her help whenever Sinners find themselves in trouble, though everyone seems rather displeased by it.

Anyone who accepted her help would soon get a follow-up request: hand in a document, swap a shift, pass along a message...

None of it was particularly major, but no one could tell what trouble it might bring them.

You can't just let Eirene continue unchecked, so you decide to talk to her.

- Begin the conversation

  Chief: Eirene, someone tells me you've been trading in favors around the Bureau lately.

  Famorene·Eirene: A complaint? How surprising.

  Famorene·Eirene: They should've known there'd be a price to pay when they accepted my help.


  - Forbid it outright

    Chief: As of today, you're barred from demanding repayment from others this way.

    Famorene·Eirene: Fine. If those are your rules, I'll play along. But the Bureau has far more usable assets than you think.

    Other temptations will keep them walking right back onto my board.

    After that, Eirene does rein herself in considerably. Still, to guard against her next scheme, you feel your workload has only grown heavier.

  - Outwit her


  <br>

  Chief: Since you're so eager to help everyone, let's make it an official process instead.

  You award Eirene a certificate of helpful service and require that, from now on, all her acts of assistance be formally recorded, with no private compensation accepted.

  Famorene·Eirene: ...A certificate? Chief, do you really intend to turn every private debt into a public commendation with a system so childish?

  Chief: Are you willing to accept a public commendation from the Bureau?

  Famorene·Eirene: Childish, yet effective, I grant you. Your schemes have grown sharper than before.

  The certificate ended up pinned on the bulletin board. From then on, Eirene's "helpful service" has noticeably dwindled.

### 「Trump Card List」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Eirene suddenly tells you that she can help the Bureau draft a few contingency plans for high-risk incidents, and lighten some of your unnecessary workload while she's at it.

It sounds thoughtful enough until you see the document access list she's attached to her request.

It includes not only the detailed files of every S-Class Sinner, but also several top-secret documents and the Bureau's core emergency contingency plans.

- Ask for the reason

  Chief: You sure you need all this just to draft some contingency plans?

  Famorene·Eirene: Of course. How else am I supposed to design a plan worth your while without knowing the Bureau's real trump cards?

  Famorene·Eirene: Chief, you cannot seriously expect me to win you a truly dangerous game with nothing but a handful of public records.


  - Reject the request

    Chief: I'll save you the trouble, then. I think our current plans are enough to get us through any crisis coming our way.

    Chief: What you really want is access to the top-secret files, isn't it?

    Famorene·Eirene: What a shame. It seems I won't get what I want today. Still, it's only a matter of time before I find another way to get everything I need.

    You reject Eirene's request and add her recent document access records to your watch list.

  - Provide a redacted version


  <br>

  Chief: I can give you a redacted version of the documents. If you're really working on a contingency plan, this should be enough.

  Famorene·Eirene: A redacted version? Chief, your sincerity does have its limits.

  Famorene·Eirene: Still, your instinct for classified information isn't bad. Do keep up that vigilance.

  Eirene eventually accepted the redacted documents. A few days later, you receive the new contingency plans she submitted.

  The document is well-organized and genuinely useful, except for one crucial page that's missing, replaced instead by a private dinner invitation.
