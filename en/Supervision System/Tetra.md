# Tetra Supervision System

### 「Hack Attack」

[System Notice] Warning, warning. The Bureau's network has been attacked by a hacker. Entering defense mode. Please handle it quickly.

Chief: Tetra, what's the situation? Nino insisted on joining the countermeasure team. I couldn't persuade her otherwise, so I had to bring her along. I hope it won't affect you.

- Ask about the specific situation

  Nino: Hey, how can you say that? I'm a genius, how can I possibly slow you down? I can handle this alone!

  Tetra: This level of attack is a piece of cake. Though... it seems that the attacker's goal is not what we think.

  Chief: Not what you think? You mean, this is not an attack targeted at the Bureau?

  Tetra: Yes. They aren't trying to disrupt the system, but rather, they're trying to convey something by force...

  Nino: Hey, don't ignore me! And where did you get that idea? I don't see it at all! I think you're overthinking it.

  Chief: Can you analyze what kind of information it is?

  - Question Tetra further

    Tetra: It should be able to be decrypted. "Stealing other people's live stream signals and fanning the flames, you are really something. Here's your gift"...

    Everyone in the room turns to look at Nino at the same time.

    Nino: Oops! I just remembered I've got something to do. Gotta go!

    Chief: It seems we need to give her another lecture on internet use...

### 「Plan Hacker」

Now, monthly update on Sinner Matilda's whereabouts: Recreation Area visits, zero; Outdoor Activity Area visits, zero...

Chief: ...That's the situation. She's all about pre-planned routines and strict adherence. Getting her to pencil in some fun time is a real challenge, and nudging her to switch up her plans on the fly? Near impossible. Tetra, got any ideas on how to tackle this?

- Request for help

  Tetra: Huh... So, you're telling me our job is to swipe some time off the calendar of Miss Perfect Planner? Sounds like a fun little challenge. Let's have a look...

  Tetra: Hmph, obviously. I've got the elite's mindset down to a science. Hit them where it hurts, and you'll see instant results. Anyway, she'll be there for tea with you tomorrow. Just you wait.

  Try as you might to pry into the details of the convincing, Tetra always says a phantom thief's methods are off-limits to you, but she's sure it'll work. So there you are, half-doubting, half-hoping, in the greenhouse, waiting for some kind of magic to happen.
