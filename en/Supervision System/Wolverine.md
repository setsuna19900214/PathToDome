# Wolverine Supervision System

### 「Fitness Goals」

You notice that many of the male staff at MBCC have set their phone wallpapers as pictures of Wolverine. After asking, you find out that they are trying to motivate themselves to bulk up.

- Oh, I see.

  Chief: It should work.

### 「Full Moon Night」

On a full moon night, Wolverine sits alone on the grass in the Outdoor Activity Area, watching the moon.

- Join him

  You sit beside him, and you both silently watch the moon together for the night.

### 「Reward」

Wolverine made great contributions during his mission, so as a reward, you asked the chef to prepare his favorite steak. He is very happy and eats ten pieces in a meal.

- Do you want more?

### 「Survivor」

On your way back to the office, you hear Wolverine's cry from around the corner.

Wolverine: I'm warning you, don't come any closer!!!

- What's going on?!

  You rush around the corner and find him in a standoff with Pippy and a cleaning robot.

  Wolverine: Pippy! Run! I'll stop it!

  Pippy: ...

  Wolverine: I'm not scared! I'm worried about you! What if it eats you?!

  Pippy: ...

  Wolverine: You don't believe me?! You... Ahh!!!

  The robot moves forward a bit, and Wolverine abruptly retreats several steps, bumping into you.

  Wolverine: Why are you here too?

  Chief: Um...

  Wolverine: Don't be afraid! Stand behind me! I'll protect you!

  After that, he pulls you behind him and raises the crossbow in his hand toward the robot, but you push it down.

  Chief: Don't!

  Chief: If it breaks, we'll have to buy a new one!

### 「Birthday cake.」

You hold a cake in front of Wolverine. He sniffs it, then looks up at you with a puzzled expression.

Wolverine: What is this?

- A birthday cake.

  Wolverine: What's a birthday cake?

  - Explain

    Chief: Well... it's kind of a habit or tradition. Everyone eats a birthday cake on their birthday.

    Chief: It's a celebration of growing a year older, and also to hope for a better future.

    Wolverine: Oh...

    Wolverine: But I don't have a birthday.

    - Pat his head

      Chief: I know, so I took the liberty of treating the day you came to MBCC as your birthday.

      Chief: If you don't mind, why don't you give it a try? It should be quite delicious.

      Chief: It's a gift from all of us. It's a blessing for you.

      Wolverine: ...!

      Wolverine: T-thank you...

### 「Balanced Diet」

During lunch, faced with a table full of good food, Wolverine is about to grab it with his hands.

- Sit across from him

  Chief: It's better to use utensils. It's more hygienic.

  Wolverine: ...That's so annoying.

  You hand him the utensils. He looks at you and reluctantly takes them.

  Wolverine: Ribs! Lamb chops! A big chicken leg!

  Chief: Cough cough...

  Wolverine: If you don't feel good, go see a doctor. Why are you watching me eat?

  Chief: Did you forget something?

  Wolverine: ...

  He struggles for a moment, then awkwardly pushes his full bowl of meat towards you.

  Chief: Hmm...

  Wolverine: I don't like people who steal my food. B-but you... you can have some.

  Chief: I'm not trying to steal your food.

  Wolverine: Then what do you want?


  - Give a stern talking to

    Chief: You can't just eat meat, you have to eat more vegetables. Vegetables can provide vitamins and trace elements, lower blood lipids, improve intestinal function, and contribute to health...

    Wolverine: Fine, fine, fine! I'll eat it already! Stop telling me random things!!!

  - Serve him vegetables


  <br>

  You put several spoonfuls of vegetables into his bowl and push it back to him.

  Chief: You can't just eat meat, eat more vegetables for some vitamins.

  Wolverine: Sigh...
