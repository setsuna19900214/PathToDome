      Chief: Poffy, is it because you always serve people so much food that everyone's too scared to eat with you...

      Poffy puts on an expression of deep anguish.

      Poffy: Chief, think about it, the cafeteria staff worked so hard to cook this fragrant rice, and if they saw you doing this... sob...
