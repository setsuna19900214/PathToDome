### 「Imaginative Training」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

One lazy afternoon, Deren lies on the lawn in the outdoor activity area looking at clouds.

- Sit down next to her

  Deren: Chief, you guys have created an impressive simulation here. Look at the sky; it looks uncannily real.

  Chief: It's a real-time simulation of the view outside. The naked eye can't tell the difference.

  She chuckles and points at a cloud directly over your heads.

  Deren: What does that cloud look like to you?

  - Think

    Chief: Hmm... It looks like a man with a pipe in his mouth, and he has a dog on a leash.

    No sooner have you finished describing than the cloud starts changing shape.

    Deren: What does it look like now?

    Chief: The dog... It's becoming a man with a beard... Wait, it's changing back to a dog.

    Deren: Now let your imagination run free and see if you come up with an interesting story according to the changes in the shape of the cloud.

    Chief: Hmm...

    Your mind is a blank; you shake your head.

    Deren: Here is a random example. A great detective who can crack any case, no matter how insolvable it looks, but that's all thanks to his dog.

    Deren: As far as the detective's concerned, his dog is a disheveled middle-aged man who can be called upon at any time to whisper answers in his ear.

    Chief: That's... unorthodox.

    Deren: It's a great imagination exercise. It's fun, isn't it?


    - Indeed

      Chief: You really are an incredible director. You can think up a movie simply by observing a cloud.

      Deren: But if you want to learn more about filmmaking, I'm more than happy to help.
