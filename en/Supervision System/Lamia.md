# Lamia Supervision System

### 「Legend of Sirens」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠

Ever since the swimming pool facilities were opened in the Recreation Area, Lamia often spends the entire day submerged in the pool. She occasionally scares other Sinners who come to swim. After all, it's indeed a bit eerie when she does not move at all.

- Swim occasionally.

### 「Emergency」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area / Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠

During a gathering organized in the Bureau, Lamia habitually triggered her ability, causing several Sinners to experience collective hallucinations and causing chaos. The event was urgently canceled.

- Let her have some alone time

### 「New Eating Champion」

Location: Greenhouse

In your memory, Lamia has always been someone with a small appetite and elegant eating manners, until tonight when you witnessed her single-handedly devour all the fish made in the cafeteria.

### 「Dangerous Pet」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You're at your desk, focusing on handling documents, but after a series of sparks flicker above your head, the Bureau suddenly loses power again. This situation has happened multiple times before.

- Check if it's EMP's doing

  Chief: EMP? Are you in there?

  EMP: No way...

  EMP: My new record...

  After opening the door for you, EMP slumps back into her chair like a lost soul. It seems that her game was interrupted by the power outage.

  Chief: ...You didn't cause the power outage, did you?

  EMP: Of course not! My game didn't even save...

  Just as you were about to ask something else, there's suddenly a strange disturbance in the distance.

  - Where's this noise coming from?

    Following the sound, you find Lamia's room, where strange sparks and lightning are flickering. You turn on your flashlight and rush in... she's battling a giant electric eel you've never seen before.

    Lamia: Damn it... Behave! Stop releasing electricity, I'm warning you!

    Chief: What's wrong, Lamia?! I'm coming!

    Chief: ...What is this?

    Chief: Where did you get this thing?

    Chief: ...Forget it.

    While trying not to provoke the electric eel or Lamia, you manage to separate them.

    After making quite a mess, the electric eel obediently returns to the aquarium.

    Chief: Alright, Lamia, could you tell me what's going on?

    Lamia: The data clearly states that giant electric eels are highly intelligent, so I thought it should obey me, or we could be friends, but I didn't expect...

    - Comfort her

      Chief: It's not a big deal. Look, there are many people like you in the Bureau, perhaps you could interact more with them?

      Lamia: But I...

      Chief: First, go and change your clothes. You're soaked through and you'll catch a cold.

      Later, you help her clean up the mess in her room, and her mood seems to improve a bit.

### 「Specialty」

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Etti's new work, a retro tabletop RPG, has been very popular in the Bureau recently. And for today's game, she invited you to be the game master.

Chief: Let me see, who's playing today... Lamia is also on board? That's great news.

It seems that living in the Bureau has gradually made her less reclusive.

### 「Ocean World」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Lamia seems to have recently taken a liking to watching television. You often see her on the sofa in front of the TV screen in the Recreation Area.

- Go take a look

  Chief: Good afternoon, Lamia. What are you watching?

  Lamia: Good afternoon, Chief.

  Lamia: I'm watching a documentary from the Outland called "World of the Ocean."

  Lamia: It introduces various ocean terrains and marine life, it's really interesting!

  Chief: Oh?

  Lamia: For instance, the documentary mentioned a fish in the ocean that swims very fast and can "fly."

  Lamia: Its fins are large and can open like wings, allowing it to break the surface of the water and glide through the air when it's fast enough!

  Lamia: There's also a jellyfish that's said to be "immortal." It reverts back to its juvenile form when it dies, allowing it to rejuvenate countless times!

  As she talks about these things, Lamia's face is full of animated expressions, and her eyes are sparkling.

  Chief: That does sound interesting, Lamia. You really like the sea, don't you?

  Lamia: Yes, I've always been drawn to that azure world.

  Lamia: It's kind of funny, I've been an "Ocean Messenger" for so long, always talking about the "ocean" to others, but I've never actually seen it with my own eyes.

  Lamia: Do you think... I'll have a chance to see the ocean for myself?


  - It's difficult.

    Chief: I'm sorry, Lamia.

    Chief: The DisCity is nowhere near the sea after all. Fulfilling this wish might be quite difficult...

    Lamia's mood seems to have visibly dropped, but she still manages to give you a smile.

    Lamia: It's okay, Chief.

    Lamia: I was just speaking off the top of my head, you shouldn't worry about it.

  - There will be.


  <br>

  Chief: Lamia, actually, I've never seen the sea either.

  Chief: But that's alright. Even though there's no sea in DisCity, we still have a long future ahead of us.

  Chief: One day, we'll have the opportunity to go to the Outland and witness that azure world with our own eyes.

  Lamia starts to laugh, she seems happier.

  Lamia: Hehe, Chief, it's a promise then. I'll be waiting for you to take me to see the sea.
