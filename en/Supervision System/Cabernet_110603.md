### 「Kinda Nervous」

You've grown accustomed to the messy and noisy environment of the Bureau's cafeteria, but things have started to change since Cabernet's arrival...

Like that table situated right in the center of the restaurant, it's an elegant and luxurious dark wood dining table she brought from home to the Bureau.

Sinner A: Hey, look at that table!

Sinner C: That expensive? ...No wonder hardly anyone dares to eat at that table.

Cabernet: Chief, thank you for approving my request to invite a friend over to prepare today's dinner.

Cabernet: Come, Chief, this seat is reserved for someone as important as you.

Ignis: ...This is my latest masterpiece, Nirvana. It's an innovative dish using poultry, eggs, and several red vegetables. I've put a lot of effort into this dish, so I hope Chief and Miss Cabernet will give it a try!

Chief: It looks quite good. Ignis, I didn't know you had such knife skills.

Maintaining her elegant and polite smile, Cabernet listens to Ignis's long and flowery introduction to her dish.

Chief: Cabernet, no need to rush. At least try a bit of everything first.

- It's very delicious, of course.

  Chief: If you ask me, it's both visually stunning and delicious. Her knife skills are just right, allowing the spices to fully penetrate the poultry. The taste and texture are both distinct and harmonious. I would give this dish a perfect score.

    - Quickly dissuade

### 「Don't Eat Me」

For some reason, you haven't received any dining invitations from Cabernet in the past few days.

Although this greatly reduces your mental burden, it's indeed unusual. Driven by professional spirit, you decide to investigate.

Chief: Supervision System, please help me locate Cabernet.

Chief: ...?!

Chief: What's going on?

The Supervision System shows you Cabernet's location on the screen, she's in Kelvin's room.

She has her back towards the camera, seemingly cornering Kelvin.

You rush to the scene and find Kelvin trembling in the shadows.

Kelvin: Please, don't eat me...

Cabernet: Ah, dear little chef, your cooking leaves much to be desired, it is quite a dilemma.

Cabernet: Although the food is poor, it seems your taste is... rather good. Come, let me have a taste, just a bite...

### 「Scent of the Soul」

Due to a large number of Sinners being out on assignments, the cafeteria is unusually quiet tonight.

However, Cabernet is very satisfied with this situation. Unable to refuse her warm invitation, you are having dinner with her.

Cabernet: What a lovely evening, Chief.

Cabernet: Having you dine with me is truly an honor.

Chief: But haven't we been eating together these past few days?

Cabernet: Hehe, but not every day can we dine in such peace.

Chief: That's true...

You see Cabernet examining you again. This isn't the first time, but she seems particularly interested in you today.

Cabernet: Chief... may I say something?

Chief: Yes?

Cabernet: Chief, I smell something on you that doesn't belong to you.

Cabernet: Anxiety, shampoo, coffee, hand soap... and bleach.

Cabernet: These smells are quite unpleasant and don't suit you. Please, come with me.

She decisively walks away from the table, leaving behind a table full of dinner, and takes you to her room.

Cabernet takes out from a small box two small bottles with gorgeous decorations and exquisite craftsmanship.

Cabernet: Chief, allow me to present you with a gift.

Cabernet: Please choose one.
