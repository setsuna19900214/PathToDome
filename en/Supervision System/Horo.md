### 「Old Clothes」

The Legion sent a package to Horo. The package's been examined for security.

Chief: Horo, do you have a moment?

  Chief: The Legion sent a package and said it was something you forgot there. They thought you might need it, so they sent it to us.

  Horo: Oh, okay. Thank you.

  Chief: No problem. I'll be on my way then.

  Horo: What's the rush? You are, in a way, a Legion member as well. Stay here and see what's inside.

  Horo: I reckon it's Boss Zoya's. Well, let's open it and find out.

  - This is...

    Chief: It's cute.
