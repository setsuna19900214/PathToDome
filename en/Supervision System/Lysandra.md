### 「Nocturnal Animal」

Urgency: ⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

The lights in the room of Sinner Lysandra have been detected off for 72 hours. Go and take a look?

- Yes

  You enter Lysandra's room. It is pitch-black inside.

  - Look around

    You barely take a step before feeling a grip tighten around your neck. In an instant, you're thrown to the ground.

    Lysandra: What are you sneaking around for in my room?

    Chief: Cough... cough cough... I noticed you haven't turned on your lights for days, so I came to check if you're alright.

    Lysandra: I don't like using lights. Darkness helps me think clearly.

    Chief: Is it because you're used to tombs? If these electric lights are too harsh, they could be replaced with something softer.

    Chief: We should still have some light. It's so dark in here, I can't even see your face.

    Lysandra: You want to see my face? That's easy enough, I have light.

    Chief: Yes?

    The moment she finishes speaking, a blinding white light flashes in front of you. You immediately shield your eyes.

    Chief: What the—!

    Lysandra: Clear enough for you now?

    Chief: Crystal clear. Though the brightness is making my eyes water.

    Lysandra: That's easy to fix too. I have medicine. Wait here.

    Lysandra turns off the flashlight at your suggestion. In the darkness, you hear the rustling sounds of her rummaging through her belongings.

    Chief: What kind of medicine do you have?

    Lysandra: A Fraser folk remedy. Made from dried sandworms ground into powder. Works much better than anything from your DisCity.

    Chief: ...Thanks! My eyes suddenly don't hurt anymore!
