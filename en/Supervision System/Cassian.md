# Cassian Supervision System

### 「Wine Cellar Request」

Impressed with the Bureau's subterranean environment, Cassian wants to build a wine cellar to store his private brews.

- Too costly, and poses a security risk. Denied.

  Considering the special nature of Cassian's work, you approve a mini-fridge for his room to store his prized vintages.

### 「Reluctant Pianist」

After much persuasion, Cassian finally agrees with just one condition: 24-hour access to the piano room.

- Approve

  Cassian: It's been years since I last played; my hands are rusty. I don't want to let you down, so I'll need some practice.

### 「Drunken Deeds」

Many at the Bureau are wary of Cassian. The rumor is that he knows everyone's most embarrassing drunken moments. The list includes, but is not limited to: vomiting, tearful calls to an ex, impromptu pole dancing, and bellowing folk ballads...

- Ask if the rumor is true

  Cassian: Whether it's true or not depends on how much you trust me, Chief.

### 「Memories in a Glass」

At the party, Levy leans against the bar, watching Cassian work.

Levy: I remember you. At those high-society charity galas, you were the kid they'd call over to show off your mixing magic.

Levy: Getting one of your cocktails wasn't easy back then. The nobles kept calling you a "genius mixologist" and tossing coins in your glass. They ruined so much good liquor... Tsk.

Cassian: If you'd walked up and asked for a drink, I would have made you one.

Levy: I knew better. I could see it in your eyes. You were mentally cursing them with every shake of the shaker, all the way back to their great-great-grandparents.

Cassian: ...

Chief: Fancy seeing you two chatting.

Levy: Hey there! Just asking Cassian to mix me a drink. You want one, Chief?

- Can I?

  Chief: Can I?

  Cassian: ...Of course.

  Cassian: Now, I'm more than happy to make drinks for my... "colleagues."

### 「Drink Promotion」

With alcohol sales in a slump, Faye has organized the biggest drink promotion in Bureau history to boost business: a drinking championship.

The last one standing gets the tab on the house. Once the fliers go out, the Sinners are all ready for a fight.

Sinner A: Back in the day, I drank an entire gang under the table. This is child's play.

Sinner B: I didn't spend all those years hustling on the streets for nothing.

Sinner C: Damn right! Where's the champ? Time to show 'em what an iron liver looks like!

By the time you arrive on the scene, the floor is littered with drunken bodies. The only one still standing is Cassian, alone on the stage.

- Express doubt

  Chief: You drank all of them under the table? By yourself?

  Cassian seems a bit embarrassed and just nods. Faye, who's hosting the event, jumps in to explain.

  Faye: Oh, I just dragged him here to help with the promotion! Look at all these drink orders, we've made a fortune! I mean, your Supply Office made a fortune!

  Chief: I had no idea you could hold your liquor so well!

  Cassian: Indeed. From now on, whenever you feel like drinking, Chief, you'll always have company.

### 「Mood Special」

Night has fallen. After a dreary day of paperwork, you decide to unwind at the Bureau's new bar.

- Say hello

  Cassian: Good evening, Chief. You've had a long day. What can I get for you?

  Chief: The usual. One Mood Special?

  Chief: Need me to come closer? It's pretty dark in here. Can you read my mood?

  Cassian: Clear as day...

  He studies you intently for a moment before turning away. Even the simple act of selecting bottles is done with unhurried, mesmerizing grace.

  Cassian: You look worn out. Have you not been getting enough rest?

  Chief: That obvious?

  Cassian: These two blends come to mind. Which one will it be?


  - Something to help me forget it all for a while.

    Cassian: As you wish.

    Cassian strains the clear liquid into a glass, garnishing the rim with mint, salt, and crushed ice. He slides the drink over to you.

    Cassian: Enjoy.

    That night, you sleep without dreams. You wake up refreshed, the troubles of yesterday gone. You're ready for a new day.

  - The courage to stay sharp and keep going.


  <br>

  Cassian: An excellent choice.

  Cassian combines vodka, coffee liqueur, and crushed ice, shakes the mixture, then pours it out. A burning cinnamon stick rests on the rim of the glass. He slides the drink over to you.

  Cassian: Enjoy.

  You down the glass in one go.

  Instantly, your mind clears. Ice and fire course through your veins, igniting a passionate will to fight on. You know that not even death stops a workaholic from clocking in, let alone a little buzz.
