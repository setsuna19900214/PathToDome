# Lichen Supervision System

### 「Snack Supply」

Every time you run into Lichen, you end up with a few snacks. You usually get apples and oranges, but sometimes it'll be nuts and preserved fruits carefully wrapped in little red pouches.

- These aren't... ritual offerings, are they...?

### 「Divine Will」

You notice that whenever Lichen appears in the elevator lobby, the other Sinners automatically line up behind her. The elevator she chooses is always the first to arrive.

- This is divine will.

### 「Astrology Platform」

Lichen seeks you out and formally requests permission to use the Bureau's Astrology Platform.

Lichen: Young friend... from a recent divination, I saw that your fate is obscured... I fear you are to face a great calamity soon... Thus, I wish to borrow the Astrology Platform, so that I may unravel this mystery.

- Ask for details

  Chief: Astrology Platform? Does the Bureau even have one of those?

  Lichen: Hm... Utilizing an Astrology Platform requires exceptional talent, the capacity to bear celestial secrets, to be exact... I have surveyed the northeast corner of the Bureau... and located several ideal locations for stargazing, perfectly suited to my needs...

  Lichen: If you would be willing to accompany me, young friend, that would be most ideal...

  - Agree

    You still have no idea what Lichen is talking about, but accompanying her is better than letting her wander around the Bureau unsupervised.

    It isn't until Lichen stops in front of the lab's latest high-end research algorithm terminal and reaches out to touch its chassis that you finally understand what she was talking about.

    Lichen: Just as I suspected... The spiritual energy here is pure and untainted... This is the Astrology Platform, where the forces of heaven and earth converge...

    Chief: This is a data algorithm terminal. Uh... in other words... a computer.

    Chief: Let me apply for access to a high-end computer for you.

    Lichen: I am most grateful... The heaven and earth bear witness to your kindness, young friend...

### 「Eastian Mysticism Research」

Once again, you head to the research department to assist Bureau researchers with the project "The Scientific Basis of Eastian Mysticism."

Upon entering, you find the researchers gathering around a replica of the Imperial Bell, visibly vexed.

- Find out what's wrong

  Lichen: Young friend... what might it be that troubles you so? Would you care to tell me what weighs on your mind...?

  Chief: I'm not the one troubled. Rather, the researchers want to know why their replica of the Imperial Bell—faithfully recreated down to every detail of its physical composition—doesn't seem to work as well as yours.

  Lichen: In the Daoist arts, the chime of a bell... serves as a conduit between heaven and earth... it is a power that dispels evil and impurity... Yet, in accordance with the natural order... different cultures have emerged... Perhaps... this would prove more worthwhile...

  - Relay her suggestion

    You relay Lichen's words verbatim, then present the researchers with a decorative jingle bell typically used during the winter holidays. Their despair is palpable.

    Chief: Maybe you could also try leaving some offerings for the Imperial Bell, just as they do in Eastian mysticism?

    Researcher: But that would be completely unscientific... Is there really no other way?

    Lichen: Perhaps science and mysticism... share the same origin after all... For which of the two is true and an illusion, only the heavens may know.

### 「Five-in-a-row with Skills」

A bizarre trend has swept through the Bureau lately.

Every Sinner with an interest in board games has become obsessed with Five-in-a-row—worryingly so. Initially, you thought it was just a harmless revival of a classic pastime, until you walked past the Recreation Area today...

Sinner A: Sandstorm! Now I get to move one of your pieces—you're finished!

Sinner B: What?! Then I'll use Mountain Mover to reset the game! I'm black, so I go first.

- Approach and ask

  Chief: What the... Is this how people play Five-in-a-row now?

  Lichen: Young friend... The art of Five-in-a-row holds wonders of its own. How can one know its joys without indulging in the game? Come... let us play a round...

  Chief: You mean that version of Five-in-a-row from just now?

  Lichen: Worry not, young friend... I shall guide you through the basics... For this first game, I will not act until you play three pieces.


  - Agree to play

    Even with a lead of three pieces, you still struggle against a Five-in-a-row master like Lichen. Not to mention the absurd skills that keep getting in the way of your victory.

    But as you finally get the hang of the rules, the head start also begins tipping the scales in your favor.

    Chief: Four in a row. This round is mine.

    Lichen: I believe you have a saying that goes... "it's not over until the fat lady sings..."

    Lichen has already used up all her skills. She couldn't possibly have any other tricks up her sleeve. But just as you're about to close out the game, a shout rings out from behind you, followed by a game board flying through the air—landing squarely on your pieces and scattering your perfect setup.

    Chief: Lichen... Was this part of your plan too?

    Lichen: No... but it seems the heavens willed it...

  - Keep pushing your luck


  <br>

  Chief: How about letting me place four pieces at the start?

  You make the outrageous request as a joke, but to your surprise, Lichen nods in agreement. With four black stones to start, victory seems all but guaranteed—yet Lichen simply smiles and forms a hand seal.

  Lichen: Yin and yang, reversed... Young friend, you now play white...

  Chief: Huh? How's that fair?

  Lichen: The art of exchanging turns can only be used at the opening. Young friend, you shall find that this next game is not so easy...

### 「Daily Divination」

You notice that lately, Sinners have developed the habit of seeking out Lichen for a divination first thing after waking up.

Rumor has it that Lichen has her own rules for divination. She doesn't answer specific questions, but instead offers vague instructions—from advising Sinners on whether today is suitable for exercise, to what color of clothes to wear, and other such trivial matters.

Everyone who receives her guidance goes on to have a smooth and pleasant day. And so, you decide to test out this mystical Eastian divination for yourself.

Lichen: Young friend... I have expected your arrival...

- Explain why you're here

  Chief: I'd like a reading too, if you don't mind. Could you divine something for me?

  Lichen: Hmm... I offer nothing beyond the little things of the day...

  Lichen: However, young friend... You shall know there is no need to visit the cafeteria for your meals today.


  - Press for a reason

    Chief: Why? Is something going to happen at the cafeteria again?

    You keep pressing, your eyes brimming with the fervor of someone determined to nip trouble in the bud. Left with little choice, Lichen relents and offers you another hint.

    Lichen: This concerns none but you, young friend... You are favored by food on this day... Travel as you please, for fine delicacies shall find their way to you... that is all the heavens have revealed.

    Chief: I think I understand now... So, where would you like to eat?

    Lichen: ...The greenhouse offers a most wonderful view in the afternoon. Perhaps that will prove to be an ideal location, young friend.

  - Trust her advice


  <br>

  Though you have no idea what Lichen is really up to, you decide to take her word for it this time.

  But mealtime comes and goes, yet the so-called destined meal does not find its way to you. You remain in your office, so hungry you're getting dizzy.

  Chief: Haha, who knew even Lichen could be wrong like this?

  Lichen: Apologies for the wait, young friend.

  Chief: So... hungry...

  Lichen: The heavens often reveal naught but the shape of things... Yet, the whims of the human heart leave no trace to follow. Young friend, do heed my advice and rely less on divine will... here, this Eastian cuisine should satisfy your hunger...
