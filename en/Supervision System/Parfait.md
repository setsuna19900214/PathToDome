# Parfait Supervision System

### 「Fickle Heart」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

Parfait has been hosting dinner parties late at night in her room, prompting other Sinners to file noise complaints.

- Talk to Parfait

  Parfait offers you a sincere apology and promises to hold her parties during the day from now on.

### 「Market Risks」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

There's always a long line of people outside Parfait's room.

- Investigate

  They're all Sinners struggling with the stock market, coming to ask Parfait for advice.

### 「Parfait's Lunch」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You walk past the cafeteria and see Parfait having a meal.

- Approach

  Parfait: Oh, Chief! Your timing is absolutely perfect. I was just wondering if you might honor me with your company for lunch?

  Parfait: Please forgive me for not extending an invitation in advance. The weather looked so lovely today that I was suddenly inspired, so I asked the kitchen to prepare four exotic dishes.

  Chief: Really? Our cafeteria managed to whip up gourmet cuisine?

  Parfait: My dear friend, you just need to sit down and try a bite—then you'll see I'm not making this up.

  - Sit down skeptically

    Chief: This is... hash browns, steamed fish, greens, chocolate brownies...

    Chief: Isn't this just today's cafeteria menu?

    Parfait: Not only that, I specifically asked them to serve it using the classical ceramic tableware I brought from home.

    Chief: So in other words, you just put cafeteria food on fancy plates, and the portions are half the size?

    Parfait: Exactly. You see, what determines quality of life isn't wealth, but attitude.

    Parfait: Even the simplest food can provide a premium experience.

    Parfait: So then, my friend, what's your take on today's cuisine?

    Chief: Hmm... I think this could sell to Eastsiders.

### 「Misvalued Goods」

Location: Training Room / Outdoor Activity Area / Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You pass by Parfait and notice she's carrying a rather stylish canvas bag.

- Compliment

  Chief: Good morning, Parfait. That's a nice-looking bag you have there.

  Parfait: Thank you, Chief. Once again, I must commend your impeccable taste.

  Parfait: This is a lovely piece I just picked out at Miss Faye's shop. Here, feel it—the material is sturdy, and the craftsmanship is exquisite.

  Parfait: Oh, by the way, this was a freebie. If you like it, please take it.

  Chief: Oh, thank you... Wait, how is this... Parfait, Parfait!

  Parfait: What's wrong my friend? Don't you like it?

  Chief: I think you might have gotten things mixed up. Isn't what you gave me the shop's bestselling screaming duck?

  Chief: Now that you mention it, the bag you're holding seems to be the actual freebie, right?

  Parfait: Haha, as far as I'm concerned, the one I didn't take a fancy to is the real freebie.

  Parfait: Please just take it. It would be perfect to brighten up your office.

### 「Parfait's Gift」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Today is the day the shop gets its monthly new arrivals. You decide to go take a look and happen to run into Parfait, who looks somewhat troubled.

- Inquire

  Chief: What's wrong? Did someone try to push another sales pitch on you?

  Parfait: Oh, my dear friend, quite the opposite.

  Parfait: I've received so much care from everyone during my time here, so I wanted to buy some gifts for my friends at the Bureau.

  Parfait: But your Supply Office has so many wonderful things, I'm having trouble choosing between them all.

  Parfait: After all, you know that in Miss Faye's opinion, everything is wonderful.

  Chief: She would probably want you to buy one of everything.

  Parfait: I've always trusted your judgment, so how about you help me choose?

  Parfait raises her hands, showing you the items she's holding in each hand.

  In her left hand, she holds a cashmere scarf with an exceptionally soft texture, free of unnecessary patterns—dignified and understated.

  On her right wrist, she wears a gold bracelet with embedded gems that sparkle brilliantly, just like Parfait herself.


  - Choose what you think is suitable as a gift

    Chief: I think the scarf would be very suitable as a gift.

    Parfait: Hehe, I just knew you'd choose that one. I even suspect you might have one exactly like it.

    Parfait: However, my friend, don't you think this scarf's color and style are a bit too ordinary?

    Chief: You can't go wrong with this style.

    Chief: Besides, scarves are more practical too. Even if the recipient doesn't love this gift, they at least won't hate it.

    Parfait: As expected of you, your thinking is very thorough. Speaking of which...

    Parfait drapes the scarf over your shoulders.

    Parfait: Just as I thought, this scarf fits you so perfectly, it looks like it was tailor-made for you.

    Parfait: That's settled then! This one will be your gift.

  - Choose what you think Parfait would give


  <br>

  Chief: I think this bracelet on the right is quite nice.

  Parfait: Oh? How interesting, this really isn't at all what you usually prefer.

  Chief: I'm not picking a gift for myself. I just think this bracelet really suits the impression you give people.

  Chief: This way, even if you don't see each other again afterward, whoever receives this gift will think of you, Parfait, whenever they see this bracelet.

  Parfait: I hadn't thought of it that way. That's rather touching to hear.

  Parfait removes the bracelet and puts it on your hand.

  Parfait: I had thought you certainly wouldn't like such flashy jewelry, but since you put it that way...

  Parfait: Here's to our eternal friendship!

### 「Planning Expert」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

The Bureau is preparing a team-building party, and Parfait volunteered to help with the planning. She comes to your office to discuss the details.

- Take Parfait's Event Proposal

  Chief: Our party is three hours long, so why is there only... one sheet of paper here? And it's not even double-sided.

  Parfait: The greatest banquets often require only the simplest of plans—as long as you find the right professionals.

  Parfait: Take a closer look, my friend, every person I've arranged for you is among the most renowned experts in their respective fields.

  Chief: A fireworks show opening, followed by a ballet performance, exotic animal acts... and you're seriously planning to invite the entire Eastside Philharmonic Orchestra?

  Chief: Our Bureau simply can't accommodate that many people.

  Chief: And that's not even the main issue—the Bureau has confidentiality protocols. You can't bring in that many outside personnel.

  Parfait: Don't worry, I plan to charter a cruise ship and hold it there. Publicly, we'll just say it's my private dinner party.

  Chief: ...Parfait, I'm afraid I have to reject most of what's in your proposal.

  Chief: This is an internal event. We can't invite outsiders. All the performances should be done by our own staff and Sinners.

  Chief: And you've planned way too many performances, so we'll need to cut some of them.

  Chief: The symphony is definitely out, and the fireworks show... that's a safety hazard. Nightingale would never approve of it.

  Chief: Also, we've only allocated time for one musical performance this time.

  Chief: So between dance and music, we can only keep one.


  - Keep the music program

    Chief: Let's go with a music performance then. We have quite a few Sinners here who love music.

    Parfait: Oh? Do we have Sinners who are proficient in orchestral music?

    Chief: Depends on whether you consider the suona as an orchestral instrument.

    Chief: Besides that, we also have people who do rap, punk rock, and ASMR... What's going through your mind right now?

    Parfait: I'm wondering... if I want to hear beautiful string solos at the party, would I have enough time to master an instrument myself through cramming and practice?

  - Keep the dance program


  <br>

  Chief: How about we try a dance performance instead? We have several dancers among our Sinners.

  Parfait: Do we have anyone who can perform Swan Lake?

  Chief: We have a Sinner here named Hamel who's a top-tier dancer.

  Parfait: Hmm... one person isn't enough.

  Chief: Well, we also have people who can do lion dance, and serpent dance...

  Parfait: So you have all these talented individuals here! Performing Swan Lake would be such a waste of their unique abilities. How about... we change it to Animal World instead?
