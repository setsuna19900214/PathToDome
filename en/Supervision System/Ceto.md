### 「The Old Man and the Sea」

Ceto seems entranced by a film. On screen, towering waves merge with the night, the pitch black pressing against a grayish fishing boat that seems on the verge of destruction. An emaciated old man stands ramrod straight on the deck.

- Watch with her

  You take a seat nearby and quietly watch with her. It's an old film that most young people would find tedious. You and Ceto are the only two viewers, spending two hours together in peaceful silence.

  During the film, Ceto's brows alternately furrow and relax, her concern for the protagonist's fate evident. It's rare to see her so emotionally affected in daily life.

  The film ends. Ceto's expression turns crestfallen.

  Ceto: So he just brought back a skeleton? All that effort for nothing. Total defeat.


  - I think it was a victory.

    Chief: He succeeded in catching the fish. The fact that his prize was stolen afterwards is a separate matter.

    Ceto: He was left with nothing but his worthless old life.

    Chief: Partial success is still success. If your prize gets stolen, you can always take it back next time.

    Ceto: How has a ridiculous optimist like you managed to survive in this world for so long?

    Ceto: No... it's actually people like you who deserve to survive...

    You're not sure if it's your imagination, but Ceto seems to smile briefly.

  - Some would consider that a victory.


  <br>

  Chief: The boy still looked forward to going fishing with him. At least in the boy's eyes, he was successful.

  Ceto: And then what? Let that kid become another version of him? Another fool fighting a losing battle against the sea?
