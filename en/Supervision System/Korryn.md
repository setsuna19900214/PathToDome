### 「Split-Second Precision」

Location: Energy Furnace

Urgency: ⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Korryn is waiting outside your office. Permit entry?

- Yes

  Korryn: Good morning, afternoon, and evening to you, my dear Chief!

  Korryn: What a waste to spend your prime years cooped up in an office. Just looking at you makes me feel suffocated!

  Korryn leans against your desk, her gaze unwaveringly fixed on you.

  Korryn: Hey, why not hang out with me? We could drive around, grab some ice cream, and see where the day takes us... What do you say?

  Bzzz bzzz... The terminal on your desk starts to vibrate.

  - Check

    You bow your head to inspect the vibrating terminal, but Korryn, unsatisfied, cups your face in her hands and steers your gaze back to her.

    Korryn: Eyes on me, sweetheart. I'm still talking to you!

    Korryn: Here, look into my eyes—

    Chief: Stop it. I've got a message to check...

    Disregarding your protest, Korryn tilts her head to listen to the sound of two groups of footsteps passing each other outside the door, which then gradually fade into the distance.

    Korryn: Alright—all clear now! Go ahead and check your message!

    She springs up joyfully and walks away from your side.

    Korryn: Your help is appreciated! See you around, my dear Chief!

    Chief: What exactly was that all about...?

    After the unexpected move, Korryn strides away. In confusion, you pick up your terminal to check the message.

    PUBLIC NOTICE:

    At 13:22 today, Miss Korryn staged a magic performance in the Greenhouse District, managing to trick 33 individuals into surrendering their helmets. These items have yet to be returned.

    The Bureau has dispatched two security teams to investigate. If you see Miss Korryn or the helmets mentioned above, please contact us promptly.

    Chief: ...I see.

    - Contact Security

      You dial Security's terminal number, ready to share some good news.

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠

"Sheriff": "Hello there, esteemed and generous MBCC Chief!"

In front of a dining tray, a bright-eyed reptile flicks out its tongue to lick its eyes.

"Sheriff": "Hmm... Now that I'm done buttering you up, can I be real with you?"

- Please, go ahead.

  "Sheriff": "Honestly, the food here is pretty good, but the drinks could use some work..."

  Korryn cradles Sheriff in her hands, maneuvering its little front legs to execute a miniature salute.

  "Sheriff": "In my humble opinion, you should serve something more authentic, more pure... something us desert bandits really dig..."

  Korryn: That's right, something deliciously tempting!

  Chief: Unfortunately, the Bureau won't authorize the diner to serve alcohol.

  Korryn: What? No, no, no—I never touch that stuff!

  Korryn squints her eyes and scrunches up her nose like a cat.

  Korryn: Can't be a sharpshooter if you're wasted! For me, milk will always reign as the best drink ever!

  Korryn: Milk tastes good, looks good, and it's good for health! Get me some better milk, and who knows? I might even grow a bit taller!

  With each word uttered, Sheriff's glossy eyes inch a little closer to you.

  - Hold on.

    Chief: Hold on, are you suggesting there's an issue with our milk? We've always relied on a trustworthy food company for our supplies. That shouldn't be...

    Korryn: The milk here is clearly off. Can't you taste the difference?

    Korryn: Look at it—

    A glass of milk is pushed to the center of the table.

    Korryn: It's not translucent, the taste is way too rich, and it even leaves a film on the glass when you swirl it... Are you sure you didn't get scammed? Real milk isn't supposed to be like this at all!

    Chief: Translucent and watery...

    Chief: Well... Is it possible that all the milk you've had before was actually watered down?

    Korryn: Watered... down?

    Korryn: N-no way...

    "Sheriff": (Licks its eyes)

    Korryn: ...

    "Sheriff": (Licks its eyes again)

### 「WhiteSands Survival Code」

Location: Training Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Right on the bullseye, a perfect shot!

Korryn beams with excitement, patting her rifle's stock as if praising a puppy.

Korryn: That's my girl! Packing a punch, aren't you?

- Praise

  Chief: You're doing great on your training scores.

  Korryn: I might not be the best shooter here, but back in Tuco, I was one of the top marksmen around!

  Korryn: But... all this sudden praise—could it be that you want me to school you in some shooting techniques, little Chief?


  - I'm just passing by.

    Chief: Actually, I just happen to be passing by.

    Chief: I'm here to assess the defense team's latest shooting practice outcomes.

    Korryn: How blunt of you, Chief! Can't you at least play along a bit with me?

    Chief: ...

    Chief: Alright, alright. I'm very impressed by your shooting practice. Would you mind sharing your training methods?

    Korryn: Oh, sure thing—I'd love to!

    Korryn: Let's kick things off with aiming. Don't underestimate it—aiming is the link between guns, shooters, and targets. It's the cornerstone of any outlaw's life...

  - You got me there.


  <br>

  Chief: Many people are curious about your flawless accuracy. I'm here on their behalf.

  Korryn: Wow, didn't realize I was such a big deal! Should I hold an autograph session while we're at it?

  Korryn sits beside you, placing her hunting rifle across her lap as she cleans the powder residue from the muzzle with a soft cloth.

  Korryn: Alrighty then! Let this expert markswoman drop some shooting knowledge on you all!

  Korryn: But I'll make one thing clear first: I can guide you, but your success is on yourself. Just copying my movements won't get you anywhere.

  Chief: Could you elaborate on that?

  Korryn: Sure, let me break it down for you. Shooting is all about aiming, and aiming is all about locking your eyes on the target.

  Korryn: In the swirling winds of WhiteSands, clarity is a luxury. But for anyone aiming to make it out alive, they've got to see—see their target, their objective, their sights, all crystal clear.

  Korryn: You gotta see it so vividly that it's seared into your eyes. And when the time's right, squeeze that trigger... Bang!

### 「Sandstorm Horror」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Korryn: Wow, that was intense! Horror movies are definitely meant for late-night viewing!

Movie nights are always a hit, but when horror films pop up on the schedule, a bunch of folks can be seen sneaking away from the Recreation Area with their shoulders hunched.

- Do you enjoy this?

  Chief: Do you enjoy this type of movie?

  Korryn: Haha, I enjoy anything that gets my blood pumping!

  Korryn: Driving, adventures, shooting, horror stories... Oh, yeah! That jogs my memory about something...

  Korryn: One time I was out for a drive when I ran into some out-of-town raiders. We had a little... friendly exchange of fire, and believe it or not, we ended up hitting it off. They told me this scary story later...

  Chief: It's like you just strung all your favorite things together into one convenient story...

  Korryn: Hey! Quit with the suspicion, you bureaucrat! Life is full of surprises and coincidences—just roll with it!


  - Listen to her story

    Chief: Perhaps you could share this story with me.

    Korryn: Well, well, looks like someone's curious! You better listen up, then!

    Korryn: Ahem... So, this story begins on a night so quiet, even the silence had a weight to it...

    Korryn: A young guy was out delivering in the deserted wilds of WhiteSands, all by his lonesome. He zipped along on his motorcycle, soaking in that chilly night air...

    Korryn: Out of the blue, he caught an odd sound, a faint rustling that sounded like... someone was crying!

    Korryn: ...

    Korryn goes quiet, locking eyes with you in a piercing gaze.

    Chief: Why did you stop?

    Korryn: Oh, come on... You're no fun at all.

    Korryn steps back, the intentionally enigmatic look on her face disappearing completely.

    Korryn: You aren't scared of any of this, huh, Chief? Not even a smidgen of fear showing on your face!

  - Express doubt


  <br>

  Chief: Either way, the credibility of the storyteller seems quite sketchy...

  Korryn: Oh... So our dear Chief has no trust in me. Ah, of course, I'm just a lousy swindler, aren't I? Totally understandable that you wouldn't buy a word of my story...

  Korryn: And there I was, baring my soul to you... Sob...

  Korryn lowers her head, her eyes shimmering with what seems like tears, her shoulders subtly quivering...

  Chief: ...

  Chief: You should know by now this won't work on me.

  Korryn: ...Tch, you're too sharp! This move usually works like magic!
