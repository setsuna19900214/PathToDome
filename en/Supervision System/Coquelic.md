# Coquelic Supervision System

### 「An Unlikely Savior」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠

While perusing the Sinners' behavior analysis report today as a matter of routine, you find that the places Yao has frequented this month differ from those in the previous months.

Chief: Why has she suddenly taken a liking to the greenhouse? She used to avoid it, worried about her effect on the growth of plants.

- Go to the greenhouse to find Yao

  Coquelic: These few are in much better shape now. They should be able to bloom when the time comes. You've been a great help. Thanks!

  Yao: You're very welcome. I thought I would never get the chance to appreciate flowers up close. I never imagined such a peculiar plant existed. I hope you'll invite me back when it blooms.

  Coquelic: Of course I will. Anyone who appreciates the beauty of flowers is welcome to my garden.

  Chief: Is that... Coquelic? She seems to be getting along with Yao. Is she the reason why Yao has been frequenting the greenhouse lately?

  Chief: Yao, Coquelic, what are you two doing here? This is the cactus that was delivered here last month... Does it bloom?

  - Approach and ask

    Yao: Yes. I heard this peculiar plant doesn't need a lot of water but needs plenty of sunlight, which means I can get close without killing it, so I thought I'd come by and tend to it.

    Coquelic: Tend to it? You saved it. Different plants need different conditions to grow and bloom. The fellows who delivered this cactus knew nothing about plants. They delivered it while the sunlight simulator was being serviced, and without the right conditions, it couldn't grow.

    Chief: I see... That's why you have been spending so much time in the greenhouse lately. You're here to provide heat and light for the plant, right?

    Yao: Yes. I'd thought my presence would cause the plants and their flowers to wither. But to my surprise, they thrived with less water. It's strange.

    Coquelic: There is no lack of water and fertilizer around here. Some "botanists" come almost every day to water them. Thanks to their loving care, these plants have been "drowning." You help evaporate some of the water. I believe they'll grow better now.

    Chief: I see... Yao, I guess you don't have to give the greenhouse a wide berth anymore, right? You can come here more often to relax.

### 「"Trump Card"」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Chief: Coquelic, I have a list of Sinners who might be related to the Garden, could you please—

Coquelic: Hmm?

After a great deal of effort, you manage to escape her maze of fresh flowers, only to find Coquelic lazily sprawled on her bed, fiddling with a foreign language book, looking quite laid-back.

Coquelic: Please pour me a cup of tea.

Chief: Check the list for me, will you?

Coquelic: The tea.

Chief: ...

You sigh, getting her help is never easy.

- Just do as she says

  Chief: Alright, which tea would you like?

  Coquelic: Hmm, the little jar on the third shelf, second row from the top. Remember, only two scoops.

  Coquelic: ...

  Coquelic: The water temperature is off... How can you make good tea like this?

  - Guess it's time to play the trump card

    Chief: By the way, I remember that famous dried flower shop in Eastside you mentioned last time. It seems that their new stock has sold out.

    Coquelic: Hmm?!

    Chief: But if you're willing to do me a small favor, I might be able to get you some...

    Coquelic: Hand it over.

    Chief: ...What?

    Coquelic: Tsk, the list, hand it over!

    You chuckle to yourself. It seems she won't be giving you a hard time for a while.

### 「Potential Assessment」

Location: Training Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Sinner A: Um... Excuse me, are you Ms. Coquelic?

Coquelic: Uh-huh.

Chief: Coquelic, we're here to assess her potential.

In order to ask Coquelic, who was once the Mentor, to assess the potential of the new Sinners, you went to great lengths to buy her the finest flower cakes in the city.

But she doesn't seem to take this task to heart at all. She just chews on the flower cakes while staring at the somewhat shy newcomer.

Coquelic: Just average.

Sinner A: What do you mean by average...?

Coquelic: Hmm, just average.

Coquelic: We're finished here.

Coquelic: Escort me to my room. I'm tired of sitting.

- Give her a small hint

  Chief: By the way, she used to be a member of the Garden.

  Coquelic: ...Wait a minute.

  Coquelic: We have met before?

  Sinner A: M-Mentor, I-I joined a bit late...

  Coquelic: Oh my, what a coincidence, why didn't you say so earlier?

  Coquelic: Come here, let me take a good look at you.

  The usually lackadaisical Coquelic suddenly seems like a different person. She jumps off the couch and gets a close look at the Sinner from all angles.

  Coquelic: Hmm, you've got some potential!

  Coquelic: Your vision is also good... no tendency to be short-sighted... and you responded quickly to my test.

  Coquelic: You're just a bit on the short side.

  Coquelic nods in satisfaction, then turns to you.

  Coquelic: She's one of my girls from now on. You better keep an eye out. Don't let others bully her.

  Chief: Yes, yes...

### 「Moonbathing」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Before going to bed tonight, you plan to relax at the Outdoor Activity Area, but unexpectedly find Coquelic sitting on the grass.

- Greet her

  Chief: Good evening, Coquelic. What brings you here?

  Coquelic: Oh, it's you.

  Coquelic: This is the only place in the Bureau where you can see the moon. I'm here to moonbathe.

  Chief: Moonbathe?

  Coquelic: Hmm... Only the moonlight can truly highlight my beauty.

  The usually lazy and fussy Coquelic seems a bit gentler.

  Only now do you notice that she has carefully dressed up, even though it's late at night.

  Coquelic: What do you think, doesn't the moonlight tonight suit me well?

  Chief: I must admit, indeed it does.

  Chief: You look beautiful under the moonlight.

  Coquelic: Hehe... You have good taste.


  - Head back

    Chief: I need to get going. I have to get up early tomorrow.

    Chief: You should go back to sleep too. It's quite cold in the Outdoor Activity Area at night.

    Coquelic: You're right. I've had enough moonlight for today.

    Coquelic: Let's go together. You should escort me back.

    Chief: Do I have to...? The Bureau is very safe, and you're quite capable of protecting yourself...

    Coquelic: What, you don't want to?

    Chief: ...Fine, I'll escort you.

  - Stay a bit longer


  <br>

  Coquelic: How's that for a plan of action?

  Chief: Yes?

  Coquelic: I meant moonbathing. Don't you feel more awake under the moon?

  Chief: I think so, now that you mention it.

  Coquelic: How about joining me for some moonbathing more often?

  Coquelic: To be honest, I quite like you. I think you'd make a good moonbathing buddy.

  Chief: Let me think about it...

### 「Prepare the Bath」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Today, just as you've finished dealing with all the miscellaneous tasks at hand and are planning to catch up on your work, an uninvited guest appears at your office.

Coquelic: The bath bomb you bought last time was good, so I bought some more myself.

Coquelic: I'm about to take a bath. Give me a hand.

Chief: Uh, give you a hand...? Isn't that a bit inappropriate?

Coquelic: What's inappropriate about it? Just come.

Coquelic: In this Bureau, you're the only one who knows my preferences. Come help me prepare the bath.

Chief: Prepare the bath...

- Try to refuse

  Chief: It's not that I don't want to, it's just that I have too much work piled up. There are a lot of documents I need to look at...

  Chief: The logistics department's quarterly bills, the hypnotist's treatment plan for the Sinners, field mission reports...

  Coquelic: You won't be able to finish all that in one night anyway.

  Coquelic: Stop wasting time and come with me.

  Chief: ...

  You can't refute that, so you sigh and follow her.

  And you start preparing the bath.


  - Do it hastily

    Coquelic: ...I asked for cold milk. Why did you bring me hot?

    Coquelic: Today we should use white rose petals. Why did you use the red ones?

    Coquelic: Also, the water temperature obviously isn't 113 °F.

    Coquelic: Tsk, you've really disappointed me...

    Distracted, you hastily escape before she can blow her top.

    Chief: Catching up on work is more important!

  - Prepare carefully


  <br>

  Chief: Cold milk with two ice cubes.

  Chief: Keep the water temperature at 113 °F.

  Chief: Sprinkle a bag of white rose petals.

  Chief: Prepare the bath towel, shower cap, and bathrobe...

  Watching you dutifully playing the role of a "servant," and meticulously arranging the bathroom, Coquelic, who has been standing behind you, speaks up.

  Coquelic: There are quite a few Garden members in the MBCC, right?

  Chief: Yes.

  Coquelic: I've seen them. They're all doing well here.

  Coquelic: You arranged all this, didn't you?

  Chief: I'm just treating every Sinner equally.

  Coquelic: Haha. Maybe you really can accomplish something.

  Coquelic: ...

  Coquelic: Thank you for taking care of my flowers.

  Her last sentence is said very softly.
