### 「Scientific Dispute」

Location: Energy Furnace

Urgency: ⚠⚠

While passing by the Energy Furnace, you spot Hypatia and sense Mania Flux.

- Approach and ask

  Chief: Feeling overworked?

  Hypatia: I'm fine, thank you for your concern, Chief.

  Hypatia: Oh, by the way, there was a young girl with pink hair and glasses who came by earlier. What was her name?

  - Summer?

    Chief: That would be Summer. What did she want?

    Hypatia takes a deep breath, and you can tell she's struggling to contain her emotions.

    Hypatia: While I was working, she stood beside me watching for quite some time. I thought she was interested, so I started explaining the principles behind my work.

    Hypatia: ...But then she asked if my experiment was just playing make-believe.

    - Comfort her

      Chief: Ah... don't be upset. Summer is also a scientist with her own strong convictions.

      Chief: Perhaps we could approach this in a more scientific manner? Let's treat it as a challenge from a fellow scientist.

      Hypatia: Hmm... you make a valid point.

      Hypatia: Tonight I will begin writing a paper to refute her prejudice.

      With her composure restored, Hypatia returns to her work.

### 「Unfettered」

Location: Training Room / Darkroom

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You encounter Hypatia in the hallway and, while greeting her, notice her robe is hopelessly wrinkled.

- Mention her wrinkled robe

  Chief: Is the iron in your room working properly?

  Chief: If it's not working, you can request a replacement.

  Hearing your question, Hypatia looks puzzled.

  Hypatia: The iron in my room is working perfectly. I use it every night.

  Hypatia: If I may ask, Chief, why do you inquire about this?

  Chief: You use it every night... Oh, um, I see.

  Hypatia seems perplexed by your tone. Following your gaze, she looks down at herself, then awkwardly tugs at her severely wrinkled robe.

  Hypatia: ...But I just ironed this last night.

  - Offer assistance

    Chief: There are several Sinners here who are skilled with household chores. You could ask them for help if you need it.

    Hypatia: No! That won't be necessary. I was reading until very late last night and rushed through everything, so I wasn't as meticulous as usual.

    Hypatia: How embarrassing. I'll go and rectify this right away.

    Chief: Hmm...

    An awkward silence falls between you two as your eyes briefly meet. You decide it's best to take your leave.

    Later that day, you spot Hypatia with her robe draped conspicuously over her arm, drawing more attention to what she's trying to hide. Without a word, you hand her a note you've prepared with Mira and Jelena's room numbers.

### 「Equivalent Exchange」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Passing by the greenhouse, you're surprised to find Hypatia—who absolutely cannot cook—busily working in the kitchen.

- Go up and check

  Chief: What are you doing?

  Hypatia: Good afternoon, Chief.

  Hypatia: Since that incident concluded, I've been reflecting on my approach. I shouldn't conduct research in an ivory tower, but rather I should apply my studies to improving people's daily lives.

  Hypatia: So when I heard about the recent meat price increases and procurement difficulties, I realized my abilities might be able to help.

  Chief: How does your ability have anything to do with meat?

  Hypatia: By decomposing and reconstructing matter, I can produce meat.

  Hypatia: For instance, I could decompose this spoon and then reconstruct it into beef.

  Hypatia: This way, we wouldn't have to worry about food prices anymore.

  - Sounds too good to be true

    Chief: Won't there be any consequences from doing this?

    Hypatia: The primary concern would be inflation, of course, but the quantity of meat we need shouldn't be enough to impact the market.

    Hypatia: Secondly, I cannot create matter out of nothing, so we would need to purchase large quantities of other materials for equivalent exchange.

    Chief: ...I was actually asking about food safety.

    Hypatia: Well, this would actually be my first time using my abilities to create something for people to consume. All outcomes remain unknown variables.

    Hypatia: Would you let me try it?


    - Let her experiment

      Hypatia: As I expected, you truly are full of courage. I'll bring some beef stew to your office shortly.

      Chief: Wait, your "try" means I'm the one trying it?

      Half an hour later, Hypatia delivers the beef dish to your office as promised. It looks remarkably appetizing. Despite your misgivings, you decide to give it a try.

      That night, you spend endless hours attached to the toilet bowl in the bathroom, followed by three full days confined to bed. After finally recovering, you immediately shut down this experiment.

    - Tell her to forget about it


    <br>

    Chief: It doesn't seem particularly safe. Besides, we're not quite desperate enough to start eating our silverware yet.

    Chief: If you'd like to help out, perhaps you could ask if they need assistance at the Secret Chamber?

    After careful consideration, Hypatia accepts your suggestion.
