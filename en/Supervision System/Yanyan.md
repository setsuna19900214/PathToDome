# Yanyan Supervision System

### 「Save the Light」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Malfunction detected in the lighting system of the Outdoor Activity Area. Go and check?

- Yes.

  Upon reaching the malfunctioning area, you spot Hestia and Yanyan standing under a flickering light.

  Yanyan: Hestia, why does the light keep flickering?

  Hestia: For... people have strayed from devotion... thus, the god, in a fit of anger, has reclaimed the blessing of light...

  Yanyan: Is there any way to make the god not angry...?

  Hestia: Present more tributes to god... to demonstrate sincerity...

  Yanyan: Oh, that's all? Seems like the god in DisCity is just like the ones in Eastia, wanting offerings regularly.

  Suddenly, with a "pop," the corridor plunges into darkness as the flickering light tube finally gives out.

  Yanyan: Ahhh! The light went out!

  Hestia: This is... the result of neglecting to offer tributes to god... Only by pleasing the god will the light be restored...

  Yanyan: I'll go tell everyone right away, so they can bring some tributes! By the way, Hestia, what would the god like?

  Hestia: Food... the more, the better...

  Yanyan: I'll keep that in mind!


  - Step in to stop

    Seeing their conversation veering into absurdity, you step in to interrupt.

    Chief: It's alright, Yanyan, you don't have to worry about finding tributes. I'll take care of it.

    Yanyan: Really? But the light...

    Chief: It's likely just a burnt filament; replacing it should fix the issue. It's nearly dinner time, and I heard there's a feast in the cafeteria today. Why don't you go take a look?

    Hestia: A feast... I want to go...

    After managing to persuade the two to leave, you submit a maintenance request to the logistics department.

    Chief: It appears necessary to enhance scientific education for the Sinners in the future.

  - Call repair personnel


  <br>

  The repair personnel arrive quickly and replace the light tube in less than five minutes. The corridor becomes bright again.

  Yanyan: Wow... Is this another kind of magic to make god happy again?

  Chief: No, technically speaking, it's a "repair skill"... more useful than offering tributes. If you're curious, the Bureau offers a basic course on it.

  Yanyan: I'll think about it... Hestia, do you want to take the course with me?

  Hestia: I... refuse...

  Hestia: That is the wisdom bestowed upon humanity by god... As the priestess, I do not require further instruction... I can simply commune with god...

### 「A Master Storyteller」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You've been receiving daily notifications of gatherings in the recreation room recently. You guess it might be some team-building activity organized by the Sinners, so you've chosen to stay out of it.

A few days later, however, the therapist's office, which is normally empty, suddenly becomes overcrowded, much to your bewilderment.

- Investigate

  After talking to the therapist, you come to the recreation room, where Yanyan is telling a story to a small crowd in the dim light.

  Yanyan: This story has its origins in my hometown Eastia. Ready?

  Once upon a time, there were these two good friends who traveled together in the mountains. It grew dark, and they couldn't find an inn...

  Yanyan: What did they do next? They searched and searched. Finally, they stumbled across a dilapidated abandoned house. Since they didn't have a better choice, they decided to make do...

  Yanyan: As soon as they stepped into the house, they saw a human-shaped shadow flash by!

  The two fled, screaming. It was dark. Neither of them looked to see if the other was running in the same direction.

  Yanyan: After running for a long while, one of them stopped to catch his breath. Looking up, he found himself in a dark forest.

  To his relief, his friend was not far away, also panting.

  Yanyan: He ran over to where his friend was; he wanted to check the map with his friend to determine where they were.

  At that moment, his friend, who hadn't made a sound since they left that spooky house, said...

  Yanyan: "Take another look. Do I really look like your friend?"

  As she utters the last word, Yanyan opens her eyes wide, and lunges forward with her hands held out in front of her.

  Crowd: Ahhh... Help! The ghost is really here!

  Some members of the audience get scared and make a beeline for the door. You are starting to understand why the therapist's office has been so busy lately.

  Chief: But... can someone be so scared by ghost stories that they need to see a therapist?

  You're thinking about how to tactfully convince Yanyan to switch to a different type of story, when a sudden change in her expression shocks you.

  Yanyan: Look! Do I look like a ghost? Hahaha...

  Yanyan's face looks grotesque and menacing, and her voice no longer carries that subdued tone she adopted for storytelling. It is as if she has allowed the evil ghost from the story to inhabit her body.

  The members of the audience who have managed to remain in the room now scramble to get to the door, screaming as they go. It is a scene of total chaos.

  Chief: This is... a Mania Flux? Oh dear! She has turned herself into a channeler!

  - Forbid Yanyan from using channeling in her storytelling

    Chief: No wonder the therapist's office is always packed these days... Yanyan, you must stop using channeling in your storytelling.

### 「An Impressive Magic Item」

Location: Energy Furnace

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Yanyan seeks you out while you're alone, excitement writ large on her face.

- What's the matter?

  Yanyan: I was exploring the Bureau just now, and I discovered an awesome magic item. It's just around that corner!

  Chief: Really? You mean, inside the Bureau?

  Yanyan: Yes. Come on, let's go! I'll show you the way.

  Yanyan grabs your hand and starts dragging you to where this supposed magic item is located. After many twists and turns, the two of you finally arrive at the room that houses the Energy Furnace.

  Chief: Is this the magic item you're talking about?

  Yanyan: Yes! I touched the surface just now. Cold to the touch. "Cold as jade," the book says.

  Chief: ...

  Yanyan: And the red light... "Strikingly bright, with dots of scarlet," the book also says.

  Chief: ...

  Yanyan: And look at its shape. Narrow at the top and wide at the bottom, with many straight lines and curves. This *is* Immortal's Alchemy Furnace. I saw a picture of it in the book.

  I'm telling you, it's a very powerful magic item.

  Chief: ...

  Chief: Yanyan, do you know what an Energy Furnace is?

  Yanyan: ?

  With some difficulty, you more or less get through to her what the Energy Furnace is for, and how it is an important piece of equipment for the Bureau, not something to be meddled with.

  Yanyan: So this isn't a magical item? Are you sure? It looks exactly like the one I saw in the book.

  - Take the opportunity to spark Yanyan's interest in technology

    Chief: Yanyan, there may be a resemblance between the two in appearance, but their functions couldn't be more different.

    Chief: There are books on this subject that provide detailed descriptions and illustrations of this sort of device.

    Chief: You can borrow them from the library, if you're interested. How does that sound?

### 「Homesickness」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

One day, walking past the Recreation Room, you catch Yanyan sitting in the room all by herself.

- She is uncharacteristically quiet; find out what happened

  Yanyan is staring at the television, motionless as a statue, her eyes rimmed with red.

  You follow her gaze. A colorful, noisy advertisement is playing on TV, a stark contrast to the melancholy mood of the room.

  Chief: What's wrong? Was someone being mean to you?

  Yanyan turns to look at you. She shakes her head, wordless.

  The advertisement ends. The documentary *The Eastia Chronicle* comes back on. Now you understand why she is gloomy.

  Chief: Yanyan, are you feeling homesick?

  Yanyan lowers her head, her body trembling. She bites her lip to keep the tears at bay, but to no avail. She bursts out crying.

  Yanyan: It's not your fault, Chief. It's nobody's fault, really.

  Yanyan: DisCity is great, you are great, the others are great, including that big fellow in white armor... but this isn't my home.

  Yanyan: I know I can't go home until the mission is completed; I don't plan on leaving now... Sob... It's just that I... I miss my grandma's beef soup.

  Looking at Yanyan's tear-stained face, you decide to do something to cheer her up.


  - Organize an Outlands cuisine party

    You decide to throw an Outlands cuisine party for Yanyan. Many of the Sinners, upon hearing the news, are eager to participate; it's a chance for them to show off their culinary skills.

    Yanyan: Wait... Don't throw the taro in just yet. We want to make sure the final product has a soft, glutinous texture. Give it to me.

    Yanyan: Hey, hold it! You should score the tomatoes first, which will make it easier for them to give extra flavor to the soup. What? You don't know how to score them?

    ...

    The dishes are ready. Yanyan has been the busiest one among the bunch. Nevertheless, she is in a much better mood now.

    Yanyan's dishes are well received. Afterward, those Sinners who are from Eastia come together to form a hometown association, and Yanyan is often invited to cook and enjoy meals with them.

  - Gather all the Sinners of Eastian origin together


  <br>

  You gather all the Sinners of Eastian origin together. Yanyan is also present. After you briefly explain why you've gathered them together, one of them floats the idea of a hometown association.

  Yanyan: Yay! Let me be the president!

  Yanyan: Let me see... What should we do together as members of this new hometown association? Ha! Let's go search for miracles!

  Chief: Yanyan, searching for miracles is your assignment. It's work. Why don't you think of something more relaxing?

  - Dissuade Yanyan

    Yanyan: You're right. The people of Eastia would usually celebrate a memorable occasion like this with a good meal. All right then, let's have a meal!

    There are going to be a lot of busy days ahead, you think as you look at the unbridled enthusiasm on Yanyan's face. Well, as long as she's happy.
