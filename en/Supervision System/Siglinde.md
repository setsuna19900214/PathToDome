# Siglinde Supervision System

### 「Encrypted Intel」

Siglinde always wears a pair of headphones. According to certain sources, she listens in on everyone's private conversations, then encrypts and uploads any Chief-related intel.

- Ask for details

  Siglinde: Indeed. According to my latest intercepted intel, the cafeteria has only five portions of Chief's favorite dish left. You may still be able to get it if you hurry.

### 「Unfortunate Eavesdropper」

A rumor about Siglinde is circulating throughout the Bureau. She allegedly identified a spy attempting to eavesdrop on DisCorps intel by saying a single sentence, forcing the culprit to turn themselves in.

- Ask about it

  Siglinde: Oh, it was nothing special. I simply asked him, "Your bug is running low on battery. Would you like me to charge it for you?"

### 「Light Stick Crisis」

Siglinde finds that her mobile transmitter is missing. It's eventually found at a Sinner's concert, where fans are enthusiastically waving it in the air, somehow using it as a glowing light stick that illuminates the entire venue.

- Find the culprit

  Siglinde: It's fine. At least it cheered up the crowd at the concert. In the Bureau, it only ever picks up useless noise anyway.

### 「Snack Attack」

Lately, Siglinde has been getting really popular in the Bureau, especially among younger Sinners. Wherever she goes, there's bound to be several young Sinners eagerly trailing after her.

One day, as you pass by the greenhouse, you're surprised to find Siglinde there alone, seeking refuge from all the attention.

- Greet her

  Siglinde: Phew... It's you, Chief. You startled me.

  Chief: Are you... hiding from the others?

  Siglinde: Yes. According to my analysis, offering snacks in the workplace is a form of highly effective social interaction. To better integrate into the Bureau, I implemented a strategy of distributing snacks to everyone I encountered. Unfortunately... there have been some miscalculations in my execution.

  Chief: ?

  Siglinde: The plan went well. However, during the snack distribution, my stocks somehow kept growing to a bothersome degree. I couldn't consume them all or waste them, so I gave out the surplus to the young Sinners in the Bureau.

  Siglinde: But now these little ones seem to think I constantly have snacks available, and they follow me around all day...

  Suddenly, it all makes sense to you.

  Chief: Oh, don't tell me... our esteemed liaison officer Siglinde can't handle a few clingy kids?

  Siglinde: It's just... I find it rather uncomfortable. The children surround me all the time. I've never experienced this kind of behavior in DisCorps. The mere thought of this continuing makes my skin crawl.

  Siglinde: Therefore, I must entrust you with distributing the remaining snacks. Please do me a favor, Chief!

  Chief: ?!

  Siglinde shoves the bag of snacks into your hands and swiftly disappears. You glance behind you and, sure enough, spot a pack of young Sinners eyeing you hungrily.

  Chief: ...

### 「Stranded」

You and Siglinde are driving to a tactical meeting at an outpost when the car suddenly groans loudly before breaking down.

Driver: Sorry, but the car's going to take some time to fix. You two will have to wait a while.

Chief: No worries! We can help fix the car too. More hands make light work, right?

Siglinde: Yes, please allow us to assist.

The driver immediately crawls under the car to inspect it and quickly pokes his head back out. He needs your help with some tools, except...

Driver: Excuse me, miss! Could you hand me that large adjustable wrench next to you? It's in the top tray of the toolbox! Ah... I meant the one on the right, yes, that's it...

Driver: Could one of you please help me tighten this screw? The one right next to my hand, turn it clockwise! Wait... no, the other way, you're loosening it...

After your *helpful* assistance, the driver has politely declined any further aid. You and Siglinde can only awkwardly step back, having learned the hard way that sometimes more help doesn't in fact make light work.

Chief: The driver just told me one jack isn't enough and we need to find another. Do you know where we might find one around here?

Siglinde: I don't know, but... we probably won't need to look for one.

Having just said that, Siglinde casually lifts the car's axle with one hand, as if it were nothing more than a stack of files, and effortlessly places some heavy rocks beneath the wheels.

- All good?

  Siglinde: Yes, thank you for your concern, Chief. A liaison officer's duty is to clear all obstacles hindering communication, and that includes, of course, broken-down vehicles.

  Chief: You made it look so easy. How did you even do that?

  Siglinde: By carrying the mobile transmitter around...

  In that instant, the damaged axle completely splits in two, falling from beneath the car onto the ground with a loud crack.

  Chief: ...

  Siglinde speaks calmly.

  Siglinde: Ah, seems I might have gone too hard with my training.

### 「Cooling Expert」

The Bureau lounge's air conditioning is undergoing routine maintenance for half an hour. The scorching summer heat is almost unbearable, so you decide to ask Siglinde to tell some bad jokes to help everyone cool down.

Siglinde: Will that really cool things down?

- Of course, I have faith in you.

  Siglinde clears her throat.

  Siglinde: A DisCity spy has nothing but DisCoins in his pocket. When he passes through a security checkpoint, the alarm goes off. Can you guess why?

  Chief: ...Because only DisCity uses DisCoins?

  Siglinde: No, it's because it's... DISrespectful.

  Chief: ...

  An awkward cold shudder instantly runs through you.

  Chief: Actually... that's kind of funny, really. Ha-ha-ha.

  Siglinde: You like jokes like this, Chief? I can share a few more.


  - Let her continue

    Siglinde: So this DisCity spy gets caught. Guess how he is executed?

    Chief: Strangled?

    Siglinde: No, they throw him into the sea, because... they want him to DISappear.

    Chief: ...

    Siglinde: Still not enough? Let me try another one...

    Siglinde talks until her mouth runs dry, while everyone gets chills from how bad her jokes are. When the air conditioning finally comes back on, there's a collective sigh of relief.

  - Stop her


  <br>

  Chief: Wait! No more, please! I think we've heard enough!

  Siglinde: Did it actually work? Well then, before the air conditioning is fixed, I'll visit the other rooms and help everyone cool down.

  Chief: What a torture...
