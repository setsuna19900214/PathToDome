### 「Insomnia Regular」

Location: Hypnotherapy Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

This quarter's treatment report from the Hypnotherapy Room has been sent. Please check it promptly.

- View the report

  Chief: ...The frequency of hypnosis treatments over the past few months is a bit too high. That and... the average treatment time per person has doubled? This warrants a thorough investigation.

  The moment you step into the Hypnotherapy Room, you hear the hypnotist earnestly trying to reason with someone.

  Hypnotist: Ms. Clementine, hypnotherapy is only meant to be a treatment option. Overusing it will harm your health. You've been coming in every single day for the past two weeks...

  Hypnotist: Oh, Chief, thank goodness you're here! Ms. Clementine keeps thinking about what to write in her novel during hypnosis sessions. I've already been cast as a corpse in her dreams three times now... Please, you have to help me. At this rate, I'll end up with insomnia too.

  Clementine looks at you with a wry smile.

  Clementine: I promised you I'd stop relying on substances to fall asleep, but... I can't see the night sky from my room. I can't see the moon and stars that used to keep me company during those sleepless nights. So... I had to turn to hypnotherapy instead.

  You recall Clementine's concerning medical report. You once made her promise that, for the sake of her health, she would stop using substances to numb herself to sleep. You never expected her to actually honor that promise.

  Chief: I'm surprised you actually kept your promise.

  Clementine raises an eyebrow.

  Chief: But the situation seems to have... gone beyond what qualifies as normal treatment. Frequent hypnosis sessions in such a short span will only harm your body. You know this.

  Clementine: (chuckles) Compared to lying awake in the darkness until dawn, this at least lets me see something else. Including that hypnotist who keeps appearing as a corpse in my dreams... I find everything quite entertaining.

  Chief: So, what you really need is a distraction?

  Clementine: You've seen right through me... Sometimes the darkness is harder to endure than any nightmare. And now that I can't turn to pills or alcohol... You've put me in quite a bind, Chief.

  Chief: I understand that you're going through a tough time, but hypnotists have their limits too. Perhaps... we can find another solution together? For instance, when you can't sleep, you could come to my office instead.

  Chief: The moon and stars from my window... maybe they're a sight to behold.

### 「Dangerous Signing」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Abnormal gathering detected in Sinner Clementine's room. Go and check?

- Go there

  Chief: You did hear quite a commotion outside today. Perhaps you should go see what's happening?

  You follow the directions on the system to Clementine's room, only to find a long queue of people, all excitedly clutching books. Posters are plastered on the walls nearby, promoting "Bestselling Crime Fiction Author Clementine's Latest Work."

  Chief: Looks like it's a book signing session for her new novel...

  Clementine shakes hands with her readers one by one, signs their books, answers their questions... However, you can't shake the feeling that something is off.

  Reader A: Ms. Clementine, are the modus operandi in your books actually viable?

  Clementine: I haven't tried them personally yet. If you're interested, you can reference a book I often use. It's called "108 Essential Tips for Committing Crimes."

  Reader B: Ms. Clementine, what if the perpetrator isn't strong enough to kill someone in a single strike?

  Clementine: Why not try other methods? Poisoning, suffocation, staged accidents... I'm sure one of them will suit your needs.

  Reader C: Ms. Clementine, does a perfect method exist that would allow a perpetrator to escape suspicion entirely?

  Clementine: A detective once said that everything leaves a trace, and the key lies in whether you have the eyes to see through the discrepancies. So, my answer is no. Regrettably, a culprit can only employ various methods to avoid being captured, but they can never erase all traces.

  The more you listen, the more uneasy you feel. Clementine carries herself like an expert in criminal methodology, writing everything from crime type classifications to techniques for handling bloodstains on the whiteboard behind her in detail.

  Clementine: As the saying goes, it's easy to start a fire but hard to put it out. You must pay close attention during cleanup—there are always more details to consider than you'd expect. How should you handle the crime scene? How do you fabricate an alibi? How do you...

  The readers listen with rapt attention, and many even begin taking notes in earnest.

  Reader D: Well then, Ms. Clementine... if I wanted to create a new type of poison...
