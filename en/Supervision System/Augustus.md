### 「Junior Troops」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Sinner Augustus has been found to frequently gather young Sinners with disciplinary issues for meetings. The purpose of these meetings remains unknown. Investigate?

Given Augustus's notorious history of inciting internal conflicts within the Bureau, you decide to intervene.

- Go

  You push open the door to the lounge and, sure enough, chaos reigns inside. But... it's not quite the kind of chaos you were expecting.

  Young Sinner A: Pillow collection complete! Ready to engage in battle anytime at the Supreme Commander's orders!

  Young Sinner B: The flank units are in position. Ready to surround the enemies!

  You watch the young Sinners eagerly awaiting Augustus's commands amid the chaotic pillow fight, a headache slowly setting in.

  Chief: You little troublemakers again?

  Augustus: Hey, Chief... Here for an inspection?

  Chief: I thought you were recruiting children for your army again. Didn't expect it to be... this kind of army.

  Augustus: Are you looking down on my junior troops?

  Chief: I'm just worried about them. They lost a competition last time and were depressed for quite a while. They've only just started to feel better...

  Augustus: Loss breeds the desire for victory. Come on, kiddos, show them what you're made of.

  Chief: Wait... what are you planning to do?

  Augustus: I'm going to lead them to victory, of course. The shame of defeat can only be cleansed by triumph. Come on, kiddos. I'll take you to claim your vengeance.

  Chief: ?!!

  The lounge once again erupts into fierce war.

  Augustus: That's right, Number One, sneak up from the rear and strike the left leg to destabilize your target. The rest of you, launch your assault from the blind spots and aim for vital points.

  Augustus: Don't advance recklessly! Concentrate your forces and tighten your formation. See their leader? To defeat the enemy, take down their commander first. Prepare to attack together on my signal!

  The disorganized and undisciplined adult Sinners are quickly defeated, bringing beaming smiles to the faces of the young Sinners at last.

  Augustus: Well? What do you think of my junior troops now?

  You stare at the lounge covered in goose down and cotton stuffing, momentarily speechless.

  Chief: They're impressive... though it would be even better if you taught them to clean up the battlefield as well.

### 「Bounty Ranking」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

The dark web has recently revealed a bounty ranking list with detailed targets and their prices. Many of them are notorious felons connected to Mania incidents, names that have long been on your wanted list. Yet for various reasons, you haven't found the opportunity to apprehend them.

Upon hearing about the bounty list, Augustus curiously asks you about her own ranking on it.

Augustus: I came to ask you, where do I rank on the dark web's bounty list?

Chief: Only long-standing criminals can make it onto that list. You're certainly not among them... Wait, what's this face?

Augustus: ...An annoyed face.

Chief: ...?

Before you can stop her, Augustus has already thrown on her coat and is heading out the door, sword in hand.

Chief: Hold on, wait, where are you going?

Augustus: I'm going to pay these guys a visit. Let's see what skills they actually possess to deserve spots on that list.

Augustus: I've already looked into them. Just some arms dealers and gang leaders. Nothing but parasites sucking the life from this land. Eliminating them would lighten your workload a bit.

Augustus: Don't worry, I can handle this.

Several days later, Augustus returns to the Bureau. She jumps out of her vehicle, marches straight toward you, and shoves a set of keys at you.

Augustus: Count them. That should be everyone.

- Ask why

  Augustus: The top ten most notorious criminals on the dark web bounty list. Excluding myself, that's nine individuals. They are all chained up in the car. Feel free to count the heads yourself.

  Chief: Excluding you?

  Augustus laughs.

  Augustus: I've vanquished all of them now. Which means, in a sense, I'm the one who deserves the number one spot on that bounty list.

  Augustus: Now all the top ten criminals have been rounded up by the MBCC in one fell swoop. Chief, you can report mission accomplished.

### 「Unsolicited Gift」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

The Bureau's security department has recently confiscated a batch of antique blades and firearms smuggled from the black market. Inspect them?

- Go

  As you enter the depot where the confiscated items are stored, you find Augustus already there, casually examining an ornate antique blade in her hands while watching you with an unhurried, expectant gaze.

  It seems the culprit has already shown herself.

  Chief: I should have guessed it. Such a collection of priceless antique weapons, this has your signature all over it.

  Augustus: Oh, you're here? Perfect timing. Come see my collection. These are all precious items I acquired at an auction in DisCity a few days ago. Pistols, scimitars, daggers, and swords. Judging by their design, weight, and colors, quite a few would suit you perfectly.

  Chief: For... me?

  You hadn't expected that the antique weapons Augustus went to such lengths to acquire were meant as gifts for you.

  Augustus: Please. If I hadn't intentionally let the confiscation happen, do you really think that little security team at your Bureau could have seized them?

  Augustus: As my favored subordinate, how will others know you're mine without an antique blade from me strapped to your side? Come, choose whichever you like.

  Chief: I don't think that's appropriate. These are far too valuable. Antiques like these belong in a collection room under careful preservation. I'll ruin such a luxurious gift if I carry it around with me everywhere.

  Augustus simply smiles as she slides the blade back into its sheath.

  Augustus: Oh, I'm sure you will take good care of it. And don't forget that these are weapons made to spill blood on the battlefield.

  Augustus: Those conceited nobles who procure them just to have them locked away in vacuum-sealed display cases, meticulously maintained and protected from the slightest scratch—that's the real ruin of the purpose of these fine weapons.

  Augustus: I bought them precisely to save them from such a pathetic fate. And I'm offering them to you to fulfill their true purpose. Don't disappoint me.

  You continue shaking your head. Accepting such a lavish gift would be highly inappropriate for a DisCity civil servant like yourself.

  Augustus shrugs.

  Augustus: Fine, I can't force you to accept the gift. But since I've brought them here anyway... let's have a sparring match. Help me sharpen their edges.

  Chief: ...What?!

  Augustus casually picks up two weapons from the collection.

  Augustus: Go on, pick one.


  - Pick up the sword

    Augustus: Excellent choice. The sword's previous owner was said to be royalty from a small nation. After being captured during a war, he used this very blade to slit his own throat.

    Chief: Are you implying something?

    Augustus: Quite the contrary. I'm betting you'll make this sword worthy of going down in history as a conqueror's blade, instead of a suicide tool for the defeated.

  - Pick up the scimitar


  <br>

  You hear Augustus's whistle.

  Augustus: Perfect choice. Clearly, you haven't heard of the curse of this blade. All its owners met grisly ends. The first was gutted by enemies. The second lost his grip on it accidentally while chopping wood and split his own skull open...

  Chief: Sounds ominous...

  Augustus: It's only an object... On the contrary, it should fear you.

### 「The General's Ball」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Following the success of a major operation, the Bureau has organized a grand ball to celebrate its victory. You navigate through the crowd with a drink in hand, but your gaze keeps getting drawn to Augustus, who is no doubt the center of attention tonight.

Guest A: Miss Augustus, I've heard so much about you. Might I have the honor of sharing a dance with you?

Guest B: Miss Augustus, my family runs an arms-dealing business in WhiteSands. I wonder if I might have the honor of...

Surrounded by the crowd, Augustus has an almost luminous presence, like a star drawing the planets into its orbit. Everyone unconsciously revolves around her. Your own gaze, too, often lingers on her longer than intended.

Augustus: Your proposals are intriguing, ladies and gentlemen. However, I have more pressing matters to attend to at the moment. Please excuse me.

Augustus: You don't have to steal glances at me like that, you know. You're welcome to admire me openly.
