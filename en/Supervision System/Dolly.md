# Dolly Supervision System

### 「Nobody's Wrong」

Location: Training Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Inside the Bureau, Roulecca and Dolly are arguing. Celine stands nearby, listening quietly.

- What just happened?

  Roulecca: Don't pin the armored car accident on me! I clearly heard Dolly shout "left"!

  Dolly: I didn't say that! I said there was an enemy on the left, so you should watch out. How was I supposed to know you'd yank the wheel and drive straight into a wall?!

  Roulecca: Hmph. I heard it plain as day. Wait—Celine was in the car too. Let's ask her!

  Dolly: Yes! Head Librarian Celine's memory never fails. She'll prove I'm innocent!

  - Hear what Celine has to say

    Celine: I believe... neither of you misremembered. You simply interpreted the same call differently.

    Celine: For future field operations, perhaps we could agree on this: the spotter reports enemy positions using clock directions only. How to maneuver from there is left to the operator's judgment.

    Celine: That way, the call stays precise without prompting an instinctive reaction to the word itself. What do you think?

    Roulecca: Not a bad idea! From now on, if you want Roulecca to shoot somewhere, just use that fancy lingo.

    Dolly: Got it. Next time I'll call "enemy at nine o'clock" instead of "left." Roulecca, I—

    Roulecca: No worries. Roulecca doesn't sweat the small stuff. Besides, the Chief's covering the repair bill for the armored car anyway, right?

    Celine: What do you say, Chief?

    - Add Celine's suggestion to the protocol

      You incorporate her suggestion into the Sinner field operations manual. According to the data department's feedback, mission success rates rise by about three percentage points.

### 「Wall Destroyer」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You're walking in the Outdoor Activity Area after dinner when you suddenly hear a loud "bang" coming from the southwest corner.

- Go take a look

  You push through the gathered crowd, only to see Dolly in the middle, holding a baseball bat. Opposite her, a hole has been punched into the wall of the Outdoor Activity Area, and a smoking baseball is deeply embedded within it.

  - Inquire about the situation

    Chief: What's going on? Explain yourself.

    Dolly: I didn't do it on purpose! I just wanted to play baseball since it's been such a long time. I just tapped it.

    Chief: "Tapped" it? Are you sure?

    You sigh, just about to call the repair team, but Dolly stops you.

    Dolly: Wait, I can handle this mess I made. You don't need to call anyone.

    Chief: Really? I don't really think...

    Dolly: I said it's okay! Just shut up and watch!

    Later, Dolly calls over the little Sinners in the Bureau, and with several buckets of paint, they turn the area into a graffitied wall.

    Chief: That's actually a pretty good solution.
