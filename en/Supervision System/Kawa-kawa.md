### 「Cocooning」

Location: Training Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Mantis is often in the hot seat for her untamed survival tactics, and you've grown quite accustomed to it. But this time, the beef someone has with her seems a bit off the beaten track.

Complaint ticket: Mantis and her cohorts have commandeered a section of the gym, keeping other Sinners from their fair share of the workout gear.

- Go check the situation

  Mantis: And then... if you thread it through here... voila, that's a wrap... The sturdiness is commendable... Seems like it could hold its own against a sandstorm...

  Kawa-kawa: Incredible! I've got to take this baby for a spin the first chance I get!

  Chief: What in the world are you two up to?

  - Interrupt

    You've caught Mantis red-handed in a corner of the gym, seemingly crafting a massive cocoon, with Kawa-kawa screeching up a storm nearby.

    Kawa-kawa: Ah, Chief, there you are! Word on the street is that Mantis is a pro at making do in rough conditions. I'm picking up some tips on crafting an emergency shelter!

    Mantis: That's right... You interested in... picking up this skill... maybe...?

    Chief: This shelter's tied up a bit tight, don't you think? It's like you're prepping to metamorphose or something...

    Mantis: But this way... it'll hold up stronger... useful for bracing against... storms and the like...

    Chief: Alright, after all's said and done, how's Kawa-kawa gonna hatch from this cocoon? He's not equipped with your kind of cutters.

    - Ask why

      Kawa-kawa: Spot on! Chief, you've got a real knack for this!

      Mantis: Looks like we need to... take this for another spin...

      Chief: Keep at it, you two. But make sure to take down the cocoon after your experiment; don't hog the gym space.

### 「Tales of the WhiteSands」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Kawa-kawa is reading a book titled *Tales of the WhiteSands* when you come upon him.

He laughs as he reads.

Kawa-kawa: Hahaha, so hilarious, these stories.

Kawa-kawa: Chief, listen to this: there is this turtle in the WhiteSands, it crawls at midday for one hour every day to boost its stamina.

Kawa-kawa: It can't be that stupid! Crawling at midday... it'd soon become turtle jerky.

Kawa-kawa: Here is another one: A clever traveler runs out of food, and he finds a sandworm nest and eats the sandworm eggs.

Kawa-kawa: Sandworm eggs are highly poisonous. He would die in a minute.

Kawa-kawa laughs so hard that tears come out. He may be reading a joke book, for all you know.

Kawa-kawa: Hahaha, this is the funniest book I've ever read, so funny.

Kawa-kawa: Ooh, this story is about... the Eternal Oasis.

His face takes on a sad expression.

- What is it?

  Kawa-kawa: I've heard about the legend of the Eternal Oasis.

  Kawa-kawa: Legend has it that there's a route in the WhiteSands that takes you to an oasis where the weather is always fine, and water and food are always abundant.

  Kawa-kawa: It's called the Eternal Oasis.

  Kawa-kawa: When I was a kid, I often wondered if the reason my father didn't come home was that he'd found the Eternal Oasis.

  Kawa-kawa: It's said that whoever enters the oasis will be so stunned by the heavenly landscape that they will never want to leave.

  Kawa-kawa: Chief, does the Eternal Oasis really exist?


  - Yes.

    Kawa-kawa: Do you really think so? I always thought it was a made-up story.

    Kawa-kawa: But I've also heard that the safe route through the WhiteSands isn't real.

    Kawa-kawa: Maybe the oasis is real.

    Chief: If it is real, you and I will find it.

    - Encourage him

      Kawa-kawa: Mmm!

      Kawa-kawa nods his head to you with enthusiasm.

  - No.


  <br>

  Kawa-kawa: That's what I figured; you don't think it is real either.

  Kawa-kawa: I believed it was real as a kid. It might have been a way of comforting myself.

  Kawa-kawa: I liked to think that my father was trapped in an oasis and was waiting for me to rescue him.

  Kawa-kawa: Though the Eternal Oasis is not real, the safe route through the WhiteSands is. Its existence has been proven by my grandfather and father.

  Kawa-kawa: My father, he must be waiting for me at the end of the route.

  - Must be.

    Kawa-kawa: Mmm!

    Kawa-kawa nods his head to you with enthusiasm.
