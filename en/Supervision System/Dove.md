### 「Strategic Retreat」

Location: Management Center / Darkroom

Urgency: ⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Sinner Dove appears to have had a confrontation with someone in the photo room. Go and check?

- Yes

  You head in the direction indicated by the Supervision System. From a distance, you see an MBCC staff member standing arms akimbo, confronting Dove as she exits the photo room.

  Staff Member: There you are at last! What are you doing here? Where are my documents?

  Dove: I was just collecting the photos a sender wanted to mail...

  Dove: Please calm down first. What documents are you looking for?

  Staff Member: Calm down? Sure! But I'm waiting for the Supply Office's receipts and invoices that should have been delivered by noon. Why haven't I received them yet?

  Dove blinks and finally seems to comprehend the situation. She bends down and rummages through her messenger bag for quite some time before finally retrieving an envelope stuck at the bottom.

  Dove: ...

  As you begin to understand what's happening and prepare to intervene, Dove beats you to it. Realizing her mistake, she presents the envelope with both hands and bows deeply in apology.

  Dove: I'm so sorry, I... I shouldn't have kept you waiting for so long...

  Dove: It's just that I've got a huge batch to deliver today. I've been running around non-stop since six in the morning. I haven't even had time for lunch.

  Dove: I rushed here as fast as I could, but I still let you down... It's all my fault!

  Staff Member: Uh... I was just a bit anxious, since this is part of my job...

  Her heartfelt apology and remorseful tone have clearly overwhelmed the young staff member. Yet Dove sniffles and continues.

  Dove: Of course, these are no excuses! I'm truly sorry for delaying your work. You have every right to file a complaint against me.

  Dove: ...In, in fact, you don't need to, I'll do it myself right now! I'll go and get my penalty from Chief! I should be put in the confinement cell!

  Dove says vehemently and stamps her feet, turning to head toward your office, but the staff member frantically grabs her arm to stop her.

  Staff Member: Wait, it's... it's not that serious! I was just getting impatient. I didn't mean to report you! We're all just trying our best here!

  Hearing this, Dove's face lights up with a surprised, endearing smile.

  Dove: Thank you for your understanding. I'm so touched... You're such a wonderful person! If only everyone could be as kind as you are.

  Dove excuses herself to continue her mail route, leaving the young staff member standing there in a daze, envelope in hand.

  Staff Member: Dove has to deliver so many letters every day. She's working so hard. I failed to understand that and was being so harsh to her... Ugh, what a terrible person I am!

  Staff Member: Oh right, didn't she say she hasn't had lunch yet? No, she may faint from hunger if she keeps this up. I should go buy her something to eat...

  Chief: ...

  Chief: Looks like my concerns were completely unnecessary.

### 「Plant Terminator」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You find Dove crouching in a corner of the greenhouse, staring at several flower pots with visible distress.

- Go and ask her what happened

  Chief: Dove, what's wrong?

  Dove: Oh, Chief... Well, you see, I bought two orchid plants last week.

  Dove: I've been taking care of them every day, but for some reason they don't seem to be doing well...

  As she speaks, Dove points to the two plants at her feet.

  Upon closer inspection, you see that almost all the white flowers have fallen off, and the leaves, which should have been a vibrant green, are now yellow and withered, hanging limply from the stems.

  Chief: Um... how have you been caring for them?

  Dove: I'm following every instruction in the care guide that came with them.

  Dove: Orchids need bright light but should avoid direct sunlight, so I've placed them in a shaded corner of the greenhouse.

  Dove: I water them twice a week, and I'm using a well-aerated orchid potting mix.

  Chief: ...That all sounds correct, so what could be the problem?

  Dove lets out a sigh and lowers her head dejectedly.

  Dove: Sigh, I was never any good at farming back home. Everything I planted either withered or yielded poor harvests, and it was the same with animals. They all somehow escaped from their pens quickly.

  Dove: I suppose I just don't have a way with plants and animals... Such a shame, I really liked these orchids...

  - Comfort her

    Chief: Don't worry too much. Why don't we let Sumire take a look at them? They might still be salvageable.

    Chief: Next time you take care of plants, you should invite her to join you. She can observe your methods and see if there's anything to adjust. I'm sure she'd be happy to help.

### 「Treasured Belonging」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

It's a pleasant afternoon. In the sunlit Outdoor Activity Area, Dove is washing her bicycle.

- Lend a hand

  Chief: Dove, washing your bike?

  You walk over and simply grab the cleaning cloth from Dove's hand, starting to help her wipe down the bicycle frame.

  Dove: Oh, hi, Chief.

  Dove: I've got no mail to deliver today, so I finally have some time off. Thought I'd take the opportunity to give my bicycle a wash and oil it up a bit.

  Dove gives you an appreciative smile, then removes the chain to soak it in cleaning solution before taking up a brush to clean the dust stuck in the pedals.

  This is your first close look at Dove's bicycle, and you notice how old it appears to be. The handlebars and seat are badly worn, and much of the paint has peeled off the frame.

  Chief: You've had this bike for a long time, haven't you? Why not get a new one?

  Dove: I've been riding this same bike all these years, so I'm used to it now. It still works fine, so there's no need for a new one.

  Dove: Besides, this bike... my parents got it for me back then.

  Dove: They didn't leave much behind, so I can't really bring myself to replace this bike.

  Chief: Ah, I see it now...

  Dove: Alright, Chief, thanks for your help. The frame's all cleaned now, and I can handle the rest myself. You should get back to your work.


  - Leave

    Chief: All right then, I'll head back now.

    Chief: If you need any help, feel free to come find me anytime.

    After saying goodbye to Dove, you head back to your office, still thinking about her bicycle.

    Chief: Didn't the chain and some of the gears look rusty?

    Chief: I should put in a request with the Supply Office when I get back, get some new parts, and replace them for her next time...

  - Stay


  <br>

  Chief: It's no trouble. I don't have much on my schedule today, so I might as well stay and help out.

  Chief: What part needs cleaning next?

  Dove: Well, if you insist, Chief... Would you mind applying some oil to the cleaned chain?

  You spend the entire afternoon working together, finally finishing cleaning the bike.

  As the sun sets, Dove rides her freshly reassembled bicycle across the grass, waving at you with a smile that makes her look like a free little dove.
