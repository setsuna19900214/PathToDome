# Shalom Supervision System

### 「Human Observer」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠

Since who knows when, Shalom has started to call herself the president of the "Human Observation Society," even though she's only been listening to vinyl records and watching movies in the Recreation Area every day. Amazingly, it didn't take long before she gathered a bunch of club members.

- Club members? More like minions.

### 「Bad Role Model」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

Since Shalom came to the Bureau, she's been ignoring all rules and regulations. This "laid-back" style has attracted the attention of all the Sinners. Some reckless Sinners start to imitate her... and the results are as expected.

- Better not to follow her lead.

### 「Plant Slayer」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area / Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

[System Notice] Warning! The key monitoring subject, Shalom, has recently met with multiple contacts in secret room. It is suspected that a handover of items has occurred for unknown purposes. Please intervene as soon as possible!

Chief: Just watching the footage, I can't make heads or tails of what Shalom's handed over to them. Anyone she trusts enough for these errands must be pretty good at keeping secrets...

- Check Security Footage

  Chief: Looks like we've got only two plays here: catch them in the act or just go ask them straight up...

  Shalom: So you're combining both tactics. Solid thinking, savvy choice. You're really coming into your own... Quite impressive.

  Having figured out Shalom's exchange schedule and habits with the other Sinners, you catch her red-handed at the entrance of the secret room, just after her "smuggling" escapade.

  Chief: Given you seem pretty impressed with me, you might spill the beans, right? What's with all the hush-hush? What are you guys really trading? Better not be anything against the rules.

  - Cut to the chase

    Shalom: Don't worry, I've got nothing to hide from you, and I won't play word games to skew your thinking... Take a good look and see for yourself what I've got here.

    Chief: ...This is... an air plant? The kind of plant that lives just on a mist of water?

    Shalom: Heh, a touch disappointed? There's no big plot brewing here. My little clandestine meet-ups are just down to a dash of vanity...

    Shalom: Let's face it, not being able to get even a hardy cactus to flower isn't something to brag about. And switching out room plants all the time? Doesn't exactly scream "respect for life," does it?

    Chief: Is this why you've been all hush-hush, getting other Sinners to sneak Tillandsias in for you...

    Shalom: Bingo. Perhaps plants that can bloom have their own sense of pride, shunning care from those lacking heart or soul... But... I still want to give it another shot.

    Shalom: Until I can grow a gorgeous flower myself... You, who've peeled back my scars and seen the truth, maybe you'd be up for being my "partner in crime"?

### 「Filling the Gap」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

System has identified an attempt by certain Sinners, including Hella, to congregate and view movies with inappropriate content. Swift measures have been taken to halt this activity.

Chief: It seems like such things have become a lot more common since Shalom started letting other Sinners borrow from her movie collection... Maybe it's time to cut off the supply?

When Shalom joined the Bureau, she didn't leave behind her massive trove. She has a wide range of interests and her collection is all-encompassing, which explains why Sinners keep ending up with some weird reads and flicks.

- Go find Shalom

  Shalom: Hmm... I get where you're coming from and I'm happy to lend a hand. But you know, my stash is a mixed bag from different eras and sources. Combing through it all on a tight deadline? That's gonna be tough.

  Chief: But we can't have Hella and young sinners treating your stash like their jackpot. Seems like a few of them are purposely picking out some stuff that's really inappropriate for their age. This should really be stopped.

  Shalom: How about we flip the script? Let's think about it this way. There's meaning behind every move... Their "calculated" choices could be pointing you towards the right course of action.

  Chief: You mean...?

  - Doubt

    Shalom: If they're intentionally going for something not suitable for them, it only means they don't really know what they should be reading or watching at this point. They need a nudge in the right direction. Maybe we can get the staff to do some edutainment that speaks their language?

    Shalom: Curiosity about the unknown is just part of being human. If you want to do good to them, steering them in the right direction works way better than just blocking stuff... You get what I mean, don't you?

    Chief: You know, your point actually makes a lot of sense. I'll step up our borrowing checks and at the same time, get the team on board to guide and educate.

    Shalom: I'm happy to help. Chief, I don't like seeing you so weighed down all the time... Take it easy. Why not see this as a chance for something new?

    Shalom: Everyone who visits my collection has a purpose. We're always chasing what we don't have... And that's how you can really get to know Sinners better, by seeing what they choose.

    Chief: I've been wondering... why collect all these different kinds of music, movies, and books, only to lend them out willy-nilly? Are you trying to find something in them? Maybe something you're missing yourself?

    Shalom: Hehe, relax, it's not a battle. Music, movies, and books are all means people use to express their thoughts and feelings. And the way people choose and connect with them? That's like a shared emotional echo.

    Shalom: I've never really meddled in the lending process, just watched from the sidelines. And even if I get what I want out of it... it's not like it's going to hurt you, right?

### 「Gift」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

It's almost Shalom's birthday, and you want to get her a small gift.

Chief: But what would she like...

Chief: She seems to be good at photography and flower arrangement? Maybe you can try your luck.

- An instant camera

  You've ordered a brand-new instant camera, a type of camera she's never used before.

  On her birthday, you find her in the library. She's engrossed in a book about porcelain patterns, but she immediately notices you.

  Shalom: Good afternoon, Chief. Can I help you with anything?

  Chief: Nothing in particular... Wait, you're interested in porcelain now?

  Shalom: Not really, I'm just interested in new things I've never come across before.

  Chief: Is that so? Then you might like this...

  Chief: I heard you're good at taking photos, so I got you a camera. It's a bit different from the ones you usually use though.

  You pull out the box-like device, and Shalom smiles gently.

  Shalom: Indeed, I've never used this kind of camera before. You can get the photo immediately after taking it? It sounds perfect for me, thank you.

  Shalom: Would you mind if I take a picture of you?

  Chief: Of course.

  Although it's her first time using this camera, unlike a beginner, she operates it skillfully.

  After a few adjustments and the flash of light, a photo comes out from the bottom of the camera.

  Shalom: ...Let me share a little secret with you, Chief.

  Shalom: I have taken photos for many people. But... most of them... have already left this world.

  Chief: ! ?

  Shalom: But I hope you can be a beautiful exception, Chief.

- A bouquet of flowers


<br>

After much consideration, you pick a large bouquet of flowers and put it in a gift box.

On her birthday, you find her in the library. She's engrossed in a book about porcelain patterns, but she immediately notices you.

Shalom: Good afternoon, Chief. Can I help you with anything?

Shalom: Ah, don't answer me just yet.

She closes her eyes and takes a deep breath.

Shalom: It smells wonderful. It must be from the box you're holding.

Chief: Yes.

Chief: A lot has happened these days... Let's commemorate it with this bouquet.

You place the box on the table and open it, instantly filling the room with the scent of flowers.

At the same time, you see a smile on Shalom's face that you've never seen before.

Shalom: It seems you do understand me quite well... I really like it, Chief.

Shalom: I don't think I've told you why I like flowers.

Shalom: I'll tell you just this once.

She pauses for a while, as if to tease you, then keeps talking.

Shalom: I believe that flowers are like people: the language of flowers is the language of people, and the fragrance of flowers is the heart of people.

Shalom: Emotions, personalities, thoughts... all of these things fascinate me. Don't you think so too?

Chief: While I may not love flowers as much as you do, I can somewhat understand what you mean.

Shalom: Hehe. Do you really?

### 「Little Trick」

Location: Secret Chamber

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Recently, you've started to find Shalom's behavior suspicious.

After some investigation, you find that at a specific time every day, she always sneaks off to a corner of the secret room, doing something secretive.

Chief: This doesn't look good.

- Take action

  You time her entrance into the secret room and prepare to ambush her in there. But to your surprise, she's already inside.

  Shalom: Didn't expect this, did you, Chief?

  Shalom: Hehe, what a cute expression.

  Chief: ...What exactly have you been doing here?

  Shalom: Do you really want to know, Chief?

  Shalom: As the saying goes, curiosity killed the cat... Knowing too much may not necessarily be a good thing for you.


  - Just tell me.

    Chief: What if I still want to know?

    As if she's been waiting for you to say this, Shalom pulls out a notebook from behind her.

    Chief: What is this?

    Shalom: My diary.

    Chief: ...So you've been sneaking around here just to write your diary?

    Shalom: Hehe. Chief, your expression right now... it's quite amusing.

    Shalom: It's been a long time since I've seen you looking both nervous and curious, yet also somewhat worried.

    Shalom: What a rare sight...

    Chief: ...So you've been doing all this just to see my reaction?

    Shalom: Exactly, dear Chief.

  - Never mind.
