### 「Magical Pockets」

Location: Training Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Recently, the Bureau has introduced some delicate multifunctional training equipment, leading to a new rule in the Training Room: Sinners must temporarily hand over personal electronic devices, such as mobile phones, to the staff for safekeeping during training sessions to prevent signal interference with the equipment.

Chief: Shawn, please hand over your phone as well.

Shawn: Is it mandatory?

Chief: Yes, it's the new rule of the Training Room.

Shawn: ...Alright. But can I send a message to my darlings first?

Shawn: They'll get worried if they suddenly can't reach me for hours.

Chief: ...Alright, alright.

You stand quietly with the bag. After she finishes sending her messages, she puts the phone inside the bag.

You nod and turn to leave, but she grabs your arm.

- Hmm?

  Shawn: Wait, there's more.

  Chief: ???

  You watch in astonishment as she pulls out four other phones from the pockets of her coat, shirt, and pants.

  Shawn: Alright, that's five in total, all here.

  Chief: ...

  Chief: This outfit sure has a lot of pockets.

### 「Hydrate」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Today, while taking a stroll in the Bureau, you receive a voice message from Shawn on your terminal.

- Play

  Shawn: A quick reminder—the temperature's really gone up. Be sure to drink plenty of water to stay cool and avoid heatstroke.

  Chief: ...Being thoughtful, huh.

  Chief: It's been quite hot lately. The heat always leaves you feeling thirsty. The Recreation Area is just ahead; You decide to get some water from there.

  When you enter the Recreation Area, you see Shawn on the couch, chatting with several people on multiple phones.

  When you greet her, she hastily says "Just a moment" and then dives back into her phones.

  Shawn: Haha, how could I ever find you annoying? I want to know everything about you.

  Shawn: Do you know? You're the most special one to me.

  Shawn: Hmm. I'll be here whenever you need me.

  Shawn: ...

  Chief: She's really busy...

  While getting water, you notice Shawn clearing her throat before sending a voice message, her voice sounding a bit hoarse.

  Chief: Is she thirsty?

  You mumble, then look back at her. Her chapped lips seem to confirm your guess.

  - Get her a cup of water too

    Chief: And here you are reminding me to stay hydrated. Your voice has turned hoarse.

    Chief: Here, have some water.

    You offer the water to Shawn, but she simply stares at your hand with a complex expression.

    Shawn: ...I don't drink anything that's handed to me.

    Chief: Ah...

    You then remember that Shawn has never accepted water, beverages, or soup handed to her; she only drinks water boiled from her own kettle.


    - Withdraw your hand

      Chief: Sorry, I forgot about that.

      Chief: I noticed your voice wasn't quite right and your lips were chapped, so I thought you might need to hydrate...

      Chief: Remember to boil some water for yourself later.

      Just as you are about to withdraw your hand, Shawn suddenly holds on to it and takes the cup. She then hesitantly takes a sip.

      Shawn: But if it's from you, I guess I can give it a try.

      Shawn: ...Thank you.

    - Explain


    <br>

    Chief: Don't worry, the water is from that dispenser over there. No one would dare mess with the drinking water in the Bureau, so it's safe.

    Shawn: ...

    She stares at the cup in your hand for a long time, your arm getting sore from holding it.

    In the end, she finally takes the cup from your hand and hesitantly takes a sip.

    Chief: That's right. Drinking more water is good for you.
