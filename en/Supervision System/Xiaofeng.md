### 「Dressing Like a Human」

Xiaofeng's fashion sense has always leaned toward the careless side, but today she's outdone herself in dishevelment.

Her limbs emerge from entirely the wrong openings, while an assortment of accessories has tangled itself into a chaotic knot.

- Call out to her

  Chief: Whoa, Xiaofeng... did you get dressed in the dark?

  Xiaofeng: Huh? What's the problem? Isn't this how I always look?

  She doesn't seem the least bit bothered, casually running her fingers through her equally messy hair. Though on the bright side, it really shows off how healthy it is—no tangles whatsoever despite rarely being combed.

  Chief: Yeah, but today's next-level messy... Are your clothes on backwards? I can't even find the sleeves.

  Xiaofeng: Sleeves? Uh... sleeves... Oh! Sleeves! Here, look!

  After some fumbling and tugging, she finally manages to work a corner of the sleeve free from where it was tucked around her waist.

  Xiaofeng: You humans and your fancy clothes. So many holes, and every piece has a front, back, left, and right! What a pain!

  Xiaofeng: And don't get me started on all these strings, buttons, and weird little pockets! Even tying a knot has a million ways to do it!

  Xiaofeng: I just throw 'em on however. Still look pretty good, right?

  You note her healthy, radiant complexion and strong physique. The rumpled clothing certainly adds a touch of wild beauty to her appearance. But...

  Chief: Yeah, you actually pull it off. But at least wear things right-side up. You're gonna trip at this rate.

  - Help her fix her clothes

    Surprisingly, Xiaofeng doesn't put up a fight. She obediently raises both arms and lets you fuss over her clothes and accessories.

    After you've finally wrestled the red threads, ribbons, and chains into order, you glance up and catch her quietly sniffing your hair. A guilty look crosses her face; she stares skyward and starts whistling.

    Chief: ...How's it look now?

    Xiaofeng: Whoa! You're something else!! It actually looks better!

    She shakes off the awkward moment at once, twisting and turning to admire what you've done.

### 「Big Cat」

You're hurrying to work when you spot Xiaofeng, leaning against her tiger, the two of them dozing side by side in perfect contentment under the sun.

Sensing your gaze, she stirs, propping herself up halfway to wave at you. Her beast-taming bells chime softly with the movement.

Xiaofeng: Hey, Gray Hair! C'mere, let's soak up some sun together!

- Approach

  Chief: You two really get along!

  Xiaofeng: Mm-hmm. Tigers raised me, after all.

  The tiger looks your way, eyes slitting with lazy contentment as she tucks her paws beneath her belly. Xiaofeng copies it without missing a beat, arms folded under herself as she lounges across the tiger's plush fur.

  Chief: So tigers tuck their paws too, just like cats?

  Xiaofeng: No danger here, so why not kick back and relax?

  Xiaofeng: And hey, you've got it backward. Cats are the ones copying tigers!

  She gestures for you to lie down with them. You figure you're close enough by now that it should be safe. Throwing caution to the wind, you settle beside them.

  Xiaofeng's fluffy head immediately finds you, nuzzling close and rubbing against your cheek. A rumble rises from her throat, deep and rough, yet you can just make out the affection beneath it.

  The tiger's low purr also travels through her fur, seeping into your chest. Two humans and one tiger, content in the quiet together.


  - Let me pet the big cat!
