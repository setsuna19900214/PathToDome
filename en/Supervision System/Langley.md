### 「Coffee Room Chat」

Isomer's cup of black coffee looks bitter enough to be ink. Langley is calmly grinding beans, each motion as deliberate as if she were handling classified intel.

Isomer: I have nothing against hand‑ground coffee... but if the goal is waking up, the process could stand to be a little more efficient.

Langley: You're not wrong. If all you want is a jolt, plain black coffee works. But coffee's value isn't limited to staying awake.

- Step in and grab a cup

  Langley: What do you think, rookie? As fellow leaders who rely on coffee to get through the day, we'd love your take.
