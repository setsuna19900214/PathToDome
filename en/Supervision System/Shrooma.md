### 「Mushroom Feast」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You walk into the kitchen and spot Shrooma standing on a small stool, emptying a basket filled with mushrooms onto the preparation area.

- Greet

  Chef: Chief, you're here. The mushrooms today are exceptionally fresh. How about we have a mushroom feast for dinner?

  Chief: Where did you get all these mushrooms?

  Chef: Shrooma deserves all the credit for helping me find such a great mushroom source.

  Shrooma: There were so many fresh mushrooms growing in the greenhouse. It felt like a shame to let them go to waste, so I washed them up and brought them to the chef.

  You gaze upon the cooking table piled with an array of mushrooms, each displaying vibrant hues and emitting an almost eerie yet captivating beauty.

  Chief: These mushrooms have such vibrant colors. It's my first time seeing ones like these.

  Shrooma: Of course, I chose the prettiest ones to pick!

  Chief: Hold on! You picked these?

  Shrooma: Yeah, see this red one? It looks so adorable, like a little red umbrella on a rainy day.

  As you gaze at the mushroom that Shrooma mentioned, memories of a peculiar and funny Eastia folk song, frequently sung by OwO, come flooding back to you.

  ...

  *Red umbrella, white stem, let's share together.*

  *Then we'll rest, under nature's cover.*

  ...

  - Stop the chef from cooking

    Chef: Chief, you don't like mushrooms?

    Chief: These mushrooms might be poisonous. Please pack them all up, and I'll have the medical staff collect them later.

Urgency: ⚠⚠

### 「Picky Eater」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

You see Shrooma sighing at the food on her plate.

- Ask

  Chief: Did you come across something you don't like?

  Shrooma: Chief, can you make sure that broccoli never appears on my plate again?

  Chief: The Bureau's recipes are designed by professional nutritionists, ensuring they are nutritionally balanced to cater to your daily training requirements.

  Shrooma: But I don't like broccoli. I want to eat my mom's onion meat rolls every day.

  Chief: ...

  Chief: But this diet is tailored for you. What if you don't end up growing tall from being a picky eater and not getting enough nutrients?

  Shrooma: You adults can be so hypocritical. You have foods you don't like as well...

  Shrooma storms off in frustration. Faye offers a comforting pat on your shoulder as she walks past.

  Faye: For children, being picky at times is normal, but Chief, perhaps you could consider preparing the food she doesn't like in a different way.

  Recognizing the validity of Faye's suggestion, you and the nutritionist work together on improving some recipes.

  Shrooma: I'm really glad, Chief. I can tell you did take my suggestion to heart.

  Chief: Yes?

  Shrooma: The Bureau's menu now includes meat rolls. Hmm, not as delicious as my mom's, but the flavor overall is quite close. I'm satisfied.

  Chief: How so?
