# Celine Supervision System

### 「Living Search Engine」

In the Bureau's reading nook, Christina paces in front of the shelves, visibly straining to remember a book.

Christina: I remember a detective novel about a wealthy man who vanishes with a fortune in Hypercubes... and in the end, the reader—meaning you—is the killer. What was it called again?

- Ask Celine

  Celine: *You Are the Killer*, by Angela P. Lorder.

  Christina: Yes, yes, that's the one! Thank you, Celine!

### 「Natural Born Driver」

The Bureau is hosting an online tournament for *Ultimate Racing: Skyline*, and after enough pestering from Nino, Celine finally gives in and signs up.

Nino: Yes! A gentle, soft‑spoken Head Librarian paired with a technical gaming powerhouse like me—this tournament is going to be legendary!

Celine: The livestream does sound fun... I just don't have much confidence in my skills.

Nino: Relax! With me carrying us, first place is guaranteed!

In Nino's streaming room, the two pick up their controllers and get started.

Nino: Hahahaha! First place again, just as expected! Eat my dust! Celine, where are you?

Nino: Wait, you haven't even finished the first lap?!

Celine: Sorry... I'm not much of a driver. Apparently not in games either.

A flicker of dejection crosses Celine's face, and you decide to help.

- Recommend a different game to Celine

  Chief: How about *Death Rally: Violence Frenzy*? Maybe your driving talents will shine there instead.

  Celine: ?

  A month later, in the Bureau's online *Death Rally: Violence Frenzy* tournament, Celine takes first place with the highest Violence score.

### 「Nobody's Wrong」

Inside the Bureau, Roulecca and Dolly are arguing. Celine stands nearby, listening quietly.

- What just happened?

  Roulecca: Don't pin the armored car accident on me! I clearly heard Dolly shout "left"!

  Dolly: I didn't say that! I said there was an enemy on the left, so you should watch out. How was I supposed to know you'd yank the wheel and drive straight into a wall?!

  Roulecca: Hmph. I heard it plain as day. Wait—Celine was in the car too. Let's ask her!

  Dolly: Yes! Head Librarian Celine's memory never fails. She'll prove I'm innocent!

  - Hear what Celine has to say

    Celine: I believe... neither of you misremembered. You simply interpreted the same call differently.

    Celine: For future field operations, perhaps we could agree on this: the spotter reports enemy positions using clock directions only. How to maneuver from there is left to the operator's judgment.

    Celine: That way, the call stays precise without prompting an instinctive reaction to the word itself. What do you think?

    Roulecca: Not a bad idea! From now on, if you want Roulecca to shoot somewhere, just use that fancy lingo.

    Dolly: Got it. Next time I'll call "enemy at nine o'clock" instead of "left." Roulecca, I—

    Roulecca: No worries. Roulecca doesn't sweat the small stuff. Besides, the Chief's covering the repair bill for the armored car anyway, right?

    Celine: What do you say, Chief?

    - Add Celine's suggestion to the protocol

      You incorporate her suggestion into the Sinner field operations manual. According to the data department's feedback, mission success rates rise by about three percentage points.

### 「Fireproof and Waterproof」

Several design blueprints are spread out before Celine and Flora, who are deep in discussion.

Flora: Containing the fire immediately while keeping the tomes intact... that's a tall order.

Flora: Inert gas suppression is one option, but parts of the Bureau aren't sealed well enough. Used carelessly, it could cause oxygen deprivation.

Celine: I once read about chemical agents that vaporize rapidly and absorb heat. Could rapid cooling drop the temperature below ignition?

Flora: You're on the right track! Beyond those agents, we could also consider a water‑mist system. It's less demanding on the environment, though there's still a risk of dampening the tomes.

Celine: If possible, I'd prefer the tomes come away without any water marks. Even the slightest trace would be a shame.

- What are you chatting about?

  Celine: Chief, we're discussing a fire suppression system suited for library areas—one that can fight fires while keeping the tomes safe.

  Flora: The proposal is still in discussion. If you're willing to approve a modest research budget, I can run a small‑scale trial and see if it's worth implementing.


  - Approve the funding

    You approve the funding. The fire suppression system Celine and Flora developed works exactly as hoped, and the Bureau's server center adopts it as well.

    Celine later brings the proposal to the Central Library and secures a collaboration. The finance department sends its sincere appreciation for your discernment.

  - Reject the funding


  <br>

  The Bureau's budget has been stretched thin lately. Somewhere in the corner, the budget sheet quietly weeps. So you let them down gently and decline the request.

  Celine eventually brings Flora's proposal to the Central Library instead—with the promise that if the research succeeds, the Bureau will be the first to benefit.

### 「Bookplate」

Celine sits at the reading nook table, several illustrated catalogs open before her. Her fingertip rests on a vermilion seal impression.

- Ask what she's looking at

  Celine: Oh, Chief. This is an Eastian seal catalog Yanyan brought for me. In Eastia, people carve their name, their study's name, or a motto into a seal, then press it onto their books.

  Celine: Unlike DisCity's pasted bookplates, you stamp it by hand—a small, personal pact between reader and book.

  On the desk sits a seal stone, a carving knife, and several draft sketches. She's clearly put a lot of thought into this.

  Celine: I'd like to carve a bookplate seal of my own. I just haven't decided on the inscription yet.

  Celine: Chief, would you help me choose?


  - Celine's Collection

    Celine: Celine's Collection... A traditional Eastian bookplate—simple and clear. It suits my work well.

    Celine: You know your Eastian culture, Chief. A fine choice.

    Celine: If you don't mind, stay a little longer? I'd love your thoughts on the carving style, font, and border.

    Celine: Having you help makes it all the more special.

  - Read Once, Remembered Forever


  <br>

  Celine: Read Once, Remembered Forever... It's not about owning the tome. It's about carrying what you've read long after the pages close. A very Eastian sentiment.

  Celine: How does that saying go? "Books pile up like mountains, but reading through them is like pulling silk." Sometimes I get so caught up in collecting that I forget to actually read.

  Celine: A common affliction among readers, perhaps. So then, Chief... stay and read with me?
