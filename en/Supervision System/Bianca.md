### 「Naturally Feisty」

Bianca: Lalala, lalala, I'm a professional journalist!

Bianca: Oh! It's you! Good morning, Chief!

This morning, you bump into Bianca humming a song in the corridor. She runs over to greet you cheerfully.

You can't help but envy her energy and decide to ask her for her secret.

Chief: Morning, Bianca.

Chief: Can I ask how you manage to stay so energetic all day?

Bianca: Of course! First off, get plenty of sleep!

Chief: True, sleep is important.

Bianca: Go to bed before 1 a.m., wake up at 6:30 a.m., a good five-and-a-half-hour sleep!

Chief: ...? Is five and a half hours enough?

Bianca: Yep, you can sleep a bit more, like six hours, but definitely not more than that! It'd be a waste of time!

Chief: ...A waste of time?

You start to feel something is off, but she continues excitedly.

Bianca: After breakfast at 8 a.m., I go on missions; if there's no mission, I catch up on the news. Then I walk around the Bureau to jot down what I can gather.

Bianca: I lunch at noon, then train at 2 p.m. The time in between is great for hanging out with friends, or hitting the dance machine in the Recreation Area for some exercise.

Chief: So, you don't rest at all during lunchtime...

Bianca: After dinner, I watch a movie with everyone, listen to some music, play a few rounds of games, have a couple of drinks, and then work on my social media account...

You finally conclude that Bianca is born with this boundless energy. Her experience and schedule can't provide any useful information.

### 「Visiting Hours」

Bianca is one of the few Sinners who maintain good relationships with their families. Today, her parents are visiting her at the Bureau again.

Bianca's Mother: Bianca, my darling, it's been almost a month since we last saw you. Do you miss your gorgeous mom?

Bianca: Of course, Mom! Your new dress is so pretty, it's dazzling!

Bianca's Father: What about me? You can't forget about your dad just because your mom is here!

Chief: Hmm...

Bianca: Well, I've been training every day, writing news articles, and then hanging out and playing games with my new friends here. It's been so much fun!
