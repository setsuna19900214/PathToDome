### 「Devil or Angel」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

A crowd of stray animals has recently moved into the area surrounding the Bureau. To prevent these *uninvited guests* from damaging sensitive equipment or coming to harm, the Bureau has decided to organize a capture operation to neuter and rehome them.

Many Sinners have eagerly volunteered for the task, and Synex is no exception.

Chief: ...You're not planning something devious, are you?

Synex: Oh my, Chief! Your words wound me deeply! Everyone knows I'm the biggest animal lover in the Bureau! Nothing can stop me from feeding and caring for them diligently every day!

If scaring small animals almost to death counts as caring for them... You keep the sarcastic remark to yourself. With no real reason to say no, you approve Synex's participation in the operation.

- Approve

  The results come unexpectedly fast. In less than a week, every stray animal in the area has been rounded up. Mission accomplished! You fall into stunned silence as you watch Synex standing before you with a dog in one arm and three cats in the other.

  Chief: That's it?

  Synex: Gotta be quick to save these little homeless darlings, right?

  Staff A: Thanks to Synex... the capture operation has been extremely efficient!

  Chief: Oh? What makes you say that?

  Staff A: Strays like these are usually incredibly difficult to catch. They can be hiding in every nook and cranny, staying hidden all night long. The moment you look away, they're gone.

  Staff A: But since Synex joined us, all these animals, whether they were running around, hiding in holes, or perched in trees, all fled as if they'd seen their worst predator! All we had to do was set up nets at the end of her path and wait.

  Chief: You really are like the devil of the animal kingdom...

  Synex: I'd say more like a guardian angel?

  Synex: After all, these fragile creatures are barely meaty enough to serve as snacks for an apex predator. So when a powerful beast encounters such tiny critters, it'll tend to handle them... with more care.

### 「Vigilant Tail」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Synex: Hey Chief, you're here for the meeting too?

- Say hello

  You nod in response, but your gaze is involuntarily drawn to the large tail swaying behind Synex.

  You've long noticed that Synex's tail seems to have a will of its own. It sometimes rises up and sometimes droops low, occasionally thrashing about like a massive whip. It doesn't appear to move entirely in sync with Synex's moods either.

  During a recent combat mission, her tail became alert on its own and pulled you back to safety when a stray bullet was flying your way.

  Synex: If you like my tail, just say so, Chief. I don't mind letting you touch it.

  She gives a few impish chuckles, then half-jokingly swings her tail, letting it brush lightly against your arm.

  - Change the subject

    Chief: Ahem... Now that everyone's here, shall we begin the meeting? Today's agenda is...

    The meeting proceeds as planned. However, you soon notice something strange. While Synex appears calm and attentive, the tail behind her is swishing so rapidly it's almost become a blur! This is exactly how she reacts when detecting a major threat. Could she be warning you about something?

    Your heart tenses with alertness, and you immediately scan the entire room without showing any outward concern. Under the desk, the projector, filing cabinets, even behind the dense potted plants... Your gaze sweeps across every corner of the room, yet you can't detect even the slightest sign of danger.

    Synex quietly leans in close to your ear.

    Synex: Chief, have you noticed anything suspicious?

    Chief: ...No, have you?

    Synex: That's right, I've made a critical discovery! Look at this sentence on the screen. There's a hidden message.

    Chief: Uh-huh.

    You hold your breath and stare intently at the presentation, not even blinking as you wait for her to reveal this *major threat*.

    Synex: According to the mission logs, A and B were out on assignment last Saturday afternoon, right? But I got a tip that B was actually on a date with C at that trendy café downtown at the exact same hour!

    Chief: Huh?

    Synex is completely absorbed in her gossip discovery, her tail now standing straight up in excitement, slapping against the chair back and swishing at lightning speed.

    Synex: How could someone be in two places at once? Go on, guess. Did B get someone to cover his shift so he could sneak off on a date? Or maybe... B has a secret twin brother nobody knows about? Oh my, this goes deeper than I thought! Tsk tsk...

    Chief: ...

### 「Hair Loss Crisis」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Sinner Synex is at the clinic to seek treatment for severe hair loss. Would you like to visit her?

- Go

  You push open the door and are instantly greeted by Synex's wildly disheveled hair sticking out in all directions. On top of her head, a small patch of her scalp is almost visible through thinning hair.

  Chief: How's it going?

  Doctor: We've determined that excessive salt intake is causing the hair loss. This Sinner's unique physiology appears to lead to an intolerance to foods high in fat and salt. She should follow a customized meal plan prepared by our nutritionist going forward.

  Next to the doctor, Synex deflates further at these words. You rarely see her looking so utterly dejected, with her head hanging low like this. It's almost comical.

  Chief: I'll have special meals prepared for you. From now on, you're on a low-fat, low-sodium diet. And don't even think about trying anything clever. I'll be keeping my eye on you.

  Synex: What a shame. I was planning to sample every delicious dish the Bureau had to offer...

  Her protests fall on deaf ears. The next day at noon, you arrive at the cafeteria as promised and watch Synex dutifully finish every last bite of her healthy meal.

  Then comes the third day, the fourth day, the fifth day... Until finally, several months later, new hair is reappearing on that small bald patch on Synex's head.

  Chief: Alright, the ban is lifted. But don't forget this hard-learned lesson. Be more mindful of what you eat from now on.

  To your surprise, instead of making her usual bizarre remarks, Synex pulls out a fuzzy wool felt keychain and dangles it in front of you.

  Synex: It's a memento I made with my fallen hair. Garofano showed me how. What do you think? Looks just like me, doesn't it?

  A memento? To remember losing so much hair? You see Synex's proud expression and swallow your deadpan comment.

  Chief: It does look like you.

  Synex: Why don't you have it then, Chief? Sooner or later I'll shed hair again, so I can just make another one.


  - Accept

    Chief: Sure, perfect. It'll remind me to keep a closer eye on you.

    Synex: My absolute pleasure.

  - Reject


  <br>

  Chief: Sorry, but I can't accept it.

  Synex: Care to explain why? Is this measly gift from a lowly Outland commoner too cheap for our DisCity bigwig?

  Chief: The memento should stay with you, so it can constantly remind you to take good care of your hair. I'd rather not see your hair flying all over the Bureau again.

### 「Seeds of Discord」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

The Bureau has received a shipment of rare goods. Word spread quickly, and by early morning, there's already a long line of people outside the Supply Office. You see the supplies are nearly sold out and are about to leave empty-handed when Synex waves to you from a distance.

Synex: Hey! Chief, here to check out the new goods too?

Chief: Yeah, heard Faye got her hands on some good stuff this time. But I'm late... probably won't be able to get any now.

Synex shrugs, but her eyes dart around restlessly. It's obvious she's cooking up some mischief again.

Synex: Don't be so sure.

Before you can object, Synex pulls you into the line with her. She makes a shushing gesture, her tail perking up with mischievous intent.

Chief: What are you gonna do?

Synex: Shh... Now, just sit back and enjoy the show...

A ringtone sounds. The person standing in front of you reaches into their pocket and pulls out a comms device, looking utterly bewildered.

Staff A: Uh, what is this...

Staff B: Hey, isn't that my comms device? Did you just pickpocket me?!

Staff A: Of course not! I have no idea either! How did this thing end up in my... Ow! What's your problem, shoving me like that?!

Staff B: Who shoved you? Making a scene now, aren't you, thief?! I'm reporting this to my supervisor. The Bureau can't tolerate petty theft like this...

The two keep taunting each other, and with neither willing to back down, that's a fistfight waiting to happen there.

Watching them, Synex is laughing so hard she's nearly doubling over, her troublemaking tail swishing wildly behind her.

Synex: Hahaha... See? Isn't this fun? Once those two get dragged away for fighting, you'll have two competitors eliminated without lifting a finger...

Chief: ...
