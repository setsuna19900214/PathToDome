Today, you receive a document from Norma: *Proposal on Low-Frequency Noise Reduction*.

Norma: May I bring your attention to this, Chief? I believe irregular low-frequency noises have a lasting interference effect on the Sinners' conditions. In severe cases, it may even increase the probability of violent incidents.

- Review

  Chief: Loose screws in the B3 sector ventilation ducts, overall deterioration of the top floor exhaust fans... How did you even find all this?

  Norma: It's quite simple. I conducted multiple detailed inspections, then cross-referenced the data to isolate the sources of anomalies.

  Norma: I can conduct the same inspections in the office area as well if you deem it necessary, Chief.

  - Thank her

    Chief: Thank you, but I'm not particularly sensitive to noise. I'll have the detention area's equipment optimized as soon as possible.

    Chief: ...Though if you really want to continue with this project, go ahead.

    Norma: Understood. I'll get on it right away.

### 「Quantifying Emotions」

After a moment's thought, you nod. But Norma doesn't seem satisfied with this answer.

Norma: I can understand the confusion that arises from your first encounter with the emotion quantification device... What I'm more curious about is the source of your resignation.

- Doubt

  Chief: Can human emotions really be detected and analyzed with quantified data?

  Norma: A person's heart rate, pupil dilation, body temperature, and respiratory frequency all change according to their emotions. I believe that with a sufficient sample size and a stable database model, these subtle changes can be identified and categorized.

  - Compliment her

    Chief: I look forward to seeing your results.

    Norma: Current reading indicates 70% appreciation, and... unidentifiable components.

    Norma: Thank you for your cooperation, Chief. I'll continue collecting more samples to refine these undefined emotion categories.

### 「Unreasonable Request」

Ever since Norma joined the Bureau, you frequently run into her around the office area.

Normally, you both just exchange brief nods after making eye contact, but today, Norma stops in her tracks.

Norma: Chief, could you please refrain from causing congestion in the corridor?

Chief: ...?

- Why?

  Sensing your obliviousness, Norma begins laying out her argument seriously in front of the other onlooking Sinners.

  Norma: The optimal route from the detention area to the office area overlaps considerably with your daily area of activity, Chief. However, you seem to constantly trigger various incidents that result in large-scale gatherings of Sinners. This negatively impacts the Bureau's internal traffic.

  Norma: This phenomenon significantly slows down the circulation of staff and disrupts the overall workflow.

  Norma: Based on the above analysis, I hope you could take this issue seriously, Chief.

    Norma: I will consider adding daily social interactions to my schedule and logging my observations.
