### 「Inescapable Romance」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Lynn: Director... The camera crew is ready, but we're still waiting on our cast. Where's our female lead?

OwO: W-what?! Did I forget to cast one...? No worries, it's fine. There's plenty of talent here... Oh! That pretty blonde lady over there looks like a natural star! Come, I can cast you as the lead!

A bewildered Milly is dragged into the film crew.

Lynn: Director! What kind of movie are we shooting? I can't wait!

OwO: No rush. Let me think... Since we're entering the genre market, how about a thriller? No, wait, perhaps one of those critically acclaimed art films that nobody watches...

OwO: You're our lead. What kind of movie would you like to make?

Milly: I'm up for anything! This is my first time acting, so I want to try it all!

OwO: W-well then, little clapper! You decide!

- A thriller.

  After a lot of scrambling on set, Milly, dressed in a tight leather combat suit, holds a plastic gun and crouches solemnly behind a wall.

  OwO: The mood is perfect, hold it! Okay, next up... The blonde agent shoots the villain from behind the wall and moves in to check the body.

  Milly fires the prop gun, then neatly jogs over to the fallen villain.

  OwO: Good, keep it up! Now search the body for ammo and supplies... Wait! Why are you crying over the villain?! No, no, that's not it!

  Milly has thrown herself onto the villain's body, tears streaming down her face.

  Milly: The day you asked me to run away with you... I said no because... I thought it would save us both. I had no other choice...

  Milly: But... I was a fool. I only realized what I'd lost after you're gone. I swear to you, the gun that took your life will put a bullet through my heart as well. I...

  OwO: CUT! You can't change the script like that! We're making a thriller, and now it's turned into a romantic drama! One more take!

  But you notice that no matter how many times you reshoot, Milly always finds a way to insert a romance plot into the most unlikely scenes.

  In the end, the exhausted director gives up. When the movie is screened at the Bureau, it's officially classified as a romance.
