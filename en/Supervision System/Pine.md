### 「Dimensional Pockets」

Location: Training Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

As you wrap up a post-training analysis session with Pine, your eyes are drawn to her outfit—covered in an unusual array of pockets.

Noticing your gaze, Pine arches an eyebrow.

Pine: Out with it. What else do you want to ask?

Chief: Alright... I've been meaning to ask for a while now—just how much can those pockets of yours hold?

Pine lets out a scoff, her tone carrying a mix of pride and mockery.

Pine: Not that much, really, but still more practical than that sorry briefcase you carry around.

Chief: ...

Pine: Fine, I'll humor your curiosity.

Seemingly amused by your speechless reaction, a smirk plays across Pine's lips. She begins pulling various items from her pockets and tossing them onto your desk.

Pine: Ten miniature listening devices, ten micro cameras, a voice recorder, a backup recorder...

Pine: A jumbo power bank, charging cables, an ID holder, my launcher...

You stare in disbelief at the growing pile.

Chief: ...Don't tell me you're carrying your entire arsenal with you.

Pine responds with a noncommittal shrug and continues rummaging through her pockets.

Pine: I'm not done yet. Hold on... Ah yes, and here's the crown jewel—three backup phones.

Chief: You... you even have backup phones?! Three of them?!

Pine: What else would you expect? Many places don't allow phones inside, but with my setup, even if I voluntarily hand one over and another gets confiscated, I still have two more ready for action.

You stare at the equipment strewn across your desk, a blend of amusement and disbelief crossing your face.

Chief: ...I'll give you this one—your pockets are the real secret weapon here, aren't they?

Pine flashes a defiant smile as she returns each item to its designated pocket.

Pine: My investigations rely on wit, not gadgets—though it never hurts to be prepared, you know.

### 「A Secret on the Tree」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

On this clear day, a group of little Sinners fill the Outdoor Activity Area with vigor as they fly their kites in the breeze.

Sinner A: Run faster! Faster!

Sinner B: Haha! It's flying! Look, guys... Ah!

An abrupt exclamation pierces through the air, causing the cheerful atmosphere to evaporate instantly.

Chief: ...Is there anything wrong?

- Go check it out

  Sinner B: ...Chief! Our kite is stuck up there. Could you please help us get it down?

  A child runs up to you, gently tugs at the hem of your clothes, then points at a nearby tree with a worried expression.

  Following the child's gesture, you look up to find their swallow kite hopelessly hooked by a branch.

  The branch is far too high—even after several determined jumps, you still can't reach it.

  Chief: It's okay... Don't worry. I'll go find a stick...

  Pine: Don't bother.

  After some rustling in the leaves, a purple-haired head suddenly pokes out from the dense foliage. In the next moment, she grabs the kite and leaps down, landing as gracefully as a cat.

  Chief: ...Pine?!

  After the children thank you both and run off, you stop Pine as she is about to walk away.

  Chief: Hold on, Pine! Care to explain why you were up in that tree?

  Pine: Enjoying the view.

  You find Pine's answer rather suspicious. After all, this tree stands right in the center of the lawn, with its unobstructed crown providing a perfect view of the entire Outdoor Activity Area.

  Chief: Are you sure you weren't spying on anyone? Or have you planted cameras in the tree again?

  Pine: Hmph, as if I had time for that nonsense.

  She lets out a dismissive snort and ignores you, stuffing her hands in her pockets before striding away. Something about her retreating figure suggests her trying a bit too hard to look innocent.

  Chief: Looks like I'll need to grab a ladder and check up there myself...

### 「"Fuel"」

Location: Management Center / Black Market

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Today, you have received the quarterly expense report from the Supply Office, detailing everything from equipment purchases to personal items requisitioned by Sinners.

Chief: ...Hmm? What's this?

As you quickly flip through the pages, the section related to Pine suddenly catches your eye.

Chief: Nothing but instant noodles and lollipops? And the quantity—several boxes every month? How does she manage to consume so much?

Chief: Is that all she eats every day?

- Check Pine's cafeteria consumption records

  As you suspected, you can probably count on one hand the number of times she has visited the cafeteria in the past few months.

  Chief: Could it be that she doesn't like the cafeteria food?

  - Talk to Pine about it

    After searching all over MBCC, you finally find Pine nestled in a quiet corner, thoroughly absorbed in her laptop.

    Pine: This lead is promising... Just need to spice it up a bit, and it'll be enough to bring them down completely...

    Pine: That hypocritical mask... Soon I'll strip away every last bit of it...

    Chief: Ahem, Pine... Do you have a moment...

    Pine: Speak.

    She responds without looking up, her fingers continuing to fly across the keyboard.

    Chief: I was thinking, maybe you're not a fan of the cafeteria food?

    Pine: Not really.

    Chief: Is that so? Then why have I barely seen you at the cafeteria all month, while you've been living off instant noodles and lollipops?

    Pine: First of all, your cafeteria's hours are too rigid. Most of the time, I miss the meal periods because of work.

    Pine: Plus, how can cafeteria food compete with instant noodles? Just add hot water anywhere, anytime, and you're good to go in five minutes.

    Chief: But don't you ever get tired of eating the same thing every day?

    At your question, Pine pushes up her glasses and finally looks up, giving you a genuinely puzzled look.

    Pine: Why would I get tired of it? It's just fuel to keep my body running. One type's as good as another.

    Chief: ...

    Chief: That's certainly the first time I've heard someone refer to food as "fuel"...


    - Try to reason with her further

      Just as you're about to launch into a speech about the importance of healthy eating habits, she cuts you off.

      Pine: Stop. I know what you're going to say, but could you please wait?

      Pine: I'm extremely busy right now, and you're breaking my concentration by talking to me about this trivial matter.

      Pine: ...Fine, if you absolutely must lecture me, at least let me finish writing this article first.

      Chief: ...How much longer do you need?

      Pine: An hour at most. You can head back first—I'll come find you when I'm done.

      She pulls a lollipop from one of her pockets and tosses it to you, then waves you away with a shooing gesture.

      Pine: I solemnly swear on this lollipop that I won't forget. Happy now?

      Chief: You better not forget this time...

      You let out a sigh and turn to leave, eyeing the lollipop in your hand with deep doubt.

    - Very well, then.


    <br>

    You know well enough that she won't listen to anything you say right now, so a tactical retreat seems best for the moment.

    Chief: I'll leave you to it. Good luck with your writing.

    She gives you a quick thumbs up before hunching back over her keyboard, fingers flying across the keys with rapid-fire clicks.

    Chief: ...

    Chief: ...She really shouldn't keep surviving on nothing but instant food and sweets like this.

    The thought lingers in your mind as you make your way back to your office.

    Chief: Maybe I could ask the cafeteria to prepare some nutritious grab-and-go options tailored to Pine's particular needs?
