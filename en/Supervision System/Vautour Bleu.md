# Vautour Bleu Supervision System

### 「Taste」

Vautour Bleu often orders all sorts of Eastside desserts through the Supply Office. You've had the chance to taste some, but you really can't handle how sickeningly sweet they are.

- To each their own.

### 「Stand-in Accountant」

You're just worrying about who will handle the accounts during the Bureau's accountant's two-day sick leave when Vautour Bleu volunteers to take over the work. After agreeing on her compensation, she gets everything done with remarkable efficiency and accuracy.

- She's worth every penny.

Staff Member: Yes, I've attended my nephew's wedding. Thank you for your suggestion! They loved the gift.

Staff Member: Here, I've brought back some wedding candy for you...

Taking a walk in the Outdoor Activity Area, you notice Vautour Bleu exchanging friendly greetings with everyone she meets.

Despite having been in the Bureau for only a few days, she seems to have already memorized the names of every Sinner and staff member and know them well enough.

- Greet her

  Chief: Good afternoon, Vautour Bleu.

  Vautour Bleu: Oh, what a coincidence! Out for a walk as well?

  Chief: Yes, I saw you chatting with everyone from down the way.

  Chief: Looks like you're settling in quite well. You've gotten to know so many people in such a short time.

  Vautour Bleu: Hehe, it's a habit I've developed over the years.

  Vautour Bleu: Whenever I meet someone new, I make sure to observe and remember everything about them. Their preferences, their habits, what they desire...

  Vautour Bleu: After all, investing in people's hearts is always the best bet for one's capital.

### 「Massage」

Today, you're personally attending Vautour Bleu's regular psychological consultation. Used to her warm and friendly public demeanor, you're surprised to notice traces of fatigue in her expression now that you're alone together.

- Ask if she's fine

  Chief: Vautour Bleu, you don't seem to be feeling well today?

  Vautour Bleu: Really? I hadn't even noticed myself.

  Vautour Bleu seems taken aback by your words as you gesture at her face.

  Chief: You've got dark circles under your eyes. Did you not sleep well last night?

  Vautour Bleu: Hehe, paying close attention to me, aren't you?

  She smiles, tilting her head slightly as she considers for a moment.

  Vautour Bleu: Hmm... Now that you mention it, I did sleep in a weird position last night. My neck and shoulders are rather stiff.

  Chief: I see... Looks like a massage is the only therapy you need today.

  - Offer to give her a massage

    Chief: If you don't mind, I could give you a massage.

    Vautour Bleu: Oh, that'd be my pleasure, little Chief.

    You move behind her and place your hands on her left shoulder, beginning to massage it carefully.

    Chief: ...Is this alright?

    Vautour Bleu: A bit harder, please.

    Chief: OK.

    ...

    Chief: Feel better now?

    About twenty minutes later, you withdraw your now-tired hands. Vautour Bleu rolls her neck and shoulders slightly.

    Vautour Bleu: Thank you so much. That was wonderfully relaxing. I had no idea you were so skilled at massage, Chief.

    Chief: I wouldn't say skilled. I just spend a lot of time at my desk, so I've learned to work out the knots myself.

    Vautour Bleu: Is that so... Well then, next time you need a massage, why don't you let me return the favor?

    Vautour Bleu: I'd be more than happy to be of service, dear Chief.

### 「What Dessert Are You?」

After spending some time with Vautour Bleu, you notice she has quite an unusual habit when it comes to addressing people.

Chief: Vautour Bleu, may I ask why you always use desserts and sweets as people's nicknames?

Chief: Ignis is Little Pudding, Mess is Macaron, Hamel is Soufflé, Eureka is Popping Candy, and so on.

Vautour Bleu: Hehe, well... Don't you think they're quite felicitous?

Vautour Bleu smiles and explains her reasoning to you patiently.

Vautour Bleu: Ignis makes the most divine caramel pudding in the Bureau. It's simply impossible to get enough of it. And she herself is just like her signature dish: a bit reserved, but quite the sweetie underneath.

Vautour Bleu: As for Mess, with her vibrant outfits and accessories, and that perfectly colorful personality, what else could she be but a macaron?

Vautour Bleu: And Hamel, isn't it obvious? Her beauty and that delicate, dreamy air about her make her a soufflé.

Vautour Bleu: And Eureka, she's truly one of a kind. She may look meek and unassuming, but she'll surprise you with an unexpected pop and fizz once you get to know her.

You find yourself nodding along, intrigued by her convincing analysis.

### 「One-Day Observer」

In the ever-bustling Recreation Area filled with chatter and laughter, Vautour Bleu sits alone in a corner booth, swirling her glass and looking out of place amid the lively atmosphere.

- Go and chat with her

  Chief: Hi there, Vautour Bleu.

  Chief: They've just installed some new entertainment equipment in the Recreation Area. Looks kind of fun. Wanna check it out?

  She waves off the suggestion with a gentle smile.

  Vautour Bleu: Oh no, I should just let the young ones enjoy it. I'm quite content just watching from here.

  Vautour Bleu: By the way, wouldn't you get into trouble if your adjutant doesn't find you at your desk working?

  Chief: Ahem... Well, these documents aren't particularly urgent, so I thought I'd sneak in a quick break.

  Vautour Bleu: Perfect timing then. Care to join me for a while?

  She pats the empty seat beside her. Once you're settled, she produces another drink as if by magic and presses it into your hands.

  Vautour Bleu: Do you know what I love most about this place of yours?

  Vautour Bleu: These kids here, most of them have such clear goals and dreams, all brimming with hope for tomorrow.

  Vautour Bleu: There's nothing I love more than watching people chase their dreams with such burning determination.

  Chief: ...You prefer to keep your distance, don't you? Watching everything from the sidelines?

  Vautour Bleu: Indeed I do. You should try it sometime.

  Vautour Bleu: Step off the stage and just become an observer who watches how the story unfolds. It's a good way to pass the time.

  Taking Vautour Bleu's advice, you step into the role of an observer, joining her in watching everything that's happening in the Recreation Area.

  You find yourself tensing up as you watch the competitive games, your heart warming at the sight of people sharing snacks. You can't help but smile when someone's playful antics send their companions into fits of laughter.


  - I think I understand now.

    Chief: It's quite an unusual feeling... All these details I'd never noticed before are suddenly coming into focus.

    Chief: This way, you not only see more, but in a sense, you live more too.

    Vautour Bleu raises her glass and gently clinks it against yours.

    Vautour Bleu: That's exactly the feeling. You get me now.

  - Don't you ever feel lonely?


  <br>

  Chief: In the end, these are other people's stories we're watching. At its core, none of it truly concerns us.

  Vautour Bleu: Loneliness is perhaps inevitable at first, but you grow accustomed to it. In fact, what follows is a liberating peace.

  Vautour Bleu: Besides, I have your delightful company today, don't I?

  Vautour Bleu stretches contentedly.

  Vautour Bleu: It's getting late... Shall we have dinner together?
