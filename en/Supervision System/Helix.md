# Helix Supervision System

### 「Violation」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

A staff member finds Helix in her room, arranging shredded paper into symbols characteristic of the Underground.

- Confiscate the items

### 「Special Request」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

Helix has submitted a request to you. It contains only one line: "Please allow me to contribute to the Great Presence."

- Reject and tighten supervision

### 「Peers」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠

Helix shows curiosity toward other Sinners in the Bureau around her age, but every time she tries to strike up a conversation with them about her faith, it ends in failure.

- Encourage her to socialize normally

### 「Wet Clothes」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

After the rain stops, you see Helix walking slowly down the corridor, drenched from head to toe. Water still drips steadily from her hair and sleeves.

She walks slowly, her wet shoes making faint splashing sounds against the floor, yet she seems in no hurry to do anything about it.

- Express concern

  Chief: Helix, why don't you go get changed?

  Helix: Why? It's not like the clothes are getting in my way. Just a little irritating when they cling to me.

  Chief: If it's uncomfortable, that's your body telling you something.

  Staying in wet clothes could make you sick. And it would make people worry about you.

  Helix: Worry...?

  Helix: Is something this small really worth your worry?

  - Keep persuading

    Chief: Of course it is.

    Chief: Not everything has to be a trial or training. Taking care of yourself doesn't mean turning your back on your faith.

    After your persuasion, Helix finally changes into a fresh set of clothes.

    When she returns to the activity room and quietly listens to the others chat, her expression seems a little softer than before.

### 「Crack Treatment」

Location: Hypnotherapy Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

During the routine checkup, the medical team discovers that the porcelainized cracks on Helix's body have visibly deepened. Some of them have reached the areas around her joints. Immediate medical treatment is recommended.

But Helix is extremely resistant to treatment. She even starts cursing the staff who try to approach her.

Unable to get close to her, the medical team has no choice but to halt the procedure and request your intervention.

- Convince her

  Chief: Helix, if the cracks keep deepening, they'll affect your ability to move. This has to be treated now.

  Helix: No! These cracks aren't ordinary wounds...

  Helix: I won't let you destroy these marks!

  - Force treatment

    Helix's obsession runs too deep for any reasoning. On the medical team's strong recommendation, you make the unilateral decision to put her through forced treatment.

    From that day on, every time Helix sees you, she glares at you fiercely while muttering under her breath.

    Helix: ...Bearer of the shackles... deceiver of lambs... Despicable!

    Since Helix shows no further aggression or destructive behavior, you choose not to pursue the matter further, only noting her hostility for future observation.

### 「Family」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Helix still hasn't given up trying to explain the ideology of the Underground to fellow Sinners her age. Strangely enough, though, she does seem to have made a real friend—Thani.

You find this a little surprising. Out of concern, you decide to see how they're getting along for yourself.

- Go check

  When you find them, Helix is sitting across from Thani, speaking with grave seriousness about how this world is steeped in sin, and how only the arrival of the Herald can bring a truly just future.

  But Thani doesn't seem to understand what she means.

  Thani: Helix... have you been feeling down lately?

  Thani: Is it the food? Or is your room uncomfortable?

  Helix: That's not what I meant!

  Helix: Are you even listening? This world's been rotten for ages. The rules, the laws, everything—

  Thani: Then someone's been picking on you?

  Helix: ...No!


  - Step in to mediate

    Seeing Helix teetering on the edge of losing control, you decide to step into their conversation.

    Chief: Helix, please calm down.

    Helix: I am calm! I just want my friend to see the truth about this filthy world. Is that so wrong?

    Chief: Thani doesn't understand what you're saying. She just thinks something's bothering you. You shouldn't snap at someone who's worried about you.

    Helix looks away, murmurs a quiet reply, and pushes the dessert in front of her toward Thani.

  - Keep observing


  <br>

  Instead of approaching right away, you stay at a distance and keep watching how the two of them interact.

  Helix: You don't get it. Once you see how filthy this world really is, you'll stop falling for all those pretty lies.

  Thani: ...So is it a home you want? Do you want to be my family?

  Helix: What?

  Thani: Life in the refugee camp was rough, but everyone helped each other and figured out how to survive together. We were family.

  Thani: With family, you can be happy anywhere. And if you're not ready for that, we can just start as friends!

  Helix gives a small nod. It seems the two of them have reached some kind of understanding.

  You watch from a distance as they move on to other topics, and decide not to interrupt this strange but seemingly safe exchange for now.
