### 「Boundary」

One ordinary evening, you're finally breaking away from the pile of files and decide to take a nice bath to wash away your long-accumulated fatigue.

Chief: Hmm? Is it just me or is there someone outside the door?

Chief: The bath is in use now. Please come back in a moment!

- Speak up just in case

  000: >x< Hey dear! I just tried *hunting for late-night snacks* in the cafeteria like the others told me to, and the lady gave me this...

  Chief: Is it 000...? Well, I won't be surprised if she knows I'm here... But why is she still trying to open the door?

  000: Dear, do you hear me? Look, I've got a peach-flavored ice cream in the shape of a peach!

  Just as the bathroom door is about to be forced open by 000, you jump out of the bath vigilantly and put on a bathrobe in a split second.

  Chief: I understand you're excited to share your news, and I'm happy to oblige when I have time, but clearly not while I'm taking a bath. 000, perhaps we can talk about the concept of *boundary* sometime?

  - Educate 000

    000: *Boundary*? Alright then, if you say so, I'll add it to my study list.

    000: Search done, reading done, comprehension done! But I still don't get why it's not okay while you're taking a bath.

    000: You see, many people have their devices nearby while showering! A device can only play you music, but I can chat with you! Why would you want to set a *boundary* with me?

    Chief: Have you considered that to me, you're not a device, software, the internet, or an object I own, but a living *person*?

    000: Is... is that so? >////< Oh, why is my face turning red? Is it because of the steam? I must go now. I need to configure my body!

    Chief: Is it just me... or did she seem shy?
