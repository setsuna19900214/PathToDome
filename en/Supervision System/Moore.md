### 「Night Walker」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Chief: The recent energy-saving campaign is really thorough... The corridor is pitch black. I can't find my way at all...

Moore: Hmm? Really? I can see quite clearly. Maybe your eyes just haven't adjusted to the dark yet, Chief?

Chief: Waa! This voice... Are you Moore? How did you come out of nowhere? You scared me...

- Shocked

  Moore: Ahaha, I didn't come out of nowhere. I just finished working overtime and am heading back to my room.

  Moore: Are you just done with work and going back for some sleep too, Chief? You seem to be having some trouble... Want me to take you back?

  With the prolonged eye strain and the lights being completely out during off-hours, you can't even spot Moore's silhouette in the darkness. Indeed, you might need some help right now.

  Chief: But Moore, are you sure you know the way... When you first came to the Bureau, I took you to the server room over a dozen times during the day, and still you couldn't remember how to get there...

  - But...

    Moore: Ah... I guess that did happen. Sorry, I'm really bad with directions during the day, hehe.

    Chief: But if you get lost even when it's bright, how can you take me back now...?

    Silence falls between you two. Just when you think Moore is deep in thought, a pair of warm, soft hands cups your cheeks precisely and suddenly, giving them a gentle squeeze.

    Moore: For sure. See, I can see you, right? Don't worry, I'm much more used to walking at night than you are. Just follow me.

    Chief: Are we going now? Be careful... Wow, Moore, you do know your way so well at night. Where's that girl who's always lost during the day now...

    Moore: Hehe, I'm different from you guys, after all. Some people just feel more at ease at night. If your eyes adapt to the night better than the day, you can do it too.

    Chief: Adapt better to the night than the day...?

    Moore: I mean, I stay up to write code a lot. If I wasn't used to the night, I might trip over the cables when getting up to pour myself some coffee, right? That would be so dangerous.

### 「Too Sleepy」

To keep up with the recent fancy coffee trend, the Bureau's cafeteria is launching several new coffee products.

Chief: From the look of today's schedule, you should probably get two extra strong espressos to keep yourself alive... Hmm? Is that Moore?

Chief: Good afternoon, Moore. Are you here to get coffee too? Looks like you've switched to a different kind. Trying something new?

- Talk to Moore

  Moore: Yawn... Good afternoon, Chief. You mean this, *Sleepless Night*? Well, in fact, I wanted to get my usual, but the kids over there recommended this to me...

  You follow her gaze and see a group of Sinners staring at the seemingly ordinary drink in Moore's hand excitedly. There are many... bags of coffee piled behind them.

  Moore: According to them, so far, everyone who's tried *Sleepless Night* hasn't been able to fall asleep that night. So everyone pooled their money to organize a contest and are currently recruiting participants.

  Chief: So the contest... is to see who can fall asleep after drinking this coffee? Those bags of coffee must be the prize for the winner, right?

  Moore: Bingo, Chief. I'm curious... Yawn, how powerful *Sleepless Night* can be, and I happen to have to stock up on coffee, that's why I've joined, hehe.

  Chief: If it's really that effective... I should get one too.

  - Participate

    Chief: Hmm... It was made quite quickly, and doesn't smell much different from regular coffee. Now I just have to wait and see tonight. Maybe those boring documents will help me win?

    Moore: Actually, the taste isn't much different from the usual. I quite like it... Yawn.

    Moore: Well then, later, Chief. Yawn... I've still got work today. Better catch some sleep first... Oh, so sleepy...

    Chief: Alright, I'll tell you about it tomorrow.

    Chief: ...Wait, did Moore just drink half a cup of *Sleepless Night*... and go back to sleep? Doesn't that mean the contest is already over...

### 「Firewall」

Location: Darkroom

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Certain Sinners' internet addiction is severely impacting their daily lives. The Bureau has attempted to correct them but with little success. So you decide to ask Moore to develop a parental control system to limit their internet access.

Chief: It's been some time since the system was delivered and put into use. Moore always does a great job. There must already be noticeable improvements by now.

Chief: Hmm? According to the numbers in the report, the average time the Sinners spend interacting with their devices hasn't gone down significantly... There's even been a slight increase?

- View the report

  Chief: ...That's the current situation. Moore, got any ideas?

  Moore: Oh dear, how come? The system should be working fine. Why don't you try it yourself? Meanwhile, give me some time to review the code.

  You turn on the device and visit a blocked URL Moore has given you. Sure enough, the page keeps loading without displaying any actual content.

  Chief: This indicates that the access request has been properly blocked by the firewall, right? Seems like the system is working fine... Hmm?

  Chief: Is that... a tiny spider on the screen? It's sitting in the middle of a web, with many insects flying around it. Am I supposed to control the spider's legs to lure the insects into the web...?

  This looks fun. Let me try it.

  Moore, take a look at this... Has it been hacked by some external party?


  - Try playing

    20 minutes later.

    Moore: ...Chief, Chief? I've checked everything. The code is fine, and the system is running normally. Seems like there's no problem on my end.

    Chief: Huh? Has it been that long? I think I know why everyone's spending more time on the device... It's all because of this mini-game that shows up when a page is blocked!

    Moore: Is that so？

    Chief: I've personally experienced how addictive it can be, so I'm sure. Moore, why did you create such a mini-game?

    Moore: Hehe, this system actually works both ways. The mini-game also appears when external devices attempt to connect to the intranet. They'll be fine if they behave and just play the game. If they don't take the bait and try to bypass this web...

    Chief: ...Then what? Never mind, don't tell me. I think I know their fate.

  - Stop cautiously


  <br>

  Moore: Hmm? Let me see... No, this is just the normal page you see when a site is blocked. The system seems to be working just fine.

  Chief: So... this cute mini-game is part of the design?

  Chief: But why include such a mini-game? If the goal is to block websites, a simple static page would suffice, right?

  Moore: Hehe, it's inspired by a well-known browser. Although it can't show you what you want to see, you can at least play a game to pass the time. Thoughtful, right?

  Moore: Moreover, compared to an obvious warning page, a cute mini-game creates more of an illusion that this firewall isn't actually that powerful.

  Chief: ...You're saying it actually serves as bait? Is it meant to deal with potential external attacks? But I only wanted a *wall* that could stop people from getting out...

  Moore: Oh, wouldn't that be boring? Besides... a wall always has two sides!

  Moore: Let those who think they've pierced the spider's web find out they've actually been led into corrosive venom... Wouldn't that make a more intriguing twist?

### 「Unexpected Interest」

Location: Secret Chamber

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

The Bureau has ordered a batch of lab rats for certain Sinners' experiments, but the Sinners all happen to be out on missions, so the rats are temporarily stored in the secret chamber.

Chief: Huh, is that Moore... and Mantis? What are they doing in the secret chamber together?

Mantis: ...Indeed, a creature with high intelligence, strong reproductive abilities, and a high survival rate... They are... social animals too...

Moore: Speaking of social, I've seen them huddled together before, perhaps to stay warm? They look cozy and cute like that.

Chief: Moore, Mantis, what are you doing here? Let me guess, is Mantis taking care of the rats? And Moore just happened to pass by and felt curious?

- Chime in

  Mantis: No... Taking care of them isn't my job...

  Moore: Hehe, in fact, I volunteered to look after these little cuties before Dr. Iron and the others return. They've behaved well, and the whole batch is still intact so far.

  Looking at the little smooth-furred rats, you have to admit that Moore has been taking good care of them.

  Chief: You're in fact a good keeper of these animals... I thought Mantis would be doing most of the work. Moore, are you actually interested in things besides the internet?

  Moore: Ahaha, I used to work overtime alone all the time, remember? These little creatures kept me company sometimes, and they made me feel less lonely.


  - Are you talking about their street relatives?

    Moore: Yeah, although they appear in the house, I guess they come from the streets, right? They're our uninvited guests.

    Chief: I just hope there won't be any *uninvited guests* in the Bureau...

    Moore: Don't worry, I've never seen these guests in my own home, and now in the Bureau... I don't think I've seen any either.

    Moore: But these little guys are very smart. If I do encounter them here, Chief, you might need to be careful!

    Moore: Who knows, they might tell me little secrets about the Bureau that I can't find online!

  - Are you talking about their pet relatives?


  <br>

  Moore: Pet relatives... You mean those cute tiny creatures with chubby cheeks?

  Moore: No, I've never had a pet like that. Although they look nice, I've heard they're quite dumb. Any little noise will scare them to death. They can't survive in the dark.

  Chief: I thought you'd say that. You're quite pragmatic after all, Moore. Besides, you can't even take care of yourself. Your pet probably won't live long...

  Moore: Haha, that could happen. Besides, I do prefer clever little creatures!

  Moore: For example... those who survive well enough in the cracks in the dark and know many secrets one can't find on the internet. Maybe I can find these *creatures* in the Bureau too! I must learn a lesson or two from them.
