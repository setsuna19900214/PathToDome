### 「Prank Backfires」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

A young Sinner can't open a jar and comes to you for help.

Little Sinner: Can you help me open this jar? I'm not strong enough to do it.

- Help

  You're reaching out to help when a familiar figure stops you. He tips his hat with a gentlemanly flourish, revealing a face you know well. It's Rust.

  Rust: Allow me. It's my honor to be of help.

  Rust takes the jar and pries the lid open effortlessly with a mere flick of his fingers. Halfway through, he pauses, as if suddenly remembering something.

  Rust: Oh, I almost forgot to warn you. I hear some thugs have been lurking around here lately. Do be careful.

  Little Sinner: Oh... yeah? Never heard of that...

  Rust: I hear they disguise themselves as children to earn your trust. Then they ask you to help them open a jar, getting you to pry off the lid.

  The young Sinner breaks into a cold sweat.

  Little Sinner: I-I'll keep an eye out... Thank you, sir. And thanks for the warning...

  Rust: Ah, but I'm not finished. In the end... the victim will open the jar, inhale the gas inside, and begin laughing uncontrollably, making a complete fool of themselves in front of the whole Bureau... Am I right?

  Little Sinner: You...

  Rust secures the lid and places the jar back into the young Sinner's palm.

  Little Sinner: Wh-what?

  A giant fake mouse suddenly springs from the jar, sending the young Sinner scrambling in a panic. Rust withdraws his hand calmly and smooths out the wrinkles on his shirt from crouching.

  Rust: My apologies. Next time, consider picking a less dangerous target for your pranks.

### 「Autopsy Expert」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

An assembly of Sinners is detected. They're trying to watch TV. Go and maintain order?

- Yes

  This detective TV series has become a sensation in Eastside, and the Sinners at the Bureau are hooked as well. Their frequent watch parties have become a problem. To prevent them from disturbing others at night, you find yourself rushing over to keep order every time.

  Sinner A: Whoa... A plot twist! So this guy isn't the killer after all?

  Sinner B: So exciting! Who's gonna die in the next case?

  Amid the crowd, you spot Christina and Rust. Unlike the others caught up in the excitement, they are having some kind of serious discussion in a corner.

  Rust: If livor mortis is used to determine the time of death, then only one of those present had both the time and opportunity to commit the crime... But experience tells me that lividity isn't the only piece of evidence here.

  Christina: Exactly. Many factors can delay the onset of lividity. Low temperature, for instance, or repeatedly moving and repositioning the body. All of these can interfere with the estimated time of death. Given the killer's timeline, the actual death must have occurred at least two hours earlier than determined.

  Recently, Christina has noticed something peculiar about this Sinner called "Rust." He seems remarkably well-versed in the study of corpses. He knows exactly how a corpse changes under what conditions and in what timeframe.

  Rust: You have a point about low temperature delaying the lividity. Though... the actual feel of a body is different from what's shown on screen. The show makes it look too stiff.

  Christina: How do you know how it feels?

  Christina: And why do you know so much about corpses anyway, Rust? You're not a detective buff, and you certainly don't seem like an anatomist...

  Rust: Let's just say... I've had plenty of hands-on experience. After all, the best knowledge comes straight from the scene.

  The "scene"? Inspired, Christina rushes off in search of a fresh crime scene to study firsthand, but you quickly grab hold of her before she can put her newfound enthusiasm into practice.

  Chief: Easy there. The kind of "scene" he's talking about... probably isn't what you have in mind.

### 「Winner Takes All」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

The Bureau has been cracking down on high-stakes card games recently, but the prohibition has proven futile. Unauthorized games continue to spring up everywhere. Every day, the cafeteria is filled with pitiful Sinners who've gambled away their monthly budget, left to drool at the meat dishes with their empty pockets.

You resolve that this time, you will put a stop to it for good. Just then, someone volunteers to help...

Rust: Leave this to me, Chief.

Chief: You have a plan?

Rust: Of course. Though there will be... a small price.

He extends his hand to you.

Chief: ?

Rust: I'll need some funds to initiate the plan, Chief.

Chief: How much do you need?

Rust: Not much. Just 100 DisCoins.

A little skeptically, you pull out a bill and hand it to him.

Over the following week, true to Rust's word, the unauthorized card games vanish without a trace. A month later, the supervision system confirms that no one has attempted to organize or participate in any gambling activities.

- Ask Rust

  Rust: Gambling addiction thrives on the cycle of winning and losing. People keep chasing that chance to turn things around. The surest way to break that habit? Destroy the cycle entirely.

  Chief: Could you be more specific?

  Rust: To put it simply, I just made sure they kept losing.

  Chief: ?

  He looks at you and reaches into his coat pocket, producing a dazzling array of items, including dozens of glittering gems and gold bars, several luxury watches, keys to two sports cars... and even a deed to a property in downtown Eastside.

  Chief: You won all of this?

  Rust: You just need to know how to read people's faces. It's not a very special skill. But here's where it gets interesting: Should these be classified as "evidence" seized during an undercover operation, or "ill-gotten gains" I won by exploiting my position?


  - Evidence

    Chief: Evidence. These are worth a lot. Looks like the Bureau cafeteria won't be going bankrupt feeding all those gamblers who lost their food money. That's good news. How about a meal together? My treat, as thanks for your help.

    Rust: Dining with you would be my pleasure. But are you certain you wish to be seen with a man who just wiped out every unauthorized card game? After tonight, I suspect more than a few will speculate that the esteemed MBCC Chief has handled this with some... illegitimate means.

    Chief: Then let them speculate. Though indeed, if everyone knew I had such a capable operative working for me... it would only make your missions more difficult.

    Rust: I'm flattered, Chief. These words alone make me happy to continue being the ace up your sleeve.

  - Ill-gotten gains


  <br>

  Chief: We've got witnesses and evidence, and the ill-gotten gains are all right here. I think it's time to make some arrests.

  Rust: Every participant has been documented. If I recall correctly... the list should already be in your inbox, Chief.

  Chief: And I haven't forgotten there's a big fish sitting right here.

  Rust: The big fish? I believe he swam right into your net the moment he walked through your office door.

### 「Photoshoot Invitation」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

You receive a call from the editor-in-chief of a renowned men's fashion magazine. Over the phone, you learn that the editor encountered a Mania incident while out on an assignment and was rescued by Rust, who happened to be on a mission nearby.

Editor-in-Chief: The courage he showed swooping in to save me! That effortless poise! Those strong, reassuring arms... Oh, his silhouette has been seared into my very soul! And I swore to myself I simply must have this gentleman grace the cover of our magazine!

Editor-in-Chief: That's why I'm calling to make this rather bold request. If... we could have him as our cover star this month, it would be an absolute honor...

Chief: Well... I believe he should be the one making the decision. Let me ask him first, and I'll get back to you.

You hang up the phone and turn around to find someone sitting on the sofa behind you, elegantly swirling a glass of wine. You spread your hands with a shrug.

Chief: So, what do you think? They provide excellent accommodations and meals during the shoot, not to mention the pay is quite generous too. Hmm, looks like it's more than my annual salary... The only catch is the long shooting schedule.

Rust is in no hurry to answer. He swirls the wine in his glass, his expression unreadable.

Rust: Do you want me to go, Chief?

Chief: ...Me?

Rust: But of course. I'm yours now, after all. Whether I go or not is entirely up to you.
