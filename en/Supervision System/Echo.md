Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠

### 「Too Much Optimism」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

Echo meets a staff member at the door of the Chief's office. He has a lot of late clock-in records that need the Chief's signature to make up. The staff member is somewhat anxious and uneasy, and dares not go in to find the Chief. Echo encourages him to be bold.

Staff Member: ...I still want this job at the Bureau.

### 「Unfathomable」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠

The Bureau's cafeteria serves meals at fixed times every day. In principle, they do not wait for latecomers, but many Sinners do not eat on time. Only Echo never misses a meal.

Echo: Mmm! This sunny-side-up egg is cooked so well! The bacon is also very delicious! Auntie, can I have another... no, five plates?

Chief: Good morning, Echo, you always have such a big appetite in the morning, it's really enviable.

- Greet

  Echo: Because the Bureau's food is delicious! I could eat this delicious food all day, every day, and finish all of Auntie's stock!

  You thought her words were just rhetorical, so you laughed and sat down next to her. By the time you finish your coffee, Echo has already devoured three plates of fried eggs, bacon, and beans like a whirlwind.

  Chief: ...

  Chief: It seems like the lunch menu also suits your taste... I remember you have a few records of visiting the infirmary. Were those times because you overate and made your stomach sick?

  Echo: Hmm, I'm not sure why my stomach hurts, the doctor mentioned something about eating less but more frequently. I don't understand, if I eat less, wouldn't I be hungry! Besides, I love all the food that Auntie in the cafeteria makes!

  You remember, the cafeteria lady subtly mentioned once, Echo had eaten someone else's food. Perhaps, children who have starved don't have much control over their appetite.

  Chief: I'll have to remind the cafeteria lady to make a bit more...

  Chief: Echo, from now on, I'll accompany you to eat whenever I'm free. If I'm not free, just grab anyone to eat with you.

  Echo: Chief, do you think I eat too much?

  Chief: No, it's not about the food, we can afford to feed you in the Bureau. Someone has to be there to remind you about diet control.

  Chief: I want you to find someone to eat with because, isn't it more fun to eat delicious food with someone?

  Echo: Hmm... That makes sense! Besides, I've figured out all the delicious dishes in the cafeteria! The typhoon shelter prawns are fragrant and fresh, the steak is crispy on the outside and tender on the inside, I've never eaten meat this delicious before. Just dipping in the leftover sauce from the dish, I could eat a basket of bread!

  Echo: I can recommend all the delicious dishes to everyone! 10 out of 10!

  Chief: Yes, people who eat with Echo will definitely enjoy their meal.

  After a while, you go to inquire about the situation. Due to Echo's relentless efforts in finding dining partners, the cafeteria's dining rate has increased. Echo no longer appears in the infirmary with an overfull stomach.
