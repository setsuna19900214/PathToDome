# Murphy Supervision System

### 「Regulated Weapon」

On the list of newly added regulated weapons on the Bureau's bulletin board, Murphy's messenger bag makes an appearance.

- ...So this day has finally come.

### 「Before She Appears」

You hear a sharp clatter of metal, ceramic, and acrylic colliding against each other. The noise fades in, then gradually disappears toward the other end of the corridor.

- Murphy rushes this way, then tumbles that way.

### 「The Storage Room Incident」

You notice Murphy crouched at the end of the corridor, fiddling with something on the ground.

Her arms and knees are scraped up pretty badly. She doesn't look so good.

Murphy: Oh... Hi Chief!

Murphy: Is there a meeting coming up? I'll make sure to stay far away from the conference room!

- Ask about her injuries

  Chief: How did you get hurt this badly in the corridor?

  Murphy: Um... the floor was a little slippery. I tripped.

  - Really?

    Murphy nods vigorously, but the storage room door behind her slowly swings open, betraying her mercilessly.

    You take in the tilted shelves, the cleaning tools leaning haphazardly against the wall, and the miscellaneous items strewn all over the floor. Clearly, it wasn't just a simple slip and fall.

    Murphy: Hehe, guess you caught me, Chief.

    Murphy: So I opened the door and tripped over the broom that was sitting there... and then everything in the storage room just started falling like dominoes...

    Murphy: I thought you probably wouldn't believe me! And you're so busy, it'd take forever to explain...

    Chief: I believe you. The meeting hasn't started yet. Let me take you to the infirmary.

    Murphy: Aww... You're the best, Chief!

### 「Luck-Enhancement Ritual」

Murphy stares blankly at the notebook in her hands, brow furrowed as she runs her fingers through her hair, as if wrestling with her own thoughts.

Murphy: It must've been something important... I wrote it down and everything, so why can't I remember?

- Check on her

  Chief: What is it?

  Murphy: Chief, could you take a look at this? I wrote down something really important, but I was in such a rush that I only jotted down a few keywords...

  Murphy: But then my pen leaked and smudged out the whole middle section. I think it has something to do with you though. So maybe you can help me remember what it was.

  You take the notebook and see scrawled on it: *3 p.m.*, *Chief*, *luck-enhancement*.

  Holding it up to the light, you examine the page carefully and can just barely make out two more words beneath the ink smudge: *stay away*.

  - Make her tell the truth

    Chief: I can't make it out either, but you're not hiding anything from me, are you?

    Murphy: ...Oh! I remember now!

    Murphy: I read on the internet about a super effective luck-enhancement ritual I could do at 3 p.m. today, but it said it wouldn't work if someone important to me knew about it!

    Chief: Well, it's past 3 p.m. now. Did your ritual work?

    Murphy: Uh... I mean, you can't believe everything you see online, right? Besides, today hasn't been particularly unlucky so far, so I'll call it a win!

### 「Manifestation」

Murphy has been avoiding you these past few days.

Wherever you go, the moment you appear, Murphy makes a quick exit in increasingly spectacular fashion. She hasn't gotten hurt in the process so far, so you haven't given it much thought.

That is, until today, when you cross paths at the elevator. Head down, Murphy doesn't seem to notice you're there.

- Take the elevator

  Murphy: Ah! It's you, Chief!

  Murphy: Oh no, the doors closed! What do I do?! Okay, let's go to the nearest floor.

  Murphy: Haha, hi there, Chief...


  - Ask her directly

    Chief: Have you been avoiding me lately?

    Murphy: No, no! It's just... things have been going way too smoothly for me lately, and with my luck, that usually means something bad is about to...

    Before Murphy can finish her sentence, the elevator grinds to a halt with an ominous hum.

    Murphy: ...is about to happen. Ugh! I got you caught up in my bad luck. If I'd known this would happen, I would have just stayed in my room today!

    Murphy: And of course the emergency button's broken too... We're in trouble, Chief.

    Chief: It's fine. My personal device still works. Nightingale and the others will be here to get us out soon.

    Murphy: Really? ...I always feel so lucky when you're around, Chief!

  - Remain silent


  <br>

  When you don't respond, the atmosphere in the elevator grows a bit awkward.

  Murphy: I'm not trying to give you the cold shoulder or anything... It's just that if you're around me, you might get caught up in something weird again.

  Murphy: Lights might suddenly go out, or the elevator might get stuck, or worse...

  Murphy has barely finished her sentence when the elevator lights flicker back on.

  Murphy: Oh! It's working again! Then... then I should probably get going. Bye, Chief!

  Later that day, you receive a notification that Murphy has gotten herself trapped in an elevator all by herself.

### 「Meow-velous」

Murphy has recently taken to feeding the rescue kittens in the Bureau Recreation Area, though her attempts never seem to go quite smoothly.

Murphy: Just a little pat on the head, nothing to dread... Ow!

The little tuxedo kitten that has just been rolling over and acting cute suddenly springs up, knocking over the food bowl in the corner as it bolts into the bushes. Cat hair and kibble scatter all over the peaceful little nook.

- Hold back laughter

  Chief: ...Pfft.

  Murphy: Chief? I saw you laughing!

  Murphy: Okay, fine. It is pretty funny, given that I already picked the quietest, most comforting spot for the kittens, but it still ended up like this...


  - Try a different approach

    Chief: How about trying a different feeding strategy?

    You lead Murphy away from the corner and use a remote feeding device to get the job done.

    Chief: You won't be able to interact with the kittens directly, but at least it won't cause another disaster.

    Murphy: Let me try!

    Murphy takes the phone, and the interface suddenly glitches for a few seconds before barely managing to complete the feeding.

    Murphy: It's lagging a little, but it's still way better than me going near them myself! Thanks, Chief!

  - Help her


  <br>

  Chief: Let me help you. You always say I bring you luck, right?

  Murphy: Really...? Then just hold my bag for me, Chief. I'll clean up the mess in no time!

  You end up helping Murphy clean up anyway, and even pick out one of her many lucky charms to hang by the bushes.

  The next day, you receive a photo from Murphy of her with the kittens, along with a message.

  Murphy: Chief! Feeding time was a breeze today thanks to you!

  Murphy: But lucky charms' effect wears off over time, so next time, please come feed the kittens with me again, Chief!
