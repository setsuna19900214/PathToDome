### 「Food Safety」

The communal kitchen fridge has become completely full recently. The staff members cleared out a pile of unclaimed fresh ingredients, which the Whitestone crew has been eyeing for a while now. They're trying to get your permission to use them for a hot pot.

Che: Chief, just look at these leaves, and this tofu skin! They're so fresh! Not to mention these boxes of meat cutlets with such even marbling... If we don't finish everything, the leftovers can always be made into soup too! Of course, the first bowl will go to you, Chief!

Chief: The lost-and-found notice has only been up for half a day. What if the owners come looking for their stuff? You can't expect them to fish their belongings out of your hot pot.

Bai Yi: Oh, come on, Chief! You gotta seize the day! Just look at this quality meat—what if it goes bad? Think about what a waste that would be... You might as well hand it over to us!

Just as Che and Bai Yi continue their relentless pestering, Forensica pushes the door open and steps in. She glances around the room, her gaze settling on the bubbling hot pot and the boxes of meat slices beside it.

Forensica: What are you guys doing?

Che: Perfect timing! We cleared out a bunch of unclaimed ingredients from the fridge, and we're planning to have hot pot. Want to join us?

Forensica: Hot... pot?

Forensica: Oh, you mean... those few boxes of experimental samples I just put in there?

Chief: Samples?!

Forensica: That's right. Samples for supravital reaction observation, special toxin penetration testing, animal cadaveric rigidity comparison, ******* *******, ******* *********,  and so on. They all require experimentation on fresh tissue.

Forensica: Theoretically, the meat should be safe to eat after being sterilized at high temperatures. Just make sure you contact the infirmary before you dig in. Don't say I didn't warn you.

The Whitestone duo falls completely silent. They stare at the bubbling crimson broth as their faces drain of all color, turning from flushed to pale to a sickly green. After that day, they ate nothing but vegetables for an entire month.

As for you, for the sake of food safety at the Bureau, you approved an emergency budget that very night and had a brand-new dedicated freezer delivered to the premises.

### 「Battle of Disciplines」

Forensica rarely eats with others. The last time she did, you received a complaint that very evening. Someone reported that she had shown her colleagues photos of stomach contents from a corpse during the meal.

So, when she appears in the cafeteria, you're taken aback. But what surprises you even more is who's gathered around her—Iron, Anne, and Uni. The group seems to be talking to a young Sinner about career choices.

Iron: If you ask me, neuroscience is the pinnacle of medical studies. High returns, excellent job prospects... and if you enroll, you might even end up as my junior colleague.

Little Sinner: Oh...

Anne: Nursing is a wonderful choice too. Administering treatments, watching patients recover bit by bit under your care... it's incredibly rewarding.

Little Sinner: Wow!

Uni: What about cosmetic surgery? Eastside is perfect for that kind of business. With the right skills, you'll be raking in money hand over fist.

Little Sinner: Ooh, money!

Forensica: Don't listen to them. Forensic science is the most compassionate field in medicine. It also demands the highest standards.

Uni: And your reasoning?

Forensica: Sure, the workload is brutal, the pay is terrible, the environment is grim, and job openings are scarce... but it has one advantage no other field can match.

Forensica: Simple patient relations. Your patients will never file complaints, let alone show up and cause a scene.

Chief: Indeed... Forget complaints—your clients can't even open their mouths.

Forensica: So you see, forensic science is the best specialization in the medical field. This is an indisputable fact.
