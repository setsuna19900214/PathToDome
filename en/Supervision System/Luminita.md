# Luminita Supervision System

### 「The Art of Placement」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Outside Luminita's room, you catch her sorting through a box of strange little ornaments, ready to toss them all away.

She tells you they were sold to her cheap as "evil-warding" treasures, but after taking them home, misfortune followed her everywhere: shattered mugs, vanished keys, one thing after another. Clearly, they had been marked down for good reason.

Luminita: Cleansing, incense, prayers... I've tried everything I could think of, and none of it worked! They weren't exactly cheap either, but honestly, dumping them is the safest bet!

No sooner has Luminita finished than Chengxiao, who has appeared behind her unnoticed, extends his hand and plucks one of the small ornaments from the box, studying it with quiet attention.

Chengxiao: ...Misplaced. The energy's scattered... This one doesn't need to go.

- Inquire

  Chief: You're into these ornaments too?

  Chengxiao gives a measured nod, selects several ornaments from the box, and sets them on the cabinet in a careful sequence.

  They are the same odd little objects, but once he has rearranged them, there is a strange harmony to how they look.

  Chengxiao: The shape itself means nothing... it's the arrangement that matters... Like this... much better...


  - Suggest Luminita trust Chengxiao

    Several days on, you find that Luminita has stocked up on cheap ornaments, drawn arrangement diagrams, and started selling them together as curated sets.

    Chengxiao seems rather pleased with how things turned out.

    Chengxiao: Money follows the Dao as well... Used properly... it grows.

  - Leave


  <br>

  A few days later, as you and Chengxiao are having tea, Luminita shows up again.

  Luminita: Master! My hero! Can you show me your arrangement method one more time? I only tweaked the angle a little to make them look prettier, and now the bad luck is worse than ever!

  Chengxiao: The old omen has been disturbed... Keep them... and you'll only draw more trouble.

  Chengxiao: Get rid of them... and cut the karma.

  Rather than adjusting the ornaments again, Chengxiao helps Luminita dispose of them altogether. Only then does the wave of misfortune that had been following her finally break.

### 「Item Development」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Luminita: Hey Chief! What can I do for you today?

- Hand the complaint emails to Luminita

  Chief: I've been receiving numerous complaints from Eastian Sinners that you've been following them during their daily activities. It's starting to disrupt their normal lives.

  Luminita: Disrupting them? I'm so sorry! I never meant to bother our Eastian friends. I was just testing whether my talismans would work properly.

  Luminita: After all, a top-notch real estate agent must keep up with the times! What if clients from Eastia need my professional expertise in the future?

  - Question her

    Chief: I doubt your talismans would even work on DisCity's local ghosts, let alone those from elsewhere.

    Luminita: Absolutely not! If they didn't work, I would have lost my reputation as DisCity's premier haunted house agent ages ago.

    Luminita: I've even conducted surveys with several Eastian clients. They reported experiencing rapid heartbeats, dizziness, and tightness of the chest when I presented my talismans... Some even said they went to bed several minutes later than usual!

    Chief: Eastians are generally very polite. Maybe they just didn't want to disappoint you.

    Luminita: Hmph, polite or not, customer feedback is always the most honest feedback. If my customers say it works, then it works!

    Luminita: If you'd be willing to invest in my new talisman development, Chief, I could offer you a special discount on future purchases!

### 「Well-Read」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Luminita frequently visits the library to borrow books about various places' cultures and language learning materials.

Chief: Are you interested in the languages and cultures of other places?

Luminita: Absolutely! In a cosmopolitan metropolis like DisCity, how could I possibly provide the best service to my customers without learning the languages and cultures of other places?

Luminita: It's just that the Bureau's library collection is rather limited. Why not authorize my trip to the DisCity public library to borrow some books, Chief? I'll be quick about it—in and out, promise! And I'll make sure nobody thinks you're giving me special treatment.

- Question her motives

  Chief: What foreign languages have you studied? Can you demonstrate some for me?

  Luminita: Uh... that's a trade secret. Here, I'll whisper in your ear.

  Chief: ...These all sound like incantations for warding off evil spirits?

  Luminita: Exactly, which is why they can't be spoken carelessly. These incantations are extremely powerful, and there are taboos against reciting them casually. Now, about my request to visit the library...

  Chief: Once you've properly mastered the basics, I'll consider approving your request to leave the Bureau.

### 「Home Away From Home」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

You have a new email: Application for room change.

You have a new email: Chief! I need a new room!

You have a new email: Chief, although it's only been a week since my last room change, I really need to move to a new room as soon as possible...

- Confront the troublemaker

  Chief: Luminita, I've been getting constant room change requests lately. Have you been encouraging everyone to switch rooms?

  Luminita: Chief! I was just about to talk to you about this very issue.

  Luminita: I've been recommending new rooms to all the Sinners based on their living habits. Business is booming, with a 100% customer satisfaction rate!

  Luminita: It's just that a few clients are rather picky, they want to try out all the room options I've recommended before deciding where to settle down.

  Luminita: All these room change requests must be creating so much extra work for you, Chief! So I thought, why not delegate the authority and approval process to me? I promise! I'll handle everything perfectly! Everyone will be comfortable and satisfied, making the Bureau feel like home!


  - No.

    Luminita: Actually, Chief, I also have some excellent recommendations for your office.

    Luminita: A well-designed office can cut your fatigue in half, boost your productivity, supercharge your career fortunes, and double your chances of promotion and pay raises!

    Chief: No. Every room assignment goes through the Bureau's planning and approval process. They can't be changed on a whim.

    Luminita: What a shame! But if you ever change your mind, Chief, please come find me anytime! I'll arrange everything according to your personal preferences.

  - Hand over the keys.


  <br>

  Luminita: Hand over what? What are you talking about, Chief? Have you been possessed by a spirit that's making you talk nonsense?

  Chief: You're a little too knowledgeable about the details of my office and quarters. Have you already made copies of my keys?

  Luminita: What keys? I have no idea what you're talking about! But look, I brought gifts for you, Chief—a bracelet made from lightning-struck wood and an amethyst figurine. Both are excellent for warding off evil spirits. Which one do you prefer? Oh, who am I kidding? They're both for you!

  Chief: The staff members you've been bribing with these trinkets have already told me everything. Hand over the keys now, Luminita, and I'll let this slide for now.

  Luminita: As expected of our Chief, so fair and impartial! The keys will be delivered shortly. Hehe, I'll be on my way then! Do take care of yourself, Chief!
