### 「Fake At First Sight」

You hear a staff member calling your name.

Staff: Chief, you're just in time! Come and take a look at this painting.

- Go take a look

  Staff: I recently bought this from an art store, and it's a new work by a new pioneering artist who has recently become popular.

  Staff: Not bad, right? And there should be a lot of room for appreciation.

  Chief: Wait. Something's off.

  You receive the exquisitely framed painting from the staff, only to find residual reactions of Sinner ability on it. In order to verify your suspicions, you called McQueen.

  McQueen: Ah, oil painting apprasing? That's my specialty.

  She looks at the painting in your hand for a second, and then gives a sly smile.

  McQueen: You have indeed been tricked, the painting is a fake.

  Staff: What? This painting has a certificate of authentication from a professional institution!

  McQueen: I produce the top forgeries, and it's normal that those incompetent fools can not identify them.

  Chief: You made that?

  McQueen: Yes, but I didn't expect you to be able to identify it.

  McQueen: You know what, do you think it's because you've been with me for a long time that your eyes have also become sharper?
