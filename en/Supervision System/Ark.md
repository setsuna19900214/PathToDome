# Ark Supervision System

### 「Nickname」

Recently, Ark's earned the new nickname "Cabernet Junior" after she was reportedly seen smiling sweetly at everyone's photos while mumbling: "Drool... drool..." The vibe was very creepy...

- Tell Ark

  Ark: Huh? Someone needs to get their ears checked! I need to explain what "zulu zulu" means to everyone right now!

### 「Overkill」

You've noticed that recently, whenever Ark shows up at the shooting range, everyone immediately takes off frantically. Apparently, Ark always finds the practice gun not powerful enough and ends up hauling out her cannon.

- ...Firepower dependency is an illness.

### 「The Ark's Keeper」

[System Notice] Supervision System:

The Logistics Department reports that since Ark has participated in inventory and resource allocation tasks, the department's overall efficiency has doubled and error rate has decreased by 30%.

- Thank Ark

  Ark: Piece of cake! You can leave it all to me when it comes to resources. After all, I'm the one who runs the Ark!

### 「Broadcast Critique」

You happen to find Ark listening to DeadLover Radio. Her expression seems troubled and pensive.

- Do you like this radio program?

  Ark shakes her head resolutely.

  Ark: Their broadcast contains too little useful information.

  Ark: The two hosts often get carried away and forget what they're supposed to be talking about.

  Ark: This kind of broadcasting is unacceptable in WhiteSands.

  Chief: Maybe the banter and casual jokes are their selling point?

  Chief: The listeners in Syndicate need to unwind.

  Ark: Hmm... you do have a point. The harsher the environment, the more people need a bit of humor.

  Ark: Maybe I should try slipping in some jokes during my broadcasts too, but only after I've finished delivering all the important information.

### 「Homesick」

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Sinner Ark is detected to be still awake with heightened anxiety.

- Go and check

  Ark is curled up on her bed, head tucked between her knees. Her eyes glimmer faintly in the dim glow of the hallway's lights. It's already very late.

  - Having trouble sleeping?

    Ark: It's too quiet here. I can't hear the wind or the whoosh of the sand ship moving.

    Ark: The air here is so humid. When I breathe in, there's no dry scratch of sand in my throat.

    - Feeling homesick?

      Ark buries her head deeper, anxiety about being away from her sand ship evident in her tightly furrowed brow.

      Ark: I'm just worried about how everyone's managing without me. Have they been counting the supplies properly? Inspecting the weapons and artillery correctly? Are they fighting with each other...

      Chief: They're all family to you, aren't they?

      Ark: Of course! There are no slackers or weaklings on our ship! Everyone is so badass!

      - Smile at Ark

        Ark: I get what you mean. I should trust them more.

        Chief: Even the devoted mother hen of the Ark needs to relax sometimes, you know? Like getting a good night's sleep.

        Ark: Yeah. I'll try...

        With you by her side, Ark finally drifts into a peaceful slumber.

### 「Surveillant」

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Multiple newly admitted Sinners have reported Ark for spying on their activities.

- Talk to Ark

  Ark: The innocent have nothing to fear. If they have nothing to hide, why are they so bothered by me keeping an eye on them?

  Chief: That's not quite fair. It's perfectly natural for people to be upset when they're distrusted without reason.

  Ark's expression darkens, her usual cheerfulness vanishing as she fixes you with a piercing stare.

  Ark: Don't you think we're taking in too many newcomers lately? Chief, aren't you being a little too trusting?

  Ark: If some backstabbing bastard slips in, are you ready to deal with the consequences?

  Realizing her words were too harsh, Ark turns her face away.

  Ark: I'm sorry. I just... don't want you to go through something like that.


  - Thank Ark

    Chief: Now that you mention it, a few of the arrests did feel a little fishy. Thanks for your reminder.

    Chief: With you around, bad things like that are never going to happen to us.

    Chief: It's true that you can never be too careful. But there must be a gentler approach that keeps us safe from potential enemies without hurting friends. I'm sure you can nail that, right?

    Ark: Of course! I guess my approach was a bit too heavy-handed...

    You pat Ark's head, and the tension in her visibly melts away.

  - Comfort Ark


  <br>

  Chief: The Bureau has adequate contingency plans and extensive experience dealing with dangerous Sinners.

  Chief: There are many Sinners here who are just as kind and capable as you, Ark. Together, we all protect this sanctuary we call home.

  Chief: And if things really get dangerous, isn't that what you have me for?

  Chief: Hey, I'm the MBCC Chief who famously tore down the Black Ring. Trust me, will you?

  Ark: Fine... you are indeed incredible, and no one's tougher than you. But you must be careful!

  Ark: If real danger comes, I won't let it get to you.
