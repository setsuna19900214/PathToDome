### 「Woman with the Sunglasses」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You encounter a glum-looking Helga in the hallway.

- Inquire

  Chief: What's the matter?

  Helga: Ah, it's you, kid. Good timing. I was hoping to have a word. Lately, I always get the feeling someone's watching me. Take right now, for instance. Three o'clock, there's a kid pushing a cart, sneaking glances my way. I don't think I'm imagining it.

  You steal a glance out of the corner of your eye. Indeed, the person at three o'clock is looking in your direction. She didn't imagine it.

  Chief: It's true. That person is definitely watching you.

  Helga: Hmm... And it's not just the staring. Plenty of folks have been avoiding me, too. Last week, a kid practically jumped out of her seat just to get out of my way. Do I really look that unapproachable to the younger generation? ...Surely not?

  Helga removes her sunglasses and gazes into the distance with a heartbroken look. Gasps erupt around you. Everyone witnessing this scene has their jaw on the floor.

  Staff A: Helga... you can see?!

  Staff B: Oh my goodness, we've never seen you with your eyes open, or without those shades. We all thought you were blind...

  You exchange a glance with Helga, spotting the amusement in each other's eyes.

  Chief: So... They're all keeping an eye on you in case you need help, avoiding you so they won't bump into you, and giving up their seats in the cafeteria so you can have somewhere to sit?

  Helga: I wear these shades indoors only to protect my eyes. Never thought I'd be mistaken for a blind person... Guess I need a more obvious sign to clear that up.

  A few days later, you run into Helga in the corridor again. She's still wearing the sunglasses, but this time, no one's confused. Perched conspicuously above the frames is a sign that reads: *I CAN SEE*.

### 「Sharpshooter」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

A brawl has been detected in the Outdoor Activity Area. Go and check?

- Yes

  The moment you step into the Outdoor Activity Area, you spot Helga grabbing the shoulders of two grubby children, cracking jokes to ease the tension between them.

  Helga: Come on now, what's there to fight about? It's just a little shooting game...

  Young Sinner A: It's not that simple! Just now... if he hadn't pushed me, I would've won that plushie!

  Young Sinner B: I-I already said I didn't do it on purpose...

  Young Sinner A: But I only have three tries left. This target moves! It's so hard to hit! I almost had it just now...!

  So it's a squabble over a shooting game prize. Helga gives both children a reassuring squeeze on the shoulders, then picks up the gun.

  Helga: Easy now. Three shots are more than enough.

  Bang. Bang. And now each child clutches a giant plushie, running off while squealing with delight. Helga stays where she is, leisurely wiping down the gun before turning to you, the quiet spectator.

  Helga: So, what do you think, kid?

  You give her a heartfelt round of applause.

  Chief: Incredible marksmanship. All bullseyes.

  Helga: Ha! That's not what I was asking. What do you think of those plushies?

  Chief: ...The plushies?

  Helga: I've got one shot left. Want me to win you one too?

  Chief: Me...? No, I'll pass. I'm not a kid anymore. I don't need prizes.

  Helga: If I say you're a kid, then you're a kid. After a long day's work, you deserve a little something. That's my rule.

  Chief: Umm...

  Helga: What's there to be shy about? If anyone asks, just tell them your granny insisted.

  She raises the rifle, aims, and fires with practiced ease. The balloon pops instantly, the pellet piercing right through the bullseye.

  Helga gives the plushie a casual toss, and a quirky-looking hyena lands squarely in your arms. You look down at it, then back up at Helga.

[System Notice] Supervision System:

It is recommended that you follow Sinner Helga's daily schedule starting tomorrow to improve your physical condition as soon as possible.

Chief: ...Fine, I guess.

You decide to accept the challenge. After all, you refuse to be outdone by someone decades your senior. What follows, however, is a day of "health management" that completely redefines your understanding of the word "wellness."

7:00 a.m.

The alarm drags you out of bed. After a hasty ten-minute wash, you rush to the Outdoor Activity Area, only to find Helga already jogging in the morning light, a thin sheen of sweat glistening on her brow.

Helga: Morning, kiddo! Here to work out? Wanna join me?

Chief: Sure... Just let me know if you get tired. I'll pace myself to match you.

Helga: Oh, don't you worry about me. You set the pace. I'll keep up just fine.

07:30 a.m.

Chief: G-Granny... slow down, please... I really... can't keep up...

You can barely breathe, while Helga has already breezed through ten kilometers and moved on to a set of vigorous military calisthenics. She cheers you on without missing a beat, her voice strong and steady.

A sinking feeling settles in your gut, but this grueling day has only just begun.

12:00 p.m.

04:00 p.m.

The final drill ends. You collapse to the ground.

Helga: Want me to bring you along again tomorrow?
