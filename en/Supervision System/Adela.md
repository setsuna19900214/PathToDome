# Adela Supervision System

Urgency: ⚠

Soon after joining the MBCC, Adela has transformed her room into her new hair salon. She's even recreated the decorations of her original hair salon the best she could.

- Perhaps she's feeling nostalgic.

### 「Hair Savior」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area / Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠

Adela has recommended a suitable shampoo for each hair type, and the overall scalp health has significantly improved in the Bureau.

- Thanks, Adela!

### 「Hand Cream」

Location: Hypnotherapy Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Nurse: Your wound is a little inflamed. I've cleaned it and redone the dressing. Don't forget to drop by every day to change the dressing.

Adela: I will, thank you.

Nurse: By the way, avoid water before it heals completely.

Adela: It can't get wet, I see...

The conversation from the infirmary grabs your attention. When you get closer, Adela happens to walk out.

- Ask

  Chief: Adela, did you get hurt?

  Adela: Oh, it's nothing, just a small crack on the hand.

  Adela: It'll heal soon. Please don't worry about it.

  Adela reaches out the hand to you. There's a one-centimeter crack on her fingertip. Ointment has been applied, but it still looks a bit swollen.

  You also notice the skin on her hand is dry and rough.

  Adela: It's a bit annoying I can't get my hand wet though. I still gotta shampoo the hair of my clients...

  Chief: Why don't you take a few days off from the hair salon? Let your wound heal.

  Chief: Your hand is cracked and inflamed because you shampoo too many clients' hair every day, no?

  Chief: I keep a few good hand creams in my office, I'll bring them to you later. Remember to use them once your hand has healed.

  Adela: Got it. Thanks, Chief. That's too nice of you.

### 「Handywoman」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You've recently heard a rumor about Adela. She can seemingly produce anything from her counter.

Out of pure curiosity, you decide to go and verify the legends about this mysterious counter.

- See what Adela's up to

  Chief: Good morning, Adela. Busy today?

  Arriving at Adela's room, you find her humming a tune and cleaning her hair salon with a vacuum cleaner you've never seen before.

  Adela: Hey, Chief. The weather's lovely today. Thinking of trying a new hairstyle?

  Chief: Um... I'm quite happy with my current hairstyle. I do have a favor to ask though, can you help me?

  Adela: Of course, Chief. Whatever you need, just name it.

  - Start with something simple

    Chief: Um, I'm feeling like a bottle of peach juice. Have you got some by any chance?

    Adela: Be right back!

    Adela returns to her counter and you can tell from the sound that she opens a fridge. A minute later, she's back, holding a glass of fresh peach juice.

    - Let's take it up a notch

      Chief: I know this sounds spontaneous, but I was also wondering if I could borrow a vinyl of the band Spaceship...

      Before you finish, she gets behind the counter and somehow produces a record. Exactly the one you're asking for.

      Chief: ...Oh? Um, yeah, that's the one, thanks...

      - Alright, this is getting a bit confusing

        Chief: I'm curious, is there anything you can't get from behind that counter?

        Adela: Seriously, yes. These are all unwanted items left behind by my clients. They serve no purpose.

        Chief: The trimmed memories, huh...

        Adela: Just some worthless objects. By the way, do you still need anything else?

        Chief: Ahem, no. I'm only here to check out your counter. It's indeed as fascinating as the rumor suggests.

### 「Mother's Pendant」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Adela, who's usually easygoing and chatty, is pacing in the Outdoor Activity Area sullenly. Something seems off.

- Ask if she's okay

  Chief: What's going on, Adela? Is something bothering you?

  Adela: Oh! It's you, Chief.

  Adela: No, it's nothing serious. I'm just slightly distracted by this thing...

  Chief: What is it? Would you mind telling me?

  Adela: Actually...

  Adela: I've had this gem pendant for years, but I only found out today that it's in fact a locket with a photo of a young woman hidden inside.

  Adela: I'm sure it's nothing important, but I somehow feel like I've seen this photo before...

  You recognize that's Adela's mother.

  She's severed all her memories about her mother, including the early ones when Adela was still a child, but the related emotions surely cannot be so easily removed.


  - Just a beautiful stranger.

    Chief: It's just an ordinary woman. Though I must say, she resembles you in some ways.

    Adela: Oh? Now that you've said it, I do have her eyes.

    Adela: It's just a little weird that I'm so preoccupied with this...

    Adela: Chief, do you know why?

    Chief: Hmm... Can't say I do.

    Adela: Sorry for keeping you for so long. I'm sure it doesn't have any special meaning. I'll go back to work now.

    Adela: Remember, you're always welcome at my salon, Chief.

    Perhaps it's not time yet.

    Watching Adela return to her hair salon humming a melody, you can't help feeling a pang.

  - Perhaps she's someone important to you.


  <br>

  Chief: She could be someone important to you. Otherwise, why would you have kept her photo for so long?

  Adela: But... I... I'm confused...

  Adela: She's only a stranger... Why do I feel so upset all of a sudden...

  Tears suddenly begin falling from Adela's eyes for some reason even she doesn't comprehend.

  She seems a bit flustered at first, but soon collects herself.

  Adela: Sorry, Chief. I'm such a mess.

  Chief: ...

  Adela: I should head back to my room and take a rest.

  Adela's steps seem particularly heavy. You watch her back and sigh softly.

### 「Trimming」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Your hair seems to be getting a bit too long these days, but you're not in the mood to either visit a hair salon outside or trim it yourself.

After thorough consideration, you finally decide to *risk it* and ask Adela for help.

Chief: Hopefully, this time I'll only get my hair trimmed off.

Chief: Small troubles can be enjoyable sometimes, after all.

- Visit Adela

  From afar, you see Adela in her apron, concentrating on cutting the hair of a Sinner who's dozing in the seat.

  Chief: How professional... I'll sit here and wait for her.

  Adela: Voila, it's done!

  Adela: Rise and shine, handsome! Now, what do we think of this?

  Sinner: Zzz... Oh!

  Sinner: Hey, it's dope. You know what you're doing, Adela!

  Sinner: Wait, isn't that Chief in the mirror? Are you here for a trim too?

  Adela: Oh hi, Chief. Excuse me, I didn't see you earlier.

  Sinner: I'll leave you two alone. I can rinse my hair myself, don't worry about it.

  Chief: Hi Adela, I'm actually here for...

  Adela: Say no more, you're here for a trim. Come take a seat! And don't you worry, I'll only cut what you ask me to this time.

  Adela: ...And I'll do it for free.

  Chief: Since when can you read minds...

  Adela: I've been in this business long enough to tell with a simple glance.

  Adela: Anyway, what hairstyle do you have in mind, Chief?


  - Smart.

    Chief: I think something smart and refreshing will suit me more.

    Adela: No problem.

    Adela: But I sense that your mood is the exact opposite of that, isn't it?

    Chief: Pardon?

    Adela: You're self-conscious about your hair, aren't you? No worries, I'll rid you of your trouble right away!

    Chief: Ugh... Hold on...

    Snip, snip.

    Adela: It's done, Chief. Are you sure you don't wanna open your eyes and check out your brand new hairstyle?

    You try to feel if something's missing in you and are surprised to sense nothing wrong. You open your eyes to find that Adela really means what she says literally. She's given you this new hairstyle so you can feel more confident with your hair.

    Chief: ...Maybe try to word it differently next time?

    Adela: Glad to be of service, Chief!

  - More volume.


  <br>

  Chief: Can you add more volume to my hair?

  Adela: Certainly. That may take a while though, is that okay?

  Chief: Sure, I'm quite free today.

  As Adela says, this hairstyle takes time to create.

  Luckily, time passes easily enough with the laid-back chatter between the two of you.

  Adela: Chief, do you sometimes think that peaceful, steady days like this are as precious as gold?

  Chief: I agree. But these quiet days are in fact quite mundane, when you think of it.

  Adela: What do you mean?

  Chief: What make these days precious are the conversations and interactions with those who matter to you. Don't you think?

  Adela: You're exactly right, Chief.

  Adela's busy hands pause for a moment. Then, they resume working dexterously on your hair again.

  Adela: Just like what we have now, right?
