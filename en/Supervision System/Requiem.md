# Requiem Supervision System

### 「Perfect Pitch」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

You hear Requiem softly humming a melody. It's short and strangely familiar, yet you can't quite place where you've heard it before.

Chief: That sounds like my ringtone!

- That sounds like my ringtone!

### 「Instrument Maintenance」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

Before joining the Bureau, Requiem always had someone else look after her violin. Now that she's decided to do it herself, she quickly learns it's far harder than she imagined.

- Encourage her

### 「Endless Rain」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Requiem seems to really enjoy sitting in the sun at the Bureau's greenhouse.

One rainy day, passing by the greenhouse, you hear Requiem practicing by the window. The damp, low melody makes passing Sinners stop without meaning to, their expressions turning melancholy.

- Ask how she's feeling

  Chief: Are you feeling down?

  Requiem: Chief, there's no sunlight today. And the greenhouse is so quiet.

  Requiem: I only wanted to make this place feel a little less lonely. But... it seems my violin carried the rain into everyone's hearts instead.

  - Invite her

    Chief: Want to have afternoon tea with me?

    Chief: Sometimes it helps to switch things up and take your mind off it.

    Requiem: Afternoon tea... with you, Chief?

    Requiem: I'd like that. With you here, even the rain sounds softer somehow.

### 「Memory Anchor」

Location: Hypnotherapy Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

During a routine Bureau checkup, the medical team attempts a low-intensity hypnotherapy session on Requiem, to determine whether the boundary between her own memories and those inherited from other virtuosos remains stable.

Shortly after the session begins, Requiem suddenly falls into a trance. The readings on the monitor fluctuate sharply, and her expression gradually becomes that of a stranger.

As her behavior begins to turn aggressive, the medical team urgently summons you. According to the on-site records, she shows almost no response to any outside stimulus. Only your voice can still draw a faint reply from her.

- Call Requiem

  At the sound of your voice, Requiem finally ceases struggling against the restraints.

  Her eyes remain tightly shut, but she turns her head anxiously, searching for the source of your voice.

  - Touch Requiem

    Ignoring the staff's attempts to stop you, you step forward and lay your hand upon Requiem's cheek.

    Her breath falters for a moment, and at last she reclaims control of her own body.

    Requiem: ...Chief?

    Requiem: It seems I wandered into someone else's memories again... Their performances were all so wonderful. But I could not hear my own violin, nor could I find you...

    Chief: Don't worry. You're back now.

    Afterward, the medical team updates Requiem's treatment protocol: under no circumstances is hypnotherapy to be administered to Requiem without your direct supervision present.

### 「Voice of a Wish」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

After an approved off-site assignment, Requiem fails to return to the Bureau as scheduled.

Her signal lingers in an old residential district. By the time you arrive, you find her standing in a narrow room, playing the violin for an elderly woman who lives alone.

- Wait for her to finish playing

  The old woman sits quietly by the window, nodding slowly to the gentle melody. You cannot bring yourself to disturb them.

  Not until the final note fades does Requiem lower her bow and turn to face you.

  Chief: Requiem, why did you leave the team and act on your own?

  Requiem: ...I'm sorry, Chief.

  Requiem: As I passed by, I heard this lady's wish.

  Requiem: There was a piece she heard at a concert when she was young, and she wished to hear it again. I did not want to disappoint her.

  You have no idea how Requiem heard that "wish," but you can tell she had no intention of fleeing supervision.


  - Remind her to report

    Chief: I get that you wanted to help her. But going off on your own makes everyone worry about you. Next time you want to do something, report it first.

    Requiem: Report... like announcing the program and the time before a performance?

    Requiem: I'll keep that in mind. Next time, I'll tell you first, Chief.

  - Arrange assistance


  <br>

  Chief: If something like this happens again, don't go off on your own.

  Chief: I'll have someone screen these requests in advance and set things up so you can perform safely and by the book.

  Requiem: I understand. I'll do as you arrange, Chief.

### 「Lullaby for the Sleepless」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Recently, the Bureau has dealt with multiple nighttime emergencies in a row, and the long night shifts have thrown off the routines of many staff members and Sinners alike.

A few days later, the medical team begins receiving reports of widespread insomnia, and some even show clear signs of mental exhaustion.

When Requiem hears of this, she offers to perform lullabies over the Bureau's broadcast system.

- Arrange a low-risk test round

  Under the joint watch of the medical team and monitoring staff, Requiem switches to standard strings and gives a short broadcast performance.

  Most of the insomniacs' moods do settle, yet the monitors still catch the faintest of fluctuations.

  At the staff's signal, you stop Requiem just as she is about to begin a second lullaby.

  Chief: Thank you. The lullaby worked well. That's enough for now.

  Requiem: But... some people still haven't fallen asleep.

  Requiem: Music carried through a broadcast is not the same as hearing it live. I'd like to meet these listeners in person. Even if I can't play for them, just sitting and listening to them talk would be enough. Is that okay?


  - Agree

    That night, accompanied by the monitoring staff, Requiem talks for a while with the Sinners still unable to sleep.

    She listens quietly as they speak of their exhausting work, their uneasy dreams, and the noise that will not leave their minds.

    Within the hour, even the last insomniac leans back in their chair and drifts off. Requiem stays beside them for a long while, rising only once she is sure their breathing has steadied.

    And so, with Requiem's help, the mass insomnia quietly subsided.

  - Reject


  <br>

  Chief: Sorry, but the test results aren't fully confirmed yet. I can't let you meet them alone right now.

  Requiem: I see... I'll do as you say, Chief.

  A few days later, once the staff and Sinners have settled back into their routines, the mass insomnia finally comes to an end.

  And yet, during the nightly broadcast hour, Requiem still asks whether you need her to play a lullaby.
