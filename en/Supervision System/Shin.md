### 「Mental Contamination」

Location: Hypnotherapy Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Incident detected during the hypnosis treatment of the Sinner Shin. Go investigate?

- Go immediately

  You rush to the hypnosis room, and before you can even enter, you hear bouts of screaming and sobbing coming from behind the door.

  - Break down the door

    The one wailing on the floor isn't Shin, but the hypnotist. Shin herself is gently comforting her own doctor.

    You immediately arrange for other staff members to take the hypnotist to the medical room for treatment.

    Chief: What did you do to him?

    Shin: He wanted to understand me on a deeper level, so I granted his request and revealed to him a glimpse of... the truth about the world.

    Shin: Sure enough, human minds are all too fragile.

    Shin: But... I know you're different. If you're willing, we can continue discussing what I didn't get to say earlier.

    Chief: I don't think that's necessary.

    Shin: Hehe, very well then. As you wish.

    With the help of medical staff, the hypnotist finally regained his sanity after several days.

    After the incident concluded, you canceled the rest of Shin's hypnotherapy sessions.

### 「Your Choice」

Location: Training Room

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

While you and Shin were conducting simulated training, you suddenly felt waves of pain in your wrist.

- View

  You discover a fairly deep cut on the back of your left hand, though you have no idea when it happened.

  - Conceal

    You try to slip your hand inside your sleeve, but Shin seems to have already noticed.

    Shin: Are you hurt?

    Chief: Looks like it's just a scratch.

    Shin: Let me take a look.

    Before you can refuse, Shin has already lifted your hand and begun examining it carefully.

    Shin: This wound is quite deep. It should be treated as soon as possible. Let me help you.

    Chief: Wait a minute...

    You instinctively want to pull your hand back. Shin doesn't react, simply watching you and quietly waiting for you to decide.


    - Accept Shin's help

      Chief: Please go ahead then.

      Shin smiles faintly and pulls bandages from her personal bag.

      To your surprise, she simply treats your wound using standard first aid procedures.

      Chief: ...Thank you.

      Shin: Hehe, did you think I was going to use some special treatment methods?

      Shin: There's no need to apologize. I'm delighted. You clearly had doubts, yet you still chose to trust me—that's precisely what makes you so special.

    - Refuse Shin's help


    <br>

    You're not sure whether Shin's "help" is the kind that humans can safely endure. After thinking it over, you decide to politely decline her offer.

    Chief: No need to trouble yourself. I'll head to the medical room right now to get it treated. Just wait here for me.

    Shin makes no attempt to stop you, merely standing in place with a smile as she watches you leave.

    Shin: Very well. I'll accept whatever you choose.

### 「Call of Darkness」

Location: Confinement Cell

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Sinner Shin has been in the confinement cell for dozens of hours. Go check the supervision footage?

- Yes

  Video Playing

  ...

  The footage shows Shin entering the Confinement Cell alone, turning off the lights, and staying inside without ever coming out.

  - Head to the Confinement Cell

    Chief: Shin? Are you alright?

    Shin's drowsy, half-conscious voice drifts out from within the Confinement Cell.

    Shin: I'm fine... I'm listening to Its teachings.

    Chief: Good.

    Shin: Hehe, are you worried about me?

    Chief: Some Sinners in the past couldn't handle the dark confinement room and passed out. I was worried the same thing had happened to you.

    Chief: I didn't expect you to be so fond of it.

    Shin: Like it? No, it's not that kind of feeling.

    Shin: But I know that I belong to the darkness. You do too. In darkness, your true self manifests.

    Shin: Would you like to come and try it with me?


    - Stay

      The fanaticism and obsession in Shin's voice are deeply unsettling. But perhaps this will help you understand her a little better.

      Chief: Alright, I'll give it a try.

      You step into the confinement room, sit down beside Shin, and close your eyes in the darkness.

      You have a pure black, indescribable dream.

      When you wake up again, you find yourself lying on the couch in your office. You have no idea who brought you back.

    - Leave


    <br>

    Even though you are inside the Bureau, Shin's power is still dangerous.

    Chief: No, I have my own methods to study the darkness.

    Chief: But I'll stay out here with you, just in case.

    Shin: Hehe, suit yourself.
