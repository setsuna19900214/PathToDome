# Angell Supervision System

### 「Silent Treatment」

The hypnotherapist sends you a message saying that Angell is guarded during their sessions, a tendency that is undermining the efficacy of the therapy, and that you should consider getting involved in helping her get better.

- All right, I will.

### 「A Guinea Pig for New Dishes」

The cafeteria has invented quite a few new dishes lately, all of which taste peculiar and turn stomachs. Angell is the only person who doesn't seem repulsed by these dishes, and, as a result, the middle-aged lady in the cafeteria always gives Angell a larger serving.

- As long as they're not doing her body any harm...

### 「Sleep-inducing Music」

The activity log for the Recreation Area shows that Angell has fallen asleep on the public sofa multiple times. When the staff asked her why, she was unforthcoming.

- Go ask Angell

  An old song is playing in the Recreation Area. Angell is sitting on the sofa, her eyes heavy and her head dipping. She is on the verge of falling asleep. You approach her.

  Chief: Angell, why don't you go to your room to sleep on your bed?

  Angell: Hmm, this place is nice.

  Chief: Care to elaborate?

  Angell: It's quiet here. The sofa is soft. And the song... it's a good song.

  Chief: The song?

  Things start to make sense.

  The song is playing in a loop on the record player. You listen carefully for a while. It is a slow, soothing song.

  - You like the song?

    Angell: Not exactly. I just think it's very soothing.

    Angell: When I have nothing better to do, I come here to pass the time, and I fall asleep sometimes.

    Chief: I'm glad this place makes you feel comfortable and relaxed, but you shouldn't sleep on a sofa, not in the long term; it may do you harm.

    Chief: I'll have a record player installed in your room. If you have similar needs in the future, you can come to me.

### 「Sleep Easy」

After completing a punishing mission, Angell went straight to her room. She hasn't been seen in public for three days now.

- Pay Angell a visit

  The curtains are drawn, and the blanket is in a bundle. The room is silent but for the barely audible sound of regular breathing.

  - Wake up Angell

    Chief: Wake up, Angell!

    Angell: Who's there?!

    Sensing someone approaching, Angell quickly sits up, relaxes upon seeing you, and rubs her eyes.

    Angell: What brings you here?

    Chief: I haven't seen you in days. I was a bit worried about you.

    Angell: I'm fine. I've been sleeping.

    - For three straight days?

      Angell: What's the matter?

      Angell: After a major mission, I feel totally drained, so I sleep, in order to get back on my feet. I also like the feeling of not having to think about anything.

      Angell: And I sleep better at the Bureau, knowing that you're never far away.

      Chief: Still, sleeping for three days in a row is a bit extreme, don't you think? You should at least get out of bed every once in a while and get some food in your stomach.

      Angell gives a non-committal nod. Then she pulls the curtains tighter, ready to fall back into sleep.

### 「An Automatic Blade Sharpener」

Holding a small device in her arms, Eureka explains something excitedly to Angell, who nods or shakes her head from time to time, making occasional replies.

It is rare to see Angell interacting with another Sinner. Your curiosity is piqued.

- Go over and listen

  Eureka: This automatic blade sharpener can double as a massage device. So while your blade is being sharpened, you can enjoy a massage to ease the knots in your neck.

  Angell: The ratio of iron to carbon varies from sword to sword. Does this device work for all ratios?

  Eureka: Of course. It's equipped with a wide range of combinations of abrasive materials; you just choose the one that suits your needs best. And this little thing here is the cherry on top; freshwater squirts out as the sword is being sharpened. It's like watching a miniature fountain show!

  Angell: Does it help extend the life of the blade?

  Eureka: Hmm, let me think. In theory... I need to keep the self-cleaning function and angle adjustability without compromising the compact design... Ha, I've got it! I'll go modify it right now.

  Eureka: I've got other tools for blade maintenance at my place. I'll bring them over. Be right back.

  Eureka runs off, overjoyed to have found a kindred spirit. Angell turns to look at you.

  Angell: Need anything? You've been standing here for quite a while.

  Chief: Oh, I was just curious about what you two were talking about. I never thought you would be interested in Eureka's inventions.

  Angell: About an hour ago, she recommended a blade sharpener to me, said it was very useful, and insisted I try it. And I did.


  - Ask about her experience with the device

    Chief: Was it any good?

    Angell: Very useful. My blade has been sharpened nicely.

    Chief: It doubles as a massage device, and it squirts water while sharpening your blade, putting on, as Eureka put it, a miniature fountain show. Don't you find that odd?

    Angell: I didn't try the water-squirting function. Like I said, it's a very useful tool.

  - Ask about her blade maintenance routine


  <br>

  Chief: Have you used an automatic sharpener before? How do you usually maintain your blades?

  Angell: I use a whetstone.

  Chief: Do blades sharpened with an automatic sharpener feel different to you? Some people may find them not as comfortable to use as the hand-sharpened ones.

  Angell: Either works for me. They both get the job done.

### 「A Missing Cat」

A newly-adopted kitten goes missing on the premises of the Bureau. The staff and Sinners search high and low, but the cat, clearly an expert at the games of tag and hide-and-seek, is far from being caught.

As you look at the staff and Sinners, flustered and out of their depth, you think of the one person who hasn't gotten involved in this wild goose chase.

- Commission Angell

  Angell accepts the job without much delay and comes to the greenhouse to learn about the details from you.

  Chief: Here is the situation in a nutshell: It's a black cat. Went missing about three hours ago. Last seen in the detention area. It's a fierce creature that won't let any human come near it.

  Chief: The other guys are unwilling to return to their rooms, because they're worried about the cat. We're counting on you now.

  You hand Angell a list of all the places that have been searched.

  Chief: These are the places we've searched.

  Angell: No need. I know where the cat is.

  You follow Angell to the cafeteria's kitchen.

  The two of you pass row upon row of racks until you reach the bowels of the kitchen, where in the corner stands a tall cabinet whose top almost touches the ceiling.

  Chief: I never knew there's such a spot in the Bureau...

  Angell glances up at the gap between the cabinet and the ceiling.

  Angell: It's not here. Next stop.

  The next stop is the Outdoor Activity Area. Angell crouches down beside a wall and grabs a handful of loose dirt from the base of the wall.

  Chief: There are black cat hairs in here...

  Angell: The cat is no longer here. Next stop.

  You follow Angell to the Recreation Area. She comes to an abrupt stop, turns to you, and places her index finger against her lips.

  You look around but see no sign of the cat.

  You give Angell a confused look. Angell shakes her head, and approaches the boxing ring slowly and silently. That's when you see a boxing glove that pokes out of the gap between the ring and the floor.

  The moment Angell squats down, a black shape flies out from inside the glove. Angell darts. Before you know it, she is holding the black cat in her arms.

  The little creature squirms, then quiets down, licking her hand affectionately.

  Angell: Mission accomplished.


  - Express surprise

    Chief: How did you know where to find it?

    Angell: Cats are intelligent, sensitive creatures. This little fellow here probably felt insecure and ran away.

    Angell: Unexpected; with objects to hide in or behind; easy to blend in with the surroundings, especially at night... There aren't many spots that fit this description around here, so I figured that a simple process of elimination would eventually lead us to this cat.

  - Express admiration


  <br>

  Chief: And you know all these spots?

  Angell: This is a smart one. It chose a well-concealed spot that no one thought to look. Well, no one but me.

  Chief: Could you do me a favor and make a list of all these spots? The Bureau's security needs tightening. I'm gonna place an order for the necessary equipment.
