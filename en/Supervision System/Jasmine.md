After completing an operation with Jasmine, you sit alone in your office, yet you keep catching faint traces of her delicate fragrance.

  You discover a jasmine flower in your coat pocket.

### 「Taste of Home」

Location: Greenhouse

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You're reading the monthly report submitted by the cafeteria.

Staff Member: "—In conclusion, this month's newly introduced DisCity-style meal sets have received an enthusiastic welcome from the Sinners."

Staff Member: "The only exception is Sinner Jasmine. After she came once at the beginning of the month, she has never dined in the cafeteria again."

Staff Member: "We sent staff to personally invite her, but she showed strong resistance."

- Talk to Jasmine

  Chief: Have the cafeteria dishes been to your taste lately?

  Jasmine: Did you notice, Chief? Jasmine is so happy.

  Jasmine: But you don't need to worry about it too much. I'm already used to this kind of thing.

  Chief: You don't need to endure it. If there's anything you don't like, you can always speak up.

  Jasmine: I don't dislike it at all. The DisCity meal sets... hehe, how wonderful they are. I see everyone around me eating so happily.

  Jasmine: This is DisCity meat, DisCity vegetables, DisCity soup, it tastes like, like...

  Jasmine seems to have started muttering to herself.

  - Wait quietly for her to continue

    Jasmine: It tastes like, like real food...

    Jasmine: But that's not right... I keep feeling something strange. This isn't like the DisCity food I've eaten before...

    Jasmine: And then, you know, that day I was eating fish. I don't know how to eat fish. My homeland had no fish. A fishbone cut my tongue, it bled, and I discovered...

    Jasmine: This, this is the real taste of DisCity food, the taste I once experienced lying on the ground, lying beside my parents, tasting DisCity food... ha, hahaha...

    - Hold her hand

      Chief: Jasmine... Jasmine! Look at me...

      Chief: It's okay. All of that is in the past now. You're safe here. I'm here with you, you're safe.

      Jasmine's face relaxes into that familiar smile you know so well.

      Jasmine: I'm fine, I... Jasmine is satisfied with everything.

      Chief: ...

      Jasmine's situation weighs on your mind for a long time. Eventually, you have the cafeteria open a dedicated Outlands cuisine window, for all those who find themselves far from home.

### 「Forbidden Summit」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

A Sinner rushes breathlessly into your office to report: Jasmine and Hamel got into a fight in the Recreation Area after a disagreement.

  Rather than the main combatants dancing with blades and flourishing sleeves, what catches your eye first is the helpful citizen Demolia standing between them, trying to break up the fight.

  Demolia: Stop! You two need to quit fighting!

  - Join Demolia

    Chief: You two need to quit fighting!

    The two Sinners stop their movements and look at you in confusion.

    Chief: The Bureau strictly prohibits fighting between Sinners!

    Jasmine: I'll do whatever you say.

    Jasmine: Jasmine simply didn't know that this wasn't allowed here either.

    Hamel: But Chief, we were just dancing...

    Hamel: Jasmine said she wanted to teach me the twin blade dance.

    Chief: ...I see. I understand now.

    Chief: When dancing in the Bureau, don't bring weapons.

    Chief: There are people coming and going here constantly. If you two want to dance, you should go to the dance studio instead.

### 「Afternoon Hours」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Sinner Jasmine has been out of supervision for five hours. Go and inspect?

- Yes

  After some searching, you finally find Jasmine at the Bureau's pet boarding facility. She's actually asleep, curled up with the cats.

  - Approach quietly

    Jasmine: ...Chief?

    Chief: Sorry, did I wake you up?

    Jasmine stretches lazily, then her eyes flutter closed again.

    Jasmine: I'm happy to see you when I open my eyes.

    Jasmine: Did you come looking for Jasmine for something?

    Chief: We just hadn't seen you in your room and were a bit worried.

    Chief: Are you sleeping here? With the cats?

    Jasmine gently strokes the kitten lying in her arms, and it purrs contentedly under her touch.

    Jasmine: Yes, I like animals. They're pure, not complicated like humans.

    Jasmine: Sleeping here, Jasmine feels very at peace.


    - Suggest she sleep elsewhere

      Chief: I'm glad you're alright. But aren't you taking over their beds?

      Jasmine: Hmm? I think it likes me quite a bit, and I like it quite a bit too...

      Chief: It'll get cold here later when the temperature adjusts.

      Chief: How about coming to my office? I have blankets there.

      Upon hearing your words, Jasmine smiles and gets up almost immediately, obediently following behind you.

      Just before leaving, you pause to think for a moment, then borrow a cat from the staff.

    - Stay to keep her company


    <br>

    Chief: Is it comfortable sleeping like this?

    Jasmine: You might not be used to this feeling... but they're so warm when they curl up next to you.

    Jasmine: But what's most comforting of all is... shh, listen—that gentle breathing. It means they're sleeping peacefully.

    Jasmine: Would you be willing to... give it a try?

    Chief: Alright, then I'll rest for a while too.

    You carefully lie down beside Jasmine, leaning against the wall without taking up any of the cats' spots.

    Under the warm glow of artificial sunlight, you gradually grow drowsy, with Jasmine's steady breathing beside you.

### 「The Cat's Gratitude」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

After one mission, you find a broken necklace at your office door.

After another mission, you find a damaged leather jacket at your office door.

After yet another mission, you find a bloodied ring at your office door.

You're absolutely certain this ring still holds the warmth of its previous owner.

- Investigate Surveillance Footage

  The footage shows Jasmine's figure skipping up to your door, setting down the trophies, then tiptoeing quietly away.

  - Wait and see

    The next day, after your mission ends, you don't return to your office immediately, but instead hide nearby to observe.

    Finally, a familiar figure comes into view.

    Chief: Ahem...

    Jasmine: Oh my, you caught me.

    Chief: Oh my, you found me.

    Jasmine: Look, these are things I just picked up from my "guests" today. Aren't they pretty?

    Jasmine: If you hold them up to the light, they even sparkle!

    Chief: We thoroughly checked everyone when you returned, so how on earth did you manage to hide these...

    Chief: But what I really want to ask is, why did you put these things at my door?

    Jasmine: You... don't like them?

    Jasmine: I just thought these things were too beautiful, it felt like a waste to leave them on those people.

    Jasmine: And besides, thinking about how gentle you are whenever you're with Jasmine...

    Jasmine: Jasmine has nothing... and no idea how to ever repay you...


    - Say you're a bit worried

      Chief: Actually, I'm a bit worried.

      Jasmine: Huh? I... I made you worry? I'm sorry, I...

      Chief: These things often have unknown contamination residue on them, and keeping them close to your body all the time is bad for your health.

      Chief: I don't really like that kind of thing.

      Chief: On the contrary, what I like is for you to be healthy and take good care of your body.

      Jasmine: Hmm...

      Chief: Next time you find these things, remember to give them to me or other staff members, okay?

      Jasmine: Jasmine understands... I will take good care of myself.

    - Say you really like them


    <br>

    Chief: Thank you, I really like them.

    Chief: Next time, can you hand them to me personally? Preferably right when you've just gotten them.

    Chief: I want to hold them in my hands right away.

    Jasmine: Really? That's wonderful!

    Jasmine: From now on, I will give them to you as soon as I get them.
