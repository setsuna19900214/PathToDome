# Wuhuanzi Supervision System

### 「Yugu's Troubles」

Yugu: Sigh...

Across the dining table, Yugu picks up her chopsticks only to set them down again, letting out a long, weary sigh. You pause your meal and look up at her.

- What's wrong?

  Yugu: Ever since joining the Bureau, I've barely seen our little herbalist. But we used to run into each other all the time at the fete.

  Yugu: In all my years of wandering, I've made but a few close acquaintances. After all we've been through together, I thought we had forged a bond through our shared tribulations.

  Yugu: Alas... how delicate are the ties that bind us together as people.

  Chief: There must be a reason for this.

  You recall running into Wuhuanzi in the hallway the other day, when she voiced relevant concerns...

  Wuhuanzi: My friend, here at the Bureau... I aim to steer clear of any entanglements with that taleteller.

  - Ask about the reason

    Wuhuanzi: Nothing good ever comes from crossing paths with her. Well... I mean, I'm terribly unlucky even without her around, but that's a different kind of misfortune altogether.

    Wuhuanzi: Not only does she drag me into bizarre tales without warning, but I have to listen to all her disturbing accounts. It's always about destruction this, death that, and horrifying monsters...

    Wuhuanzi: With my awful luck, despite all my wandering, in this vast world we somehow always end up in the same place. And now we're even working together at the Bureau...

    Wuhuanzi: If I can't stand against her, I'll simply steer clear. And if avoidance isn't an option... well, I can always just stay in my room, right?

    Afterward, Wuhuanzi recounts several of her miserable experiences to you.

    - Reason with Yugu

      Chief: Wuhuanzi has been through a lot of hardships. She needs time at the Bureau to find her emotional footing. When she's ready, perhaps she'll be able to face you properly.

      Chief: Ahem... Just because you find someone interesting doesn't mean you should keep pulling them into your stories. You need to consider how they feel about it.

      Yugu gives you a meaningful look.

### 「Travel Not Advised」

Wuhuanzi originally planned to go out to purchase medicinal herbs today. However, after breaking a cup, getting a paper cut, and finding a hole in her pocket, she decides to withdraw her request and stay in her room all day.

- Not a good day, isn't it?

### 「Unknown Substance」

Wuhuanzi has brewed a batch of herbal tea for the Bureau staff and Sinners, claiming it helps with fatigue and humidity. She made sure to deliver an especially large cupful right to your desk.

- Thanks, I'm good...

  Trying as you might to mentally prepare yourself, you still can't bring yourself to take a sip.

### 「Rumor」

Several staff members have spotted Wuhuanzi muttering to herself while repeatedly stabbing a piece of meat with needles, her expression grim. They're convinced it must be some mysterious Eastian ritual.

- She's probably practicing acupuncture?

### 「Stellar Example」

Lately, Wuhuanzi has been constantly seen around Hestia. You wonder when they became so close.

You notice that Wuhuanzi pulls out a notebook, her expression grave as she scribbles and sketches in it.

- What are you writing down?

  Wuhuanzi: I am studying, my friend.

  Chief: ?

  Wuhuanzi: This Sinner, Hestia, is called a "priestess" by everyone. Even though she looks about my age, people trust everything she says, while I... I'm always dismissed as some quack doctor. She must have something special about her.

  Chief: ...In a way, yes, she does.

  Wuhuanzi: If I am to help more patients, I need to appear more reliable. Among any group of people, there must be something to learn from... I suppose I could gain some insights.

  Wuhuanzi: So... I plan to write down everything she says and study each word carefully when I return. Perhaps then I can master the art of gaining people's trust.

  - Intervene

    Chief: There are probably other things worth learning from her.

### 「Medical Instructions」

Wuhuanzi: My friend, if I may speak frankly...

The Sinner before you furrows her brow in concentration as her fingers rest on your wrist, moving gently to read your pulse.

- Is it something serious?

  Wuhuanzi: It has been more than a fortnight since your last treatment, yet your pulse shows no signs of improvement.

  Wuhuanzi: My friend, have you been taking the Herbal Decoction Version 2.0 as scheduled?

  Chief: ...No.

  Wuhuanzi: Have you been doing exercise every morning as prescribed?

  Chief: ...No.

  Wuhuanzi: Have you been maintaining a proper early bedtime?

  Chief: ...No.

  Wuhuanzi: ...

  Wuhuanzi: Though fate may govern life and death, I consider you a kindred spirit. I would hate to see your journey cut short prematurely.

  Wuhuanzi: Since you are too busy to follow my medical instructions, I suppose I have no choice but to...

  Wuhuanzi lets out a sigh as she slowly takes out her acupuncture kit. She arranges the needles in a neat row—their impossibly fine, sharp tips gleaming under the light.

  Wuhuanzi: Please lie down. It's just a few needles—this won't take long.

  - Calm down!

### 「Ground of Good Fortune」

Mysterious talismans have been appearing throughout the Bureau lately, with your office being particularly affected.

Finding these talismans oddly familiar, you decide to stay late and wait for the "culprit." Sure enough, you hear rustling sounds from outside your door.

- Open the door

  Wuhuanzi: ...My friend? Why are you still here...

  Chief: Well, I was "talisman hunting."

  You've caught the petite girl red-handed, and for once, her perpetually gloomy face shows a hint of panic.

  Wuhuanzi: Please don't misunderstand, my friend. These are all properly modified talismans. They won't attract any strange entities...

  Chief: How so?

  Seeing that you're not upset, Wuhuanzi enters your office and carefully arranges her talismans across your desk, one by one.

  Wuhuanzi: Behold, my friend. This is a fortune-turning talisman—it wards off evil spirits and maintains the balance between yin and yang.

  Wuhuanzi: Ever since I moved in, it seems that both you, my friend, and the Bureau have been affected by ill fortune... While I'm used to living with it, I couldn't bear to see it impact you all.

  Wuhuanzi: This is a longevity talisman—it bestows blessings and protection while warding off misfortune and hardship.

  Wuhuanzi: My friend, you constantly venture into dangerous territories. Even when faced with near-certain death, you still boldly march in alone...

  Wuhuanzi: I had hoped to avoid meddling too much with worldly affairs, planning to slip this talisman into your pocket secretly, but it seems... I'll have to give it to you directly.


  - Accept

    Chief: You don't need to be so secretive about helping us in the future.

    Chief: Say... do you happen to have any talismans for attracting wealth?

    Wuhuanzi: Ah, but ill-gotten gains would diminish one's merit...

    Seeing Wuhuanzi's troubled expression, you suppress the urge to ask her to hand over all her talismans.

  - Decline


  <br>

  Chief: Don't burden yourself with karmic debts that don't exist.

  Chief: Haven't I grown healthier since you arrived? You've treated both our staff and the Sinners. If anything, meeting you has been our good fortune.

  Wuhuanzi: I... this is all for my merit. You must accept this no matter what, my friend.

  Wuhuanzi forces the talisman into your hands, then promptly turns and runs away.
