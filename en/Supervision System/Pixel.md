# Pixel Supervision System

### 「Digital Hello」

You run into Pixel in the Bureau hallway. Her head-monitor flickers—a tiny cartoon hand waves.

- Is that a hello?

  You nod back. The pixels rearrange into a grinning face, blinking cheerfully.

### 「Soap Opera Intoxication」

Pixel's monitor plays dramatic confessions, wealthy family feuds, amnesia, rebirths... Could this be a new virus?

- Ask about the reason

  Turns out Pixel just finished a 36‑hour soap opera marathon and hasn't fully recovered.

### 「A Suspicious Helping Hand」

Mid‑call, the Bureau loses power. Pixel immediately offers her head‑monitor as your screen.

You spot a Preview window not fully closed—your serious face now sporting fluffy cat ears.

- That was close. Almost fell for it!

  You switch to backup power without missing a beat. Exposed, Pixel flashes a sulking ellipsis and starts plotting her next scheme.

### 「Impromptu Reviewer」

Passing by Pixel's room, you find her with glasses displayed on her monitor, flipping through papers with unusual seriousness.

Pixel: Oh, Chief! Good morning, afternoon, and evening! This paper's quite something. Want to take a look?

Pixel: Don't give me that look. Sure, I crawled out of DisCity academia's massive **** ages ago, but reading papers is good for the mind. Everyone says so.

You glance at the title page—a logistics staff member is pursuing further studies, and this is their first draft.

- You wouldn't...

  Pixel: Hey, hey! You'll break my heart saying that, Chief. I haven't ghostwritten a single thing in 134,252 seconds.

  Pixel: He writes slowly, and revising is agony—but every word is his.

  - So you're just giving him feedback?

    Pixel: Of course. I point out the cracks. Fixing them is his job.

    Pixel: Like here—the argument's so flimsy it's basically papier‑mâché. And here—he's trying to skip the proof with "it's common knowledge." Hehe. Dream on.

    She scribbles annotations, fully absorbed.

    - Then I'll leave it to you.

      Pixel: Relax, Chief. Today I'm just a reviewer, not a ghostwriter.

      Pixel: Papers like this are the most fun—clumsy but earnest. Like treasure tossed out by someone who didn't know its worth. A little polish and it shines.

      You leave Pixel to her work. As you head out, you hear her muttering.

      Pixel: This part's good. Keep it. Don't hold back—good scholarship takes nerve...

### 「Open Mic Night: Bad Jokes」

The Recreation Area is hosting Bad Joke Night, and Pixel signs up with boundless enthusiasm—even printing notecards.

Pixel: A PhD student asks their senior labmate, "Why can't I replicate your data?"

Pixel: And the senior labmate says, "What's strange about that? I can't replicate it either!"

The room falls silent. Two sharp coughs cut through the air, each one more painfully distinct.

Pixel: Haha, a real groaner! Okay, next! Why do soap operas use laugh tracks?

Pixel: Because otherwise, how would the audience know when to laugh?

Pixel cracks herself up, her monitor scrolling "hahahaha." The audience stays silent. The mic screeches.

EMP: Those are like cold jokes... Wait. Why is it actually cold? I've got goosebumps.

- I'm freezing too.

  Pixel: Looks like everyone's catching on! I hacked the Bureau's air‑conditioning! If the crowd is cold, the room might as well be too.

  EMP: Pfft—cheating! At this rate, the A/C tells better jokes than Computer Head!

  Pixel: Wrong. This is immersive performance art. Central A/C, drop the temperature around EMP by five degrees!

  EMP: Whoa! It's freezing! Freezing! I take it back! I was warming up the crowd for you, Computer Head! Mercy!

  A few scattered laughs finally ripple through the room. You, however, are turning into a popsicle.

  - Ask Pixel to turn up the heat

    At your insistence, Pixel begrudgingly disables the A/C's "Cold Joke Mode." The temperature rises, and the show continues.

    From that day on, the Bureau's stand‑up comedy application form included one new rule: "No physical methods may be used to aid the effect of a bad joke."

### 「Sticker Artist」

Rumor has it that a new sticker pack is everywhere. The art is simple, the captions are sharp, and they spread like wildfire. People love them. Some even sneak them into their work reports.

Whenever you walk through the office, you catch stifled laughter. And the instant you turn around, everyone acts perfectly innocent.

- I have a bad feeling about this...

  Pixel: Oh my, Chief! The moment I heard you were looking for me, I came running!

  Chief: I heard a new sticker pack is making the rounds. Did you make them?

  Pixel: Wow! My masterpiece has spread that far already? Quick, download them, Chief—they're incredibly versatile!

  - Download the stickers

    You open the sticker list. A blank‑faced person in gray lies flattened under a mountain of documents: "Buried in work."

    Next, the same figure stands calmly in the Bureau's chaos, coffee in hand, hair on fire: "Everything is basically under control."

    In the third one, their head fills the entire frame, blown up to absurd proportions: "What a headache."

    Chief: Why are all your stickers based on me?

    Pixel: Because your face is incredibly versatile, Chief!

    Pixel: Work, meetings, overtime, silence—one expression covers everything. Drawing you is a dream!


    - One more and you're under arrest!

      Pixel: Loud and clear, Chief! I'll stamp out this vile trend myself!

      Later that evening, a new sticker appears: a person wearing gray holds up a sign, stubby fingers pointing straight at the viewer: "One more and you're under arrest!"

      Chief: Sigh...

    - Let it go


    <br>

    You decide to let it slide. Pixel soon moves on to drawing other Sinners.

    Chief: Crisis averted... at least for me.
