### 「The Seating Challenge」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

On the eve of the Bureau's gala, Wynn sits surrounded by piles of name cards and seating charts, scratching her head in visible distress.

Chief: Running into trouble?

Wynn: Chief... the seating arrangement is impossible. These two Sinners had a falling out during their last mission. They'll be at each other's throats if put together. These two squads despise each other and don't get along at all. And then there are these super introverted Sinners. If we seat them together, that table won't hear a peep until the party's over...

Wynn: What do I do, Chief? Please help me!

You find yourself equally stumped. While you're mulling over the problem, a gentle voice chimes in.

Margaret: It seems our dear Wynn has run into trouble. If you wouldn't mind, perhaps I could give it a try? I happen to know a thing or two about our colleagues' temperaments and preferences.

- Leave the arrangements to her

  Chief: Then we'll leave it to you, Ms. Margaret.

  Margaret: Happy to be of service.

  She takes the guest list, her fingertips gliding lightly across the paper as she makes the occasional note. Once she finishes, she hands the revised seating chart back to Wynn, then turns to you with a reassuring smile.

  Margaret: Rest assured, Chief. The gala will proceed without a hitch.

  On the night of the gala, the atmosphere is remarkably harmonious. Every table buzzes with lively conversation. No awkward silence, no drama. You survey the venue with satisfaction, your gaze eventually settling on the person most responsible for this success. Margaret, however, isn't seated at the head table. Instead, she's at the bar in the distance, effortlessly exchanging toasts with several guests.

  You notice that the small group around her includes a prominent financial figure, an influential artist, and a well-known philanthropist. They're passing around an elegantly designed charity auction proposal with keen interest.

  Guest A: The ambience tonight is exceptional. From the seating arrangements to the music and lighting. Every detail feels just right. All thanks to Chief's meticulous planning.

  Guest B: Indeed, every detail has been so thoughtfully considered. Otherwise, we wouldn't be having such a pleasant chat. Ms. Margaret's charity proposal just now is truly moving.

  Margaret: Oh, I should be the one thanking you... For the children still suffering, for a brighter future, my efforts alone are far from enough. I can only hope for your support, so that this glimmer of hope might reach a few more dim corners.

  Amid the clinking of glasses, signatures fill the proposal. As you approach, she tucks away the last document, wearing a perfect, decent smile.

  Margaret: Chief, are you pleased with how the evening has turned out?

  Chief: Of course. Though... I guess you should be the one who's most pleased tonight? Was this table your target from the very beginning?

  Margaret: Is it not better to accomplish several things at once? The gala's harmony is preserved, and a worthy cause finds its patrons. After all, guiding the right resources to where they're needed most has always been... an elegant art.

  Margaret raises her glass to you in a silent toast. In the flickering light, you cannot quite make out the smile on her face.

### 「In Vino Veritas」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

There you find Margaret, who appears to have slipped out with the same excuse.

- Greet her

  Chief: Hi there. Here for a quiet break too?

  Margaret: Oh, Chief, I know you'll get me. The banquet is lively, but sometimes one just wants to admire the peaceful moonlight.

  She settles beside you, a faint, crisp scent of wine wafting near in the night breeze. Your conversation drifts from the moonlight to the Bureau's daily affairs, from idle small talk to the worries you each carry.

  Margaret: Sometimes, the higher one's position, the harder it is to speak one's mind, isn't it? After all, not everyone can truly understand the weight you carry...

  Margaret: You know, sometimes I envy your decisiveness. Take that "sensitive matter" you handled last month, for instance. Had it been me, I fear I would have hesitated too long for fear of complications... Especially given how delicate the backgrounds of those involved were.

  Chief: Just doing my job.

  You don't respond, deftly steering the conversation elsewhere. You believe you've handled it well. She simply keeps you company, downing glass after glass of margarita, watching the flush creep across your cheeks and around your eyes.

  The next morning, you wake to find a note with elegant cursive writing pinned beneath a wine glass. It outlines her thoughts and advice regarding that "internal matter," and you find that they are the same as yours.

  You finally understand her true purpose in mentioning that "sensitive matter" last night. Not to pry words from your lips, but to study your reactions and read your unspoken thoughts.

  After all, it seems you were the only one who truly couldn't hold your drink.

### 「Broken-Winged Dove」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

You receive an SOS from Pricilla and Tetra. Their magic show dove has been injured and is currently being transported back to the Bureau. Upon hearing this, Margaret volunteers to help.

Margaret: Oh, the poor little thing. It must be in such pain and fear, badly hurt like that...

Margaret: Chief, please send it to me. I've raised quite a few birds at home, so I have experience caring for them. It will be in good hands, I assure you.

After some consideration, you entrust the dove to Margaret's care, along with a few reminders.

Chief: Pricilla mentioned this dove is quite clever. It can open its cage and escape on its own. Definitely keep a close eye on it.

Margaret: Rest assured, I'll return it to you in perfect condition.

A few months later, when you see the dove again alongside Pricilla and Tetra, you are nearly struck speechless by its current state.

The word "dove" no longer seems adequate to describe it. Looking at its robust frame and plump breast, you feel that "feathered bowling ball" would be far more fitting.

Chief: This is...?

Margaret: The same little guy that was injured. When I first brought it home, it was so tiny and pitiful-looking. Just look at how lively and healthy it is now!

Chief: It got this fat in just a few months?

Margaret: The bird feed is specially prepared by my family's butler and delivered here. Let me see... There are rare seeds air-shipped from the farlands, imported fresh fruits, Fraser-grown nuts, and competition-grade olive oil from The Great Expo...

Chief: ...Well, that does sound nutritious.

You watch as the "feathered bowling ball" flutters twice in its nest. Its pitiful wings, now comically disproportionate to its massive frame, fail to support its weight and droop down in defeat.

Margaret: This little guy was quite the troublemaker at first. It kept escaping before its injury had healed, and nearly broke another bone outside. Poor thing.

Margaret: Then it occurred to me. Whatever the breed, if a bird can't fly, it will never escape or get hurt again... Hehe...

Margaret: You see, I worked so hard to keep it from escaping.

### 「The Story of a Masterpiece」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Margaret has recently acquired a famous painting from before the Nightfall at an auction and invites you to the viewing held at the Bureau.

Margaret: Behind the birth of this painting lies a sorrowful tale. A child was about to be executed during the war when his pet dove broke free from its cage and threw itself in front of him. The splatter of blood stained the paper the child clutched to his chest.

Margaret: The child's grandfather brought the bloodstained paper to the artist, who was greatly grieved by the event. With bold, sweeping strokes, he created what would become an immortal masterpiece—a dove soaring through the sky, an olive branch in its beak.

Margaret: This painting is both a tragedy and a warning. It exposes the brutality and violence of war. And it is precisely because we have witnessed such horrors that we find the courage to embrace warmth and peace. To the peace that shall one day be ours!

Margaret stands beneath the spotlight as the event's host, delivering her speech and raising her glass. The crowd erupts in applause and cheers. You clap along, genuinely moved, until Margaret's glass clinks yours crisply, drawing back your attention.

Chief: Congratulations on acquiring such a masterpiece, Ms. Margaret. That was a truly moving story.

Margaret: Thank you. In this day and age, this version of the story might sound a touch clichéd and old-fashioned... But I believe in turbulent times, people especially need this kind of peace.

Margaret: However, Chief, there is actually another version of this painting's origin. Would you care to hear it?

Chief: Please, do tell.

Margaret: The other story, well... it's rather underwhelming, I'm afraid. The artist took creative liberties with the death of a child who had perished in the war, fabricating that touching tale of a dove protecting its master.

Margaret: It was because... the artist believed a striking image was needed to ignite the anti-war sentiment among the masses. You see, Chief, sometimes the most compelling "symbol of peace" requires the most meticulous calculation. Wouldn't you agree?

Margaret: Now then, Chief, which story do you find more believable? The touching tale of a dove sacrificing itself to protect its master, or... a carefully choreographed performance, staged by an artist who wished to wake the world?
