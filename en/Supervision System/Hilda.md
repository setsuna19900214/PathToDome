### 「Noise-Canceling Earphones」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Another day of thunderstorms. Remembering Hilda's severe storm anxiety, you go to check on her.

You find her at her desk, posture rigid. A peal of thunder rolls; her pen stutters to a stop. It's only then she seems to feel your presence, looking up.

Hilda: You're here, Chief... Are you worried about me?

Chief: How are those noise-canceling earphones working out for you?

Hilda: It was very thoughtful of you, Chief. Truly...

Hilda: But if I'm being honest, they aren't any more effective at sound isolation than my monitoring headphones. And the fit is... less than ideal. I found myself not using them.

- Express doubts

  Chief: You did remember to turn on the noise-canceling mode, right?

  Hilda: ...Noise-canceling mode?

  Chief: Here, let me show you. Pair them, switch the mode, and then you put them on.

  Chief: Better? The thunder should be gone now.

  Hilda: ...It's gone. The ambient noise has been completely eliminated.

  Hilda: It's not just blocking sound... it's like being suspended in a vacuum. It allows for an unprecedented level of mental clarity. This is... perfect. Thank you, Chief.

### 「The Homebody's Focus」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Sinner Hilda has not left her detention area for 72 consecutive hours. Go and inspect?

- Yes

  You arrive at Hilda's room to find her neatly dressed. She is quietly flipping through the Bureau's new puzzle book with a freshly brewed cup of black tea at her side.

  - Check on her

    Chief: I heard you haven't left your room for days?

    Hilda: ...Has it really been that long?

    Hilda: My apologies for the alarm. There's no need for concern. I've simply been engrossed in these new ciphers. I suppose I lost track of time.

    Chief: Doesn't it get to you, being cooped up in here for so long?

    Hilda: Not in the slightest. The environment is controlled, and all my needs are met. Logically speaking, I could remain here indefinitely without issue.

    Hilda: ...Though, prolonged stillness is unhealthy. It would be logical to move now.

    Hilda: Chief, would you like to join me for a walk?

    Chief: Sure, let's go.

### 「Game Experience」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Etti has thrown herself into organizing another TRPG session for the Sinners, her passion evident.

Etti: Chief! Your arrival is most propitious! You simply must supervise Hilda. She's treating our delightful game as if it were a matter of life and death!

- Agree

  You agree and settle in to watch. You quickly notice that even though this is just a game, Hilda's concentration is absolute and her play is ruthless.

  There's a chilling seriousness to her, as if the fate of city-states, not points, hung in the balance.

  Etti: And so, the narrative reaches its crescendo! Through a series of unfortunate alliances and creative interpretations of loyalty from our dear EMP, the party finds itself in utter disarray!

  She stands alone, shields shattered, with but a single, desperate chance for survival remaining. Hilda, the choice rests in your hands...

  EMP: Hey! That's a totally unfair way to put it! I was just adding some narrative spice! Have a heart, save me—!

  Hilda ignores EMP's pleas and instead turns her head, directing her gaze toward you.

  Hilda: Should I save her, Chief? What is your judgment?


  - Save her

    Chief: Go on and save her. She did help you earlier, after all.

    Hilda gives a silent nod.

    As EMP's cheers of relief fill the air, her character not only escapes death but, in a final act of grace, surrenders the crucial item that secures Hilda's victory.

    Chief: See? A little mercy paid off in the end.

    Hilda: ...Indeed. It seems the rules of engagement in a game are far more forgiving than those on a real battlefield.

  - Remain neutral


  <br>

  Chief: It's your call.

  With an unreadable calm, Hilda eliminates EMP's character, bringing the adventure to a swift but hollow conclusion. The outcome was never in doubt, yet the victory feels empty.

  Etti: And thus, with a single, precise spell from our mage, the dragon is felled, and our grand tale concludes...

  Very well! Next time, I shall craft a narrative robust enough that at least half the party might live to see the dragon's lair.

  Hilda: My apologies. I will attempt to... moderate my approach in the future.

  Hilda: Chief, I suspect your participation would introduce a much-needed variable to the game's logic. Shall we begin another round?

  The other Sinners answer with a chorus of groans, hastily dispersing from the Recreation Area like startled birds.

### 「Beyond the Samples」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Anonymous Email:

Chief, you gotta do something about the new Sinner! She's camped out by the outing prep room like she owns the place, just staring a hole through everyone. She even made me show her my snacks! And because of her, my entire secret candy stash got found and wiped out by the others!

Chief: What's going on here?

- Go and check

  A review of Hilda's movement logs confirms the last six hours of perfect stillness at the entrance, her vigilant gaze cataloging every soul that passed.

  Only when she sees you does she close the notebook resting on her lap.

  Chief: I thought today was your day to go out. Why are you posted up here?

  Hilda: Chief... I find I prefer observing people from here to going outside myself.

  Hilda: Everyone's clothing, their mannerisms, their personal effects... they all serve as fascinating "samples" of the outside world.

  Hilda: For me, this is more interesting than participating. And safer. Even from here, I can still learn what's trending out there.


  - Encourage her to go out

    Chief: The samples others bring back can only tell you so much.

    Chief: Wouldn't you get a much clearer picture and find what you're really looking for if you went out and saw it for yourself?

    Hilda: ...You're right. First-hand observation is always more reliable than second-hand information.

    Hilda: I'll take your advice, Chief.

  - Offer to accompany her


  <br>

  Chief: People-watching has its charms, but you can't really feel the city's pulse until you're out there walking its streets.

  Chief: If you'd rather not go alone, I can clear some time to join you next time.

  Hilda: ...Thank you, Chief. That's very thoughtful of you.

  Hilda: I'll look forward to it, then.
