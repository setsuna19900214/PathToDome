### 「Stealth Game」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

A group of Sinners is playing a newly released game, but many are stuck on its challenging stealth quest. You haven't managed to beat it either. The crowd next to you happens to need two more players, and you're eager to join in.

EMP: I'll team up with Etti, and Chief can... Eh? Anyone want to play this game?

Everyone watching waves their hands in refusal. The reason is simple: The game is simply too hard. The player has to stare at a pitch-black screen and rely only on sounds to determine their position. The crowd shuffles and Thani gets pushed onto the couch.

Thani: Huh... What?! I, I don't know the first thing about gaming! I'm gonna mess everything up. Maybe someone else should play?

EMP: Hey, this is perfect... Ahem, I mean, no worries! Chief definitely won't mind carrying a beginner, right?

- I don't mind.

  To keep Thani from feeling discouraged, you reassure her.

  Chief: Don't worry, I wasn't very good when I first started out either. All you need is some practice, you'll see...

  Before you can finish your sentence, the victory screen pops up on your side. Everyone's jaw drops as they stare at Thani in disbelief, who has successfully eliminated her two opponents.

  EMP: But... didn't you just say you've never played video games before?!

  Thani: It's all about listening closely! Your footsteps gave away your positions. Plus there's tons of cover in this level. I just had to hide behind something and you couldn't see me at all!

  Chief: That's impressive.

  Thani sticks out her tongue, then sneakily leans closer to you.

  Thani: It's nothing special, I'm just good at being stealthy. A newbie like me wouldn't have stood a chance if it'd been a different type of game. But don't let them know, Chief!

### 「Best Cleaner」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

The Bureau will select a *Best Cleaner* from all the cleaning staff. Ever since the announcement, you keep running into Labyrinth lurking and spying around Thani's assigned cleaning area.

- Inquire

  Chief: What are you doing...?

  Labyrinth: As the master's most valuable maid, Labyrinth is evaluating and analyzing this Sinner's cleaning abilities to determine if she poses a threat to Labyrinth's victory.

  She's barely finished her sentence when a loud thud echoes from the corridor as something falls to the floor, followed by the sound of Thani muttering to herself.

  Thani: Oh no... I messed up again! I used the disinfectant instead of the detergent and damaged the floor. Now I'm really in trouble...

  Chief: ...She doesn't sound like she can threaten you, does she?

  Labyrinth: This Sinner is allegedly highly manipulative. She can make people willingly do her bidding, which might influence the judges' scoring. She cannot be underestimated.

  The supposedly *manipulative* Thani glances around, then spots you both. Her eyes light up as she scurries over, water bucket in hand, her footsteps pattering rapidly over the floor.

  Thani: Oh, how lucky I am! I find my saviors right when I need them most! It's Labyrinth and Chief! Please, please, please help me!

  Thani: Labyrinth! You're the most amazing, most perfect super maid in the entire Bureau! I've been dying to see your legendary skills up close!

  Thani buzzes around Labyrinth like a little ball of sunshine, chattering excitedly with stars in her eyes. Even from where you stand, her enthusiasm is almost scorching. Out of sympathy, you glance at Labyrinth.

  As expected, Labyrinth's mechanical body becomes more rigid than usual. Even her typically precise electronic voice suddenly sounds glitchy, and her language module begins to malfunction.

  Labyrinth: L-l-logical c-c-conflict detected. Considering the *Best Cleaner* competition... Labyrinth should refuse to assist her potential competitor. However, Labyrinth calculates that helping the master manage unexpected environmental contamination caused by a Sinner... also falls within the duty of a responsible maid...

  Labyrinth: Conclusion: Sinner Thani, Labyrinth agrees to assist you with the cleaning task.

  Thani: Yay! I knew it! You really are the most responsible, most reliable maid ever! Taking on this disaster without even flinching! You're simply incredible!

  Labyrinth beams with pride at Thani's flattery.

  Labyrinth: Labyrinth is flattered. Labyrinth is indeed the master's most indispensable maid.

  Thani: I'll leave the cleaning to you then! Let's work hard together! We'll definitely get this whole area spotless!

  You watch the two of them walk away together and suddenly begin to wonder... What did Labyrinth come here for again?

### 「Job Hunting」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

[System Notice] Supervision System:

Sinner Thani has requested a meeting with Chief. Permit entry?

- Permit entry

  Command confirmed. A moment later, the office door swings open with a whoosh, and Thani bounces in like an energetic little cannonball, her face beaming with anticipation.

  Chief: Thani? What can I do for you?

  Thani: Chief! I'm looking for a job! Please give me a chance to shine!

  Chief: What kind of job are you looking for?

  Thani: Anything! Don't you forget, I used to be the star janitor of Aurum Secretum Research Institute! I've got the experience! I'll be the most cost-effective hire! You'll definitely get your money's worth!

  Chief: Um... how about this? You could start by rotating through different departments as an intern. Get a feel for different positions and their benefits, then decide what truly suits you.

  Thani: No problem! You can count on me! You're the best, Chief!

  Thani throws herself into her internship across the Bureau's different departments with boundless enthusiasm. However, before her passion can even properly take root, in less than a week, your comms device is flooded with reports from the various divisions.

  - View complaints

    System:

    Complaint: While assigned to the Logistics Department, the subject demonstrated exceptional cleaning skills. However, in her attempt to remove stains, she completely scraped off the promotional poster, resulting in the erasure of the entire wall display. Comment: This is destruction of public property. Termination is recommended.

    - Next report

      System:

      While working at the Supply Office, the subject has demonstrated a positive work attitude. However, in an attempt to make the shelves more orderly, she took it upon herself to completely empty all supplies from them and reorganize the items in the corridor by colors and sizes, resulting in disruption in the depot system. Comment: Her overzealousness has resulted in a disastrous outcome. Termination is recommended.

      - Next report

        System:

        After transferring to the Security Department, the subject's combat performance has been up to standard. However, her senses remain hypersensitive even during breaks, and she often misinterprets innocuous noises as potential threats, successfully shocking at least four security guards on break. Comment: The subject poses unpredictable risks. Official recruitment not recommended!

        You turn off the screen and press your fingers between your eyebrows in frustration.

        Chief: Are there any departments she hasn't wrea... um, applied to yet?

        System:

        Only one department remains: New Staff Training Department. This position primarily involves training facility maintenance, training equipment organization, and other basic tasks. Warning: Extremely high-risk. Approve Sinner Thani's rotation to this department?


        - Yes, let her try

          Chief: Basic tasks... equipment organization... These are exactly the janitorial tasks she excels at. Surely nothing can go wrong this time, right?

          With one last sliver of hope, you approve the transfer. Thani bounds excitedly to her new position. Sure enough, the system issues a warning on her fifth day.

          System Warning:

          Sinner Thani, stop performing combat demonstrations using your own abilities. The situation has triggered the Bureau's emergency alert. Drop your weapon immediately and surrender...

          Thani's job-hunting journey comes to an unsuccessful end.

        - Staff safety matters, after all


        <br>

        You refuse to let Thani take on any other roles in the Bureau. As it turns out, since her departure, everything has returned to normal. Except for herself.

        Thani: Chief says... there are no more job openings? But I could've sworn everyone needed my help...

        Thani's job-hunting journey comes to an unsuccessful end.
