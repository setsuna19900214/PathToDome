# Chengxiao Supervision System

### 「Friendly Match」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠

Passing by the Recreation Area, you catch the sound of harsh, snapping impacts. Jade crashes against the board, each strike so violent it seems to throw off sparks.

- The uncle and niece are at it again.

### 「Morning Routine」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠

A complaint comes in from one of the Sinners. Every morning, without fail, Chengxiao's five o'clock alarm drags them out of sleep.

- Move Chengxiao to a different room.

### 「A Timely Reminder」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Lately, it seems every time you look up from your work, Chengxiao is there at the door.

When your eyes meet, he murmurs a few soft words of Eastian wellness advice.

Chengxiao: Sitting too long... stagnates the blood and qi...

Chengxiao: At the appropriate time, one should stand... and walk a few paces...

- Respond to the reminder

  Chief: Are you here to remind me to take a break?

  Chengxiao sets a steaming cup of tea by your hand, then raises a finger and gestures toward the green plant at the edge of your desk.

  Chengxiao: Overusing one's eyes... exhausts the mind... and water must be drunk warm...

  - Express your gratitude

    Chief: I'll keep that in mind. Thanks for looking out for me.

    With a pleased nod, Chengxiao takes his leave.

    In the days that follow, you find that his advice has quietly become habit. You pause to stretch, sip warm tea, and rest your eyes at steady intervals.

    But Chengxiao no longer seems to linger at your office door the way he once did.

### 「The Way to Unwind」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

According to the researchers, Chengxiao's M-value spikes at specific, predictable times throughout the day.

Routine calming measures aren't working well, and more forceful suppression might provoke an even stronger reaction. With few options left, you've been summoned to the detention area to assist in observing him.

Chengxiao: ...Qi all tangled... the spirit unsettled... something to occupy the hands... to ease the restlessness...

- Ask what he needs

  Chief: What do you want?

  Chengxiao: ...

  There is no answer from Chengxiao. One of the researchers tells you that with his M-value in such an unstable state, he is barely able to process words.

  All the while, his fingers curl and release in a slow rhythm, as if grasping for something to hold onto.

  - Act

    You recall how Chengxiao often likes to turn small things over in his hands. Taking the two walnuts he had pressed on you not long ago, you offer them back to him.

    He pauses, then accepts them. Slowly, his fingers start moving again, rotating the walnuts with a quiet rhythm. As he does, his M-value begins, little by little, to calm.

    Researcher: Logging update. Initial conclusion: spherical wooden objects appear to have a notable calming effect on this Sinner.

    Next step: determine whether other Eastian Sinners exhibit similar preferences for such objects.

    Chief: Wouldn't count on it. His hobbies are pretty niche.

### 「The Art of Placement」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Outside Luminita's room, you catch her sorting through a box of strange little ornaments, ready to toss them all away.

She tells you they were sold to her cheap as "evil-warding" treasures, but after taking them home, misfortune followed her everywhere: shattered mugs, vanished keys, one thing after another. Clearly, they had been marked down for good reason.

Luminita: Cleansing, incense, prayers... I've tried everything I could think of, and none of it worked! They weren't exactly cheap either, but honestly, dumping them is the safest bet!

No sooner has Luminita finished than Chengxiao, who has appeared behind her unnoticed, extends his hand and plucks one of the small ornaments from the box, studying it with quiet attention.

Chengxiao: ...Misplaced. The energy's scattered... This one doesn't need to go.

- Inquire

  Chief: You're into these ornaments too?

  Chengxiao gives a measured nod, selects several ornaments from the box, and sets them on the cabinet in a careful sequence.

  They are the same odd little objects, but once he has rearranged them, there is a strange harmony to how they look.

  Chengxiao: The shape itself means nothing... it's the arrangement that matters... Like this... much better...


  - Suggest Luminita trust Chengxiao

    Several days on, you find that Luminita has stocked up on cheap ornaments, drawn arrangement diagrams, and started selling them together as curated sets.

    Chengxiao seems rather pleased with how things turned out.

    Chengxiao: Money follows the Dao as well... Used properly... it grows.

  - Leave


  <br>

  A few days later, as you and Chengxiao are having tea, Luminita shows up again.

  Luminita: Master! My hero! Can you show me your arrangement method one more time? I only tweaked the angle a little to make them look prettier, and now the bad luck is worse than ever!

  Chengxiao: The old omen has been disturbed... Keep them... and you'll only draw more trouble.

  Chengxiao: Get rid of them... and cut the karma.

  Rather than adjusting the ornaments again, Chengxiao helps Luminita dispose of them altogether. Only then does the wave of misfortune that had been following her finally break.

### 「Mahjong Stand-In」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Chengxiao has never once lost at mahjong in the Bureau. His reputation is so complete that no Sinner will share a table with him anymore, leaving him to watch from the sidelines in silence.

Passing through the Recreation Area today, you come across a table that's short one player. The others wave you over, and before you know it, you've filled the empty seat.

Since it's your first time playing, the tiles are still unfamiliar. You try to absorb the rules while clumsily organizing your hand. It's only then that you notice Chengxiao standing just behind you.

A few rounds in, you pick up on a pattern. Whenever you reach for a tile to discard, the walnuts in Chengxiao's hand make a small, distinct click.

- Test it out

  The sound is faint, but by now you've learned to read it. Under Chengxiao's quiet direction, your play begins to flow with growing confidence.

  Then, at a pivotal moment, you pull a tile from the wall, and the walnuts behind you go quiet.


  - Wing it

    A moment passes, and still Chengxiao makes no sound. Forced to act on your own, you rely on your shaky grasp of the rules and pick a tile to discard.

    Chengxiao: The match is already settled... So, my friend, care for another round?

    Chief: I need a break.

    Chengxiao: Extended play exhausts the mind...

    Chengxiao: Rest a while... then we play again.

  - Wait for a cue


  <br>

  You glance at Chengxiao, only to find his gaze still fixed on the mahjong table. He appears to have withdrawn his guidance entirely.

  Chengxiao: One may not yet see the path... but the game has already reached its end...

  Chengxiao: Your own will... is the correct move...

  A moment passes, and still Chengxiao makes no sound. Forced to act on your own, you rely on your shaky grasp of the rules and pick a tile to discard.

  Chengxiao: The match is already settled... So, my friend, care for another round?

  Chief: No, I need a break.

  Chengxiao: Such a pity...
