  Chief: This is... a swimming pool? Wedding?

    Graves: Hmm, that's a tough decision. Looks like my wedding plan still needs some refinement!

### 「Midnight Melody」

Graves often plays the pipe organ in the Recreation Area. She can effortlessly perform wedding marches that sound beautifully melodious.

But recently, you realize you haven't heard her play for quite some time.

- Investigate the situation

  You arrive at the Recreation Area at midnight and hear intermittent music coming from the music room. Whoever is inside seems to be struggling with a complex musical passage, practicing it over and over again.

  Chief: Are you practicing a new piece?

  Graves: Chief, what brings you here... I'm practicing a new dance piece, but it's not going very well. It's so late, did my practice startle you?

  Chief: Not at all. Just keep practicing at your own pace. It takes time to master any skill.

  Graves: That... yes, you're right. It makes me happy to hear you say that.

  Chief: Why did you suddenly start learning new pieces?

  Graves: I heard that in some places, there's a tradition where the bride and groom dance together after the wedding ceremony... So I thought learning dance music would be something a proper wedding officiant should know.

  Graves: It's just that these dance pieces are harder to learn than I expected... How can I make my playing more lively and spirited...


  - Keep her company while she practices

    Chief: I'll practice with you. Is there anything I can help you with?

    Graves: Hehe, just having you sit beside me, Chief, is the greatest support I could ask for.

    You feel Graves cautiously shifting her body a little closer to you.

    Graves: Thank you... I can feel it again, your warmth...

  - Offer assistance


  <br>

  Chief: This piece is meant for the piano. It would be much easier to master on a piano, especially the staccato notes. I'll authorize access for you to the piano room.

  Graves: Piano... so that's the answer. If you hadn't pointed that out, I would have struggled with this for ages.

  Chief: It's perfectly normal to not know. Don't be too hard on yourself.

  Graves: Thank you, Chief. You're always so kind!
