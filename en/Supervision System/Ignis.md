### 「Some Progress」

Ever since Cabernet joined the MBCC, Ignis's passion for cooking has reached a new level.

Despite frequent kitchen accidents and Cabernet's sharp critiques, Ignis remains undeterred on her culinary journey.

Ignis: ...This is my latest masterpiece, Nirvana. It's an innovative dish using poultry, eggs, and several red vegetables. I've put a lot of effort into this dish, so I hope Chief and Miss Cabernet will give it a try!

Chief: It looks quite good. Ignis, I didn't know you had such knife skills.

Maintaining her elegant and polite smile, Cabernet listens to Ignis's long and flowery introduction to her dish.

Chief: Cabernet, no need to rush. At least try a bit of everything first.

Cabernet: What do you think about the dish then, Chief?

- It's very delicious, of course.

  Chief: If you ask me, it's both visually stunning and delicious. Her knife skills are just right, allowing the spices to fully penetrate the poultry. The taste and texture are both distinct and harmonious. I would give this dish a perfect score.

  - Ask Cabernet

    Chief: I can't tell what you think from your expression, Cabernet.

    Cabernet: In conclusion, it's no different from the dishes I've tasted at street stalls.

    Chief: Ah?!

    Ignis: ...

    Ignis's face turns pale, and she panics.

    Cabernet, watching all of this, just smiles even more sweetly.

    Ignis: Could... could you tell me why?

    Cabernet: The poultry is overcooked. Ten minutes would have been enough, but you used twelve minutes and thirty seconds. You didn't remove the pulp of the tomatoes, which leaves an unpleasant sour taste. As for the capsicums, you cut open the seeds while chopping, resulting in a subtle bitterness in the side dish.

    Ignis: This... how...

    Cabernet's critique hits Ignis hard. This strong-willed and hot-tempered young girl now looks utterly defeated and on the verge of tears.

    - Quickly dissuade

      Chief: No, Ignis, Cabernet is just...

      Cabernet: However, it's not completely inedible. The handling of the soup is perfect, and the flavor is rich. Although there is a lot to improve on, I believe this is the first dish you've made that I can swallow.

      Cabernet: Hmm, a slight improvement.

      Ignis: Thank... thank you for your critique!

      Cabernet: What an interesting girl. You might be even more delicious than this dish...

      Chief: ...
