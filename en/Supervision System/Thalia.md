# Thalia Supervision System

### 「Broadcast Mishap」

Thalia told you in advance that she would be doing a mukbang stream with Poffy today. But when it was time for the stream to start, you didn't receive any notifications.

- Go and take a look

  At the streaming location, you find Thalia crouched in deep thought in front of a pile of products, with Poffy looking dejected beside her.

  Chief: Hasn't the stream started yet?

  Thalia waves her hand, motioning for you to crouch down beside her.

  You discover the ground is covered with snack packages, each one bearing obvious bite marks, with the food inside vanished without a trace.

  Chief: Why do all these snacks look like they've been gnawed on? Don't tell me the Bureau has a cockroach problem?!

  Poffy: I'm not a cockroach! I'm a seal!

  Thalia: Oh, so those are baby seal's teeth marks.

  Chief: Poffy, you cleaned out everything here? I thought the Bureau was feeding you properly.

  Poffy: Thalia gave me a sample to try, but it smelled so good that I kept eating and eating, and by the time I came to my senses...

  Chief: So you ate up all our inventory?

  Thalia reaches into the pile of debris and rummages around, pulling out a relatively intact package and waving it at you.

  Thalia: One final pack. Would you like to try some?

  Chief: ...Thank you. Will these need to be compensated for?

  Thalia: No worries about the compensation; I'll cover it. The most urgent thing is our coming livestream, but look at what we have now...

  Poffy: Sob... I messed up, I'll definitely find a way to make up for it!

  Chief: Here's what we'll do. I'll go ask Faye if there are any extra snacks in the Depot. They won't be the specialty snacks we originally planned to feature, but I think Poffy's eating style alone will be entertaining enough to draw plenty of viewers.

  Poffy: Yeah! I'll do my best! I'm actually something of a mukbanger now!

  Poffy: I, I can also heat up food with my bare hands! That'll definitely draw in lots of viewers!

  Thalia: Sounds... feasible. Now you two get ready. We don't have much time. Live in fifteen minutes!

  Chief: "You two"...? I get to join in too?!

### 「It's a Trap!」

During lunch break, you remember that Faye mentioned earlier that the library got a batch of new books, among which there happened to be some you're interested in. You decide to go take a look.

In the library, Thalia is reclining on the sofa in an exaggerated pose, like a star shooting for an ad.

- Greet her

  You greet her, then go to the bookshelf to look for the books you want, but you don't find them, so you prepare to leave.

  Thalia stands up, blocks your path, and strikes a pose.

  - Walk around her

    Thalia shifts slightly and happens to block your path again.

    Chief: Could you let me through?

    Thalia: Oh my, I'm so sorry.

    Although Thalia says this, her body doesn't move an inch.

    - Question her

      Chief: What exactly are you trying to do?

      Thalia: Chief, what do you think of this outfit?

      Chief: Hm, it looks quite nice.

      Thalia: Right? This is a classic design, just look at this craftsmanship. And it's unisex too.

      Thalia: If you want one too, you can go to the Supply Office and ask Faye about it.

      Thalia: If you mention my name, you can even get a fifty DisCoin voucher.

      Chief: So this is your trap. What did Faye bribe you with?

      Thalia: Don't put it so bluntly. I'm just helping her move some off-season merchandise from the depot.

      Thalia: She said if I can get you to open your wallet, she'll consider a long-term partnership with me. That's what you call a win-win situation.

      Chief: And where exactly do I win in all this?

      Thalia: I'll give you a 20% discount on the next collaboration!

### 「In High Demand」

You encounter Thalia in the hallway, dressed up formally.

- Inquire

  Chief: Thalia? Where are you going dressed up like that?

  Thalia, wearing an evening gown, takes out a gold-embossed envelope from her handbag.

  Thalia: I'm on my way to Miss Parfait's room. She invited me to dine with her.

  Chief: Now? But it's been way past dinner time for a while.

  Thalia: It took me a very long time to choose the right outfit.

  Thalia: For a dinner of this caliber, dressing up properly is a matter of mutual respect.

  Thalia: Miss Parfait mentioned in her letter that she's wanted to meet with me privately for a long time. An opportunity like this is once in a lifetime.

  Thalia: If I can make a good impression on her this time and build a strong relationship, perhaps I can cultivate her as one of my sponsors.

  Thalia: Who knows, maybe someday in the future, she'll even invite me to share our success stories with the public together!

  Thalia: I've even thought of a title for our co-authored book—"Secrets of Wealth: A Master Class by Parfait and Thalia." What do you think?

  Looking at Thalia, you wonder how to tell her that Parfait's invitation was sent to everyone, without crushing her dreams.

  Chief: Well, you'd better hurry then. The line of people wanting to dine with Parfait is about to reach my office door.

### 「Growing Pains」

You are reading the monthly survey questionnaires that staff just collected from the Sinners.

In the questionnaire, Thalia indicated that compared to the past, her quality of life at MBCC has drastically declined, and she feels extremely dissatisfied.

- Talk to Thalia

  You arrive at Thalia's room. She sits with her legs crossed and arms folded across her chest, looking like she's been expecting you for quite some time.

  Thalia: Ever since I moved in here, my account has been losing followers for several months straight.

  Thalia: A lot of people are commenting that after following me for so long, they feel like I've changed, that my posts are getting more and more half-hearted.

  Thalia: Whether it's livestreams or sponsored content, I haven't changed my background in ages.

  Thalia: They're also saying that maybe I got too many followers and became arrogant, so I don't even bother responding to them anymore.

  Thalia: I wouldn't dare do that, or a certain Chief would make me recite the NDA again.

  Chief: Well, that... you know how things are at the Bureau...

  Chief: How about... considering a change?

    Thalia: My audience might not buy it. They love nitpicking, and they'll definitely say I'm pretending to know things I don't.

    Thalia: At the very least... I'd need a professional to back me up.

    Chief: You mean...?

    Thalia: Chief! How about being my first guest after I pivot?
