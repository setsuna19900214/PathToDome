- Ask her to be mindful of her facial expressions.

### 「Building Blocks Resurrected」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

EMP drags you to a spot where Irrheia sits, calmly reading a book.

EMP: Chief, it's a catastrophe! You gotta see this! Irrheia totally wrecked my limited-edition Hallowing building blocks! I'm, like, actually gonna die!

- What happened?

  Irrheia: "Wrecked" is such a pedestrian critique. I just returned your forgotten props to the stage, allowing them to fulfill their true purpose.

  You dismiss their argument and head to EMP's room to inspect the building blocks yourself.

  Before you can turn on the light, a ghastly pale face looming behind the door startles you.

  In the dimness, you see that Irrheia has reassembled the once-cute ghost blocks into a deathly white portrait. The crude construction technique makes the figure in this makeshift artwork appear more terrifying than an actual ghost.

  EMP: See?! Even Chief jumped! So you see, right? It's not just me! This isn't a "me" problem!

  - You probably shouldn't do this again.

    Irrheia: Heh. Slaving over an instruction manual is such a bore. But if this particular act fails to entertain, we can always move to the next.

    Irrheia: Shall we craft a ghostly pale hand? Or perhaps a lamp, with a skeletal grin for a shade? The choice is yours.

    EMP: Waaahhhh! Chief—!!

### 「Face Blindness」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You receive an email from Irrheia.

Irrheia: Chief, our stock of Hallowing candies has been exhausted again. I request a double supply of caramel crucibles and ghost marshmallows.

- Again?

  Chief: You're saying that over a hundred Sinners lined up and took all the candy in just ten minutes?

  Irrheia: Precisely. That's the scene as it played out. My role was simple: offer a treat to anyone who presented a name not yet in our ledger.

  - ...I'll help you distribute the candy.

    Your arrival with Irrheia in the Outdoor Activity Area prompts most of the candy-obsessed Sinners to fall in line, though a few still dare to defy authority for the sake of sweets.

    Hella: Hey, no fair, Chief! How come I don't get any? The name "Hella" hasn't been on your list even once!

    Chief: True, "Hella" isn't registered. But you're the one who registered all these other names, aren't you?

    Chief: "Chief Dummy," "Syndicate Rat Queen"...

    Hella: Whoa, okay, okay! I give up! No more candy for me! Jeez, it's so embarrassing when you just read them out like that!

    Irrheia: Hehe, so you're Hella? What a delightful twist in the script. I'll be sure to remember your role from now on.

    Chief: In that case, I'll help you get to know everyone in the Outdoor Activity Area. Once you can recognize them all, no one will be able to claim candy under false names.

    Irrheia: A guided tour of the cast? Now that promises to be a refreshing new chapter.

### 「Doom Game」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

A weathered notebook has materialized on your desk. Its pages are a mosaic of doodles and text, offering no clue to its owner or how long it has been circulating through the Bureau.

- Examine carefully

  As you open it, a wave of divine knowledge reveals the immutable rules:

  You are now the holder, and you will be haunted by misfortune.

  The curse cannot be broken, only transferred.

  Your only path to freedom is to complete the task written by the last owner, create a new task for the next, and pass the notebook on.

  Chief: The task I have to complete is... giving everyone at the Bureau a day off...

  Chief: I could use a day off myself...

  You've already guessed who's behind this prank. Now, the choice is yours.


  - End this charade

    After you contact her, Irrheia promptly arrives at your office. She flips through the notebook with delight, completely absorbed in admiring her handiwork.

    Irrheia: What a delightful cast of characters our Bureau has... Tell me, Chief, did you call me here because you're afraid the next act of this tragedy will feature you?

    Chief: You've gotten enough material by now, haven't you? Time to end this prank?

    Irrheia: Oh, I suppose. It has been quite entertaining.

    Irrheia: Now, the real question is... how should I gather material next time? Hehe.

  - Play along


  <br>

  After writing down a new task, you pass the notebook to Irrheia.

  Irrheia: Chief, either you've fundamentally misunderstood the nature of this game, or you're making a deliberate, and quite fascinating, attempt to subvert its very architect. Which is it?

  Chief: The day off is on the calendar for next week. Your new task, until then, is to make sure this "misfortune" doesn't come knocking at my door.

  Irrheia: Oh, you are a clever one... Just when I think I've predicted your moves, you change the rules of the board.

### 「Evil Cuisine Contest」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Approval System Notification:

Title: Evil Cuisine Contest

Time: Weekend XX

Location: Bureau Recreation Area

Organizer: Irrheia

Please refer to the attached files for detailed event schedule and participation requirements.

- Put request on hold

  Seeing Irrheia's eager smile before you, you can no longer hold back your growing doubts.

  Chief: Irrheia, are you trying to destroy the Bureau?

  Irrheia: Of course not. With you at the helm, Chief, do you truly believe a mere culinary contest could unravel this grand institution?


  - Try to understand

    Chief: Why do you want to hold an "evil cuisine contest"?

    Irrheia: Traditional cooking is a cage, a mindless recitation of recipes. It binds the chef's soul to the page.

    But evil cuisine... it is an uncharted frontier. The one, true catalyst for culinary evolution, waiting just beyond the pale of common understanding.

    Chief: Isn't it wasteful if the food is so unusual that no one wants to eat it?

    Irrheia: On that point, you can rest entirely assured. I've already secured the most adventurous of Sinners as our willing taste-testers. We shall tailor the portions to their formidable appetites; not a single, revolutionary morsel will be wasted.

    Chief: Since you've already made all the arrangements, I suppose I have no reason to stop you.

    Irrheia: I do so wonder what fascinating expression will grace your face when you sample our creations, Chief...

  - Decline politely


  <br>

  Chief: I understand you enjoy experimenting with unusual foods, but isn't holding an "evil cuisine contest" a bit too extreme?

  Irrheia: An ordinary contest would simply reinforce the old walls. Only by confronting the "extreme" can we truly shatter the shell of culinary habit!

  Irrheia: The Bureau is brimming with remarkable chefs, Chief. Surely you have faith in their transformative potential?

  Chief: Okay...

  Irrheia: I am certain this little contest will provide the necessary perspective for you to finally appreciate the exquisite worth of evil cuisine.

### 「Unreasonable Request」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

Ever since Norma joined the Bureau, you frequently run into her around the office area.

Normally, you both just exchange brief nods after making eye contact, but today, Norma stops in her tracks.

Norma: Chief, could you please refrain from causing congestion in the corridor?

Chief: ...?

- Why?

  Sensing your obliviousness, Norma begins laying out her argument seriously in front of the other onlooking Sinners.

  Norma: The optimal route from the detention area to the office area overlaps considerably with your daily area of activity, Chief. However, you seem to constantly trigger various incidents that result in large-scale gatherings of Sinners. This negatively impacts the Bureau's internal traffic.

  Norma: This phenomenon significantly slows down the circulation of staff and disrupts the overall workflow.

  Norma: Based on the above analysis, I hope you could take this issue seriously, Chief.


  - Invite her

    Chief: Would you like to join our casual chat?

    Norma: ...Huh?

    Chief: Relationships are maintained with small talk and regular interactions. Want to give it a try?

    Norma: ...Very well. Logically speaking, stable emotional connections can indeed reduce friction in communication.

    Norma: I will consider adding daily social interactions to my schedule and logging my observations.

  - Tell her to relax


  <br>

  Chief: You don't have to stay alert all the time. It's okay to take a break and chat with people once in a while.

  Irrheia: Heh, instead of a gradual adjustment, why not dive into a thrilling full-day murder mystery experience with me?

  Norma: I'll have to decline... I still don't quite understand the point of this type of interaction that lacks clear objectives.

  Norma: However, since it's your suggestion, Chief, I will add it to my list of items-to-explore.
