### 「Art of Tree Climbing」

Location: Outdoor Activity Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You pass by the tallest tree in the Outdoor Activity Area and look up to see Jichuan resting among its branches.

- Call out to her

  Jichuan notices it's you, and her face lights up with a warm, cheerful smile. With effortless grace, she leaps down from the branches.

  Chief: You can actually climb trees?!

  Jichuan: It's nothing to admire, young hero. The manor had trees as well. A child climbs often enough, and the body remembers.

  Noticing your rapt attention, she slows her movements as she winds her way up the trunk, turning, swinging her legs, and landing in one fluid motion.

  Jichuan: Tree climbing is simply reading the tree. The knots and grain show you where to hold. With your waist as the pivot, hands and feet follow in turn...

  Jichuan: Would you like to try, young hero?

  - Climb the tree

    It sounds hard, and it proves to be exactly that. Your fingers soon begin to throb with every grip, and that's when you know you're in trouble.

    Chief: ...A little help here?

    Jichuan smiles gently, wraps her feet around the branch, and leans down with an easy grace, her hand stretched out toward you.

    - Grab her hand

      With Jichuan's help, you ease onto the branch beside her. She gives a soft smile and gently smooths down your tousled hair, her slightly cool fingertips brushing against your forehead from time to time.

      Chief: If someone knew qinggong, they could probably go much higher.

      Jichuan: When I was small, I always wished to climb higher. I was certain that from up there, I would see a much broader world.

      Jichuan: And now... I suppose I have.

### 「Master of Disguise」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠

You've finally wrapped up your work and are about to set off for the annual celebration when Jichuan calls you.

- Answer

  Jichuan: You must be weary from your labors, young hero. When might you be able to slip away?

  Chief: Just finished. Heading over now.

  Jichuan: How timely. A few days ago, I received some traditional robes from Eastia. I've grown quite fond of them. I dressed in them today, especially.

  Jichuan: There is still some time before the gathering begins. I thought you might like to see them properly.

  Chief: Okay! I'll be there right away!

  After hanging up, you imagine her dressed in ornate traditional robes. A flutter of anticipation quickens your steps.

  - Look for Jichuan

    You weave through the bustling crowd but can't spot Jichuan anywhere. Just then, someone taps you from behind.

    ??: Chief, are you searching for someone?

    Chief: Yeah... Whoa, who are you?!

    You spin around and jump back, startled. A tall figure in festive robes with a magnificent beard stands before you, but no Sinner you've ever seen looks like this.

    ??: Chief, do you not recognize me?

    Chief: Wait. You can't be... But your height...?

    You can barely trust your eyes, but that voice is unmistakably the one from the phone call.

    Jichuan hikes up her robe to show the stilts underneath, then gives the gleaming fake beard a self-satisfied stroke.

    Jichuan: So? What do you think of my disguise?

    Chief: I give up...

### 「Tea by the Hearth」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

You're burning the midnight oil when Jichuan and Yao show up at your office.

Chief: What brings you two here this late?

Jichuan: We went out shopping today. On our way back, we happened to pass by and saw your light still on. It seemed only right to look in on you.

Her gaze sweeps over the stacks of documents on your desk and your crammed schedule, admiration glowing in her eyes.

Chief: ...Would you like something to drink?

She looks around, and her eyes settle on the black coffee sitting on your desk—no sugar, no milk, as stark and potent as it gets. Without hesitation, she says she'd like the same.

Chief: I don't think you'll like it... It tastes about as bad as your medicine.

Jichuan's gaze shifts to one of sympathy.

Jichuan: You're devoted, young hero. But devotion shouldn't come at the cost of rest.

Jichuan: We happened to buy some tea supplies today. Please, Chief, sit. Have tea with us.

Jichuan arranges a small brazier and a tea set, puts in what are unmistakably premium tea leaves, and pours in water. Only then does she turn to Yao.

Jichuan: If you would be so kind.

Yao gives a nod and kindles a small flame in the brazier. Almost immediately, the warm fragrance of tea begins to permeate the air.

Yao, I didn't know you had that kind of control over fire!

Fancy tastes you two have got!

  Jichuan: The cold never feels quite so bitter when I'm near her.

Chief: Won't it be too cold outside?

### 「Readers' Corner」

Location: Recreation Area

Urgency: ⚠⚠⚠

You're just taking a breather in the library when Jichuan and Yugu's argument pulls you in.

After eavesdropping for a while, you figure out they're going back and forth over a novel set in the martial world that's recently blown up in Syndicate—*The Vagrant Hero*.

Yugu: The world is a stage of thorns and shadows. How could tales of shattered dreams and lovers torn apart not strike the deepest chord?

Jichuan: Even the smallest lantern can pierce the longest night. Surely those who strive for justice leave a mark upon this world.

Yugu: Ah, so they are but moths chasing a flame? How exquisitely tragic!

Jichuan: You... I fear I must respectfully disagree. I have never believed there exists an injustice too great to be overcome...

Neither is willing to back down until Jichuan is racked by a sudden coughing fit, her balance wavering.

Yugu's expression softens; she stops arguing at once and gently helps Jichuan sit down with a warm smile. Then, without missing a beat, she calls out to you as you try to quietly slip away.

Yugu: And where do you think you're going? Come, settle this for us. What ending would best befit *The Vagrant Hero*?

Chief: Well... uh...

Jichuan: Young hero, please, speak freely. I would be glad to hear your thoughts as well.


【选项1】

- Wait for the author to write it

  Chief: Perhaps we could... just wait for the author to write it?

  Yugu: That simply will not do. That elusive author of *The Vagrant Hero* drifts like morning mist. Who knows when they might grace us with another chapter?

  Yugu: Then again, perhaps the longer we wait, the more likely it shall end as I have anticipated.

  Jichuan: That will not happen. The ending has already been written... for I am the author.

  Yugu: Oh my! So you are actually "Tide Traveler"! Why ever did you keep this to yourself?

  Overjoyed far beyond what anyone expected, Yugu forgets the argument altogether and latches onto Jichuan, pestering her relentlessly for updates.

- Every reader holds their own martial world in their heart.


<br>

Yugu: Well said. As the verse goes, each flower, a world unto itself. And I have always adored ruin and decay.

Jichuan hears you out, a strange look crossing her face. A long moment passes before she finally speaks.

Jichuan: What you say is not unreasonable. A reader's heart should be honored... I will convey this to the author.

Yugu: Oh? You know "Tide Traveler"?

Jichuan: ...One might say so.

Overjoyed far beyond what anyone expected, Yugu forgets the argument altogether and latches onto Jichuan, pestering her relentlessly to find out when the author will update.
