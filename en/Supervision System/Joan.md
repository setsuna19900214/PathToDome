### 「Work and Rock」

Location: Management Center

Urgency: ⚠⚠

Joan walks into your office.

Joan: Hey, Chief. I admit you have okay taste in music. But don't you get bored looping the same songs in the office all the time?

Joan: Here, try these songs I've picked for you. I'm sure you'll get hyped up right away.

Deafening rock music echoes in the entire office.

Joan: How is it? Powerful, isn't it? This is real music! This is rock & roll!

You begin grooving to the rhythm of the music and you don't feel like working anymore.

Joan: Right, that's it! Embrace that rhythm in your heart!

- Join Joan

  You join Joan and the two of you continue feeling the power of rock together.

  Three hours later, you're working after hours under Nightingale's supervision to finish what you shoved aside in the afternoon.

  Chief: Rock is definitely not the best background music for working...
