# Knight of Hearts

## III

The Castle of Lies

## Chief captures Horo and the wisps with a trap

1. Chief and Hella get out safely.
2. Chief encounters the possessed Horo.
3. Chief escapes by luring Horo into a trap.

Chief: This is... a maze?

In an instant, the entire Red Heart Garden was transformed into a massive labyrinth.

The vine walls surrounding you are nearly three meters tall, with faint light occasionally flickering through the gaps in the vegetation, as if ready to swallow anything that approaches.

Hella: Nooo! My garden! Bastards, what have they done to my castle?!

Hella continues to curse vehemently while you furrow your brow in concern.

The situation is worse than you anticipated. That wisp was more fully formed than the previous one, with clearer intentions behind its actions. It was not merely reacting on instinct, but consciously reinforcing its defenses.

Besides...

**【Advance】**

- During its attack, it made no effort to spare Hella.

**end**

The wisps are becoming increasingly aggressive. An anxiety stirs within you, but for now, you must focus on finding a way out.

Inside the labyrinth, you've attempted numerous paths, only to be mysteriously guided back to your starting point each time.

The dense vegetation blocks out most light. Without even a whisper of wind around you, even the air feels heavy and thick. As your repeated attempts to find an exit fail, tension begins to build among the group.

After passing the same junction yet again, Hella furiously kicks the wall in front of her. The vines and stonework don't budge an inch, while she hops around clutching her foot in pain.

Hella: ****! Who the hell gets lost in their own backyard? Damn it!

Hella: Forget it! If I break something, we can fix it later. Ninety-Nine!

The great sword swings, striking the wall with a sharp clank. Sparks fly as the vines emit a piercing shriek, like the agonized cry of a wounded creature.

Hella: No way out? Then I'll just cut one out!

A wave of agitated heat descends from above. The wisp suddenly materializes over your heads with an explosive squeal.

Little Wisp: !!

Chief: Watch out! It's going to attack!

You pull Hella behind you protectively, but the ball of flame doesn't immediately attack. Instead, it shakes and expands vigorously, its core growing brighter and brighter, as if on the verge of explosion.

Then, it suddenly charges toward another figure that has appeared at the end of the path.

**【Advance】**

- Is that...

**end**

Hella: Horo?!

Horo has somehow appeared at the end of the maze. She stands silently in the middle of the corridor, still dressed as a Knight of Hearts, axe gripped in her right hand, resembling a cold, stern stone statue.

But the moment the wisp touches her, the look in her eyes changes dramatically.

**【Advance】**

- Wait! Something's wrong with her!

**end**

Purplish-red flames snake up her neck, rapidly corrupting her face as her once-resolute expression gives way to a grotesque contortion. She slowly raises her axe, fixing her gaze on your group.

Horo?: Intruders... you shouldn't be here...

Horo?: If you won't leave... then you can all die!
