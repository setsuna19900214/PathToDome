# Empty Promise

## III

The Castle of Lies

The possessed Horo swings her giant axe menacingly, aiming directly at your group.

**【Advance】**

- Watch out!

**end**

Chief: This isn't the real Horo! She's being controlled!

Horo's movements are more swift and fierce than ever before, the edge of her axe tearing through the air with formidable power. Her eyes show no hesitation, only the dark haze of the wisp's manipulation.

Horo?: You... shouldn't be here...

Horo?: All who disturb the dream must be punished!

Hella: Huff... huff...

The wisp finally scatters and seeps out of Horo's body. She collapses to the ground as the battle temporarily subsides.

Though it has lost its former grandeur, the little wisp still flickers stubbornly, cursing with its tiny voice. Its flame has dwindled to a mere speck that could be easily extinguished with just a wave.

Hella walks over to the fallen little wisp, crouches down, and gazes at the feeble flame.

Hella: Well? Still not ready to give up?

Little Wisp: *$#@#$&*$@*%!

Hella: Hmph, so stubborn. But I think I get it now. Your goal is not just to sabotage our plan, is it?

Hella: I met one of your friends before. It said you guys just wanted to play with me? Or maybe you wanted me to play with you a little longer?

The cursing stops. The wisp wavers uncertainly, like its secret has just been exposed.

Hella: Dummy. That's not how you ask for a favor.

Hella: I don't know why you had to make such a big fuss, even ruining my garden... But well, I'm feeling generous! I've seen a lot of crazier things. Your little prank doesn't scare me.

Hella: As the Queen of Hearts, granting a tiny wish for one of my subjects is child's play!

Hella reaches out and gently cups the tiny flame in her hands.

Hella: Give me back my crown, and I'll play with you! Let me show you what the goddess of mischief is capable of!

Little Wisp: ...

The little wisp's flame trembles slightly. Slowly, its pulsation eases, and after a moment, it speaks.

Little Wisp: No... You're wrong...

Little Wisp: I've done nothing wrong... You're the one who's wrong...!

Hella: Wh-what?!

The wisp's body trembles several times, then expands all of a sudden, transforming into a massive ball of bursting flames.

**【Advance】**

- Hella, come back!

**end**

Little Wisp: You said... you would keep your promise! You've forgotten everything, but you've promised us, all of us! But you didn't keep your word...!

Hella: What? No, I just wanted to make you all happy, I...

With one final roar, the flames burst open thunderously. The sparks quickly disperse into specks of light, rushing toward the castle.

**【Advance】**

- Hella, what did you promise them?

**end**

Hella stares blankly as the wisp dissipates. Her head droops lower, her face filled with complete bewilderment.

Hella: I... don't remember. Did I... forget something?

Hella cradles her forehead painfully. She seems to be trying to recall more, but the headache prevents her from doing so.

Chief: ...Let's go. We need to keep moving. Your castle is just ahead.

Hella says nothing, only nodding silently with her head down. As you notice her dejected expression, a wave of concern rises within you.

This role play is drawing to a close. Of the three wisps that prevented you from using the shackles, only one remains now. It's most likely waiting for you in the castle.

Once you defeat the last wisp, you'll be able to use the shackles on Hella and end this dream once and for all...

Chief: ......

Your intuition warns you that perhaps things aren't so simple.

Meanwhile, the palace gates ahead stand wide open, waiting for your arrival.
