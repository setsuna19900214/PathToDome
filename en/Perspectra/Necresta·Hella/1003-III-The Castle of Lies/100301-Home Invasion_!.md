# Home Invasion?!

## III

The Castle of Lies

The team presses on. With the little wisp gone, your only option is to follow Hella toward the royal castle.

At first, the castle is merely a spire peeking out of distant treetops. Soon, the view opens up, allowing you to behold the castle in its entirety.

Hella: Look! That's my castle! What do you think? Cool, right?

The spire pierces the clouds, while the red and white stone walls are covered with doodle-like patterns. These designs normally only belong in a children's picture book, yet in this dream, they somehow look surprisingly natural.

**【Choose One】**

- Impressive!

  Hella: Hehe, well, of course it is! Its designer's got great taste, after all!

- It's alright, I guess.

  Hella: Tch, someone has no taste.

- I could draw it better.

  Hella: Rude! Bet your version won't look half as good! Hmph!

**end**

But just as you're about to pass through the courtyard gates, a troop of knights suddenly appears from both sides. They form neat ranks, wielding long spears bearing red heart emblems, completely blocking your path.

Knight of Hearts: Who goes there? None shall trespass on the royal grounds!

In their gleaming, pristine armor, their movements are perfectly synchronized, their commands loud and clear. At the front of the formation stands a lone figure, a massive axe resting on her shoulder, scanning the area with a cold gaze.

Chief: Is that...

**【Advance】**

- Horo?

**end**

Hella: That's right. That's the leader of my Order of Hearts, Commander Horo! She's my most loyal warrior. Once I reveal who I am, she'll definitely help us. Horo! Horo, over here!

Horo: Huh? You are...

Horo turns at the sound. She stares at Hella for several seconds, her brow slightly furrowed. You have an ominous feeling in your gut. Sure enough, the next second, she swings her massive axe, pointing it directly at you.

Horo: Guards, remove these impostors at once!

Hella: Huh? Horo?! What are you doing?!

Horo: Her Majesty has just granted me an audience. She's inside the castle right now. How could she possibly be consorting with a bunch of beggars? Remove them at once!

EMP immediately bristles at that, leaping to her feet in outrage.

EMP: Hey! How dare you look down on us! Whoa, wait, not with the axe, I'm sorry I'm sorry I'll see myself out...

Horo gives you no chance to explain yourselves. The knights quickly close in, threatening you with their sharp spears, forcing you out of the royal castle.

You're driven all the way out of the castle, finally tumbling into a shallow ditch beyond the walls. The ground is covered with slippery moss, and as you brace yourself with one hand, you nearly lose your footing.

Hella gets to her feet, glaring furiously at the towering castle walls.

Hella: Those ******* guards... Follow me!

Hella: The main gate's a no-go, so we'll sneak in elsewhere. There must be a way...

Hella: They're keeping me out of my own castle?! How dare they?!
