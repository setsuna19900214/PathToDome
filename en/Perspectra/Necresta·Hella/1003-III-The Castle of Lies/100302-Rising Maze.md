# Rising Maze

## III

The Castle of Lies

## Hella's secret infiltration into the garden fails. Surrounded by wisps.

1. Chief sneaks into the garden following Hella.
2. Unfortunately, they are discovered by the wisps.
3. Everyone gets trapped in a maze by the wisps.

Under Hella's guidance, you follow a hidden path to bypass the guards. With weeds reaching above your ankles, the trail is barely visible, forcing you to fumble cautiously along the outline of the castle.

Finally, a low wall appears before you. After climbing over it, you land in the garden at the rear of the royal palace.

Stone-paved paths branch out in all directions. The garden is flanked by intricately shaped shrubs and meticulously trimmed flowerbeds. Vibrant purple roses bloom in abundance, dazzling like gems, yet utterly devoid of life.

EMP: Phew! We made it here in one piece. That tiny girl with the axe probably can't catch up to us now, right?

You're just about to relax when you realize something feels off.

Chief: ...Wait, I sense something else nearby.

The energy flowing through the air suddenly freezes. The next moment, a ball of flame erupts without warning from deep within the flowerbed!

Another wisp. This time, it is larger and burns more fiercely, with a restless vortex twisting at its core.

Little Wisp: Stay away... No one approaches the castle...

Little Wisp: Leave! Go away!

The little wisp rises suddenly, its flame pulsating rapidly. Immediately, the ground begins to shake. The stone tiles beneath your feet crack. Vines shoot up frantically from below, intertwining and entangling as they grow instantaneously from the earth all around you!

You can only watch helplessly as the plants transform into towering green stone walls, row after row, sealing shut like living creatures to imprison you within.
