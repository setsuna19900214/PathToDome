# Into Fairyland

## I

Hi There, Gray Rabbit

After the BR-005 incident

The command room of the Minos Bureau of Crisis Control

Beep beep... Beep beep...

You and Nightingale stare at the electronic screen with solemn expressions. The lines fluctuate up and down, and red alerts pop up incessantly, every anomaly seeming deeply disturbing.

**【Advance】**

- These are...

**end**

Nightingale: ...Sinner Hella's biometrics.

Above you, another screen displays a view of the monitoring room. Hella lies at the center, surrounded by countless instruments, her eyes firmly shut, completely unconscious.

Chief: ...

June 25, N.F. 115: BR-005 erupted in the farlands. Sinner Hella, who had left the MBCC for a training expedition in WhiteSands, was caught in the incident and suffered deep corruption from the Black Ring's Mania.

August 4, N.F. 115: The fifth Black Ring was detected. Two days later, Sinner Hella arrived at the outskirts of DisCity, following the moving Black Ring. Border tensions escalated.

August 23, N.F. 115: BR-005 was successfully contained. Sinner Hella officially returned.

Nightingale: As the MBCC's first Mania Intensify case, Hella underwent immediate physical examination upon her return.

Nightingale: The Mania concentration in her body was far higher than previous readings, but with the shackles' intervention, the energy fluctuations remained mostly under control. However...

Nightingale: Twelve hours ago, she suddenly fell unconscious and couldn't be awakened. Even your shackles can't properly assess her condition.

Nightingale slows down as her words grow heavy with concern.

Nightingale: Reports did mention she had hypersomnia-like symptoms in the past, but we never imagined the problem would become this severe.

Nightingale: Right now, the Mania concentration in her body is steadily rising. If we don't stop it soon, I'm afraid...

Chief: ...

Your fingertips rest against the cold screen. On the other side, Hella lies in the monitoring room, her face deathly pale, eyelids fluttering as though trapped in an inescapable nightmare.

After returning to the MBCC, Hella seemed just like her old self, carefree and chattering away all day. But during field missions, she became noticeably more reliable, mature, and level-headed. It should have been a positive change, so...

**【Advance】**

- What exactly went wrong?

**end**

What else have you failed to notice? What signs did you dismiss? What can you still do to help?

You close your eyes and take a deep breath. Footsteps approach from behind, and when you turn around, a familiar figure is standing there.

9th Agency Agent: It's been a while, Chief ${PlayerName}.

**【Advance】**

- There's no need for pleasantries.

**end**

Chief: Is everything ready? Then lead the way.

You follow the man until he stops next to a piece of equipment.

The metal device has a cold, sleek design. Placed among the other equipment beside the monitoring pod, it sticks out like a sore thumb.

**【Advance】**

- This is...?

**end**

9th Agency Agent: Latest technology from Paradeisos: Perspectra. It evolved from the original Mirage system, and this iteration can access a Sinner's mental realm less intrusively while delivering higher accuracy.

9th Agency Agent: During the examination, you can still dive into the Sinner's dreams. Through this device, her mental state will be visualized as colors and images, allowing continuous monitoring even when you're outside her dreams.

9th Agency Agent: All preparations are complete. We can begin whenever you're ready.

You walk to Hella's side and take her hand. It feels soft and cold, just like when she grabbed yours in the Black Ring. The shackles stir slightly, but you sense no solid response, like reaching into a dead swamp.

Beside you, the man speaks again.

9th Agency Agent: A second awakening in a Sinner is extremely rare, so please exercise utmost caution during the observation. Everything ahead is uncharted territory. You may encounter unprecedented dangers in the dreams.

9th Agency Agent: Even with these risks, do you still wish to use it?

**【Advance】**

- Of course.

**end**

Chief: I must go.

Hella is Syndicate's most free-spirited and energetic little mouse. After all the effort to rescue her, you won't let any harm come near her again.

Chief: Whether it's the Black Ring or residual Mania affecting her... I'll find out. Then I'll fix it.

9th Agency Agent: Very well. Then, I wish you safe passage.

Your consciousness flows through darkness, dispersing then reforming.

Soon, specks of light flicker before you, and faint sounds reach your ears. If you open your eyes now, you will come face to face with the dream that's now holding Hella captive.

Chief: ...

Whatever awaits you, you are ready.

**【Advance】**

- Open your eyes

**end**

Blue sky and white clouds. Gentle breeze and green grass.

You lie on your back over soft grass, feeling the sunlight shower down with gentle warmth. No gunfire. No spirits. Everything is just as beautiful and dreamlike as in a fairy tale.

**【Advance】**

- ???

**end**

Chief: What the heck is this place?!
