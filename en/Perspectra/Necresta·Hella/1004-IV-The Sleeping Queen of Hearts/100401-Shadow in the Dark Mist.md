## IV

The Sleeping Queen of Hearts

The journey after passing through the maze is surprisingly smooth. High walls no longer block your path, and the knights have ceased to appear. The castle gates have opened silently, as if welcoming the return of their sovereign.

The closer you get to the castle, the more agitated Hella becomes. Her pace quickens with each step, practically rushing everyone forward in her haste.

But the unease in your heart only grows stronger.

Little Wisp: But... sorry... not now... I must... stop you... You can't leave yet...!

At first, playing along with this charade was merely a tactic to buy time. But as the wisps vanished, doubts have continued to pile up in your mind.

These soul fragments attack intruders in the name of *protection*, preventing Hella from leaving... But what exactly are they protecting Hella from?
