# Bottom of the Dream

## IV

The Sleeping Queen of Hearts

## Find the crying Hella

Chief follows the trail of light and finds the crying Hella.

You continue to fall endlessly.

Sounds warp and images fracture. Darkness surrounds you, occasionally punctuated by shards of color. Up and down, left and right have long since lost meaning as you sink deeper into this constantly shifting labyrinth of mirrors.

Just as you teeter on the edge of unconsciousness... a faint light suddenly pierces through the chaos.

A tiny wisp hovers before you, swaying gently.

The surrounding darkness begins to shift as countless points of light emerge. Innumerable wisps appear, encircling you from all directions while emitting soft chimes.

You try to speak, but your throat feels constricted by something invisible, allowing only faint, breathy sounds to escape.

**【Advance】**

- Reach out

**end**

Boom. The moment you touch the flame, a wisp explodes as a vision flashes before your eyes.

Girl's Voice: I've heard that in those big cities without sandstorms, there are so many storybooks they can fill an entire house!

Girl's Voice: Maybe... someday I'll learn to read like you, so I can enjoy those stories on my own!

Girl's Voice: Tell me more about your adventures, Hella!

The borrowed memory fades away in an instant. The wisp dissipates instantly, transforming into a pitch-black shadow.

The shadow stands at your side, silently pointing toward the deeper reaches of the dream.

**【Advance】**

- Follow the guidance

**end**

You struggle to your feet as the ground beneath you begins to stabilize.

You follow the shadow's guidance, encountering a new wisp with each step. The flames flicker and sway as countless memories flash before your eyes.

Child's Voice: When I grow up, I'll be stronger and tougher! I'll become a real man who can protect everyone!

Young Man's Voice: As the second greatest inventor in Furor, one day my inventions will reach every corner in WhiteSands, improving the lives of everyone!

Woman's Voice: I... I just want to see my child again. If there's any chance, I wish he could return to my side...

Indistinct shadows hover by your side. As the wisps gradually go out, the shadows' forms become increasingly clear and complete.

Voices of Souls: These were the promises we once made. She swore she would help us make them come true, but most of them did not...

Voices of Souls: But... we don't blame her...

Voices of Souls: The seeds have been planted, and our wishes fulfilled... Our homeland is long gone, so all of WhiteSands will become our new home. We've left behind the power to revive it...

Voices of Souls: But Hella... she still can't let go of us... that's why she can't break free...

In the distance, a mirror-like crack flickers faintly, resembling a lighthouse guiding the way.

**【Advance】**

- Is Hella there?

**end**

You can't make a sound, but the shadows seem to understand your thoughts. The ground reshapes into a path, while the wisps flicker to guide your way.

Voices of Souls: She's such a kindhearted kid. She deserves to be happy...

Voices of Souls: We know. We remember... you are the person she cherishes most...

Voices of Souls: Give her a gentle pat on the head for us... She needs to move on. It's time... to say goodbye.

You take a deep breath and step forward to follow the path.

The deepest part of the dream lies just ahead, and Hella is there, waiting for you.
