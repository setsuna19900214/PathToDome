# Beneath the Starlight

## IV

The Sleeping Queen of Hearts

Following the light formed by the wisps, you venture into the heart of the dream. Your hand touches a smooth barrier that feels like a mirror's surface, yet somehow different.

This mirror is covered with cracks. Instead of reflecting your image, it shows the faint silhouette of a smaller, thinner figure.

**【Advance】**

- Hella...?

**end**

Hella is curled up inside the mirror.

Tiny specks of light float around her as Hella tries to gather them back together, but whenever she loosens her grip, the small flames she's assembled immediately scatter again.

Hella: One more try... one more try...

She refuses to give up, but each time, the flames inevitably fall apart, dissolving into formless ashes.

If you had been stronger back then, could you have saved us...?

Hella: It's because I wasn't strong enough back then that I couldn't save them...

Voices echo from all directions, hastening Hella's movements.

If you had been stronger, perhaps everything would have been different...

Hella: I'm strong enough now! There's gotta be something I can still do...!

Poof. Another failure. Another spark shatters into nothing.

Hella: What... what can I still do for you guys...?

The mirror's surface is ice-cold as another crack rips it open. Points of light flicker, and you can't tell whether they're reflections from the mirror or tears sliding down Hella's cheeks.

The Hella who returned to the MBCC has grown mature and strong, but deep in her dreams, she still can't reconcile with the regrets in her heart. Her guilt and self-blame are like those wisps that can never be revived, those rifts that can never be closed.

Trapped in the depths of her dreams and tortured by her own conscience, she is her own prisoner.

**【Advance】**

- Call out to Hella

**end**

You gently tap on the mirror's surface. The figure inside stiffens for a moment. She senses your presence, but doesn't turn around.

Chief: Hella, I've come to bring you home.

Hella: ...No, I can't leave yet. I still have things I need to do!

Hella: Everyone's still waiting for me. I'm strong enough now. If I try again, I know I can save them all! I...

Hella: I...

Hella: If I had been strong enough back then... If I were as tough as you, could I have saved them?

**【Choose One】**

- ...

- I don't know either.

**end**

No one knows the answer, and you can't give Hella one either.

Hella: ...I know this is nothing but a dream.

Hella: We defeated the Black Ring together and returned to the MBCC. They're already dead. I can only fulfill their wishes and keep my promises in my dreams... This is all I can do.

Hella: I just... can't accept it.

Hella: ...What if you were me? What would you do?

You sit down against the mirror's surface. Though you can't see behind you, you hear some soft rustling, then feel a gentle warmth. Someone has come to lean against your back.

Chief: ...After you were gone, a lot happened at the MBCC.

Chief: Disputes between cities, clan conflicts... The hatred involved spanned across generations and became too complex. I could only watch as people got hurt and died right before my eyes... and there was nothing I could do.

Chief: Just like you, I couldn't help them either.

Hella: ...So what am I supposed to do? If even I give up, no one will remember them anymore...

You tilt your head back slightly, feeling the hard glass surface against the back of your skull.

Chief: It's okay if you can't move on, and it's okay to hide under the covers and cry when you're sad. You're already doing the best you can.

Hella: ...Am I?

Chief: Of course. No one will blame you for taking a break.

Chief: Whenever I have to say goodbye to someone, I can't help but think: *If I had been a little stronger, could I have done better?*

Chief: But I can't go back to the past, nor could I have foreseen all the other possibilities.

Chief: All we can do is remember them... Remember every warrior who fought for the future, and every companion who fell along the path forward.

Chief: Death isn't the final farewell. The living will carry on their will, and they will become the beacon guiding us all forward.

Chief: At the end of this long journey... I believe they'll be waiting for us there, and we'll meet again.

Chief: But until then...

You turn around to face Hella, who stands behind you.

**【Advance】**

- Reach out to Hella

**end**

Chief: Keep moving forward, and look to those who are still by your side, those who are still alive, who can still be protected and saved.

Chief: Hella, we're all waiting for you to come back.

Hella stares at your outstretched hand, a flicker of hesitation crossing her eyes, but she doesn't pull away. Her fingertips reach forward, pressing against yours through the mirror.

Suddenly, a faint reverberation comes from the mirror's surface as cracks begin spreading from Hella's palm, light shining through them. In an instant, the mirror shatters, and you can finally take Hella's hand.

**【Advance】**

- Let's go home.

**end**

Hella: ...Yes! Let's go home!

The dream is falling apart, with layers of darkness peeling away to reveal the dawn of the outside world. The platform beneath your feet splinters, and you plunge down with Hella, ready to return to reality.

But at that very moment, something flashes in the darkness. A *shooting star*, streaking across the pitch darkness, plummeting toward you both.

Hella: That's...!

Flecks of starlight descend from above, transforming into a galaxy before your eyes. The little wisps!

Flames leave bright, long trails behind them as they land next to Hella, vanishing into gentle whispers of farewell.

Thank you... for playing with us and joining our adventures. Thank you for remembering us...

We must go now... Your story has to continue, and we'll be waiting for you at the journey's end...

Goodbye, Hella... Farewell...

The souls perished in the Black Ring transform into streams of light, guiding you both forward while bidding you farewell.

Hella: ...Farewell!

Hella: We'll definitely meet again!

Starlight envelops you and Hella, pulling you both into its radiance.

Hella: Mmm...

Hella slowly opens her eyes. The light is somewhat blinding, and before her vision can fully adjust, she hears a commotion.

Hecate: Hella!

EMP: Thank god!!! You're finally awake!!!

EMP rushes forward excitedly, but Ninety-Nine has her by the collar. Hecate stands by the window, her eyes filled with concern.

Ninety-Nine: She hasn't fully recovered yet. She needs rest.

Hella sits up. Behind everyone else, a figure in a gray coat leans against the wall, smiling and waving at her.

Chief: Well, look who's finally back. Everyone's been worried about you.

EMP: You bet! Hey, and why did I look like a clown in your dream?! I mean, yeah, I am a fan of Bobo's Bizarre Adventure... but you didn't have to make me look like an idiot, did you?!

Hecate: And I don't sleep all the time either...

The Sinners start bickering again. Their conversation jumps from Hella's absurd dreams to her adventures in WhiteSands. A smile finally emerges on Hella's face as she suddenly springs up from her bed.

Hella: Alright, I will tell you more about the invincible Hella's great adventures in WhiteSands...

Hella suddenly lunges at the crowd around her bed, and the playful quarrel erupts once again.

Sunlight streams into the room, warming Hella's Mania-corrupted body and bathing everyone's smiles in its glow.

MBCC-SS-098-1109115

Case Archive: Scourge Sinner Hella

Following the conclusion of the BR-005 events, Sinner Hella has returned to the Bureau.

Currently, her Mania fluctuations have come under control, but symptoms of hypersomnia persist.

Hella: Oh come on, I just wanna take a nap! I won't end up in a coma this time, I swear!

Hella frantically pounds on the desk in front of you, determined to snatch what she considers to be a tattletale report about her condition.

Hella: Besides, I won't just be sleeping! This time I've actually got important stuff to do!

Hella: I wasn't the only one who got lost in that Black Ring. Horo and some other guys from Legion still haven't made it back.

Hella: I don't know where exactly Horo and the others are, but...

While her consciousness was still connected to the Black Ring, Hella could occasionally access a mysterious higher realm.

In that place, time and space twisted together, losing all meaning. In her trance-like state, she even sensed a few familiar presences and people...

Hella: After leaving the Black Ring, this connection has weakened, but I still want to try.

Hella: Horo and the others must still be alive! Just like you found me... I'll definitely find them and bring them all back.

The conversation is interrupted by commotion outside. Hella is called away by the other Sinners. Today is their regular game day. You listen to the laughter drifting in from outside the room and can't help but smile.

Hella: Come on, dummy! Play a round with us! And don't say I didn't warn you, I'm gonna crush you today!

**【Advance】**

- Put down the documents

**end**

Chief: Oh, don't be so sure.

You leave the room together.

*Old wounds don't heal easily.*

*Yet there are still precious things worth protecting and warmth worth pursuing.*

*And so you press forward and continue fighting for what matters. Even when wounded, you rise again.*

*Endlessly. Tirelessly.*

*This path ahead is long, its end not in sight.*

*But thankfully... you don't have to walk this path alone.*

In a place known to no one, someone smiles. The wisps dance in the void, vanishing into the radiance of that peaceful expression.

*My child, the chosen one, the blessed one...*

*From this day on... may you be forever happy and safe.*
