## Save Hella and approach the throne

1. Chief and Hella enter the castle.
2. The wisps trap Hella, holding her captive.
3. Chief removes the trap and rescues Hella.
4. Hella approaches the throne and discovers the mysterious figure's true identity.

The hall is vast and empty, with a red carpet stretching straight to the steps at the far end. Black canopy curtains hang down from above, and the throne sits where they meet the steps. Flickering candles cast unsteady light, leaving the figure seated on the throne shrouded in shadow.

You lag behind Hella, barely catching glimpses of her figure as she races through the empty hallways toward the throne.

Hella: You ******* bastard, give me back my crown!

You jerk your head up. There is indeed someone sitting on the throne, or rather, a blurry *shadow*.

It remains motionless, its entire body shrouded in black mist, revealing only a vague human silhouette. The figure keeps its head bowed, ignoring Hella's shouts, seeming to be waiting for her approach instead.

Above its head sits a crown forged with black gold, inlaid with gems that seem to be ablaze, casting a blinding, sinister glow.

Chief: Is that...

The figure seated on the throne slowly raises its head, meeting your gaze.

It is none other than *Hella*.

This is neither the carefree little mouse of Syndicate, nor the boisterous, crown-obsessed Queen of Hearts. Her eyes are hollow, and her body completely still, like a lifeless statue.

**【Advance】**

- ?! Hella, don't go near it!

**end**

Hella: ...Huh?

But it's already too late. Hella's hand is already reaching for the crown on the throne.

The throne shakes violently as dense black mist erupts from the crown like a ravenous beast, instantly engulfing Hella's entire body.

Chief: Hella?!

The dark shadow swirls like a vortex, tightening around Hella's body. She struggles to turn her head toward you before being completely swallowed by the shadow, vanishing from sight.

Boom!

A powerful shudder rises up from the ground beneath. The entire throne room begins to collapse, the floor tearing apart like a sheet of paper, stone debris scattering in all directions. The ceiling caves in, and the walls twist into impossible shapes.

Chief: No!!!

The ground beneath your feet suddenly gives way, and your body plummets. With nothing to grab onto, you're helplessly dragged into the void by the collapsing dream.

The castle disintegrates around you. The throne crumbles, and the towers shatter. The very foundation of this dream realm is forcefully torn apart as flames and black mist intertwine in the sky.

Soon, all light is devoured, leaving only you in endless freefall, surrounded by the fading echoes in your ears.

Everything falls to silence.

Boundless, weightless, directionless. The dream has been torn open into an even deeper rift, and you're being cast into the abyss at its very core.
