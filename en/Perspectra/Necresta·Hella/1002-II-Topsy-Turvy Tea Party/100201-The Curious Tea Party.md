The corner of your mouth twitches. In your memory, it wasn't a rabbit, but some purple flames floating in the air.

You've read *Adventures in Fairyland* before and still remember the gist of the story.

A young girl, the protagonist of the story, falls down a rabbit hole in her garden and enters a whimsical kingdom named Fairyland.

Wanting to return home, she follows the rabbit, attends the Mad Hatter's tea party, meets the Knights of Hearts, and eventually breaks into the castle of the villain, the Queen of Hearts.

The Queen of Hearts wants to detain the girl, but she only wishes to go home.

In the end, she is startled awake to discover it has all been merely a dream, and she has simply been napping in the garden for a short while.

It closely resembles the plot of Hella's dream. The difference is that this time, Hella plays the role of the Queen of Hearts who has lost her crown and all her power.

So... who's the villain in this story then? The ones who stole her throne? Or those wisps preventing her from leaving?

Hella: Hey! I'm talking to you! Why are you zoning out again?

Hella waves her hand in front of you, yanking you out of your thoughts.

The shackles won't work here, but you also can't seem to escape from this game to *reclaim her throne*. Might as well go along with it, find the true culprit behind this dream, get rid of them, and bring Hella back.
