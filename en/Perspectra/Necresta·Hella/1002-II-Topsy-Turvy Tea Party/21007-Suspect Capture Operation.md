# Suspect Capture Operation

## II

Topsy-Turvy Tea Party

Farewell in Splendor

> Description after completion:
> O spirit brimming with sorrow, please grieve no more.

O spirit brimming with sorrow, please grieve no more. The one in your dreams will return home in the galaxy's embrace.

Ninety-Nine & Hecate: ...

...Zzz...

The tea party table has been overturned in the chaos, and all of you are now covered to varying degrees in cream and dirt. EMP scratches her head, trying to mask her embarrassment with awkward laughter.

EMP: Uh... So that's what happened! It's all just, um, a misunderstanding! I see!

EMP: Of course, I'll always be the first one to stand up for my friends in a fight! ...Uh, but right now is really bad timing. Some jerk stole my precious treasure, and I just can't spare the time for anything else.

**【Advance】**

- What did you lose?

**end**

Hella: Hey wait, don't ask that...

Too late. Your question has flipped some kind of switch in EMP.

EMP: Hmph... Since you've asked so sincerely, I shall graciously divulge the whole story!

EMP: Back in the day, that very treasure was the reason the three of us gathered here. To savor that legendary masterpiece that will go down gloriously in history...

EMP: That's right! What was stolen is none other than the holy relic of our tea party, the complete manga collection of Bobo's Bizarre Adventure!

**【Advance】**

- ...

**end**

Chief: That's it?

Hella: (Whispers) To be honest, she's the only one who actually likes reading that stuff. Hecate just wants a comfortable place to nap, and as for Ninety-Nine...

You look to the side. The white-haired woman's gaze is still fixed on the desserts that have fallen to the ground.

Hella: They've got the best desserts in all of Fairyland here. Would be a waste not to come.

**【Advance】**

- ...What kind of misconceptions do you have about these three?!

**end**

Seriously, what a ridiculous dream. Swallowing your snide remark, you continue questioning EMP.

Chief: What about the culprit? Do you have any leads so far?

EMP: Ah, I saw one of them! That thing ran faster than me. A tiny ball of purple substance with sparks flickering around its edges...

**【Advance】**

- The wisps?

**end**

Hella: Aha! I knew those bastards were trying to ambush us!

Hella and EMP quickly form an alliance, furiously cursing the culprits who caused their misfortune. Meanwhile, you sink into contemplation.

If you could catch one of those little wisps... you might be able to grill it for more information about this dream.

**【Advance】**

- You can count on me.

**end**

Chief: Perfect. I have some questions for them too.

???: Once upon a time, the Fairyland Kingdom was a beautiful and peaceful place. Under the rule of the Queen of Hearts, people lived happily, until...

???: A bunch of nasty traitors betrayed her! They stole her crown and seized her castle, and even drove her to the edge of Fairyland, imprisoning her in the forest...!

**【Advance】**

- And then they hung you on a tree?

**end**

Hanging upside down from the tree, Hella scowls.

Hella: Ahem, that's not important! Anyway, as you can see! I am Hella, ruler of Fairyland, master of the Red Heart Castle, the kind and wonderful Queen of Hearts!

**【Advance】**

- But you're dangling from a tree right now.

**end**

Hella: Join me now! Just help me fight my way back to the castle and reclaim my throne. After we succeed, we can split the rewards 70-30. Wait, no, I mean I'll reward you with a high position, make you a minister! Or just pay you if that works!

**【Advance】**

- Still dangling from a tree.

**end**

Hella: Hey, are you even listening?! This is a sweet deal that could make you rich!

**【Advance】**

- From. A. Tree.

**end**

Hella: ...Ahhh!!! Whatever!

The girl struggles violently, but the vines binding her are far too sturdy. Despite her desperate efforts, all she manages to do is shake the canopy, sending leaves fluttering to the ground.

You've figured it out by now. Perhaps due to being in a dream, Hella's self-perception has taken a peculiar turn. Here, she doesn't recognize you, but instead believes she's the Queen of Hearts who's recruiting allies and preparing for revenge.

Adventures in Fairyland. You've seen this book before in the Bureau's Recreation Area. It's about a young girl who accidentally stumbles into the Fairyland Kingdom, undergoes countless adventures, confronts the arrogant and haughty Queen of Hearts, and finally manages to escape the dream realm.

Back then, Hella was laboriously looking up the words in the picture book... Who would have thought she'd end up identifying with the villain of the story?

Unfortunately, her revenge plan meets its demise at the very first step. Facing the ridiculous scene, you can't stifle your laughter in time.

Chief: ...Pfft.

Hella: What?! How dare you laugh at me?!

Hella: What are you staring at? Hurry up and get me down, you dummy!!!

...Well? Should you help her out?
