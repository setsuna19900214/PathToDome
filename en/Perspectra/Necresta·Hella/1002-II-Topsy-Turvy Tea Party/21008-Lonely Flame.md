But the link is unstable, as if hindered by another force, making it intermittent and difficult to maintain. This obstruction doesn't feel like Black Ring contamination, yet it's strangely familiar, almost like...

Hella: Oh right, I haven't asked you yet. This forest is so remote. How did you end up here? Were you also set up by someone?

Hella: Looking for someone? In a forest in the middle of nowhere? Who the hell would live in this godforsaken place?

Chief: Right? Beats me. I also wonder how she ended up here.

Chief: But it's someone important from my family. Though she's brave and kind, she always thinks she's too weak, so she set off on a long trip to prove herself.

Chief: But truthfully... she's already impressive enough. She doesn't tolerate pain very well though, so I'd hate for her to get hurt again.

You bend down and gently brush the last bits of dust from Hella's dress, then look directly into her eyes.

Chief: She wandered off too far, and just when she finally made it back home recently, she accidentally got lost again.

- Hella, I came here for you.

**end**

You notice Hella's pupils trembling, as if something suppressed within her is beginning to awaken.

Hella: ...Me? You're... looking for me?

You hear a fresh quiver in Hella's voice. The barrier restraining the shackles seems to loosen with your words, as the consciousness of Hella begins to stir.

**【Advance】**

- Yes, I came to get you out of here.

**end**

Chief: *Fairyland* isn't a real place, and neither is this *Kingdom* you keep mentioning.

Chief: You come from Syndicate. You're a Sinner at the MBCC, and my most reliable companion, the person I trust more than anyone.

Hella: Wait, MBCC...? W-what are you talking about...

The barrier in her consciousness shakes violently. You can almost reach Hella on the other side of the shackles.

Chief: This is all just a dream, Hella. You've been dreaming for so long that you can't wake up.

Chief: Wake up, Hella. Everyone misses you. Ninety-Nine, Hecate, everyone at the MBCC... and me.

The barrier between you and Hella teeters on the edge of collapse. Just a little more, just a little more, and your shackles will reconnect with Hella...

**【Advance】**

- Come home with me.

**end**

Hella: ...Home?

Hella: ...I'm in a dream? No, that can't be true. I still have things I need to finish. I can't possibly be dreaming...

Hella: That's right... I am the Queen of Fairyland. I must reclaim my crown... Fairyland is in danger, and I have to protect everyone!

Your shackles are forcefully deflected by some unknown force. In the next moment, the whole dream realm begins shaking violently.

The surroundings start to warp and crumble, splitting open as if on the verge of collapse. Your movements are restrained by an invisible force. The air compresses around you as the entire world begins to reject your presence.

A tiny spark ignites beside Hella. Next, flames erupt.

**【Advance】**

You can't scare the wisp too much, or you might get kicked out of the dream again... You bend down close to the cage and lower your voice, keeping some distance from Hella.

Chief: Who exactly are you? Why are you so determined to prevent Hella from leaving this place?

Little Wisp: ...

- ...

**end**

The little wisp continues to burn quietly. Sparks dance and gather into a tiny ball within the cage. Its words are incoherent, and it doesn't even seem fully conscious, but you remember this being and its companions.

You remember its kind. Once gathering together during the BR-005 incident, they are spirits from the farlands that accompany Hella.

When your consciousness was fading, you heard their blessings for Hella. Now, most of these spirits have departed with the Black Ring, but either due to strong attachment or because they were too young, some fragments remained, becoming this tiny wisp before you.
