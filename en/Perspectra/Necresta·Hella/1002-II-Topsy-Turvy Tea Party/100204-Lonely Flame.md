# Lonely Flame

## II

Topsy-Turvy Tea Party

Little Wisp: ?!

The trap drops, and the wisp trembles violently inside. It struggles frantically in all directions, but cannot escape from the cage that confines it.

Hella: Where are the manga? Give them back now!

Little Wisp: !!!

Hella rolls up her sleeves and brandishes her fists. The little wisp begins trembling again, whimpering helplessly.

**【Advance】**

- Wait a second.

**end**

Chief: Let me handle this. I have some questions for it.

These wisps clearly have a better understanding of the nature of this dream than you do. Now that you've finally caught a stray one, you need to seize this opportunity to get some answers.

You can't scare the wisp too much, or you might get kicked out of the dream again... You bend down close to the cage and lower your voice, keeping some distance from Hella.

Chief: Who exactly are you? Why are you so determined to prevent Hella from leaving this place?

Little Wisp: ...

Seemingly sensing no hostility from you, the flickering flame gradually steadies itself. After a moment, you hear a tiny whisper.

Little Wisp: Must protect Hella... protect this place...

Little Wisp: But... not long ago, everyone left. Only a few of us remain...

Little Wisp: Being alone is scary... I... don't want to be left behind... So lonely...

**【Advance】**

- ...

**end**

The little wisp continues to burn quietly. Sparks dance and gather into a tiny ball within the cage. Its words are incoherent, and it doesn't even seem fully conscious, but you remember this being and its companions.

You remember its kind. Once gathering together during the BR-005 incident, they are spirits from the farlands that accompany Hella.

When your consciousness was fading, you heard their blessings for Hella. Now, most of these spirits have departed with the Black Ring, but either due to strong attachment or because they were too young, some fragments remained, becoming this tiny wisp before you.

It burns in solitude, yearning for company.

**【Advance】**

- But if this continues, she won't last long.

**end**

Chief: Hella... her body is gradually being consumed by Mania. The longer she stays in this dream, the more dangerous it is for her. I don't know how much longer she can go on like this.

Chief: I came here to bring her back.

Little Wisp: ...

The flame shakes vigorously, but unlike last time, you aren't immediately banished from the dream. You seize the chance and keep pressing it.

Chief: You care about her too, don't you? You don't want anything to harm her either, right?

Little Wisp: I... like Hella... but... don't want her to leave...

Little Wisp: Must... protect her... Want to... stay by her side... Want to hear her tell stories again...!

Bang! The little wisp breaks open its cage forcefully. Before you can react, it rushes out and flies toward Hella.

Instinctively, you move to stop it, only to find that the wisp simply stops in front of Hella.

Little Wisp: Must... protect Hella... Don't go... Hella...

The voice grows weaker, quivering with sobs that make the words nearly unintelligible.

Hella: ...

Hella moves past you and gently crouches down.

Hella: I get it now. You just don't want to be alone, right?

Little Wisp: Hella...?

Hella: If you're lonely, just say so! You can't blame me for getting annoyed when you've been stealing stuff and snatching my treasure! No wonder nobody wants to play with you.

Hella: That's not how you make friends, got it?

The tiny wisp sways slightly, shrinking dejectedly as it emits soft, whimpering sobs.

Hella: But given my big heart, and since everyone in Fairyland deserves a chance to turn over a new leaf...

The little Queen of Hearts extends her hand, offering an invitation to the flame.

Hella: Don't you want to play with everyone? Then show yourself and apologize sincerely. Ask for their forgiveness.

Little Wisp: You're... not mad at me...?

Hella: Of course I am, you dummy! I'm furious at you!

Hella: That's why you need to apologize properly! You have to show us you're genuinely sorry!

Little Wisp: ...

The little wisp falls silent. Its flame flickers and, after a moment, it extends a tendril warily, seemingly wanting to reach forward and grasp Hella's hand.

Little Wisp: Hella... I'm sorry...

Little Wisp: But... not now... I must... stop you... You can't leave yet...!

Hella: What? Wait!

Boom! The instant they touch, the flame explodes. You shield Hella behind you, only to discover the wisp has transformed into tiny specks of light, escaping toward the castle.

**【Advance】**

- Hella? Are you hurt?

**end**

Hella is still in a daze, her eyes vacant as she stares blankly at the spot where the flame disappeared.

Chief: Hella?

Hella: Hmm?

As if suddenly coming to her senses, Hella snaps out of her detached state.

Hella: I'm fine. We let it get away though. Should've asked it if there were other ambushes waiting for us... Jeez, what a hassle.

Hella: But, um, it seemed really sad...

Hella's voice dwindles. She falls silent for a moment, then picks up the manga that had fallen to the ground and turns to leave with you.

EMP: Ah! My manga! Oh, thank god, they've finally returned to me!

Hecate: Ugh, so noisy...

As the stolen manga are returned to their overjoyed owner, Hecate, who has been sleeping all this time, is awakened by the commotion as well.

The tea party trio has joined you. Your squad is finally strong enough to venture to the castle and reclaim the throne.

Nevertheless, the little wisp's final message still lingers in your ears, leaving you unsettled.

Little Wisp: Must... protect her... Want to... stay by her side... Want to hear her tell stories again...

Little Wisp: But... sorry... not now... I must... stop you... You can't leave yet...!

Is this dream really trying to protect Hella...?
