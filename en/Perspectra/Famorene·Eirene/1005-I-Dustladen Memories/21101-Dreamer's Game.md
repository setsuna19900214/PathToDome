Pearl of WhiteSands

Tick. Tick. Tick...

In the heart of the Bureau's central care unit stands a silver hospital bed, its sheets hollowed where a frail figure lies half-swallowed by the bedding, breathing so faintly it barely stirs the air.

Around the bed, instruments crowd in by the dozen, their wires sprawling like cobwebs, streaming data onto monitors with no regard for privacy. Now and then, a digit flickers on a screen, the only proof that the person in that bed is still alive.

The stillness holds for a long, long while—so long that when two of the screens suddenly stir to life, it almost slips past unnoticed.

Two staff members hurry over to investigate. The readouts plainly show that the anomaly traces back to the Perspectra device of the 9th Agency.

Staff Member: Huh. That's weird.

Staff Member: The Perspectra device is supposed to be for monitoring Scourge Sinners. The only reason we've got it on Chief is that a bunch of S-Class Sinners are out cold while still connected to the shackles...

Staff Member: Don't tell me Chief's consciousness is still linked to one of them...?

Staff Member: The readings are off the charts this time. A Scourge consciousness has already started bleeding through. That's the only reason the device caught it.

The staff member turns toward the figure on the bed. The room lies in gloom, its only light the reflected colors of the machines.

The Chief's eyelids twitch faintly, as though slipping into a dream.

<ins>Staff Member: Looks like Chief's deep link is still up. Should we wake him? Is he... gonna be okay? Maybe we should report this...</ins><br><ins>Staff Member: Looks like Chief's deep link is still up. Should we wake her? Is she... gonna be okay? Maybe we should report this...</ins>

Staff Member: Not yet. Chief's blood pressure and heart rate are both normal. The bleed-through hasn't done any damage. Let's just keep watching for now.

The dream monitor flickers first with blurry black-and-white static, then skips through a flurry of images: bloodshed, clashing figures, then a grid of black and white squares that's unmistakably the shape of a chessboard.

Woman's Voice: Go on. See if you can beat him.

Man's Voice: You're just like me.

Girl's Voice: Don't make a fool of yourself.

Without warning, the picture plunges into darkness. Out of it rises a choir's hymn, threaded through with the shattering of glass, until everything breaks off at a gunshot.

Staff Member: Quick, get this down. The Perspectra's information is spreading! And Chief's consciousness is catching it!
