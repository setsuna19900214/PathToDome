# Pearl of WhiteSands

## I

Dustladen Memories

Endgame of the Long Night

White dust sweeps across the vastness, and in the sunlight every grain shimmers, as though this boundless desert were made not of sand, but of diamonds.

And who could blame anyone for thinking so?

Vroom, vroom—

Out across the white desert, a sand ship vast as a leviathan carries its laden hold toward a city-state of stone that rises from the sands. Its powered sails surge against the heat haze, kicking up endless clouds of dust.

Beyond the walls, cranes swing up and down while sand ships crowd the entrance, queuing for their turn to enter. As far as the eye can see, the entire port lies thick with vessels, and from every deck sailors drop down, streaming along cobblestone paths crossed by handcarts into the alleys beyond.

Vendor: DisCity cigarettes, top-shelf stuff! Fifty, and they're yours.

Sailor: Move it! We're unloading here. Hold up our shipment and you can't cover the loss.

Whistles, the screech of brakes, and the hoarse shouting of vendors all braid together into a single chorus. Anyone who saw it would call it what it is: a thriving oasis metropolis, a pearl embedded in the golden heart of the desert.

Behind this bustling facade, a cluster of towering buildings stands sealed within ornate walls. Grapevines climb in from beyond, threading through small skylights to tease the wind chimes hanging beneath red-tiled eaves.

The roof is shaped into the form of a great sail, its flanks gilded and blazing gold under the sun. The archways, hewn from white sandstone, are fashioned into two crossed oars. Along the outer walls are engraved the names of every ship the Campbell family has ever built, together with their years of service and the routes they sailed. Below, servants haul buckets of water and scrub at the stone tiles.

Then the fountain plaza before the main hall: droplets fracture the sunlight into tiny rainbows, and children with hair in varying shades of gold shriek and chase one another, their dresses and sleeves grazing the fountain's rim. Everything is steeped in a happiness so saturated it borders on the unreal.

Servant: Young Master, please stop running. You'll dirty your clothes!

Servant: Oh, Miss, you mustn't go in there!

By virtue of their shipbuilding craft, the Campbell family commands a monopoly over eighty percent of the shipping lanes across WhiteSands, trading with DisCity thousands of miles away. Even the satellite towns surrounding DisCity lean upon Campbell technology and trade.

It is for this very reason that the Campbell family has grown so many offshoots and heirs; the courtyard is never without the laughter of children.
