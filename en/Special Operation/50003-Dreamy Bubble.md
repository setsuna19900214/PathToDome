# Dreamy Bubble

Roaring Dragon: I'll... destroy... Huh?!

You swing your fist, but even though you don't expend much strength, this behemoth gets knocked into the air. It hits a pitch-black gable and falls to the ground.

This paper-like "dragon" defies all your fears. You finally remember where you've seen this striking style—In a game from EMP's second-hand retro game console.

It's not surprising for a mischievous fun-seeker like Mess to have such a dream. But unlike Roulecca, she doesn't appear in her own dream.

**【Advance】**

- Mess? Are you here?

**end**

No response. Instead, the dragon climbs up again, blocking your way. You can't find a way to beat it completely.

Roaring Dragon: Grrr... Grr... Destroy—arghhh!!

At that moment, an angelic light shines through the darkness. A tall figure strides forward, and a righteous voice fills the cartoonish cavern...

??: Stop, Draco—Don't hurt the villagers of Ladatom! Your true enemy is the Hero!

So it really is Draco... Villager? Is the voice talking about you? Is this jagged Hero going to save you from the claws of the paper-like Dreadlord?

Frightened Dragon: O Hero... Are you holding... the Sacred Sword?!

The Hero draws the sword from its sheath. The dazzling light instantly blinds your eyes.

Hero: The evil Dreadlord has awakened. He is spreading darkness and corrupting hearts. But justice will triumph, because the Sacred Sword that will defeat Draco is here...

Hero: This is the final battle! O Sacred Sword, stand by me to defeat the Dreadlord and save the world!

Frightened Dragon: Nooo...!!!

The weak but unbeatable dragon is finally defeated this time! Its body begins to break up as cartoonish music plays in the background. The cracks spread into the world, reaching you.

The only thing that is not torn apart by the light is the Sacred Sword and the Hero in the center of the light. Is this world going to be destroyed?!
