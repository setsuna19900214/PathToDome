## Honey-101 Back Stage Conflict

As the indulgent feast draws to a close, cleaners and dancers trade places on the stage.

The show now over, the performers collapse in exhaustion, removing makeup and changing clothes in a heavy, oppressive silence. Sallie: Hnngg...

To survive, she has to adapt. Mustering her strength, Sallie drags her body toward the huddle of performers and pricks up her ears.
