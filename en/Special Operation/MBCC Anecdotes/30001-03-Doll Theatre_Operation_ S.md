# MBCC Anecdotes｜Doll Theatre
Operation: S

## SID-301 Doll Theater Ⅰ

Chief's Office.

After returning from a work trip lasting several days, you lock yourself in the office to tackle the backlog of reports.

Going through this month's financial statement left you dazed. After a brief pause, you reached out and grabbed the comms device.

${PlayerName}: Wynn, why are this month's expenses double last month's?

Wynn: Eh, did you forget, Chief?

Wynn: You were worried that Sinners would get bored in the Bureau, so you suggested we organize some events. Many are quite enthusiastic about this.

Wynn: Sinners wanted to hold a gala, and they bought a lot of things for their performances.

Wynn: I did show you the program and the shopping list. You approved them! QAQ

You scroll up through the conversation history and indeed find the lists among the files.

You, who was fretting about West District redevelopment at the time, simply replied,, "As long as they don't buy any dangerous or prohibited items."

${PlayerName}: ...I did forget. How's the gala preparation going?

Wynn: Everyone knows you're busy, so we've been moving forward on our own!

Wynn: The gala will be held in the activity area. You should come and join us if you have time!

You put away the device. Worried about more surprises, you decide to check out the activity area.

The activity area is much livelier than usual. Through the tightly closed glass doors of the small rooms, you catch the sounds of electric guitars and suonas, along with loud rap and rock music.

A mix of laughter and screams is heard everywhere. Walking past one glass door after another and accustoming yourself to the sight of Sinners busily engaged in their practices, you can't help but smile.

Chief: Well, just a bit of funding. It's good they are having fun.

Roulecca: No, this doll is supposed to be Chief. It should wear a trench coat, not a dress... Ugh, how did an idiot like you even survive in Syndicate?!

Hearing the mention of your name, your gaze shifts to the small stage being set up in the hall.

EMP: Tsk, what do kids know about mix and match? This is how Syndicans dress!

EMP: Trust me, this will make your performance stand out at the gala.

OwO: No, it's ugly!

<ins>OwO: Little clapper is the male lead in OwO's script, but this doll the blondie made doesn't look anything like him!</ins><br><ins>OwO: Little clapper is the female lead in OwO's script, but this doll the blondie made doesn't look anything like her!</ins>

Approaching, you see Roulecca and OwO trying to snatch something from EMP's tight grasp. EMP is resisting defiantly.

Chief: What's the matter?

OwO: Oh, little clapper!

Roulecca: Chief!

Seeing you, Roulecca's eyes light up as she runs over and shoves something soft into your hands.

Roulecca: You came at the right time, Chief! Come help Roulecca prepare the doll theater!

Roulecca: Blondie sucks and the dolls she made don't match the characters in the script at all. We need to redo all of them! At this rate, our doll theater won't be ready when the gala starts!

EMP: Shut your big m...

OwO: But! If little clapper help us, I'm sure we can perfectly recreate the characters OwO needs!

OwO: It's settled then, you'll help make the dolls for our theater, little clapper!

You look at the doll in your hand. It has a crooked pink wig on its head and is dressed in a white robe in Hecate's style, but also wears a familiar pair of glasses.

You shoot a doubtful glance at the doll that EMP, who's been pushed aside, is holding. There's certainly not a trace of resemblance to yourself.

EMP: D-don't look at me like that! I was trying to give you a makeover.

You've grasped the situation now. Thinking about the pile of unfinished work, you are inclined to refuse them. But then you see Roulecca and OwO grabbing your arms, looking up at you with their hopeful little faces.

OwO: Little clapper, you're the main character in OwO's script this time. You must be looking forward to it too!

OwO: If we use the doll made by blondie, no one will recognize it's you, and OwO's story will go to waste!

Roulecca: Please, Chief, Roulecca has put a lot of effort and money into this show. You wouldn't want all that effort squandered, right?

...Indeed.

Looking at the two starry-eyed kids and hearing the other Sinners busily preparing their shows, you're unable to utter the refusal.

Chief: ...Alright, I'll help you.

Roulecca: Really?

OwO: Yay! Little clapper is the best! OwO will give you a treasured item as a reward!

Watching the two lively kids, you smile.

Chief: So, my job is to create dolls needed in your play based on my impressions, right?

OwO: Exactly! Here's the list of characters.

Roulecca: I knew Chief needed no hand-holding. You're so smart!

OwO: Not like blondie... Huh? Where did blondie go?

Not far away, trying to sneak away on tiptoe, EMP stops in her tracks.

Roulecca: Hey! Don't you dare sneak away, blondie! You gotta help Roulecca with the theater stage as well!

EMP: Isn't Chief helping you? I've got other things to take care of. Bye!

OwO: Liar! I took you in because you didn't have your own show. You can't leave!

OwO and Roulecca chase after EMP until they disappear at the end of the corridor. You can't help but chuckle, shaking your head as you pick up the list and approach the scattered doll parts nearby.

Chief: Then, let's get to work.

## SID-302 Doll Theater II

EMP: Oh... I'm exhausted... Finally...

Roulecca: Finally, all done!

OwO: Wow, it looks amazing! Hey blondie, you can be useful sometimes!

OwO: Hehe, so? I'm pretty talented, aren't I?

Chief: Impressive, indeed. I didn't expect you to pull it off so well.

EMP: It was all my work. Stop taking the credit!

Roulecca: Ah, you're done with your dolls too, Chief!

Roulecca: Great job, Chief! They are just perfect. Our little theater will sure be a smash hit at the gala! It'll win the audience's hearts!

OwO: Oh! A smash hit! Win the audience's hearts!

Roulecca: Look forward to it, Chief!

OwO and Roulecca drag the slumped EMP onto the stage energetically and start planning with the dolls. Their happiness is infectious, and you find yourself smiling too.

Chief: Yeah, I'm looking forward to it.
