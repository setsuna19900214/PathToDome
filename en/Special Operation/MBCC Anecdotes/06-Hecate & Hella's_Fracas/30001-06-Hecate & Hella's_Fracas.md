## SID-600 Project: Dark Episodes

Turning back the clock to when Hella was still at the Bureau.

Hella: Hey Hecate, what are we even supposed to be looking for here? We've been all over Area C and haven't found anything special.

Hecate: Shh, keep your voice down. People are reading here.

Hella: Blah blah blah. Hecate's lecture again...

From the shelf, Hecate removes a book titled *A Study on the Impact and Spillover Effects of Mining Robots on Corporate Hypercube Production* so Hella can see Quinn's President elegantly browsing through books in the reading area.

As if sensing something, the lady in red slowly looks up toward Area C. Hella immediately presses herself flat against the bookshelf.

Hella: ****... Ahem... Maybe we should check out the other shelves? Ahaha... Ouch!

Barely holding herself together, Hella stumbles and knocks even more books off the shelf, with one of them landing right on her head.

Just as she's about to act up, Hecate quickly picks up the book from Hella's head.

Hecate: This is the series I'm looking for.

Hecate: *BOOKS* so gripping, they pull you into their world. Welcome to *Project: Dark Episodes*.

Hella: Why did you say *BOOKS* like that?

Hecate: Simply because the book is only one of its forms of existence...

Hecate turns the book open, and a blinding white light engulfs the library.

## SID-601 Incoming! The Slacking Ducks!

Incoming!

The Slacking Ducks!

Hella: Whoa, that's amazing... We just got inside the book like that? You know what, this place is actually pretty cool.

Hecate: It's not a book. This is a Hologram.

A round and chubby Hella is talking to the equally round and chubby Hecate next to her.

Hecate: These are our virtual avatars, like the one Nino uses.

Hecate: And everything here is just a *projection*. They've got nothing to do with our original world.

Hella: Oh...? So you're saying I can do whatever I want here without consequences?

Hecate: Theoretically speaking, yes.

Hella: Then it's decided! I'm going to defeat the strongest enemy in this world of projection!

Hecate: That's an admirable goal, but we should deal with our current situation first.

The two look around to find themselves surrounded by a ring of ducks, all quacking nonstop.

Duck: Quack... Sinner Hecate detected. Initiating detention.

Hella: What the... Why are they coming after Sinners even in Project: Dark?! And why are you their first target again?!!

Hecate: Stories often take inspiration from reality and surpass reality.

Duck: Quack... All unauthorized individuals, please stand back. Do not interfere with law enforcement!

Hella: Hey! I'm a Sinner too! I'm not just some unauthorized individual!

Duck: ...

Hella: ...You just avoided my eyes, didn't you? Do you think I'm not good enough to be a Sinner?

Seeing the duck avert its eyes, Hella's grip around the pipe tightens.

Duck: Quack quack! Proceed with detention! Ignore this chatterbox—she can't even make it out of the detention room! Quack!

People sometimes appear unusually calm before they erupt in anger.

Uncharacteristically, Hella turns to Hecate and asks a question—this time, with surprising patience.

Hella: So... we can do anything we want here, right? After all, this is just a Hologram, right?

Hecate: That's correct.

Hella: ****! I'm gonna give these ******* ducks a hundred bumps on their heads!

Hella: Now talk! Who's the unauthorized individual now?

Duck: Quack... Q-quack... I am...

Hella grabs the duck with one hand and draws it close to her face.

Hella: And remind me, who the hell am I?

Duck: Quack... You're Sinner No. 98...

Hella: Good, you've done your research... Now say it again. I couldn't hear you!

Duck: You're Sinner No. 98! A legend of Syndicate! The right hand of the great MBCC Chief!

Hella: Spot on! Now that you've offended someone as famous and powerful as me, shouldn't you offer some compensation? That dummy Chief hasn't bought me any new clothes lately, hehe...

Duck: Quack quack! Don't push your duck too far!

Hella: Then buff me and Hecate's stats! You know, make us deal 999,999 damage with one hit!

Duck: Quack... What kind of clothes do you want again?

Before the conversation can finish, the ground suddenly trembles with a violent quake. A deafening roar erupts, sending the ducks scattering in a frenzy of flapping wings and panicked quacks.

Hella: ...What's going on?!

Duck: Quack! This is bad—A Sinner has broken free!

Hecate: Oh.

Hecate nods but continues to walk further away.

Duck: Don't go, quack! If you guys leave, she'll tear this whole hologram apart! Please save us!

The ducks flop down in a pleading attempt to block their path, making it clear they won’t take no for an answer.

## SID-603 Begone! Banner Blackhole

Begone!

Banner Blackhole!

Hella: Hecate, what am I looking at...?

In the void, a huge mural was silently suspended in mid-air, like a creation of the gods.

Hecate: This is the distribution center for Hypercubes, a place where wishes are born and granted, and also a breeding ground for vice and temptation.

Hella: Speak English, okay?

Hecate: It's the gacha interface.

Hella: The ga... what?

Hecate: The gacha interface. You and EMP's lot only play offline games, so I guess you haven't seen this before.

Hecate: Here you can spend Hypercubes to obtain the most powerful S-Class Sinners.

Hella: O-kay...? But isn't that pretty cool? All you need to do is spend some Hypercubes and you get powerful Sinners.

Hecate: It's not that easy. *Luck* rules this place. Some people get exactly what they want with just one tap, using a free Warrant they earned as a reward.

Hecate: While others still have their hands empty after eighty pulls.

Hella: Well, when it comes to luck... I guess... everyone has their unlucky moments, right?

Hecate: By the way, Hella, you're just a B-Class Sinner in Project: Dark. People sometimes get nine copies of you in ten pulls.

Hella: ****! Hecate, let's smash these stupid buttons to pieces!

After smashing the buttons with her pipe and melting them with laser beams, Hella suddenly looks pensive.

With a turn of the page, she got transported into a world both foreign and familiar.

Everything is absurd and puzzling here, and perhaps she's wasting her precious time in the Bureau...

But it's fun. Adventures with friends are always fun.

Where should she go next? What other surprises await her in this pixelated version of DisCity?

Hecate: ...It's coming.

Hella: What?

Hecate: The source of all evil, and the very foundation this world is built upon.

Hecate: It's there, waiting for us.

Hella: Hold on, hold on... There should be tons of other fun places to explore in this world, right?

Hella: Like the Erica Villa in Eastside, or the Council in Paradeisos! I never get to visit these places in the real DisCity. Let's check them out while we're here!

Hecate: Those places don't exist here.

Hella: Huh?

Hella: ...Oh, maybe we can only unlock these areas after defeating the final boss? Or do we need to finish exploring the current area first? That's how these games usually work, right?

Hecate: No, I heard they didn't have the budget to build them.

Hella: Oh.

## SID-605 What? A Villain with Manners!

What?

A Villain with Manners!

??: No one is going anywhere until you pay for the repairs.

Hella: Pay for the repairs? Hecate, isn't this just a hologram? Since when do holograms need repairs?

Hecate: "Please note that certain reparations will be required if excessive damage has been caused to the world."

Hecate produces a manual and points to the fine print at the very bottom for Hella to see.

Hella: Oh come on, that's so not fair! Who can even read text that tiny?!

??: A rule is a rule. If you use our product, you must honor the terms of service.

??: According to my rough estimation, repairs will cost 100 million DisCoins.

Hella: 100 million?! You might as well rob us at gunpoint!

??: Your destruction has caused immeasurable damage to this product of Enterprise A. The hundred million is only a rough estimate, the number of critical bugs your rampage has triggered is beyond calculation.

Hella: Hey, don't blame us for your shoddy work! If you hadn't made everything so ridiculous here, we wouldn't have had to blow the Bureau to smithereens!

In the face of Hella's accusations, the man who claimed to be from Enterprise A paced slowly, revealing his true colors.

Enterprise A Rep: No further talk is needed. What's done is done. You must take responsibility.

Enterprise A Rep: If you're currently experiencing financial difficulties, we offer an installment plan. You'll only need to pay 100,000 DisCoins per installment, with an annual interest rate of...

Hella: (Gasps) We won’t be able to pay it off until our next life!

Hecate: But sounds like the remedial classes have really paid off. You've gotten so much better at math.

Enterprise A Rep: I see you're not thrilled about the payment plan. Fortunately, we have an alternative proposition.

Hella: What kind of proposition?

Enterprise A Rep: After I defeat you, you'll fix everything you broke in Project: Dark and get rid of all the bugs before you leave!

Hella: Can you do that again? Yeah, that.

Enterprise A Rep: Our sincerest apologies for the inconvenience! We'll assign more staff to fix the bugs right away!

The Enterprise A representative drops to his knees, bowing until his forehead nearly touches the ground.

Hecate: Lower. I've seen other Syndicate gangsters do that better.

Enterprise A Rep: Our sincerest apologies for the inconvenience! The server maintenance compensation has been sent to your inbox. Please note that the email expires in seven days. Thank you for your understanding and continued support!

Hella: ...What is he going on about?

Hecate: No idea. Probably just survival instinct kicking in.

Hella: So... do we still have to pay?

Enterprise A Rep: No, no... Please keep playing our games once we’ve fixed the bugs—that’s all we ask!

Hella: Sounds good. So when are you going to add Eastside and Paradeisos to this world, anyway?

Enterprise A Rep: Um… Well, we’re working hard to improve our capacity. We sincerely apologize!

The Enterprise A representative's forehead meets the ground again with an audible thud.
