## SID-903 Wish-Granting Magic

BOOM—The bomb hits the last fleeing duck carrying a treasure chest. You've successfully recovered all the stolen treasures.

Duck: Quack quack, quack quack quack quack, quack quack...

(Sob... We'll never steal again! We'll return all the treasures, please spare us...)

Though you can't understand what they're saying, the pleading looks in their tearful little eyes speak volumes. You can't help but pity them.

Etti: Hmph, I haven't even punished you for upsetting the balance of my game world yet... But I've had my fun, so I suppose I can let you off for now—

${PlayerName}: But why did they steal the treasures in the first place?

As you ponder this question, a mob of townspeople suddenly rush out with pots and pans. You and Etti are caught off guard, and before you can react, they form a protective circle around the ducks.

Townsperson A: Who do you think you are?! Leave them alone!

Townsperson B: So that's why our poor ducks kept coming home crying—it was you! You monsters, I'm reporting you to the authorities!

You find yourselves completely surrounded by the seething crowd. The townspeople are furious, determined to seek justice for their beloved ducks.

${PlayerName}: (Whispers) What do we do now?

Etti: (Whispers) I didn't expect giving the NPCs more behavioral autonomy would backfire like this... Hmm, let me think...

Etti clears her throat.

Etti: Ahem! These ducks snuck into my cave and stole my treasures. I'm simply here to take back what's mine. Are you really going to harbor thieves?

A brief silence falls over the crowd. Then, they begin to whisper to each other.

Townsperson A: Stealing treasures... Could they be...

Etti: What?

Townsperson B: A few days ago, we saw these poor ducks huddling around a few matches for warmth on the street. They were nearly freezing to death, so we took them in. Though we couldn't understand their language, they showed real intelligence—they've been helping us with our work, as if trying to repay our kindness.

Townsperson B: In years past when harvests were good, our village would gather as families around the hearth for a feast, filling children's stockings with sweets and presents. But this year... everyone is sick with worry. We can barely afford enough food, let alone gifts for the children.

Townsperson B: They must have understood what was happening. When they disappeared for days, we thought they'd been kidnapped by criminals... But now I get it—they were trying to repay our kindness by stealing the dragon's treasures from the mountain.

You watch as the townspeople cradle the sniffing ducks in their arms. You can't help but chuckle at their bruised, pitiful state. But... after hearing their story, you find yourself not only reluctant to take back the treasures, but actually wanting to help both the poor ducks and the townspeople.

${PlayerName}: (Whispers) Let them keep the treasures. I know they're just characters in your game, but I still want them and the ducks to have a warm and happy holiday together. Can you use [Etti's Wish Bottle]—the reward for clearing the level—to grant this wish for me, Etti?

Etti glances at you with a resigned smile.

Etti: What kind of evil overlord do you take me for? Of course I'll grant such a simple wish...

She extends her hand, and countless gold coins gather at her command. Their golden radiance bathes the shivering villagers in the cold night with warm light.

Etti: After all, everyone here is part of my favorite game world!

As her words fade, dusk settles and streetlights flicker to life. Snowflakes begin to fall across the town as empty dinner tables fill with steaming dishes. The hearth fires burn bright and warm as the townspeople join hands with the ducks to welcome an evening of joy with family and friends.

From that day forth, a legend spread through the town: when night falls on that special evening, a miraculous mage will descend from heaven—adorned with dragon horns and a beautiful gown. Together with a dragon rider, they scatter coins of good fortune over every home, blessing all with happiness.
