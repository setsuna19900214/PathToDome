## SID-201 Puzzled Phone-In

The night has settled in, but you remain immersed in your work at the office. EMP and Hella had vowed to stick around until you wrapped up. Yet, they casually tune into the radio, seemingly oblivious to how it might distract you.

DeadLover Radio: Hey there! A big shoutout to all our listeners. I've got some juicy updates for you—wait, scratch that, I mean news!

DeadLover Radio: You know, there's this Ghost Call going around Eastside. Word is, in the dead of the night, when everyone is sound asleep, some ghost lady might ring you up.

DeadLover Radio: But, trust me, you don't wanna pick up. Because if you do, she's gonna... well, you get the idea. Heh heh.

DeadLover Radio: So, what's the ghost gonna do? Give a cheeky chuckle like "hehehe"?

A phone's abrupt ringtone cuts through the airwaves, instantly hushing the captivated audience.

Hella: Who's calling this late?

EMP: Uhh... It's not that spooky Ghost Call we just heard about on the radio, right?

The phone keeps on ringing relentlessly, but the two sitting nearby don't seem inclined to pick it up.

**【Choose One】**

- Let Hella answer the call

  You ask Hella to pick up the phone, but it is evident she has something else in mind.

  Hella: Huh? I was hoping to keep listening to DeadLover Radio. EMP, you get it.

  EMP: What the—why me?! What if it's some creepy ghost on the line? You're literally right next to the phone!

  The two engage in a heated back-and-forth... Ultimately, you step in and answer the phone yourself.

- Let EMP answer the call

  You ask EMP to pick up the phone, but she immediately appears disheartened.

  EMP: Ugh, no can do. My stomach's suddenly killing me; I can't take the call... Ohhhh, it's so painful...

  Fed up with EMP's dramatic display on the couch, you decide to answer the phone yourself.

- Answer the call yourself

  You signal to Hella to turn down the radio, then grab the phone off the table.

**end**

...It's merely a procedural call from FAC about Corruptor in the Rust—nothing pressing.

As you set the phone down, you sense Hella stealthily drawing nearer from behind.

Hella: Heh, about that Call at 3 AM? I caught some spicy gossip just now!

Hella: On the radio, they were saying if you get a call from a ghostly lady, she'll challenge you to guess her name. She'd go like—

Hella: *"Hey... Is this ${PlayerName}?... Try and guess my name... You've got three shots..."*

Hella: *"That's not it... Nope, that's not my name, either... One last shot... And still, you're off..."*

Hella: *"All three guesses are gone... Well, I'll let you in on it, my name is—"*

Hella imitates the eerie voice of the ghostly lady, whispering ever so close to your ear.

Hella: Wahhhhhh!

Hella: HAHAHA, gotcha, didn't I?

**【Choose One】**

- That was terrifying.

  Chief: Man, that's seriously creepy. You really caught me off guard there.

  Hella: Tsk, you don't look scared at all. You're just pulling my leg...

- It was kind of funny.

  Chief: I don't know why, but I can't help but smirk.

  Hella: Seriously, can't you just humor me for once?

**end**

Hella gives you a playful punch on the arm, but out of the corner of your eye, you notice EMP on the sofa. She looks a little ghostly pale.

Chief: EMP, are you okay?

EMP: Ha! You think Hella's little stunt can spook someone like me? I was just reminded of some online urban legends.

EMP: Rumor has it, if you dial certain numbers, you'll hear the ghostly lady's voice! Tons of these numbers are floating around the web!

Chief: Is that so...

Hella: Whoa there, don't tell me you're diving into another investigation? Can't you take a break from your workaholic ways for once?

You're about to have a rare, yet short-lived break, but the notion of midnight Ghost Call is hard to shake off.

Could this be another one of those Mania events? If I've got some time to spare, might as well dig up some clues online and see where it leads.
