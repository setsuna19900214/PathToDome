# MBCC Anecdotes｜Chocolate War

## SID-401 Chocolate War I

The alarm in the Bureau blares, and the ground trembles slightly.

Alarm: Warning: Fluctuations in the M-value have been detected within the Bureau. Please identify the source of the fluctuations...

The broadcast is cut off mid-sentence, drowned out by a louder rumbling noise.

The trembling continues for a while before stopping. You open the office door, hoping to see what exactly happened in the Bureau.

Just then, countless beautifully packaged gift boxes flood into your office. It seems they are the cause of the disturbance.

Driven by curiosity, you unwrap the gift and take out what's inside.

It's a normal piece of chocolate.

**【Advance】**

- Hmm...

**end**

Did something go wrong with the Supply Office? Why else would so many chocolates appear out of nowhere?

You rush to the Supply Office and find that a considerable number of Sinners and staff are already there, all confused about the situation.

Logistics Staff A: So many chocolates. I won't ever have to worry about food again...

Logistics Staff B: ...You'll get high blood sugar if you finish all that.

——!

??: Attention to everyone in the Bureau, I believe you all must have received a significant amount of chocolate by now.

A mysterious voice comes through from the broadcast, drawing the attention of everyone.

??: Of course, this is just the beginning... You must collect as much chocolate as possible, and by the end of today, whoever has the most chocolate will be the winner.

??: And the prize for the winner is...

??: A week alone with the MBCC Chief!

**【Advance】**

- ...

**end**

Like a stone thrown into a pond, the announcement creates ripples throughout the Bureau—some are doubtful, some are thrilled, and you just feel a headache brewing. When did Sinners become bold enough to plot something targeting you?

EMP: Hey! How do we know you're not bluffing?

<ins>??: If I manage to fill the Bureau with chocolate, I most certainly have the means to let you spend a week with him.</ins><br><ins>??: If I manage to fill the Bureau with chocolate, I most certainly have the means to let you spend a week with her.</ins>

Ariel: Alone...?

Quite a few naive Sinners seem to have taken the bait.

Roulecca: I have questions!

Roulecca: Can we only pick up chocolates? Can Roulecca defeat others and then take their chocolates?

??: Of course you can, but to make the game fair, you must all follow the Chocolate War Rules.

Roulecca: Chocolate War Rules?

??: Simply put, during a duel, the side with more chocolates will get all of the chocolates from their opponent.

??: And you can't use your abilities during the duel.

As you listen to these absurd rules, your urge to find this troublemaking Sinner only grows stronger.

**【Advance】**

- I want to participate too.

**end**

??: Oh, the prize wants to play as well?

Chief: You can't control me, I am also a member of the MBCC, and...

Chief: All Sinners in the MBCC are equally important to me.

??: Interesting. Let me add one more rule then.

<ins>??: If you want the MBCC Chief to win, then give him your chocolates.</ins><br><ins>??: If you want the MBCC Chief to win, then give her your chocolates.</ins>

??: Well, that's about all the rules, now... let the Chocolate War begin!

As the PA system falls silent, marking the beginning of the competition, many interested Sinners spring into action.

In order to restore the Bureau to its original state, you also begin searching for the mysterious Sinner in the broadcast.

## SID-402 Chocolate War II

After dueling with many Sinners and winning their chocolates, you finally catch a glimpse of the mastermind behind this.

??: What's the matter, Chief? Have you reached your limit?

She's not wearing her usual outfit. Her eyes sparkle with a strange red light, exuding a dangerous aura.

**【Advance】**

- Stop it, Macchiato.

**end**

Macchiato: Stop? What are you talking about?

Macchiato: Watching everyone desperately fighting for one thing, that's what I live for!

Macchiato's power surges, and colorful gift boxes dance wildly on the ground.

It seems Mania has taken control of her mind. Her actions have become unpredictable.

You reach out your hand, trying to restrain her with the shackles.

Macchiato: What's the hurry, Chief?

She dodges the shackles with a swift movement, then picks up a piece of chocolate and places it in your mouth.

Macchiato: Come on, open up. This is a reward for your hard work... let's enjoy it together.

Macchiato: You're so tense. Relax. I will take everyone to the peak of ecstasy at just the right moment...

Macchiato: Your shackles are useless against me. In this Chocolate War, I *am* the rules.

Macchiato: Unless you can beat me within my rules.

**【Advance】**

- I will.

**end**

The bittersweet taste of chocolate melts in your mouth.

To contain the spread of Mania and prevent Macchiato from losing control, you approach her without hesitation.
