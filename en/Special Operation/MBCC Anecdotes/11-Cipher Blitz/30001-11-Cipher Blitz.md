# MBCC Anecdotes｜Cipher Blitz

## SID-1601 Beep Beep Beep Click Click

Christina: Christina, Eastside's most dedicated acting inspector, is back on the case! And today's mystery is...

Christina: Wait! What do we have here? Could this be... an antique cipher machine?

Christina: How absolutely thrilling! This is just the kind of puzzle a classy detective should face!

Christina: (Sniffs) It's... emanating a mysterious energy.

Christina: Well, too bad for you, encryptor! You're up against Christina herself. A little cipher like this is no match for me!

## SID-1602 A Bizarre Cipher Machine

After Christina enters the code, the cipher machine spits out a slip of paper.

Christina: Let's see... there's a website address written here. Let's check it out.

Christina: Huh? It looks like completion records for a game demo... "eatmydust," "GetARealJob," "DarkStick69"...

Christina: Wow, these usernames are... pretty aggressive.

Christina continues scrolling, and the webpage confirms that all the usernames point to a single IP address, exposing a collection of one player's alt accounts.

Christina: So many accounts... "RebornAsGamingGenius," "ReincarnatedForTheWin," "OneLifeRun32," "OneLifeRun56," "OneLifeRun108," "*****Dev"...

Christina: Looks like this player's gaming journey has been... quite the struggle. Let's see where this IP is located.

The search results appear, revealing the device is located inside the Bureau. Its name is displayed with audacious clarity: Nino Sama #2.

With a sigh, Christina navigates to Nino's livestream channel, only to find it offline. A banner announces tomorrow's event:

"Blind playthrough of HollowSong's Ironman Mode, absolutely no spoilers! Come witness Nino Sama complete the game without dying!"

Christina: 340 failure records... Hmm... I don't think I'm misunderstanding what "blind playthrough" means...

Nino: Aaaaah! Christina, what are you looking at?!

Christina: Oh no...! Run!

Nino: Stop right there!!!

Nino: Pant... pant... pant... how are you... so fast...

Christina: Well, I am an acting inspector! I have daily physical training. To catch criminals and bring them to justice, you need exceptional fitness and athletic ability!

Nino rolls her eyes.

Nino: What's there... to be so proud of...

Christina: Don't worry, your secret is safe with me.

Christina: By the way, I glanced at your streaming analytics and noticed a pattern: the more attempts you fail, Nino, the better your viewership and chat engagement get. Constantly failing seems like a pretty clever streaming strategy!

Nino: Just shut up!!!

Christina: The crescent moon hangs in the night sky, a maiden pretends to sleepwalk by... Could the intel be hidden in this little rhyme?

Christina: Sneaking into the Chief's private space, finding little love secrets all over the place. Hehe... no wonder my assistant is so popular, but nothing escapes the great detective Christina!

Christina: When it comes to sleepwalking, the first person that comes to mind has to be...

Letta: Mmm... zZZ... mmmm... snoop... evil snoop... heart invader... mm... found you...

Letta: Must... zZZ... eliminate...

Christina: Whoa! Things have already escalated to elimination?!

Christina: Wait-wait-wait-wait—! I wasn't trying to spy on your secrets on purpose; this was completely an accident!

Christina: I just thought this cipher machine would reveal crucial intel about major unsolved cases! That's the only reason I started decoding it!

Christina: This happens in detective novels all the time! The agency receives a mysterious package that turns out to be a puzzle challenge from some brilliant criminal mastermind!

Christina: Don't worry, I've already destroyed both the original code and the decrypted results. Your secrets are absolutely safe; I give you my word of honor!

Letta: Wha... Christina...? Where am I? Did I sleepwalk again... so tired, going back to bed...

Letta: Sleepwalk... Sleepwalk... Head so fuzzy... Don't remember anything...

Christina: ...

## SID-1604 Magical, Isn't It?

Christina: After all that trouble, the information from this cipher machine turns out to be nothing but gossip. Who in the world would collect intelligence like this...

Christina: Could it be... the owner of this machine is actually a tabloid journalist? But who...

Mess: Young adventurer Christina! The legendary sword you requested is complete! Hand over that cipher machine in exchange!

Mess suddenly materializes before Christina. In her left hand, she wields a foam sword studded with light strips that pulse with dazzling rainbow colors. In her right, a speaker blasts her powerful entrance music.

Christina: Mess... Why are you here? I never asked you to forge a sword. Detectives don't even use swords...

Christina: And besides, that thing in your hand...

Mess: Cut the chatter and hand it over!

Whap! The foam sword lands with perfect precision on Christina's head, the impact as crisp as a ping-pong ball. As Christina clutches the stinging spot, Mess's hand darts past her to snatch the cipher machine.

But the sting is fleeting. The young detective shakes it off, tightens her grip on the device, and breaks into a sprint once more.

Christina: Not a chance! This cipher machine absolutely cannot fall into your hands!

Mess: This device doesn't even have the intel you wanted, so what's the harm in giving it to me? Wouldn't it be better to put it to good use?

Christina: No way! If this cipher machine falls into your hands, it would throw the entire Bureau into chaos!

Mess: That's exactly what I want! Chaos! You pull my hair, I slap your face—isn't that super—duper—FUN?

Christina: How can you say that! That's absolutely terrible!

Just as Mess is lost in her excitement, a flash of green darts into the noisy hallway. A girl draws her revolver in one smooth motion—"pew"— and a small, pink flower blooms on Mess's forehead. With elegant nonchalance, she raises the barrel to her lips and blows away imaginary smoke.

Roulecca: In the Bureau, no one touches Roulecca's interests without permission. Understand, darling?

Mess: Roulecca, you jerk! Paintball guns still hurt, you know!

Roulecca: Mess, I regret to inform you that in this game... you have been eliminated.

Roulecca: Pow—pow—pow—pew-pew!

Soon, the hallway becomes a vibrant rainbow of colors.

## SID-1605 Biggest Secrets

Christina: Thanks for that, Roulecca.

Roulecca: There we go! That silly Mess has been chased away. Now, be a dear and hand over that cipher machine to Roulecca!

Christina: Does this cipher machine belong to you?

Roulecca: No. But as a reward for that brilliant performance just now, this machine should rightfully go to Roulecca!

Roulecca: Roulecca happens to know this little device records the deepest secrets of anyone who touches it, you know...

Christina recoils as if electrocuted, her hands flying away from the cipher machine she had been clutching so protectively a moment before.

Christina: W-what do you mean?

Roulecca: Roulecca saw with her own eyes how Letta and Nino were fiddling with this thing for a while, and not long after, you managed to decode their secrets...

Christina: *Oh no... I promised them I'd keep it confidential.*

Roulecca: So Roulecca will place it in the Chief's room, hehe.

Christina: No, absolutely not! I must protect my assistant's privacy!

At the mere mention of "Chief," the fear and worry flee from Christina's eyes, making way for unwavering determination.

Roulecca: Then play a round with Roulecca. Whoever wins gets the cipher machine. Let's see which side Lady Luck favors.

Roulecca deftly twirls the revolver in her hand, the cylinder popping out with a click.

Christina: Nobody can win against you!

Even before Roulecca's performance can reach its finale, Christina is already in motion, clutching the cipher machine tightly as she makes her escape.

## SID-1606 Not A Puzzle

Christina: Pant... pant... What in the world is going on today...

Christina's face flushes a deep red as she gasps for air. In the distance, she spots two familiar figures approaching. They move like saviors in a doomsday wasteland. Never before have they seemed so righteous.

Christina: Nino... Letta... help me stop Roulecca! She's trying to steal this cipher machine and use it to invade the Chief's privacy!

No sooner does Christina speak than a crystalline gleam lights Letta's dreamy eyes. They snap shut, and she begins to drift forward, moving with the silent, aimless quality of a specter.

Letta: Mmm mmm... cipher machine... find the cipher machine...

Nino: On second thought, this cipher machine could provide some explosive material for my livestreams!

Christina: You... you guys!

Christina is left utterly speechless. She hasn't even planned an escape route when Mess materializes out of nowhere.

Mess: Roulecca, are you really going to let them butt in like this? And Christina, the cipher machine and an inspector like you are such a perfect package deal, you know!

Mess: But hey, Nino's just doing this for her livestream career, and I support that! As for Letta... well, nothing a sleepwalker does should surprise anyone.

Mess: Come on, I want to see bloodshed!

Roulecca: Don't run, pew pew pew! Roulecca has you in her sights!

Christina: Assistant—! Where are you?! The Bureau's in chaos!

Christina runs desperately until her steps grow unsteady and her vision blurs. Staggering forward in a daze, she collapses into a familiar embrace.

The figure staggers back a few steps with a wince. The impact wasn't just from Christina, but from the sharp-edged cipher machine she was clutching.

Christina: My assistant! You're finally here! Help me!!

Chief: Maybe you should help me first...

Your legs buckle, fighting for balance. Cradled in Christina's arms, the so-called weapon is revealed as an old cipher machine, its surface a web of scratches. From it radiates a disorienting aura of Mania.

You probe it with your shackles and instantly recognize the source: Hilda.

Christina: This cipher machine has the power to reveal people's secrets. It ab... absolutely can't fall into their hands!

Chief: They?

Christina: Yeah, it's... Mess and the others... wait... where did they go?

Christina turns. The hallway lies dead silent and utterly deserted. From around the corner, Letta cautiously peeks out, but the moment her eyes meet Christina's, she instantly ducks back into hiding.

Christina: Ugh, those guys...

Chief: Let me take the cipher machine. I'll return it to its rightful owner.

Christina: Do you know who owns this cipher machine?

Chief: Yes, she's a brilliant cryptographer. I'll introduce you to her when I get the chance. By the way, you did a great job today!

You gently smooth down Christina's wind-tousled bangs, then turn to face the shadowed end of the hallway, your voice cutting clearly through the silence.

Chief: As for the rest of you, no dinner until this hallway is spotless.

The hallway remains empty, the silence broken only by the disgruntled mumbles drifting from around the corner.
