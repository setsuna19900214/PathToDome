## SID-101 Youthful Mirth I

June 1

Chief's Office

Your communication screen has been flashing non-stop with incoming messages for a while now.

**【Advance】**

- View

**end**

Wynn:Chief... Would you mind coming over? We've got a situation here! QAQ

Eugene:...Chief, help me out, please! If this doesn't stop now, I'm gonna lose my life savings!

Eugene:Help, Chief!!!

Wynn:Chief!! Chief!!! Check your phone, I beg you!!! TAT

When you see the SOS messages, you rush to the scene at once.

You arrive to find a particularly tiny figure laughing smugly, arms akimbo.

She's surrounded by staff with agonized expressions alongside heaps of trophies. The moment they see you, the two city arrest team members dash to you as if they're grabbing a lifeline.

Wynn: Boohoo, Chief, here you are finally! You have no idea what I've just been through!

Eugene: Chief... Does this count as a workplace injury?

Roulecca: Hehe, one more round, one more round! Roulecca isn't done yet!

Feeling the tiny figure's attention turning toward you lot, the two immediately hide behind you fearfully.

Chief: ...Is it you, Roulecca?

Roulecca: Of course it's me, you idiot, Chief! Don't tell me just because I've shrunk a little, you don't recognize Roulecca, the cutest girl in the world?

**【Advance】**

- I'm just a little surprised.

**end**

Chief: What happened to you?

Roulecca: Hmm, I couldn't resist and ate a piece of Summer's cake this morning, and suddenly I turned into a child... I think some others ate that cake too!

Roulecca: Anyway, enough with that small matter... Roulecca just wants to keep playing!

The tiny Roulecca claps her hands to signal the start of another round of the game. The people around her immediately cover their pockets and beg you for help with their eyes.

Chief: Um, Roulecca, you know, they can't just ignore their tasks and play with you all day...

Roulecca: I don't care, it's *Day of Youth* today! Endless sweets and endless games, hehe, the privilege of being a *child*!

Roulecca: I won't stop unless... I win my dream prize!

Watching her sulk like a little kid, you come up with an idea.

**【Advance】**

- How about this?

**end**

Chief: Let them go, I'll play with you. And whether you win or lose, I'll gift you a teddy bear.

You take out the toy Faye has purchased for Day of Youth and wave it in front of Roulecca.

Roulecca: ...A teddy bear!

You manage to impress the girl with the plush teddy bear. She goes on to announce cheerfully.

Roulecca: Alrighty, then let's play... Cookie Guess!
