## SID-500 Duck Invasion I

One day in Chief's Office, MBCC

The Bureau is as tense and busy as always today. After a long day, you're finally about to wrap up the tasks in the office and get to catch your breath.

Chief: Once I'm done with these tasks at hand, this week's work is settled.

EMP: Chief! Chief! A raid at the Bureau!

Chief: It's not the first time the Bureau has been *raided*. Even if the alarm goes off now, I can probably stay calm...

Announcement: Warning! Warning! The underground area of the Bureau has been breached by external forces! Combat force, proceed to the area immediately!

EMP: Holy ****, Chief! You just predicted the future! Is that mouth of yours this magical?

Chief: ...I'll take that as a compliment. Anyway, let's go see what's going on.

The Bureau's Underground Corridor

EMP: Like I've told you, when I passed by the Supply Office earlier, I felt like those ducks were acting weird. Talk about being observant! That's the Archer Queen of Syndicate for you.

Chief: The ducks? But surely they can't get armored and start blasting bullets everywhere within the Bureau, can they?

Berserk Duck?: Quack! Quack quack! Quack quack! Quack quack quack!

Chief: ...

EMP: Wow! Sick. Since when has the Bureau been equipping mascots with guns?

Chief: Quit joking around. It's seen us. We need to get it under control!

The duck's firepower is unbelievably strong! Your attack seems unable to crack its defense no matter how hard you try. It's almost as if it's using some kind of cheat code.

EMP: It's locked its HP?! That's cheating! Are you serious?!

Berserk Duck: Quack quack quack! (Duck spin kick!)

EMP: Damn it... It hit me twice! Even Hella wouldn't dare to do this to me...

Berserk Duck: Quack, quack quack quack... (Hesitation is defeat...)

The Bureau's Underground Confinement Room

??: Chief, Chief! Can you hear me? Is everything okay?

You wake up to a voice from the comms and find yourself and EMP locked in a pitch-dark room. That's Nightingale's voice from the communication device you carry.

Chief: I'm fine. EMP is conscious too and looks okay. How's the situation now?

Nightingale: That's good. From what we understand, the ducks at the Supply Office have acquired mecha armor and weapons through unknown means, and they've launched an attack on the Bureau.

Nightingale: I've closed all internal passageways in the Bureau and activated the internal alert. But while trying to isolate the ducks in the underground storage area, it seems I've also trapped you there.

You look around and find that aside from a locked door, there's indeed no other way out. With the room being filled with cardboard boxes, EMP's ability could cause a fire and is thus unusable. You must find a way to open the door from the outside.

You hear the movement of mecha armor outside the door and realize that the storage outside is full of patrolling mecha ducks.

Chief: Did you just say they launched the attack after acquiring the mecha armor?

Nightingale: Yes.

Chief: So... if we can make them lose their mecha armor unknowingly, we'd have a chance to strike back?

Nightingale: ...Got it. I'll arrange for competent Sinners to infiltrate the underground storage. Please help them pinpoint the energy reaction coordinates of the ducks' mecha armor!

??: Hehe, silly Chief! I knew you couldn't do anything without me! And look how pathetic this Syndicate's disgrace has become now!

Chief: Hella? They sent you?

EMP: Damn it, have I really fallen so low that I must rely on Minos's trash to save me?! Chief, can't you ask for someone else?!

Hella: Hey! I'm literally risking my life to come and check on you guys. You better appreciate it! Don't forget to thank me when you get out!
