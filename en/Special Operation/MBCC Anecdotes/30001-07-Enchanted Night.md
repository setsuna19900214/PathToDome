## SID-701 Candy Emergency

Chief's Office

Dudu: BOOOSS...

You close your weary and sore eyes, rubbing them to avoid the longing gaze from across the desk.

Dudu: BOOOSS... Oh, pretty please, give it another thought, won't you? Why rush to say no so soon?

Apparently, your effort proves futile. You sigh and look again at the blueprint that Dudu claimed to have meticulously drawn throughout the night. It resembles a peculiar house, with skewed lines and revised numbers filling the page...

Dudu: You see, I just need... eh, seven... 720 more pieces of candy to bring this candy house blueprint to life! Pleeeeeeaaaase~~~

Dudu: Hehe, once you approve it, I'll swiftly put in a request to the Supply Office! The house will be standing by tonight!

**【Choose One】**

- This many?

- Building a house within a house?

**end**

Chief: Hold your horses... Let's not even talk about whether Faye can get hold of that many pieces of candy in one go...

Chief: MBCC probably doesn't have the space for such a big candy house.

Dudu: Wait, you think this is big, boss? It's not big at all!

Chief: That's beside the point... What I'm wondering is, why the sudden interest in a candy house?

Dudu: Well, just think about it for a second—a grand candy house at MBCC! How stunning would that be? Plus, I'll deck it out with colorful lights and bells...

Dudu: Whenever you swing open that door, hehe, the sweet candy house will definitely lift your spirits and send you off to dreamland on a high note!

"Sweet candy house," "colorful lights," "bells"... You suddenly recall walking past Dudu's room last night, when you overheard Mess and Etti enthusiastically recounting a story from a picture book to Dudu.

She must have created that blueprint based on the picture book—upon this realization, you break into a smile.

Dudu: Hehe, I've noticed how hard you've been working lately, so I thought I could help you relax a bit.

Dudu: According to the picture book, you can sleep soundly and have the sweetest dreams in a candy house... It's the best idea I could come up with...

With your hectic schedule these days, you have indeed been continuously on the go for many days, severely sleep-deprived and never getting proper rest. It appears that Dudu has been closely observing your state.

Dudu: Besides, you played with Mess and Etti for a long time last year. This year, I just have this tiny little request, and you can't say yes?

She can't contain her excitement, leaning closer to you. You reach out with a smile... and give her forehead a gentle flick.

Dudu: Ouch! Are you really gonna say no?!

Chief: I appreciate the thought... but about the candy house, I'll have to say no. Those picture book tales all tend to exaggerate things...

Chief: But if you want, I can buy some sweets, and we could all enjoy them together, just to unwind.

Chief: ...How about this? I still have a few documents to take care of. Once I'm done, we can head out together to get them. How does that sound?

Your indirect comfort evidently fails to dampen Dudu's enthusiasm.

Dudu: No, I'll figure out a way! Wait for me, boss!

Dudu gives you a resolute look before turning to dart away.

In the distance, you hear a barrage of suona sounds resounding intensely in the corridor, as if the musician is fervently encouraging herself.

The ceiling light in your office suddenly flickers, and you feel a growing sense of suspicion, unsure of what intentions she might have in mind.

At night...

A brief, crisp sound of bells softly rings in your ears, coaxing you to open your eyes.

Chief: ...

Uncertain if it's a dream, you find yourself enveloped by mist.

The ground is carpeted with thorns, and in the dense forest ahead, creatures appear to be stirring. Night owls and bats with glowing eyes lurk in the darkness, signaling an impending sense of danger.

Raising your gaze, you notice a blood moon hanging above the highest branch. A person sits there, her form obscured by the thick mist. Swinging her legs, she seems to be in a cheerful mood.

???: Hey, bo—oh, wandering servant, are you the gift those villagers sent my way?

**【Advance】**

- ...Dudu?

**end**

Dudu: It's the Great Witch. Calling me by my name is so not cool...

Dudu: Ahem, moving on, tonight marks the blood moon, and the villagers living below the mountain should be sending an innocent servant to serve me, the Great Witch. So, looks like you're the lucky one, ${PlayerName}!

Chief: Witch, villagers, servant... What are you talking about, and where am I?

Dudu: Oh, a servant full of questions, I see.

Dudu, or rather, the Great Witch, grins mischievously, lifts the suona in her hand, and begins playing a little tune to herself.

Chief: ...Does a witch also play suona?

The discordant melody sounds strangely familiar to you. Before you can think further, the thorns beneath your feet begin to wildly grow, and a white floating figure suddenly glides past in the depths of the forest. You cautiously step back.

Dudu: Scared?

Dudu: Don't fret. I'm quite nice to my servants. The tasks I'll need your help with are few, in fact, just one...

She blinks, her eyes gleaming with mischief.

Dudu: I need you to collect a basket full of candy, so much that it's almost overflowing—!

Dudu: Then, we'll build a fragrant, sweet, and golden candy house!

**【Advance】**

- Hmm...

**end**

Chief: Why does Dudu want to build a candy house so badly... Wasn't she supposed to help me relax? Collecting candy sounds like a lot of work...

In a heartbeat, all the thorns and the white floating figure surge towards you.

Chief: ——!!!

Dudu: Oh, by the way, my little pet loves candy too, so you better not let it catch you, or all your efforts will be in vain...

Dudu: You've got this! My candy house is all on you!

## SID-706 Good Dream Candy House

MBCC Chief's Office

Your aching body wakes you up, and in your grogginess, you seem to really catch sight of a "candy house."

Dudu: Boss! You're awake!

Chief: ...Dudu?

Dudu: Well, I don't know what you dreamt about last night, but you kept kicking the blanket, so I had to stay up all night watching over you.

Dudu: But that's not important. Look over here, boss! Here!

Dudu, with dark circles under her eyes from another sleepless night, gestures towards something...

It's a candy house ornament on the table, decorated with colorful pieces of candy that appear dazzling under the glow of the vibrant lights.

**【Advance】**

- This is...

**end**

Dudu claps her hands, a hint of pride in her tone.

Dudu: This is the candy house I promised to make for you! What do you think? Isn't it just so impressive and gorgeous?

Chief: Eh...? Didn't you say you needed 720 pieces of candy to build a super big candy house?

Dudu appears momentarily perplexed, and you both exchange glances.

After a brief moment of silence, Dudu suddenly comprehends the situation and then appears slightly embarrassed, scratching her head.

Dudu: W-well... I must have made a mistake after all those revisions. The paper was a mess, and I might have wrote an extra zero...

Dudu: No wonder you said no! Hahaha...

**【Advance】**

- ——!!!

**end**

Chief: ...Now, it all makes sense.

You gently pick up the candy house, glued together in a haphazard manner. Despite the rough craftsmanship, Dudu has meticulously carved out the doors, windows, and chimney with a small knife.

Chief: Thank you, Dudu.

Chief: I love it.

Dudu: Boss, check this!

As Dudu opens the door to the candy house, the bell on it jingles.

Dudu: It's all made of candy! I wanted to make it feel like a real home...

You see a tiny Dudu witch in a hood, a little white ghost, and a little you inside the candy house.

...Isn't this the dream from last night?

Chief: Did you do all of this last night after I fell asleep? Right here?

Dudu: Yeah. What do you think? Aren't they so cute?

Dudu: These are characters from the picture book, like the witch and the little ghost... I designed the witch to resemble myself, hehe... Doesn't it look very like me?

...You must have glimpsed the little ornaments before fully falling asleep and dreamt about them... You smile, recalling the peculiar dream.

Dudu continues to talk excitedly.

Dudu: So, boss, whenever you open the door of the candy house and see us, it'll lift your spirits.

Dudu: Speaking of which, did you have a good dream last night?

**【Choose One】**

- Yes, I did.

- I can't remember, but I'm still tired.

**end**

Chief: Uh... yes... and no...

Chief: Actually, I guess I did.

Chief: But I thought you didn't have any candy. Where did you get these from?

Dudu seems to be dodging your gaze.

Dudu: Um... Well...

Suddenly, a commotion erupts from the end of the corridor, with one voice audibly gritting her teeth, closely followed by another voice.

Mess: Where is the candy I saved from last year...? Did any of you see my candy?

Etti: Wait, mine is gone, too!

Dudu: Ahaha, boss, I got a thing to do somewhere else, so I'll be off!

She scurries out of your room. You gaze down at the candy house, its colorful lights casting a soft glow on your face. The sweet scent wafts through the room, and despite the candy showing signs of melting, you feel reluctant to put it down.

Dudu doesn't go far before poking her head back in through the open door, beaming at you.

Dudu: By the way...!

Dudu: Boss, not just tonight, but I hope you have sweet dreams every night from now on!
