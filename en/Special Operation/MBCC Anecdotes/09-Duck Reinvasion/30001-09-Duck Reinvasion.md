# MBCC Anecdotes｜Duck Reinvasion

## SID-1001 Duck Reinvasion I

One night in the Bureau's corridors, MBCC

After a long day, you walk down the hallway back to your office. You let out a long sigh as you flip through a new schedule you just received.

Chief: Great, another week without weekends. Why do these tedious meetings always have to be scheduled during off hours...

Suddenly, a loud crash echoes from the end of the corridor, and the lighting system instantly fails. Emergency red lights on the walls flash incessantly, and the Bureau's alarm system immediately blares to life.

Announcement: Warning! Warning! Attack from an abnormal source detected in the Bureau's underground area! Relevant department personnel, proceed to the area immediately!!

Chief: Well... here we go again...

Just then, a familiar figure grows larger in your field of vision, stumbling and panicking as they frantically rush toward you.

EMP: Chief! Help me, something terrible has happened!!

Chief: What did you do this time?

EMP: No, no! This time it really isn't my fault! Eek—!

Before the words leave her lips, an electric arrow grazes over both your heads and strikes the flashing warning light with pinpoint accuracy.

The attacker's burly, rotund silhouette emerges from the darkness, gradually approaching you. In the chaos, you notice that it just used EMP's ability.

Sovereign Duck: Quack quack quack quack, quack, quack quack quack quack... (The one-year term is up... I am no longer who I once was...)

Chief: ?

Chief: What happened?

EMP: I-I-I don't know either! I just saw the ducks looking a bit down after finishing their work in the Depot, so I shared a novel I'd just bought with them. I had no idea they'd turn out like this after reading it!

EMP: It even copied my Syndicate the Grim Reaper ultimate technique... Chief, you don't think that novel could actually be some kind of martial arts manual, do you? I didn't even have the chance to read it myself!

Sovereign Duck: Quack! Quack quack quack quack! (Today! I will reclaim everything that belongs to me!)

Strange cards flutter around the duck, and with each one it throws, it unleashes a Sinner's ability—blazing flames, ice crystals, butterflies, and mushrooms appearing one after another in endless succession.

The confrontation reaches a temporary stalemate. If the duck gains the upper hand before reinforcements arrive, the Bureau's operations could be disrupted for days to come.

Chief: The duck clearly came prepared. The two of us probably can't hold it off!

EMP: Huh? Really? I feel like I can still give it a little more effort...

Chief: I'll hold it off by myself. EMP, you go find Nightingale first. Once we've restricted its range of movement, we can take our time and carefully plan our counterattack.

EMP: What are you talking about, Chief! I won't just ditch you like that. I can still fight. If you still insist on making me leave...

Her voice sounds like it's coming from somewhere far behind you. When you look back, EMP, who was fighting alongside you just moments ago, has already sprinted dozens of meters away without looking back.

EMP: I'm going, Chief! Just hang in there—I'll bring help and come back for you!

Chief: ...

After sending EMP away, you turn to face Sovereign Duck once more. Accompanied by the sounds of cards brushing against each other, it pulls out another thick stack of cards and flashes you a crooked smile.

Sovereign Duck: Quack, quack quack quack quack? Quack quack, quack quack quack quack. (Heh, playing hard to get? Foul humans, you shall not escape!)

## SID-1002 Duck Reinvasion II

Bureau Confinement Room

When you regain consciousness, you find your hands tied behind your back and yourself locked in the confinement room. There are no guards outside the door, but you can faintly hear the sounds of the duck patrolling in the distance.

The knots aren't secure. You break free from your restraints and take out your communication device, which already has its positioning activated.

Chief: Nightingale, can you hear me?

Nightingale: I can hear you clearly! I've already sealed off the area passages within the Bureau based on the message you had EMP bring back, and I can also track your location in real time. Chief, are you alright over there?

Chief: Don't worry, I'm fine. Have you figured out why the ducks launched this attack?

Nightingale: We tried negotiating with them, but they keep repeating something like "The world belongs to the ducks" and "All hail the return of the Duck King." Normal communication is impossible.

Nightingale: Moreover, based on the current intelligence we have, the enemy appears to have mastered some kind of technology for stealing Sinners' abilities. We currently can't completely rule out the risk of Mania contamination, so we can't act rashly.

Chief: It's right to be cautious. After all, we've never faced anything like this before. Taking more time to investigate and playing it safe is wise.

Chief: Before the incident, did the ducks come into contact with anything, or were there any abnormalities?

Nightingale: Surveillance shows that before they encountered EMP, they had been helping organize and repair some instant imaging equipment in the Depot.

You recall the recent battle with the Sovereign Duck. The cards it threw out each time when using its abilities closely resembled photo paper in both size and material.

Chief: If that equipment had been contaminated by Mania, then photos taken of Sinners might just produce the effect of copying their special powers.

Chief: Although this is still just a hypothesis, if it's true, we can avoid the risks of direct combat. As long as we slowly exhaust the ducks' photo papers, we'll be able to restrict their actions.

Nightingale: Understood. I'll dispatch Sinners to support you based on your movement route. Please make sure to stay safe.

EMP: Chief! The legendary Minos Goddess of War EMP is back to save you! Were you so scared you started crying earlier?

K.K.: Stop bragging already. Just now, I wonder who was hiding in the back.

EMP: I-I-I-I was doing ranged DPS... I rarely get to look cool, so don't call me out!

Nightingale: Chief, I'm detecting that you've successfully rendezvoused with two Sinners.

The duck squad here has fallen unconscious from your successful ambush. As you listen in on their communications, you rummage through their pockets—but to no avail.

Nightingale: For your safety, I'll plan the shortest route for you to escape the encirclement right now.

Chief: No need. The priority is to confiscate those dangerous items that enable the ducks to use Sinners' abilities.

Nightingale: But, Chief...

Chief: Just send me the locations of the nearby ducks for now.

Nightingale: Understood. I'll act according to your judgment.

## SID-1003 Duck Reinvasion III

After a fierce fight, the ducks' offense gradually fades. You rush to their retreat and find both used and unused photos left behind.

K.K.: So these are the weapons they use for attacks? They seem disposable. Each photo can only activate an ability once.

EMP: But seeing my signature move perfectly replicated by these ducks is still really weird! It's giving me some serious chills!

EMP: And why do they have so many photos of Sinners?! Look—There's tons of Chief too!

K.K.: Chief's photos aren't that surprising though, after all, they are in short supply in the...

Chief: ...Hm? What did you just say?

K.K.: Ahem... I mean, there are quite a few shortcuts in the Bureau. Wouldn't taking this path here save us some time?

K.K.: Looks like Chief was right. Once the ducks use up all these photos, they won't be able to use Sinners' special powers anymore.

EMP: Hey hey hey, don't jinx it! You never know if the final boss might pull out some cheat ability or ultimate attack!

## SID-1005 Duck Reinvasion V

Though it took quite some time, by using coordinated tactics, you successfully subdued most of the ducks, and now you finally stand before the last enemy duck.

Sovereign Duck: Quack quack, quack quack quack quack, quack quack quack quack quack! (Finally, you have come! The ultimate battle between ducks and humans was inevitable!)

Sovereign Duck: Quack quack quack quack quack—quack quack quack quack quack quack! (I was reborn and returned here! After enduring countless hardships to reach this place, I declare to the Bureau—the Ducks will decide their own fate from now on!)

Chief: Actually, I've been wanting to ask this for a while... EMP, what novels did you show them?

EMP: Ah, you know those novels with tons of tags and ridiculously long titles that go on forever? So it really is true—the longer the title, the stronger the power?

A quack of absolute fury interrupts your conversation. The duck facing you, seething with rage, pulls out its remaining photos and fans them out menacingly.

Sovereign Duck: Quack quack quack quack! Quack quack quack quack quack quack! (Don't underestimate us Ducks! I'll show you right now that heaven's mandate belongs to Ducks!!!)

Sovereign Duck: Quack quack quack quack, quack quack quack! (My turn, I play my cards!)

Sovereign Duck: Quack... Quack quack quack quack quack quack quack... (No... were all the things in the books really just lies...?)

*After the situation was resolved, the Bureau conducted tests on the confiscated instant cameras and photos, confirming the presence of trace amounts of Mania elements in them. The ducks were already mentally unstable after working so much, and this became the final straw that pushed them over the edge.*

Nightingale: We've already performed special cleaning on the problematic cameras and photos. However, during the final recovery and inventory, staff reported that far fewer photos were recovered than initially recorded—and the vast majority of the missing ones are yours, Chief...

Chief: Go ask K.K. —she might have some idea where the photos ended up.

Chief: Most importantly, we need to keep stepping up our Mania detection, and also make sure we're managing the ducks' workload properly. Plus, we can't let up on reviewing the content of the books we purchase.

Chief: I really didn't expect this incident would take up so much time. Looks like I'll have to miss those social obligations originally scheduled for this weekend.

Nightingale: ...

Nightingale stands to the side, holding a stack of documents, watching you with something clearly on her mind but hesitating to speak. Her gaze makes you feel a bit guilty—you can't shake the feeling that your dear adjutant has seen right through your attempts to avoid work ever since the duck attack began.
