The dark clouds have dissipated completely. The Furorians pin the broken insignia back onto Hypatia's chest. It's no longer pristine and perfect, yet somehow it's reflecting a light more brilliant than ever before.

Everyone is talking and laughing. The sandy wind shrouding Furor has finally disappeared, and sunlight streams down from above, illuminating the land.

Tomorrow will be a new day.

Hella scans the area, eventually spotting Hypatia in a quiet corner away from the crowd.

Hella: Hey! What's the great hero doing, spacing out here?

Hypatia: ...It's nothing. Maybe I'm just overthinking things.

Hypatia is gazing at the recent battlefield. The ground has been blasted open into a crater that leads straight underground, and the once-sophisticated Aurum Secretum furnace now lies destroyed, tilting among the ruins.

Hypatia: The crisis is over, but we can't linger in Furor. Don't put too much trust in Synex. She may have helped us then, but she could still rat on what happened here. We should leave as soon as possible...

Synex: Mmm-hmm, that's right, no thanks to the anonymous benefactor.

Hella & Hypatia: !!

Synex: What, after everything I've done, am I still not welcome here? That stings!

Synex: I was hoping to catch an informant while I was at it, but unfortunately she got away. Anyway, I heard our permanently poker-faced Hypatia is finally starting to look a bit more like a living young girl... Can't miss the chance to witness that kind of wonder, can I?

Synex: And you, clever little thing from DisCity...

Synex turns her gaze toward Hella, her eyes gleaming with heightened interest.

Synex: So you're Hella? I thought you were just some fragile dummy, but you've got more wits than I gave you credit for. Strays are always welcome at my place, and I treat my pets very well. Consider the offer, will ya?

Hella: ??? Ugh, no, take that offer back please. Sounds super dodgy!

Synex bursts into laughter. Then suddenly, she looks up into the distance, her smile turning enigmatic.

Synex: Oh my... Looks like I won't be the only one making offers... Lucky you, little Hella.

Hella: Eww! Stop with the cryptic nonsense, you weirdo.

Hella isn't done calling her out yet, but a sudden commotion erupts among the crowd nearby. The Furorians grow restless, gathering with mixed expressions of joy and shock around something—exactly in the direction Synex has been looking.

Someone is arriving? Hella stands on tiptoes trying to sneak a peek, but the crowd blocks her view of whatever is causing the commotion. She can only hear the person's voice.

???: You've protected the research, saved the people of Furor, and upheld your honor... You've grown so much. The Campbell family will be proud of you.

Someone is approaching. Hella feels Hypatia's hand suddenly tighten around hers. Hypatia's voice trembles, her fingertips ice-cold.

Hypatia: Didn't you say... you were heading to DisCity?

???: Indeed, I've received an order from the city, on top of my many other official duties. But I still seized the chance to request half a day off to visit my baby sister while I'm passing by the area.

???: You've made a good friend from far away, Hypatia. I'm happy for you.

The man moves through the crowd and stops directly in front of the three of them.

Adelaide: Hello, Hella, and friends from the Legion. I am Adelaide Campbell, advisor to Fraser, nominal head of the Campbell family, and Hypatia's brother. My sister has been in your care, and I must express my deepest gratitude to you all.

Adelaide: Would our esteemed guests like to visit Fraser? I believe it was your intended destination all along...
