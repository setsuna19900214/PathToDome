This place seems to have drawn in all Eastsiders looking for some fresh thrills. Young students, elite professionals, and weary individuals like yourself stealing a respite from the daily grind.

Looking at the bustling crowd inside and outside the place, you suddenly get cold feet. Sure, getting out and relaxing is good for your mental and physical health, but you haven't seen this many people in so long that it triggers your anxiety, your introverted tendencies suddenly intensifying.
