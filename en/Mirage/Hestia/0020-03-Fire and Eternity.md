### Hestia 3.2-Conversation 1

The followers stop talking and silently march towards the village.

The night wind drowns the sounds of their movement as the girl stands alone on the hillside.

Soon, sounds of weapon clashing, gunfire, and screams echo through the air, gradually overpowering the howling wind.

### Hestia 3.2-Dialog 2

The girl takes a few steps forward, the sounds getting louder.

This place once provided her shelter. Despite having no good memories of this place, she still wants to witness everything.

### Hestia 3.2-Dialog 3

The girl continues walking.

Suddenly, she hears a faint, sharp sound that sounds like crying or wailing.

Amid the chaotic noises, that sound stands out distinctly.

### Hestia 3.2-Dialog 4

The girl has walked much further without realizing it.

She wants to capture that sound and feel that pain.

She thought she had gotten used to all this, to being a liar. She had deceived many people.

But she can't deceive herself—she wants to find the source of that sound.

But the sound becomes more distant and faint, as if it could be overwhelmed by the surge of battle cries at any moment.

The girl tries to catch up, moving closer to the center of the battle.

But the sound has already died out, like a flame in the middle of WhiteSands, fated to be engulfed by the darkness.

She has reached the center of the battlefield.
