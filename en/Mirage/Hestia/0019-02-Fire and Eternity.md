# Fire and Eternity

At first, fire is a scorching scar, a signal of danger, a source of suffering.

Fire and Eternity II

## AZ-10001 Fire and Eternity

### Hestia 2.1 - The Beginning

???: Today's prey is in this direction. Take the blind girl there.

???: Stop acting on your own, you hear?

Two thin, frail girls are trekking under the scorching sun of WhiteSands.

Tia: Come to think of it...

Tia climbs the hill, panting as she talks to her friend ahead.

Tia: We used to... wander around like this too...

Loye: ...Because the *adults* ordered us to.

### Hestia 2.1-Dialog 1

Tia: No... I mean before that.

Tia: Long before we met them...

Tia: Back then, no one was ordering us around... We ate whatever we found...

Tia: I couldn't see, so I couldn't steal anything, but everyone still shared food with me...

Tia: Sometimes Atto would find some storybooks, and we'd gather around the campfire while he read to us...

Tia: I still remember those stories...

Tia: Those were good times...

Loye: And then he stole something he shouldn't have, and we all got caught because we lived together.

Loye: It was Atto's fault, but everyone else had to suffer the consequences.

Tia: ...

### Hestia 2.1-Dialog 2

Tia: It smells rusty... Have we reached the old oil rig?

Loye: Mmm.

Tia: I still remember. This is where the *adults* found us, right?

Loye: ...

Tia: We had just escaped, and I was so hungry I couldn't walk... You carried me all the way here.

Tia: Without you, I would have died long ago...

Loye: It made no difference...

Loye: We just went from one hell to another.

### Hestia 2.1-Dialog 3

Tia: I wish my eyes could see...

Loye: ...You're not completely blind.

Tia: But I can't see beyond a meter.

Tia: If I could see... you wouldn't be so tired.

Loye: What does that have to do with me?

Tia: You wouldn't have to escort me.

Tia: You could stay in the village and face fewer dangers.

Loye: There's no danger.

Tia: ...

Tia: If you say so.

### Hestia 2.2-The Beginning

After who knows how long, the girl stands up.

There's no light, and the cold, dense darkness envelops her.

Tia: I don't want to die... I can't die here...

As she speaks, it feels as if the darkness is a hand gripping her throat, suffocating her.

A pang of fear spreads in her chest.

She must run. She doesn't know where to go, but she must run.

She must not let the darkness catch her.

### Hestia 2.2-Light 1

Tia gropes her way through the dark desert, the smell of charcoal filling her nose.

The scent leads her to a campfire that's about to go out. Unfortunately, the people here have long since left.

Tia gathers some dry branches and adds them to the campfire to warm her freezing limbs.

### Hestia 2.2-Light 2

She walks into a dilapidated camp, slowly touching everything, hoping to find something useful.

There's nothing but a very short piece of candle.

She finds a few remaining matches and lights up the small piece of candle.

### Hestia 2.2-Light 3

A jeep covered in thick dust sits on the side, its bumper crushed and the trunk ruined. But the car key is still in the ignition.

Remembering what the *adults* do, she slowly turns the car key.

The vehicle shows no signs of starting, but one of the headlights has flickered on.

### Hestia 2.2-Light 4

A few fireflies dance like twinkling stars under the night sky.

Desert plants seem hazy and mysterious under the glowing lights. Tia reaches out to touch them.

As she reaches out, the lights scatter, illuminating the surrounding darkness.

### Hestia 2.1-The End

As night falls, the temperature in WhiteSands goes down.

Tia rubs her exposed arms.

Tia: Loye... The prey is really far this time.

Tia: How long have we been walking? It feels like it's already dark.

Loye: Yes... It's dark, and we've walked so far.

Loye: If I leave you here, you can't possibly find your way back.

Loye: After all, you're just a blind girl who can't do anything without me.

Tia: What... what are you talking about, Loye...

Tia: I don't understand...

Loye: You don't? I'll say it again.

Loye: I'm going to leave you here and go back alone.

Tia: What did I do wrong? Did I upset you? I can change...

Loye: You did nothing wrong.

Tia: Then why... We're... we're...

Loye: Friends.

Tia: Friends should take care of each other, support each other. Haven't we always done that?

Loye: Yes.

Loye: So friends are "good people," right?

Tia: ...

Loye: You said good people are at the mercy of others, only bad people can survive in this world.

Loye: I want to live, so I will be a bad person.

Loye: A bad person doesn't need friends.

Tia: But... but...

Loye: As long as you're here, I'll always be beneath you, a sidekick that can be tossed aside anytime.

Loye: If you're gone, the *adults* will treat me better because they have no other choice.

Loye: Do me a favor, Tia. Don't come back.

Loye shrugs off her hand, and Tia collapses to the ground, but Loye doesn't look back.

Tia: Don't leave me, please...

Loye: If you want to survive, become a bad person too.

Loye: You're smart. Be someone clever and bad, go trick and deceive others.

Loye: Isn't that what you've been doing? You're a natural liar, stop pretending to be a good person.

After saying this, the girl walks away, leaving her "friend" alone in the cold windy WhiteSands.

Amid the wind, and the sound of the girl's footsteps gradually fades.

Tia knows she has lost her forever, her only "friend."

### Hestia 2.2-The End

Tia wanders through the WhiteSands for a long, long time.

From dawn to dusk, from dusk to dawn.

Eventually, Tia loses track of how much time has passed.

But whether it is dawn or dusk, Tia can feel that darkness has its eyes on her.

Fear has become her last fuel. Tia runs desperately but can't shake it off.

In the final moments of this frightening memory, she sees a flickering light in the dark.
