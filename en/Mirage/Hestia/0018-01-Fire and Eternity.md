### Hestia 1 - Beginning

???: Ugh... Mmm... Argh...

When the girl returns to her familiar "home," she instinctively feels something is wrong.

The ground is wet and sticky, making a squelching sound with every step.

There is no laughter or arguments. The house is completely dark and unusually quiet, with only faint groans audible.

Tia: Atto... Luca... where are you?

Tia: Hey... don't scare me like this...

The girl's voice startles some *thing*. It crawls towards the girl, its painful groans growing more intense.

A sticky object clings to the girl's ankle, growling lowly.

???: Help... me...

In a corner of the vast WhiteSands, the girl wakes up from a nightmare.

After waking up, instead of staying put, she stands up and starts calling for help.

Tia: Is anybody there?

Tia: Dad, Mom, where are you...

Tia: Don't leave me here. I'm scared...

Her pupils are dull, and her figure is frail.

In the unforgiving environment of WhiteSands, this weak and harmless creature will undoubtedly be buried under the sands if she doesn't receive help soon.

Tia: Someone... help me...

The girl refuses to give up. Her will to survive seems boundless.

Despite her voice becoming hoarse, she continues to call for help.

???: ...

Chaotic footsteps slowly approach from behind the sand dunes, barely audible in the relentless wind of WhiteSands.

But the girl can hear them. Her sight loss otherwise grants her extraordinary hearing.

Tia: Anyone... help me, please.

They are a few weary men in tattered clothes. Severe malnutrition has left their eyes sunken and their faces gaunt—typical doomsday wanderers.

One of them instinctively reaches for the rusty machete at his waist upon hearing a stranger, but when he sees the girl calling for help, he warily looks at his companions.

Wanderer A: Is there anyone else nearby?

The men scan the area until they're sure there are no other living beings within a few hundred meters, and then they turn back to their companions.

Wanderer A: What do you think? Should we check it out?

Wanderer B: A blind kid alone in a place like this? Too suspicious.

Wanderer A: But there's no one else around... At the very least, we could see if she has food.

After a brief silence, the men finally walk towards the girl.

Tia: Is... is someone there?!

The girl's face lights up with joy as she stumbles towards the wanderers.

Wanderer A: ...

Wanderer A: What are you doing alone here, little girl?

Tia: There was a sandstorm. I got separated from my parents. I'm blind... so I couldn't find them.

Wanderer A: You poor thing... Where do you live?

Tia: To the south!

Seeing the girl so confident, one of the wanderers sneers.

Wanderer B: You can't see, how do you know which way is south?

Tia: By the sunlight.

Tia: I just can't see, but I can still feel.

The others snicker, but the man doesn't take any offense.

Wanderer B: That's tricky. We're heading northwest, and our supplies are low. I'm afraid we can't help you.

Tia: We have supplies in our village!

The girl grows visibly anxious.

Tia: Our village doesn't have many people, but we have plenty of food and water.

Tia: If you can take me back, my parents will surely treat you well, sir.

Wanderer B: ...

Wanderer A: We have to talk about this first.

The men walk away a little and start talking in hushed voices. But of course, the girl can still hear them.

Wanderer B: It could be a trap... Have you heard of any village to the south in this area?

Wanderer C: I have, but it's hard to find...

Wanderer C: Do we even have to think about this? Just grab whatever she has and let's move on.

Wanderer A: Come on, it's not every day we get to see bread and hot soup. I don't want to miss it.

Wanderer A: A secluded village is actually better. The easy ones have already been ransacked by bandits.

Wanderer A: Stor Town is at least a hundred kilometers from here. What if her village really has some supplies, huh?

Wanderer B: ...

Wanderer B: Fine, let's take her there.

The man touches the blade at his waist and lowers his voice even more.

Wanderer B: Hmph, we'll see if those villagers are smart enough. Better they are, but if not...

Wanderer C: If they're not, we'll just take what we need ourselves.

The men reach a consensus and put on a smile as they walk back to the girl.

Even though the girl can't see their smiles.

Wanderer A: Tell me, how do we find your village?

Tia: Follow the ridge of the highest mountain and head south. You'll see an abandoned oil rig.

Tia: Walk five more kilometers in the direction of the fallen tower, and you'll reach the village.

### Hestia 1-Dialog 1

Loye: Rest for a bit, sir. I'll make you some tea.

In the room, the wanderers sit around the table.

After the two girls leave the room, the men's expressions grow serious.

Wanderer B: Something's off.

Wanderer C: What?

He asks, casually picking up the knife on the table and putting it into his bag.

Wanderer B: This village is too wealthy.

Wanderer C: What's wrong with being wealthy? They might reward us well.

Wanderer B: How can they be rich in times like these?

Wanderer B: Even Stor Town doesn't have this much food and such big houses.

Wanderer B: This is just a small village. How many people do they have? How many guns? How can they hold all this?

Wanderer C: That's true.

He nods as he shoves the gilded oil lamp on the table into his bag.

Wanderer A: So you're saying...

Wanderer B: Before the *adults* come back, take as much as we can and leave...

Tia: Sir!

A sudden voice interrupts their conversation.

The wanderers turn together to the blind girl at the door.

Wanderer A: What is it, little Tia?

Tia: Um...

Tia: Can I trade with you?

Tia takes out a shiny kukri.

Wanderer B: Trade... Where did you learn about that?

Tia: Our village has a trading post to the south. The adults make a living by trading.

Wanderer B: What do you want in exchange?

The man stares intently at the kukri in Tia's hand, unconsciously swallowing. It's much better than the weapon he has.

Tia: Do you have canned food?

Wanderer B: Canned food?

The man looks around.

Wanderer B: Surely you have a lot of those here.

Tia: But my parents don't let us eat them. They say we are saving those for hard times.

Tia: I want Loye to try it. She's never had one before.

Wanderer A: What a good girl...

The man opens his bag, rummages for a moment, and takes out a can of syrup.

The girl takes the can, her fingers caressing the surface as her face lights up with joy.

The man takes the kukri from the girl and hands it to his companion.

Wanderer B: Whatever.

Wanderer A: Let's see what the *adults* are like. If they seem...

Loye: Tia, let me through!

Another girl stumbles into the room, carrying a tray too large for her size.

Loye places three cups of tea on the table and picks up a dark, unidentifiable object from the tray before walking away from the table.

Loye: I'll cut the bread. Have some tea first, sir.

She picks up a handsaw from the floor and starts sawing the unidentifiable object.

The wanderers glance at the murky liquid in their cups. One of them drinks it down without hesitation. Another hesitates for a moment before taking a small sip.

The man sitting in the center watches everything coldly without saying a word.

Wanderer C: Have a drink?

The wanderer glances at him, but he shows no reaction, so the wanderer grabs the cup and finishes the tea.

Wanderer C: It's quite good, actually... kind of sweet...

After two cups of tea, the man feels his eyelids grow heavy.

Wanderer C: This is... weird... Am I... too... tired...

Meanwhile, Loye continues sawing the bread, seemingly oblivious to the situation.

Wanderer B: !

The man's gazes turn dangerous in an instant. He grips the kukri that they just got from the "trade" and swings at Loye.

But he misses. Somehow, the blind girl senses the wanderer's move and pushes her friend out of the way.

Wanderer A: You brat! How dare you...

The other sober wanderer finally realizes what's happening. He jumps up and grabs the girl lying on the ground.

Bang!

A gunshot rings out from somewhere, and the man's head explodes like a watermelon, splattering the girl.

Wanderer B: !

The last wanderer quickly sizes up the situation. He snatches the overstuffed bag from his drugged companion and bolts for the door.

???: Ha, quick thinker.

The *adult* in the village has finally returned.

He leisurely takes the final drag of his cigarette, flicks the butt to the ground, and stomps it out before raising his hunting rifle.

Bang—

Smoke curls from the gun barrel as the wanderer in the distance collapses to the ground.

???: Hmph.

### Hestia 1 - The End

The *adults* just finished a "hunt" and are celebrating in the room.

Time flies. Outside the room, the two girls huddle together under the night sky, sharing their dinner—a piece of dark, rock-hard bread.

Loye: ...Does it still hurt?

Tia: It's okay.

The bandage on the girl's arm is crooked, and the wound is still bleeding. It doesn't look "okay."

Sensing the silence of her companion, Tia takes out a small metal can from her pocket.

Tia: Look what I have here!

Loye: This...

Tia: Keep it down... Can't let the adults find out.

Tia lowers her voice.

Tia: Good stuff should be shared with friends, right?

The two girls pry open the can. The overly sweet syrup melts on their tongues, making the hard bread easier to swallow.

Tia moves closer to her friend, who's struggling with every bite because of her swollen cheek.

Tia: Listen...

Tia: You don't need to take their words to heart.

Tia: See, I'm just a blind girl. Without you, I wouldn't even know which way to go. I'd die in WhiteSands in less than a day.

Tia: If it weren't for you, I wouldn't have escaped back then. Now it's just the two of us. No matter what happens, we are a team.

Tia: No one can replace you...

Loye: ...Really?

Tia: Of course, I wouldn't lie to you.

Loye: You also told Atto you wouldn't lie to him, but in the end, you ran away anyway.

Tia: ...

Her friend's words leave Tia speechless. The air falls into silence.

Loye looks at the can of syrup and the black bread in her hands, then glances at the room where the *adults* are reveling.

After a while, she says softly:

Loye: I wish there were gods in this world.

Tia: ...Gods. Even if they exist, they must be mean and cruel.

Tia: Good people like Atto and Luca died for nothing... but these bad people can live comfortably.

Tia: Alas... Life's so unfair.

Tia sighs, unaware of her friend's gleaming eyes.

Loye: Bad people... huh...
