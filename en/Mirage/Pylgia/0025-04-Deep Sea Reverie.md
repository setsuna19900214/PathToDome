## AZ-10001 Deep Sea Reverie

The ocean is a casket that buries secrets, the paradise lost where she slumbers. All sorrows eventually fade into the fathomless depths.

### Diver 4-Starting point

In the early morning, her parents are occupied loading the final few items into the car, its trunk already packed full with daily necessities and simple furniture.

At last, they have decided to depart from this small town that once held their entire lives, heading inland. Despite the uncertainty that lies ahead, it's better than staying in this increasingly desolate place.

Mother: Py? Py?

With everything packed, the woman goes to call her daughter to the car, only to find the house completely empty.

Pylgia now stands at the highest point of the breakwater, waves crashing against the rocks below her feet with thunderous roars.

A damp chill hangs in the air, heralding an approaching storm.

Yet Pylgia's heart remains perfectly calm as she gazes at the familiar sea before her, a profound emotion welling up in her eyes.

She closes her eyes gently. The whispers of the waves echo in her ears, resembling the beckoning calls of her friends beneath the surface.

Pylgia: I'm coming back.

She whispers softly, as if answering those long-awaited voices.

Without a moment's hesitation, Pylgia leaps from the highest point of the breakwater into the cold embrace of the sea.

### Diver 4-Dialogue 1

Crab: Hey! You're back!

A crab excitedly emerges from beside the coral, waving its tiny claws in greeting. Its shell reflects a faint glimmer in the water—a sign of its advanced age.

Pylgia: How... have you been lately?

Crab: Haha, not too great, to be honest... The sea has been wild and stormy lately. But now that you're back... at least I'll have someone to talk to, making these last few days of mine a bit brighter.

### Diver 4-Dialogue 2

The true danger lies hidden from view. As she swims toward a deeper patch of coral, the water suddenly surges. A forceful current sweeps through with devastating force.

Her body is abruptly seized by a violent force that nearly throws her off balance.

She flails her arms desperately, fighting to stay steady, but the current—like a massive invisible hand—keeps dragging her relentlessly towards an unknown oceanic abyss.

Squid: Quick, grab onto me!

A squid appears in her view, its tentacles swiftly extending—one firmly anchoring itself to a reef, while another reaches out toward her.

Pylgia quickly reaches out, gripping the squid's tentacle tightly. With its help, she manages to steady herself, finally breaking free from the powerful current.

The squid cautiously pulls her to safety, finding shelter in a pocket of calmer waters.

Squid: You haven't changed.

Squid: That question from before... Do you have a new answer now?

Pylgia freezes for a moment—years of life on land have long since washed that question from her memory.

Squid: Haha... Don't worry about it. Now that you're back, you can take your time finding the answer.

Pylgia: Yeah...

### Diver 4-Dialogue 3

As she descends deeper, the waters grow darker, and the surrounding seascape starts to take on a mystical and otherworldly quality.

Suddenly, a massive deep-sea fish emerges from a nearby crevice, circling around Pylgia.

Grumpy Fish: It's you...

Grumpy Fish: Since you left, I haven't had treats as delicious as yours. You'll have to make it up to me!

Pylgia nods and once again takes out her metal box. This time, however, only a handful of bait remains inside.

Grumpy Fish: What? That's all...? Oh well, forget it! If you're coming back, treats will be a daily thing! Who cares if there are fewer today!

The deep-sea fish darts away from her side, vanishing just as swiftly as it did on the day they first met.

### Diver 4-Dialogue 4

As she enters the water, surging waves and rolling currents carry her further out to sea.

Pylgia rides the currents with natural grace, deftly avoiding the sharp rocks that loom like hidden traps in the darkness.

Yet Pylgia knows these dangers all too well—she glides between the obstacles with the grace of a nimble fish in its native environment.

### Diver 4-Dialogue 4

She finally returns to this familiar place, where every detail remains as vivid as in her memories.

But this time, there is no lost fishing rod, no yellow croaker tangled in a fishing line.

Everything feels familiar, yet everything feels strange—all that the sea brings to life, it also takes away.

Pylgia: Are you... still here?

The sea remains silent, with only the soft whispers of the currents flowing past her ears.

The coral reefs paint a brilliant, otherworldly landscape. Yet, she knows she will never see it again. She is leaving, away from this bay forever.

Watching as she swims towards the surface, the sea creatures surge up around her, pleading for her to stay.

Crab: Py, we've been waiting for you to come back. Stay with us—this is your real home.

Grumpy Fish: That's right, stay with us!

Crab: Here you'll find no painful reality—you can stay with us forever, embarking on the most wonderful adventures.

The crab gives Pylgia a gentle nudge with its pincer.

For a moment, she is transported back to her childhood, when the sea was pristine and enchanting—a stark contrast to what it has become.

Together with these creatures, she had explored the mysterious depths of the sea, sharing countless magical adventures.

Just as this perfect moment seems to stretch endlessly, two familiar voices suddenly reach her ears.

Mother: Py! Py! We're here to find you! Please come out!

Father: Py, where are you?! Where are you?!! If there's anything on your mind... we can talk about it!

The voices from above snap her back to reality. She looks up, barely making out a weathered little boat on the surface, where her parents sway precariously in the storm, searching for her.

Her mother, always uneasy on the water, clings tightly to the boat's edge, while her father drives the rust-eaten vessel that he had to buy back just to search for her.

Though broken and weathered, everything above the surface is just as much a part of who she is.

Squid: Are you really leaving? Are you really abandoning this haven where you can seek refuge from everything? When life treats you unfairly, when you struggle to make a single friend... don't you yearn to come back here?

The squid's voice whispers near her ear as its tentacle gives Pylgia's arm a gentle touch.

For a hushed moment, she gazes at her beloved friends of the sea, who have kept her company all this time, her heart weighed down by the sorrow of parting.

Yet in the end, she nods.

Pylgia: Farewell.

### Diver 4-Dialogue 4

Soaked to the bone, Pylgia finds herself wrapped tightly in her parents' embrace, their bodies trembling with fear and concern.

Behind them, the wind and rain persist in their tumultuous symphony, while fierce waves relentlessly pound the shore, a testament to the lingering wrath of nature raging across the sea.

Yet in this moment, their embrace shields her from all the chaos of the world.

Her parents remain silent, holding her between them in a desperate embrace, as if loosening their grip for even a moment would let Pylgia vanish into the raging storm once more.

Mother: Sorry...

Her mother's voice drops to a whisper, her words barely making it past the lump in her throat.

Mother: We know you love the sea, and we know you want to go back...

Father: But... we can't... we just can't!

The man gently strokes Pylgia's hair, his touch trembling yet his gesture filled with warmth and care.

Father: We want to give you everything that's best for you, but... we just can't! As parents, we can't bear to see our child risk her life in the most dangerous place in the world.

As the boat slowly makes its way to shore, Pylgia closes her eyes.

### Diver 4-Dialogue 5

A strong current suddenly surges towards her. Pylgia quickly spins around, only to find a shark gliding past, mere inches away.

She takes refuge behind the nearby rocks to evade the shark's charges. After four or five failed attempts, the ocean's overlord finally loses interest.

### Diver 4-Dialogue 6

Terrifying roars echo from the depths as massive, unknown creatures emerge one after another.

Nevertheless, Pylgia displays no fear, steadfastly advancing towards the origin of these emerging beings.

Pylgia stands by the lake, seeking solace from the emptiness of the evening. She always attempts to envision it as the sea, but each time her feet touch the water, harsh reality crashes in with brutal clarity, shattering her illusions.

As the woman slowly raises her head, Pylgia's pupils contract sharply in recognition.

Her memories burst open like a floodgate, fragments of the past rushing back to her consciousness.

That storm-ravaged night, the research vessel striking the reef, the perilous voyage—everything comes rushing back with startling clarity.

Shin: It's me—I was part of the crew on that research vessel that almost went under. You saved us back then. I'm Shin.

Pylgia: I...

Pylgia's voice quivers—it has been ages since she willingly spoke to anyone.

Shin: Why did you give up the life you had?

Pylgia: I... I had no choice... The sea is too dangerous... I have to... stay on land.

Shin: Oh? Too dangerous? Then how did you manage to save us during that calamity and guide the research vessel safely to port?

She pauses, her gaze resolute and soul-piercing.

Shin: I need you, the finest diver in the world.

For years, she has not felt the touch of seawater; even the salty sea breeze exists solely in her dreams.

Pylgia: You... you must have the wrong person. I'm not...

Shin: We're planning to explore uncharted waters—a mysterious abyss teeming with secrets waiting to be uncovered.

Shin: We need the most skilled diver there is, and you're exactly who we've been looking for.

Pylgia: Don't you think... the sea is dangerous...?

Shin: I just recently found the answer to a question.

Pylgia: What question...?

Shin: What is hard to recover once it's lost?

Pylgia: It's time.

Shin: No, there's another answer to this question.

Shin: It's a chance to return to the sea. More than that—it's a chance to return to the real sea.

A long-lost spark ignites in Pylgia's eyes, as if something deep within her has finally awakened to life once more.
