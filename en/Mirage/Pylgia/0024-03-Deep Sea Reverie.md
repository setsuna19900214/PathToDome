## AZ-10001 Deep Sea Reverie

The ocean is a casket that buries secrets, the paradise lost where she slumbers. All sorrows eventually fade into the fathomless depths.

### Diver 3-Starting point

?? ??, N.F. 19

Port School

A few years have gone by, and Pylgia's life has undergone a complete change.

After that thrilling rescue, she yielded to her parents' wishes and never ventured into the sea again. That world that once gave her endless adventure and freedom now lies sealed away in her memories.

Though she still lives in the same seaside town, colors have long since faded from her life, leaving behind only monotony and gloom.

The townspeople, who once made their living from the sea, have now been forced by the calamities to seek their fortunes elsewhere.

Her father hasn't set sail for many years now, but whenever he gazes out at the vast ocean, his eyes betray a mix of melancholy and resignation.

Having sold his fishing boat a while back, he has steered clear of the water ever since. Meanwhile, her mother takes up any available part-time work at the town's modest restaurants, barely managing to support the family.

The weight of their circumstances bears down on the entire family, and Pylgia, too, has started to feel its gravity.

Days blend into months, then years. The curious little girl Pylgia once was has long since faded into a withdrawn teenager.

Every morning, she traces the same well-worn path through the familiar streets to the town school, and today is no different.

The morning air hangs heavy with moisture as dark clouds loom low on the horizon, harboring the promise of an approaching storm.

Pylgia walks with her head bowed, her steps weighed down, as if all meaning has been drained from the world around her.

The streets lie empty, the silence broken only by the distant cries of seabirds cutting through the heavy air.

Walking along the breakwater, she finds herself unconsciously stopping whenever she passes the harbor—once a hub of activity, now forsaken.

The fishing boats moored at the harbor have rusted beyond recognition, reduced to little more than abandoned hulks of metal.

The memories of fishing and sailing with her father feel like they belong to another lifetime, surviving only as fleeting fragments that drift through her mind.

Pylgia shakes her head to clear away these lingering thoughts, then pushes open the school gates.

### Diver 3-Dialogue 1

Teacher: Turn to page 78. Today we'll be covering Unit Three.

The scattered sounds of turning pages pierce Pylgia's ears, each rustle grating against her nerves.

And then, as if on cue, come the inevitable whispers.

Classmate A: Ugh, you know what? That Pylgia is such a weirdo!

Classmate B: Huh? What do you mean? She seems pretty normal to me?

Classmate A: She never talks to anyone! And when you try to talk to her, all she does is glare at you!

Classmate B: Seriously? She's that stuck up? Let's write something on her desk next time!

Teacher: You there, whispering in the back! Stand up and answer the question!

Caught by the teacher, the whispering students fall silent. Pylgia thinks she might finally get a moment of peace, unaware that her nightmare is only beginning.

Classmate A: Teacher, Pylgia was the one talking to us! Why don't you ask her first?

Teacher: Alright then. Pylgia can answer a question too. One question each—that's fair.

The two students turn around, their faces gleaming with triumphant malice, sending another wave of anxiety coursing through Pylgia's nerves.

### Diver 3-Dialogue 2

Pylgia rises to her feet yet stands frozen in place, the answer completely beyond her reach.

Teacher: Why aren't you answering? This is the easiest question I could ask.

Teacher: If you can't even handle this, you might be better off going back to middle school for more practice.

Classmate C: Teacher, she's just illiterate!

Classmate D: Haha... illiterate... hahaha!

Like ripples turning to waves, the jeering spreads through the classroom, swelling into thunderous roars that hammer against her skull with each cruel echo.

Pylgia raises her hand to wipe away the tears trickling from her eyes, yet they stubbornly persist, flowing unabated despite her efforts to dry them.

Classmate E: Look, she's crying! She's actually crying! Who knew she could even cry?!

As she looks down at her hands, a strange dizziness washes over her, and she feels the gentle flow of seawater slipping between her fingers.

She blinks rapidly and shakes her head, trying to clear her mind, but that strange sensation only grows stronger.

The students' voices fade into the muffled sounds of flowing water, and the classroom light grows dim. When Pylgia lifts her head to look at her classmates, their forms begin to shift before her eyes.

The girl in front of her, who had turned around to sneer with cruel delight, now appears in Pylgia's vision with fish fins sprouting from her back.

Her fingers are no longer human, having transformed into tentacles as soft and sinuous as eels.

Meanwhile, the girl sitting next to Pylgia has turned into a crab, her massive claws gesturing as she whispers with the others.

In an instant, Pylgia feels herself returning to the sea. She draws a deep breath and closes her eyes, letting the world around her fade away until there's nothing left but the warm embrace of the sea.

Gentle waves brush against her body as soft sand caresses her feet below. In the distance, vibrant coral reefs paint the seascape while seaweed sways gracefully with the current.

She sees those familiar faces once again—fish, crabs, and squids. Some of her friends playfully dance through the water while others watch her intently, as if welcoming her home.

Pylgia: Hello... everyone...

She imagines herself gliding through the endless ocean, completely free and unbound. The water pulses with life around her, washing away all her worries and fears. At last, a smile returns to her face.

However, reality shows no such mercy.

The dismissal bell pierces through the air like a blade, violently yanking Pylgia from her reverie. Her eyes snap open, only to meet the harsh, cold glare of classroom lights.

As her classmates pack their bags to leave, Pylgia's heart pounds in her chest, the dull echo of the bell still ringing in her ears.

She gazes down—the pen in her hand now feels as cold and alien as metal, a stark contrast to the warm embrace of her ocean reverie. Her breathing grows rapid as cold sweat breaks out across her palms.

The classroom settles back into its dull routine as the teacher's figure vanishes from the podium. Outside, the sky lingers in a murky grey, as if the sun may never shine through again.

On her way home from school, Pylgia finds herself on the breakwater again, but her feet suddenly refuse to move forward.

Waves lap softly against the rocks as a cool sea breeze brushes across her face.

She stands there, gazing out at the endless sea, her eyes piercing through the veil of time to days brimming with adventure and freedom. The sea still shimmers with that familiar blue hue, but she no longer belongs to its depths.

She loses track of time as she stands there, watching darkness creep across the sky. Heavy clouds hang low as the last glimmer of light fades from the sea's surface.

Mother: Py? Py?

...Until her mother's voice breaks through the silence from behind. Pylgia turns slowly to meet her worried gaze, but the void within her remains unfilled.

Mother: Let's go home now. It's getting dark.

Pylgia: Hmm...

Her mother steps forward, gently taking her hand. Pylgia nods mechanically, casting one last look at the sea before allowing herself to be led away from the breakwater.

### Diver 3-Dialogue 4

Mother: Have you made any friends at school?

Mother: I know it's not easy adjusting to a new environment. If anything's bothering you, I can talk to your teachers about it.

Pylgia: Um... I... I'm fine.

Though Pylgia's mind brims with unspoken words, all she can ever manage to say is "I'm fine."

Mother: Are you sure? Do let me know if anything's on your mind, okay?

The woman gazes at Pylgia's pale face, desperate to comfort her daughter but at a loss for how to reach her heart.

### Diver 3-Ending point

The dim light inside the house does little to dispel the heavy silence that hangs in the air.

Pylgia sits at the table, silently listening to her parents' hushed conversation in the kitchen.

Mother: If things go on like this, we'll starve to death.

Mother: People can't make a living through fishing anymore. Everyone's looking for ways to leave, and the town's becoming empty.

After hearing his wife's words, the man falls into a long silence before finally speaking.

Father: I've given it some thought. We have to move away.

Father: Move away, inland. At least there I might find some other way to make a living.

Pylgia's heart plummets as if struck by a heavy blow. She looks up, her eyes wide with disbelief.

Pylgia: Move... inland...?

She can barely comprehend her father's words. For Pylgia, moving isn't just about changing homes—it means forever leaving behind everything she's ever known...

The sea that lives in her memories, the sanctuary that belongs to her alone.

Father: Yes.

Father: Life won't be easy in the big cities inland either, but it's better than starving here. If we wait any longer, I won't be able to compete with younger workers anymore.

Pylgia: But... but didn't you say we could go back to the sea once the calamities have subsided?

Pylgia's voice quivers as she fights to suppress her emotions, desperately hoping for the answer she wants to hear.

Yet, her father can only shake his head in response, silently leaving the kitchen to begin packing the family's belongings.
