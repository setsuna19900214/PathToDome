Deep Sea Reverie I

## AZ-10001 Deep Sea Reverie

The ocean is a casket that buries secrets, the paradise lost where she slumbers. All sorrows eventually fade into the fathomless depths.

### Diver 1-Starting point

There is no suffocation, no panic—just an eternal descent.

The cold, dark waters drown out all sensations and emotions impartially.

?? ??, N.F. 14

Santis Port

The azure sky stretches endlessly overhead, with wisps of white clouds scattered across it like casual brushstrokes on a canvas.

Sunlight cascades from above, blanketing the entire sea surface. A fishing boat glides slowly across the glittering waters, its hull gently swaying with each wave, as if moving in rhythm with the ocean's breath.

A gentle, salt-tinged breeze caresses the passengers' cheeks as the boat's sail billows in the wind, resembling a white bird soaring between sea and sky.

??: Yawn—what a beautiful day!

The girl stretches her arms wide. Sitting beside her mother at the boat's edge, her hair dances in the sea breeze, occasionally brushing against her mother's shoulder.

Mother: Py, what would you like for dinner? How about some potatoes with the seaweed I pickled yesterday?

Girl: Hmm...

The girl furrows her brow, deep in thought about today's dinner choice. After a few seconds, she suddenly claps her hands as if struck by an idea.

Girl: How about... beef?

Mother: Beef?

Girl: Today my classmates were talking about eating "beef" before. I've never had any, so I kept quiet...

Girl: If I could try some, I'm sure I could join their conversations... and then I could go swimming in the sea with everyone...

The girl's eyes drift downward, a hint of sadness crossing her face.

Mother: Now, dear, you're such a sweet girl that everyone would be lining up just to talk to you, let alone go swimming with you! You see, beef is something quite rare around here—you can only get it in the satellite towns.

Girl: Hmm...

Rather than finding solace in her mother's words, the girl curls into herself even more. Her mother can only turn to tap the shoulder of the man sitting attentively at the boat's bow.

Mother: Don't you agree, darling? Our dinner tonight is all up to you.

Father: Huh? Oh... yes... Wait—! Look out!

The tap startles the man from his concentrated fishing at the bow. In that brief moment of distraction, a sudden gust of wind sweeps across the sea.

Abandoning his fishing rod, he quickly reefs the sail and adjusts its angle, bringing the boat back to stability in the wind.

Father: Is everyone all right?

Girl: I'm fine, but... your fishing rod...

The girl gestures towards the spot on the deck where the fishing rod had rested, now vacant.

Father: Don't worry about it. The safety of you two is what matters most. I can always get another finishing rod.

Girl: No! That was Mom's gift to you, Dad. I have to help you find it back!

The girl excitedly raises her hand to volunteer, eliciting knowing smiles between her parents—it's yet another one of Pylgia's endless reasons to get into the water.

Father: Once you find the fishing rod, don't stay in for too long. I'll give you two hours—we should head back early with all this wind lately.

Girl: Okay!

Mother: Go ahead, Pylgia.

Pylgia: Roger that! Diver Pylgia, ready to deploy!

With permission granted, Pylgia takes a deep breath and plunges straight into the sea. Her parents can only exchange fond, resigned smiles at their water-loving daughter.

### Diver 1-Dialogue 1

Crab: Why are you still hanging around? Do you want to learn how to blow bubbles from me?

Pylgia: Blowing bubbles! That sounds fun! I'll come back and learn from you next time!

Pylgia waves goodbye to the crab with a cheerful gesture.

As Pylgia descends deeper into the sea, the scenery transforms around her. Coral reefs of various shapes and sizes emerge from the sandy seabed, while vibrant schools of fish dance around her in welcome, as if greeting an old friend.

Pylgia waves to the fish before continuing her descent, her eyes scanning the waters for the lost fishing rod.

She delicately brushes her fingers over a purple coral, sensing its intricate texture beneath her touch, as if the coral is acknowledging her presence. Suddenly, a crab emerges from behind the reef, brandishing its large claws in a proud display.

Crab: Oh... if it isn't our little Py! What brings you down here today?

The crab speaks, yet Pylgia shows no surprise. She carries on with the conversation as effortlessly as if talking to sea creatures is the most ordinary thing in the world.

Pylgia: Dad's fishing rod is lost, and I'm here to find it! Mr. Crab, did you see anything fall down from above?

Crab: Fall down...? Come to think of it, I did see something...

Pylgia: Where? Tell me!

Pylgia bubbles with excitement, a stark contrast to her reserved demeanor on land.

Crab: Oh, I remember now—something did fall from the surface and sink into these coral reefs this morning.

Crab: But I didn't catch where it landed exactly. Maybe you could ask those fish over there or that squid nearby? They swim around here all day long, so they might have seen more than I did.

Pylgia: I see! Thank you, Mr. Crab. I'm off!

After her hasty thanks, the girl sets off towards the vast expanse of coral reefs ahead.

### Diver 1-Dialogue 1

Pylgia: Miss Squid, can I ask you a question? You have so many hands. Doesn't it make you tired to control them all?

Squid: Not at all. You have ten fingers, but you don't get tired of using them, do you?

Pylgia: Oh, I see what you mean...

A squid lounges on the reef, its tentacles coiled up, appearing lost in thought. Pylgia swims closer and greets it with a smile.

Pylgia: Hello, Miss Squid! Have you seen a fishing rod around here? You know, the thing used for catching fish!

Squid: Well... I have.

Pylgia: That's wonderful! Could you tell me where it is?

Squid: I can tell you, but first you must answer a question.

Pylgia: Alright, I'd be more than happy to!

Squid: What is hard to recover once it's lost?

Pylgia: Hmm... Things dropped in the sea?

Squid: No, think about it some more.

Deep in thought, Pylgia suddenly recalls the words her father said earlier.

Father: We should head back early with all this wind lately.

Pylgia: It's time! Once time is wasted, you can never get it back. Even though I'm having fun out here, I should listen to Dad and get back on time!

Squid: Indeed, time it is.

Squid: But there is more than one answer to this question...

Pylgia: ?

Pylgia tilts her head curiously, but the squid shakes its head in response.

Squid: Never mind. It may be beyond your understanding now, but someday you'll figure out the other answer.

Squid: The fishing rod is by the coral in the sixth row. Go on now.

Pylgia: Thank you, Miss Squid!

### Diver 1-Dialogue 3

Grumpy Fish: Burp...

Pylgia: Whoa... What a messy eater...

Nearby, a fish dozes by the reef, its scales capturing the filtered sunlight and casting rainbow-hued reflections across the water.

Pylgia: Hello! I've lost a fishing rod—have you seen it anywhere?

Grumpy Fish: No!

The fish rolls its eyes at Pylgia with obvious irritation.

Grumpy Fish: You humans are always trying to catch and eat us, and now you have the nerve to come looking for your killing weapon in our territory?

Pylgia: I-I'm sorry... but this is something very important to my dad. I have to find it.

Grumpy Fish: No means no! Now, get lost.

Pylgia: I have worms.

Grumpy Fish: ...

Pylgia: And grasshoppers.

Grumpy Fish: Twenty.

Pylgia seems to see the fish in front of her drooling.

Pylgia: Ten.

Grumpy Fish: Deal! It's near the coral in the fourth row on the left, but you'll have to figure out the exact one on your own. Now that I've told you, time to hand over my payment.

Pylgia takes out a metal box and tosses it to the fish, who eagerly opens the lid and devours all the bait without even bothering to count it.

### Diver 1-Dialogue 3

With the help of the sea creatures, Pylgia arrives at the coral reef.

On the fringe of the reef, Pylgia notices a familiar silhouette—a slim fishing rod peacefully nestled on the coral. She quickly swims over and grabs it.

??: Help...

Just as she's about to leave with the fishing rod, the girl hears a faint cry for help.

??: Help... Help...

Following the sound, she catches sight of a yellow croaker ensnared in a fishing line.

Without hesitation, Pylgia deftly unravels the fishing line and frees the fish.

Yellow Croaker: Haah... huff... huff... I'm saved... Are you the one who saved me?

Blowing bubbles, the yellow croaker studies the strange girl before it.

Pylgia: Yes, that's right!

Yellow Croaker: That makes us friends, then! Feel free to come play around this coral reef anytime!

Pylgia: Thanks! That would be great! And if you'd like, I can show you the beach as well!

Yellow Croaker: Hehe, sounds awesome! I've been wanting to get some fresh air!

Pylgia: And also... Oh no! I promised I'd be back soon!

Pylgia: I'm sorry, Mr. Yellow Croaker, but I have to go now. I'll come back to play with you again!

After a bow, Pylgia hurriedly swims back towards the shallow waters.

### Diver 1-Dialogue 4

On her way back, Pylgia sees all sorts of fish swimming by.

Some are familiar, others strangers, but Pylgia greets each of them with equal warmth.

Is it really time to return to shore?

Pylgia asks herself.

Can she make as many friends on land?

Will anyone on land talk with her so freely, understand her just the same?

The answer seems to be no, but...

She made a promise to Dad, so she has to keep her word.

Pylgia nods to herself and swims towards the light above.

### Diver 1-Dialogue 5

Pylgia comes upon the ruins of a sunken ship. Its rusted hull flickers in and out of view through the water, almost as if murmuring stories of days gone by.

Navigating cautiously through the ship, she discovers ancient artifacts strewn about. By a shattered display window, she notices a starfish trapped inside.

Without hesitation, Pylgia reaches out and frees the starfish. Once released, it sparkles with dazzling light, as if thanking her for the rescue.

### Diver 1-Dialogue 5

As she dives deeper, Pylgia encounters a pod of friendly dolphins. They leap through the water around her, almost beckoning her to join their play.

Though she still needs to find the lost fishing rod, Pylgia can't resist spending a few moments playing with them.

### Diver 1-Dialogue 5

A strong current suddenly surges towards her. Pylgia quickly spins around, only to find a shark gliding past, mere inches away.

She takes refuge behind the nearby rocks to evade the shark's charges. After four or five failed attempts, the ocean's overlord finally loses interest.

### Diver 1-Ending point

By the time Pylgia surfaces, night has already fallen. As she climbs back onto the boat, she finds her parents gearing up with diving equipment.

Mother: Py!

Upon spotting Pylgia, the woman tosses aside her diving gear and pulls her daughter into a tight embrace.

Father: You're forty minutes late.

The man shakes his head, but his eyes betray his concern.

Pylgia: Here you go!

Pylgia brings out the fishing rod and hands it to her father.

Father: I can't believe you actually did it... That's deep water there, not just the shallow end. If I had known you were doing something so dangerous...

Mother: That's amazing, sweetie! You're Mom's hero now! But please, never put yourself in such a dangerous situation again!

Pylgia: I ran into Mr. Crab, Miss Squid, and a grumpy fish along the way, asked them for directions, and finally found the fishing rod hidden deep in the coral reef!

Pylgia: And the fishing line was all tangled up around Mr. Yellow Croaker. After I helped him get free, we became good friends!

Her parents exchange fond smiles at their daughter's tale.

Mother: Our Pylgia is truly the Daughter of the Sea—no matter what dangers you face, you'll always make it through.

Mother: Right, darling?

Father: Sigh... Right, right.

Pylgia responds with a sheepish giggle, neither accepting nor declining the grand title given by her mother.
