# Escape

The clang of swords, the roar of monsters,
resound in the depths of dreams.
The boiling blood cannot be cooled.

Even in the depths of dreams,
she perches atop a mountain of bones and a sea of blood.
She drops the sword in her hand.
And lets the rain wash away the blood.
Embracing her scars, she escapes from her own abyss.

In your blurry sight, a scarlet beast is charging at you, growling.

Seeing the heaps of corpses before you, you hold back the instinct to puke with all your might while spurring your legs on to begin moving.

Something is different this time. Undefeated in the past, she suddenly looks like she's trying her best to endure something.

Witnessing Ninety-Nine's unusual behavior, you decide to catch up with her quickly.

But then you scan around—in the shadow, the monsters' mad screams are incessant. Can you really make it out safe and sound?

### Endpoint

She keeps dragging on her tattered body and worn out sanity.

She's trudging toward the edge of the mirage step by step.

But no floral fragrance awaits there.

Only pouring rain spattering on the desert.

Ninety-Nine: Is it... over?

You nod and point toward the exit on one end of the mirage.

Ninety-Nine: At last... it's over.

The blood and filth on her body are flowing down along with the rain, and the boiling blood that's been scorching her also dies down.

The huge sword in her hand slides down and is buried in the sand.

Finally, no need to swing the huge sword again, and no more blood stains from others.

Chief: What to do after getting out of here?

Ninety-Nine: I don't know.

Ninety-Nine: But I don't want to be the person I was in Parma anymore.

Biting down on the pain, she tears down the brand on her body with blood and flesh, tossing it on the sand.

Ninety-Nine: Thank you, for staying with me all the way here.

She leaves the mirage and never looks back.

Ninety-Nine: I hope we can meet again somewhere else, Chief.

Before vanishing, she leaves a final message.

She's a human being, a person who craves freedom.
