## AZ-10001 Defying Fate

### test

Lyu Buzhou: You're saying your sister has already... died once?

Woman: No! Those quacks said she was gone... but she isn't! Look! She can move, she responds...

The woman can barely speak, her sentences spilling over each other in panic. Li Xianling signals the assistant with a look, and the assistant moves to the woman's side, murmuring words of comfort.

Assistant: Professor Lyu is Master Lichen's own uncle, a renowned scholar. If anyone can help, he can...

While the assistant stays behind, Lyu Buzhou and Li Xianling slip out of the room. Lyu Buzhou gently closes the door, muffling the sound.

Li Xianling: Your read?

Lyu Buzhou: She resembles a "man-fiend" I've seen before.

Li Xianling: Only resembles?

Lyu Buzhou: She's calm... not aggressive.

Li Xianling: And why do you think that is?

Li Xianling takes a yellow paper talisman from her pocket and passes it to Lyu Buzhou.

Lyu Buzhou: ... An evil-binding talisman? And it actually works?

Li Xianling: I've sold a fair few. Celestial Master Daoxuan's official seal, eight hundred eighty thousand apiece. Rich believers can't get enough.

Li Xianling: You said it yourself in your paper: belief makes it real. So I tested it, and it held.

Lyu Buzhou: ...Am I meant to applaud your nerve?

Li Xianling: Thanks for the compliment.

Li Xianling: ...But seriously, do you think the girl can still be saved?

Lyu Buzhou: I don't know. But I'm willing to try.

Li Xianling: That's enough.

Saying nothing further, Li Xianling picks up a pen and a sheet of paper and passes them to Lyu Buzhou.

Li Xianling: I've got most basic supplies here. Write down whatever else you need, and I'll go find it.

After a moment's thought, Lyu Buzhou begins to speak, his pen moving across the paper as he lists the items one by one.

Lyu Buzhou: The list isn't long, but...

His eyes drift to the partly open door, where the girl lies in a state barely recognizable as human. At last, Lyu Buzhou gives voice to the thought he had been holding back.

Lyu Buzhou: ...Go get a gun.

### test

The night has deepened into the small hours by the time Lyu Buzhou's preparations draw to a close.

Over two hundred paper talismans, divided into sixty-four types, lie sorted and ready. Beside them rest the ritual implements: a peachwood sword, a Pellucid Bell, command flags and tablets for issuing orders to the spirits.

There are also cruder defenses: black dog's blood, donkey hooves, and other apotropaic objects from folklore. Every charm he has ever read about, from ancient records to modern belief, has been gathered into this room.

Thunder rolls outside, and rain hammers the window as Lyu Buzhou turns to the final implement: a coin sword bound from red threads and Five Emperor Coins.

Boom!

A deafening crack of thunder splits the sky, and the room plunges into darkness.

Lyu Buzhou: ...There goes the power.

Ever prepared, Lyu Buzhou strikes a match and lights the candles.

As the faint light spreads across the desk, he becomes aware of a stinging pain in his palm. When the thunder had boomed, the rusted, sharpened edge of a rusty coin must have sliced his skin.

The last three coins, not yet woven in, lie on the desk stained with his blood, their arrangement unmistakable...

Gorge.

The pain comes late and feels deeply unnatural, a searing bolt that shoots upward and bursts behind his eyes.

He wavers, and in that unsteady moment, visions flicker through his mind, remnants of a far-off dream he had all but forgotten.

Li Xianling: How's it coming? ...What on earth happened to you?

Li Xianling enters after bringing the backup power online, her eyes falling at once on Lyu Buzhou's bloodied hand. She quickly retrieves the first-aid kit from the cabinet and searches for bandages and iodine.

As she does, Lyu Buzhou calmly wipes down the three coins and slots the final pieces into the coin sword.

Li Xianling: Honestly. Priestess Daowei always calls you the careful one. I'm beginning to doubt her judgment.

Lyu Buzhou accepts the iodine and bandages and begins cleaning the cut, never pausing in his speech.

Lyu Buzhou: A Daoist priest's vital blood is a powerful ward. It has long been used to consecrate ritual implements.

Li Xianling: Is that so? First I've heard of it.

Lyu Buzhou: And yet another reason you shouldn't have skipped morning lessons... no, forget it.

Lyu Buzhou: The tools are ready.

Hearing this, Li Xianling's playfulness vanishes, her features settling into a solemn calm.

Li Xianling: Then we...

Lyu Buzhou rises to his feet, twirling the coin sword in a smooth, practiced arc. The blade's edge sings softly as it parts the heavy stillness of the room.

Lyu Buzhou: Right. Let's begin.

### test

Talisman after talisman flows from Lyu Buzhou's brush. Around him are heaps of yellow paper, cinnabar, dog's blood, peachwood, copper coins, and other ritual implements, including objects he cannot name. Li Xianling must have collected these strange relics of forbidden practices during her field research.

The office, once clean and understated, is now buried in clutter, caught somewhere between a Daoist altar and a chaotic bazaar.

Li Xianling: The girl's getting worse... Are you done with the evil-binding talismans?

Li Xianling hurries to his side, and Lyu Buzhou, not once raising his eyes, hands her a bundle of yellow paper dense with talismanic script.

Li Xianling: This many?! My dear uncle, do you have any idea how hard aged cinnabar is to come by?

Lyu Buzhou: The evil-binding talisman remains our only verified method. And...

Li Xianling: And?

Lyu Buzhou looks up, each word measured and deliberate.

Lyu Buzhou: The talismans you drew contain four errors. I'm not certain whether those flaws improve or worsen their effect on the "man-fiend." So I made four extra copies following your version as well.

Li Xianling: Ever the professor, thorough to a fault.

Lyu Buzhou: You could be too, if you'd skipped fewer morning lessons.

Li Xianling: Haha...

Clutching the talismans, Li Xianling turns to go, only to halt after a few short paces.

Li Xianling: You saw shifu, didn't you?

Lyu Buzhou: You mean that mass hysteria?

Li Xianling: I prefer to think of it as the "immortal's guidance."

Li Xianling: You know me. It's how I make my living. A little grift, a little faith.

Lyu Buzhou lifts his eyes and glances at the words hanging on the wall, written in the hand of his father, who is both Li Xianling's grandfather and the current head priest of the Daoist sect.

*Abode of the Immortals*

Lyu Buzhou: I haven't seen any immortals.

Li Xianling: Neither have I.

Their eyes lock for a brief instant, and then, all at once, they smile.

Li Xianling: So I need to go see for myself.

Lyu Buzhou: To the west?

Li Xianling: Yes.

Lyu Buzhou: Across the sea?

Li Xianling: Yes.

Lyu Buzhou: A gamble with near-certain death. Hardly a sound plan.

His hands never pause, so Lyu Buzhou doesn't catch the glare Li Xianling throws over her shoulder.

But he knows, without seeing, precisely what look is on her face.

Lyu Buzhou: When do you set out?

Li Xianling: Spring of next year. I've already seen to the arrangements.

Lyu Buzhou: ...

Li Xianling: Coming with me?

Lyu Buzhou: Heaven's will is beyond our grasp, and fate is never to be defied.

Li Xianling: So you did cast a divination. What, did you not like the result? Since when do you just sit back and let things happen?

Lyu Buzhou: Verification.

Lyu Buzhou cuts her off, raising his brush to point at the door.

Lyu Buzhou: Certain things demand verification.

Lyu Buzhou: The "man-fiend" crisis is still at our feet. We're in no position to go "chasing immortals."

Li Xianling: And if we resolve it?

Lyu Buzhou: Then perhaps we...

Lyu Buzhou: Perhaps we will find the one thread of hope on a road that leads nowhere.

Li Xianling: ...

A long quiet settles between them before Li Xianling raises her head and offers a cheerful, open smile.

Li Xianling: Then I suppose we'll see if we've got what it takes.

Lyu Buzhou: We?

Li Xianling: Fine, fine—if you've got what it takes. Better?

Li Xianling: Now get back to work. I need to go test whether your new talismans are worth the paper they're drawn on.

Without lingering, Li Xianling disappears into the bedroom, and Lyu Buzhou is left by himself in the quiet office.
