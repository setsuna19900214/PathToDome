# Wilderness Trek

In the inescapable wilderness
She chases both hope and death

Wilderness Trek II

## AZ-10001 Wilderness Trek

### Mantis 2.1 - Start

Marguerite: There's a colossal, unidentified creature just 50 meters ahead! Mil, watch out!

The yellow sand ahead obstructs their view, thus the driver can't quite make out the details of the "unidentified creature," but without hesitation, he veers to the right.

The off-roader, zooming forward, swerves right with a piercing screech, just missing some unknown obstacle.

Driver Mil: My sweet Jesus! There's actually something lurking out there! Almost crashed into it.

Driver Mil: Just so we're on the same page, I'm not footing the bill for any damages to the ride, way beyond my paygrade.

Navigator Raziel: Enough with the details, focus! Our lives are hanging by a thread here!

Driver Mil: Seriously? Marguerite spotted that obstacle miles away. How'd you miss it, rookie? Maybe you're the one who needs to keep his eyes peeled, Mr. Navigator.

Laila is totally focused on her instruments, not bothering to join in the argument.

It's clear to Marguerite, though she doesn't say it out loud, that everyone in the car is on edge.

Amidst Tide of Ashes, a thick blanket of dust darkens the sky, making it nearly impossible to see.

Marguerite: Keep heading straight. In about two kilometers, there'll be a highway in sight.

Driver Mil: How in the world can you make out any road in this soup?

Marguerite: Grabbed a map from some locals before we set off. I've got all the roads down pat.

### Mantis 2.1-Event 1

The off-roader barrels ahead, jolting violently as it crosses a ditch. It's so rough that the car almost flips over.

Lucky enough, the driver's top-notch skills steadies the car in time, dodging a disaster.

Driver Mil: Ugh, if the damn GPS was up and running, we wouldn't be gambling with our lives like this.

Navigator Raziel: You know, if those nav satellites were still around, we wouldn't be in this mess. We'd be capturing these beasts from orbit.

Mil continues driving, paying no mind to Raziel. Deciding not to embarrass himself further, Raziel shifts his attention to the world outside the window.

Navigator Raziel: There it is, the highway! Got it in sight!

Driver Mil: Quiet down, it's obvious to anyone. Marguerite, what do we do now?

Marguerite glances over at Laila, who is sitting next to her.

Professor Laila: ...These readings are all over the place. Looks like we're dealing with some kind of energy interference. Pinpointing Tide of Ashes's center is impossible.

Navigator Raziel: And it's not just your gadgets, even our compass is doing loop-de-loops. Absolutely no use to us.

Marguerite: Hey Laila, recall that theory we had earlier?

Professor Laila: What theory are you referring to?

Marguerite: The patterns in the Apostles' actions, the way they move.

Professor Laila: The Black Ring...

Marguerite takes a quick look at her watch.

Marguerite: Mil, take the highway and head northeast.

Driver Mil: Northeast, you sure about that?

Marguerite: Absolutely sure. We just skirted the epicenter of Tide of Ashes; we need to catch up now!

Driver Mil: Got it, I'm on it.

Mil's hands are clenched on the steering wheel.

### Mantis 2.1-Event 2

The off-roader trudges on, and with every mile, the world around becomes murkier.

### Mantis 2.2-Starting point

Navigator Raziel: Can it be... are these... the Apostles?

Everyone is transfixed by the scene before them, as if their very breaths are suspended.

Marguerite is the quickest to react, urgently reminding:

Marguerite: Laila, the data! Make sure to log it now!

Professor Laila: Ah... got it! Yes! I'm doing it right now...

Laila comes to her senses, hurriedly and somewhat clumsily working the nearby devices.

At the same time, Marguerite is busy capturing the moment with her DV camera, eager to record a scene never before seen by humankind.

Out of nowhere, a shrill scream splits the air, causing Marguerite to nearly black out and almost drop her camera.

Vague Voice: Return... the Corpus...

Vague Voice: to completeness...

Marguerite: To completeness?

Marguerite tries desperately to make sense of the voice. At that moment, Raziel's tremulous scream echoes in her ears.

Navigator Raziel: Are they... looking at us?

Guided by Raziel's pointing, they see the enormous humanoid monsters gradually turning. Their eyes, if you can call the glowing spots on their faces that, are undoubtedly fixed in their direction.

Professor Laila: Looks like they have indeed spotted us...

Driver Mil: Ha, the moment has finally arrived.

Mil slams down on the clutch, cranks the wheel hard, and the car sketches a beautiful arc on the pavement.

Driver Mil: There's always gotta be a thrill like this in every adventure, huh?

### Mantis 2.2-Event 1

Marguerite: This is our moment, Mil!

As the Apostles are preoccupied with the terrain, barely noticing the team, the K1α roars past them at breakneck speed.

Marguerite: Victory! We've collected a ton of data!

Vague Voice: Roarrrr—!

As Marguerite turns towards Laila, making a triumphant "V" sign, she jumps at the Apostles' thunderous roar.

Vague Voice: &#*%......%*(!

The massive beast, like it's been agitated, is hurtling towards the team at great speed.

Navigator Raziel: Doesn't this seem... a bit unusual?

Driver Mil: Yep, figured as much—this gig's all about living on the edge.

Mil slams the gas pedal to the floor, and the K1α roars to life, stirring up clouds of dust.

### Mantis 2.2-Event 2

Navigator Raziel: Apostles up north as well! Do we sneak underneath or take the long way around?

Driver Mil: Decide fast, Marguerite! The one over there isn't going to wait around for you!

Marguerite looks at the massive creatures before her. She's keen to gather data, but handling two Apostles at once is overwhelming.

Recalling the earlier trench that had ensnared the monster's attack, she decides to test her luck. If she can trap one, she might be able to gather data from the other.

Marguerite: Mil, steer us south—the trench there will slow it down!

Driver Mil: Got it, your call!

### Mantis 2.1-Exit

Somewhere along the line, the sand and dust that blanketed the air have mysteriously disappeared.

It feels like entering the calm at the center of a storm, with their surroundings slowly coming into focus.

In this eye of the storm, the team finally gets a clear look at the real nature of the monsters they've been facing on their journey.

They're like towering shadows reaching for the clouds, black silhouettes packed tightly together. They move at a pace that seems slow but is strangely synchronized.

### Mantis 2.2-Exit

Navigator Raziel: They're still on our tail, faster, go faster!

Mil casts a somewhat helpless look at the navigator. The off-roader is pushing its limits, its engine's roar now sounding distressingly like a cry of despair.

They've gained some ground on the Apostles, but shaking off these behemoths completely is another story.

Driver Mil: Apologies, the K1α is a warhorse, not a racing car. I haven't hit my limit, but this is as fast as it gets.

The navigator looks back at the monsters that appear slow but are doggedly chasing them, crying out and holding his head in anguish.

Marguerite: Mil, you still got those old stunt driving tricks up your sleeve, don't you?

Driver Mil: Absolutely, how could I ever forget the skills that fed me?

Marguerite: Mil, what do you say to taking your beloved K1α out for a spin in the canyons, your favorite thrill?

Driver Mil: Ha! Love that idea!

Mil reaches into his pocket, grabs a metal flask, and gulps down a swig. The K1α leaps into the air, soaring above the cliff edge—

Driver Mil: Everyone, hold on tight!

Following a few seconds of feeling weightless, a massive impact hits as they land.

In an unlucky turn, Marguerite's head hits the car window, rendering her unconscious.

When she finally wakes up, the sky-obscuring sandstorm is nowhere to be seen.

Marguerite, not bothering to check her head wound, anxiously scans the surroundings but can't spot her teammates.

After unbuckling and stepping out of the car, she sees her teammates standing on a slope, reveling in the warmth of the sun, relieved to have made it through.

Professor Laila: You're up?

Marguerite: What about the data? How did it turn out?

Professor Laila: No worries, it's all logged.

Professor Laila: The volume of data we've collected this time is off the charts. We might be able to validate your theories on the Apostles' behaviors.

Marguerite: That's fantastic, just fantastic...

Navigator Raziel: Marguerite! Thank goodness you're safe, we all survived...

Marguerite: Yeah, we all made it through.

Navigator Raziel: Today's been the most perilous day I've ever lived through.

Marguerite: What are your thoughts?

Navigator Raziel: Not again, please... My heart can't handle another round.

Marguerite: I always knew I was right about you, Mil.

Driver Mil: Oh? What makes you say that?

Marguerite: If it weren't for you, we wouldn't have made it out.

Driver Mil: It was really your quick thinking that did it.

Marguerite: This isn't the time to be modest. We'll need you going forward as well.

Driver Mil: Haha, alright, I've got this.

Marguerite: That reminds me.

Flipping on her DV camera, Marguerite browses through the previously shot footage.

But here's the weird part—the DV didn't pick up any shots of the Apostles in human form, just a blur of flashing lights, like a glitchy mirage.

Driver Mil: Huh, figures. It's no surprise no one has ever managed to film them. There's something really off about these "Apostles."

Marguerite: Weird, right? And the sound? Did it fail to record that too?

Navigator Raziel: What kind of sound are you talking about?

Marguerite: It's like... I mean...

Marguerite, rubbing her throbbing head, desperately tries to recall.

Marguerite: Ah well, if it's not coming back to me, no point fussing over it!

Marguerite: Better to think about our next move than to brood over what we missed recording.

Saying this, Marguerite turns the DV camera to face herself.

Marguerite: The truth is just around the corner. Faced with what we've just been through, what move will our team make next?

Marguerite: Should we run from peril, or face our fears?

Marguerite: Don't miss out on "Marguerite and Friends' Wilderness Trek" Season 4, Episode 5—"Back to Tide of Ashes"!

Navigator Raziel: You're resolving your own dilemmas too fast, aren't you? Then what's the point of leaving us hanging at the end?

Navigator Raziel: Besides, we just made a narrow escape. Are we seriously considering heading back in?

Marguerite: Of course! We've all had a decent break, haven't we? As soon as we're ready, let's hit the road!

Professor Laila: After getting to this point, everything I've been studying for years is just around the corner... Sitting idle isn't an option for me.

Driver Mil: What more is there to talk about? Hop in, let's go!

Navigator Raziel: You all... I just... oh, never mind...

Marguerite: Destination: Tide of Ashes...

Marguerite: Time to roll out!

The car speeds to a dead end, screeching to a stop before an immense cliff. Everyone is out of options, feeling trapped and helpless.

They're left to stare at the giant looming behind them, relentlessly closing in, one thunderous step at a time.
