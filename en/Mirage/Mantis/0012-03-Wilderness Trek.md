# Wilderness Trek

In the inescapable wilderness
She chases both hope and death

Wilderness Trek III

## AZ-10001 Wilderness Trek

### Mantis 3-Start

Unknown Voice: *Destination: Tide of Ashes...*

Unknown Voice: *Time to roll out!*

Unknown Voice: *An Apostle... here? Why?*

Unknown Voice: *We're about to crash... Swerve, quick!*

Unknown Voice: *Bad news... This isn't just the limit for the car, but for me too...*

Unknown Voice: *Marguerite... do something...*

Unknown Voice: *No! This can't be happening... I...*

The only thing left in Marguerite's mind is a single, overwhelming thought.

Marguerite: I was wrong.

A boundless darkness finally engulfs this catastrophic failure.

Regaining consciousness, every inch of the body is wracked with excruciating pain.

Not far away, an object is glowing faintly.

The DV camera appears to be still working. Using this sliver of light, Marguerite manages to navigate through the darkness.

### Mantis 3-Event 1

Unknown Voice: *The birth of the Apostle... it needs to be documented...*

Unknown Voice: *I need your help...*

Marguerite: Who's out there?!

There is no answer, and the voice dissolves into the darkness again.

### Mantis 3-Item 1

Tracking the sound, a figure bathed in a gentle light appears before her eyes.

The sight of this figure brings with it an intense fear, nearly bursting forth from her chest.

Marguerite: The Apostle!

Surprisingly, it doesn't attack. It just lingers beside an object, as if refusing to move away.

**【Advance】**

- Pick it up

**end**

It's Laila's notebook, filled with information about the Apostles.

The Apostles from every corner have their own routes, but they all lead to the same place.

The crash site of the Perishing Star, the lifeless zone known as the Black Ring...

The moment notebook is touched, that humanoid Apostle just disappears into nothingness.

Professor Laila: Your point resonates with me—it's the unknown that breeds fear...

Professor Laila: And if science calls for a sacrifice, shouldn't we be the ones to step up?

Laila's voice is ringing.

She ditched the easy life at school to take on real risks with us. I owe it to her not to screw this up.

Marguerite: Now's not the time to dwell on the past... I need to find her...

### Mantis 3-Item 2

Marguerite spots a telescope and immediately grabs it.

It's Raziel's, a memento from his father. Ever since she's known him, Raziel always keeps that telescope hanging around his neck.

Navigator Raziel: It's like having my old man still around, a comforting presence, you know?

Navigator Raziel: But really, it's you who's been our good luck charm. Since crossing paths with you, we've had a knack for dodging trouble.

Navigator Raziel: Fear hasn't crossed my mind in ages, all thanks to my belief in you to navigate us through any storm...

The Apostle guarding the area is nowhere to be seen. Marguerite picks up the telescope.

Marguerite: This belongs to your father; we can't just abandon it here... Hang in there, I'm coming to find you...

### Mantis 3-Item 3

A tiny photo is affixed to the cover of the silver pocket watch.

Could it be his wife? I haven't seen her before. Yet, every time Mil talks about her, his face just beams with joy.

Driver Mil: A guy like me, living on the edge, could end up with a woman like her... must be some kind of divine favor.

Driver Mil: Since she became my wife, I've turned into a worrywart. Always looking over my shoulder at work, haunted by the thought of leaving her heartbroken...

Driver Mil: Haha, it's not something a young lady like you would get.

### Mantis 3-Event 2

Marguerite: I'll track you down... I have to... We're getting out of here together...

Even with her vision blurred and direction uncertain, Marguerite grits through the pain, relentlessly pressing on.

### Mantis 3-Event 3

Unknown Voice: *The Apostles might not be as incomprehensible as we think... Born from human emotions, they surely hold onto a slice of human consciousness...*

### Mantis 3-Event 4

As awareness fades to a blur, the pain slowly recedes to a distant echo, feeling almost as if the soul is on the verge of slipping away from the body.

### Mantis 3-Event 5

The ground gives way, and the sky fractures. Marguerite finds her way forward obscured.

But she presses on, holding onto the belief that her companions are out there, waiting for her as they always have.

### Mantis 3-New Event 1

Unknown Voice: *Apostles, they have their habits... Adapting their choices to the situation at hand...*

Unknown Voice: *I knew it... they can be reached, they're not impossible to communicate with...*

Unknown Voice: *...We can all make it out of this alive... just believe in me...*

### Mantis 3-New Event 2

Unknown Voice: *I'm convinced they're not just spirit-eaters. The Apostles must have sprung from the depths of human consciousness.*

Unknown Voice: *They always first appear near human settlements ravaged by disaster. That's no random occurrence.*

Unknown Voice: *The true nature of the Apostles... may be shattered spiritual energy. Pulled together by some strong emotional force, they take on a shape made of pure energy... It's for this reason their appearances usually align with what humans can comprehend.*

Unknown Voice: *I realize it's a gamble... We've faced countless dire situations, yet here we are, always making it through unscathed...*

Unknown Voice: *Hunting the Apostles, it looks like sheer madness to anyone else... But that's precisely what you want... Right?*

### Mantis 3-New Event 3

Marguerite: You're mistaken, Mil. I get it more than you think...

Marguerite: Your wife's waiting for you back home. You can't afford to die here...

### Mantis 3-Exit

As time becomes a blur in the vast darkness, shadows start to materialize.

Marguerite: At last... I've found you all...

Marguerite: Where have you been... I... I've searched everywhere for you...

Marguerite: Let's leave this place together... just believe in me... we have what it takes...

No answer from the shadowy figures.

Then, after what seems like ages, an unfamiliar voice cuts through the silence.

Unfamiliar Voice: Who are you? Why are you here all by yourself?

Marguerite: My name is... Marguerite... I'm tasked with... finding and researching the Apostle of Ashes...

Marguerite: I'm looking for... my friends... we've lost each other...

Unfamiliar Voice: ..Looks like you could use some help, miss.

Marguerite: You... you're not one of them, are you?

Unfamiliar Voice: I'm with the DisSea Expedition Team, a guide. You're badly hurt. Let's head back to our camp, and we'll take care of those injuries.

Marguerite: No... I can't just walk away alone... My friends... they're still out there... waiting for me...

Marguerite's voice becomes fainter with each word. With the last word barely whispered, she can no longer keep herself upright and crumbles to the ground.

Unfamiliar Voice: ...

Unfamiliar Voice: Your friends...?

The stranger gazes beyond Marguerite, finding nothing but a vast expanse of silent, desolate wilderness.
