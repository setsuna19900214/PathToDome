### Mantis 1-Interaction 1

Marguerite effortlessly climbs up the dry tree.

But people next to her are so engrossed in mapping and studying the landscape. They're completely oblivious to everything else around them.

Navigator is up in his favorite tree, just like always, scanning the horizon with his trusty binoculars.

Marguerite: Hey Raziel, what's got you so engrossed?

Navigator Raziel: Whoa!!

Caught off guard, Raziel nearly jumps out of his skin, almost falling off the tree in surprise.

Navigator Raziel: Marguerite! You nearly gave me a heart attack... How about a little heads-up before you pop up out of nowhere?

Marguerite: What, you didn't see me coming?

Navigator Raziel: I'm keeping an eye on that storm over there.

Raziel gestures towards the horizon, where a storm is visibly gathering, its tumult visible even from this distance.

Marguerite: Seen anything suspicious yet?

Navigator Raziel: No... all's quiet on this front.

Marguerite: That should be good news, right?

Navigator Raziel: No news is bad news in this case. We're heading straight into the lion's den!

Marguerite: Exciting, right?

Navigator Raziel: Exciting my a**! I've been kicking myself every day since I signed up for this gig... I mean, I'm actually about to enter that nightmare...

Navigator Raziel: We're doomed...

### Mantis 1-Interaction 2

Marguerite walks through the campsite and spots a large tent adorned with the "Wilderness Trek" show logo.

This tent, originally a gift from a sponsor during the first season, is now in the possession of her senior, Laila, from university days, to whom Marguerite had gifted it as a token of gratitude for her care and guidance.

Marguerite notices someone pacing inside the tent. She unzips it and walks in, immediately recognizing the face before her.

Marguerite: Hey, Laila—Oops, my bad, I mean, Professor Laila now, right?

Professor Laila: Being called "Professor" just doesn't sit right. How about sticking with "Laila"? Sounds better, don't you think?

Marguerite: Why do you say that? You're a prodigy, becoming a full professor at 32. That's not something you hear every day!

Professor Laila: Well, it's more like I was the only option left...

Professor Laila: Had you stuck around at the school, you could've climbed the ranks even quicker than me.

Marguerite: I'm just a youngster, not quite my time in the spotlight yet.

Marguerite: And I could never be tied down to a classroom. Office life isn't for me; I'm meant for the wild!

Professor Laila: That's just your way, always has been.

Professor Laila gently tousles Marguerite's hair in a fond gesture.

Professor Laila: Even though Professor Darien never stops griping about his star student turning into a TV survivalist, deep down, everyone's actually jealous of you.

Professor Laila: It's incredible how you've come up with such a clever way to secure funding for our research. You truly stand out as a genius in our college.

Marguerite: Hehe, I never expected you to join our show's crew.

Professor Laila: I've dedicated ten years to studying Tide of Ashes. I'm determined to get my hands on the most current data.

Meanwhile, two other team members entered the tent.

Driver Mil: I overheard y'all talking about Tide of Ashes. Seriously, when do we get the green light? I'm itching for some action.

Navigator Raziel: Can't you just chill? Is it the kind of place you can waltz into? We need to be fully prepped for this.

Marguerite: Great timing, folks. I was just about to dive into the nitty-gritty of the plan.

Professor Laila: It's getting late, why don't we grab some food first? It's tough to think on an empty stomach.

Marguerite: Hmm... Laila's got a point. How about a good old bonfire bash!

She cracks open the tent flap just enough to see someone inside, lost in deep contemplation.

I'll check in with Laila later. When she gets into her thinking mode, she can be lost in it for ages.

Of course, all these improvements mean it costs a pretty penny more than the K1.

And there, right next to this mechanical marvel, stands a friend Marguerite has not seen in years.

### Mantis 1-Exit

The evening's creeping in, and the bonfire under the starry sky looks extra radiant in this wide-open wilderness.

Professor Laila: To be honest, I've always wondered, what's your fascination with those Apostles?

Marguerite: Um... it's hard to explain. After the Apocalypse, all the early attempts to study Tide of Ashes ended in failure. Since then, whenever people bring it up, there's this fear in their voices, and no one dares to talk about restarting research on Tide of Ashes.

Marguerite: I think it's not right. We shouldn't shy away from the unknown. At least, someone should take action.

Marguerite: I'm determined to unravel the mystery behind Tide of Ashes, and it's crystal clear that the Apostles hold the answers.

Marguerite: While chasing after the Apostles, my mind has been swirling with peculiar questions: What exactly constitutes them? How do they think and act? Do they possess intelligence... or, in other words, a basis for communication?

Driver Mil: When you bring up this stuff, well, the navigator boy and I are a bit lost. Just tell us what the plan is.

Marguerite: Locate Tide of Ashes's position, venture inside, and gather all the useful data and visual footage you can—easy as pie.

Navigator Raziel: ...It does come across as a rather straightforward and efficient way to get ourselves killed.

Driver Mil: I believe it's a good plan. I have faith in Marguerite. We've been through so many seasons of "Wilderness Trek," and she's always found solutions to any problem. And you, Professor?

Professor Laila: Of course, I don't see any issues.

Marguerite: Hehe, it's nice to know you all trust me this much. Truth be told, I have some doubts about this myself.

Navigator Raziel: Now's not the time for those kinds of words... You're the one who founded this show, and you're the last person who should be feeling uncertain.

Marguerite: Yeah, you're absolutely right!

Marguerite: I'll make sure we get the filming done, no doubt about it!

Driver Mil: We were counting on you to say that.

Driver Mil: This bonfire get-together is a rare treat. You should raise a toast, it's what everyone enjoys.

Marguerite: Sure thing!

Marguerite raises her juice can high in the air.

Marguerite: Let's hope for a smooth ride—Laila and I will secure the data we need, Mil will have a blast driving, and Raziel... Raziel will make it through in one piece!

Marguerite: In advance, I wish for the success of Season Four of "Marguerite and Friends' Wilderness Trek!"

Marguerite: Tomorrow, let's head towards Tide of Ashes!

Marguerite checks the sky, realizing it's not break time yet.

She's surrounded by many old pals from their "Wilderness Trek" filming days. A good old catch-up with them seems like a nice idea.
