## AZ-10001 Heartstrings

She stares at the glass wall of the sterile ward,
ever expecting anyone to visit.

As you open your eyes, blooming roses fill your sight. Looking at the bright red color all over the place, you're sure you're in a mirage now.

You stand up slowly, getting off the headstone you've been leaning on. The words on the stone have long faded.

You can only recognize several words: "May roses bloom always."

You feel a stinging pain and quickly remove your hand.

You find that before you realize, a thorn has already pierced into your palm.

Just as you are to examine this thing resembling a rose stem, you hear a soft singing voice.

The melody sounds melancholic and lonely, as if she wants to be found by someone, but also as if it comes from someone who's already used to solitude.

You scan around and find that the voice is coming from the building at the far end.

In a mirage, all strangeness comes from the dreamer. To quit the mirage, one must follow the strangeness and locate the owner of the dream.

The mirage has become even more unstable.

The monsters have become even stronger and more brutal.

Even though they are just strolling in the garden, their power seems to be strong enough to shred the mirage anytime. You must hurry up.

Chief: Shh! These monsters are not your father.

### Incident 1

The item sustaining the mirage must be there.

Otherwise, this mirage would have been wrecked to total destruction by these monsters long ago.

### Endpoint

Having braved the arduous journey, you finally lead Victoria back to the rose garden at the start.

The young girl fingers the name on the headstone gently.

Victoria: May roses bloom always... I see.

The girl pierces the thorn into the earth.

Blood spatters on this nail resembling a rose stem.

As if life has been breathed into it, the thorn is growing rapidly, finally becoming the most beautiful and vibrant rose.

At last, the girl isolated from the world meets the greenhouse flower.

She removes her face mask and takes a deep breath of this fragrance of life.

Victoria: Thank you, Daddy.
