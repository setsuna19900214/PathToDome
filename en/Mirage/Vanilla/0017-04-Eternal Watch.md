# Eternal Watch

Within the watchtower, time ceases to flow.
A young guardian, forever on the lookout alone.

The young guardian,
sends out a message after billions of years.
But couldn't save herself
from the eternity of time.

Eternal Watch IV

## AZ-10001 Eternal Watch

### Vaniara 4.1 - Starting point.

Dreams are the ties that connect the past with the future.

Waking up marks the start of a new day.

It's time to go to work.

### Vanniella 4.1 - Finish

Girl: So much work was done in just half a day.

Vanilla: It's all thanks to you. It would have taken me until evening on my own.

Girl: Hehe, I've been useful.

Girl: Then let's eat now! I'm starving.

Vanilla: Yeah.

Vanilla lights the stove, and soon, two dishes and a soup are prepared and served at the dining table.

Vanilla has even added a bit of minced meat to the cauliflower, a rare treat.

The two eat in wordless harmony. Silence looms over the dining table.

When they finish eating and start cleaning up, the girl speaks first.

Girl: So, I guess... it's time to say goodbye.

Vanilla: I'll walk you out.

Girl: No need. There's nothing dangerous outside now. I can take care of myself.

Vanilla: Sure.

Vanilla doesn't insist further.

Girl: Then I'm off.

The girl makes for the door but then turns back as if remembering something.

Girl: Vanilla, do you... plan to stay here forever?

Vanilla: ...

Girl: This outpost was originally established to support scientific expeditions and provide humanity with data of DisSea, right?

Girl: But now... where are the expeditions? And other humans? ...Does civilization really still exist?

Girl: Vanilla, you probably have no reason to stay anymore, have you?

Vanilla: ...

Vanilla: But if I don't stay here, where else can I go alone?

Hearing Vanilla's response, the girl asks cautiously.

Girl: Do you want... to come with me?

Vanilla: Come with you?

Girl: We're companions, after all.

The girl shows a radiant smile.

Girl: We'll leave together, go outside, and find the others.

Girl: Let's go and see what the world has become. See the future.

Girl: Let's go...

The girl's voice suddenly stops. The familiar surroundings are fading.

The childish, cheerful face suddenly cracks, revealing a deep, dark void inside.

Girl: You can't go anywhere.

Girl: Meal time! Vanilla, I'm so hungry I could eat a cow...

Vanilla: Just one moment. I'll cook once I'm done with the work here.

Girl: Then let me help you! That way, we can eat sooner!

Vanilla: ...Okay.

### Vanniella 1 - Schedule

The old whiteboard lists the day's work schedule, the ink from the marker already fading.

Observe DisSea's movements and record the data.

Go to the hydroponic lab to check on the crops.

Turn on the radio and ensure communication is unobstructed.

Eat.

Girl: Vanilla, have you always been handling all this work yourself? You must be exhausted.

Vanilla: You get used to it.

After they leave, the paper disappears as if it never existed.

### Vanniella 1-Instrumentation

Vanilla operates the equipment while manually recording the observation data.

Girl: Huh? Don't these data usually come with a storage device? Why copy it down?

Vanilla: I'll lose all the data if the device breaks down.

Vanilla: But paper lasts a long time.

Girl: ...Oh, I see.

Girl: Let me help. I might not know how to operate this equipment, but I know how to write.

Vanilla: Sure.

Vanilla doesn't refuse the girl's offer. This saves a lot of time for today's observation work.

After they leave the observation room, the items inside disappear one after another, until it becomes an empty shell.

### Vanniella 1-Data

Girl: There's no one on any frequency.

Vanilla nods.

Vanilla: That's how it is normally.

Girl: Then why do you still turn it on every day?

Vanilla: ...Just a habit.

Girl: Right. After all, if it hadn't been for this habit, I might have died alone in the storm.

After they leave the communication room, the white noise disappears, and everything returns to silence.

### Vanniella 1 - Plants

Girl: Wow! How cool.

Girl: Did you plant all of these?

The girl wanders around the lab and naturally sees the flower in the corner.

Girl: So beautiful...

Looking into the girl's eyes, Vanilla says slowly.

Vanilla: You can have it.

Girl: Really? Thank you, Vanilla!

After they leave the hydroponic lab, the plants inside disappear one after another, leaving only a handful of yellow soil.

### Vanilla 4.2 - Start point

Vanilla feels as though she had a very long dream.

When she wakes up, she finds herself holding the control stick of a mecha, racing alone across a vast wasteland.

Does she regret her choice?

### Vanniella 4.2 - Finish

Vanilla pays no mind to the whispers in her ear.

Or rather, she can no longer understand the meaning of these words.

The only thought left in her mind is to leave this place and warn others of the danger.
