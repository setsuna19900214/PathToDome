# Eternal Watch

Within the watchtower, time ceases to flow.
A young guardian, forever on the lookout alone.

Eternal Watch III

## AZ-10001 Eternal Watch

### Vanilla 3.

Girl: Are you sure they left ten years ago?

In the car, the girl suddenly turns her head and asks Vanilla a question.

This old jeep has been idle for many years. Vanilla has fixed it up specially to help the girl find her parents.

However, due to the lack of shock-absorbing springs, the ride is quite bumpy, causing the girl's voice to tremble as well.

Vanilla: I'm sure.

Vanilla: I watched them leave ten years ago.

Girl: I see...

Girl: They're not very good at their job as parents. Can't they at least drop back to check on me?

The girl fakes a laugh, shrinking back into the seat.

These days, Vanilla has accompanied her to search all the possible locations.

But traces of her parents are nowhere to be found.

Girl: This world is just too big... It's virtually impossible to find two lost people.

This time, their destination is a small town 500 kilometers west of the outpost, as once mentioned by the middle-aged man.

After crossing the vast desert, the town is already faintly visible on the horizon.

Girl: If we can't find them this time... let's just give up.

Girl: I can't keep bothering you forever.

Vanilla: ...Don't mention it.

Vanilla reaches out a hand and pats the girl on the head.

Vanilla: We'll find them.

The moment they step into the town, their spirits plummet.

The town is long deserted, with only dilapidated houses everywhere.

Seeing the girl's pained expression, Vanilla is at a loss for words. She can offer only futile words of comfort.

Vanilla: Maybe we can... look around.

Vanilla: Who knows, we might find some clues.

### Vaniella 3.

Girl: They're not here...

The sun sets, and the sky gradually darkens.

The once-dilapidated town now seems to have regained some of its former luster.

Vanilla: Let's look elsewhere...

Girl: No, thank you.

Girl: You've already helped me so many times. We'll never find them... Let's not waste any more effort...

Girl: In the end, it's all the same everywhere. Whatever or whoever I'm looking for is nowhere to be found...

Girl: I should have just stayed in. I wouldn't have been so sad had I not gotten my hopes high...

Girl: I'm just a fool... I...

The tremor in the girl's voice worsens, until she finally can't hold back her tears and starts crying loudly.

Vanilla steps forward to hug her gently and stroke her hair. Tears wet her clothes on the chest.

In the desolate town, the girl's sobs are the only sound that remains.

Girl: I've been thinking about... what to say to my parents when they finally come back to me...

Girl: They are scientists... They would sacrifice everything for human civilization... for this world... I know those things are more important...

Girl: I should keep waiting... but I've already waited too long, I can't wait any longer...

After a long moment, the girl's sobs gradually subside. She lets go and escapes from Vanilla's arms.

A smile reappears on her tear-streaked face.

Girl: I don't want to wait anymore.

Girl: Let's go back.

Girl: Vanilla, there are still places we haven't been to!

Girl: It's too early to leave now. Let's keep looking!

Vanilla: Yeah.

Vanilla nods and lets the girl drag her back into the town.

### Vanilla 3, village 1.

The huge floor-to-ceiling windows are already shattered. Only the floor covered in dusty glass shards and paper scraps can prove that this place was once lively.

The shelves in the shop are all askew and empty.

Next to the cash register, an empty candy jar is already covered in dust.

Girl: My parents... probably wouldn't have come to a place like this.

Vanilla: Yeah.

Vanilla: Let's look somewhere else.

### Vanilla 3-Village 2.

The middle-aged couple once said that books were the fruits of human civilization.

As long as knowledge can be passed down, civilization itself will never perish.

Girl: ...

The bookstore is much larger than the library at the outpost. But unlike the neatly arranged library, most of the books here are haphazardly discarded on the floor.

The girl picks up a book from the ground and opens it.

Girl: It's unreadable.

Having been eaten away by insects and rodents over the years, these books can no longer serve their purpose.

The *fruits of civilization* simply lie on the ground, silently rotting away.

### Vanilla 3-Village 3.

Girl: W-what is this place?

Rows of seats are neatly arranged, all facing an unimaginably large wall.

Vanilla: Probably... a cinema.

Vanilla: I've read about it in books.

Girl: A cinema?

Vanilla: Before Nightfall, people sometimes gathered in a room to watch something called a *movie*.

Girl: That sounds wonderful...

Girl: If only I could watch a movie with everyone too.

### Vaniara 3-4.

Girl: Did people use to eat here?

Seeing the symbols of a knife, fork, and plate at the door, the girl asks.

Vanilla: Yes.

Vanilla: There were people here whose job was to cook, and servers who served meals.

Girl: These kinds of jobs existed?

Vanilla: Yes, they were very common before Nightfall.

Girl: Like how when I was little, Mom would cook and Dad would bring the food to the table?

Vanilla: Right, something like that.

Girl: Haha, it must have been fun to eat here.
