### Vaniara 1 - Beginning

???: We're going.

At the outpost's entrance, a middle-aged couple finishes packing and waves goodbye.

The man puts on his coat and hat, and gives the girl behind him another worried glance.

Middle-Aged Man: It's dangerous outside now. Don't go running around.

Middle-Aged Man: Keep an eye on the equipment at the outpost. If there's a problem you can fix, do so. If not, wait for us.

Middle-Aged Man: Oh, and remember to water my plant. Don't let it die...

Middle-Aged Woman: Always your endless chores.

The wife interrupts his rambling reproachfully.

Middle-Aged Woman: Don't listen to him. Just take care of yourself.

Her gaze toward the girl is regretful.

Middle-Aged Woman: I hate to leave the young girl to watch the place alone, with no one beside her.

Middle-Aged Woman: That's too cruel...

Middle-Aged Man: Are you suggesting we bring her along on our suicide trip?

Middle-Aged Woman: Shut up!

The woman glares at her husband.

Middle-Aged Woman: And keep it that way. No good things ever come from that mouth.

Middle-Aged Man: Alright then.

The man walks over to the girl and says with a smile.

Middle-Aged Man: Just wait here for now.

Middle-Aged Man: And if the wait gets too long, if we still don't return when the storm outside has calmed down, then leave this place.

Middle-Aged Man: There's a town 500 kilometers to the west. I know some folks there. When you get there, they will take you in.

After hearing the man's words, the girl ponders for a moment, then asks a question.

Girl: How long is too long?

At this question, the couple looks at each other.

After a moment, the man laughs and squats down to ruffle the girl's hair.

Man: When you can't bear to wait any longer... That's too long.

The girl nods thoughtfully. Smiling, the man stands up and walks away with his wife.

Dreams are the ties that connect the past with the future.

Waking up marks the start of a new day.

It's time to go to work.

### Vanniella 1-Instrumentation

The image on the display flickers with static, but Vanilla is already used to it.

She presses the switch, but the observation equipment shows no response.

Vanilla: There's a problem with the power supply. I need to check it.

Vanilla replaces the worn-out power cord and re-inspects the electrical substation's wiring.

She presses the switch, and the old instrument hums as it turns on at a comically slow speed.

The largest capacitive screen shows a blurry image of the distant DisSea.

Vanilla: A storm is coming.

There's no need to even check the display. Just open the window and look far into the distance, and you can already glimpse the signs of a storm.

Vanilla: Need to reinforce the outer walls in the next two days. The fixed weapons must also be serviced.

### Vaniella 1-Data

Static noise assaults Vanilla's eardrums. She reaches out to turn it off, but then decides to just leave the room and close the door behind her.

Hiss...

The radio makes a brief sound and then falls silent.

Vanilla: Did the capacitor burn out again?

Vanilla skillfully dismantles the radio's casing, and the familiar burning smell rushes into her nostrils.

She turns on her DIY multimeter, sets it to the resistance gear, and patiently performs one test after another.

Vanilla: It's overflowing... That's the one.

Such problems aren't difficult to solve. But due to the lack of resources, the old wiring can't be replaced.

This has caused the power supply system to frequently malfunction in the past two years. But she can only patch it up with the limited materials on hand.

She takes out the soldering iron and melts the solder. Then she pulls off the damaged capacitor and finally finds a fitting replacement after searching all over a few spare parts. After confirming its polarity, she quickly solders it back onto the circuit board.

Having completed the measurement and ensured everything is done correctly, Vanilla breathes a sigh of relief. She cuts off the capacitor's needle and reassembles the radio's casing.

But she presses the switch and only hears white noise from the radio.

Nothing changes no matter which frequency she tunes into.

Vanilla: ...That's all I can do for now.

Vanilla shakes her head, realizing the radio won't be picking up any external signals for a while.

### Vanilla 1 - Plants

The floral fragrance in the hydroponic lab lightens Vanilla's mood a little.

This hydroponic lab was the product of cutting-edge technology from decades ago, used for rapid crop cultivation. It was even illuminated with LED grow lights.

But since the fuel reserve was completely depleted seven years ago, the underground engine has become a mere decoration, and luxuries like grow lights can no longer be used.

Now, the ceiling of the hydroponic lab has been completely removed, and the lab has been transformed into a sunroom.

Vanilla: The growth looks good.

After checking on the crops, Vanilla doesn't forget the desert rose in the corner.

This is the only plant in the hydroponic lab that is grown in soil. It's well cared for, with lush branches, thick leaves, and exceptionally bright flowers.

After carefully watering and fertilizing it, the work here is done.

### Vaniara 1 - End Point (Restaurant)

Today's work is done. Meal time.

She casually picks a few different vegetables from the hydroponic lab and walks into the canteen.

She opens the fridge to find two heads of cauliflower and a small piece of frozen beef that's been there for who knows how long.

Vanilla: ...Hmm, no. Can't finish a whole feast on my own.

Just as Vanilla is closing the fridge, a series of strange noises suddenly comes from the radio room.

???: ...Can you hear... me...

???: I'm at... I need...

Vanilla: This is...

A person's voice. Though soft and blurred by the noise, it's still clearly there.

???: The storm... broken... need...

???: Is anyone there...

She adjusts the frequency band, and the voice gradually becomes clearer.

It's a call for help.

The huge floor-to-ceiling windows are already shattered. Only the floor covered in dusty glass shards and paper scraps can prove that this place was once lively.

The shelves in the shop are all askew and empty.

Next to the cash register, an empty candy jar is already covered in dust.

Girl: My parents... probably wouldn't have come to a place like this.

Vanilla: Yeah.

Vanilla: Let's look somewhere else.

The middle-aged couple once said that books were the fruits of human civilization.

As long as knowledge can be passed down, civilization itself will never perish.

Girl: ...

The bookstore is much larger than the library at the outpost. But unlike the neatly arranged library, most of the books here are haphazardly discarded on the floor.

The girl picks up a book from the ground and opens it.

Girl: It's unreadable.

Having been eaten away by insects and rodents over the years, these books can no longer serve their purpose.

The *fruits of civilization* simply lie on the ground, silently rotting away.

???: What is that... Ahhh...

After a hoarse scream, the radio finally falls silent again.

That's it then. It's been many years since she's seen another human being.

Perhaps it's only right to continue living alone like this.

Girl: W-what is this place?

Rows of seats are neatly arranged, all facing an unimaginably large wall.

Vanilla: Probably... a cinema.

Vanilla: I've read about it in books.

Girl: A cinema?

Vanilla: Before Nightfall, people sometimes gathered in a room to watch something called a *movie*.

Girl: That sounds wonderful...

Girl: If only I could watch a movie with everyone too.

She opens the window. The storm in the distance is growing more tumultuous. The person asking for help is out there. Going alone is simply suicidal.

Vanilla has no reason to save her whatsoever. But before she knows it, the radio handset is already in her hand.

Vanilla: This is the DisSea Observation Outpost. Come in.

???: ...Where? Who are you...

Perhaps the storm is interfering with the signal transmission, and the other party can't hear her clearly.

Vanilla: Tell me your exact coordinates. Over.

???: What are you saying... I'm at... Help me...

???: That's... Ahhh...

The communication cuts off in the middle of a hoarse scream.

Without the exact coordinates, it's evident that the person is beyond rescue amid a perilous storm.

Vanilla puts down the handset and heads straight for the main gate of the outpost.
