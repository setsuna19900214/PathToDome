# Eternal Watch

Within the watchtower, time ceases to flow.
A young guardian, forever on the lookout alone.

Eternal Watch II

## AZ-10001 Eternal Watch

### Vanniella 2 - Starting point

Girl: Ahhh! Help! Monsters!

Girl: Please help me!

This is the first sentence the young girl utters after waking up from unconsciousness.

But once she opens her eyes, she finds herself on a soft bed, the surroundings both familiar and strange.

Girl: This is...

??: Relax. There are no monsters here.

The sudden voice startles the girl. She turns to see a stranger standing in front of her.

She seems to have suffered quite a few injuries, bandaged all over, but her expression is surprisingly calm.

Girl: You... It's you?!

Joy fills the girl's voice.

Girl: You saved me, didn't you?

Vanilla: Yeah.

Vanilla: Stay here and don't move. I'll escort you back once I finish up here.

After dropping these few words, Vanilla hurries out of the room.

Girl: Hey? Don't go! I still have so many things to ask you.

The girl rushes out only to find herself inside a large building.

The person who saved her is standing at a high place, one hand on a fixed machine cannon, the other holding something resembling a telescope. She's looking outside with a worried expression.

### Vanilla 2 - Destination

After what feels like an eternity, the noise outside finally quiets down.

Girl: Heh... Ha...

Gasping for air, the girl sits exhausted on the ground.

Girl: It's finally over.

Seeming a bit uncertain, Vanilla checks from all angles again. After confirming there's no danger, she sits down on a nearby chair.

This battle wasn't easy for her either.

Girl: Do you often face this kind of situation?

Vanilla: No.

Vanilla: This level of intensity is rare. Normally, it only happens three to five times a year.

Girl: That many?!

The girl's eyes widen in surprise.

Girl: You have to face this kind of life-and-death situation three to five times a year?

Girl: And you've always dealt with it on your own?!

Vanilla: Yeah.

Looking at the bandages on Vanilla, the girl suddenly feels a pang of guilt.

Girl: Is it... because...

Girl: Did you get in trouble while saving me...

Vanilla pauses for a moment, then smiles and ruffles the girl's fringe on her forehead.

Vanilla: No.

Vanilla: You've been a big help.

Girl: Really?

Excitement sparkles in the girl's eyes.

Girl: Then we share a bond forged in battle now.

Vanilla: Yes, we're companions now.

Girl: Companions... Hehe.

The girl lifts her head and smiles, then suddenly lowers it again a little bashfully, her gaze darting over.

Girl: If we're buddies... could I ask you for a favor?

Vanilla: Go ahead.

Seeing Vanilla agree so readily, the girl stands up and comes closer.

Girl: In fact, I'm looking for my parents.

Girl: They are both scientists. They left home when I was little and haven't been back for many years.

Girl: I've waited so, so long. So long that I can't bear to wait any longer. So, I set out to find them myself.

The girl pauses, observing Vanilla's reaction before continuing.

Girl: I remember they said they were going to a place called *DisSea Observation Outpost*.

Girl: I figured from the name that it must be close to DisSea. So I searched all the way around here, never expecting to encounter a storm...

Girl: But looking on the bright side, if it hadn't been for that, I wouldn't have met you.

Girl: This place seems to be quite close to DisSea. Do you happen to know where this *DisSea Observation Outpost* is?

Hope fills the girl's large eyes.

Vanilla: For the past ten years, it's been only me here.

Hearing Vanilla's irrelevant answer, the girl looks somewhat puzzled.

Vanilla: I mean...

Vanilla: This is the DisSea Observation Outpost.

Vanilla: Your parents... They're not here.

### Vanniella 2. Battery 1.

Girl: Bullseye!

The girl yells excitedly. Vanilla has no time to look back. She has enough on her plate.

Girl: Hehe! Weren't you guys quite fierce in the storm? Not anymore, I guess?

Girl: My turn now!

Vanilla: A chance. We've almost cleared them out in the middle. Now's the time to break through.

Girl: Charge!

Somehow, the girl even seems a bit excited.

Vanilla and the girl fight smoothly together, so the menacing Corruptors can't harm them.

Vanilla: Move forward. There are still monsters there. Can't relax yet.

Girl: Mmm!

### Vanniella 2. Battery 2.

Flames pour down, and the monsters are obliterated by the large caliber bullets.

Girl: Look! I can do it too!

Vanilla: ...Okay.

Girl: But how come these creatures keep coming... How long have we been fighting? It feels endless.

Girl: Anyway. We'll beat them all. I'm not scared!

The shooting goes on for a while until there's no more movement beneath the high ground.

Girl: Hmph, they were no match for us.

Vanilla: Let's go to the other side. Don't linger in battle.

Vanilla pulls the girl's clutching hand off the trigger.

### Vanilla 2, turret 3.

Girl: Ah!

The red-hot gun barrel emits a dull sound.

Girl: It exploded...

Vanilla: Leave it, and don't touch anything. There's a spare barrel behind. Wait for me...

Girl: Like this... right?

Vanilla turns back to find the girl tossing aside the damaged barrel and skillfully replacing it with a spare one.

Girl: Told you I'm good with machines.

### Vanniella 2 - Step.

Girl: You seem stressed. Is the storm close by?

Vanilla: Yes.

Vanilla: The storm will arrive in about half an hour.

Girl: Is there anything I can help you with?

Vanilla: No need.

Vanilla: You're injured. You should rest.

Girl: But you're injured too, aren't you?

Vanilla: ...I'm fine.

Girl: It's an extra pair of hands, after all. Don't judge me by my appearance. I'm quite good with machines.

Vanilla's attention doesn't seem to be on the girl. Something in her outward gaze suddenly changes, and she pulls the trigger without hesitation.

Da-da-da-da...

The machine cannon emits a deafening sound. The girl leans over for a look and sees a grotesque monster not far away blasted into a pulp by the bullets.

However, that's not their sole enemy. The rest are charging at Vanilla fearlessly.

Girl: There are so many of them... You surely can't handle them all by yourself.

Although the fixed machine gun is powerful, its shooting angle is quite limited.

The girl looks around and just as expected, her rescuer has thought of that too. More machine cannons have been set up at other positions.

Girl: I can shoot. Let me do it!

Vanilla turns her head, her expression somewhat reluctant. But this time, she doesn't stop her.
