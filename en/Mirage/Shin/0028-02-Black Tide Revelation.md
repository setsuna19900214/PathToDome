Emily: When the sun goes down, it's black; when the sun comes up, it's gray... Before we left, I never imagined the pole would be like this. I mean, it's great that we're safe and all, but I'm so antsy now that there's nothing going on.

Shin: But that's only human, Emily... If anything, I'm the selfish one who dragged all of you onto this ship just to satisfy my own desires.

Shin: A miracle of life that transcends humanity's shallow imagination.

Emily: Right, right! Whether they believed it before or not, everyone's committed now. After all, if your dream comes true, all of us will get what we want!

As Emily chatters on, fantasizing about the future, Shin's gaze remains fixed on the glacier on the horizon. Her eyes gleam faintly, as if an invisible rift lurks there, leading to her secret craving.

Joseph glares at Shin through gritted teeth, breathing heavily, but he doesn't lash out. Clearly, he too understands that Shin is one step ahead. He no longer controls the fate of this voyage. He has lost that power.

A brief silence ensues. The crew processes the compromise. Some lower their heads; others clench their fists with gritted teeth. Finally, they slowly begin moving to adjust the ship's course.

Emily: Miss Shin... if we go deeper, will we make it back alive? My... my family is waiting for me to send money home...

She trails off, her voice skittish. When she looks up, she sees Shin's gaze on her. There is no reproach in those serene eyes, calm as the ocean, but Emily panics all the same.

Emily: Oh, look at me! I-It's not that I don't trust you, Miss Shin! I know you'll bring us home safe! I'll... I'll help you. Anything you need, you just ask me!

Shin: Easy, Emily, I understand. But we've come this far. If disaster awaits ahead, we can only face it head-on... And if it's a miracle—well, that's something we'll only discover after taking the risk.

Whether it's the perilous polar waters or the consequences of the crew's mounting tension, she anticipates it all with fierce eagerness, relishing the intoxicating blend of despair and danger.

Outside the porthole, the gray sea stirs. A dark current writhes between the peaks of ice, like a vast, unopened eye.
