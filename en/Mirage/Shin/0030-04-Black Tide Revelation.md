# Black Tide Revelation

Newborn whispers are hidden within the black tide.
She follows these sounds, peering into the abyssal depths, seeking only to witness the world's hidden truths.

As that resonance transforms into a cold pulse, piercing every inch of her consciousness,
she finally understands. The future is meant to be this painful, this beautiful.

Shin: And I kept thinking, what is the responsibility of the one who survives? All of those records, all of that data... they were bought with the lives of my team. I will not allow anyone to waste them. Not even myself.

Shin: What I want to say, through this program, is that we cannot stop at that one chance encounter. If we abandon the pursuit, if we stop our research, we will never know whether what we heard was an echo of evolution, or a true calling.

Shin: As pioneers for humanity, it is the duty of researchers like me to pursue it, to study it. It's a mirror, a doorway, a possibility of what we could become. It will change everything, even... the very way we exist.

Her tone is measured, never rising in passion. But even the crew is captivated by her sermon, irresistibly drawn to the future she paints, the evolution she describes. The seed has been sown.
