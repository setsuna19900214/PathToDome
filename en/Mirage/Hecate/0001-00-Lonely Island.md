# Lonely Island

Transparent glass, icy instruments.
Countless vials and test tubes.
All piled high into a lonely island that never sees the light of day.

In the end, the maiden fails to escape the lonely island.
But the candies given to her, the helping hands lent to her,
Will be forever etched in her heart.

## AZ-10001 Lonely Island

Transparent glass, icy instruments. Countless vials and test tubes. They pile high into an islet that never sees the light of day.

When you realize, you've already fallen into a state of chaos. It feels familiar, much like the mutated spiritual space you've entered before.

But you were in your own office just now, away from any contamination source and didn't hear any warning of intruders.

Whose spiritual space are you in? How can you get out of here?

Still confused, you hear a voice.

Hecate: Why... are you here, Chief?

Hecate's figure is vaguely visible in this space.

Hecate: Even you...

You want to catch up with Hecate to ask her more, but her figure has transformed into a ray of blinding light, pouring down and lighting up the entire unknown space.

Hecate is right in front of you—dark blue hair, a somewhat sheer lab coat, and an overly frail body.

This is Hecate when she was younger. With her eyes tightly shut and an agonized look on her face, she's lying on a lab bench, firmly bound by fastening belts.

Even blocked by the thick crystallized glass, her groans still crawl into your ears.

The voice belongs to the young Hecate. You're knocking on the glass desperately, wanting to ask her what's happened.

Still in a deep slumber, Hecate doesn't respond. Only the voice in your head keeps churning.

Hecate: ...Get out of here.

Hecate: You must get out of this... nightmare...

From the reflection on the glass, you find you're not wearing the Chief's uniform.

You are clad in lab garb.

You search all over yourself and realize all the belongings you carry in the Bureau are now gone.

Only in the pocket of your shirt do you find a strawberry candy, left there by who knows who.

??: What are you doing? Head over to the experiment now.

An unknown man's cold voice comes from the headset, demanding all your attention.

Chief: You are...?

Supervisor: I'm the person-in-charge of this experiment and your supervisor.

Supervisor: The experiment is about to begin. You have only one job, operate the apparatus to increase the dosage. Anything besides that is not your business.

**end**

Supervisor: You need only to fulfill your duties.

You've thoroughly activated Hecate's ability, and all the protective measures in the lab have been paralyzed in an instant.

Hecate: Ahhhhhhhhhhh!

Amid the painful growl, she awakes.

Hecate's voice pierces through the crystallized glass. She destroys everything before her.

In the lab's corridor, she's pacing, taking one step after another.

As if venting all the pain she's had to endure, she's pouring out her power without control.

No matter how you operate the apparatus, you can't stop the explosion of Hecate's ability.

Even after you've ripped off the headset and put on earplugs, Hecate's tortured voice still keeps echoing in your brain.

Hecate: Destroy... Destroy...

You hear the sound of your body being peeled off bit by bit.

Your skin, your hair, and your nails.

Through the camera, you meet each other's gaze.

This is the last image left on your retina.

You press the button. A few seconds later, the girl's painful screams come piercing from the lab bench, and the space in your vision is completely distorted.

As if possessed by a sudden madness, the shadow in the isolation ward slams itself onto the glass. The floor on the south of the ward collapses and the passage is uprooted, devoured by Hecate's ability.

Supervisor: Stimulation by chemical compounds boosts ability reactions. Recorded.

—This is Hecate's ability, but right now it can't be contained by your shackles. It's tearing this space apart following her unstable emotions.

You feel your hands shaking slightly, unsure if you're fearing this terrifying power or realizing the degree of pain you've just imposed on her.

...Are you really going to increase the dosage further?

The experiment procedures are all finished. You let out a sigh of relief.

Supervisor: I thought you would resist like the person before you did.

Supervisor: Congratulations, you've completed the experiment on the subject Hecate.

**【Advance】**

- Let her go

**end**

Supervisor: Haha... Of course I'll let her go, but don't you forget your code of conduct as a lab technician.

Supervisor: Don't think I didn't notice your little tricks.

Supervisor: Doing that won't turn you into a hero. You're only prolonging her suffering.

Chief: ...

Supervisor: By the way, remember to get the drug.

Supervisor: The experimental subject is a little damaged. This pill can help her quickly recover back to the baseline required by the experiment.

**【Advance】**

- The experiment has to continue?

**end**

Supervisor: Certainly, we need more data.

You look at the desk. It's a blue pill.

Just like the pill that makes you forget everything in Alice in Wonderland.

As you put the pill in your pocket, your hand touches the candy inside.

You sigh and take it out.

Instead of the cold blue pill, the sweet candy should have been her gift.

### Instrument B

You press the button. A few seconds later, the attack comes as expected. The floor on the west of the isolation ward is torn by the overflowing shadow and engulfed by darkness.

Supervisor: Stimulation by electric currents boosts ability reactions. Recorded.

When everything quiets down, you hear Hecate's voice.

Her cry shouldn't be able to penetrate the safety glass, but it's exploding in your brain right now.

### Instrument C

You press the button. Her energy erupts immediately, wrecking nearly half of the cameras in the room in a split second.

Supervisor: Stimulation by reducing agent boosts ability reactions. Recorded.

Amid a whine induced by extreme pain, the crystallized glass in the room is quaking unceasingly, and the passage outside has long been obliterated by her ability.

If the experiment keeps going on, the entire lab will be blown up by her ability.

Too late.

Anyway, it doesn't matter anymore, for you can't hear anything. As one of the abusers, this is a fairly nice ending for you.

At last, what you feel from the palm is not coldness, but warmth.

As if your soul has left your body, you see the technician's body lying on the floor.

Your hand is still guided by a force, until it finally pulls you out of this unknown space.
