## AZ-10001 Fall Into the Stars

### test

The dreamworld is no longer bleak.

It now has a starry sky, as well as a lake that holds the stars.

Seated on the surface of the lake is a little girl, writing away in her journal by starlight.

You allow yourself a small smile and walk toward her.

Chief: Dreya?

Young Dreya: Yes?

The little girl looks up at you.

Young Dreya: Who are you?

Chief: Hmm, I'm from the future. We're friends in the future.

Chief: I want to be your friend now also. Do you want to be my friend?

Young Dreya: You're weird.

Young Dreya: You seem like a nice person, though.

Young Dreya: Okay, we can be friends.

### test

You know that her longing for knowledge will one day lead to her death...

Along with many other scientists, by throwing themselves into the deep end in search of a cure for humanity.

They all will become names on a monument.

But as you look at the starry sky reflected in her eyes, you realize that's what they're destined to do.

And by giving their lives for a noble cause, they will become a beacon of hope for future generations.

You raise your hand and activate the shackles. A chain of red thorns comes out of your palm and wraps itself around your hand.

Chief: You will become a great scientist.

Young Dreya: Ah, what's that? It's red, just like the red star.

Chief: You'll become a great explorer, and your pursuit of knowledge will show you the way ahead.

Chief: You'll become an honest-to-goodness scientist, one who is willing to risk her life to seek the truth.

You've been sinking since the moment you activated the shackles and the water sucked you in.

<ins>Young Dreya: Where are you, sir?</ins><br><ins>Young Dreya: Where are you, miss?</ins>

Now you have sunk into the bottom of the lake. Young Dreya searches for you frantically, but you're nowhere to be seen.

And then she sees a shining red star on the spot from which you vanished.

She reaches out to touch it. But it's only a reflection.

She looks up, the celestial globe and journal clutched to her chest.

Chief: Don't be afraid. One day, you'll become a knowledgeable scientist.

Chief: It's true that you and the rest of the expedition won't get out at the end of your adventure, but you will live on in our hearts forever.

Chief: Because you will bring hope to humanity, to the city.

Chief: So your undertaking will not be a failure, but a great success.

Your voice reverberates around the dreamworld. The red star shines brightly, moving across the sky slowly.

You realize at that moment that the red star Dreya saw when she was a little girl has not been her downfall but her guiding star.
